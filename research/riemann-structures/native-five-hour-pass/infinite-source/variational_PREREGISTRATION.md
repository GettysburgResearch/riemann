This is a new, data-informed certificate contract. The floating L32 scout
is used only to choose the fixed center below. No floating output is an
acceptance predicate. The complete physical infinite Gram is imported
from the separately reviewed and directed root acquisition, not rebuilt
by this small variational consumer.

  `lambda=-0.08213318656215898`, `mu=1.1219897873534914`.
- Both-clipped profile; nominal coordinate radius `1/100000000`.
  The actual outward-rounded box and its full displacement are retained.
- Arb precision 192 bits. Exact input rational strings have at most

- Terminal mark `s=99/100`; early height `epsilon=1/100`.

The fixed consumer authenticates both captures before parsing. It checks
the directed acquisition status, primitive manifests, dimensions and
normalization, and retains the inherited physical frame bounds. It does
not claim an independent replay of the infinite Gram producer.

Success requires an inward Krawczyk enclosure of an actual zero of the
two infinite self-consistency equations, an invertible rational
preconditioner, contraction strictly below one, strict both-clipping,
and positive planar quadratic coefficient. The pointwise support theorem
then proves a global minimum among all actual paths with `w` last.

All eight full-path cone outcomes are recorded separately; they are not
required to be positive. For the new countervariation, the fixed mark
must lie strictly on the terminal `v=1` plateau, its true first-order
coefficient must be strictly negative, and the complete original-energy
change at the fixed positive height must have a strictly negative upper
bound. Both its vector quadratic expression and expanded quartic are
retained. This proves a legal three-dimensional path beats the best
entire `w`-last family; it does not prove the improving path globally
optimal among all paths.

Any failed root, clipping, support or descent gate remains a refusal under
this identity. No radius, center, mark or height is adapted after failure.
The endpoint formulas and `C/2` plane normalization are literal. Gram
uncertainty remains in root, gradient and energy expressions. No
independence of repeated occurrences of the unknown candidate or Gram is
assumed: interval expressions enclose their common actual values.

The output records exact outward rational endpoints, source and producer
hashes, every acceptance margin and explicit scope flags. The root owns
execution and independent replay; no result is asserted by writing this
preregistration.

## Independent acceptance checks added before final binding

After the first accepted directed run, the coordinator requested two
verification additions without changing its numerical inputs. Replay
now compares JSON recursively with exact types, rejecting Boolean/integer
aliases. A separate rational polynomial integrator evaluates every one
of the twenty literal moment one-forms on the old and new terminal
piecewise-linear paths. It also evaluates all sixty-four literal
`2 integral x^b d(x^a)` currents and compares them with the exact source
decoder. No clipped-root formula enters this terminal calculation: the
common earlier path cancels, and the fixed mark is separately certified
to lie on its terminal plateau. Both additions must pass on final replay.
