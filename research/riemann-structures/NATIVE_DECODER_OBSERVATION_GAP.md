# Native decoder: the observation and norm still to be preserved

Status: SOURCE-INTERFACE AUDIT, not a new theorem packet.
Scope: The eleven exact sources below, read on 2026-08-31. This is not a
repository-wide nonexistence claim and does not revalidate every inherited
RH reduction.
What was done: Read the named source definitions and resolve their exact Git
blobs. No new numerical experiment, producer, test, source-native residual or
principal-moment estimate was run or claimed here.

The canonical Boolean arithmetic candidate is now known to have a growing
principal moment [F]. The next problem is therefore not to assume that its
complete native decoder is a small perturbation. It is to identify the actual
readout and the norm in which any change of source representative is paid.

The sources provide an exact Boolean source, exact fixed observations of
that source, and an exact principal-family construction once its complete
amplitudes are supplied. They do not, in the statements audited here, identify
the latter amplitudes with the canonical coefficients by deleting the other
labels. The concurrent decoder memo [D] explicitly leaves this identification
open.

## 2. The missing equality has a definite type

For a physical tuple \(\mathfrak a=(P,Q,g,c,d)\), the canonical coefficient
in [D,F] is
\[
 z^B_{\mathfrak a}(t)
 =
 \frac{B_U(gc)B_U(gd)}
 {g^2cd\sqrt{PQ}}\,
 e^{it\log(Pc^2/Qd^2)},
 \qquad
 B_U(a)=\frac{b_U(a)}{\binom{\omega(a)+2}{2}}.
\]
For the actual finite native label set, with its source measures understood,
the comparison would instead begin with
\[
 Z_{\mathfrak a}(t)
 =
 \sum_{\omega:\pi(\omega)=\mathfrak a}z_\omega(t).
\]
Writing this sum does not determine its summands. A continuous colour,
temperature or other source parameter must retain its native integral; the
notation is not permission to replace that integral by a counting sum.

For every physical-only mask the aggregation identity
\[
 C M_{m\circ\pi}=M_m C
\]
is exact finite linear algebra [D, sections 1--2]. It resolves the order of
this mask and this aggregation. It does not establish
\(Z_{\mathfrak a}=z^B_{\mathfrak a}\), and does not control the norm of \(C\)
after the conductor family has been assembled.

In particular, the following are different objects:

1. the exact squarefree Boolean coefficient;
2. a complete native atom carrying a Boolean factor among its weights;
3. the sum of all such native atoms over one physical tuple;
4. the sum over all tuples in one principal member, followed by its square.

The source-first adapter and canonical diagonal theorem address the first
objects and their declared diagonal resolutions. The complete candidate
growth theorem [F] reaches the fourth operation for the defined canonical
arithmetic presentation. No item in the table equates that presentation with
the complete native fourth operation.

## 3. Why “empty colour, no renewal” is not an authenticated decoder

A proposed regional comparison must supply more than names of labels set to
empty.

* It must start with a named finite source occurrence and its regional
  physical observation. The original carrier compensation and incidence
  assignment must be known before the region is selected.
* It must specify the actual coefficient and measure on that occurrence.
  [M] retains \(\omega_i\); [A] retains the complete anchor amplitudes.
  Neither declaration sets the remaining weights to one after a subset of
  label names is removed.
* It must retain complementary source terms or prove their cost in the
  requested norm. The probability average in [C] and the quotient in [H]
  do not make the removed terms orthogonal in principal Mellin \(L^2\).
* It must keep the atomization of the Wick diagonal explicit. Equal physical
  coefficients may recombine before a readout, but the literal diagonal is
  then different from the diagonal of newly grouped coefficients.
* It must distinguish raw source and completed physical coordinates.
  The completed atom \(y_p=p^{-1}U_{p^2}\) in [E] is not the raw
  squarefree atom \(x_p=p^{-1/2}U_p\). A raw mixed-monomial cancellation is
  not a decoder for the already completed canonical coefficient.

These are requirements on a proposed proof, not claims that no useful native
region exists. The sources audited here do not authenticate such a region
merely by saying that its colour and renewal labels are empty. A later
construction may supply one, but its coefficients, observation and retained
measure must then be part of the construction.

## 4. The actual target norm and a falsifiable next step

The original principal Hilbert space is
\[
 \|W\|_{\mathcal H}^2
 =
 \sum_{\iota=(g,\ell,\rho,\sigma,\tau)}
 g^2\ell\rho\,c_\ell c_\rho
 \int_{\mathbb R}|W_\iota(t)|^2
 \frac{|\widehat\kappa(t)|^2}{2\pi}\,dt .
\]
Every carrier dependence on \(t\) remains inside \(W_\iota(t)\).
A scalar autocorrelation formula for bare exponentials must not silently
replace inner products of the actual carrier-weighted functions.

The audited sources give no implication of the form
\[
 \|\mathcal O_{\rm fixed}(R)\|_{L^1(dX/X)}
 \text{ is subpower}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|\mathcal D_{\rm principal}(R)\|_{\mathcal H}
 \text{ is subpower}.
\]
This is a statement about the absent implication in the named sources,
not a theorem that such a transfer is impossible in every case.

There is now a quantitative test of one proposed transfer. For the complete
canonical arithmetic candidate in [F],
\[
 P_B(Y)\gg Y^{1/2}/(\log Y)^{11}
\]
for its specified ratio-eight mask; the fully unmasked candidate satisfies
\[
 P_B^{\rm all}(Y)\gg Y^{1/2}/(\log Y)^{14}.
\]
If a proposed decoder supplies the same-space identity
\(W_{\rm nat}=W_B+R\) and also targets \(P_{\rm nat}=Y^{o(1)}\), then [F]
forces, respectively,
\[
 \|R\|_{\mathcal H}
 \gg Y^{1/4}/(\log Y)^{11/2},
 \qquad
 \|R\|_{\mathcal H}
 \gg Y^{1/4}/(\log Y)^7.
\]
Thus a subpower-error decoder to that candidate cannot simultaneously deliver
the proposed subpower native moment. The alternatives are a materially
different readout/candidate, a large correction retaining native cancellation,
or a weaker conclusion-facing estimate that does not pass through this
positive moment. This does not say which alternative the actual source uses.

The next executable diagnostic remains the one in [D]: choose its exact
dyadic zero-Boolean tuple and reconstruct every native weight over it from a
declared source occurrence. Prove the full weighted cancellation or exhibit
the actual source-defined residual. Arbitrary history weights are not a
counterexample. One resolved tuple would validate or falsify a local decoder
rule; it would not establish the global error norm above.

A separate global comparison must then handle whole fibres and their measure.
The first unspecified occurrence, carrier coefficient, incidence assignment,
or observation parameter is a stop condition for that calculation, not
permission to substitute the canonical coefficient. No full native residual,
WCADD/WCKUM estimate, or RH consequence is supplied by this audit.
