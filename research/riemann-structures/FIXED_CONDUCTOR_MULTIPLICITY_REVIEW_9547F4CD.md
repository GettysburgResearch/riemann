warning into a live fixed-conductor obstruction, with a lower bound
comparable to Y^(1/3)/(log Y)^2 along the declared dyadic horizons. A faithful

over a growing source space. It does not prove native coefficient freedom,

noncancellation, an unpaid analytic error, ONEPLACEWEIL, RELTRACE, RH, or
GRH. No external priority claim is verified. Summaries must retain these
limitations and the precise owner-disjoint class in the matching upper bound.
