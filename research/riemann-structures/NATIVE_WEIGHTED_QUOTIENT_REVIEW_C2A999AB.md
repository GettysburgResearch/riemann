## Deterministic renewal and the transport obstruction

For Top2 support sets A and B, an owner of A lying in B's core forces
`second(A) < second(B)`. The reverse overlap cannot occur simultaneously.
Only the lower-second-owner side loses support, its second owner decreases,
and at most two eligible choices occur per step. No extracted prime returns.
The union of supports is invariant in this declared chart.

At fixed terminal supports, ancestors in one direction are obtained by adding
a subset of the other side's missing support. For each extracted set T there
are at most `2^|T|` paths. Summing in both directions and counting the empty
path once yields the stated
`3^|B-minus-A| + 3^|A-minus-B| - 1` bound. With all original products bounded
by a fixed power of Y, the distinct-prime support bound makes this subpower.
The weighted product majorant is valid for fixed per-step factors C/p; if
both directions are included, `P_X+P_Y-1 <= P_X P_Y` gives the same bound.

This counts deterministic support paths after equal-pair shares have already
been combined. It does not count all literal owner reallocations or arbitrary
carrier histories, and it does not justify multiplying a logarithmic norm loss
over a growing number of renewal steps. Those restrictions are stated.

The concrete transport witness was checked independently. The completed
products of A={2,3,5,7}, B={5,7,11,13} are 1260 and 175175. Extracting 7
gives 180 and 25025 and preserves their ratio. Re-completion gives 60 and
175175, changing the original ratio by the factor 21. Both this ancestry
and the one starting at the re-completed pair reach the clean terminal
products 6 and 175175, with transport ratios 210 and 10.

The two original Mellin exponentials have different frequencies, so a single
t-independent coefficient at the terminal ratio cannot represent both on an
open t interval. Retaining the original ratio, the multiplicative transport
function, or the actual Hilbert-valued coefficient removes this particular
loss. It does not automatically retain other masks or exterior weights.
The note therefore correctly refuses to extend the literal common-extraction
identity through a subsequent re-completion without its operator transport.
