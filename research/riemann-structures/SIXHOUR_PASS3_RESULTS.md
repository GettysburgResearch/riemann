## What this pass changed

The main unconditional developments are source-exact Xi asymptotics and
certified companion-zero/alignment geometry. The new certificates concern
the derivative companion R5, not off-line zeros of Xi. The conditional
physical-capture consequences have a stronger hypothesis than an open
technical side condition: their literal innerness premise already implies RH.

### 0. Hypothesis-strength firewall

For the already-centered f(z)=xi_R(1/2+iz) and any ONE fixed lambda>0,

    RH <=> Theta_0=(f-i lambda f')/(f+i lambda f') is holomorphic
           and contractive in the upper half-plane
       <=> Theta_0 is inner
       <=> both Theta_0 and Theta_5 are inner.

At an upper-half-plane zero b of f of multiplicity m, cancellation gives
Theta_0(b)=-1 and Theta_0'(b)=-2i/(lambda m), contradicting the maximum
modulus principle for a holomorphic contractive function. Real symmetry
then excludes lower-half-plane zeros. The converse follows from the
real-rooted canonical product and the derivative/Rolle argument in the
accepted global-height packet. Lambda=0 is excluded.

This does NOT invalidate the conditional Hardy-space theorems or the
unconditional raw Xi certificates. It means that using those physical
capture estimates as an independent RH proof route would be circular.
Boundary unimodularity alone is not innerness; representing the native
ratio as a quotient of inner factors is not the same as assuming that
both specified raw components are inner. A weaker source-faithful metric
transport, if proposed, must be proved afresh.

See the separately reviewed
[raw-innerness/RH equivalence firewall](../exploratory/XI_RAW_INNERNESS_RH_EQUIVALENCE_FIREWALL.md).

Here lambda_(64) always means the fixed parameter calibrated at height 64,

    lambda_(64) = [(Re psi(1/4+32i)-log pi)/2]^-1,

not the number 64. Combining nodes across calibrations is not an estimate
for a single operator.

### 2. Physical low-pass capture has a different proof

[The Laplace-witness theorem](../exploratory/XI_LAPLACE_LOW_PASS_LOWER_BOUND.md)
gives an explicit lower bound for ||Pi_[0,D] P_U e_b|| using a normalized
Laplace witness at ih. Raw/reduced inner quotients are compared only
through scalar values at b and ih. There is NO projection-band Loewner
inequality obtained merely by multiplying by an inner factor.

The actual Xi imaginary-axis asymptotic pays the witness-side factor.
Under the stated RH-equivalent raw-innerness premise, any surviving node has
positive capture in every fixed positive band after a suitable witness is
chosen. Finite Hilbert--Schmidt lower bounds additionally pay a normalized
Gram/Bessel constant.

[Occupancy-weighted Carleson capture](../exploratory/XI_OCCUPANCY_CARLESON_CAPTURE.md)
replaces a crude node-count penalty by exact local geometric box costs.
The relevant box constant is not itself a Bessel norm. The theorem gives
a weighted cofinal sufficient condition and an unconditional O(log T)
unit-width companion-zero count, but actual cofinal alignment is unpaid.

[Shrinking physical bands](../exploratory/XI_FIXED_LAMBDA_SHRINKING_PHYSICAL_BANDS.md)
translates all forty fixed-parameter nodes into widths 1/256,1/1024,1/4096.
Every node has a positive best witness at each width. All 1,320 cells are
retained: 482 positive lower bounds and 838 zeros. The forty-node squared
HS strict floors are .0004038295912204, .0001432989644834 and
.0000416271711265 respectively, CONDITIONAL on the same RH-equivalent
premise. They do not prove an infinite capture statement.

Independent review recomputed every axis value using finite Dirichlet sums
with rigorous integral tails, then certified all 482 positive floors with
different monotone-corner arithmetic. All 39 full-interval gap comparisons
also pass, proving an unconditional FINITE Carleson-box constant at most
one. That geometric constant is not substituted for the Gram ceilings.

### 3. Double scaling and the global odd-order transition

[Compact-current scaling](../exploratory/XI_ODD_CURRENT_DOUBLE_SCALING.md)
derives the non-Gaussian tilted radial limit from the literal theta kernel,
with weighted L1 error. The order K is allowed to grow with xi.

[Global odd-order saddle closure](../exploratory/XI_GLOBAL_ODD_ORDER_SADDLE.md)
removes the upper cap on growing odd K. It retains the kink where the two
theta arguments meet and proves the Gaussian saddle limit with the actual
uniform domination. If K~b xi exp(gamma xi), gamma>1, then

    saddle a=(gamma-1)xi+log[b/(pi gamma)]+o(1),
    g -> 1/[2(gamma-1)],       p -> (gamma-1)/2.

The carrier transition lies at gamma=2. A balancing model with the wrong
center can miss the actual saddle by an asymptotically unbounded number of
standard deviations.

[The radial semigroup limit](../exploratory/XI_CURRENT_RADIAL_SEMIGROUP.md)
identifies the limiting Bessel-3 kernel and finite-composition convergence.
The finite-xi quantized family is NOT an exact semigroup. The same limit
can arise from a generic Gaussian replacement, so this is not by itself an
arithmetic Xi-selection mechanism or a physical time evolution.

## Current research judgment

The computational advantage should be used to discover and then challenge
patterns: the old quarter-alignment rule broke under a predeclared
extension. For the conditional inner-function model, a cofinal weighted
alignment theorem remains a meaningful operator-theory question. It must
not be presented as a missing lemma that would independently establish RH.

For an RH-directed programme, the higher priority is a source-controlled
observation/metric theorem whose hypotheses do not already imply RH,
alongside the unresolved literal retained-gamma decoder. The unconditional
kernel asymptotics, finite geography and exact source obstructions remain
useful laboratories for deciding whether such a theorem is plausible.

The generalized-L sibling programme has separately built the Segre
character/syzygy bridge, the all-fixed-depth flag ladder, fixed-weight
complex-subspace divisor theorems, and a positive source-level theta
completion. These are not consequences of the Xi experiments.
Its late native-source proofs also give a uniform effective result: every
j>=1 and even k>=96j has a real off-central pair in its corresponding
proper coefficient-source quotient. This does not settle weight24,
simplicity or the location law for that different source construction.
Source-level positive completion alone does not select critical-line zeros.

The shared [next research agenda](../exploratory/SIXHOUR_PASS3_NEXT_RESEARCH.md)
protects a block for the older native decoder alongside a source-controlled
critical-jet comparator and the generalized-L proper theta quotient.
The fitted comparator is a proposal, not an extra actual-Xi certificate.
