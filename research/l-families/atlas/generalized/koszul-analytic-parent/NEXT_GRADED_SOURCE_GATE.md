# Next source gate after the four-hour continuation

The useful next target is compatibility of the new constructible
pole-clearing operation with field extension and arithmetic operations,
before building more examples or attaching archimedean factors. Classical
étale base change already makes ordinary j_*, tensor and each finite Sym
commute with extension of constants at the sheaf level. The open question
targeted here is the resulting one-variable Euler/norm operation after
grading and closed-place splitting are retained, not an obstruction to
that classical sheaf-level square.

**Concrete first theorem target.** Fix the existing S3 source, coherent
quadratic algebra and constructible extension. Derive the exact field-
extension law with every closed-point degree, splitting multiplicity and
Frobenius power retained. Start from the commuting sheaf diagram for
extension of constants and the added standard relation sheaf, then derive
the exact induced Euler-function comparison. The degree grading
must be part of the diagram: a place of degree f splits into gcd(f,r)
places under extension of constants of degree r, and the new residual
Frobenius is raised to r/gcd(f,r). A one-variable norm identity should be
proved or explicitly rejected, rather than assumed from finite Artin
L-functions.

Small exact hostile controls already come from the current source.
For the three unramified classes, the proposed quadratic local norm
comparison F_g(t)F_g(-t)/F_(g^2)(t^2) is respectively

    (1-4t^2)/(1+2t^2),   1/(1+2t^2),   1.

The degree-two defects are precisely sensitive to the actual second
relation module. These equations are local calibration identities, not
an assertion that this is the full global base-change law. The standard
clearing factor itself also has a nontrivial comparison: writing
C_Q(t)=P_(E,Q)(t^2), one has

    C_Q(t)C_Q(-t)/C_(Q^2)(t^2)=P_(E,Q)(t^2)/P_(E,Q)(-t^2).

A successful bounded continuation would explain those defects by an
explicit graded source operation, retain full inertia at all old and new
branches, and expose which one-variable identities cannot hold. A stop
condition is a proof that the requested operation needs two grading
variables or a specified new functor; that is a useful obstruction,
not a reason to fit a compensating scalar factor.

**Archimedean limit.** The present full Euler sources have a unit-circle
meromorphic natural boundary. Under z=Q^(-s), a nonzero meromorphic
gamma/rational multiplier that extends across a boundary point cannot
remove this obstruction: dividing by it would meromorphically continue
the original source. Thus a bridge to the contemporary Rankin--Selberg
or native Mellin programmes must change or enlarge the source observable,
not merely append gamma factors. Compare their actual periods or
cohomological operations, with a held-out twist/base-change/ramification
test. No transfer to a number-field critical line is implied by the
current finite-field construction.

This proposal asks for no additional curve family, large point-count
sweep, or new scalar special-function genre. Its deliverable is one
operation diagram, a proof or no-go statement, and exact hostile source
controls using the already authenticated examples.
