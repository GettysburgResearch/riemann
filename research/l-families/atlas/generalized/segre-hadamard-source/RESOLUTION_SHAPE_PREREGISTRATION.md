# Cheap source-weight preflight for the remaining resolution degrees

This separate acquisition counts domain weight multiplicities only. It
does not rerun degree six, construct an evaluated matrix, eliminate a
matrix, verify the cache mathematically, or certify any kernel or map.
The existing512-column cap and every frozen producer remain unchanged.

The85 D2 generators are read from the exact certified-cache Git blob
`3f1689a7388de3b4cf3f33e3cc7057d51a94118b` at
`742d68b6d3c37191589b0e6463bc122af6d90f76`. Only their ordered degrees
and three-coordinate weights are used. The ten polynomial-variable weights
are the lexicographic weak compositions of3 in3 coordinates, as specified
by `polynomial_weight` in frozen `resolution_replay.py`, blob
`610f50d0e7ef2a47e361ba61a6b4caf53b0fb543` at
`1a5a63f1148fb884e9ae7aae9e9324a40aee9f33`. No executable source is imported.

Both complete blobs are authenticated. To avoid building every cached
kernel object in memory, a JSON decoder reads only the unique D2_columns
array in the exact authenticated cache text. The full blob identity, unique
field, literal generator types, shifts65 in degree4 and20 in degree5,
and weight sums are checked. This is source extraction, not a substitute
for the independent full cache verifier.

For each degree6 and7, enumerate all monomial multiples of these85
generators. Retain every total-weight multiplicity, all maximizing weights,
the total dimension and whether the measured maximum is at most512.
An independent complete-homogeneous dynamic product over the ten variable
weights must give the same distribution. The total dimensions are also
checked against the elementary binomial counts3775 and15400.

A maximum exceeding512 is retained as a failed eligibility result for
the unchanged current elimination contract. It is not permission to
raise that cap or a failure of the proposed mathematical resolution.
No rank or nullity is inferred from these dimensions.

Caps: two degrees,85 generators,10 variables, monomial multiplier degree
at most3, at most16000 enumerated columns in either degree, at most512
distinct weights, source16MiB and output256KiB. Root may run one micro
worker below64MiB under the existing free-RAM gate. The author runs no
scientific job. Output includes both complete source identities and the
new producer/declaration hashes, with no final-resolution success flag.
