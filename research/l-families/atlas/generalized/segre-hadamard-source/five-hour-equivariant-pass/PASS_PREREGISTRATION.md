## New burden

Compute actual maps that Euler characters do not determine:

- the complementary representation action on Chow-base Tor;
- selected change-of-rings homology and, where justified, differentials or
  a proved degeneration range;
- the marked-to-equivariant comparison for the accepted ternary-cube complex,
  including the Hessian-dual top relation when an exact comparison is obtained.

Initial bounded targets are the quotient algebra of Hilbert series
`1+17T+11T^2`, the `340 -> 65` action block, and selected internal-degree
four/five representation blocks. Raw Tor characters do not determine these
maps or prove spectral-sequence degeneration. An exact unresolved block is
reported as unresolved, not as vanishing.

## Acquisition and review

Each producer adds its own primitive-source binding, basis convention,
coverage domain, arithmetic and strict acceptance rule before acquisition.
Accepted maps require exact source replay, composition/compatibility checks
and independent review; matching a dimension or fitted trace is not enough.
The root agent alone commits and pushes reviewed checkpoints. Older untracked
continuation drafts and caches are preserved outside this pass.

Only one memory-heavy job across this five-hour pass runs at a time. The
algebra lane initially holds that slot, uses streamed blocks, and requests
coordination before a job expected to exceed 1 GB or five minutes. Proof work
and small independent checks can proceed alongside it.

The final report records discoveries and failed identifications, exact versus
exploratory evidence, independent replay, checkpoint SHAs and remaining
theorems. Local syzygy geometry implies no global purity or RH conclusion.
