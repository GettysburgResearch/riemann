# The full invariant base repairs finiteness, but not finite freeness

Status: proposed source theorem and bounded replay, outside canonical integration.
Source: the binary cubic Chow module of `a895f47628b0bc7c7ee5e0392df2f79c24166f92`.
All fields have characteristic zero. Gradings below are internal, not cohomological.

## 1. The actual source and its invariant polynomial base

Let V=k e_+ direct-sum k e_- and let I=C2 act by e_+ -> e_+,
e_- -> -e_-. Retain the diagonal action on

    R_r=(Sym^r V) tensor (Sym^r V) tensor (Sym^r V),
    W=Sym^3 V -> V tensor V tensor V,
    S'=Sym W.

The inclusion uses the literal symmetric orbit sums from the frozen source.
Write the two positive W generators as x1,x2 and the two negative generators
as y1,y2. Every generator has degree one. The full invariant base is

    A=(S')^I=k[x1,x2,u,v,w]/(uw-v^2),
    u=y1^2, v=y1 y2, w=y2^2,                              (1.1)

where deg x_i=1 and deg u=deg v=deg w=2. To prove the presentation, reduce
every monomial modulo uw-v^2 to one with exponent of v at most one, using
v^2=uw. The images are distinct monomials y1^a y2^b with a+b even. These
are exactly the invariant monomials. Tensoring with the polynomial x ring
proves both surjectivity and that there is no further relation.

Let M=(S')^- be the anti-invariant A-module. It is generated by y1,y2 and
is not an algebra. Binary Chow freeness gives an equivariant free splitting
R isomorphic to S' tensor B, where the quotient B=R/S'_+R is canonical.
The splitting is not canonical. Its two I-character series are

    B^+(T)=1+2T,           B^-(T)=2T+T^2.                  (1.2)

These follow either from the frozen descent/major-index formula or from
the actual W multiplication maps in degrees zero through three. Taking
invariants of the complete tensor, rather than each factor separately, gives
an A-module isomorphism

    R^I isomorphic to A direct-sum A(-1)^2
                       direct-sum M(-1)^2 direct-sum M(-2). (1.3)

Here A(-j) has its generator in degree j. This is an existence statement
about a module decomposition; no preferred splitting is asserted. In
particular R^I is finite over the full invariant base A. It is not finite
over the smaller k[x1,x2]: its Krull dimension is four, not two.

This is a concrete continuation of the classical Chow-module construction,
not a new finiteness or binary-freeness theorem. The source and the free
quotient are treated in Raicu--Sam--Weyman,
[On some modules supported in the Chow variety, §2 and §3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.10910).

## 2. An explicit infinite minimal resolution

Define pi:A(-1)^2 -> M by pi(a,b)=a y1+b y2 and put

    D = [ -v  -w ] .                                      (2.1)
        [  u   v ]

Over k[x1,x2,u,v,w], direct multiplication gives
D^2=-(uw-v^2) Id. Thus (D,-D) is a matrix factorization of uw-v^2.
Over A the repeated D maps form the graded complex

    ... -> A(-7)^2 -> A(-5)^2 -> A(-3)^2 -> A(-1)^2 -> M -> 0.
               D              D              D          pi (2.2)

Exactness here has a direct source proof. In the polynomial domain
S'=k[x1,x2,y1,y2], pi(a,b)=0 implies a=-c y2 and b=c y1 because y1,y2
are relatively prime. Invariance of a,b implies c is anti-invariant.
Every anti-invariant polynomial is c1 y1+c2 y2 with c1,c2 in A.
Consequently (a,b)=D(c1,c2), so ker pi=im D. Moreover

    D(a,b)=(-y2 pi(a,b), y1 pi(a,b)).                       (2.3)

The domain property shows ker D=ker pi, proving exactness in every
homological degree. All entries of D lie in the homogeneous maximal ideal.
The resolution is minimal, and hence

    Tor_i^A(M,k)=k^2 concentrated in degree 1+2i,
                    for every i>=0.                        (2.4)

In particular pd_A M and pd_A R^I are infinite. This does not contradict
the finite polynomial-base resolution of R: A is now singular, and the
invariants of a free S'-module need not be free over A.

The module M is maximal Cohen--Macaulay of rank one. Indeed x1,x2 are
regular on it; in the remaining odd-monomial module, u=y1^2 is injective
and w=y2^2 remains injective modulo uM. For the latter assertion, w does
not change the y1 exponent of any monomial, so wm in uM forces m in uM.
Thus the displayed regular sequence has length four=dim A. Over Frac(A),
y2=(v/u)y1, giving rank one. Its two minimal generators show it is not
free at the homogeneous vertex.

This is the classical A1 hypersurface matrix factorization, applied to
the actual Chow-source module. The general theory is due to Eisenbud,
[Homological algebra on a complete intersection, with an application to
group representations, §§5--6](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1980-0570778-7).
The special exactness proof above is independent of a general periodicity
theorem; bounded rank calculations are checks, not its justification.

## 3. Hilbert series and the false finite-free replacement

Put L=(1-T)^2(1-T^2)^2. Actual even and odd monomial counting gives

    H_A=(1+T^2)/L,            H_M=2T/L,
    H_(R^I)=(1+2T+5T^2+4T^3)/L.                           (3.1)

Independently, the original source has

    dim (R_r)^I=((r+1)^3 + 1_(r even))/2.                  (3.2)

The first values are 1,4,14,32,63. Equation (3.1) follows from (1.3),
and (3.2) follows from the literal C2 action before any quotient.

The minimal A-free Betti polynomials of R^I are

    beta_0(T)=1+2T+4T^2+2T^3,
    beta_i(T)=4T^(2+2i)+2T^(3+2i),       i>=1.              (3.3)

Every higher row is nonzero. Replacing R^I by the free A-module on just
its minimal generators incorrectly gives degree-four dimension 67,
whereas the actual source gives 63. The first missing relations have
dimension four in degree four. Equivalently,

    H_M/H_A=2T/(1+T^2)

is not a polynomial: a finite graded free resolution would yield a finite
alternating polynomial. The infinite resolution has coefficientwise
well-defined Euler series because its generator degrees tend to infinity;
this does not turn it into a finite complex or a finite determinant.

Thus replacing Sym(W^I) by (Sym W)^I fixes the finite-generation defect
but exposes a genuine infinite-resolution phenomenon. The finite MCM
module remains a valid source object. No arithmetic L-function, spectral
positivity, archimedean completion, or unrestricted source-canonicity
conclusion is inferred from this local invariant-base example.
