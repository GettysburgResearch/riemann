## 3. Original coordinates without the full target dictionary

The target basis remains generator order followed by lexicographic weak
compositions in the ten degree-one variables. For a composition `e` of total
degree `R`, its zero-based lexicographic rank is accumulated, for positions
`i = 0,...,8` and `k = 10-i`, by

`binom(R+k-1,k-1) - binom(R-e_i+k-1,k-1)`, then `R := R-e_i`.

- at most **640 columns and 640 selected rows** per exact block;
- at most 4096 supported original rows per streamed full block;

As in the frozen arithmetic formula, these guards do not instrument every

The same source formula is separately copied for the column modular rank.

bound. The selected matrix has exactly that many rows. Its rational kernel
is calculated, primitive triangular independence is checked, and **every
kernel vector is multiplied by every original full row**. Thus the proposed
kernel is included in the full kernel while restriction gives the reverse
inclusion; the certificate proves equality and completeness. A bad first
prime's nonzero full residual is retained before the fixed fallback. If both
fail, both failures are saved and acquisition refuses.

## 5. Durability, acceptance and scope

Every new weight checkpoint binds the complete source identities, accepted
degree-six identity, new algorithm and declaration bytes, complete target and
domain digests, full matrix digest, full weight table, and that block's
original column indices and coefficients. Writes are temporary-file,
flush/fsync, atomic replace. Incomplete temporary files are never reused.
Completed blocks skip only elimination: row selection, modular rank,
primitive kernel normalization, independence and all full compositions are
checked again. Numeric aliases, floats, duplicate JSON keys and nonfinite
values are rejected at external acceptance.

Degree seven is complete only after all blocks, the actual old quotient,
all polynomial compositions and complete dual Tor weights pass. The separate
global-exactness proof then promotes these actual minimal covers to the full
resolution by the independent Tor support, dimension shifting and graded
Nakayama. A signed Hilbert numerator alone does not establish exactness. The
chosen marked bases are not claimed GL3-equivariant. Resource stops or cap
refusals are retained as such, never converted into mathematical outcomes.
