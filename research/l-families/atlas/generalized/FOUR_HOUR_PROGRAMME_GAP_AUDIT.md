For finite function-field grades, the construction establishes source-defined local factors, ramification/conductor coherence, actual finite-dimensional cohomology, duality and the classical weight theorem. This is meaningful L0-L4 and finite-grade L6-L9 progress in that category. There are no archimedean places in these examples; it does not settle the number-field gamma/root-number problem of L5.
