# Regularized Frobenius tower: pre-execution targets

Declared before a new producer runs. The actual input is the F7 a=1,b=0
cohomological source from freeze64075f1a and its Frobenius powers. This
is not a fresh prime-field search or a fitted scalar product.

1. Compare PBW/Adams multiplicities with the restricted odd-divisor formula
   for every even grade2 through64. Verify the first omitted-grade convention.
2. Use field-extension exponents e=1 through8 and regularization orders
   p=1 through8. Derive Frobenius power traces from the integer recurrence
   of 1+7T^2, independently of the parity formula. Retain the predicted
   first nonzero trace order, rational branch exponent, and exact radius label.
3. Compare finite canonical products and log/exp recurrences to coefficient
   cutoff64 with generator cutoff16. Preserve all finite-head zeros formally;
   do not apply a global nonzero logarithm to a factor with zeros.
4. Check cyclic norms for d=2,3,4 using exact polynomial reduction modulo
   X^d-1, or the equivalent roots-of-unity coefficient sieve; no floating roots.
5. Check transitions p to q, coefficientwise regularization limit, and a
   deliberately wrong unchanged-order base-change formula with exact witness.

Use rational/integer arithmetic, strict type-sensitive serialized acceptance,
frozen source hashes, and meaningful mutation tests. No all-field sweep, dense
matrix, or large eigenbasis allocation is needed. Root runs one job at a time
under the active RAM policy. The proof's natural analytic domains, line/frame
distinction, and global source scope must be reviewed separately.
