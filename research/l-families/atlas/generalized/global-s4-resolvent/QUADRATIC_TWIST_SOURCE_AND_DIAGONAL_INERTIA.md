# The actual quadratic twist of the S4 source

Let Z -> P1_u be the frozen Bring-quartic S4 cover, with p>3,
b!=0 and Delta=256c^3-27b^4!=0. Take the Kummer character chi_u from
t^2=u. This packet constructs explicit quotient curves for all four
nontrivial representations tensored with chi_u. It does not replace the
fixed twist chi_u with the different graded twist chi_u^n.

## 1. Joint source and the infinity inertia that must be retained

The covers Z and P1_t are linearly disjoint over P1_u: the latter
ramifies at u=0, where Z is unramified. Their connected compositum
Ztilde has group S4 x C2. On Z, u has simple zeros at 24 geometric
points above zero and poles of order two at the twelve points above
infinity. Thus Ztilde -> Z is ramified at precisely those 24 zeros.
Riemann--Hurwitz gives

    g(Ztilde)=2g(Z)-1+24/2=49,
    dim H^1(Ztilde)^minus=98-38=60.                   (QT-1)

At infinity the joint inertia is the diagonal order-two subgroup
generated by (double transposition,-1). It is not C2 x C2. Consequently
the twisted stalk is the *anti*-double-transposition subspace of rho.
In the order 1,sign,two,std,tw its dimensions are (0,0,0,2,2).
The Frobenius traces there are

    Q=1 mod4:  (0,0,0,2,2),
    Q=3 mod4:  (0,0,0,0,0).                          (QT-2)

In the latter case each of the two nonzero stalks has eigenvalues +1,-1,
so its local denominator is 1-T^2, not 1. In general those two infinity
denominators are (1-T)(1-chi_Q(-1)T). One can see this directly from the
commuting inertia/Frobenius root permutations of the original quartic.
Their first trace being zero does not mean the stalk has disappeared.

At u=0 all twisted stalks vanish, since chi has nontrivial scalar
inertia and the S4 cover is unramified. At a nonzero finite branch,
inertia is still the S4 transposition and Frobenius on its invariants is
multiplied by chi_(k(v))(u). Elsewhere the unramified Frobenius action is
multiplied by the same residue-field character. These rules specify all
local factors of rho tensor chi_u.

The joint infinity fibre has 24 rational points when Q=1 mod4 and none
when Q=3 mod4. Relative to the old 12/0 fibre it splits twice whenever
the old points are rational; it is unramified over Z there.

## 2. Four explicit quotient sources

Use the frozen notation f=x^4+bx+c and g=-16x^6-40bx^3-27b^2.

The twisted sign factor is the genus-three hyperelliptic curve

    Dchi: s^2=u[256(c-u^2)^3-27b^4].                  (QT-3)

The degree-seven polynomial is squarefree. The biquadratic cover with
square roots of u and the old discriminant has genus-zero chi quotient,
old genus-two sign quotient D, and genus-three sign-chi quotient Dchi.
It follows that L(sign tensor chi_u,T)=P_Dchi(T), of degree six.

For standard take the genus-three quartic cover

    X: w^4=f(x),        X -> E by y=w^2.              (QT-4)

The anti-invariant H^1 of X -> E has dimension four and determinant
P_stdchi=P_X/P_E. Its projective infinity has 3+chi_Q(-1) rational points.
This ratio is the determinant of an actual anti-invariant cohomology
space before any finite polynomial division is done.

For the pair-partition representation use

    Rchi: 4z w^4=-z^3+4cz+b^2.                       (QT-5)

The map to the frozen resolvent curve is (z,Y)=(z,2z w^2), with u=w^2.
As a degree-four Kummer cover of the z-line, it has simple branching at
the three numerator roots and at z=0, and order-two inertia at infinity.
Thus 2g(Rchi)-2=-8+4*3+2=6, so its genus is four. There is one rational
point over z=0, not visible in the displayed affine equation, and
1+chi_Q(-1) points over z=infinity. The anti determinant P_twochi=P_Rchi/P_R
has degree six.

Finally take the normalized connected source

    Cchi: w^4=f(x), v^2=g(x),   Cchi -> C by y=w^2.    (QT-6)

It is a double cover of the frozen genus-seven A3 quotient C. It
ramifies at its eight geometric points y=0 and is unramified at its
four infinity points, hence g(Cchi)=17. Its infinity contribution is
4(1+chi_Q(-1)), namely eight or zero. The permutation representation on
S4/A3 is 1+sign+std+tw. Its twisted trivial factor is the genus-zero
Kummer cover and contributes no H^1. Therefore

    P_Cchi/P_C = P_Dchi P_stdchi P_twchi,              (QT-7)

where P_twchi is an actual degree-ten cohomological determinant. As with
the untwisted packet, this means the multiplicity space
Hom_S4(tw,H^1(Ztilde)^minus), not its three-times-larger isotypic piece.
The anti cohomology of Cchi has dimension 34-14=20, and 20-6-4=10.

All four S4 representations are rational orthogonal and chi is quadratic.
The alternating Poincare form induces alternating perfect pairings on
their multiplicity spaces. Hence their determinants are integral
Q-reciprocal Weil polynomials of degrees 6,6,4,10. These are geometric
theorems using the stated classical cohomology inputs, not conclusions
from a finite numerical root test.

## 3. Complete cohomology and primitive local trace formulas

The tame conductor formula provides an independent dimension check. For
rho of dimension d and characters chi_t,chi_tt at a transposition and
double transposition, respectively,

    dim H^1(P1,j_*(rho tensor chi_u))
         = (5d-6chi_t+chi_tt)/2.                      (QT-8)

This gives zero for the trivial representation and 6,6,4,10 for the
four nontrivial ones. Their regular multiplicities sum to
6+2*6+3*4+3*10=60, agreeing with (QT-1). In particular

    P_Ztilde/P_Z = P_Dchi P_twochi^2
                         P_stdchi^3 P_twchi^3.         (QT-9)

For every extension field let S_rho be the complete sum of twisted
stalk traces, including the infinity rule (QT-2). Direct quotient counts
give

    S_sign = #Dchi-Q-1,
    S_two  = #Rchi-#R,
    S_std  = #X-#E,
    S_tw   = #Cchi-#C-S_sign-S_std,
    S_1    = 0.                                       (QT-10)

At a finite u, if R_u is the normalized old regular fibre count, the
joint regular fibre has R_u points at u=0 and (1+chi_Q(u))R_u points
at u!=0. Its infinity correction is 24/0 as above. This independently
gives #Ztilde, which is compared to #Z+sum d_rho S_rho.
These are normalized regular-character counts of the proved covers;
the replay does not enumerate an affine splitting-variety model for Z
or Ztilde.

## 4. Coverage and scope of the bounded replay

The producer counts every point of Dchi,X,Rchi,Cchi over F_(5^n),
n=1,...,6, for (b,c)=(1,1). It also counts the two frozen F7 examples
through extension degree four, retaining the example (b,c)=(3,4) where
u=1,-1 are rational branches with different quadratic twist signs.
Every field and point histogram is reconstructed from primitive data;
the largest field is 15,625, using the already frozen bounded helper.

For each source it sums every finite u trace and compares the complete
sums, including projective corrections, against the direct quotient
counts. This is not a separate per-u quotient-fibre comparison. Its cohomological
polynomials use the first half of the traces and the proved reciprocity.
The full p5 degree-ten polynomial uses the first five traces and
predicts the sixth. For p7, only four traces are available: the smaller
three factors are complete, but the degree-ten factor is explicitly
recorded only modulo T^5, not represented as a complete determinant.
The large p5 Cchi and Ztilde determinants are constructed from the
proved decompositions, not reconstructed from 34 or 98 point counts.

This packet authenticates the arithmetic twist, especially its diagonal
infinity inertia. Analytic infinite-grade completion belongs to the
separately frozen analytic sequel. It does not claim an RH/GRH result or
external priority for Kummer covers, Artin factorizations, Prym spaces
or the classical Weil theorem. The cohomology and duality inputs are
the same as those cited in the frozen untwisted source proof.
