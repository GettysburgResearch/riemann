## Central question

What remains after exact universal compact-group channels are removed, and
can the residual be made simultaneously

1. sensitive to a named arithmetic or subgroup channel;
2. source-faithful to the physical-squareclass RH bridge; and
3. stable under a declared prime-by-prime aggregation rule?

The pass is deliberately plural: exact detector design and the corrected
function-field bridge form the core, while three one-hour moonshots test
renormalization, interferometry, and guarded cohomology inference.

## Resource contract

- No broad finite-field, zero, Groebner, or trace-cube sweep.
- New public enumerators must refuse before more than 4,096 source atoms.
- Symbolic and exact-rational calculations are preferred.
- Every generated claim must replay under ordinary Python and `python -O`.
- Existing locked atlas data are inputs; they are not silently regenerated or
  reweighted.

## Handoff rule

This file remains the front door.  Each completed packet must state its exact
quantifiers, dependencies, replay command, strongest negative result, and
smallest next theorem.  The final synthesis will distinguish `PROVED`,
`EXACT FINITE`, `CONJECTURE`, and `SCOUT` records.
