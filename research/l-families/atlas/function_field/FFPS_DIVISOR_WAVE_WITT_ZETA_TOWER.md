## 0. Outcome

The predecessor proves, for the untruncated squarefree divisor polynomial,

\[
 D_\xi(N)=\prod_{p\mid N}(p^{i\xi}+p^{-i\xi}),
\tag{0.1}
\]

and initially for `Re(s)>1`,

\[
 F_\xi(s)=\sum_{67\nmid N}{\mu(N)D_\xi(N)\over N^s}
 =\prod_{p\ne67}\left(1-(p^{i\xi}+p^{-i\xi})p^{-s}\right).
\tag{0.2}
\]

Its first factorization extracted only the two degree-one shifted zeta
functions and left a correction normal for `Re(s)>1/2`.  The local polynomial
has a complete **free-Lie/Witt tower**.

Put `z=p^(i xi)` and `x=p^(-s)`.  Let `V` be the two-dimensional torus
representation with weights `z,z^-1`, and let `L_n(V)` be degree `n` in the
free Lie algebra generated by `V`.  Write

\[
 \ell_{a,b}
 ={1\over a+b}\sum_{d\mid(a,b)}\mu(d)
 {((a+b)/d)!\over(a/d)!(b/d)!},
 \qquad a+b\ge1.
\tag{0.3}
\]

This is the multiplicity in `L_(a+b)(V)` of weight `z^(a-b)`.  The PBW
identity gives the exact local factorization

\[
\boxed{
 1-(z+z^{-1})x
 =\prod_{n\ge1}\prod_{a+b=n}
  (1-z^{a-b}x^n)^{\ell_{a,b}}.}
\tag{0.4}
\]

Consequently, in the common absolute-convergence half-plane,

\[
\boxed{
 F_\xi(s)
 =\prod_{n\ge1}\prod_{a+b=n}
 \zeta^{(67)}\!\left(ns-i(a-b)\xi\right)^{-\ell_{a,b}},}
\tag{0.5}
\]

where `zeta^(67)(u)=zeta(u)(1-67^-u)`.

For any fixed `R`, extracting only the factors `n<=R` leaves an Euler product
`E_(R,xi)(s)` whose local factor is

\[
 1+O_R(p^{-(R+1)\operatorname{Re}s}).
\tag{0.6}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
 F_\xi(s)=E_{R,\xi}(s)
 \prod_{1\le n\le R}\prod_{a+b=n}
 \zeta^{(67)}\!\left(ns-i(a-b)\xi\right)^{-\ell_{a,b}},}
\tag{0.7}
\]

with `E_(R,xi)` normally convergent for

\[
 \operatorname{Re}s>{1\over R+1}.
\tag{0.8}
\]

Equivalently, the residual threshold is `1/(R+1)`.  Thus every finite right half-plane can be reached by extracting finitely many
explicit shifted-zeta factors.  The first four packets are

```text
n=1: weights +1,-1;
n=2: weight 0;
n=3: weights +1,-1;
n=4: weights +2,0,-2.
```

In particular,

\[
\boxed{
 F_\xi(s)
 ={E_{2,\xi}(s)\over
   \zeta^{(67)}(s-i\xi)\zeta^{(67)}(s+i\xi)
   \zeta^{(67)}(2s)},}
\tag{0.9}
\]

where `E_(2,xi)` is normal for `Re(s)>1/3`.  The universal degree-two factor
`1/zeta^(67)(2s)` was hidden inside the predecessor's half-plane correction.
At `xi=0`, the first layer is therefore

\[
 F_0(s)={E_{2,0}(s)\over\zeta^{(67)}(s)^2\zeta^{(67)}(2s)}.
\tag{0.10}
\]

This is a factorization theorem, not a bound.  At the critical coefficient
line `Re(s)=1/2`, the degree-one factors still carry the individual zeta-zero
obstruction.  The degree-two reciprocal factor vanishes at `s=1/2`, but an
individual zeta zero at `1/2+i\gamma` produces poles in both shifted
`n=1` factors when `xi=\gamma`.  No cancellation statement is inferred.

## 1. PBW proof

The tensor algebra on `V` has torus-character series

\[
 \operatorname{ch}T(V)={1\over1-(z+z^{-1})x}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

The Poincare--Birkhoff--Witt theorem identifies `T(V)` with the symmetric
algebra on the free Lie algebra `L(V)`.  A weight `z^(a-b)` in degree `n=a+b`
with multiplicity `ell_(a,b)` contributes

\[
 (1-z^{a-b}x^n)^{-\ell_{a,b}}
\]

to the symmetric-algebra character.  Inverting (1.1) proves (0.4).
The multigraded Witt formula gives (0.3); all multiplicities are nonnegative
integers.

Substitute `z=p^(i xi)`, `x=p^-s` prime by prime and multiply over `p!=67` to
obtain (0.5) in `Re(s)>1`.

## 2. Finite-depth analytic factorization

Truncate the product (0.4) to degrees at most `R`.  By exact equality of formal
power series, the quotient by the truncated product has no term of degree
`1,...,R`.  On the unit circle `|z|=1`, its first possible term is bounded by
`O_R(|x|^(R+1))`.  Therefore the logarithms of the local correction factors
are absolutely summable when `(R+1)Re(s)>1`, proving (0.7)--(0.8).

No infinite rearrangement outside the original absolute-convergence domain is
needed: (0.7) is obtained for each finite `R`, then analytically continued by
its normally convergent correction.

## 3. Finite-local cancellation firewall

A hypothetical zero `rho=beta+i gamma` with `beta>1/2` appears in the
`n=1` factors at `s=beta`, `xi=gamma`.  Only the local polynomials at primes
`2` or `3` can vanish there, because for `p>=5`,

\[
 |(p^{i\gamma}+p^{-i\gamma})p^{-\beta}|
 \le2p^{-\beta}<1.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Thus the mode with the finite Euler factors `2,3,67` deleted has a genuine
shifted-zeta pole unless another explicitly displayed higher-degree global
factor intervenes.  For `n>=2`, `Re(n beta)>1`, so those zeta factors are
finite and nonzero.  This is the **finite-local cancellation firewall**.

It supplies a clean individual-zero detector but no coefficient estimate for
the truncated wavelet.  Passing from the meromorphic untruncated mode to the
sharp balanced hyperbola remains the analytic burden.

## 4. Relation to the high gcd-wave gate

The sharp divisor wavelet is a difference of two balanced divisor intervals.
Smoothing or Perron expansion inserts complex shifts into `s`; formula (0.7)
then applies pointwise to every frequency and shift.  It replaces the vague
Euler correction by an arbitrary-depth explicit zeta tower.

A future proof of `HIGHFARGCDWAVE` must control the resulting shifted reciprocal-
zeta products jointly in Fourier frequency, Perron shift, gcd core, and
height block.  The tower shows exactly where the zero response lives, but it
does not bound it.
