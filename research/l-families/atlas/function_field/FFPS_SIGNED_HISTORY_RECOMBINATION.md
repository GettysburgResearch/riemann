Status: **exact source-faithful aggregation identities and a principal transfer
corollary using inherited estimates; no new signed trace estimate or RH proof**.

Scope: the frozen finite-horizon source and literal normal ordering of
L-106120/L-106131/L-106191/T-106140. Only Boolean factorization histories and

fixtures frozen at `5dc85cd5e38384efc3b8a65496f03e1c2ae599e3`.

What was actually run: exact Hermitian-polynomial coefficient matrices for
two live owner panels and the live hundred-history control, native reciprocal
weight checks, primitive Git-source authentication, and normal/optimized
unit tests. The amplitudes containing Mellin phases remain symbolic.

Smallest remaining gap: estimate the complete, signed, history-aggregated
principal current between all distinct retained groups, including equal

## 0. Decisive verdict

The negative hundred-history example is entirely a representation correction
at one arithmetic pair. It is not fresh principal cancellation. Its
**principal-weighted** complete contribution is covered by the inherited
principal diagonal and subpower representation multiplicity.

Its additive and Kummer contributions separately need not be covered by that
bound. Their correction weights contain an extra conductor-sized factor,
and the frozen source explicitly leaves the amplified equal-output problem
open. But those two dangerous corrections cancel in the signed combination
`P=A-K`, leaving precisely the paid principal correction.

Therefore the principal-specific programme may combine these history labels
without first solving the stronger, arbitrary-coefficient additive-adapter
problem. The complete uncentered principal moment is exactly unchanged;
the centered principal trace changes only by a paid error. This does not
permit residue-cell aggregation of distinct arithmetic pairs, and it does
not silently change the literal Wick convention.

## 1. Declared aggregation and normalization

Fix a source horizon `Y`, its frozen cutoff, and one complete fibre

\[
 \iota=(g,\ell,\rho,\sigma,\tau),\qquad
 c=\ell u,\quad d'=\rho v.
\]

Here `d'` denotes a reduced core; below `d_iota` is a scalar atomic weight.
Write the source-dual coefficient as

\[
 \widetilde z_{\omega}(t)
 =g\ell\rho z_{\omega}(t)
 ={\gamma_\omega(t)\over guv\sqrt{PQ}}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Partition the literal atoms by a label `a` retaining the arithmetic tuple
`(P,Q,c,d')` and every other source label except the stated representation
histories. In particular this is **not** the physical residue-cell quotient.
Let `H_a` be the remaining finite history set and set

\[
 Z_a=\sum_{h\in H_a}\widetilde z_{a,h},\quad
 E_a=\sum_{h\in H_a}|\widetilde z_{a,h}|^2,\quad
 \delta_a=|Z_a|^2-E_a,\quad
 \Delta_\iota=\sum_a\delta_a.
\tag{1.2}
\]

All terms in `H_a` have the same physical residues, additive features,
character features, and fixed owner quadratic classes. Thus combining them
leaves **every complete family member** unchanged. All coefficients,
equal-pair shares, reciprocal weights, Mellin phases, masks and signs are
summed literally; they are not replaced by independent parameters.

If a further native label affects a feature, it must be retained in `a`.
Nothing here licenses grouping incompatible source regions. The original
complete owner sums still occur before the family square.

Set

\[
 d_\iota={(\ell-1)(\rho-1)\over\ell\rho},\qquad
 p_\iota={c_\ell c_\rho\over\ell\rho},\qquad
 k_\iota=d_\iota-p_\iota,
 \quad c_q={q+1\over q-1}.
\tag{1.3}
\]

These are the **source-dual rescaled** additive, principal, and total
nonprincipal atomic weights. In the original coefficients they correspond
respectively to

\[
 g^2\ell\rho(\ell-1)(\rho-1),\quad
 g^2\ell\rho c_\ell c_\rho,\quad
 g^2\ell\rho((\ell-1)(\rho-1)-c_\ell c_\rho).
\tag{1.4}
\]

The exterior weights in T-106140 are therefore retained exactly.

## 2. `SRECOMB-1`: literal and grouped normal ordering differ by a diagonal ledger

Let `A_lit,P_lit,K_lit` be the original fixed-fibre Wick channels. Define
the **auxiliary** grouped versions using the same complete members but
subtracting `sum_a |Z_a|^2` instead of `sum_a E_a`. Then, identically in the
actual native coefficients,

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 A_{\rm lit}&=A_{\rm grp}+d_\iota\Delta_\iota,\\
 P_{\rm lit}&=P_{\rm grp}+p_\iota\Delta_\iota,\\
 K_{\rm lit}&=K_{\rm grp}+k_\iota\Delta_\iota.
 \end{aligned}}
\tag{2.1}
\]

Proof: the member squares are unchanged, and the subtracted diagonal changes
from `E` to `E+Delta`. Each channel has precisely its atomic weight (1.3).
The total nonprincipal weight is `d_iota-p_iota` by the frozen Gauss identity.
This proves all three identities without a positivity assumption.

In particular,

\[
 \boxed{
 A_{\rm grp}-K_{\rm grp}=P_{\rm grp},\qquad
 d_\iota\Delta_\iota-k_\iota\Delta_\iota
 =p_\iota\Delta_\iota.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

These are signed identities. Taking absolute values in the two channel
corrections first destroys the cancellation in (2.2).

The grouped quantities are not being substituted for the literal Wick
channels without correction. Equation (2.1) is the exact conversion ledger;
it recovers the original normalization on demand.

### Distinct arithmetic pairs are unchanged

Write

\[
 S_\iota(a,b)=
 \left(\mathbf1_{x_a=x_b}-{1\over\ell}\right)
 \left(\mathbf1_{y_a=y_b}-{1\over\rho}\right).
\]

For `a!=b`, the complete history cross term is exactly

\[
 \sum_{h\in H_a,\,j\in H_b}
 \widetilde z_{a,h}\overline{\widetilde z_{b,j}}S_\iota(a,b)
 =Z_a\overline{Z_b}S_\iota(a,b).
\tag{2.3}
\]

No part of that cross term enters `Delta`. The correction is supported only
on equal arithmetic pairs with different histories, a definite
equal-output subledger. The complete physical-output quotient `nu` may
identify additional labels; no classification of that larger quotient is
needed or claimed.

The converse identification is not available: because `a` retains every
non-history label, `a!=b` need not mean different arithmetic tuples. Equal
tuples in different retained carrier, colour, mask or other source labels
remain distinct groups. Their cross terms are not paid by this correction.
As a structural example, two such singleton groups with coefficients `1,1`
have `Delta=0` but principal cross term `2p_iota`; this does not assert that
those scalars form an admissible native input. No uniqueness of the remaining
labels is assumed. Any complete grouped principal estimate must include
these terms as well as genuinely distinct arithmetic-pair interactions.

## 3. `SRECOMB-2`: the complete principal transfer is inherited-paid

Let `m_Y` bound the sizes of the groups `H_a`, uniformly in the retained
source labels. For a group of size `m`, Cauchy gives

\[
 -E_a\le\delta_a\le(m-1)E_a,
 \qquad |\delta_a|\le(m-1)E_a.
\tag{3.1}
\]

For `m=1` the correction is exactly zero. The negative part is bounded by
`E_a` without a multiplicity factor.

For the declared history grouping, the full one-sided physical product has
a unique owner squarefree kernel. L-102883 bounds its ordered triple count
by `d_3(core)` and its dyadic block-label count by a fixed power of `log Y`.
The product of the two one-sided counts is therefore

\[
 \boxed{m_Y=Y^{o(1)}.}
\tag{3.2}
\]

This is an imported uniform representation estimate, not an inference from
the hundred-history control. All other labels are held fixed, so this count
does not absorb an unproved carrier, conductor, or geometric multiplicity.

Now use exactly T-106140's complete fixed-horizon conductor sum and measure

\[
 d\mu(t)={1\over2\pi}|\widehat\kappa(t)|^2dt.
\]

Its corrected principal diagonal is

\[
 \mathfrak D_{\rm PP}(Y)
 =\sum_\iota\int p_\iota
       \sum_{a,h}|\widetilde z_{a,h}(t)|^2d\mu(t)
 =Y^{o(1)}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

Equation (3.3) is precisely T-106140.9--.10, equivalently corrected
L-106121.9, after (1.1). It is not the unproved complete-family atomic bound.
Combining (3.1)--(3.3) proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \left|\mathfrak C_P(Y)\right|
 :=\left|\sum_\iota\int p_\iota\Delta_\iota(t)d\mu(t)\right|
 \le(m_Y-1)\mathfrak D_{\rm PP}(Y)
 =Y^{o(1)}.}
\tag{3.4}
\]

The same estimate bounds the total variation of this correction under this
particular positive principal measure. Its negative part has the sharper
bound `D_PP(Y)`. The grouped principal diagonal also satisfies

\[
 0\le\mathfrak D_{{\rm PP},{\rm grp}}(Y)
 \le m_Y\mathfrak D_{\rm PP}(Y)=Y^{o(1)}.
\tag{3.5}
\]

### Exact quantifier boundary

This uses the frozen horizon, cutoff, coefficient maps, equal-pair gauge,
source masks and fixed outer observation of the cited source. All original
physical-shell and remaining source labels stay inside their complete
members. The full `g,ell,rho,sigma,tau` sum and the original Mellin integral
are retained. Finite source summations and integrability are inherited from
that source contract.

Neither (3.4) nor (3.5) asserts a pointwise-in-`t` subpower bound. No extra
maximum over horizons, source regions, coefficients, or Mellin parameters
has been introduced, and no maximal operator has been moved through an
integral or signed subtraction. A later maximal variant would need its own
source-matched diagonal bound. This packet is a consequence of the two
named inherited estimates, not an independent reproof of their analytic
source reductions.

## 4. Principal-specific geometry may forget histories, not arithmetic pairs

Performing the complete signed recombination before absolute values yields

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak P^\circ_{\rm lit}(Y)
 =\mathfrak A^\circ_{\rm grp}(Y)
  -\mathfrak K^\circ_{\rm grp}(Y)
  +\mathfrak C_P(Y).}
\tag{4.1}
\]

Consequently the centered principal subpower target is invariant under this
history aggregation up to a paid error. Still more directly, the full
uncentered principal moment is **exactly** invariant:

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak P_{\rm PP}
 =\mathfrak D_{{\rm PP},{\rm lit}}+\mathfrak P^\circ_{\rm lit}
 =\mathfrak D_{{\rm PP},{\rm grp}}+\mathfrak P^\circ_{\rm grp}.}
\tag{4.2}
\]

Both expressions use the identical complete source member. Thus a
principal-specific object may carry aggregated history coefficients and
the paid conversion ledger, without faithfully carrying every history
idempotent required by a universal additive-channel adapter. This is a
smaller source-realization burden, not a proof that the smaller object is
constructible, uniformly bounded, or enough to estimate the current.

The direct principal bound, or a bound for the jointly assembled difference
in (4.1), still remains open. Separate bounds on the grouped additive and
Kummer channels would suffice, but are not introduced as necessary new
requirements. No third amplifier is added.

### What the principal cancellation does not pay

The amplification ratio is

\[
 {d_\iota\over p_\iota}
 ={(\ell-1)^2(\rho-1)^2\over(\ell+1)(\rho+1)}
 \asymp\ell\rho.
\tag{4.3}
\]

Hence (3.3) does not, by this argument, bound the separate corrections
`sum integral d_iota Delta_iota` or `sum integral k_iota Delta_iota`.
This is exactly the source-dual warning in L-106191; its `WCEQ106191`
diagnostic remains open. The original `WCADD/WCCORR` and `WCKUM` gates are
not declared equivalent to their grouped versions.

Generic additive/Kummer information loss under aggregation is real.
Principal-specific signed recombination cancels the dangerous relative
weight. These two conclusions are compatible and both are retained.

## 6. What to do next, and what not to pursue

The history-only negative block has served its purpose as a typing control.
Growing that example does not attack a new principal obstruction: (3.4)
already pays its principal representation ledger.

The useful continuation is the signed, jointly assembled principal current
on all distinct retained groups, keeping the original source weights and
outer observation. This includes genuinely distinct arithmetic pairs and
equal tuples distinguished by remaining source labels. A proposed geometry
should first show that it carries these aggregated amplitudes and their
principal pairing, rather than merely retaining residue-cell sums. The
four-atom live collision panels show why that last quotient still forgets
different arithmetic interactions.

Open gates remain native geometric realization, the complete cross-group signed
estimate, admissible native coefficient-family nonfactorization, `WCEQ`,
`WCADD/WCCORR`, `WCKUM`, and the RH-facing principal bound. RH and GRH remain
unproved. No external novelty claim is made for the finite aggregation
identity; the contribution is its exact source-weighted transfer boundary.
