# Research layer

The research layer is where mathematics, experiments, refutations, and open programs are meant to be read.

- [`integrated/`](integrated/README.md) contains curated proof-bearing packets extracted from exact reviewed source commits.
- [`exploratory/`](exploratory/README.md) explains the lightweight expectations for speculative work.

Existing historical source paths such as `claims/`, `experiments/`, `literature/`, `reports/`, and `audits/` remain authoritative for their exact source commits. Integrated packets do not replace or rewrite those records; they provide stable readable residency on `main`.

A packet’s status belongs to the exact source and review scope named inside it. New strengthenings should be proposed separately.
