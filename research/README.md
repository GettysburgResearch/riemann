# Research layer

This is the readable scientific layer.

- [`RESULTS_INDEX.md`](RESULTS_INDEX.md) maps the reviewed project, including local proof packets, source-pinned families, blocked interfaces, and current frontiers.
- [`integrated/`](integrated/README.md) contains proof-bearing packets extracted from exact reviewed commits.
- [`IMPORT_MANIFEST.md`](IMPORT_MANIFEST.md) gives the safe extraction order for the Weil/screw and operator stacks.
- [`exploratory/`](exploratory/README.md) explains the lightweight expectations for speculative work.

Existing source paths such as `claims/`, `experiments/`, `literature/`, `reports/`, and `audits/` remain authoritative for their exact commits. Integrated packets do not rewrite those records; they provide stable readable residency.
