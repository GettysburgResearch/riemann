# Targeted comparison for the cyclic sextic relative quotient

The family and its chosen primary FHL presentation are stated in [README](README.md) and checked in [the independent review](INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md). This is not an exhaustive priority search; all unreviewed preprint claims and the quadratic detector transfer remain inputs rather than independently established results.

For a good prime label B with `NB≈X`, [De Faveri Corollary 1.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04045v1#S1.Thmtheorem5) applies directly to the sextic FHL character ρ_B, because B is squarefree. At σ=4/5 it gives

    Σ_(B prime; NB≈X) N(4/5,T;ρ_B)
       ≪ X^(209/326+ε) T^(9/7+ε).

The same holds for its conjugate χ=ρ_B⁵ in a symmetric height window. This improves the candidate fourth-witness exponent 59/90 for the sextic factors, though it does not improve the full relative-quotient exponent 2/3, which is currently set by the cubic factors. The source's zero-density σ range and explicit T exponent must not be confused with a conductor-ordered mixed-label theorem. The primary presentation makes the cubic factor exactly the principal cubic family as detailed in the review; matching good conductors alone would not suffice.

For those cubic factors, [rStation paper 52 v5, Theorem 5.7](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/52/v5) asserts `X^(2/3+ε)` at σ=4/5 and sufficiently small positive-power heights, already used in the family result. The earlier [Blomer–Goldmakher–Louvel density theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.1650) and De Faveri's n=3 Corollary 1.5 give the weaker exponent 8/11 at this σ for squarefree prime labels. The current source proof and the conventional identification of its L-functions remain subject to the qualifications in the family note.

The [Gao–Zhao sextic paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01885) concerns central moments and one-level density of low-lying zeros; this is not a zero-count in `Re s≥4/5` and does not supply the desired exceptional bound. Work on cyclic extensions over Q or near-1 Chebotarev statements discussed in [the cyclic-field comparison](../2026-10-29-optimized-cyclic-prior-art/README.md) is not a direct fixed-base E=Q(ω), σ=4/5 comparison. The [Cureton–Thorner preprint](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00200v2) asserts a much narrower all-height zero-free half-plane `Re s>31/32` for all finite-order Hecke characters, subject to its formal and analytic interpretation; even if accepted, it does not settle a rectangle reaching 4/5. The more specific inherited Eisenstein candidate is discussed separately in the family note.

The named direct source estimates, after choosing a sufficiently small common positive height exponent, therefore give sextic 209/326, cubic 2/3 and the separately source-dependent quadratic 13/25, with their maximum 2/3. We did not locate a stronger directly applicable full relative-quotient density theorem in this targeted search.
