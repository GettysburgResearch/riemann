## 2. Top-level proof structure in Lean

The chain from the exported theorems down to the analytic inputs is short and explicit. Each arrow is "is proved from".

```text
OAI.riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_seven_eighths_lt_re,
OAI.DirichletCharacter.LFunction_ne_zero_of_seven_eighths_lt_re          (Nonvanishing.lean)
 <- ProbeFinalAssemblyUnconditional.{zeta,dirichlet}_nonzero             (Detector/FinalAssemblyUnconditional)
    = {zeta,dirichlet}_of_certified detector_certified_bands
 <- detector_certified_bands : DetectorCertifiedBands                    [Lemma 18.1 input]
      from Energy.CertifiedExistence.terminal_certificate with kappaPlain = 3/4+2Δ
 <- fine_moments_of_certified -> source_moments                         (FinalAssemblyCertifiedBands)
      combines three moment fields:
        source_batch_inverse_fields          [Lemma 17.1 marked + raw inverse moment]
        source_batch_plain_marked_fine       [Lemma 18.1 case z>0]
        source_batch_plain_unmarked          [Lemma 18.1 case z=0]
 <- chosen_input_of_fine (exists_high_data_fine, exists_source_data,
      source_count_parameters)                                           [Prop 20.3 choices]
 <- common_probe_of_chosen_moments: chosen_data_height [Lemma 11.1],
      fixed_high_bound [Secs 10, 19, 20 high side], probe_low [Prop 15.3],
      actual_source_analytic [eq. (10.2)]                               (FinalAssemblyChosenData)
 <- HeckeCommonProbe.beta_le_seven_eighths                               [Prop 2.1 at σ0 = 7/8]
 <- HeckeDirichlet.{dirichlet,zeta}_seven_eighths_of_hecke               [Prop 11.3 at σ0 = 7/8]
```

The contradiction is set up on `HeckeZeroSupremum.beta := sSup ({1/2} ∪ {Re s : L(s,χ)=0, Re s>0, not the pole})`, over Lean's `HeckeFamily.Character` (finite-order ray characters of `O = Z[ω]`). Assuming `7/8 < beta`, it puts `Δ := beta - 7/8`, builds `HighData Δ` and `SourceData`, and derives the `PrimitiveContract` of Prop 2.1. That is the paper's architecture (Sec. 2, Sec. 12, final paragraph 16454-16463), with one exception described next.

**The only upper bound on β used is `beta_le_one`.** `Detector/FinalAssemblyHigh.lean:115` derives `beta - 7/8 ≤ 1/8` from `HeckeZeroSupremum.beta_le_one`, which follows from the Euler product (`LFunction_ne_zero_of_one_lt_re`). There is no `11/12` or `1/24` bound on β anywhere in the closure. The only `11/12` occurrences are Gamma-function arguments in `QuadraticSieve/MainTermDifference.lean`. The Part I constants (`1021/25000`, `43/300`) do not occur, and there is no sextic large sieve. The paper instead invokes Thm 3.1 at 6812-6819 to get `Δ ≤ 1/24` and `κ ≤ 5/6`.

## 3. Node-by-node correspondence

### 3.1 Codes

* **F**: spot-checked here. A Lean declaration in the closure has the same mathematical content, with the paper's implicit constants made explicit where stated.
* **Fv**: spot-checked here. A Lean counterpart exists but is a *variant*: specialized (one σ0, one instance), restated as an interface field, stronger, or weaker. The difference is noted.
* **C**: name- or keyword-level candidate only (lean_node_candidates.py plus browsing). The statement was **not** compared.
* **B**: bypassed. No counterpart in the closure, and the Lean route does not need the node (Sec. 4).
* **N**: no counterpart found by name search. A counterpart may exist under another name, or the content may be absorbed into a larger Lean proof.

Lean declarations are given relative to `OAI.SevenEighths` and files relative to `OAI/NumberTheory/DirichletL/`, unless they start with `OAI.`.

## 4. Where the Lean development departs from the paper's architecture

1. **No Part I.** The paper's Part II starts from `β* ≤ 11/12` (Thm 3.1), which gives `Δ ≤ 1/24` and `κ ≤ 5/6` and so the bin ceiling `δ ≤ 5/6` (6812-6826).
   * Lean uses only `β ≤ 1`, so `Δ ≤ 1/8`, `κ = 3/4+2Δ ≤ 1` and `δ ≤ 1`.
   * Bins with `δ = 2a-1 > 5/6` get their own row count, `CountParameters.high`, with exponent `1-δ+78ε+ε_m`. That count is the no-slot amplified inverse estimate `e(r) - δr` with `e(r) = max{1,(1+5r)/6}`, at `r ≥ 1-76ε`.
   * Their high exponent is then `≤ -1/48 - δ/16 - Δ + losses` (`high_mixed_margin`).
   * This is the paper's own endpoint argument at `δ = α` (15928-15946), extended to the whole interval `[5/6, 1]`. For `r ≥ 1` and `δ ≥ 5/6`, `(1+5r)/6 - δr ≤ 1-δ`.
   * Consequence: Thm 3.1, Lemmas 5.8, 6.1, 6.2, Prop 6.3, Lemma 9.1, Prop 9.2 and Prop 11.2 have no role in the formal proof. This is a third way off the Part I path, besides the paper as written and the Oct 5 substitution of PART1_SUBSTITUTION.md.
   * Whether the paper's own Part II text survives `Δ ≤ 1/8` is a separate question; it was not checked here. Lemma 18.1 is already stated for `κ ∈ [3/4,1]`, so that lemma is not an obstacle as stated.
2. **Imported theorems are proved, not cited.** The paper cites Kubota-Patterson and Dunn-Radziwiłł for the cubic theta function, Heath-Brown's cubic large sieve, Goldmakher-Louvel's quadratic sieve, and Thorner-Zaman's Chebotarev theorem. Lean has native developments for each: `Eisenstein/` (spectral construction, with Rellich-Kondrachov), `CubicSieve/`, `QuadraticSieve/`, and `PrimeCounting/` (Wiener-Ikehara from PNT+ rather than Thorner-Zaman). The statements proved are the instances Lean needs. They were not compared with the cited published theorems.
3. **Slot system and order of choices.** Lean uses distinct slot lengths tied to `t < Δ/4`; the paper uses `K` equal slots fixed independently of the target. Lean also replaces `O(ε)` bookkeeping by explicit linear budgets (`count_budget`, `central_budget ≤ 49/440640`, `floor_budget ≤ 7/1200`, ...). This confirms v1 §5(b).
4. **Lemmas are proved as instances.** Lemmas 17.1 and 18.1 enter only through the three fields of `HeckeDetectorRawFiber.Moments`, normalized to row scale `U` and specialized to the parameters the detector uses. Lemma 17.1's fixed margins `c1, c2` become strict inequalities, and the arbitrary ε becomes the fixed `t`. The general statements in the paper are not formalized as statements.
5. **The same 7/8 exponent bookkeeping.** Apart from items 1 and 3, the constants are the paper's:
   * `σ0 = 7/8`, `C(s) = s - 11/16`, `b = 1/8`, `h = 13/16`, `ℓ = 1/6`, `l_x = 17/48`, `l_y = 23/48`;
   * the low exponent `3/16`, the external Mellin line `Re = 17/50`, and `κ = 3/4 + 2Δ`;
   * the plain capacity `6κz`, the inverse capacities `r+2z < 1` and `2r+8z < 3`, and the amplification slope `5/6`;
   * the Lemma 20.2 polynomial, its margin `49/440640`, the net `-(51/64)Δ` (paper 16101), and the large-δ margin `-1/48-δ/16`.

   The 7/8 threshold is reached by the same balance as in the paper. Lean does not reach it by a different exponent route.
