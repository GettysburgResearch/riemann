## 0. Verdict in brief

**(b) circular by duality, with a (c) rider.**

1. **(Q_ρ) is a statement about the same numbers as Mom(1, ρ).** The theta reflection
   (Prop. lem:reflection) is an exact identity for each row `T_h`, and it is an involution: on local
   exponents it acts by `j ↦ −j − 2 (mod 6)`, with orbits `{1,3}, {0,4}, {2}, {5}`. So
   `Σ_h |T_h|²` is one number written in two coordinate systems. The reflection changes which tools
   apply, never what has to be proved. (This corrects the phrase "the only non-involutive step" in
   DISPERSION_GRH_STEP §0 item 5 and §5.)
2. **Poisson in `h`, primal coordinates, gives back Mom(1, ρ) exactly.** This is the manuscript's own
   Lemma lem:first-transfer (reviewed PASS in R2). The off-diagonal of `Σ_h |P_h(X)|²` equals
   `(𝓗/X²)·Σ_{0 < N y ≲ X²/𝓗} |M(y)|²`, with `M(y)` the twisted Möbius sums of the original family.
   The error term `O(X)` required in (Q_ρ) is *exactly* the image of that family's diagonal, so
   (Q_ρ) ⟺ Mom(1, ρ), with no exponent gained or lost (§2.1).
3. **Poisson in `h`, reflected coordinates, gives back the same quadratic family.** Quadratic Gauss
   sums over `Z[ω]` are `G₂(y; q) = (y/q)₂ γ₃(q) √N(q)`, with `γ₃` a ray-class function. On the tested
   moduli it is a function of `q mod 4` [FLOAT]. The dual is therefore Heath-Brown's self-dual
   situation: the same coefficients, rows `𝓗³/X²`, and a requirement `δ = 2(1 − ρ)` below the new
   diagonal (§2.2).
4. **Conserved gap.** Along the whole orbit generated by Poisson (P) and reflection (R), the quantity
   `|log cols − log rows| = 1 − ρ` is invariant [EXACT]. The deficit below the diagonal is `δ = k(1 − ρ)`
   with `k ≥ 0`, and `k = 0` occurs only on the original sub-diagonal Möbius family, i.e. on Mom(1, ρ)
   itself. A Möbius family with more rows than columns, `ρ' = (6 − 5ρ)/(5 − 4ρ) > 1`, is reachable, but
   only with the requirement `3(1 − ρ)` below its diagonal (§2.3).
5. **The (c) rider: the quadratic-twist templates do not reach it.** In quadratic coordinates
   (Q_ρ) is a moment of effective degree `k(ρ) = 4(3 − 2ρ)/(2 − ρ) ∈ (4, 6)`; at `ρ = 9/10` this is
   `48/11`. Its required precision is a power `D^{−(1−ρ)}` below the diagonal.
   * Every known quadratic-twist asymptotic (Sound00, SY10, Li24, SS24) is at degree `≤ 4`.
   * At degree 4 the known error terms save only powers of `log`.
   * The large-sieve inputs of these papers (HB95, Li24 Prop. 3.2, SS24 Prop. 1.3) all have the
     row-blind shape `rows + columns`, which is Prop. R again.
   * The one direct Möbius analogue (GZ23) needs GRH, and even then works only with more rows than
     columns.
   * Li's prime-square inflation, transplanted as sixth-power inflation, is the extraction in reverse.
     Its best output is 11/12 [EXACT].

## 1. (Q_ρ) made precise

### 1.1 The completed sum and its rows [TEX]

* **Completed sum.** eq:T (line 1287) defines, for a completely multiplicative twist `Ψ` vanishing
  on `S`,

      T(X; Ψ) = Σ*_{(n,S)=1} Σ_{b ≡ 1 (3), (b,S)=1}  ᾱ(n) γ₂(n) Ψ(n) ᾱ(b)³ Ψ(b)³ / (√N(n) N(b)) · V_*(N(n)N(b)³/X),

  with `V_*(y) = √y W(y)`. Here `n` is squarefree primary and `b` is primary, unrestricted.
* **Twist.** The twist is `Ψ_k(n) = ξ(n) χ_n(k) χ_n(f)⁴` (eq:completed-twist), and `T(X; k, f) := T(X; Ψ_k)`.
* **Relation to `P_h`.** The `b = 1` part is `P_k(X) = X^{−1/2} Σ* a_ξ(n) χ_n(k) χ_n(f)⁴ W(N n/X)`,
  with `a_ξ = ᾱ γ₂ ξ`. In outline form, `T_h(X) = Σ_b w_h(b) N(b)^{−1} P_h(X/N(b)³)`, where
  `w_h(b) = ᾱ(b)³ ξ(b)³ χ_b(h)³` (eq:intro-cube-completion).
* **Rows.** The proof of Prop. prop:R writes `k = u₀ s v²` with `s` squarefree, and moves `v²` into
  the twist (`T(X; u₀sv², f) = T(X; u₀s, fv²)`). Lemma lem:squarefree-completed then writes
  `s = t k₀`, with `t | rad(g·S)` fixed and `k₀` squarefree primary, coprime to `gS`. So **the rows
  of (Q_ρ) are squarefree `k₀` in fixed ray classes**, `N(k₀) ≤ 𝓗₀ = 𝓗/N(t)`. Principal (square)
  rows do not occur.

### 1.2 The reflected form: d, α, V♯, support [TEX; checked in R3]

with `ℓ = uλ^m n b³` and the following ingredients.

  * `d ∈ {d₀, d₊, d₋}` are the Fourier coefficients of `θ̄` at the cusps `I`, `[[1,0],[ω,1]]` and
    `[[1,0],[ω²,1]]`, from Dunn–Radziwiłł (5.7), (5.13) and (5.14) (Patterson Thm 8.1).

  * `ψ` is an additive character from a fixed finite family.
  * The twist on the reflected side is the quadratic character `χ_{k₀}(·)³ = (·/k₀)₂`, a character

* **Support.** The coefficient `d(ℓ)` vanishes unless `ℓ = u λ^m n b³`, where

### 1.3 Which statement is actually needed [INF]

The exact Poisson dual of Mom(1, ρ) (DISPERSION_GRH_STEP §4) has blocks `(b, f)` with the Möbius
variable `μ(f)`. That variable cancels the dual diagonal exactly, through `Σ_{v|z} μ(v) = 1_{z=1}`.
What must be small is therefore each block's **off-diagonal**. Two versions follow.

* **(Q_ρ^P)**, the `b = 1`, squarefree-Gauss-sum version, which is the one Steps 3-5 actually need.
  For the family 𝒜(W) of eq:smoothed-energy at the block scales of eq:initial-scales,
  `X = D/(BF)`, `𝓗 ≍ D^{2−ρ}/B²` and `XF = D/B`, with main block `B = F = 1`:

      𝒜(W) = Z + O(D^ε XF),     Z = zero-frequency term of lem:first-transfer, |Z| ≍ 𝓗.

* **(Q_ρ^T)**, the completed version, which is what the theta reflection and the quadratic large
  sieve act on:

      Σ*_{N k₀ ≤ 𝓗₀} |T(X; u₀tk₀, g)|² = Z^♮(𝓗₀, X; g) + O(D^{1+ε}),

  where `Z^♮` is the sum over **principal pairs**. These are pairs `(n₁b₁³, n₂b₂³)` with
  `χ_{n₁b₁³} = χ_{n₂b₂³}` on rows coprime to both. They include `b₁ ≠ b₂` with `b₁/b₂` a square, and
  `Z^♮` is explicit, of size `≍ 𝓗₀`.

**What the "explicit secondary terms" are.**
* In reflected coordinates the natural diagonal is a different explicit number,
  `Diag_Q = Σ_{k₀} Σ_{ℓ₁ℓ₂ = unit·□} A Ā V♯ V♯`.
* Both diagonals are `≍ 𝓗₀`, but they need not agree to `O(X)`.
* So in reflected coordinates (Q_ρ^T) reads `Σ|T|² = Diag_Q + (Z^♮ − Diag_Q) + O(D^{1+ε})`. The
  "explicit secondary term" is exactly `Z^♮ − Diag_Q`.
* No other explicit term exists above the error scale. In primal coordinates, the only further
  candidate of size `≫ X` is the image of the sixth-power rows (§2.1). It is not explicit: its size is
  the conclusion.

**(Q_ρ^T) is not sufficient for (Q_ρ^P).** Cube inversion (eq:intro-cube-inversion) gives

    Σ_h |P_h|² = Σ_{d₁,d₂} μμ w w /(N d₁ N d₂) Σ_h T_h(X/N d₁³) conj T_h(X/N d₂³) (h/d₁d₂)₂,

which involves cross-scale, quadratically twisted correlations. The manuscript bounds them by
Cauchy–Schwarz in `d`. At ρ < 1 this brings back the diagonal `𝓗 ≫ X`, so a signed cube removal
would be needed on top of (Q_ρ^T).

**Required strength.**
* Relative precision `X/𝓗 = D^{−(1−ρ)}` below the diagonal.
* Equivalently, the power `(1−ρ)/(2−ρ)` of the row count.
* Against Prop. R's `𝓗 + 𝓗²/X`, this is a saving of `D^{2(1−ρ)}`.

## 2. Duality check: Poisson summation in the rows

### 2.1 Primal coordinates: back to Mom(1, ρ) exactly [TEX + INF]

Lemma lem:first-transfer (lines 2263-2349; R2 item 6 PASS, with the paired identity replayed on 161
pairs) is an exact identity:

    𝒜(W) − Z = Σ_{ξ₁} ℓ_{ξ₁} Σ_{C,t} Σ_{d|C} μ(d)μ(t) w_{C,d} Σ_f Σ_{h≠0} Σ_{x₁,x₂} p_{hf²}(x₁) conj(p_{hf²}(x₂)) 𝒦_h(N x₁/ℓ, N x₂/ℓ),

where:
* `w_{C,d} = 𝓗N(C)/(N(d)L²F)`;
* `p_y(n) = μ(n)ξ₁(n) 1_{(n,t)=1} conj(χ_n(y)) χ_n(C)⁴ χ_n(d)`, which is the original family's
  coefficient;
* `y = hf²` ranges over `N(y) ≲ L²F²N(d)/(𝓗N(C)²)`.

The Gauss sums produced by Poisson are absorbed through eq:convert1 back into `μ`, in the paired form
eq:paired-first-gauss. Take the main block `C = d = t = 1`, `F = 1`. In T-units this gives

    Off(Σ_k |P_k|²) ≈ (𝓗/X²) Σ_{0 < N y ≲ X²/𝓗 = D^ρ} |M(y)|²,   M(y) = Σ_n μ(n)ξ₁(n) conj(χ_n(y)) U(N n/X),

so `M(y)` is `A_y(D)` up to fixed ray-class data. The coupled kernel is separated by
lem:smooth-mean-square, at a cost `D^ε`. Consequences:

* **Image of the diagonal.** The diagonal of the Möbius family maps to
  `(𝓗/X²)·(X²/𝓗)·X = X`. This is **exactly the error scale of (Q_ρ)**.
* **Equivalence.** Hence (Q_ρ^P) ⟺ `Σ_{N y ≲ H} |M(y)|² ≪ D^{ε} H D`, for the finitely many twisted
  variants and uniformly in the blocks.
  * ⇐ is lem:first-transfer. At these scales its Möbius rows run over
    `N(y) ≲ X²F²/𝓗 = D^ρ = H` in every block.
  * ⇒ is the exact dual identity `𝓜_D = Z + Σ c_ξ 𝓢_ξ` of DISPERSION_GRH_STEP §4, whose blocks are
    the `𝒜 − Z` above. Lemma lem:arithmetic-poisson (R2 item 7) is the inverse map.

  This is Mom(1, ρ), at the same ρ and the same `D`.
* **Extraction rows.** The extraction rows `y = εv⁶` contribute
  `(𝓗/X²)·H^{1/6}·|A_ε(D)|² = H^{−5/6}|A_ε(D)|²` to the off-diagonal of (Q_ρ). This is `≤ X` iff
  `|A_ε(D)| ≤ D^{1/2+5ρ/12}`, which is the extraction conclusion. So the off-diagonal of (Q_ρ)
  *contains* the conclusion as a non-explicit summand (RUNG_STRENGTH §4 item 2).
* **The completed version.** For (Q_ρ^T) the same computation works with the extra variable `b`
  [INF; local factors at `p | (n, b)` not computed]. At primes of `b` only, the Fourier transform of
  `χ_b(h)³` is a quadratic Gauss sum, i.e. a ray-class factor (§2.2). The dual is then a Möbius family
  convolved with a cube-supported sequence. Its Dirichlet series is
  `L(s, ψ_y)^{−1} × (an L-series in 3s, harmless at Re s = 1/2)`. Same conclusion.

**Verdict for §2.1: circular.** In the primal coordinates, Poisson in `h` is Step 5's own first
transfer, and it lands on the original family with zero exponent loss.

### 2.2 Reflected coordinates: the quadratic family is self-dual [INF + FLOAT]

Fix the classes of `k₀` modulo `4λ·𝔪_S`. Quadratic reciprocity then gives
`(λ⁴ℓ/k₀)₂ = ε(·)·(k₀/κ(ℓ))₂`, where `κ(ℓ)` is the odd-exponent kernel of `nb`. The cross factor
`(a/b)₂(b/a)₂` is a function of `(a mod 4, b mod 4)` on all 100 tested pairs (check C).

Expand the square and write `κ(ℓ₁)κ(ℓ₂) = r² q` with `q` squarefree. Then insert `Σ_{a²|k₀} μ(a)` for
squarefreeness (or keep the manuscript's `k = u₀sv²` device) and apply Poisson summation in `k₀`
modulo `q`. The ingredients are:

* **(A) [FLOAT, 193 moduli, max error 3.6e-15]** `G₂(y; q) := Σ_{v mod q} (v/q)₂ e(yv/q)` equals
  `(y/q)₂ G₂(1; q)` for `(y, q) = 1`, and `0` if a prime of `q` divides `y`.
* **(B) [FLOAT]** `γ₃(q) = G₂(1; q)/√N(q) ∈ {±1, ±i}`. On the tested set it depends only on `q mod 4`.
  The tested moduli 4, 4λ, 8 and 12 all work; 2 and 2λ do not. The manuscript's
  `G(q) = conj(χ_q(4)) γ₃(q)` behaves the same way.
* **(C) [FLOAT]** `γ₃(q₁q₂) = γ₃(q₁)γ₃(q₂)(q₁/q₂)₂(q₂/q₁)₂`.

Three kinds of terms result.

* **Zero frequency.** This requires `q = 1`, i.e. principal pairs, and gives `Diag_Q`.
* **Shared primes.** These give Ramanujan-type sums. They belong with the zero frequency.
* **Non-principal pairs.** For `q = κ₁'κ₂'` with coprime parts, they give

      Off_Q(𝓗) ≈ 𝓗₀ Σ_{y ≠ 0} Σ'_{ℓ₁,ℓ₂} [A(ℓ₁) γ₃(κ₁') N(κ₁')^{−1/2} (y/κ₁')₂] · conj[A(ℓ₂) γ₃'(κ₂') N(κ₂')^{−1/2} (y/κ₂')₂] · 𝒦(y; ℓ₁, ℓ₂),

  with `N(y) ≲ M²/𝓗 = 𝓗³/X²`.

These are **the same coefficients** (cubic-theta coefficients times a ray-class function and a
normalising power) twisted by the **same kind of quadratic character**. Only the off-diagonal maps to
the off-diagonal: at `ℓ₁ = ℓ₂` the "Gauss sum" is a Ramanujan sum. This is Heath-Brown's
`B(M, N) ↔ B(N²/M, N)` symmetry, but for one fixed coefficient vector. In exponents:

* rows `4 − 3ρ` > columns `3 − 2ρ`, so the dual has more rows than columns;
* the required bound is `Off_dual ≪ 𝓗 = cols²/rows`;
* the deficit below the new diagonal is `δ = 2(1 − ρ)`.

That requirement lies below even the column count. Any row-blind bound fails it (single-row
alignment, RUNG_STRENGTH §3a).

The remaining move is the functional equation in the columns. It sends the quadratic twist
(`j = 3`) to the sextic twist (`j = −5 ≡ 1`) (check: reflection orbits). So it leads back to sextic
coordinates, not to a new quadratic family.

### 2.3 The orbit and the conserved gap [EXACT, script Part II]

Moves: `P` is Poisson in the rows (Möbius ↔ sextic Gauss; quadratic ↔ quadratic). `R` is the
reflection of each row's columns (sextic ↔ quadratic). `R` does not exist on Möbius coordinates,
because `1/L` has no functional equation. All exponents are base `D`, in T-units, so each row has
mean square about 1 and the diagonal exponent equals the row exponent.

| state | coefficients | rows | cols | required Off | deficit δ | rows/cols at ρ = 9/10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A = Mom(1, ρ) | Möbius, sextic twist | ρ | 1 | ρ | 0 | 9/10 |
| AP = Oct 5 dual (B, P) | cubic Gauss, sextic | 2 − ρ | 1 | 1 | 1 − ρ | 11/10 |
| APR = (Q_ρ) | cubic Gauss, quadratic | 2 − ρ | 3 − 2ρ | 1 | 1 − ρ | 11/12 |
| APRP (§2.2) | cubic Gauss, quadratic | 4 − 3ρ | 3 − 2ρ | 2 − ρ | 2(1 − ρ) | 13/12 |
| APRPR | cubic Gauss, sextic | 4 − 3ρ | 5 − 4ρ | 2 − ρ | 2(1 − ρ) | 13/14 |
| APRPRP | Möbius, sextic | 6 − 5ρ | 5 − 4ρ | 3 − 2ρ | 3(1 − ρ) | 15/14 |

How the moves act on `(δ, s)`, where `s = cols − rows`:
* **P** sends `(δ, s) ↦ (δ + s, −s)`.
* **R** sends `(δ, s) ↦ (δ, −s)`. It preserves δ because it acts on the same numbers.
* Hence `|s| = 1 − ρ` is invariant. It is exactly the row/column gap of Mom(1, ρ).
* δ is a non-negative multiple of `1 − ρ`, and `δ = 0` only at state A. Since `R` is unavailable
  there, A is reached only from AP.

The last Möbius state has more rows than columns (`ρ' = (6 − 5ρ)/(5 − 4ρ) > 1`), which is the range
the manuscript claims to handle. But its requirement sits `3(1 − ρ)` below its diagonal, so it asks
for more, not less.

**No sequence of exact moves reaches a state that is both diagonal-sized (δ = 0) and has at least as
many rows as columns.** That is the regime in which a row-blind large sieve would suffice.

### 3.2 The three axes

1. **Family size versus length.** All unconditional quadratic-twist asymptotics stop at effective
   degree 4, where the Poisson dual is as long as the row range. SS24 call `M ≍ N²` the critical range
   where "standard tools ... are of no use" without inflation. (Q_ρ) is at degree `> 4` for every
   ρ < 1, and degree 4 corresponds exactly to ρ = 1, the Oct 5 boundary 11/12.
2. **Precision.** No source reaches a power saving below the diagonal at degree 4. SY10, Li24 and
   SS24 save powers of `log`, and Problem 19 asks for a power saving at k = 4. GZ23 under GRH reaches
   only relative `(Y/X)^{1/2}` in its regime with more rows than columns. (Q_ρ) needs `D^{−(1−ρ)}`.
3. **Structural inputs.**

| input | status for cubic-theta coefficients |
|---|---|
| Euler product | absent. The dual's self-similarity comes from §2.2 instead, so this step transfers, but only back to the same problem |
| approximate functional equation | present (Patterson), but it changes the twist type (`j = 3 → 1`), so Li's induction does not close |
| Ramanujan bound | trivially present: `\|γ₂\| = 1` on squarefree `n` |
| multiplicativity | only twisted (cubic reciprocity phases) |
| GRH-based steps | have no analogue: no `log L` exists for theta series, and GRH for the Möbius side is the conclusion |

### 3.3 Li's inflation, transplanted [EXACT on a grid]

The sextic analogue of `χ_{mp²} = χ_m 1_{p∤n}` is `χ_n(uv⁶) = χ_n(u) 1_{v∤n}`, i.e. the extraction
multiplicity. Inflate the rows by `v⁶` with `N v ≍ D^w`, apply the imported bound once
`ρ + 6w ≥ 1`, and pay `D^ρ (D^{1−w})²` for the terms `v | n`. Then:

* the best boundary over `w` is exactly **11/12** for `ρ ≤ 1/7`;
* for `ρ > 1/7` it is `6/7 + 5ρ/12 > 11/12`;
* it is never below 11/12 (script Part II(b)).

In the quadratic case inflation works because Li then proves the larger family's bound by an
induction that uses the functional equation. Here the larger family's bound is only the imported
`ρ ≥ 1` input.

## 4. Verdict

**(b) circular by duality.**

* (Q_ρ) is an exact rewriting of Mom(1, ρ), at the same ρ, with its `O(D^{1+ε})` error term equal to
  the image of the Möbius family's diagonal.
* The rewriting runs through lem:first-transfer, lem:arithmetic-poisson and the per-row involution
  lem:reflection.
* Poisson in the rows lands either on A_u (primal coordinates) or on the same quadratic family with
  rows `𝓗³/X²` (reflected coordinates).
* The orbit invariant `|cols − rows| = 1 − ρ` (§2.3) shows that every coordinate system asks for the
  same thing: a family with more columns than rows at diagonal size, or a deficit `k(1 − ρ)` below
  some diagonal.

**Rider (c).** As a standalone target in quadratic coordinates, (Q_ρ) is beyond every listed method.
The precise obstruction has three parts:

1. **Degree.** Its effective degree `4(3 − 2ρ)/(2 − ρ)` exceeds 4, where all unconditional asymptotics
   stop.
2. **Precision.** Its required saving is a power, while degree-4 results save logs.
3. **Shape.** Every large-sieve-type input in these papers has the row-blind shape `rows + columns`.

Of the transplantable devices, inflation and the Möbius-to-`1/L` duality reproduce 11/12 or GRH for
the family.

**Not (a).** No named method plausibly reaches (Q_ρ) for any ρ < 1.

**What a non-circular input would have to be.** It must carry information that P and R cannot
generate from the family itself. Two examples:
* a family zero-density estimate counted with row multiplicity, with exponent `A < 4/3`; by the
  bootstrap fixed point `1 − 1/(6A)` of DISPERSION_GRH_STEP §3(c), this would beat 7/8;
* any external arithmetic input for `Σ_{n₁≠n₂} μν(n₁)μν(n₂) Σ_u χ_{n₁}χ̄_{n₂}(u)` at relative
  precision `D^{−(1−ρ)}`.

We know of no unconditional result of either kind.

## 5. What this note does not show

* No moment bound, zero-free region or RH statement is proved. §§1.3, 2.1 and 2.2 are exponent-level
  and identity-level analyses. The coupled kernels are separated only by citing lem:smooth-mean-square,
  and the `D^ε` losses are not tracked.
* The completed (cube) version of the primal Poisson in §2.1 is schematic. The local factors at primes
  dividing both `n` and `b` (local exponent `1 + 3v_p(b) ∈ {1, 4}`) were not computed.
* Checks A-D are double precision on finitely many moduli (193 Gauss-sum moduli, 100 pairs, 60
  `γ₂`-pairs). That `γ₃` is a class function mod 4 is tested, not proved. The expected source is
  Hecke's evaluation of quadratic Gauss sums, which was not consulted here.
* The literature was read at the level stated in the header. SY10 and Sound00 were read only at
  abstract level, and their content is as reported by Li24 and SS24 §1. HB95 was not read. The
  "effective degree" is our bookkeeping device, not a notion from those papers.
* The equivalence (Q_ρ^P) ⟺ Mom(1, ρ) is as strong as the manuscript's transfer lemmas, which were
  reviewed only in a bounded agent review (R2). Mom(1, ρ) itself remains OPEN; RUNG_STRENGTH §5 gives
  finite evidence that it is true.
* Suggested follow-up (not done; existing files were not edited): in DISPERSION_GRH_STEP §§0 and 5,
  replace "the only non-involutive step" and "does not visibly return to A_u" by a pointer to §§2.1-2.3
  here, and mark (Q_ρ) as circular in AGENDA A1.
