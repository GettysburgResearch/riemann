## Exact unresolved bridge

The missing lemma is an **integrated, common-probe estimate**, not another pointwise bound on a selected contour. For each target and finite row dyad, after expanding all compensated subsets with the same local sector and mask conventions, it must provide the equality between the physical row integral and the finite main/error expansion on the row-dependent shifted contours; it must bound all external integrated tails and every horizontal join by `Z^(Bψ)T₁^(−N)` with Bψ and internal height degrees fixed independently of N; and it must supply the common-profile/sobolev moment bounds in step 4 uniformly for the retained rowwise detector and amplitude choices. On each buffered join the reciprocal of the actual denominator ψ·barχ_u may only be used inside the single cumulative height allowance. After fixing the retained z, the row partition is Borel and independent of s and w; no later z shift is permitted for that partition. Internal weighted-L¹ coefficient measures cannot be promoted to external trace coordinates without a pointwise rapid-density estimate.

Hu (6.5), (6.9), Lemma 4.8 and Proposition 4.14 support individual pieces, and the linked dynamic-contour note proves the contour transformation **assuming** exactly the above field/type, trace-density and profile interfaces. They do not by themselves supply a reviewed proof that all finite local tables, Fourier-coordinate changes, rowwise Sobolev derivatives and source coefficient classes meet those interfaces simultaneously at these scales. Consequently neither (L) nor (H) has been established unconditionally from the printed source statements by the assembled notes. In particular, positive rational margins and target-dependent late height selection cannot repair a missing physical identity or a trace estimate on a horizontal join. This is the exact obstacle before the common Mellin closure can be invoked.
