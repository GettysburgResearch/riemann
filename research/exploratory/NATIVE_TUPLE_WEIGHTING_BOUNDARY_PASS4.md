## 3. A precise test for the missing identification

Let H be the 24 already fixed Boolean histories and let
`epsilon_h in {+1,-1}` be their signs. If a complete lift over a history is
actually given by a measure `mu_h` and residual amplitude `r_h(t,zeta)`, then
its tuple aggregate has the conditional exact form

\[
Z(t)=\frac{q(t)}{60}\sum_{h\in H}\epsilon_h
\int r_h(t,\zeta)\,d\mu_h(\zeta).
\tag{T4.3}
\]

This is a specification of the data that must be acquired, not an assertion
that a complete native lift has already been constructed. Extra occurrence
multiplicity must be included in those measures. An alternative native
atomization must first be mapped to this one with its literal diagonal kept.

For the fixed actual tuple, zero is equivalent to equality of the positive
and negative sums in (T4.3). A common residual multiplier is sufficient but
not necessary. To preserve cancellation against **every** bounded retained-
label test, the corresponding positive and negative pushed-forward measures
must be equal (or the complex signed difference measure must vanish). A
single scalar identity `sum epsilon_h=0` proves only cancellation of a
constant input; it does not prove this measure identity.

In finite linear-algebra terms, put
`b(v)=sum_h epsilon_h v_h/60`. A proposed linear readout T factors through
b exactly when `ker b` is contained in `ker T`. Equivalently its values on
the history basis have the common form `T(e_h)=epsilon_h q0/60` for one
output vector q0. Proof: choose e with b(e)=1, write
`v=b(v)e+(v-b(v)e)`, and apply the kernel condition. The converse is
immediate. This formal test does not authorize arbitrary v as native
histories; it identifies the strength of an asserted universal decoder.
The actual fixed-vector cancellation can hold without universal factoring.

Physical-only masks multiply (T4.3) by the same scalar and commute with
aggregation. They cannot repair unequal residual weights inside this fibre.
The arbitrary-mask analytic theorem L-102959 is therefore not an
admissibility theorem for arbitrary history reweightings.

## 4. Honest boundary and useful next input

No source-defined nonzero residual has been found, and no full weighted zero

weights would not produce a native counterexample. The bounded audit is not

and original parameter measure, then compute the two sides of (T4.3). It
must distinguish the Beta depth parameter, complementary temperature,

summation inside a member precedes its square. The physical coercivity theorem
proved separately on this branch is for a specific 64-dimensional finite-

Thus the pass obtains a new original-physical-norm certificate, and sharpens
the older tuple's acquisition contract, without claiming that one resolves
the other or proving any principal estimate or RH statement.
