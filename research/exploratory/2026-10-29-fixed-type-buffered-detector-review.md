# Independent internal review of the fixed-type buffered detector

The derivation in [the detector note](2026-10-29-fixed-type-buffered-detector.md) was checked against Hu Proposition 3.1(iii), (3.2), Proposition 6.3 and §7.1, and OpenAI Lemmas 4.9–4.10 and 8.1. It is conditional on those source identities and the listed standard Hecke inputs; this is not an independent verification of Hu's complete sector construction.

The Euler-logarithm, Borel–Carathéodory and three-circles argument is valid for the stated fixed types: a polynomial strip bound and a zero-free disk yield an arbitrarily small power of the analytic conductor on the smaller disk. The nested maxima and rounding give the stated finite bins, and the zero-free disk fits the enlarged height range. The allocated coordinate bounds imply one common total added displacement at most T₁/2; they do not authorize renewing the allowance at successive shifts. The positive-real-part deleted Euler product and its reciprocal have the stated arbitrarily small radical-power bounds.

An earlier version incorrectly charged a left-strip deleted factor by U^(6e) under the more general hypothesis NR≪U^A₀. The current corrected cost is U^(6e A₀+ε), hence the combined reflected exponent is A₀(a−1/2+12e)+ε. The physical source case A₀=1 is unchanged.

Hu Proposition 3.1(iii), (3.2) and its proof explicitly assert the Kummer inducing character and the primitive conductor bound Nf_u≪N rad(uR)≤q_u. For fixed target and fixed Θ twists, standard local conductor submultiplicativity adds only a fixed factor, and the natural deleted radical is supported on the fixed bad set and rad(u), giving O_ψ(U) once the actual row masks have been identified. Hu Proposition 6.3(i) explicitly asserts the detector/zero-witness transfer and that every live denominator belongs to F_ω. Its proof cites the fixed-type gamma estimates and the row conductor bound. Hu §7.1 also states that the detector contains the prime presentations, conjugates and witness presentations, and that the exceptional finite inducing rows are bounded.

What remains conditional is a source-faithful enumeration of every sector and presentation in the variable-scale compensated physical expression, including their exact original zero extensions and their allocation of all Mellin/separation frequencies to the same height budget. A principal denominator or numerator must be treated separately; it cannot be inserted into the nonprincipal logarithm argument. The cited Hu statements assert the necessary transfer at their stated level, but their underlying complete sector and local calculations have not been independently replayed here. No further algebraic flaw was found.
