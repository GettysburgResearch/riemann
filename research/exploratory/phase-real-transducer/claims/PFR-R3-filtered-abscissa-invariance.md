\[
R(t)=\sum_\lambda c_\lambda e^{\lambda t}
\]

the pointwise exponential type and weighted-`L2` convergence abscissa are both

\[
\sup\{\operatorname{Re}\lambda:c_\lambda\ne0\}.
\]

preserves the global displacement `Theta-1/2`.  Small but nonzero leakage may
be delayed, but it eventually controls the infinite-time abscissa.  Exact
height localization requires annihilating all unwanted extremal modes;
`PFR-R2` rules out a universal hard open-band holomorphic cutoff.
