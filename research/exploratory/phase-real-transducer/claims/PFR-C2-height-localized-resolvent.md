# PFR-C2 — Height-localized actual-Xi resolvent

Status: **OPEN**

PFR-T5 gives a global source-defined response whose growth and weighted-energy
abscissa is the supremal off-critical displacement over all zeta zeros.

Construct a zero-free-definition localization, centered at a chosen ordinate
window, such that:

1. the object is defined from the safe phase field or the prime/archimedean
   source, not from a zero list;
2. it has a controlled and auditable prime-side formula;
3. its weighted-energy abscissa is exactly the maximum
   `|Re rho-1/2|` among zeros in the selected window;
4. leakage from zeros outside the window is quantified rather than ignored.

Gaussian or resolvent localizers that never vanish do not give an exact hard
window; compact spectral cutoffs interact with the complex displacement.  The
construction must resolve this tradeoff explicitly.
