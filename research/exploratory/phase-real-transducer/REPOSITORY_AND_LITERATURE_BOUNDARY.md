# Repository and literature boundary

## Repository overlap

This packet is deliberately not a new programme issue. It is a bounded
execution branch intended to report to existing coordination surfaces.

### Issue #17 — certified Speiser search

Issue #17 seeks a rigorous rectangle containing a zero of `zeta'` left of the
critical line. It is a counterexample-search/certificate route. The present
packet instead studies exact flower kinematics and inverse-Poisson zero-height
signals. `PFR-R1` shows why a generic local quartet cannot be assigned a
nonreal derivative critical point.

### Issue #39 — xi log-derivative passivity

Issue #39 uses `Re xi'/xi` and finite Pick matrices as RH witness criteria. The
present packet reuses the corrected completed logarithmic derivative, but adds
three different interfaces:

```text
centered-Xi flower phase velocity;
safe-line real prime cosine series;
inverse-Poisson zero response and energy abscissa.
```

It does not claim a new pointwise passivity equivalence.

### Programme #744 and PR #729

Programme #744 and PR #729 contain endpoint/winding ledgers, Pick/Loewner
geometry, phase overlap, anti-Poisson descent, Herglotz measures, and shell
energy. Exact locked comparison points include:

- PR #729 head `14f3ef3cb40854fb559deb2528932e5113f72038`;
- `L-105601`, backward Poisson evolution for an extremal derivative-ratio
  Herglotz field;

The present packet is not a replacement for these results. Its finite theorem

## External literature

Primary or near-primary starting points:

- K. A. Broughan and A. R. Barnett, *The holomorphic flow of the Riemann zeta
  function*, Math. Comp. 73 (2004), 987--1004,
  DOI `10.1090/S0025-5718-03-01529-1`, plus the 2007 corrigendum.
- J. W. Neuberger, C. Feiler, H. Maier, and W. P. Schleich,
  *The Riemann hypothesis illuminated by the Newton flow of zeta*, Physica
  Scripta 90 (2015), DOI `10.1088/0031-8949/90/10/108015`.
- A. Speiser, *Geometrisches zur Riemannschen Zetafunktion*, Math. Ann. 110
  (1935), 514--521.
- T. Tao, *The Euler--Maclaurin formula, Bernoulli numbers, the zeta function,
  and real-variable analytic continuation* (2010), for the real-asymptotic
  interpretation and smoothing philosophy.
- Standard sources on Hardy's `Z`, Hadamard factorization, the Poisson kernel,
  and the Weil explicit formula.

The Fourier transform of a Poisson kernel, the explicit formula, and the
positive-definite characterization of Fourier transforms of positive measures
are classical. This packet claims no external priority for those ingredients.
A specialist literature review is required before asserting novelty for the
combined phase-transducer or flower-participation formulation.
