# Ranked continuation queue

## 1. Source-defined windowed response energy

Highest priority. Build an actual-Xi response from the safe-line prime field,
with a rigorously controlled height window, and prove that its `L^2` abscissa
is the largest off-critical displacement in that window. Avoid inserting zero
locations into the definition.

## 2. Certified phase/de-Poissonization experiment

Use ball arithmetic on a modest finite window to certify:

- `V_y(x)=-d_x arg X(x+iy)`;
- Fourier/Poisson evolution at selected modes;
- zeta and zeta-prime critical points;
- critical-line petal areas and angular spans.

This is a structural regression, not a counterexample search.

## 3. Petal-area concentration

Compute source-locked distributions of

```text
A_j = 1/2 integral_(gamma_j)^(gamma_(j+1)) theta'(t) Z(t)^2 dt
```

and test whether participation ratios show stable scaling. Any numerical law
must then be converted into a theorem target involving second/fourth moments,
angular gaps, or an arithmetic trace.

## 4. Global Speiser adapter

Seek an exact identity linking the de-Poissonized response defect to the
left-of-line `zeta'` count. The quartet firewall requires genuinely global
input: pole, boundary, functional equation, or arithmetic source.

## 5. Prime-side positive-definite distribution

Formulate the critical explicit-formula distribution in a test-function space
where positive definiteness and temperedness are meaningful. Audit equivalence
to Weil positivity before claiming new RH leverage.

## Stop conditions

Pause the lane if review shows that:

- the petal count adds only notation to `N_0(T)`;
- the inverse-Poisson response is merely an unmodified Weil criterion;
- every proposed finite invariant survives the quartet or a known RH-false
  zeta analogue;
- the source-defined window necessarily assumes the zero information it is
  intended to recover.
