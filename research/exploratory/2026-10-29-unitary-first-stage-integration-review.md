# Integration review: proposed first unitary stage

**Verdict:** The first-stage packet is a collection of compatible-looking *conditional* interfaces, not a verified proof of a new zero-free region. No independent derivation of Hu's fixed-field theta/local identities or the complete variable-geometry high estimate has been completed. The smallest load-bearing bridge is an actual-variable-probe theorem giving the same target-uniform positive high-side saving for all nonprincipal rows, with the fixed-type detector, selected/error masks, and one cumulative height budget. The principal and low estimates must also be independently reviewed for that identical probe.

**Primary sources:** Hu, [*A seven-eighths zero-free half-plane for unitary Weil L-functions*, v1](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00216v1); OpenAI, [*The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis*](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/paper.pdf); Xu–Sang, [arXiv:2610.15291v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15291v2). This is a source-and-interface review, not an independent replay of their theta, moment, local Gauss, or contour proofs.

## Quantitative common object

All pieces must use Hu's physical expression (6.9), the **same** finite excluded set, finite disjoint prime slots, unit-sector recombination, and its scalar normalization. The source target is oriented as in Hu Proposition 3.2 with its global norm twist removed; later conjugation and norm-translation are separate steps. For the proposed first stage,

    kappa=3/4, b=173/2000, ell=10027/60000,
    B0=69991/80000,  lx=44783/120000,
    ly=55163/120000, h=31757/40000,
    C_b(B0)=46513/240000, z0=17/50.

Direct rational arithmetic confirms `ly-ell-11b/6=16079/120000>0`, the small-row margin with `2d_min=1/50` is `-433393/6000000<-1/20`, and `ell/(h+10^-7)=5013500/23817753>21/100`. These checks do not establish any of the physical estimates or the endpoint certificate.

## Source scope and interfaces

1. **Low side.** [Variable low](2026-10-28-hu-variable-low/README.md) derives the desired exponent assuming Hu's field/type calibration, reflection, Gram, local tables and marked-energy theorem at the actual rescaled row boxes. Its scalar conductor and energy inequalities, Gaussian separation and completed-tail/additive count have separate elementary proofs. Hu's Proposition 6.2 itself is stated at `b=1/8, ell=1/6`; the new variable-geometry bound is a source-dependent adaptation, not a direct citation of that proposition. Uniformity is for a fixed slot system and type orbit; constants and thresholds may depend on the fixed target.

2. **Physical identity and regions.** Hu (6.5) has arbitrary positive `X,Y,Z` before his special geometry (6.7). Applying (6.9) produces the full quotient-free correction; the `conjugate(psi)(p)` factor, all row zeros, fixed `S`, and the same sector coefficients are necessary. The [first-region note](2026-10-29-unitary-first-euler-region/README.md) and [principal-region note](2026-10-28-hu-principal-region/README.md) give local upper bounds on D1 and the widened principal tube, conditional on Hu's full table. They establish neither the source table over arbitrary unit sectors nor the reciprocal/trace control. Nonvanishing of an individual selected factor is never needed to justify contour motion.

3. **Buffered detector.** Hu Proposition 6.3 begins **`B>7/8`** and belongs to his fixed geometry. Its detector subargument uses fixed-type strip/gamma bounds, conductors and the zero-maxima construction; it is not a separately stated variable-geometry theorem for `B0<beta<=7/8`. The [fixed-type detector note](2026-10-29-fixed-type-buffered-detector.md) reconstructs this step assuming polynomial strip bounds, a finite closed presentation family, conductor and deletion-radical `O_psi(U)`, and isolation of principal presentations. Its internal review checks the disk and nested-bin logic, but not Hu's complete physical row/sector identification. For first stage `beta>B0>51/100`, so the abstract detector's lower threshold is sufficient once those inputs are proved. A single shared bound on all Mellin and separating-frequency shifts is mandatory; an `L1` internal coefficient density is not automatically a trace-controlled external density.

4. **Witness count.** [Witness count](2026-10-29-hu-witness-count/README.md) uses Hu Theorems 4.1–4.2 and 4.14, plus Xu–Sang Proposition 4.1's scalar crossing. At this stage `kappa=3/4` is within Hu's printed moment range, and Hu's claimed finite-order `7/8` bound supplies its finite-character premise. The detector spike presentations, finite-character slot coefficients, exceptional-row exclusions, zero slots, and pointwise amplitude **upper and lower** bounds still have to coincide with the actual dynamic-contour partition. The fact that Hu's Prop. 6.3 has `B>7/8` cannot be used as a black box at the proposed stage; only the separately reconstructed detector can fill this interface.

5. **Numerator.** Hu (6.12) has finite-order numerator `L_K(w,chi_u)` even for an infinite-type target denominator. [Amplified numerator](2026-10-29-unitary-amplified-numerator/README.md) uses this fact, the paired fourth moment, the fixed-field AFE and actual prime amplitudes. The bound is conditional on an exact match of primitive inducing characters, finite deleted factors, conjugated *whole* products, conductor-dependent profiles and the same row masks. The complex-field gamma profile, ramified factors and field-dependent height degrees are not certified by the scalar Hölder calculation.

6. **Dynamic contour and local errors.** [Dynamic contour](2026-10-29-unitary-dynamic-contour/README.md) reconstructs the path without using the numerical hypothesis `beta>=7/8`, but explicitly assumes the physical identity, D1 all-height bound, fixed-type reciprocal buffer and trace-density conditions. It first truncates on a global line; it never extends the buffered reciprocal over an entire shifted vertical line. After fixing retained `z`, it bins the physical slot amplitudes, then moves `w`; any source application must preserve that order and the same masks. The [raised-line note](2026-10-29-raised-line-error/README.md) retains the `j>=2` ramified term and applies its conductor deficit once to all distinct error labels. The exact Hu field/sector realization of that complete local table remains an input. A polynomial all-height bound for the quotient-free correction alone does not prove the source buffered reciprocal or its trace estimates.

7. **Moderate rows.** [Moderate contour](2026-10-29-unitary-high-contour/README.md) and its internal review verify the exponent comparison conditional on the preceding inputs. At fixed amplitudes the numerator, row count, physical main/error factors and Mellin kernel must all refer to the *same* dyad, presentation and slot system. The actual exponent is compared to `C_b(beta)`, with the gain `beta-B0`; the certified endpoint is relative to `C_b(B0)`. The fixed scalar `8*10^-7` margin is available only before analytic losses and height factors. It is not itself the source-to-physical bridge.

8. **Outer and principal rows.** [Outer rows](2026-10-29-unitary-outer-rows/README.md) now explicitly assume a quantitative `U^epsilon` global reciprocal/deleted-factor bound in addition to zero-freeness; the [review](2026-10-29-outer-rows-review.md) checks its claimed powers conditional on that bound and the complete local table. The [principal contour](2026-10-29-hu-principal-contour/README.md) separately reconstructs the two scalar residues and uses a principal tube extending left of `7/8`. Its exact local product, the trace bounds and the identification of the principal Kummer class after **sector recombination** remain source inputs. There is no justification for assigning the original Eisenstein statement `u=1` independently in each unrecombined sector.

9. **Family closure.** [Closure](2026-10-29-unitary-family-closure.md) is a valid conditional Mellin contradiction only if the low and high inequalities are established for the same physical probe, with one slot system and positive saving fixed uniformly over later finite twists in the fixed orbit. The threshold, implicit constant and height degree may depend on the later target; the height cutoff may be chosen after that degree, but the real saving and mesh must already be fixed. A pole of a norm-character Hecke function yields a zero of the reciprocal; it is not a zero of the Hecke function. Abelian descent and Brauer induction are separate source-dependent deductions after the rank-one statement, not evidence for the missing physical estimate.

## Smallest explicit unresolved assertion

For fixed `K`, fixed infinity-type orbit, the displayed geometry, and every sufficiently fine *one common* slot system, prove directly for Hu's finite compensated expression that for every target in the orbit and its later finite twists,

    J_psi(Z) - f_psi(Z) = O_psi( Z^(C_b(beta)-rho) )

with one `rho>0` chosen independently of those later twists, after summing all nonprincipal moderate, small and distant rows and the principal remainders. The proof must establish the same source-local correction, all sector masks and finite inducing characters, cumulative height allocation, and trace-tail/AFE bounds at the variable scales. Neither Hu Proposition 6.3 nor Xu–Sang's finite-order theorem states this combined assertion for fixed nonzero infinity type. Until it is checked, combining the separate scalar inequalities is not a proof of `beta<=B0`.

**Specific caveats:** The lower-`kappa` extension is irrelevant to this first stage, since `kappa=3/4` is already in Hu's printed range; it would matter for a later refinement. The packet's unreviewed inputs include the cited source statements themselves. No claim of a new zeta or L-function zero-free region is made here.
