# Independent review of the detector/profile interface

The long-cutoff witness and common-profile proof in [UNIFORM-DETECTOR-PROOF.md](UNIFORM-DETECTOR-PROOF.md) has been checked by Families, separately from its author High_match. High_match also checked the independently developed [general long detector](../2026-10-29-general-long-detector/README.md).

The checks cover: fixed-field conductor and deleted-radical quantifiers; the finite zero-maxima buffer for a row and its conjugate; the Hecke functional-equation strip and contour setup; the Gamma convolution and one common Fourier-height budget; simultaneous saturation; and Sobolev applied to the square of the ordinary polynomial, followed by the squarefree-kernel `a=a₁v²` decomposition with overlap masks. In particular, if the buffered maximum chooses the conjugate character, splitting the two orientations and conjugating the entire common source vector uses the same n-free large sieve without changing label norms.

For saturation, the product lower bound and opposite pointwise upper bounds give `δ[m−min(m,1−m)]≪η`. Since δ is bounded away from zero by the fixed floor, this forces `m≤1/2+O(η)`; dividing by each opposite upper bound then gives the individual inverse and ordinary lower bounds. The parameter Sobolev step has only a fixed β interval and O(X^τ) unit height intervals, while common profile derivatives introduce annular logarithmic factors. All fixed polynomial height losses are absorbed by choosing τ after the desired density loss. External Fourier-tail orders are selected after τ and do not change the fixed Sobolev derivative order.

No concrete gap was found in this interface. This is an internal source-level review, not an independent proof of de Faveri's FHL construction or large-sieve theorem, the standard uniform Hecke analytic estimates, or tracker admission. Those source and formal-semantic inputs must be assessed separately; the density theorem is not an individual zero-free region.
