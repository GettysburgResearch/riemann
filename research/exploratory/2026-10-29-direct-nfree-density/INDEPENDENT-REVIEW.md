# Independent review of the direct power-free density deduction

The source-to-scalar reduction was checked against de Faveri's definition (1.2), Theorem 1.1, and the explanatory text in §1.3.1. The source explicitly places **both** the outer label and inner source sums on n-th-power-free ideals. Its variables have the orientation used in the draft. For `D≤X`, the term `X^(2/n)D^(1−1/n)` is at most `X^(1−1/n)D^(2/n)`; for `D≥X`, it is at most `X^(1/n)D`. The separate `D` term is also at most `X^(1/n)D`.

The inverse coefficients are supported on squarefree ideals. In each ordinary second or fourth moment, writing an ideal uniquely as `a=a₁v²` with squarefree `a₁` and fixing v before applying the sieve gives a common a₁ coefficient vector; the factor `χ_u(v²)` is a row contraction including its zero mask. Normalization and divisor bounds give squared coefficient mass `≪X^ε/(Nv)²`; Minkowski sums the resulting `1/Nv` losses. This check assumes the original FHL family, fixed sectors, and the common-profile/Sobolev interface stated in the draft.

For δ>2/n, the displayed t lies in `(1,3/2)` and `L=2t/3` lies in `(2/3,1)`. The increasing face is maximal at t, the other two are maximal at L, with `f₂(L)−f₀(L)=(2L−1)/n>0` and `f₂(L)=f₁(t)=R`. The inverse witness covers r≥L. If r<L, the simultaneous constraints `r+m≥t` and `m≤1/2`, with fixed small detector losses, imply `L≤2m≤1<t`, and the fourth witness applies. The global source base X is legitimate because both the conductor and deleted good radical are bounded by a fixed multiple of `Nu≤X`.

I independently simplified the exact retuned-boundary expression (7) and checked its displayed sample fractions. The class-field deduction uses the finite union of powered FHL families on the same p-free labels and at most p−1 characters per cyclic p-extension. The analytic hypothesis δ>2/p holds at the retuned θ for every odd prime p≥5; any separate restriction of the printed de Faveri comparison to n≥8 should be retained as a comparison restriction.

This review does not independently prove the uniform zero detector, Sobolev/common-profile bridge, primitive-character normalization, or the conventional interpretation of the retuned Lean candidate. The result remains an implication conditional on those named inputs and is a density statement, not an individual zero-free theorem or a priority claim.
