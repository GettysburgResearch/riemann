## 1. The 2026 frontier and its status (1–16)

**1. RH is untouched by every 2026 development.** The quasi-RH half-planes concern Re s > Θ with Θ < 1, the 2/3 theorem is a lower-density statement, and Siegel exclusion concerns real zeros within c/log q of s = 1. None of them says anything about an individual zero near Re s = 1/2, and none implies the density hypothesis below Θ, Lindelöf on the line, or GRH-strength consequences. *Status:* SYNTHESIS. *Refs:* README.md; history/recent_developments_2024_2026.md §7; audits/quasi_rh/DOSSIER.md §3 (‘What is NOT implied’); CRIT23 DOSSIER §6; SIEGEL DOSSIER §6.

**2. Quasi-RH 11/12 is the audited claim.** OpenAI's paper2 (PAPER-0161, 5 Oct 2026, human-edited) claims that every finite-order Hecke L-function over Q(√−3), and hence every Dirichlet L-function including ζ, has no zeros in Re s > 11/12. The Observatory's section-by-section Level-2 audit found 0 verified errors, 0 suspected gaps and about 35 harmless presentation items, and about one hundred arithmetic identities were confirmed in Z[ω]. The imported inputs (Patterson's coefficient formula and Kubota multiplier as transcribed by Dunn–Radziwiłł, and Goldmakher–Louvel Thm 1.1) were checked against their statements but not re-proved. *Status:* CLAIM (unrefereed; AI-generated, human-edited); AUDIT FINDING no_issue_found_in_inspected_portion. *Refs:* FND-0009; quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.2; LED-ZFR-2.

**3. Quasi-RH 7/8 is a different, unaudited argument, and its Lean claim is unreplayed.** The 199-page 7/8 paper (PAPER-0528) uses a family-wide rightmost-zero contradiction, not the 11/12 mean-square mechanism, and was read for structure only. OpenAI's Lean formalization covers the 7/8 paper, not paper2. It was not built here, and the HeckeSevenEighths challenge defines its own LFunction from shifted Hurwitz kernels, whose equality with the Dirichlet series on Re s > 1 has not been checked. A successful replay would certify the ζ and Dirichlet 7/8 statements, and hence 11/12, but not paper2's argument. *Status:* CLAIM (unrefereed; AI-generated; Lean claimed, not replayed). *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.1, §1.3; audits/lean_replay/README.md; unresolved_findings.md; LED-ZFR-1.

**4. Every refinement below 7/8 is conditional on the 7/8 inputs.** The best claimed exponent is B_tw = 0.874058503791859… (Liu, hexagon:2610.00655v3; verifier-corrected value). It follows Liu 0.874957, Xu–Sang 0.8748867, Liu 0.874886647 (all number fields) and Chang–Wang 0.874462…, and the total gain over 7/8 is below 10⁻³. All of these import the 7/8 paper's analytic inputs, so none is an independent verification, and Liu's ‘optimality’ theorems are optima of fixed semi-algebraic inequality systems that later work has bypassed. The only independent route, Ji's quartic argument over Q(i) (PAPER-0155), gives 599/600. *Status:* CLAIMS (unrefereed; conditional). *Refs:* LED-ZFR-3 (corrected), LED-LFUN-3 (corrected); quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.5, §4.2; OB-19(e).

**5. How 11/12 escapes parity, and where its risk sits.** The mechanism is a sextic-twisted Möbius mean square (Prop. 3.1). It is proved by Poisson duality to cubic Gauss sums, cube completion and cubic-theta reflection to a quadratic large sieve, followed by a finite induction. Parity information enters through the identity μ = ᾱγ₂³ (up to phases) and the automorphy of Patterson's cubic theta function; the only analytic inequality is the GL quadratic large sieve. Heuristically Prop. 3.1 is of density-hypothesis strength for the sextic family, far beyond generic large-sieve density, so a falsification would most likely surface there. *Status:* [O] reading of an audited CLAIM. *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.1, §2.2, §2.5; OB-12; AC-01.

**6. The extraction lemma makes 11/12 the floor of this architecture.** Suppose an order-n residue-symbol family satisfies the full-row mean square Σ_{N(u)≤D^h} |A_u(D)|² ≪ D^{1+ε}D^h. Then L is zero-free for Re s > β(n,h) = 1/2 + h(n−1)/(2n), so β(6,1) = 11/12, β(4,1) = 7/8, β(3,1) = 5/6 and β(2,1) = 3/4. The duality forces h ≥ 1: for H < D the dual diagonal returns DH + D², which is the large-sieve barrier. Order 6 is where Gauss–Jacobi removes μ and leaves theta coefficients known with no coprimality condition; explicit n-fold metaplectic theta coefficients are known only for n = 2, 3. *Status:* CLAIM (unrefereed paper2) for the extraction lemma, its algebra checked in the Observatory audit + [O] for the floor and for ‘why order 6’. *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §4.1; OB-19(a)–(c); CON-0014 (corrected).

**7. The 7/8 comparison system cannot go below 2/3.** In the parametrisation shared by the 7/8 follow-ups, B(ℓ) = 11/12 − ℓ/4, where ℓ is the total length of the amplifying primes. Positivity of both averaging lengths forces ℓ < 1 − |b|, so B > 2/3 however good the nonprincipal estimates are; 3/4 would need ℓ = 2/3, and 1/2 would need ℓ = 5/3. Going below the current ≈0.8741 needs one of three inputs: a better count for the critical simultaneous-large-value class, a plain fourth moment at κ < 13/18, or a new direct estimate. *Status:* [O]. *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §4.2–4.3 (C3); OB-19(d).

**8. What a half-plane genuinely adds, and what it does not.** Suppose Re s > Θ is zero-free for all Dirichlet L-functions. Then one gets: a uniform effective PNT in progressions for q ≤ x^{1−Θ−ε}; Lindelöf to the right of Θ; n(p) ≪ (log p)^{1/(1−Θ)+ε}; effective class-number bounds; and power-saving Chebotarev for fields abelian over Q(√−3). It does not improve short-interval exponents, Bombieri–Vinogradov, the density hypothesis on (1/2, Θ] or Artin's conjecture, and the proof is qualitative, so it gives no explicit region beyond the half-plane. paper2's ‘rules out Landau–Siegel zeros’ covers the classical region only when log(3q) > 12c₀, which leaves finitely many small moduli to computation. *Status:* CONDITIONAL on the unrefereed half-plane; [O] derivations. *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.1 (Q4), §2.6, §3.

**9. Some conditional consequences have been checked precisely by verifiers.**
- Linnik: with uniform density inputs, a half-plane gives exponent 7/3 + ε (12/5 + ε from refereed inputs), not 8 or 12 (CON-0016, sound_but_known).
- Piltz: at 7/8 the divisor exponents improve to α_k ≤ 7/8 − 49/(24k), which beats the records for k = 14–21 (CON-0018).

**10. Two unrefereed proofs claim that two thirds of the zeros are simple and critical.** Alpöge–Furman (PAPER-0486) and Lamzouri (PAPER-0533) prove liminf N₀ˢ(T)/N(T) ≥ 2/3 with the flat window, and ≥ C_MT = 3/2 − cot(1/√2)/√2 = 0.6725007… with the Montgomery–Taylor window. For distinct zeros the bounds are 5/6 and 0.83625. AF's argument was ‘discovered and written by Claude’ and verified by the authors; LZ's human proof was written after reading AF. The two proofs share only classical refereed inputs: AF uses the von Neumann trace inequality and Sylvester inertia, LZ a flag argument with Bessel's inequality and BGSTB24 Lemma 5. The verifier nevertheless asks that LZ be called a second proof sharing the Montgomery–Taylor extremal problem, not fully independent corroboration. *Status:* CLAIM (unrefereed; AF AI-discovered and human-verified, LZ human-written); the CRIT23 audit found no error affecting any stated result (one proof step repaired, FND-0004). *Refs:* CRIT23 DOSSIER §0–2; LED-CRIT-1, LED-CRIT-2 (corrected); LED-SPACING-18.

**11. The 2/3 mechanism is Montgomery 1973 with RH replaced by inertia.** The proof is Montgomery's pair-correlation second-moment argument, with RH replaced by an inertia (rank–trace) inequality on a finite compression of Weil's Hermitian form, and 2/3 is exactly Montgomery's RH-conditional number. The method is insensitive to o(N) off-line zeros and gives nothing at finite height. Even a 100% version would not be RH. *Status:* SYNTHESIS of an unrefereed CLAIM. *Refs:* CRIT23 DOSSIER §0, §6–7; OB-15; OB-16(g); history/evolution_of_methods.md §5.

**12. The 2/3 method has sharpness results, a ceiling and computer-assisted refinements.** For the flat window's information, 2/3 is sharp: an explicit six-site block configuration, with one third of the zeros off the line, is consistent with it, which is why the Montgomery–Taylor window does better. Every bandwidth-one certificate is said to be ≤ 0.6818287 (author-stated, not audited). Knausgård's refinements (PAPER-0166: 0.67353820 simple and critical, 0.83900460 distinct, 0.67800919 simple) depend on three branch-and-bound searches encoded as Lean axioms, which the kernel does not check; the Observatory probed them only by sampling. *Status:* NUMERICAL (sharpness configuration); author-stated (ceiling); CLAIM (refinements, unrefereed). *Refs:* OB-16(a)–(b); LED-CRIT-3, LED-CRIT-10; CRIT23 DOSSIER §3 (S10).

**13. Landau–Siegel exclusion is claimed in four written proofs with three structurally different cores, and no written proof is free of AI involvement.** OpenAI (PAPER-0529), Banks (PAPER-0160), Kerr (PAPER-0164) and Guo (PAPER-0165) each prove (1−β) log q ≥ c for every real zero of every primitive real L(s,χ). The best all-q explicit constant claimed is c = 1/64 (Guo App. A; verifier-corrected value); Banks gives 3.0·10⁻³ for q ≥ 10¹⁰, and Kimura 5.811·10⁻⁴ (Lean-reported). The arithmetic cores differ: an interpolation determinant in Z[√d,√2]; a Siegel-lemma pigeonhole with Masser's zero estimate; Stepanov-type injectivity in Z[√d]. All nine load-bearing steps were re-derived in each proof, with no verified error and no suspected gap. *Status:* CLAIM (unrefereed); AUDIT FINDING no_issue_found_in_inspected_portion (Lean not replayed; the Lu–Zaman–Zhao production run not replayed). *Refs:* FND-0010; SIEGEL DOSSIER §0–4; LED-LFUN-6 (corrected).

**14. The Siegel method's ceiling is near 0.17, and it says nothing about complex zeros.** Every proof bounds the inert-prime mass by κ log X + e log q + O(1), where the prime ideal theorem forces κ ≥ 1/2, and the zero couples to the primes with a δ(log X)² loss. The recomputed ceilings of the individual chains are 3.244·10⁻³ (Banks), 1.0196·10⁻² (Kimura) and 0.06095 (Guo as stated); with ideal arithmetic the ceiling is 0.0858, and only with ideal arithmetic plus a hypothetical inner scale y → q does it reach 3 − 2√2 ≈ 0.1716 < 1/5. The method cannot give a zero-free interval [1−η, 1), Chowla positivity, (1−β) log q → ∞, or anything about complex zeros, and Linnik's L = 5 is unchanged. *Status:* auditor computations on unrefereed constants. *Refs:* SIEGEL DOSSIER §7; OB-20; CON-0027.

**15. The effective consequences of Siegel exclusion do not need QRH.** Assuming the exclusion, L(1,χ) log q ≥ 0.0076709 for q ≥ 10, and h(D) ≥ 0.00244√|D|/log|D| for D < −4 (auditor's derivation). With Kimura's constant and EKN's unconditional count, the imaginary quadratic fields whose class group has exponent ≤ 2 are exactly the 65 known ones, and Euler's idoneal list is complete; the exact scan behind this was replayed by an independent implementation. QRH is not needed. EKN Thm 2 cannot be fed by the Siegel-exclusion constants, because every 2026 constant is at most 1/64, below EKN's 1/4; paper2's own route (the 11/12 half-plane implies EKN's hypothesis once |D| >= e^3) works only if the unrefereed half-plane holds. *Status:* CONDITIONAL on Kimura Thm 1 + Prop. 9 and EKN Thm 1; the computation is exact. *Refs:* SIEGEL DOSSIER §5; CON-0025 (sound_and_useful); strongest_connections Rank 4.

## 3. Campaign C1: what finite criterion computations can imply (26–34)

**26. RH up to height T implies λ_n > 0 for n ≤ T².** If T ≥ 100 and every zero with 0 < γ ≤ T lies on the line, then λ_n > 0 for 1 ≤ n ≤ T². RH(T₀) gives n ≤ 9.00105·10²⁴, and RH(317) gives n ≤ 100489, so Johansson's rigorous λ₁, …, λ₁₀₀₀₀₀ carry no information beyond height 317 (about 148 zeros). The statement is Oesterlé's (2000, unpublished, reported by Voros); C1 gives a complete independent proof with the Rosser tail bounds written out. *Status:* THEOREM (Observatory campaign; statement IMPORTED). *Refs:* C1 FINAL C1-L2, §3.1, §5; LED-POS-1, LED-POS-2.

**27. All implication horizons are about T².**
- Li: n ≤ T².
- Even Jensen polynomials: d ≤ ⌊T⌋² (GORTTW), i.e. 9.0·10²⁴ at T₀.
- Schoenfeld's ψ, θ, π bounds: x ≤ 1.10·10²⁶ at T₀ (Johnston, superseding Büthe's 2.17·10²⁵).
- Nicolas: x ≲ 4.36·10²³ (sketch only).

The exceptions are the Pólya-frequency and Laguerre criteria, whose horizons are linear in the height (Sra 2026, unrefereed, read as an abstract only). *Status:* THEOREM/IMPORTED per row; HEURISTIC structural summary; PROPOSITION-PROOF-SKETCH for Nicolas. *Refs:* C1 FINAL §3.3–3.6, §4, C1-ADJ-LOCAL.

**28. No published large criterion computation is informative beyond T₀.** Li coefficients to 10⁵, Jensen hyperbolicity to degree 9·10²⁴ and ψ/π tables to 1.1·10²⁶ are logical consequences of RH(T₀), and isolated values of π(x) above 1.1·10²⁶ are new only in the formal sense. Robin's inequality up to 10^(10^13.11485) is not proved to follow from RH(T₀), because no explicit Nicolas/Robin implication theorem exists. It is nevertheless uninformative by two independent heuristics: log n ≈ 3·10¹³, against a sketch horizon of 4.4·10²³ and a single-defect scale of 10^50.5. *Status:* THEOREM (Li, Jensen and ψ rows) + HEURISTIC (Robin, Mertens, Nyman–Beurling). *Refs:* C1 FINAL §5; OB-17.

**29. A proved bound Λ ≤ 0.2 adds nothing beyond RH(T₀).** PT21 Cor. 2 is derived from RH verified up to 2.51·10¹². So any constraint on zeros it implies is already implied by RH(T₀) plus unconditional theory, and this refutes the investigator's sentence that de Bruijn–Newman has no finite RH-implication; the same holds for Planat–Raymond's claim, whose RH input is also at height ≤ T₀. In the gap-lattice model, no bound ≥ 0.1013 could exclude a single quadruple above T₀. ## 6. Campaign C4: what a Weil window can see (49–54)

**49. The Nyquist-threshold heuristic is refuted.** The brief's heuristic says that a window of half-width L is blind to off-line zeros at heights T ≫ 2πe^{2L}. It fails for every fixed δ: under the single-defect hypothesis H1, the window L_B = (1/(2δ))(log log T + log log log T + O_δ(1)) already detects the defect, so L_B − L_N(T) → −∞, where L_N = (1/2) log(T/2π). The corrected asymptotic is e^{2δL}/(4δ) ∼ (0.5 + 0.704L) log T (not 1.407L), and at T = 3·10¹² the rigorous window beats Nyquist only for δ ≥ 0.2354. Danesh (arXiv:2611.10726) uses the same e^{δx} mechanism but gives no window law. *Status:* THEOREM under H1 (vacuous if RH holds; Observatory campaign C4-B) + NUMERICAL-RIGOROUS (C4-B2); the heuristic is REFUTED. *Refs:* C4 FINAL C4-B, C4-B2, C4-R, §3.3.

**50. Blindness is proved only in an astronomically thin neighbourhood of the line.** If the real-zero form satisfies P ≥ a‖g‖² on window L, then W(g) ≥ (a − 2m[σ²(δ,L) + 2 sinh²(δL)/T])‖g‖² at every height. So any bounded family of windows is blind, uniformly in height, to an explicit neighbourhood of the line. With Zhu's unrefereed a = 8.9·10⁻¹⁸ at L = 0.8, this covers δ ≤ 3.5·10⁻⁹ in the additive model; the radius is tiny because a(L) collapses doubly exponentially. Consequently, controlling all off-line zeros needs an unbounded family of windows. *Status:* THEOREM (Observatory campaign C4-ADJ-BLIND); the input a is unrefereed. *Refs:* C4 FINAL §3.3; §3.6 item 2.

**51. Whether a defect is visible reduces to a local resolvent.** For a conjugate pair, with u = e^{−iT′x}cosh(δx) and w = e^{−iT′x}δ⁻¹sinh(δx) on the window, h_g(T′−iδ)·conj(h_g(T′+iδ)) + c.c. = 2|⟨g,u⟩|² − 2δ²|⟨g,w⟩|². Hence a quadruple is visible iff 2mδ²·λ_max(E_w* Q⁻¹ E_w) > 1, where Q = P + 2mE_uE_u* is coercive. Every detection or blindness statement is a bound on this quantity D(T,δ,L), and C4-B's sinh test function is w up to a factor. *Status:* THEOREM (C4-ADJ-PAIR, elementary; equivalent to the standard Schur–Sylvester criterion C4-C). *Refs:* C4 FINAL §3.2.

**52. Near the line nothing is proved, and certified witnesses rule out height-only laws.** Arb-certified test functions show near-line defects (κ = δ log(T/2π) below κ_c = 2.1773) being detected below the Nyquist window in both synthetic injection models. In the collision model: κ = 0.22 at L_N − 0.016, and κ = 1.47 at L_N − 0.486. In the additive model: κ = 1.86 at L_N − 0.159. So no law of the form ‘L* ≥ L_N(T) − c’ holds across models, and the investigator's ‘Nyquist locality for κ < κ_c’ is REFUTED as a universal law. *Status:* NUMERICAL-RIGOROUS (model statements); REFUTED (the corrected statement in C4-R). *Refs:* C4 FINAL C4-ADJ-WIT, §3.5; precedence note in Section 0.

**53. What window positivity would buy, in numbers.** Under H1, positivity on window L excludes single defects only where the C4-B margin is positive. Beyond T_PT this needs L > 6.49 (δ = 0.49), 7.94 (δ = 0.4) or 10.56 (δ = 0.3), whereas the largest certified window is 0.8 (Zhu, unrefereed). Detection holds for all L ≥ L_N(T) + C(δ), with certified C = 4.40 at δ = 0.49 up to 313.17 at δ = 0.01, i.e. δ·C(δ) ≈ 2.15–3.13; whether C can be O(1) or O(log 1/δ) is open. *Status:* THEOREM (contrapositive C4-ADJ-INV; finiteness C4-ADJ-C) + NUMERICAL-RIGOROUS (ranges) + PROPOSITION-PROOF-SKETCH (the tail T > 10^tmax). *Refs:* C4 FINAL §3.3.

**54. Galerkin numbers are upper bounds and certify nothing.** C4's profile values of λ_min(L) are Rayleigh–Ritz upper bounds 0.3–6% above the infimum, so only 2 significant digits are meaningful. Zhu's Table 1 gives the reference values 9.34·10⁻⁷ at L = 0.5, 1.65·10⁻¹⁷ at 0.8 and 5.88·10⁻³⁰ at 1.0. Thresholds from finite bases bound L* from above and can never prove non-detection, and positive Galerkin effective matrices approach the infimum from above, so they certify nothing about the continuum. ## 7. Campaign C5: what transfers from function fields (55–62)

**55. Over F_q a single 2g×2g Toeplitz form decides RH.** Let P be an FE polynomial of degree 2g with d distinct normalised roots, m of them off the circle. ## 9. Obstructions and method ceilings (76–85)

**76. Davenport–Heilbronn is the master test (with a corrected count).** A Dirichlet series with a Riemann-type functional equation but no Euler product can have zeros off the line. For DH the off-line zeros up to height T number ≍ T; this is a density-zero subset, not a positive proportion (verifier correction), while a positive proportion of the zeros lie on the line. The question ‘where is multiplicativity used in a way DH cannot imitate?’ separates methods that could prove RH from those that cannot; paper2 uses multiplicativity at four load-bearing points. *Status:* THEOREM (classical) + verifier correction. *Refs:* LED-LFUN-14 (corrected); OB-1; quasi_rh DOSSIER §2.1; history/evolution_of_methods.md §0.

**77. Proportion results are blind to o(T) off-line zeros (‘density currency’).** Moving o(T) separated zeros off the line changes every n-level correlation sum with Schwartz test functions by a negligible amount, under the verifier's placement condition. So arguments whose only zero inputs are Riemann–von Mangoldt, correlations with o(TL) error, Levinson–Conrey mean values or bandwidth-limited trace moments cannot exclude even one off-line quadruple. Their strongest possible conclusion, ‘100% critical and simple’, is attained under the pair correlation conjecture without RH (Goldston–Lee–Schettler–Suriajaya). *Status:* formalization of folklore at the level of a theorem (CON-0030, corrected). *Refs:* OB-15; LED-CRIT-13; C3 FINAL D9.

**78. Rodgers–Tao leaves no slack for heat-flow or kernel arguments.** Since Λ ≥ 0, any valid argument must fail for every t < 0. In particular, properties of Φ that survive multiplication by e^{−εu²} cannot prove RH: positivity, evenness, super-exponential decay, log-concavity of Φ(√v) and all moment Turán inequalities are of this kind. A Fourier-side proof must use something that an arbitrarily small backward deformation destroys, such as exact theta modularity. *Status:* THEOREM (refereed) + the elimination derived in C2-29. *Refs:* OB-11; LED-DBN-6; C2 FINAL C2-29.

**79. Parity and pretentiousness bound sieve-type and multiplicative-only routes.** Type I level-of-distribution information cannot determine signed Möbius sums, even at level x^{1−ε}. Multiplicativity with |f| ≤ 1 plus prime-sum data gives savings of only (log log x)^{−3/5} to 1/log x (Granville–Soundararajan). Quasi-RH escapes parity through an algebraic identity plus theta automorphy, and Siegel exclusion escapes it through integrality via the polynomial method. *Status:* THEOREM (classical) + [O] (escape mechanisms). *Refs:* OB-12, OB-13; quasi_rh DOSSIER §2.2; SIEGEL DOSSIER §7.

**80. Mollifiers cannot reach RH, and proven moments give no half-plane.** Perfect mollification is impossible: (1/T)∫|1 − ζM_θ|² ≥ c/θ (Radziwiłł, unrefereed). By Bettin–Gonek, a mollified mean square ≪ T^{1+ε} with mollifier length T^θ gives zero-freeness for Re s > 1/2 + 1/(2θ). That has content only for θ > 1 (7/8 needs θ = 4/3), while the proven lengths are 4/7 and 17/33. *Status:* THEOREM (refereed, except Radziwiłł). *Refs:* OB-14; PAPER-0064; CON-0031 (corrected); LED-CRIT-9.

**81. Zero-density barriers: the 7/10 pivot and Kakeya-hardness.** LV(σ, τ) ≥ min(2 − 2σ, τ), so Montgomery's large-values conjecture yields the density hypothesis and nothing more without input specific to ζ. Bourgain's counterexample forces ℓ^∞ coefficients, and Montgomery's ℓ^q conjecture implies the Kakeya conjecture in every dimension. Any zero-free half-plane with θ₀ ≥ 7/10 leaves the short-interval exponents 17/30 and 2/15 unchanged, which covers every claimed 2026 half-plane. *Status:* THEOREM. *Refs:* OB-21; LED-ZDE-18 (corrected); CON-0028.

**82. Classical zero-free-region methods have their own ceilings.** Landau's extremal constant satisfies 34.468305 < V < 34.4889920009, which caps the trigonometric-polynomial method near R₀ ≈ 9.5. BTY's smoothing reaches efficiency 1.98 out of a hypothetical maximum of 2. Beurling systems show that an Euler product plus integer regularity N(x) = ax + O(x^θ) with θ ∈ [1/2, 1) cannot improve on the de la Vallée Poussin shape (DMV; Broucke Thm 1.6); for θ < 1/2 this is open (CON-0032 T4). *Status:* THEOREM. *Refs:* OB-22; LED-ZFR-16, LED-ZFR-17.

**83. Spectral realization is cheap; positivity carries the content.** Connes (critical zeros, on a Sobolev space) and Meyer (all zeros; not unitary once off-line zeros exist) give unconditional realizations. Berry–Keating's xp reproduces only the smooth counting term. The Bender–Brody–Müller momentum operator has deficiency indices (1, 0), and ψ_z ∉ L² for Re z ≤ 3/2. No unconditional self-adjoint realization exists in the 2024–2026 literature surveyed: every step relevant to RH is either conditional or a positivity statement equivalent to RH. *Status:* IMPORTED + AUDIT FINDINGS (FND-0001, FND-0006). *Refs:* OB-25; LED-OPS-3, LED-OPS-17, LED-OPS-18, LED-OPS-20; C3 FINAL D4.

**84. Almost every route's closing statement is equivalent to RH.** The archive surveys thirteen routes, among them Weil windows, operators, de Branges spaces, Li, Nyman–Beurling, Robin, de Bruijn–Newman, Jensen, quasi-RH and function-field transfer. Every closing statement is RH-equivalent except two kinds: zero density (the density hypothesis, genuinely weaker) and density-currency targets (which never give RH). Graded criteria (Suzuki's Ψ_ω, Beurling L^p density, the host's graded premises) coincide with quasi-RH at the corresponding level, so they offer no missing lemma weaker than quasi-RH. *Status:* SYNTHESIS. *Refs:* frontiers/missing_lemmas.md (summary table; cross-route remarks); CON-0007 (corrected).

**85. The 2/3 window certificates do not reach individual GL(d) L-functions.** For a single degree-d L-function the diagonal bandwidth is 1/d, and the window certificate for simple zeros on the line is negative for every d ≥ 2; the threshold for this window class is λ* = 0.5501939647. A positive bound 1/R*_{1/d} for distinct zeros survives: 0.4617 for d = 2 (conditional on a GL(2) pair-sum asymptotic) and 0.3214 for d = 3. Known critical-line results for individual GL(2) L-functions stay far lower (≥ 6.96% for each cusp form). *Status:* Observatory computation (CON-0011, CON-0035, corrected) + IMPORTED. *Refs:* OB-16(f); LED-CRIT-19; strongest_connections Rank 7.

## 10. Verified connections (86–90)

**86. Euler product plus integer regularity cannot give RH (the Beurling frontier).** For every β > 2/5 there is a discrete Beurling system with N(x) = ax + O(x^{β+ε}) for which ψ(x) = x + O(x^{1/2+ε}) fails. The proof is a case split: if RH holds, use BDR's RH-conditional construction; if RH fails, use the rational integers. The region β < 1/2, Θ ≥ 2/3, which contains 7/8 and 11/12, has no known countermodel. *Status:* [O] Observatory-proposed theorem (non-constructive; one verifier, sound_and_useful; not adjudicated), given BDR Thm 1.3 (arXiv; journal status not verified). *Refs:* CON-0032 (sound_and_useful); OB-7; AC-27; strongest_connections Rank 1.

**87. Exactly where RH enters the 19/27 simple-zero machinery.** Unconditionally N*(T) ≥ |S₁|²/(S₂ + D/2), where D is an explicit off-line pair defect. If every zero with T < γ ≤ 2T satisfies |β − 1/2| ≤ η(T)/(log T)^{3/2} with η → 0, then liminf N*/N ≥ 19/27; quantitatively, η < 0.01315 already beats 0.6725. The right comparators are simple zeros anywhere (0.67800919, with cap 0.6818287), not 0.6725. *Status:* Observatory-proposed theorem, re-derived by the verifier (CON-0006, corrected). *Refs:* CON-0006; strongest_connections Rank 3; AC-11.

**88. Nyman–Beurling approximation rates determine sup Re ρ exactly.** Let Θ = sup Re ρ and α ∈ (Θ, 1). In particular, RH holds iff s_n(α) ≪ n^{1−2α+ε} for one (equivalently every) α ∈ (1/2, 1). *Status:* Observatory-proposed proposition (both directions re-derived at proof-sketch level). *Refs:* CON-0021 (sound_and_useful); AC-26; strongest_connections Rank 2.

## 11. History and method (91–96)

**91. RH research is a sequence of changes of representation, each with a ceiling.** RH has been read as a statement about the zeros of an entire function, the error term for primes, the growth of |ζ|, mean values of Dirichlet polynomials, positivity of a quadratic form, an operator spectrum and intersection theory on a surface. Each representation made one obstacle tractable, and each stopped short of Re s = 1/2. Every method bounds something other than ‘zeros off the line’: the rightmost zero, the number of far zeros, a proportion, a finite window or a finite height. The passage from ‘few’, ‘most’ or ‘up to T’ to ‘none’ has never been made. *Status:* SYNTHESIS. *Refs:* history/evolution_of_methods.md §0, §12, §13.

**92. Method-limit theorems are the decade's most distinctive development.** Examples are the mollifier error ≥ c/θ, the bandwidth-one cap 0.6818, the tangency at 7/10, the Kakeya-hardness of the ℓ^q large-values conjecture, the h ≥ 1 floor, Λ ≥ 0, and the compatibility of the Alternative Hypothesis with band-limited correlation data. *Status:* SYNTHESIS of results of mixed status (each labelled in its own entry). *Refs:* history/evolution_of_methods.md §12; frontiers/known_obstructions.md.

**93. Refuted conjectures and failed proofs share a few mechanisms.** The refuted conjectures (Mertens, Pólya, Turán's T(x), Turán's partial sums, de Branges positivity) were sufficient conditions stronger than RH. They fell to explicit-formula arguments of Ingham type, or to numerics at small height (de Branges' condition fails at the 34th zero). The failed proofs combine an unused hypothesis, an error of support or domain, an inequality in the wrong direction, or a limit taken at the boundary of convergence. The spectral programmes stop exactly at the existence of a positive inner product. *Status:* SYNTHESIS (HEURISTIC). *Refs:* history/abandoned_approaches.md §1, §19; C3 FINAL §4.

**94. Record ladders show slow, constant-level progress on every classical front.**
- The zero-free constant R fell from 30.4679 (1899) to the refereed 5.558691, while the Vinogradov–Korobov shape has been frozen since 1958.
- Verification grew from 15 zeros (1903) to 1.24·10¹³.
- κ grew from Selberg's κ > 0 (1942) through 0.3474 (Levinson 1974) to 0.4173 (PRZZ 2020), before the unrefereed 2026 jump in κ*.
- μ(1/2) fell from 1/4 to 13/84.

*Status:* IMPORTED. *Refs:* history/chronological_timeline.md (‘Record ladders at a glance’); LED-ZFR-5, LED-VERIF-1, LED-CRIT-4, LED-GROWTH-1.

**95. Geometry proved an RH over F_q, and its positivity has no arithmetic substitute.** Weil (1948) and Deligne (1974, 1980) proved RH for curves and for smooth projective varieties over finite fields. Zero statistics transfer to number fields, but positivity does not: there is no Frobenius cohomology, no Spec Z × Spec Z and no Hodge-index positivity, and this transfer problem is unresolved. *Status:* THEOREM (refereed) + SYNTHESIS. *Refs:* LED-LFUN-17; history/evolution_of_methods.md §9, §13; PAPER-0176, PAPER-0496, PAPER-0515; OB-24.

**96. How the archive grades evidence, and what that means for citing it.** Evidence levels run from L0 (identifier verified) through L1 (full text read) and L2 (central lemmas re-derived) to L3 (advocate, skeptic and adjudicator). Only 154 of the 1000 corpus works were read, targeted L2 audits covered the 15 works enumerated in quality/evidence_audit.md §2 (which states the count as 12), and only paper2 was audited completely at L2. An error is reported only with an independently reproduced witness, and agreement among agents sharing a prompt is not independent evidence. ‘No error found’ means that AI agents found no error in the inspected portion; it is not a referee report. *Status:* QUALITY RECORD. *Refs:* methodology.md §5–6; quality/evidence_audit.md §1–2; quality/limitations.md.

## 12. Computation and certification practice (97–100)

**97. The archive's zero data is certified, and everything else is labelled.** EXP-0001 holds the first 100,000 zeros as Arb balls (maximum radius 1.7·10⁻²⁴, with Turing-method indexing), and it underlies C1–C5. Arb certificates are conditional on the correctness of FLINT/Arb, and mpmath or floating-point output is always non-rigorous high precision. *Status:* NUMERICAL-RIGOROUS. *Refs:* quality/evidence_audit.md §1; C1 FINAL §7; methodology.md §8.

**98. A smooth zero-density tail needs its boundary term.** Starting a smooth tail at the last computed zero without the term −g_n(T*)S(T*) produces errors of order g(T*)|S(T*)|, about 0.9 at n = 10⁵. That is why two independent codes disagreed by 0.91 with Johansson's λ₁₀₀₀₀₀ = 462580.782407; with the term included, they agree to 1.2·10⁻⁵. *Status:* NUMERICAL-NONRIGOROUS (methodology finding C2-ADJ-4). *Refs:* C2 FINAL §1 row A2, §5.

**99. Certificates fail in mundane ways, and the warning signs are known.** A winding-number enclosure that excludes every integer means the contour did not close: boundary points generated in floating point as a + (b−a)j/n miss the corners. Other recorded failures:
- rounding Jensen coefficients to 60 digits fabricated non-real roots at d = 100;
- acb_series' default length silently truncated series, and importing a module reset flint.ctx.prec;
- at 50 digits, a = −log|1 − 1/ρ₀| underflows for T ≥ 10¹⁰⁰ (use log1p);
- cancellation amplified tiny ordinate radii to [±2·10¹²³].

*Status:* methodology findings. *Refs:* C2 FINAL §1 row A1, §5; C1 FINAL §6; C4 FINAL §4.

**100. The fastest route to certainty about the 2026 claims is formal, but formal does not mean faithful.** A kernel replay of OpenAI's 7/8 challenges would certify the ζ and Dirichlet statements independently of the exposition. But Lean checks statements against definitions, not the fidelity of those definitions (the custom Hecke LFunction), and extra axioms escape the kernel (Knausgård's three run-axioms). Lamzouri's formalization assumes BGSTB24 Lemma 5 and Riemann–von Mangoldt as hypotheses. No Lean development was built or replayed in this archive. *Status:* SYNTHESIS (state of verification). *Refs:* quasi_rh DOSSIER §1.3, §5(2); audits/lean_replay/README.md; LED-CRIT-2 (corrected), LED-CRIT-3; unsolved A1, A2, A6, A9.
