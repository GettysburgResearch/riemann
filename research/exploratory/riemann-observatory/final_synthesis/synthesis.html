The Riemann Observatory

A map of Riemann Hypothesis research from Riemann (1859) and its antecedents to November 2026: a curated 1,000-work corpus, a verified ledger of frontier records, audits of the major 2026 claims, five adjudicated research campaigns, and registers of findings, connections and obstructions.

RH remains open. Nothing here proves or disproves it. All verification was done by AI agents (different agent instances checking each other); no human expert has reviewed the archive. "No error found" means none was found in the inspected portion.

Verified findings, in the wording the register allows

verified error Hilbert–Pólya Hamiltonian (2024)

The Stage-II similarity-transformation claim in Yakaboylu's J. Phys. A paper fails: the weight defines an indefinite form and the boundary condition admits spurious solutions where ζ ≠ 0. The paper does not claim RH. (FND-0001)

verified error Farmer, Table 4.1 (2022)

Printed Jensen-polynomial thresholds do not match the configuration described (for j = 10: certified 120, printed 119). The paper's conclusion is unaffected. (FND-0002)

localized, not load-bearing Zero-free constant 4.896 (2026 preprint)

One Table 1 entry is not a valid bound for the stated polynomial; with the corrected value the theorem still holds. We independently reproduced the constant 4.896. (FND-0003)

correct but misread Three citation hazards

Connes 1999 Theorem 5 is proved in positive characteristic only; Andrade–Keating's Theorem 1 rests on their Conjecture 5; an apparent "k ≥ 12" in Ng–Shen–Wong was our own text-extraction artifact. (FND-0006, -0007, -0008)

Research campaigns (investigator, skeptic, adjudicator)

CampaignMain adjudicated outcome
C1 Detection horizonsTheorem: RH up to height T implies λn > 0 for n ≤ T². With the rigorous verification height T₀ ≈ 3·10¹², published Li, Jensen and ψ/π computations carry no information beyond T₀.
C2 Countermodel zooCertified off-line zeros for Davenport–Heilbronn and Epstein functions. F(s) = ζ(s)(1 + c·2⁻ˢ + 2·4⁻ˢ), c > 2√2, has an Euler product and a functional equation yet zeros off the line, so "uses the Euler product" is not a sufficient sanity check.
C3 Failure anatomyExact failing steps of withdrawn proofs; a 15-mode failure taxonomy and checklist. The de Branges zero condition fails at 15,567 of the first 100,000 zeros, with an exact criterion in terms of S(γn) mod 2.
C4 Weil-window localityheuristic refuted The proposed Nyquist threshold is false: under a single-defect hypothesis a window of length L ≈ (1/(2δ)) log log T already detects an off-line zero at height T. Blindness holds only extremely close to the line.
C5 Function-field transferSharp window theorem over 𝔽q: Weil positivity on (−a, a) holds iff T⌈2a/log q⌉ ≥ 0, so RH for a genus-g curve follows from any window with a > (g − ½) log q.
