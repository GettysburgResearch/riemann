**Main result (as stated in index content).** A normalized sequence is a Polya frequency sequence iff its generating function is e^{gamma z} prod(1+alpha_i z)/prod(1-beta_i z), alpha_i,beta_i,gamma>=0, sum(alpha_i+beta_i)<infinity.
