**Main result (as stated in index content).** Self-adjoint pseudo-Laplacians on finite-volume hyperbolic surfaces giving new proof of continuation of Eisenstein series.

**Techniques.** pseudo-Laplacian

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Defines pseudo-Laplacians on finite-volume hyperbolic surfaces: self-adjoint operators obtained from the Laplacian on functions whose constant term vanishes above height a. Uses them to give a new proof of the meromorphic continuation of Eisenstein series; their eigenvalues are tied to zeros of truncated Eisenstein constant terms.
