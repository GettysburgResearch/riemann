**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Develops the theory of weights for mixed l-adic sheaves. If F is mixed of weights ≤ w, then H^i_c has weights ≤ w+i, and the weights are stable under Rf_!. Also proves hard Lefschetz in characteristic p and equidistribution theorems; these give square-root cancellation for many multivariable exponential sums.
