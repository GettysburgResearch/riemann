**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Argues that the Bender–Brody–Müller operator is not self-adjoint: momentum on the half-line has no self-adjoint extension, and the Hurwitz-zeta eigenfunctions are not in L²(R_+) for Re z = 1/2. The proposed symmetry therefore does not force real eigenvalues.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Points out that the momentum operator p on the half-line has no self-adjoint extension, that the proposed eigenfunctions (Hurwitz zeta on Re z=1/2) are not square-integrable on the half-line, and that the claimed reality argument therefore fails analytically.
