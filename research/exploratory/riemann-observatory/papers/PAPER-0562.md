**Main result (as stated in index content).** Spectral realization of Riemann zeros through a massless Dirac fermion in Rindler spacetime with delta potentials at square-free integers, with an associated Hamiltonian.

**Techniques.** Dirac operator; delta potentials

**Research potential (curator).** Berry-Keating-style operator.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Reviews spectral approaches to the zeros (xp variants, the Landau-level realization, boundary conditions encoding ζ, absorption versus emission spectra) and proposals for Hamiltonians whose discrete spectrum contains the zeros.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Reviews spectral approaches to RH (Berry-Keating xp and its regularisations, Connes, the Bender-Brody-Müller operator), with the obstacles of self-adjointness, boundary conditions and the missing arithmetic input.
