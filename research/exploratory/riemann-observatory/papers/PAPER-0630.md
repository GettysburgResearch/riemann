**Main result (as stated in index content).** Argues zeta zeros behave as eigenvalues of a chaotic quantum Hamiltonian with GUE statistics, discussing self-adjoint operator hopes.

**Techniques.** semiclassics; quantum chaos

**Research potential (curator).** Context for Berry-Keating.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proposes that the zeros behave like semiclassical energy levels of a classically chaotic, time-irreversible system with periodic orbits labelled by primes, which explains GUE statistics and predicts non-universal deviations at long range.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Reads the Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula as a Gutzwiller trace formula for a hypothetical chaotic Hamiltonian without time-reversal symmetry, with primitive orbits of period log p. Predicts GUE statistics plus prime-determined deviations and compares with zero data.
