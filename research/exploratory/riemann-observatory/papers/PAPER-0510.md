**Main result (as stated in index content).** Elementary proof of |N-p|=O(sqrt p) for y^2=f(x) over F_p via an auxiliary polynomial vanishing to high order at F_p-points.

**Research potential (curator).** Test whether auxiliary-polynomial vanishing arguments have any analogue for zero counting.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Gives an elementary proof of |N − p| = O(√p) for y^2=f(x) over F_p. The method builds an auxiliary polynomial that vanishes to high order at all F_p-points (a polynomial method analogous to Thue-Siegel).
