**Main result (as stated in index content).** Effective lower bounds for h(q) conditional on existence of many subnormal gaps between zeros of the associated class-group L-function.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Shows that if zeta(s)L(s,chi_D) has many zero gaps smaller than half the average spacing, then L(1,chi_D) and the class number of Q(sqrt(-D)) satisfy effective lower bounds (no Siegel zero).

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Shows that if zeros of certain Hecke L-functions frequently exhibit small gaps (well below half the average spacing, contradicting GUE only in a mild way), then h(−d) ≫ √d/(log d)^A effectively, so Landau-Siegel zeros are excluded.
