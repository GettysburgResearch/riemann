**Main result (as stated in index content).** Generalizing Iwaniec-Luo-Sarnak, uses one-level density theorems to estimate proportion of non-vanishing of L-functions at low-lying points via reproducing kernel Hilbert space extremal problems.

**Techniques.** de Branges spaces; one-level density

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Solves the Fourier optimization problems behind one-level-density applications with reproducing-kernel (de Branges-type) Hilbert spaces. Under GRH this improves bounds on central-point non-vanishing proportions and on the height of the lowest zero in several families.
