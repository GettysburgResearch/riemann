**Main result (as stated in index content).** Constructs Fourier interpolation bases for functions analytic in a symmetric strip, recovering f from its values at nontrivial zeta zeros and the Fourier transform at points (log n)/(4 pi) (up to normalization).

**Techniques.** Fourier interpolation; explicit formula; modular forms

**Research potential (curator).** Quantitative interpolation properties as a zero-location test.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Constructs Fourier interpolation bases for functions analytic in a symmetric strip in which f is recovered from its values at points given by the nontrivial zeta zeros and \hat f at points (log n)/(4π) (up to normalization), via a duality principle and weakly holomorphic modular forms for the theta group; analogues for Dedekind/L-functions.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Builds Fourier interpolation bases for functions analytic in a strip, in which the nodes on one side come from nontrivial zeta zeros (also zeros of certain Dedekind zeta and products of L-functions). The proof uses a duality principle with kernels of general Dirichlet series and modular integrals for the theta group.
