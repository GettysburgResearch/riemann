**Main result (as stated in index content).** Zero spacing distributions of differenced L-functions (title-based).

**Techniques.** zero spacing

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Uses de Branges' Hermite–Biehler framework to show that differenced combinations of shifted completed L-functions ξ(s ± h) have only critical-line zeros with asymptotically regular (non-GUE) spacing. The result is unconditional for h large enough and holds for all h > 0 under RH (exact thresholds as in the paper).

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Assuming RH, uses the Hermite-Biehler property of shifted xi-functions, E(z) = xi(1/2 + h - iz) with h > 0, to show that the associated differenced functions built from xi(s+h) and xi(s-h) have only real, simple, interlacing zeros whose spacing distribution tends to uniform.
