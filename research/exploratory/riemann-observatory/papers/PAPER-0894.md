**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** For discrete sets Lambda, M in R with density exponents p, q satisfying 1/p + 1/q = 1, supercritical pairs are uniqueness pairs for the Schwartz class and subcritical pairs are non-uniqueness pairs.
