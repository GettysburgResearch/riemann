**Main result (as stated in index content).** Sequences with generating function z^k e^{gamma z} prod(1+alpha_i z)/prod(1-beta_i z) are totally positive, with part of the converse proved.

**Research potential (curator).** Total-positivity tests for Taylor/Jensen data of xi.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Shows that sequences with generating function z^k e^{gamma z} prod(1+alpha_i z)/prod(1-beta_i z), with alpha_i, beta_i, gamma >= 0 and sum(alpha_i+beta_i) < infinity, are totally positive, and proves part of the converse. In particular a finite sequence is a Polya frequency sequence iff its polynomial has only real nonpositive zeros.
