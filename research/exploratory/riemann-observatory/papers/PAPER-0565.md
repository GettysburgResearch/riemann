**Main result (as stated in index content).** Under RH, for fixed h, eps>0, sup of real and imaginary parts of log zeta(1/2+it) on [UT-h,UT+h] lie in [(1-eps) loglog T,(1+eps) loglog T] with high probability.

**Techniques.** RH conditional; probability

**Research potential (curator).** Extreme-value heuristics.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Under RH, proves that max_{|h|<=1} log|zeta(1/2+it+ih)| = (1 + o(1)) log log T in probability for random t in [T,2T] (leading order of FHK), and the analogue for Im log zeta.
