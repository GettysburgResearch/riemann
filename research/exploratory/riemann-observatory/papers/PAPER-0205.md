**Main result (as stated in index content).** Maier proves oscillations in the number of primes in short intervals (log x)^A, refuting Cramer's random model uniformity.

**Techniques.** matrix method

**Research potential (curator).** Limits of probabilistic prime models.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** For each lambda>1, lim sup and lim inf of (pi(x+(log x)^lambda)-pi(x))/(log x)^{lambda-1} are respectively >1 and <1. This contradicts the Cramér random model's prediction of asymptotic 1.
