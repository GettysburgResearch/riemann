**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves that the dynamical zeta functions ζ(s) = Π_γ (1 − e^{−sℓ(γ)})^{−1} of expanding maps and Anosov flows continue meromorphically beyond the abscissa of convergence, expressed via transfer-operator determinants.
