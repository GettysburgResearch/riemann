**Main result (as stated in index content).** Locally uniform limits of real polynomials with only real zeros are entire functions of genus at most 1, of the form c z^m e^{-az^2+bz} prod(1-z/x_k)e^{z/x_k} with a>=0 (seed note).

**Techniques.** entire functions; real-rooted polynomial limits

**Research potential (curator).** Anchor reference for Laguerre-Polya class membership of xi-type functions.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Shows that locally uniform limits of real polynomials with only real zeros are entire functions of genus ≤1 type c z^m e^{−az^2+bz}Π(1−z/x_k)e^{z/x_k} (a≥0), the class later completed by Pólya and now called the Laguerre-Pólya class.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Studies real entire functions of genus 0 and 1 with only real zeros: they are uniform limits of real-rooted polynomials, and real-rootedness passes to their derivatives. This is the origin of the Laguerre-Polya class, whose characterization Polya completed in 1913.
