**Research potential (curator).** Primary source for Riemann-Siegel formula and Riemann's computational methods.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Reconstructs from Riemann's unpublished notes the Riemann-Siegel asymptotic formula for Z(t) (main sum of about sqrt(t/2pi) terms plus an explicit asymptotic correction series) and a new integral representation of zeta.
