**Main result (as stated in index content).** Computes stable homology of braid groups with symplectic coefficients; with homological stability this proves the CFKRS moment conjecture for families of quadratic L-functions over F_q[T] for large q.

**Research potential (curator).** Topological route to random-matrix moment predictions.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Computes the stable homology of braid groups (hyperelliptic mapping class groups) with symplectic coefficients. Combined with the homological stability theorem of Miller-Patzt-Petersen-Randal-Williams, this proves the CFKRS moment conjectures for all moments of quadratic L-functions over F_q(T), for q sufficiently large relative to the moment.
