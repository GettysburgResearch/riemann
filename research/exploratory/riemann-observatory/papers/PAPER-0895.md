**Main result (as stated in index content).** If log w is uniformly continuous and int log w/(1+x^2) > -infinity, there is a nonzero entire F of arbitrarily small exponential type with |F|<=w on R; yields the radius of completeness of exponentials.

**Techniques.** entire functions of exponential type; multiplier theorem

**Research potential (curator).** Apply to completeness questions for exponentials at zeta zeros.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** The multiplier theorem: a weight w with int log w(x)/(1+x^2) dx > -infinity and log w uniformly continuous admits a nonzero entire function of arbitrarily small exponential type with |F| <= w on R.
