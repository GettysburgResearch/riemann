*Basis:* arxiv_abstract. This summary is derived from index content (arXiv abstract and/or zbMATH review) or, where marked, the curator's unverified recollection. It is not an audit.

**Main result (as stated in index content).** Explicit Harris-Kudla type formulas for triples of Maass/holomorphic forms on the hyperbolic plane mod congruence groups and cocompact lattices from Eichler orders: |int phi1 phi2 phi3|^2 = constant times L(1/2, phi1 x phi2 x phi3)/prod L(1, sym^2 phi_i). Assuming subconvexity for these L-values yields QUE; the Lindelof hypothesis gives QUE with optimal rate; also decay of third moments (Random Wave conjecture link) via Iwaniec/Ivic/Jutila subconvexity and Kim-Shahidi cuspidality of sym^3.

**Techniques.** triple product L-functions; period formulas; Lindelof hypothesis; QUE; subconvexity

**Research potential (curator).** Use the period-L-value identity as a template for converting GLH-type bounds into dynamical equidistribution statements, and for testing which unconditional subconvexity inputs suffice.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Proves an explicit triple-product formula: |int phi_1 phi_2 phi_3|^2 is a constant times L(1/2, phi_1 x phi_2 x phi_3)/prod_i L(1, sym^2 phi_i). Hence the generalized Lindelof hypothesis implies QUE with the optimal rate.
