**Main result (as stated in index content).** Characterizes linear operators on multivariate polynomials preserving stability (no zeros in the product of open upper half-planes) via stability of the symbol T[prod(z_i+w_i)^{kappa_i}] (seed note).

**Techniques.** stable polynomials; Lee-Yang

**Research potential (curator).** Search for operators acting on zeta-related polynomials that preserve stability.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Characterizes linear operators on multivariate polynomials preserving stability (no zeros in the product of open upper half-planes) via stability of the symbol T[Π(z_i+w_i)^{κ_i}].

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Characterizes all finite-order linear operators on multivariate polynomials that preserve stability (no zeros in an open half-plane product) or real stability. Such an operator either has rank <= 2 of a special form, or its symbol T[(z+w)^kappa] (or T[(z-w)^kappa]) is stable.
