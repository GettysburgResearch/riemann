## Content (index-level record — the paper itself was NOT read)

*Basis:* zbmath_review. This summary is derived from index content (arXiv abstract and/or zbMATH review) or, where marked, the curator's unverified recollection. It is not an audit.

**Main result (as stated in index content).** Probably the first computation of some roots of zeta(s); extends Gram's 1895 note on smallest roots of xi(t).

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Computes the first 15 nontrivial zeros (ordinates up to about 66), shows they lie on the critical line, and introduces Gram points together with the empirical alternation later called Gram's law.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Locates the first 15 nontrivial zeros (all with t < 66) on the critical line and observes that zeros tend to alternate with the points g_n where theta(g_n)=n*pi (the origin of 'Gram points' and 'Gram's law').
