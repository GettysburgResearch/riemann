**Techniques.** period functions; transfer operators

**Research potential (curator).** Computational handle on Selberg zeta zeros.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Gives a bijection between Maass cusp forms for SL_2(Z) with eigenvalue s(1−s) and period functions satisfying the Lewis equation ψ(x) = ψ(x+1) + (x+1)^{−2s}ψ(x/(x+1)), equivalently eigenfunctions of Mayer's transfer operator.
