**Main result (as stated in index content).** Shows RH is equivalent to a Minkowski-dimension/inverse spectral statement for fractal strings, with a partial RH version.

**Techniques.** fractal strings; inverse spectral

**Research potential (curator).** Geometric reformulation.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** For D ∈ (0,1), the inverse spectral problem for fractal strings of dimension D (absence of an oscillatory term of order D in spectral asymptotics implies Minkowski measurability) holds iff ζ has no zeros on Re s = D. Hence RH ⇔ it holds for every D ≠ 1/2, and it fails at D = 1/2.
