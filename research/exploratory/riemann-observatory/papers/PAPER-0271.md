**Main result (as stated in index content).** Proves distribution results for zeros of linear combinations of L-functions with Euler products and functional equations, showing positive proportion on the line and many off it.

**Techniques.** Selberg class; zero distribution

**Research potential (curator).** Why Euler product matters for RH.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Under GRH and a weak zero-spacing hypothesis for the constituent L-functions, proves that 100% of the zeros of a linear combination of Euler products with a common functional equation lie on the critical line, using a joint Selberg CLT.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Let F = Σ c_j L_j be a linear combination of L-functions sharing a functional equation (so F generally violates RH). Assuming GRH for each L_j and a weak independence/pair-correlation hypothesis, almost all (100%) zeros of F lie on the critical line.
