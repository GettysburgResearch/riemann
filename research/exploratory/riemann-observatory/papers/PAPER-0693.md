**Main result (as stated in index content).** For a general positive sequence lambda_k -> infinity with regularity conditions, relates spectral zeta, determinant and special functions (Gamma/Barnes-type), applied to the Selberg zeta function of compact surfaces.

**Techniques.** spectral zeta regularization; functional determinants; Selberg trace formula

**Research potential (curator).** Tools for zeta-regularized products over zeta zeros (Voros later work).

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Develops zeta-regularized spectral functions and determinants for Laplacians on hyperbolic surfaces and expresses the Selberg zeta function as a regularized determinant with explicit special-function factors.
