**Research potential (curator).** Concrete no-go constraints on Hilbert-Polya-type operators.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Rigorously constructs self-adjoint pseudo-Laplacians (in the style of Colin de Verdière) on automorphic spaces whose discrete spectrum relates to zeros of L-functions. Proves limitations: Montgomery's pair correlation and the behaviour of ζ(1+it) imply that at most about 94% of the zeta zeros can appear in such a discrete spectrum.
