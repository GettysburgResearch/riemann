**Main result (as stated in index content).** For n>=4, sum over d in [D,2D] and characters of |sum_{m<=Z} chi(m)|^{2n} <= D^2 Z^n exp(log^s D) for D^{1/n}<=Z<=D^{1/(n-1)}; implies density hypothesis for zeros of L-series on average.

**Techniques.** Linnik method; zero density

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Independently proves a mean-value theorem for primes in progressions of Bombieri-Vinogradov strength (with slightly weaker Q) via density estimates for L-function zeros.
