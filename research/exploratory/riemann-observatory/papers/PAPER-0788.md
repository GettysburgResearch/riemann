**Main result (as stated in index content).** Time-band limiting operator commutes with the prolate second-order differential operator.

**Techniques.** prolate spheroidal functions

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Shows that the time- and band-limiting operator commutes with the second-order prolate differential operator d/dx (T² − x²) d/dx − Ω²x². Its eigenfunctions, the prolate spheroidal wave functions, diagonalize time–frequency concentration.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** The time-limiting then band-limiting operator on [-T/2,T/2] with bandwidth W commutes with the prolate differential operator d/dt((T^2/4 - t^2) d/dt) - c^2 t^2. Its eigenfunctions are the prolate spheroidal wave functions, with about 2WT eigenvalues close to 1 before a sharp plunge.
