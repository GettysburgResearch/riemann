**Techniques.** Fourier transform; entire functions of genus 0/1

**Research potential (curator).** Test kernel positivity families against Xi.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Gives criteria for Fourier integrals of the form integral Phi(u)cos(zu)du to have only real zeros, including the effect of multiplying the kernel by factors like e^{lambda u^2}. De Bruijn later turned this into the heat-flow deformation H_t.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Proves that a broad family of cosine transforms, including ∫_0^∞ exp(−t^{2n})cos(zt)dt for every n∈N, have only real zeros, introducing 'universal factor' techniques for kernels.
