**Main result (as stated in index content).** Proves the Riemann hypothesis for zeta functions of algebraic curves over finite fields via intersection theory on the surface C x C.

**Techniques.** intersection theory; Castelnuovo-Severi

**Research potential (curator).** Template for positivity/geometric approaches.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves RH for curves over F_q, |#C(F_{q^n}) − q^n − 1| ≤ 2g q^{n/2}. The proof uses positivity (a Castelnuovo–Severi type inequality) of the intersection form on C×C applied to graphs of Frobenius powers.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Complete proof of RH for curves over F_q using intersection theory on C×C: the Castelnuovo-Severi positivity of the trace form on correspondences gives |α_i|=√q. The companion volume 'Variétés abéliennes et courbes algébriques' (1948) gives a second proof via the Jacobian and l-adic Tate modules.
