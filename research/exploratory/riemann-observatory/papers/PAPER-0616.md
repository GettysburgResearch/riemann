**Main result (as stated in index content).** Computations of sign changes of sums of Liouville function lambda, with consequences for Polya problem and L(x).

**Techniques.** computation

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Disproves Turán's conjecture T(x)=Σ_{n≤x}λ(n)/n≥0 (first sign change near x≈7.2×10^{13}) and finds large positive values of L(x), via Ingham/Haselgrove-type analysis plus computation.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Finds the smallest x with T(x)=Σ_{n≤x}λ(n)/n<0: x=72,185,376,951,205, an explicit counterexample to Turán's hypothesis.
