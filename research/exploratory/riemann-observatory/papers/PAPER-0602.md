**Main result (as stated in index content).** For V~alpha loglog T, 0<alpha<2: measure{log|zeta|>V}/T << e^{-V^2/loglog T}/sqrt(loglog T), improving Soundararajan and Harper.

**Techniques.** Selberg CLT; large deviations

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves upper bounds for P(log|zeta(1/2+it)| > V) of Gaussian order, sharp up to constants, in the large-deviation range V proportional to log log T. Applications include moment upper bounds.
