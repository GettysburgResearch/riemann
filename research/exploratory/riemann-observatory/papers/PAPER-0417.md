**Main result (as stated in index content).** Zeros of Epstein/Hurwitz-type Dirichlet series (Davenport-Heilbronn); such series with functional equation have zeros in sigma>1 and infinitely many off the line (model recollection).

**Techniques.** Dirichlet series; functional equation; Hurwitz zeta

**Research potential (curator).** Use as test model for any criterion claiming FE+Euler-free implies RH.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Hurwitz zeta functions zeta(s,a) (a transcendental, or rational other than 1/2 and 1) and Epstein zeta functions of suitable forms (class number > 1) have infinitely many zeros in Re s > 1, despite having functional equations.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** (I) If Q is an integral positive definite binary quadratic form whose discriminant has class number h>1, then the Epstein zeta function Z_Q(s) has infinitely many zeros in σ>1. (II) The Hurwitz zeta function ζ(s,α) has infinitely many zeros in σ>1 when α is rational (α≠1/2,1) or transcendental. These are the source of the 'Davenport-Heilbronn counterexamples' to RH without an Euler product.
