**Research potential (curator).** Connect with Weil positivity and Connes' trace formula.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Proves Weil's Hermitian form is positive definite on test functions supported in a sufficiently small neighbourhood of 1 in R_+^*, and studies finite-dimensional truncations numerically.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Reduces RH to non-degeneracy (positivity) of a Hermitian form derived from Weil's explicit formula on spaces of test functions.
