**Main result (as stated in index content).** For every number field K, proves unconditionally that the unshifted Jensen polynomial J_{K,d}(X) is nonzero and has at least d - C_K sqrt(d log d) (abstract truncated) real roots as d->infinity; GRH for Dedekind zeta equivalent to real-rootedness of all Jensen polynomials.

**Techniques.** Jensen polynomials; Hermite asymptotics; Taylor coefficients of xi

**Research potential (curator).** Compare the sqrt(d log d) deficiency with Griffin-Ono-Rolen-Zagier Hermite limits and extend to other L-functions.
