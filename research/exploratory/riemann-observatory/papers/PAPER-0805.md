**Main result (as stated in index content).** Analogies between number theory and dynamical systems on spaces with a one-codimensional foliation, comparing analytic explicit formulas with Guillemin-type trace formulas; primes as closed orbits of length log N(p).

**Techniques.** foliated dynamics; leafwise cohomology

**Research potential (curator).** Cohomological framework requirements for RH.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proposes that arithmetic schemes correspond to foliated dynamical systems: R-actions on foliated spaces with leafwise cohomology, primes as closed orbits of length log N(p), and explicit formulas as Guillemin-type transversal trace formulas. RH would follow from a Hodge/Kähler-type positivity.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Surveys the conjectural dictionary between Spec Z and foliated dynamical systems, listing the required properties (leafwise cohomology, flow, transversal measure, positivity) for a dynamical proof of RH.
