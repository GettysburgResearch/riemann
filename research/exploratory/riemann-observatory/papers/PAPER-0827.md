**Main result (as stated in index content).** A Dirichlet series whose twists by primitive characters of suitable moduli satisfy the expected functional equations arises from a modular form on Gamma_0(N).

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Weil's converse theorem: a Dirichlet series whose twists by primitive characters of suitable moduli all satisfy the expected functional equations comes from a modular form on Γ_0(N). This made the Taniyama-Shimura conjecture precise, including the conductor.
