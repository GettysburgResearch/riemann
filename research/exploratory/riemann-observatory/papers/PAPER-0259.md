**Main result (as stated in index content).** Corrections to nearest-neighbour spacing distribution of Riemann zeros at finite height explained by arithmetic lower-order terms.

**Techniques.** RMT; pair correlation

**Research potential (curator).** Reproduce correction terms.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Shows that the leading finite-height correction to the zero spacing distribution matches CUE of effective dimension N_eff ~ log(T/2pi)/sqrt(12 Lambda_0) for an explicit arithmetic constant Lambda_0 ≈ 1.57, which explains Odlyzko's residual deviations.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Shows that finite-height corrections to the nearest-neighbour spacing of the Riemann zeros are reproduced by CUE matrices of effective size N_eff = log(E/2π)/√(12Λ), with Λ ≈ 1.573 an arithmetic constant (as I recall).
