**Techniques.** resonance; Dirichlet approximation

**Research potential (curator).** Compare with resonance method and extreme value conjectures.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** On RH, |ζ(1/2+it)| = Ω(exp(c √(log t/log log t))) (c = 1/20). Unconditionally, for fixed 1/2 < σ < 1, log|ζ(σ+it)| = Ω((log t)^{1-σ}(log log t)^{-σ}).

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Under RH, proves log|zeta(1/2+it)| >= (1/20) sqrt(log t/log log t) for arbitrarily large t, the corresponding Omega_{+-} results for S(t), and log|zeta(sigma+it)| = Omega_+((log t)^{1-sigma}/(log log t)^sigma) for 1/2 < sigma < 1 (with weaker unconditional forms). Conjectures these orders are sharp.
