**Main result (as stated in index content).** Epstein zeta function of a binary form expressed via zeta(2s), zeta(2s-1) and a rapidly convergent K-Bessel series; consequences include real zeros of Z_Q in (1/2,1) for suitable forms.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves the Chowla-Selberg formula: the Epstein zeta function of a binary form is expressed through ζ(2s), ζ(2s−1) and a rapidly convergent series of K_{s−1/2}-Bessel functions. Consequences include real zeros of Z_Q in (1/2,1) for suitable forms and the Γ-value formula for CM periods. A 1949 PNAS announcement preceded it.
