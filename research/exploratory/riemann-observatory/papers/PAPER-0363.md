**Techniques.** Kronecker

**Research potential (curator).** Probabilistic approach.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** For fixed sigma > 1/2, log zeta(sigma+it) has a limiting distribution in C (with continuous density) as t varies.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves that for each fixed sigma > 1/2, log zeta(sigma+it) (t uniform in [0,T]) has a limiting distribution as T -> infinity, given by a random Euler product model.
