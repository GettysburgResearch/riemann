**Main result (as stated in index content).** For compact Kahler X and endomorphism f with f^*omega=q omega, eigenvalues of f^* on H^i have absolute value q^{i/2}, proved via Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Let X be compact Kähler and f an endomorphism with f^*ω=qω for a Kähler class ω. Then the eigenvalues of f^* on H^i(X) have absolute value q^{i/2}; the proof uses the Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations (positivity).
