*Basis:* both. This summary is derived from index content (arXiv abstract and/or zbMATH review) or, where marked, the curator's unverified recollection. It is not an audit.

**Main result (as stated in index content).** Generalizes theorems on algebraic equations to transcendental ones, giving criteria for entire functions to have only real zeros; with Polya (1927), RH iff all Jensen polynomials of Xi are hyperbolic.

**Techniques.** Jensen polynomials; Laguerre-Polya class

**Research potential (curator).** Basis for Griffin-Ono-Rolen-Zagier results.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Develops criteria for entire functions to have only real zeros; with his unpublished results reported by Pólya (1927), this gives RH iff every Jensen polynomial attached to the Taylor coefficients of Ξ is hyperbolic.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Introduces the polynomials now called Jensen polynomials and real-zero criteria for real entire functions of genus ≤1, including integral criteria for Xi to have only real zeros.
