**Techniques.** Nyman-Beurling; Dirichlet polynomials

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Shows that, under RH and a mild bound on Σ1/|ζ'(ρ)|², the smoothed Möbius polynomials Σ_{n≤N}μ(n)(1−log n/log N)n^{−s} achieve d_N² ~ (2+γ−log 4π)/log N, matching the conjectured optimal (BBLS) constant.
