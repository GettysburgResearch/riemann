**Main result (as stated in index content).** Elementary proof of Robin's inequality for classes of n (e.g. odd or n not divisible by many primes); restates Robin criterion for n>=5041.

**Techniques.** Robin inequality

**Research potential (curator).** Robin programme.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Proves any n≥37 violating Robin's inequality must be even and neither squarefree nor squarefull, and (via Rosser–Schoenfeld) must be divisible by a fifth power >1; so RH holds iff every n>5040 divisible by a fifth power satisfies Robin's inequality.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Proves Robin's inequality for all odd n>9 and all squarefree n>30, and for n not divisible by certain prime powers. Any counterexample would have to be even and highly divisible.
