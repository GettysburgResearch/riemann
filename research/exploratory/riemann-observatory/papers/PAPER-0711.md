**Main result (as stated in index content).** For T^{1/2}<T_0<=T and well-spaced t_r, sum_r int_{t_r}^{t_r+T_0} |zeta(1/2+it)|^4 dt << (R T_0 + R^{1/2} T_0^{-1/2} T) T^eps, via Kuznetsov's formula.

**Techniques.** Kuznetsov formula; moments

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Uses Kuznetsov's spectral methods to prove ∫_T^{T+T^{2/3}} |ζ(1/2+it)|^4 dt ≪ T^{2/3+ε}, which recovers the twelfth-moment bound T^{2+ε}.
