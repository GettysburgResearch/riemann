**Main result (as stated in index content).** Assuming RH, linear statistics of zeros of L-functions converge weakly to Gaussian field with H^{1/2} covariance.

**Techniques.** Szego asymptotics; CLT

**Research potential (curator).** Strong Szego analogue.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** For test functions with suitably restricted Fourier support, mesoscopic linear statistics of zeta zeros near a random height are asymptotically Gaussian, with the H^{1/2}-type variance that the strong Szego theorem predicts for CUE.
