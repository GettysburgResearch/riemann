**Main result (as stated in index content).** Attaches l-adic Galois representations to weight k>=2 Hecke eigenforms via Kuga-Sato varieties, reducing |a_p|<=2p^{(k-1)/2} to the Weil conjectures.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Attaches l-adic Galois representations to weight-k ≥ 2 Hecke eigenforms via the cohomology of Kuga-Sato varieties. This reduces the Ramanujan-Petersson bound |a_p| ≤ 2p^{(k−1)/2} to the Weil conjectures.
