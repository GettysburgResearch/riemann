**Main result (as stated in index content).** Every even Schwartz function f on R is determined by f(sqrt n) and its Fourier transform at sqrt n, n>=0, via an explicit interpolation basis from weakly holomorphic modular forms.

**Techniques.** Fourier interpolation; modular forms

**Research potential (curator).** Analogues with zeta-zero nodes.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Every even Schwartz function f on R is determined by f(√n) and \hat f(√n), n≥0, through an explicit interpolation basis built from weakly holomorphic modular forms.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Every even Schwartz f on R satisfies f(x) = sum_{n>=0} a_n(x) f(sqrt n) + sum_{n>=0} a-hat_n(x) f-hat(sqrt n), with explicit a_n built from weakly holomorphic modular forms for the theta group. So f and f-hat vanishing at every sqrt(n) forces f = 0.
