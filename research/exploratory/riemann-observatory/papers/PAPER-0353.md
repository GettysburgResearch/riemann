**Main result (as stated in index content).** Trace formula and Selberg zeta with RH analogue.

**Techniques.** trace formula

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves the Selberg trace formula on compact Γ\H. It equates Σ_j h(r_j) over Laplace eigenvalues λ_j = 1/4 + r_j² with an area term plus Σ_γ log N(γ_0) g(log Nγ)/(N(γ)^{1/2} − N(γ)^{−1/2}) over closed geodesics. The Selberg zeta Z(s) built from it has nontrivial zeros s_j with s_j(1 − s_j) = λ_j, so it satisfies RH apart from finitely many real exceptional zeros (from eigenvalues below 1/4).

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Introduces the Selberg trace formula and the Selberg zeta function Z(s)=∏_γ∏_{k≥0}(1−N(γ)^{−s−k}) for compact Γ\H. Its nontrivial zeros are s_j with s_j(1−s_j)=λ_j, so all lie on Re s=1/2 except finitely many real zeros from eigenvalues λ<1/4. Also gives the prime geodesic theorem.
