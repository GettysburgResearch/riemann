**Main result (as stated in index content).** Study of the distribution of zeros of zeta^(k) near the critical line, showing most lie close to Re s=1/2 (Levinson-Montgomery).

**Techniques.** Speiser criterion; derivative zeros

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Makes Speiser's theorem quantitative: ζ and ζ' have the same number of zeros with 0<σ<1/2, 0<t<T up to O(log T); under RH, Σ_{0<γ'≤T}(β'−1/2) ~ (T/2π) log log T over zeros β'+iγ' of ζ'.
