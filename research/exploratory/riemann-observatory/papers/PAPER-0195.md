**Main result (as stated in index content).** Arithmetic expression and asymptotics for Li coefficients; RH implies lambda_n ~ (n/2)(log n + c); generalization to other L-functions.

**Techniques.** Li coefficients; saddle point

**Research potential (curator).** Quantitative RH criterion.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Proves a Li-type criterion for arbitrary multisets of complex numbers with Σ(1+|Re ρ|)/(1+|ρ|²)<∞, and uses Weil's explicit formula to give an arithmetic expression λ_n=S_∞(n)+S_f(n) for the Li coefficients.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Generalises Li's criterion to multisets of complex numbers and gives an arithmetic (explicit-formula) expression for lambda_n. This clarifies that positivity of lambda_n is a reformulation, not a simplification, of RH.
