**Main result (as stated in index content).** Local RH: zeros of the Mellin transform of a harmonic-oscillator eigenfunction have real part 1/2; analysis in connection with Berry-Keating Hamiltonian.

**Techniques.** Mellin transform; Berry-Keating

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Constructs self-adjoint (Weyl-ordered, suitably weighted) versions of xp whose eigenvalue conditions reproduce zeros of the Mellin transforms in the Bump–Choi–Kurlberg–Vaaler local RH. This gives a Hilbert–Pólya proof of local RH.
