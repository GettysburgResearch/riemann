**Main result (as stated in index content).** For Steinhaus/Rademacher f and 0<=q<=1, E|sum_{n<=x} f(n)|^{2q} is of order (x/(1+(1-q)sqrt(loglog x)))^q; in particular E|sum f(n)| is of order sqrt(x)/(loglog x)^{1/4}.

**Techniques.** multiplicative chaos; random multiplicative functions

**Research potential (curator).** Heuristic analogy for Mertens-type sums.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** For Steinhaus/Rademacher f and 0≤q≤1, E|Σ_{n≤x}f(n)|^{2q} ≍ (x/(1+(1−q)√(log log x)))^q; in particular E|Σ_{n≤x}f(n)| ≍ √x/(log log x)^{1/4}, via critical multiplicative chaos (proving Helson's conjecture).

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** For Steinhaus random multiplicative f, E|sum_{n<=x} f(n)| is of exact order sqrt(x)/(log log x)^{1/4}, and E|sum|^{2q} is of exact order (x/(1 + (1-q) sqrt(log log x)))^q for 0 <= q <= 1. The proof goes through critical multiplicative chaos.
