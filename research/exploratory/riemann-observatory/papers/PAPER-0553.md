**Main result (as stated in index content).** Constructs GMC M_gamma for log-correlated Gaussian fields; proves uniqueness, nondegeneracy iff gamma^2<2d, and the Kahane convexity inequality.

**Techniques.** multiplicative chaos; log-correlated fields

**Research potential (curator).** Model log|zeta(1/2+it)| as log-correlated field.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Constructs Gaussian multiplicative chaos M_gamma(dx) = e^{gamma X(x) - gamma^2 E[X(x)^2]/2} dx for log-correlated Gaussian fields X (sigma-positive kernels). It proves uniqueness, shows non-degeneracy exactly when gamma^2 < 2d, and proves the Kahane convexity inequality.
