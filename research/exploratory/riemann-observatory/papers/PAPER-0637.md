**Techniques.** Newton polygon; power sums

**Research potential (curator).** Instructive proved RH analogue.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves that, for each fixed y∈Z_p, the zeros of the Goss zeta function of F_q[T] are simple and lie in F_q((1/T)) (the analogue of RH), via a Newton-polygon analysis. This completes earlier work of Wan and Diaz-Vargas.
