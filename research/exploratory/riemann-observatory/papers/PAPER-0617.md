**Main result (as stated in index content).** |zeta(s)| and two related functions strictly decrease as real part increases for sufficiently large imaginary part regions (precise hypotheses in paper).

**Techniques.** monotonicity

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Proves unconditionally that |ζ(σ+it)| is strictly decreasing in σ for σ<0 when |t| is large enough (|t|≥8), and relates horizontal monotonicity inside the strip to RH, with analogues for L-functions.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Proves horizontal monotonicity (in σ) of |ζ(σ+it)| in left regions of the plane (precise ranges in paper), and relates RH to monotonicity of |ξ| for ζ and L-functions.
