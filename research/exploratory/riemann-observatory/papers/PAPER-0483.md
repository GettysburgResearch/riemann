**Main result (as stated in index content).** Sharp-order bounds for integral of |zeta(1/2+it+ih)|^2 over short windows and upper bound for local maxima of zeta on typical short intervals.

**Techniques.** multiplicative chaos; branching random walk

**Research potential (curator).** Method transplantable to other L-functions.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves unconditionally sharp-order upper bounds for the critical-line partition function integral_{|h|<=1} |zeta(1/2+it+ih)|^{2beta} dh for random t, and the upper bound max_{|h|<=1} log|zeta| <= log log T - (3/4) log log log T + O(log log log log T) for most t.
