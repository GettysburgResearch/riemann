**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Under RH, relates the mean square of zeta'/zeta near the critical line to the pair correlation function, and shows that the pair correlation conjecture implies asymptotics for the variance of primes in short intervals.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Assuming a uniform Hardy-Littlewood k-tuple conjecture, the moments of psi(x+h)-psi(x)-h over x<=N are Gaussian with variance about h log(N/h), not h log N as the Cramér model predicts.
