**Main result (as stated in index content).** Formulates a connection between certain Hilbert spaces of entire functions and RH for various L-functions, following de Branges.

**Techniques.** de Branges spaces; entire functions

**Research potential (curator).** Framework for de Branges approach and its limits.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Studies de Branges spaces H(E) with structure functions built from (shifted) completed Dirichlet L-functions, e.g. E(z) = ξ_χ(1/2 + h − iz). Relates the Hermite–Biehler property to RH and interprets the zeros via self-adjoint extensions of multiplication operators in H(E).
