**Main result (as stated in index content).** Deligne equidistribution applied to families of curves over finite fields: Frobenius eigenvalues of geometric monodromy equidistribute in compact classical groups (unitary, symplectic, orthogonal), giving the function-field analogue of GUE statistics.

**Techniques.** monodromy; equidistribution; Deligne

**Research potential (curator).** Template for the function-field model of zero statistics and RMT universality.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves for function-field zeta functions that, as q -> infinity, spacing statistics follow GUE and that low-lying zeros of families follow symmetry types (unitary, symplectic, orthogonal) set by monodromy. This motivates the Katz-Sarnak density conjectures over number fields.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** For families of curves over F_q, proves via Deligne equidistribution that as q → ∞ the normalized spacings of Frobenius eigenangles, and low-lying zeros of family zeta functions, follow Haar-measure statistics of the monodromy group (U, USp or O).
