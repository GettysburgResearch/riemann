**Techniques.** mollifiers; Landau-Siegel zeros; nonvanishing

**Research potential (curator).** Connections between central-value nonvanishing and zero-free regions.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves that at least 50% of central values L(1/2,f) in GL(2) families are nonvanishing (essentially of expected size). Shows that any improvement past the 1/2 barrier with suitable lower bounds would exclude Landau-Siegel zeros.
