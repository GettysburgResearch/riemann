**Main result (as stated in index content).** Studies zeta functions of local fields via Schwartz functions and proves a local Riemann hypothesis for zeros in a harmonic-oscillator setting.

**Techniques.** local zeta; Schwartz functions

**Research potential (curator).** Local model of RH.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves that certain local archimedean zeta integrals (Mellin transforms of Laguerre/Hermite-type eigenfunctions of the harmonic oscillator) have all their zeros on Re s = 1/2: a proved 'local RH', with an operator-theoretic interpretation.
