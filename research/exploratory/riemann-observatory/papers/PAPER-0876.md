**Main result (as stated in index content).** With xi(1/2+z)=sum gamma(n) z^{2n}/n!, J^{d,n} hyperbolic for all d,n iff RH; proves absolute K>0 with n^3 log^2(n+2) >= K d^5 implies J^{d,n} hyperbolic, and zero measure converges to the semicircle law along n,d->infinity in this region.

**Techniques.** Jensen polynomials; Hermite approximation; semicircle law

**Research potential (curator).** Test how far the wedge boundary can be pushed toward d ~ n^{3/5}; compare with exponential-threshold results.

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** Claims: there is an absolute K>0 such that n^3 log^2(n+2) ≥ K d^5 implies J^{d,n} of Xi is hyperbolic (polynomial rather than exponential threshold in d), with semicircle-law zero statistics as n,d→∞ in this wedge (unrefereed).
