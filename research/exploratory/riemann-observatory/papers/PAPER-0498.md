**Main result (as stated in index content).** For Minkowski-measurable fractal strings of dimension D in (0,1), N(lambda)=pi^{-1}|L|lambda^{1/2} - c_D M lambda^{D/2}+o(lambda^{D/2}); this refines Berry's conjecture and is linked to zeta zeros on Re s=D, giving an RH criterion for D=1/2.

**Techniques.** fractal strings; Minkowski content; renewal theory

**Research potential (curator).** Geometric/spectral equivalent of RH amenable to string constructions.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** For Minkowski-measurable fractal strings of dimension D ∈ (0,1), proves N(λ) = π^{−1}|L|λ^{1/2} − c_D ℳ λ^{D/2} + o(λ^{D/2}), where ℳ is the Minkowski content and c_D is an explicit positive constant proportional to −ζ(D).
