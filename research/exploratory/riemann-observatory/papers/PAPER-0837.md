**Main result (as stated in index content).** Explicit (p+1)-regular Cayley graphs X^{p,q} of PSL_2(F_q)/PGL_2(F_q) with nontrivial eigenvalues |lambda|<=2 sqrt p, using Deligne's Ramanujan bound.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Constructs explicit (p+1)-regular Cayley graphs X^{p,q} of PSL_2(F_q) or PGL_2(F_q) (p,q distinct primes ≡ 1 mod 4) whose nontrivial eigenvalues satisfy |λ| ≤ 2√p. The proof uses Deligne's Ramanujan bound for quaternionic forms; the property is equivalent to RH for their Ihara zeta functions.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Explicit (p+1)-regular Cayley graphs from quaternion algebras whose nontrivial eigenvalues satisfy |lambda| <= 2 sqrt(p), so their Ihara zetas satisfy RH. The proof uses the Ramanujan-Petersson conjecture (Deligne, Eichler).
