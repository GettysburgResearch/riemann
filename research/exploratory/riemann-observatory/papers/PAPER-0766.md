**Main result (as stated in index content).** New simple proof that log|zeta(1/2+it)| is approximately normal with mean 0 and variance (1/2)loglog t.

**Techniques.** Dirichlet polynomial approximation; moments

**Research potential (curator).** Reusable for L-function CLTs.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Gives a short proof that log|zeta(1/2+it)| / sqrt((1/2) log log T), for t uniform in [T,2T], converges to a standard Gaussian, by comparing to a short Dirichlet polynomial away from zeros.
