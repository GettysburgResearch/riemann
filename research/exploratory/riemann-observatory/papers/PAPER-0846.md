**Main result (as stated in index content).** A Dirichlet series with periodic coefficients and no zeros in Re s>1 equals P(s)L(s,chi) with P a Dirichlet polynomial and chi a Dirichlet character.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves that a Dirichlet series F(s)=Σa(n)n^{−s} with periodic coefficients and no zeros in Re s > 1 must be of the form P(s)L(s,χ), with P a Dirichlet polynomial and χ a Dirichlet character.
