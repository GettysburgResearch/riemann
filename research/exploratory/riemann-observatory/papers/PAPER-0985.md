**Main result (as stated in index content).** Under RH, connects the distribution of k-free numbers (M_k(x)) with zeta'(rho) at nontrivial zeros; with extra assumptions proves a limiting distribution of e^{-y/2k}M_k(e^y) and studies its tail.

**Techniques.** explicit formula; limiting distributions; zeta' at zeros

**Research potential (curator).** Analogue of Ng's Mertens limiting distribution; test tail bounds against zeta' moment conjectures.
