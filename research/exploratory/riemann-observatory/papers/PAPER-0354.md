**Main result (as stated in index content).** Weil's explicit formula is rewritten as sum_i (-1)^{i+1} Tr_i(Phi,chi) = sum_p Tr_p(Phi,chi), i in {0,+-1}, p over all primes of a number field, suggesting a cohomological (Lefschetz) interpretation.

**Techniques.** explicit formula; Lefschetz trace formula; cohomological formalism

**Research potential (curator).** Framework for Deninger's conjectural dynamical/cohomological approach to RH.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Shows that Weil's explicit formula has exactly the shape of a Lefschetz trace formula for a flow on a hypothetical cohomology H^i, with closed orbits of length log N(p) corresponding to primes.
