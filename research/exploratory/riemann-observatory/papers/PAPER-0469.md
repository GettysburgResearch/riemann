**Main result (as stated in index content).** Meromorphic continuation of zeta, functional equation for xi, N(T) ~ (T/2pi)log(T/2pi)-T/2pi, product formula for xi, explicit formula for J(x), and the statement that it is very probable all roots are real (seed note).

**Techniques.** explicit formula; functional equation

**Research potential (curator).** Primary source for exact statements of Riemann's claims.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Continues zeta(s) meromorphically to C, proves xi(s)=xi(1-s) (two proofs), asserts N(T) ~ (T/2pi)log(T/2pi) - T/2pi, a product formula for xi, and the explicit formula J(x) = li(x) - sum_rho li(x^rho) - log 2 + int_x^infty dt/(t(t^2-1)log t); states that all zeros are 'sehr wahrscheinlich' on Re s = 1/2.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Introduces xi(t), states without proof that the number of roots of xi(t)=0 with real part between 0 and T is about (T/2pi)log(T/2pi) - T/2pi, and that it is 'very probable' that all roots are real (RH).
