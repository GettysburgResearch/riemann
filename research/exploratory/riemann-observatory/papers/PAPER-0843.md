**Main result (as stated in index content).** Defines varieties over the field with one element via functors on rings plus complex-analytic data; defines zeta functions of F_1-varieties by counting functions N(q).

**Research potential (curator).** Assess which F_1 constructions reproduce the Riemann zeta completed factor.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Gives the first definition of varieties over F_1, via functors on rings together with complex-analytic data. Defines zeta functions of F_1-varieties by counting functions N(q) (e.g. ζ_{P^n/F_1}).
