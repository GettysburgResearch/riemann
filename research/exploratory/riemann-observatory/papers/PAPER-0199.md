**Main result (as stated in index content).** Disproof of |M(x)|<sqrt(x) using LLL lattice reduction on zeta zeros; limsup M(x)/sqrt(x) > 1.06.

**Techniques.** LLL; simultaneous Diophantine approximation

**Research potential (curator).** Instructive failure of RH-stronger conjecture.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Using the first 2000 zeros to high precision and LLL lattice reduction, proves limsup M(x)/√x>1.06 and liminf M(x)/√x<−1.009, disproving |M(x)|<√x.

**Expert seed note (['G']; unverified recollection).** Using the first 2000 zeros (computed to high accuracy) and LLL lattice reduction for simultaneous Diophantine approximation, proves lim inf M(x)/sqrt(x)<-1.009 and lim sup M(x)/sqrt(x)>1.06, disproving the Mertens conjecture non-constructively.
