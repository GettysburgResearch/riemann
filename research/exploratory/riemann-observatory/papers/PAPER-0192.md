**Main result (as stated in index content).** Structure of the Selberg class in degree 0<=d<=1: only Dirichlet L-functions (twists) and the constant function.

**Techniques.** Selberg class; converse theorems

**Research potential (curator).** Axiomatic classification of L-functions.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Complete classification of the extended class S^# in degrees 0 ≤ d ≤ 1. Degree 0 consists of Dirichlet polynomials and degree 1 of linear combinations of shifted Dirichlet L-functions. In S itself, the degree-1 elements are exactly ζ(s) and L(s+iθ,χ) for primitive χ.
