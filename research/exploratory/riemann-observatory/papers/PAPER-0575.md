**Main result (as stated in index content).** Integrals of E(z,s) against non-negative measures give meromorphic functions with all zeros on Re s=1/2 (with stated exceptions for constant term).

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves that for a ≥ 1 all zeros of a^s ξ(2s) + a^{1−s} ξ(2−2s) (the constant term of the truncated SL_2(Z) Eisenstein series) lie on Re s = 1/2, apart from two real zeros for large a. The threshold is a > 4πe^{−γ} as I recall.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Shows that the constant-term combination ξ(2s)y^s + ξ(2s−1)y^{1−s} of the SL_2(Z) Eisenstein series has, for y ≥ 1, all its zeros on Re s = 1/2, except for two real zeros once y exceeds a threshold (recalled as 4πe^{−γ} ≈ 7.0555).
