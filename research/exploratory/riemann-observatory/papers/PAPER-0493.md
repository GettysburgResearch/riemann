**Main result (as stated in index content).** Strategy toward RH using self-adjoint rank-one perturbations of a spectral triple, realizing zeta zeros spectrally.

**Techniques.** spectral triples; prolate operator

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Sets out a strategy toward RH centred on Weil's quadratic form and the prolate wave operator, building spectral triples whose spectra are tied to the zeta zeros (precise theorems to verify).

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Constructs self-adjoint rank-one perturbations of the scaling operator on [λ^{−1}, λ] with periodic boundary conditions and the associated spectral triples. Numerics show their spectra converging to the zeros of ζ(1/2 + is) as N, λ → ∞; a rigorous proof of convergence would establish RH.
