**Research potential (curator).** Source of the technique behind modern exponent-pair improvements.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** Proves the sharp l^2 decoupling inequality for compact hypersurfaces with nonvanishing Gaussian curvature.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** For a compact C^2 hypersurface in R^n with positive definite second fundamental form, and its delta-neighbourhood split into delta^{1/2}-caps theta, ||f||_{L^p} <<_eps delta^{-eps} (sum_theta ||f_theta||_p^2)^{1/2} for 2 <= p <= 2(n+1)/(n-1). This is the sharp l^2 decoupling inequality.
