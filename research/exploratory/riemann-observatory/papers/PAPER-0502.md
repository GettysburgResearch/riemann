*Basis:* both. This summary is derived from index content (arXiv abstract and/or zbMATH review) or, where marked, the curator's unverified recollection. It is not an audit.

**Main result (as stated in index content).** Proposes H=(1-e^{-ip})^{-1}(xp+px)(1-e^{-ip}) with Hurwitz zeta eigenfunctions; with boundary condition psi(0)=0 eigenvalues correspond to nontrivial zeros (claimed self-adjoint-like under suitable conditions).

**Techniques.** Hurwitz zeta; PT-symmetry

**Research potential (curator).** Case study in domain/self-adjointness pitfalls.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proposes Ĥ = (1 − e^{−ip̂})^{−1}(x̂p̂ + p̂x̂)(1 − e^{−ip̂}), whose eigenfunctions are Hurwitz zeta functions −ζ(z, x+1). With boundary condition ψ(0) = 0 the eigenvalues E (z = ½(1 − iE)) are the nontrivial zeros. Reality of E is argued heuristically via PT/pseudo-Hermiticity and is not proved.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Replies that non-self-adjointness was implicit in the original paper and that L²(Lebesgue) square-integrability is not the relevant criterion for pseudo-Hermitian operators. Reality of the spectrum remains unproven.
