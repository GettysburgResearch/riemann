**Main result (as stated in index content).** Effective Brauer-Siegel bounds; for K normal over Q (or in a tower of normal extensions) a possible real zero of zeta_K very near 1 must be a zero of a quadratic subfield's L-function.

**Research potential (curator).** Exceptional-zero exclusion via subfield structure.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves effective Brauer-Siegel bounds. Shows that a possible exceptional real zero of ζ_K for K normal over Q (or reached by a tower of normal extensions) close to 1, roughly within 1/(4 log|d_K|) as recalled, must be a zero of ζ_k for a quadratic subfield k (Stark's lemma). This gives effective class number bounds for fields without quadratic subfields.
