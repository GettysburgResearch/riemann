**Main result (as stated in index content).** For any a, almost all roots of zeta(s)=a lie within epsilon of Re s=1/2.

**Techniques.** a-points; Jensen formula

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves that for every complex a, all but O(N(T)/log log T) of the a-points of zeta with 0 < t < T lie within any fixed distance of the critical line.
