**Main result (as stated in index content).** xi is strictly monotone in modulus along horizontal half-lines in zero-free half-planes; corollary is an RH reformulation.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Proves RH holds iff, for every fixed t, |ξ(σ+it)| is strictly increasing in σ on σ>1/2 (equivalently strictly decreasing on σ<1/2).

**Expert seed note (['F']; unverified recollection).** |ξ(σ+it)| is strictly increasing in σ along horizontal half-lines in any zero-free half-plane to the right of 1/2; hence RH ⇔ |ξ(σ+it)| increasing in σ>1/2 for every t (⇔ decreasing for σ<1/2).
