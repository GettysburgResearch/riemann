**Research potential (curator).** Audit certified-arithmetic coverage; finite verification not global evidence.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Computationally verifies that every quadratic Dirichlet L-function of modulus q ≤ 10^10 satisfies L(σ,χ) ≠ 0 for real σ ≥ 1 − 1/(5 log q), and gives L(1,χ) ≥ 1/(8 log q) in that range. The method assumes an exceptional zero, derives an explicit inequality for L'/L at σ > 1, and checks numerically that it fails.
