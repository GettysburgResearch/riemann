## Content (index-level record — the paper itself was NOT read)

*Basis:* zbmath_review. This summary is derived from index content (arXiv abstract and/or zbMATH review) or, where marked, the curator's unverified recollection. It is not an audit.

**Main result (as stated in index content).** For a Dirichlet series absolutely convergent in a half-plane, a value-distribution theorem on vertical strips, used to show that for every delta>0 all but O(T) zeros with 0<gamma<T lie in |beta-1/2|<delta (Bohr-Landau, per seed notes).

**Techniques.** almost periodic functions; Dirichlet series value distribution

**Research potential (curator).** Origin of zero-density-near-line arguments and Bohr-Jessen/Voronin lineage.

**Expert seed note (['A']; unverified recollection).** For each fixed delta>0, all but O(T) of the zeros with 0<gamma<T satisfy |beta - 1/2| < delta; since N(T) ~ (T/2pi)log T, almost all zeros cluster at the critical line.

**Expert seed note (['B']; unverified recollection).** Proves that for every epsilon>0 the number of zeros with |beta-1/2| > epsilon and 0<gamma<T is O_epsilon(T) = o(N(T)), so almost all zeros lie arbitrarily close to the critical line.
