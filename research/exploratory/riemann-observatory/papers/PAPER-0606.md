**Main result (as stated in index content).** Slightly improves the Baez-Duarte/Balazard/Landreau/Saias lower bound for the Nyman-Beurling distance d_N, tied to the sum over zeros under RH.

**Techniques.** Nyman-Beurling; Hilbert space approximation

**Research potential (curator).** Sharpen rate bounds in Nyman-Beurling formulations.

**Expert seed note (['C']; unverified recollection).** Improves the BBLS lower bound to liminf d_N² log N ≥ Σ_{Re ρ=1/2} m(ρ)²/|ρ|² (m the multiplicity), using Hilbert spaces of entire functions attached to the zeros.

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** In the Nyman–Beurling–Báez-Duarte criterion, proves lim inf_{N→∞} d_N² log N ≥ Σ_ρ m_ρ²/|ρ|². This matches the conjectured constant 2 + γ − log 4π when all zeros are simple.
