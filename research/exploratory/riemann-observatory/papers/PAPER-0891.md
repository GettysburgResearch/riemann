**Main result (as stated in index content).** If f on R^n has f(0)=f^(0)>0, f(x)<=0 for |x|>=r and f^>=0, then sphere-packing density is at most vol(B^n_{r/2}); numerically nearly sharp in dimensions 8 and 24.

**Techniques.** Fourier positivity; linear programming bounds

**Research potential (curator).** Adapt the LP-with-Fourier-sign-constraints scheme to Weil explicit formula test functions.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** If f on R^n satisfies f(0) = f-hat(0) > 0, f(x) <= 0 for |x| >= r, and f-hat >= 0, then sphere-packing density is at most vol(B^n_{r/2}). The bound is numerically almost sharp in dimensions 8 and 24.
