**Main result (as stated in index content).** Scattering matrix phi(s)=xi(2s-1)/xi(2s); resonances at s=rho/2, RH equivalent to spectral statement.

**Techniques.** scattering; resonances

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Treats the automorphic wave equation on SL_2(Z)\H as a Lax–Phillips scattering system. The scattering matrix is φ(s) = ξ(2s−1)/ξ(2s), so resonances (spectrum of the semigroup generator) sit at s = ρ/2, and RH is equivalent to them lying on Re s = 1/4.
