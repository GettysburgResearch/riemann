**Main result (as stated in index content).** Scaling limits of CUE characteristic polynomial near its maximum; conjecture for zeta in short intervals.

**Techniques.** CUE; extreme values

**Expert seed note (['D']; unverified recollection).** Proves that suitably normalized CUE characteristic polynomials converge (uniformly on compacts, in law) to a random entire function ξ_∞ whose zeros form the sine-kernel process. Conjectures that ζ at random heights, microscopically rescaled, converges to the same limit, and derives ratio formulas.

**Expert seed note (['H']; unverified recollection).** Normalized CUE characteristic polynomial ratios Z_N(e^{2 pi i z/N})/Z_N(1) converge to a random entire function xi_infinity whose zeros form the Sine_2 process. The paper conjectures the analogous convergence for zeta(1/2 + i omega T + 2 pi i z/log T)/zeta(1/2 + i omega T) and shows consistency with the ratios conjecture.
