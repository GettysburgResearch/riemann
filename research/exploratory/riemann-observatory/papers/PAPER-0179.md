**Techniques.** l-adic cohomology; monodromy; Kloosterman sums

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Computes the geometric monodromy groups of Kloosterman sheaves Kl_n: SL_n for n odd and Sp_n for n even (p odd). Hence the angles of Kl(a;p) = 2√p cos θ_{p,a} become Sato-Tate equidistributed as a ranges over F_p^× and p→∞.
