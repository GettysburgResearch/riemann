**Main result (as stated in index content).** Automorphy of Sym^n for all n>=1 for non-CM holomorphic newforms of weight >=2, hence every L(s,Sym^n f) is entire and Sato-Tate holds.

**Expert seed note (['E']; unverified recollection).** Proves automorphy of Sym^n for all n ≥ 1 for non-CM holomorphic newforms of weight ≥ 2. Hence every L(s,Sym^n f) is entire, and Sato-Tate holds with the full strength of automorphy.
