## Failed attempts and negative results

- The report file itself was not produced. The harness refused writing REPORT.md ('Subagents should return findings as text'). I did not circumvent this, so all findings are in this structured output and the machine-readable results directory.
- No DH zero with sigma>=1 was found for t<=4000 on sigma=1 and 1.05 (min |f| was 0.184 and 0.245). This is explained heuristically by the need to align about 20 prime phases. No Kronecker/LLL-targeted search was attempted.
- The first Epstein scan Newton-polished every grid minimum with no threshold. It ran over 20 CPU-minutes, past the 15-minute budget, and was killed. It was rerun with |Z|<0.25 and with the sigma=0.52 edge row excluded; the winding count of 35.0 confirms nothing was missed in the box.
- Screening Epstein for zeros near sigma=1, on sigma=1 itself, found certified zeros with Re slightly BELOW 1: 0.99136+1652178.73i, 0.99900+2540562.30i and 0.99854+2679982.15i. These came from an interactive run whose certificates were printed but not archived, so treat them as unreproduced. Moving the screen to sigma=1.01 and 1.03 found zeros with sigma>1.
- mpmath.polyroots was too slow for Jensen polynomials (about 3 s per degree-30 polynomial; about 7000 needed). It was replaced by FLINT's rigorous complex_roots on rounded integer polynomials.
- The first Jensen runs were silently truncated by acb_series' default series length (flint.ctx.cap=10), which surfaced as an IndexError. They were discarded and rerun with ctx.cap=K.
- Rounding Jensen coefficients to 60 digits produced SPURIOUS non-hyperbolicity: 22 'non-real' roots at d=100 for quadruple 0.6+5i. These vanished at 400-digit rounding, and the xi control is hyperbolic to d=240. Negative knowledge: low-precision rounding of high-degree Jensen polynomials can fabricate non-real roots.
- epstein_zeros.py set flint.ctx.prec=128 on import, which silently lowered precision inside synthetic_xi's Epstein Taylor routine. It was caught (coefficient radii about 1e-37) and fixed by setting precision after the import.
- The Beurling toy's first exact-zero evaluation returned NaN for an inexact beta=0.6 (E1 near 0). This was fixed by evaluating the entire form e^{gamma}w e^{-Ein(w)} with a rigorous Ein series.
- Several sources were inaccessible behind bot-checks: Springer (DMV 2006), AMS (Spira 1994; Balanzario-Sanchez-Ortiz 2007), ICM/matwbn (Bateman-Grosswald 1964) and Deep Blue (the DMV abstract). DMV is stated from two independent secondary sources, and Bateman-Grosswald's threshold is not quoted.
- Rejected framing: the brief describes DMV as 'RH failing but PNT error x^{1/2}-ish'. DMV's x^theta regularity (theta>1/2) is on the integers while their primes oscillate at de la Vallee Poussin size. The x^{1/2}-integer case is Zhang 2007 and BDR 2023.
