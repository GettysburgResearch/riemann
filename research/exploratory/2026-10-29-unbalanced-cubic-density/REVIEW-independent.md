# Independent audit of the unbalanced cubic box density

The moment exponents and relaxed scalar minimax in [README](README.md) check, conditional on the stated rectangular sieve, fixed-field short detector and common-profile Sobolev inputs. In a box with smaller norm scale X^a, the source conductor sieve terms have exponents `(1+a)/3+r` and `(2−a)/3+2r/3`; the ordinary plain second moment is O(X^{1+ε}) for m≤1/2. The three affine inverse faces give precisely the two threshold constraints in the displayed formula for t_a. For δ≥2/3 the third face decreases and is controlled by the baseline, while its candidate t is ≥1. The claimed minimax is attained by relaxed witness pairs, not by demonstrated zeros. Numerical specializations σ=4/5, a=0 and a=1/4 give 8/11 and 3/4.

With ν's finite conductor supported in S, each good p|AB remains ramified, so the conductor is comparable to NAB up to a fixed S-factor. Prime ideals in fixed positive-ratio subboxes give ≫X/(logX)² distinct labels for 0<a≤1/2, with local exponents distinguishing labels. At a=0 the proof may choose a box containing A=1; for an arbitrary nonempty bounded A-box instead fix any admissible good squarefree A₀ in it and count prime B coprime to A₀. An empty box has no density-one proportion to discuss. This is a wording qualification, not a defect in the moment bound.

The named BGL comparison for the squarefree-index case is only a comparison on that subfamily, as the source note states. The inputs' original analytic proofs, the short-t detector transfer, finite-profile derivative estimates and complete prior-art search were not independently established by this audit.
