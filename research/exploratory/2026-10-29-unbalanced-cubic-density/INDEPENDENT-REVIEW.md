# Independent review

The fixed-box conditional density argument is algebraically consistent under the stated cubic sieve, detector and profile assumptions. For `A≈X^a`, `B≈X^(1−a)` and `0≤a≤1/2`, de Faveri's two off-diagonal coefficients have exponents `(1+a)/3` and `(2−a)/3`, giving the stated E_a(r). For m≤1/2 both are at most 1, so the squarefree-kernel decomposition and Minkowski give the asserted plain moment. Fixed box constants do not affect the common X base; good conductor is AB, and bad conductor exponents are uniformly bounded.

For the scalar minimax, the feasibility interval is `[t−1/2,t]`, with a maximum of the plain bound at m=t−r. The first inverse face and the plain face meet at r=t/2 and have value `1−δt/2`. The two t-thresholds in the note are exactly those needed to keep the remaining faces below that value; when δ≥2/3 the third face is decreasing and the cited argument handles it without imposing its threshold. The admissible point `r=m=t/2` proves the lower side for `t≤t_a`, while the active increasing face at r=t,m=0 proves it for `t≥t_a`. Thus the minimax formula is exact for the stated scalar relaxation. The examples 8/11 and 3/4 at σ=4/5 are correct, and the formulas give a strict gain over the balanced exponent when a<1/2.

The prime-ideal lower count is valid for 0<a≤1/2, including excluding equal A,B when the scales coincide; at a=0 take A=(1). Local good ramification distinguishes labels. The finite η, bin, Sobolev, height, and zero-multiplicity allowances have to be chosen in the stated order and stay conditional on their source-facing inputs.

For the BGL comparator at a=0, `A=(1)` gives `χ_(B²)=χ̄_B`, so its symmetric-height zero count is literally the squarefree-index comparison. The a=0 box may also include finitely many other fixed A, for which χ_(AB²) is a fixed twist of χ̄_B; comparison for those additional A requires checking that fixed-twist transfer, rather than calling their indices squarefree. This does not affect the derived conditional density exponent.
