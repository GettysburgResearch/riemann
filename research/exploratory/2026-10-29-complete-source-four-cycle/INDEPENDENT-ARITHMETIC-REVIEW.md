# Independent arithmetic audit of the small-radical and pairwise-conductor bounds

The argument in the adjoining README is valid given standard Kummer reciprocity and the Hecke functional equation; it is a special-family operator estimate, not a general selected-row estimate or a zero-free region. The following checks make the source normalization and the delicate masks explicit.

For a maximal ideal P with P not dividing 6, `CompactEnergyFamilies.lean:490` identifies the source value `idealRowHom(u,P)` with `actualSextic P` on the reduction of u. `RowCaps.lean:204` identifies this with `canonicalSextic`, which is the fixed complex embedding of `sexticChar` (`EisensteinEmbedding.lean:461`). `FiniteFourier.lean:801` defines `sexticChar` as `cubicChar^2 * quadraticCharO`; `FiniteFourier.lean:578` identifies reduction of cubicChar by the exponent `(NP-1)/3`. Consequently, for u nonzero modulo P, the reduction of sexticChar is

    u^[2(NP-1)/3+(NP-1)/2] = u^[(NP-1)/6].

Here NP is 1 mod 6, and reduction is injective on μ6 at P not dividing 6. Thus the source agrees with the standard sextic power-residue symbol, with one fixed embedding of μ6 into C. Kummer reciprocity supplies the corresponding global finite-order Hecke character; whether one calls it the character or its inverse depends only on the global Artin convention. This last global class-field identification is imported, not established by the cited Lean local identities alone.

For u/v in K×, at a prime P not dividing 6, the sixth-power map is an isomorphism on principal local units. Local Kummer theory shows that the induced character is tamely ramified precisely when `v_P(u)-v_P(v)` is nonzero modulo 6, and then its conductor exponent is 1. Unit classes can modify the unramified character but cannot introduce tame ramification. The finitely many local sixth-power classes above 2 and 3 imply uniformly bounded wild conductor exponents. Different good-valuation blocks therefore have nonprincipal pair characters; equal good-valuation blocks may differ only by six units and the 0,...,5 valuations at each of the two bad primes, so have size at most 216.

When P divides both rows with equal exponents, the primitive pair character may be unramified while the original source factors both vanish. Its Euler factor must therefore be deleted. On `Re s=-η`, each deleted factor costs at most `1+(NP)^η`; the product is bounded by an arbitrarily small power of the norm of the union of the two radicals after taking η small. Deleting these factors does not destroy the zero at s=0. The source has its own behavior at the fixed primes: the prime over 3 contributes zero, while the prime over 2 is split according to its source exponent j. Only O(log(2L)) exponents contribute, and their remaining cutoff scales are `X=L/4^j≥1/2`; each local factor has modulus at most 1. This avoids assuming that the source's wild factors equal those of the primitive Hecke character.

The completed L-function of a nonprincipal finite-order Hecke character over the imaginary quadratic field has a Γ_C(s) factor and is entire, so its L-value at 0 vanishes. This cancels the simple s=0 pole of the Mellin transform of the chosen compactly supported smooth majorant W, constant near zero. Shifting to `Re s=-η` and applying the functional equation and the absolutely convergent dual Dirichlet series gives the stated smooth off-block bounds, with small-power losses for variable Euler deletions. The source Gram matrix for any selected subset of source ideals of norm at most L is positive-semidefinite dominated by the W-weighted complete-source Gram matrix. Bounded within-block entries, the number of rows `M≪_ε R^(1+ε)`, and Gershgorin/Schur bounds then give the stated `L+R²`, `L+MR`, and pairwise-conductor estimates with the indicated losses.

The source code local equalities and the archimedean Γ-factor have been checked; the Kummer/Hecke reciprocity and functional equation are standard analytic inputs, not machine-checked consequences of these local equalities. No assessment of novelty or use in the unresolved large-radical detector is implied.
