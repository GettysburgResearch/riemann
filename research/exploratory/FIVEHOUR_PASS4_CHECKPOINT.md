## What this changes

The viable direct architecture is narrower than a generic positive-source or
operator-factor route.  Order four is globally closed, but the sign supplier
uses arithmetic zero density rather than local theta positivity.  The
canonical conditional Gram factor is necessarily nonclosable on the literal
core.  A future all-order proof must therefore preserve the exact quadratic
domain or declare and pay for a new topology; it cannot silently promote the
form to a positive self-adjoint operator.

## Exact frontier

- RH is equivalent to positivity of every finite safe Pick matrix, while this
  pass proves only ordinary orders at most four.  The first undecided literal
  Xi order is five, and the lower-order scalar package cannot settle it.
- The literal retained-gamma native tuple and its complete source measure
  remain unresolved.
- Fixed-prime coercivity does not extend uniformly in the unweighted raw
  growing-prime coordinates.
- No positivity of the full two-variable source kernel was proved.

All resident producers used strict source/artifact seals and were replayed in
normal and optimized Python.  Finite exact panels are regression controls;
they are not proofs of the infinite analytic statements.
