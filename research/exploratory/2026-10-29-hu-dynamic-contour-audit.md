# Audit of the variable-geometry dynamic contour

**Status:** Source-level audit and a conditional contour interface, not a proof of Hu's analytic inputs, the full high-side remainder, or a zero-free region. Primary sources checked are Hu, [v1 §§2, 6–8](https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00216v1), Xu–Sang, [arXiv:2610.15291v2, Appendix C.3 and Appendix F](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15291v2), and the OpenAI [quasi-RH manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Quasi-Riemann-Hypothesis-September-30-2026/paper.pdf), Definition 10.1 and Lemmas 8.1, 10.2–10.4. Their mathematical proofs were not independently replayed.

## What is actually stated

* Hu (6.3) and its Poisson expansion (6.5) are written with positive `X,Y,Z` before his specialization. Hu (6.7) then fixes `ell=1/6`, `lx=17/48`, `ly=23/48`, `h=13/16`; Proposition 6.3 and its numerical principal margins refer to that setup and assume the common family boundary `B>7/8`. His §8 gives one ordered height budget for this fixed-geometry argument. It is not a stated arbitrary-geometry high-side theorem.
* OpenAI Definition 10.1 is an **abstract conditional** exact-high representation for any fixed `lx,ly>0`, `ell>=0`, `h=1-lx+ell>0`. It requires the physical identity (10.5) and a full, quotient-free correction holomorphic in `D1(e0)` and `D2`, with an all-height polynomial bound on fixed real boxes and polynomial row ranges. The definition does not prove these hypotheses for Hu's unitary compensated probe. Lemma 10.3 as stated has `sigma0>=7/8`; its proof of the contour geometry depends instead on the displayed buffered and Euler-region hypotheses. It cannot simply be cited as a theorem below its stated endpoint without repeating that proof.
* Xu–Sang Appendix C.1 says its exact finite compensation identity is coefficientwise and does not require the original numerical `b=1/8`, `ell=1/6`. Appendix C.3 explicitly moves to amplitude-dependent `w` lines **for its finite-order Eisenstein setting**. Proposition 6.1 imposes the admissibility conditions in (29), including `b>0`, `1/6<=ell<=1/5`, `lx>ell`, `ly-ell>11b/6`, moment capacity and row-range inequalities; it is not a theorem for arbitrary positive scales or arbitrary unitary targets. The Appendix supplies a contour argument, not automatic validity of all scalar or moment estimates.

Consequently, neither Hu nor Xu–Sang supplies a ready-made proposition covering **arbitrary** positive variable geometry and all unitary targets. The contour/tail lemma itself is geometry-independent once its physical hypotheses are established; low-side estimates, principal residues, and power-saving outer ranges impose further conditions on the geometry.

## Conditional contour interface

Fix a field `K` containing `mu_12`, Hu's fixed infinity-type family `F_omega` and a later fixed unitary target. Fix positive `lx,ly`, a finite physical slot system with positive lengths `ell_i` and total `ell`, `h=1-lx+ell>0`, and `0<dmin<=d<=dmax<infinity`. Assume for this **same** compensated physical expression:

1. The analogue of Hu (6.5), (6.9) and (6.12) holds with its original primitive/nonprimitive factors, unit-sector multiplicities and full zero-extended local masks. The exact full correction is holomorphic in the first Euler region
   `Re s>=51/100`, `Re z>=17/50`, `Re w>=-1/100`, `Re(s+w)>=1+e0`, with the fixed-box all-height polynomial majorant of OpenAI (10.4). No local division is used to assert holomorphy at its zeros.
2. For each nonprincipal physical row with `Nu~U=Z^d`, its finite collection of presentations includes the actual numerator, denominator, conjugates and all twists used by the detector. Their primitive conductors are `O_psi(U)`; any principal presentations have been removed into separately treated bounded rows. A common zero-free bin has `a in [51/100,B]` and yields a reciprocal `O(U^epsilon)` on `Re s>=a+6e`, `|Im s|<=(3i+2)T1`, with `T1=Z^tau` and a fixed positive buffer.
3. The original Gaussian, Mellin and window kernels, and every **external** separating density on which a trace is taken, satisfy the uniform rapid-decay hypotheses of OpenAI Lemma 10.2. Internal measures controlled only in weighted `L1` stay inside the already fixed arithmetic estimate; they are not silently promoted to trace-controlled external densities. The fixed polynomial height and scale exponents are determined before choosing an external tail order.
4. `0<e<10^(-3)` is small enough for the source Euler regions and retained row-bin bounds, `0<tau<=dmin/100`, and the finitely many Mellin/detector frequencies entering each buffered argument share one cumulative height allowance. If their fixed real coefficient matrix is `(a_jk)` in `gamma_j+sum_k a_jk v_k`, OpenAI (10.14) gives the explicit allocation `|v_k|<=T1/[2M(1+max_j|a_jk|)]` for `M` coordinates, so the total additional displacement of each argument is at most `T1/2`.

Under these assumptions, for one finite nonprincipal row dyad and a fixed detector bin, the contour and trace argument is valid for the chosen positive exponents. First move `z` from its absolute line to `17/50` and `s` to a global line to the right of `B`; leave discarded heights on global or absolute reciprocal lines. Truncate before moving retained `s` to `a+16e`. For each retained `z`, partition the finite row set by the full slot-amplitude vector, then move `w` to

```text
Re w=1/2-vartheta,     0<=vartheta<=min{1/10,a-1/2}.
```

The source main slot sums (Hu (6.10)) depend on the row and `z`, not on `w`, so each partition is fixed during that `w` move; deterministic binning is Borel in `z`. Throughout the move `Re(s+w)>=1+16e`, `Re z=17/50` and `Re w>=2/5`, hence the path stays inside the first Euler region. The numerator is entire for these nonprincipal rows and the denominator is independent of `w`. On a horizontal `w` join the `s` coordinate must remain inside its retained buffered interval. The global numerator and full-correction bounds plus the buffered reciprocal satisfy the domain-restricted trace hypothesis. No later `z` contour move is made after amplitude partitioning.

OpenAI Lemma 10.2 then bounds both discarded external portions and the relevant joins by `O_(psi,N)(Z^Bpsi T1^(-N))` for every prescribed `N`, with the finite exponent `Bpsi` fixed **before** `N`. For finitely many retained dyads and slots, their logarithmic count is harmless. This justifies the contour transformation conditional on items 1–4; it supplies no bound for the retained row sum or a common positive saving. Raising the external smooth-tail order cannot alter the already fixed internal moment/Sobolev orders or their polynomial height degree.

## Exact remaining source-matching gate

The principal obstacle is not an identified contradiction in the path geometry. It is that the literature does not state, in one theorem, items 1–4 for Hu's **variable-geometry** compensated physical probe and an arbitrary fixed unitary target. In particular, the unitary version of the rowwise buffered reciprocal must be available simultaneously for the exact finite collection of numerator/denominator presentations, original deletion masks, binning and all cumulative frequencies. Hu Proposition 6.3 asserts a transfer of the original detector using fixed-type Hecke continuation and conductor control, but does not give a separate variable-geometry statement with all of those quantifiers. Repeating OpenAI Lemma 8.1 requires checking the fixed-infinity-type zero-count/logarithmic-control estimate uniformly in row conductor and the target-dependent constants, as well as the bounded principal-exception rows. These are concrete proof obligations rather than evidence that the bound is false.

The logarithmic-control component can be isolated in a standard conditional lemma. Fix `K` and a finite list of unitary archimedean types. Suppose primitive nonprincipal Hecke functions of those types have the standard polynomial strip bound `|L(s)| << C^A` on `1/2<=Re(s)<=4`, with `C=C_K,omega Q(3+|Im(s)|)^[K:Q]`, uniformly in conductor `Q` and in the finite twists; the fixed archimedean shifts are absorbed into `C_K,omega`. Let `a in [51/100,1]`, `0<e<10^(-3)`, and suppose the disk about `2+it` of radius `2-a-2e` contains no zero. Then on its concentric disk of radius `2-a-6e`, for every `epsilon>0`,

```text
|L(s)| + |L(s)^(-1)| <<_(K,omega,e,epsilon) C^epsilon.       (5)
```

Indeed the Euler logarithm is uniformly bounded in the fixed disk of radius `49/100` about `2+it`; the assumed strip growth bounds `Re log L` by `O(log C)` in the intermediate disk of radius `2-a-3e`. Borel–Carathéodory gives `|log L|=O_e(log C)` on radius `2-a-4e`; Hadamard three-circles between radii `49/100` and `2-a-4e` gives `|log L|=O_e((log C)^theta)` on radius `2-a-6e`, uniformly for some `theta<1`. Exponentiating yields (5). This is the proof of OpenAI Lemma 4.9 with the fixed-field analytic-conductor growth replacing its finite-order Eisenstein input. Deleted Euler factors on a fixed positive half-plane and with radical norm `O_psi(U)` cost `U^epsilon`, by the same elementary argument as OpenAI Lemma 4.10. The principal case is regularized or handled among bounded exceptional rows.

Given (5), a finite row family closed under conjugation and the conductor/deletion bounds, the nested-rectangle pigeonhole argument of OpenAI Lemma 8.1 is independent of `X,Y`: on the enlarged `3(i+1)T1` interval it chooses a gap with a zero-free disk around each retained height. With `Q=O_psi(U)` and `T1<=U^(1/100)`, (5) gives the arbitrarily small `U`-power for the buffered reciprocal after rechoosing its epsilon. This narrows the missing unitary reciprocal gate to the standard uniform strip-growth/conductor assertions and exact matching of the physical finite family and masks; the conditional lemma itself is not an independent verification of those assertions or of Hu's full contour theorem.

It is invalid to extend a reciprocal known only on the buffered rectangle to an entire shifted vertical axis: an unexcluded zero outside the buffer would give a pole. Likewise an integrated vertical-tail estimate alone does not control a horizontal join; the trace estimate is needed. Neither assigning a fresh `T1/2` budget at every detector/AFE step nor treating an internal weighted `L1` coefficient measure as a pointwise rapidly decreasing trace density is justified by Lemma 10.2.

Finally, even a completed conditional contour lemma would not certify a new boundary. One must separately establish the physical full correction and local tables for the same probe, the raised-line averaged numerator with the retained slot masks, all small/large-row and principal exponents, the low-side bound for the same geometry, and a **target-uniform positive** real saving. Fix real margins and moment/mesh choices before the later target; fix its finite profile/Sobolev orders and height degrees; choose a positive `tau` small enough to absorb them; only then choose the external tail order. Hu (8.1)–(8.2) explicitly uses this hierarchy for his fixed geometry.
