# Normal odd abelian kernels without a chosen complement

**Status:** Elementary extension of the companion [odd abelian Frobenius-kernel theorem](README.md), conditional on its named analytic inputs. No priority claim or new zeta theorem is made.

Let `H` be a finite group with a normal abelian subgroup `N` of odd order `n`, and suppose `H/N` is cyclic of order `r>=3`. Assume every nonidentity element of `H/N` acts fixed-point-freely on `N` by conjugation. Because `N` is abelian, that action is independent of the choice of lift. No splitting is needed in the following proof.

First, no prime `p` divides both `n` and `r`. Otherwise `N[p]` is a nonzero vector space over `F_p`, and an order-`p` element in `H/N` acts on it by an operator `T` with `(T-I)^p=T^p-I=0`. Its kernel is nonzero, contradicting fixed-point-freeness. Thus `N` is a normal Hall subgroup. Schur–Zassenhaus also implies that an abstract complement exists, but the character proof below does not use a chosen complement.

The quotient action on the dual `N-hat` is fixed-point-free away from the trivial character: for a nonidentity quotient element the endomorphism `T-I` is injective and hence surjective on `N`; a character fixed by `T` is therefore trivial. Every nontrivial dual orbit has size `r`. For `lambda!=1`, its inertia group in `H` is exactly `N`. Mackey's irreducibility criterion gives `Ind_N^H lambda` irreducible of degree `r`, with equivalent inductions exactly on the same dual orbit. Along with the `r` linear characters inflated from `H/N`, these give all irreducible characters by the sum of squared dimensions `r+((n-1)/r)r^2=nr=|H|`.

Since `N` has odd order, every element of `N` is a square, so `N subset H^2`. The image of the subgroup generated by squares in `H/N=C_r` is its square subgroup; hence `H/H^2` has order `g=gcd(2,r)`. Let `K/k` be a finite Galois extension containing `H` in its Galois group, `F=K^(H^2)`, `M=K^H`, and `E0=K^N`. The regular character and the preceding decomposition give

    zeta_K/zeta_F
      = product_(omega in (H/N)^, ord omega>2) L_M(s,omega)
        * product_([lambda] in (N-hat-{1})/(H/N)) L_E0(s,lambda)^r.

The first product removes the characters of `H/H^2`; the second uses Artin induction for `N subset H` and includes ramified local factors. The fixed field `K^(ker lambda)` is cyclic of degree `ord(lambda)` over `E0`, because `ker lambda` is normal in abelian `N`; it need not be normal in all of `H`. The conductor and discriminant inequalities in the companion note therefore apply unchanged, as do the Das–Stark multiplicity step and its degree condition `[F:Q]>=7`.

**Boundary:** This proves the source-level group reduction, not the imported Hecke estimates or a larger numerical constant. The fixed-point-free assumption applies to every nonidentity quotient element, not merely to a chosen generator. An arbitrary odd abelian normal subgroup with cyclic quotient need not have this property.
