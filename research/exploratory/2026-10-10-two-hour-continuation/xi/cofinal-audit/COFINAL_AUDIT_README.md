The obstruction proofs explain why the accepted signs do not silently
close those gaps. The [positive Fourier model](HEAT_POSITIVE_FOURIER_COUNTERMODEL.md)
has late off-real zeros while its complete heat and all scalar moments
are positive. The [fixed real-head model](FIXED_REAL_HEAD_RESIDUAL_COUNTERMODEL.md)
keeps that positivity after any prescribed finite real prefix is removed,
with the explicit quantifier that the source may depend on the prefix.
The fixed triple-root model in the residual criterion keeps it forever
when only one occurrence per distinct real crossing is selected. These
are different positive Fourier sources; none is an actual-Xi counterexample
or a claim about the literal theta/Euler arithmetic.

The [fixed-inventory bound](FIXED_INVENTORY_SHIFTED_GUARD_BARRIER.md)
proves that sufficiently high globally acute shifted order fails every
safe census guard if the real inventory stays fixed. Increasing order
alone therefore cannot establish cofinal progress. The
[adaptive-shift obstruction](VARIABLE_SHIFT_HAUSDORFF_OBSTRUCTION.md)
shows that every finite mixed family can pass near t=A even for an
off-real positive-Fourier model; the normalized boundary collapses to a
positive point mass at zero. The shift must remain fixed in the universal
criterion.

The [fixed-order partial search](FIXED_ORDER_COFINAL_GUARD_SEARCH.md)
makes a possible mechanism concrete: one complete C1(1) coefficient,
strict rational sign packets and a global acute projection suffice for
sound finite SIMPLE censuses. Dovetailing all finite packets has cofinal
success if and only if RH and simplicity hold, under convergent directed
evaluation. No unbounded implementation, actual progress rate or runtime
bound is claimed. This is a conditional search theorem, not a proof of
its cofinal success.

Constructive [finite Hankel witnesses](CONSTRUCTIVE_FINITE_HANKEL_WITNESS.md)
and their [Bernstein conversion](FINITE_BERNSTEIN_WITNESS_CONVERSION.md)
show that an off-real source occurrence would force some strict negative
finite universal test. No such actual-Xi test is supplied. The remaining
source problem is the universal sign mechanism or cofinal successful
certification, not a missing finite arithmetic replay in this packet.
