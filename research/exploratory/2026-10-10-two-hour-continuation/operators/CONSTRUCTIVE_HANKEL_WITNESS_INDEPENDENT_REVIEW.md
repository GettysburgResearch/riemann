The complete manuscript FW1–FW12 was read. This is an analytic review;
no additional native numerical calculation was performed.

The sum of integer multiplicity times absolute inverse-square node is
finite. This simultaneously pays the complete polynomial functional and
makes every head {|x|>eta} finite. Selecting eta<|beta| retains the target
and its conjugate; the remaining head is conjugate closed. The real
polynomial Z annihilates every other distinct head node, irrespective of
multiplicity, and Z(beta) is nonzero. There is no assumption that beta is
the node of largest modulus.

I checked the interpolation signs directly. With beta=rho^-2 and
R_M=i rho/[Z(beta)beta^M], solving A_M+B_M beta=R_M gives the stated
real coefficients because Im beta is nonzero. Thus P_M(beta)=i rho and
beta P_M(beta)^2=-1; conjugation gives the other contribution -1 with
equal multiplicity. The complete head therefore contributes exactly-2m.

The bounds for A_M and B_M each have the common factor |beta|^-M. On
the closed tail disk, |Z(x)| is bounded by the product of eta+|alpha|.
The stated C0 therefore bounds |P_M(x)| by C0(eta/|beta|)^M. Multiplying
its square by the summable absolute weights pays EVERY omitted tail
node and multiplicity. The strict choice S_tail C0²q^(2M)<m gives a
finite complete functional value below-m. No convergence of a finite
head approximation is substituted for that entire-tail bound.

The complete functional is exactly the contraction of the finite Hankel
matrix (p_(i+j+1)). For real zeros its weights are positive; for an
off-real positive-half-plane root the construction gives a negative
contraction. The actual theta-source exclusion of imaginary-axis roots
is explicitly imported and is necessary for this representative
convention. Repeated real roots are permitted; simplicity does not follow.

Strict negativity persists under rational coefficient approximation.
Consequently the proposed rational enumeration is a semidecision procedure
conditional on convergent directed evaluation of every required complete
moment and on existence of an off-real occurrence. It does not supply an
effective termination bound for the actual source. Finite removal of real
occurrences preserves the argument. At a fixed shifted parameter t>A,
the inverse shifted-square nodes remain summable and off-real for every
off-real positive-half-plane occurrence; replacing the target value by
i sqrt(1/beta) gives the identical negative pair identity. The extension
does not require unproved scalar or mixed-moment positivity.
