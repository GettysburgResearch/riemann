# Integer Möbius short-interval variance: source comparison

**Scope.** This is a targeted, nonexhaustive source check, not a claim that no relevant theorem exists. In particular, no unconditional bound of order `X H^(1+ε)` for the Möbius-weighted variance in [the proposed route](2026-10-29-additive-short-variance-route.md) has been identified at `H=X^(3/10)`. The cited almost-all results do not give that square-root-scale variance by their printed quantitative estimates.

1. Matomäki and Radziwiłł, [*Multiplicative functions in short intervals*](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v183-n3-p06-p.pdf), Annals 183 (2016), Theorem 1, treat all real multiplicative `|f|≤1`, uniformly in `2≤h≤X`. Their theorem compares short and long averages outside a quantified exceptional set. Its explicit log-scale error and exceptional-set size give subpower cancellation of normalized short sums in almost all intervals for `h=X^(3/10)`; they do not supply square-root cancellation or an `X H^(1+ε)` upper bound after integrating the exceptional set. Smooth weights require a separate partial-summation step, and are not an improvement to the quoted scale.

2. Matomäki and Teräväinen, [*On the Möbius function in all short intervals*](https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/4411198), JEMS 25 (2023), give `o(x^θ)` for every interval with `θ>0.55`. The length `X^0.3` is outside this all-interval range, and the `o(H)` scale itself is much weaker than a square-root variance bound.

3. Matomäki, Radziwiłł, Shao, Tao and Teräväinen, [*Higher uniformity of arithmetic functions in short intervals II*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-026-01408-6), Inventiones 244 (2026), concern almost-all intervals and higher-order uniformity, including Möbius at polynomially short scales. These are normalized orthogonality estimates, not a displayed square-root second-moment estimate for this variance. This source has not been exhaustively inspected for every possible corollary.

4. Nathan Ng, [*The Möbius function in short intervals*](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~nathanng/RESEARCH/mobiusshort.pdf), Theorem 1 for `k=2`, gives the random-model variance `~(6/π²)Nh` for the sharp sums, **assuming** uniform Möbius tuple correlations with power error `N^β₀`; the theorem's printed range is `h=o(N^(1−max(β₀,2/3)))`. Thus `h=N^0.3` requires in particular `β₀<0.7`; a merely unspecified `β₀<1` is not enough. This is a conditional sharp-interval result, not an unconditional result for the smooth profile in the route.

5. The established variance theorem of Gorodetsky, Matomäki, Radziwiłł and Rodgers, [*On the variance of squarefree integers in short intervals and arithmetic progressions*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-021-00557-5), concerns the centered squarefree indicator `μ²`, not signed Möbius `μ`. Keating and Rudnick's [Möbius short-interval variance](https://www.math.tau.ac.il/~rudnick/papers/sfssubmission.pdf) is for polynomials over `F_q[t]` as `q→∞`, not the fixed integer sequence.

Finally, a saving against the trivial `XH²` bound is a different claim from the desired `XH^(1+ε)` bound. The proposed route derives a zero-free half-plane from the latter if the stated uniform smooth-profile variance holds; it does not follow from almost-all `o(H)` cancellation alone. The cited comparisons are statements about source scope and strength, not a proof of the route's zero-free implication.
