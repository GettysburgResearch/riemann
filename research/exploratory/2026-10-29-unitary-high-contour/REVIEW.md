# Independent internal review

The moderate-row estimate was reviewed against Xu–Sang arXiv:2610.15291v2, equations (27)–(33), Proposition 7.1 and Appendices D–E, and the source-dependent witness, numerator and local-error companion notes. The review checks the conditional reduction and scalar arithmetic; it does not independently verify the Hu/Xu–Sang physical contour, Mellin-tail hypotheses, moments, or local tables.

The original statement identified only lower amplitude-bin bounds, although the high-side exponent also needs upper bounds for all main slots. The current statement now explicitly includes upper bounds, including the zero bin, with a width-weighted loss. It also restricts target-uniformity to later finite-order twists in a fixed infinity-type orbit, matching the quantifiers of Hu Proposition 4.14. Both corrections are necessary for the stated argument.

For the claimed geometry, h=31757/40000 and l_y=55163/120000; L=ell/d exceeds 21/100 for d≤h+10^(-7). With κ=3/4 and δ≤3/4, q≤δ/2 and the selected displacement obeys ϑ≤δ/9≤1/12 and ϑ≤δ/2=a−1/2. Xu–Sang (32) gives the full-capacity short-block condition with a fixed margin for δ≥1/8. In the small-δ branch the supply-limited displacement obeys both capacity constraints.

Formula (1) is the exponent relative to C_b(B₀), not C_b(β). Since C_b has slope one, its value relative to C_b(β) is exactly E−(β−B₀); the hypothesis β>B₀ therefore improves the stated bound. Symbolic simplification confirms that at d=h the full-capacity expression is Xu–Sang (28), while the supply-limited expression is (33). On each branch the derivative in d is positive and below 2; the two small-δ formulas meet continuously where d=6κell. Thus the extension to h+10^(-7) costs at most 2·10^(-7). The source exact polynomial certificate at κ=3/4, b=173/2000 and ell=10027/60000 gives the claimed at-least 8·10^(-7) margin before analytic losses for δ≥1/8. The quoted small-δ and floor numerical bounds are also the ones in Xu–Sang Appendix E; the floor uses a separate pointwise contour and is not an application of the nonfloor witness count.

No additional numerical or algebraic fault was found after these corrections. The height power (1+T₁)^A is explicit. Any later absorption into Z^ε requires choosing the height exponent after the target-dependent finite height order, while retaining a saving and slot mesh fixed for the source's stated orbit. The assumption that the original physical contour and all trace tails transfer to this geometry remains substantial: the source's fixed-geometry proposition cannot be cited as a literal theorem for these parameters, and a bounded reciprocal on one buffered rectangle cannot be extended to arbitrary heights. This assumption is stated in item 1 and is the principal unresolved bridge to a zero-free conclusion.
