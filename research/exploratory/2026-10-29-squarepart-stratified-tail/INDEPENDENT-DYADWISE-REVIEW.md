# Independent review of the dyadwise certificate

The source-qualified dyadwise argument and `check_dyadwise.py` were independently checked against the stated GL, sixth- and cubic-order sieves, completed-theta identity, original masks and physical-sector/Fourier assumptions. These imported analytic inputs were not independently proved. The result concerns the specified restricted dual core, not the full inverse moment.

For each cube-index dyad, weighted Cauchy supplies the prop:R or direct-sieve second-moment cap, while Abel summation supplies the fixed-p=6 or p=10 cap. Their minimum is taken on the same scale before summing the logarithmically many dyads. Boundary terms use the same prefix–theta Hölder bound as the derivative term. After a physical mask divisor e, changing the norm variable to `H₀=(Ne)^(1/3)H` preserves the completed-theta scale. The exterior squared weight cancels the increase in the prop:R conductor factor; the scale-wise direct/cubic caps do not depend on that factor. The Abel norm gains `(Ne)^(−1/3)` for both fixed powers because the GL prefix loses at least `(Ne)^(−1/6)`, the exterior norm factor is `(Ne)^(−1/2)`, and the transformed Abel measure gains `(Ne)^(1/3)`. Thus each e-dyad is bounded by the same unmasked minimum, and the divisor and dyadic sums cost only a subpower. The source's finite-ray, common-profile and separately budgeted artificial-diagonal conditions remain necessary.

The checker was rerun with one numerical linear-algebra thread. For each case it verified 1,152 nonnegative rational dual certificates and every exact dual identity, with b itself varying from `23/25` to the endpoint. The maxima of the certified dual upper bounds were `88157/84000 < 21/20` for `b≤243/250` and `5281635419/4968750000 < 19949123/18750000` for `b≤997/1000`. The program covers the entire rescaled cube interval `0≤q≤Δ/3`, and its independent choices of source, cubic, sixth-order and prefix faces correctly encode the same-scale minima. The lower range is covered by the previously stated bound. Fixed epsilon losses must be selected inside the strict numerical margins.
