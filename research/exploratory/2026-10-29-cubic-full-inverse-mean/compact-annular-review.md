# Independent review of the universal compact-annular test

The compact-annular construction and its conditional RH implication check out, subject to the stated cubic reciprocity, finite-order Hecke estimates, and ideal-counting inputs. This review does not prove the proposed inverse moment bound or RH.

* For `U_j` uniform on `[-2^(-j),2^(-j)]`, the series is absolutely convergent and supported in [−1,1]. The first N characteristic-function factors are bounded by `C_N(1+|ξ|)^(−N)`, while the rest have modulus at most 1. Therefore φ has rapid decay; Fourier inversion yields a C∞ density w. Its continuity and its interpretation as a probability density imply w≥0, and its support remains in [−1,1]. The profile `W(x)=w(log x)` is nonnegative, C∞ and compactly supported in `[e^(−1),e]`.
* For compact subsets of the s-plane, each moment-generating factor differs from 1 by `O(4^(-j))`. Thus the product converges uniformly on compacts. Each factor is zero only on the imaginary axis, and absolute convergence of the factor deviations prevents additional zeros off that axis. Mellin inversion uses `dx/x`, so `Ŵ(s)=E[e^(sT)]`; in particular it is nonzero at every ρ with Re ρ>1/2.
* The diagonal local factors are correctly `ζ_(K,S)(s)^(-1) H_S(s)`, with `H_S(s)=∏_{p∉S}(1+Np/(Np+1)·Np^(−2s))`. Each factor is nonzero and the product converges absolutely for Re s>1/2. The finitely many S-deletion factors of ζ_K are nonzero in Re s>0, so a base zero ρ in that half-plane gives an uncancelled pole of `Ŵ(s)ζ_(K,S)(s)^(-1)H_S(s)`.
* The unnormalized main term is locally finite and vanishes for sufficiently small D. Were it `O(D^(β−η))`, its Mellin transform would be holomorphic for Re s>β−η, contradicting the pole at ρ. Hence there is an unbounded D-sequence on which its modulus is at least `D^(β−η)`, giving normalized mean size `D^(β−1/2−η)`.
* For D=X^r with fixed r<1/2, the error `X^(−1/2+ε)D^(1+ε)` has D-exponent `−1/(2r)+1+ε(1+1/r)<0` after ε is chosen sufficiently small. For fixed 0<η<β−1/2 the main term grows, so the error is negligible along the Ω sequence. Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and the total squarefree family mass ≍X give the displayed exponent `1+r(2β−1−2η)`.
* The test W and r can be fixed once, before choosing any hypothetical zero. Since the assumed moment bound is required for **every** ε>0, one chooses its ε after β and η to contradict the lower bound. Functional-equation symmetry converts the resulting absence of ζ_K zeros in Re s>1/2 to RH for ζ_K.

The preliminary average identity remains dependent on its stated uniform finite-sector cubic reciprocity and smooth Hecke estimates. Its `X^(−1/2+ε)D^(1+ε)` error is consistent with the stated `D^(1+ε)` triple count, `D^(1/2+ε)` conductor bound, and the X and D^(1/2) normalizations. The unit ideal/principal presentation contributes only finitely many rows and does not alter the estimate.
