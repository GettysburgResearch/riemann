# Zero-free-region exploration: 28 October 2026

**Status:** Exploratory; no new unconditional zero-free region for zeta or the named L-function families is proved. Source claims from the 2026 preprints and tracker remain unaudited.

The [exact endpoint checks](zero_free_endpoint/README.md) and [Xu–Sang retuning](xu_sang_retuning/README.md) check transcribed rational expressions, not the underlying analytic inputs. The retuning is dominated by later claims. The [selected-norm gap](2026-10-28-selected-norm-gap/README.md) is a limitation of one proof method, not a zero-free theorem.
