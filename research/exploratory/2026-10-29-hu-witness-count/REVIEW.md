The previous wording referred to Xu–Sang amplitude classes but did not explicitly state their positive-bin lower bounds. The current statement now spells these out with width-relative losses, requires a bounded total slot-length range, and imposes no false lower bound on slots in the zero bin. This is needed to select whole positive slots uniformly: their fractional initial segment has average gain at least q, discarding one last slot costs at most the mesh times the fixed amplitude cap, and exhausting all positive slots still gives total gain qL≥qz. The remaining rowwise profile and height parameters require the uniform Sobolev/smooth-parameter bounds included in Hu's Proposition 4.14 and in the note's hypotheses; they cannot be replaced by an unrestricted supremum of pointwise moment bounds. The prime-profile and detector frequencies that enter a buffered L-argument share one cumulative height allowance. At a fixed parameter, Hu's nonnegative moment sums remain valid on any row subset.

The source's conductor/local argument removes the exceptional physical rows as stated. These are source-dependent matching hypotheses, not a consequence of the count formula alone.
