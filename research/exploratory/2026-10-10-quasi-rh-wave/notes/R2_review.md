What Prop. U.1 does prove: the integrand cannot be bounded row by row at a common contour (absolute convergence is not uniform in the family), and the Dirichlet-series representation (U.1) is not holomorphic left of β_sup. ## Verdict (≤ 150 words)

The wave's net claim is supported, with two labelling corrections. "The bound was not moved" is true and stated everywhere. "Constructions carried to identified failure points" holds for S0/T (slope-1/2 obstruction, numerically consistent) and for U routes 1-4, but U's one item labelled rigorous (Prop. U.1) proves only non-uniform absolute convergence at a common contour, not that the all-row signed mean value is "not a theorem"; the f = 0 verdict then rests on the bookkeeping ceiling (U.4) and the §3 dual-family inequality, which are heuristic and should be labelled so. After findings 1, 4, 7-12 are applied, nothing in these notes misstates the paper, and no numerical claim checked (exp1 at X = 3000, HD orientation, (9.16), R(1/50) = 1) failed.
