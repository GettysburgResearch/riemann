# Research navigation

The [cumulative results](../RESULTS.md) and [current statements with proof routes](integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md) are the scientific entry points. This index preserves the wider corpus, including older results not revisited recently, without turning historical acceptance into an exception to a later correction.

**Integrated** means the exact recorded component and hypotheses, not every file in its folder. **Inherited source-qualified** means earlier review is retained at a pinned source, with the named source/domain/extraction obligations still present. **Active** means proposed work outside those accepted scopes. A family manifest is not itself a full proof.
