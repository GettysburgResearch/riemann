## How to read this index

**Residency**

- `LOCAL_PACKET` — the statement and proof extract are physically present under [`research/integrated/`](integrated/README.md).
- `SOURCE_PINNED` — the strongest safe statement remains at an exact reviewed source commit; this page does not copy its proof body.
- `BLOCKED_INTERFACE` — useful subclaims survive, but a named source, repair, artifact, domain, or cofinal gate blocks integration.
- `ARCHIVAL_METADATA` — review or lifecycle evidence, not a theorem body.

**Status and scope**

A review status is meaningful only together with scope. `VERIFIED / finite` is not `VERIFIED / global`; `VERIFIED / cofinal conditional` does not establish the cofinal hypothesis.

## Current frontiers

1. **Canonical explicit-formula source contract.** Reconcile completed-\(\xi\), Guinand–Weil, Suzuki/CCM, Fourier, endpoint, and form-domain conventions.
2. **One strict authenticated finite violation—or an honest finite exclusion.** Freeze the predicate before final directed evaluation and authenticate every primitive.
3. **Infinite Robin tail.** Cross the unbounded canonical quantifier rather than only extending the endpoint.
4. **Square-screw or terminal-prime cofinal sign.** Prove the actual one-sided growth/boundedness theorem or a complete strict finite band violation.
5. **Complete corrected-kernel synthesis.** Establish complete capture and
   \[
   B_{K,j}-Z_{K,j}^*C_j^{-1}Z_{K,j}\ge-\varepsilon_jG_{K,j},
   \qquad \varepsilon_j\to0.
   \]
6. **Primitive and artifact assurance.** Independently regenerate source values, enforce complete coverage, and preserve durable proof objects.

## Compact glossary

- **primitive:** an externally meaningful input such as a completed-\(\xi\) ball, zero count, zero ball, prime-power manifest, or Weil matrix.
- **directed interval:** an outward enclosure guaranteed to contain the true value under its arithmetic contract.
- **proof object:** compact data that a small checker can replay to establish a finite implication.
- **source-qualified:** accepted only after naming an external theorem, normalization, data source, or implementation not fully reproved in the packet.
- **locality gate:** a theorem proving that failure of a candidate must arise inside a declared local region.
- **coverage gate:** proof that every required cell, zero shell, prime power, shard, or tree terminal is present exactly as declared.
- **cofinal:** extending through an unbounded sequence, not merely many finite examples.
- **Schur correction:** elimination of a positive block, subtracting its positive semidefinite cross term from the remaining block.
