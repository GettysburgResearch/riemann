## Packet policy

A packet must contain readable mathematics, exact provenance, review evidence, scope boundaries, source qualifications, computation/replay status, common misreadings, and the next missing step.

A packet never silently modifies a frozen claim. Corrections are separate objects. Alternate proofs and superseded forms remain discoverable through exact sources and the stable [`canonical/aliases.yaml`](../../canonical/aliases.yaml).
