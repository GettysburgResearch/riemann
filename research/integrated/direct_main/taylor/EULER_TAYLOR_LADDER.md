# Direct-main Euler-Taylor positivity ladder

The direct-main audit recovers two standalone results:

- activation-zero native powers are globally positive for every real `m>=2`;
- every Euler-Taylor remainder whose effective prime exponent is at least `3/2` is positive, giving exact alternating enclosures through the final absolutely summable prime layer.

The first prime-harmonic remainder is represented by a fixed zero-safe kernel, but its native Möbius projection remains signed. Subpower negative mass or the corresponding critical upper envelope is open and RH-equivalent.

Supercritical positivity therefore does not cross the critical carrier.
