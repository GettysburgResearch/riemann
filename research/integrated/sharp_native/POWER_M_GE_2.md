# SHARP power positivity and the critical wall

The reviewed theorem gives global positivity for every real SHARP/native boundary power
\[
m\ge2.
\]

The result is unconditional in its stated source and endpoint normalization. It is not uniform enough to pass to `m=1`.

At the critical power the owner mass changes to the prime-harmonic scale. Exact distributional descent identities survive, but the required weighted one-sided variation bound remains open and is reviewed as RH-equivalent.

Canonical use must therefore keep separate:

```text
m >= 2 positivity                    VERIFIED
distributional descent identities   VERIFIED
critical weighted variation bound   OPEN / RH-EQUIVALENT
```
