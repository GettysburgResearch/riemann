# The smallest positive gap in Zhao's field condition

Among the 2,580 rational isomorphism classes satisfying \(b_K=2(\log\xi_K)'(1)<2\), where \(\xi_K(s)=s(s-1)\Lambda_K(s)\), the minimum of \(2-b_K\) is
\[
 (3.315475517292\cdot10^{-5})\ \pm\ 3.29\cdot10^{-18}.
\]
Exactly one field attains it: the quartic field generated by a root of \(x^4-x^3-x^2+5x+2\), with discriminant \(-15788\) and signature \((2,1)\). These are unconditional comparisons of completed logarithmic derivatives. Under the Riemann hypothesis for the corresponding Dedekind zeta function, they give the same comparisons for the sum \(B_K=\sum_\gamma(1/4+\gamma^2)^{-1}\).

Exactly one further field has \(1.99990<b_K<2\). It is the quintic field generated by a root of \(x^5-3x^3-2x^2+3x-3\), of discriminant 52245 and signature \((1,2)\), with LMFDB label \(5.1.52245.1\). Its gap is \((8.400226734298\cdot10^{-5})\pm7.42\cdot10^{-18}\).

## Validating the minimum

The [replay script](../../scripts/check_zhao_minimum_margin_nov18.py) assembles all 2,579 nonrational passing polynomials from the unconditional field inventories. The rational field has \(b_{\mathbb Q}=2+\gamma_E-\log(4\pi)<1.99990\). For every other entry it evaluates the [finite Dirichlet coefficient and interval Fourier test](zhao-field-unconditional-replay-nov18.md) at \(b_0=1.99990\). Specifically its integral computes \(H'(1)-(b_0/2+1/36)H(1)\), where \(H=\xi_K\exp((s-1/2)^2/36)\). Since \(H(1)>0\), this tests the sign of \(b_K-b_0\). The factor \(|1/[w(w-1)]-(b_0/2+1/36)|\) is below 3 on all the vertical lines used in the certificate, as before. An upper error budget of 0.006 in the Dirichlet tail selection yields 2,576 strict decisions. Repeating the three outstanding comparisons with budget 0.00001 separates all three. In total 2,577 nonrational fields have \(b_K<1.99990\), and two have \(b_K>1.99990\).

For those two, the independent [logarithmic Euler contour](../../scripts/check_zhao_dedekind_contour_nov18.py) at 192-bit precision with tolerance \(10^{-16}\) gives disjoint balls for \(b_K\), respectively \(1.99996684524482708\pm3.29\cdot10^{-18}\) and \(1.99991599773265702\pm7.42\cdot10^{-18}\). The script checks their strict ordering in interval arithmetic. Coefficients fed to PARI agree with the inventories' respective ascending or descending conventions, and PARI verifies the field discriminant for every entry before generating ideal counts. No GRH estimate is used to distinguish the margins. [Adjustable-threshold checker](../../scripts/check_zhao_field_threshold_nov18.py)

Consequently the [integrated prime-ideal sum](zhao-field-sign-equivalence-nov18.md) obeys \(J_K(X)>0.0000331547\,\sqrt X/\log X\) for all sufficiently large \(X\), simultaneously over every field on the list whose Dedekind zeta function satisfies RH. Indeed its normalized profile has limiting inferior at least \(2-b_K\), and there are only finitely many fields on the list. No numerical starting value of \(X\) is asserted.
