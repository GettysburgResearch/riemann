# A pointwise 32-mode Nyquist matrix with a directed arithmetic lower bound

**Status.** Finite arithmetic certificate, proposed for independent replay. At carrier $X=100000\pi$, $N=50000$, $U=\log N$, and support radius $L=U/2$, the actual Weil matrix below satisfies $A\succ(1/10)I$. No zero census or synthetic zero locations enter. The radius is exactly $\tfrac12\log(X/(2\pi))$.

## Primitive inputs and test space

Let $d=32$, $t_j=X+(2j-d+1)\pi/U$, $0\le j<d$, and $w=N^{-3}$. Take any real smooth $\chi$ in $[-L,L]$ equal to one on $[-L+2w,L-2w]$, zero beyond $[-L+w,L-w]$, between zero and one, and monotone through each endpoint transition. The basis is $g_j(u)=U^{-1/2}\chi(u)e^{-it_ju}$. The ordinary Hermitian matrix $A_{jk}=W(g_k,g_j)$ uses the complete arithmetic formula (gamma, both poles and all prime powers). The positivity statement is uniform over all tapers meeting these conditions. This finite space is not asserted to contain the projector guaranteed by the asymptotic search theorem; its unevaluated constants and its cutoff and dimension differ.

For the auxiliary full box, define $S(t)=\sum_{n\le N}\Lambda(n)n^{-1/2}\sin(t\log n)$. After conjugation by the unitary diagonal $(-1)^j$, the symmetric box prime matrix $P$ has entries
\[
 P_{jj}=2\sum_{n\le N}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}(1-\log n/U)\cos(t_j\log n),
 \qquad
 P_{jk}=-\frac{S(t_j)-S(t_k)}{\pi(j-k)}\quad(j\ne k).
\]
Indeed on shifting by $s=\log n$ the overlap length is $U-s$, its phase midpoint is $-(t_j+t_k)s/2$, and its sinc factor at difference $j-k$ is $\operatorname{sinc}(\pi(j-k)(1-s/U))$. The product-to-sum identity gives the displayed divided difference.

The checker enumerated all 5,217 prime powers up to $N$ with their prime bases and checked the integer identity $\prod_{p^k\mid n}p=n$ at every integer $1\le n\le N$. At 160-bit Arb precision it assembled the matrix, verified overlap with four direct trigonometric autocorrelations and performed symmetric interval Schur elimination on $U I-P-(3/20)I$. All 32 pivots had strictly positive lower endpoints. This proves the box prime reserve $U I-P\succ(3/20)I$.

## Directed conversion to the smooth full form

The cut and uncut overlap integrals at each shift differ by at most $8w/U$ entrywise, since only endpoint strips of total length at most $8w$ can be affected in the product. Thus the prime operator perturbation is at most $16dNw$, using $\sum_{n\le N}\Lambda(n)/\sqrt n\le N\log N$. The Gram matrix of the cutoff modes is at least $(1-4dw/U)I$, by the trace bound on the lost nonnegative form.

Write $R=X/50$, $B=(d-1)\pi/U$. For every coefficient vector of norm one, the normalized squared Fourier mass outside $|t-X|\le R$ is at most
\[
 p=\frac{4d}{\pi U(R-B)}.
\]
Indeed integration by parts and $\|\chi'\|_1=2$ bound each squared amplitude by $4/(U|t-t_j|^2)$; sum over $j$ by Cauchy–Schwarz and integrate both tails with factor $1/(2\pi)$. At the two poles the same argument bounds their combined possible loss by $8d\sqrt N/[U(X-B)^2]$.

For $\mu(t)=\Re\psi(1/4+it/2)-\log\pi$, the digamma series gives $\mu(t)\ge\psi(1/4)-\log\pi>-7$. At $t\ne0$, Binet's derivative formula at $s=1/4+it/2$ gives
\[
 |\psi(s)-\log s|\le(2|s|)^{-1}
   +2\int_0^\infty\frac{v\,dv}{|v^2+s^2|(e^{2\pi v}-1)}
 \le\frac{4}{3|t|},
\]
using $|v^2+s^2|\ge|t|/4$ and $\int_0^\infty v/(e^{2\pi v}-1)dv=1/24$. Throughout the central band $\mu(t)\ge U+b$ with $b=\log(1-R/X)-4/[3(X-R)]$. The gamma lower reserve relative to $UI$ therefore costs at most $-b+(U+b)4dw/U+(U+b+7)p$. The checker encloses this loss below $0.030891$, the pole loss below $0.000000054$, and the prime smoothing loss below $0.000000205$. Subtracting them from $3/20$ leaves more than $0.11910$.
