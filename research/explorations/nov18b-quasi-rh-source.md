# The zero-free strip used in this investigation

For the Riemann zeta function and every Dirichlet L-function the half-plane of real part greater than $7/8$ is zero-free, with the principal pole at one treated separately. The primary manuscript proves this first for finite-order Hecke characters over the Eisenstein field and transfers it by quadratic base change. The pinned Lean export includes `OAI.riemannZeta_ne_zero_of_seven_eighths_lt_re` for Mathlib's zeta function. The condition on the real part is strict. ## Correction to the Mertens-product draft

Write $\Theta=\sup_{\zeta(\rho)=0,\;0<\Re\rho<1}\Re\rho$. The remaining case considered in our first draft, $\Theta=1$, cannot occur for the ordinary zeta function. Zhao's argument for $1/2<\Theta<1$ therefore covers every failure of RH. Together with the RH direction and its finite lower endpoint this gives the positivity criterion for the integrated normalized Mertens product directly. The first draft's conditional near-one oscillation argument supplies no additional information about zeros of the ordinary zeta function. [Zhao, Theorem 2 and §8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40993-025-00640-y) · [Earlier proof and finite endpoint](zhao-third-mertens-nov18.md) · [Corrected draft](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/1032)
