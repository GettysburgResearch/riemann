# Research checkpoint, 18 November 2026

These are proposed results for review on the branch based on main `f99d9e3`. RH remains open. The references below identify the assumptions that would still be needed for a resolution. Computations use outward interval arithmetic where the individual notes specify Arb. Published zero censuses, prime bounds and previous Robin verification are imported.

The finite Robin proof proceeds from the [verified low range and integrated explicit formula](robin-finite-horizon-nov18.md), through the [arbitrary-integer tangent identity](robin-tangent-nov18.md) and [averaged bounds](robin-smoothed-nov18.md), to the bandlimited estimate. The Li proof uses the [quartet and projected-zero formulas](observatory-nov18.md). Neither finite calculation proves positivity at all orders.

Other connections include the [least-common-multiple version of Robin's criterion](robin-lcm-nov18.md), the [prime-variance threshold for simplicity](causal-mean-nov18.md), the [Laguerre prime-energy formula](laguerre-prime-energy-nov18.md), and [heat positivity on the full verified-center window](heat-nov18.md). Each is a separate proposed mathematical statement with its own hypotheses.

The [indexed zero computation](../../experiments/X-nov18-verified-height/README.md) extends the published endpoint with archived dyadic brackets and endpoint counts. The [Möbius prefix certificate](newton-prefix-certificate-nov18.md) includes two replays through 4,294,967,295. The [fixed-window LCM argument](robin-lcm-positive-kernel-nov18.md) and the [single-column trace estimate](trace-single-column-nov18.md) describe local consequences of an off-line zero. The [Laguerre saddle bounds](laguerre-prime-saddle-law-nov18.md) quantify the remaining cancellation on the prime side.

The [Robin exceptional-set count](robin-violation-count-nov18.md) gives an unconditional upper bound improving the 2025 Luca–Pomerance–Solé estimate. Assuming RH fails, it proposes an exact growth exponent for the number of violating integers. Counting integers within an explicit square-root tolerance gives a positive counting function on either side of the RH dichotomy.

At tolerance $(\log n)^{a-1}$, the [sharp near-equality count](robin-census-constant-nov18.md) gives the leading constant $\pi\sqrt{2/3}$ if all zeros have real part at most $a$, with a converse. At the stricter zero tolerance, a [boundary profile](robin-census-entropy-nov18.md) distinguishes whether the supremum of real parts is attained. These claims remain proposed.
