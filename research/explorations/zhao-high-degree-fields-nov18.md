# Degree-six, seven and eight fields in the strict spectral condition

Under Dedekind GRH, exactly **487 sextic, 68 septic and two octic fields** satisfy Zhao's strict condition \(B_K<2\). Here \(B_K=\sum_\gamma(1/4+\gamma^2)^{-1}\) includes both signed ordinates and multiplicities. The two octic fields have LMFDB labels \(8.0.1257728.1\) and \(8.0.1265625.1\). The latter is the fifteenth cyclotomic field. The inventories specify every field by a defining polynomial, with coefficients in ascending order, and its LMFDB label. Separately, **none of these 557 passing fields has a nontrivial real zero**, unconditionally. To see the test, write \(D_K(s)\) for the logarithmic derivative of the pole-cancelled completed Dedekind zeta. Its real value at \(s>1\) is a sum of nonnegative real parts of \(1/(s-\rho)\). A real pair \(\beta,1-\beta\) contributes at least \(2/(s-1/2)\) by convexity. A central real zero has even multiplicity by the symmetric functional equation. Thus an upper bound \((s-1/2)D_K(s)<2\) excludes all nontrivial real zeros. Truncating the negative absolutely convergent Euler logarithmic derivative after finitely many primes gives such an upper bound. The verifier found a strict inequality at one of the five specified rational \(s>1\), using primes through at most 64 for every field here. The inventory's real-zero witness for each label gives the prime cutoff, \(100s\) as an integer and the upper-bound expression as an Arb interval. The other octic has a class-field description. Let \(F=\mathbb Q(i)\) and \(\mathfrak p=(1+4i)\), of norm 17. Since \(F\) has class number one and its four units inject modulo \(\mathfrak p\), its ray class group at \(\mathfrak p\) is \(\mathbb F_{17}^{\times}/\mu_4\), cyclic of order four. Every nontrivial ray class character has conductor exactly \(\mathfrak p\), since the ordinary class group is trivial. The relative conductor-discriminant formula thus gives \(\mathfrak d_{L/F}=\mathfrak p^3\) for this ray class field \(L\). Its absolute discriminant is \(4^4 17^3=1,257,728\), and it is totally imaginary of degree eight. The exhaustive table contains just one field of this signature and discriminant, identifying \(L\) with \(8.0.1257728.1\). Replacing \(\mathfrak p\) by its conjugate gives an isomorphic field over \(\mathbb Q\).
