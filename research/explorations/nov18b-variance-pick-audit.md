Reflection across the critical line preserves each of those bands.
- In the coordinate \(x=2\gamma/T-3\) the main zero density is \(T\log(\gamma/(2\pi))/(4\pi)\). The Legendre Hilbert–Schmidt derivative bound follows from \(\int(P_n')^2=n(n+1)\). At \(\Re x=\pm1\), the ellipse with parameter \(9/8\) extends vertically beyond 0.0138, sufficient at every accepted height.

The relaxed displacement contribution \(\sqrt{1.5}(\exp(0.054/2)-1)\) is below 0.03352. Adding the rational approximation error leaves the stated 0.035. The real-part lower coefficient in the final quadratic form exceeds 0.463. A nonzero proper rational test has positive supremum on each band, and therefore a nonzero approximating polynomial.
