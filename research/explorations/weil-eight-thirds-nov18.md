# Full-space Weil positivity at radius 8/3

**Status.** Proposed theorem for independent review. For $L=8/3$ and every complex $g\in C_c^\infty(-L,L)$ the actual Weil form obeys
\[
 W(g)\ge(F_*/20)\|g\|_2^2>0\qquad(g\ne0),
\]
where the explicit positive $F_*$ is given below. The original arithmetic cutoff is $e^{16/3}$ (all integer prime powers through 207 are admitted by the support bound). Inputs are the [Platt–Trudgian verified height](../../formal/registry/deltas/C_EXTERNAL_SOURCE_STATEMENTS/EXT.XI.PLATT_TRUDGIAN.2021.txt), the [flat-passband argument](weil-window-positivity-nov18.md), and its [positive mixture of compact filters](weil-schur-window-nov18.md). The finite computation is a certified upper bound on a continuum operator, used together with a sharper uncertainty bound. It does not sample candidate tests.

## A finite-difference inequality

For positive integers $m,M$ and any sequence $u$ supported on at most $M$ consecutive sites, let $\Delta u_j=u_j-u_{j-1}$. Then
\[
 \|u\|_2^2\le
 \frac{\binom{M+2m}{2m+1}}{\binom{2m}m}\|\Delta^m u\|_2^2 .
 \tag{1}
\]
Both norms sum over all integer sites. To prove this, identify $u$ with a polynomial of degree less than $M$ on the unit circle by translation and Fourier series. Its unweighted squared norm is $\|u\|_2^2$, and the squared norm with weight $|1-z|^{2m}$ is $\|\Delta^m u\|_2^2$, using normalized Haar measure. Define the monic polynomials
\[
 \Phi_n(z)=\sum_{j=0}^n {n\choose j}
 \frac{(m)_{n-j}(m+1)_j}{(m+1)_n}z^j ,
\]
where $(b)_j$ is the rising factorial. They are orthogonal for this weight. Indeed the moments are $(-1)^r\binom{2m}{m+r}$, zero outside $|r|\le m$. For $0\le k<n$, the inner product with $z^k$ is, up to a constant, the alternating $n$th binomial difference of the polynomial
\[
 [(m+j)(m+j-1)\cdots(m+j-k+1)]
 [(m+n-j-1)\cdots(m-j+k+1)].
\]
Its factors are falling factorials of respective lengths $k,n-1-k$. Zeros of these products also implement the missing binomial terms. Its degree is at most $n-1$, so the difference vanishes.

Put $h_n=\|\Phi_n\|^2_{|1-z|^{2m}}$. The reversed polynomial $\Phi_n^*(z)=z^n\overline{\Phi_n(1/\bar z)}$ is orthogonal to $z,\ldots,z^n$. Thus $\Phi_{n+1}-z\Phi_n$ is proportional to $\Phi_n^*$ by dimension, and evaluation at zero gives the factor $c=m/(m+n+1)$. Orthogonality now gives $h_{n+1}=(1-c^2)h_n$ and $\Phi_{n+1}(1)=(1+c)\Phi_n(1)$. From $h_0=\binom{2m}m$, $\Phi_0(1)=1$, it follows that
\[
 \Phi_n(1)^2/h_n=\binom{n+2m}{2m}/\binom{2m}m .
\]
All coefficients of $\Phi_n$ are nonnegative, so its maximum modulus on the circle occurs at one. Cauchy–Schwarz in the orthogonal expansion consequently bounds every squared point evaluation by the weighted polynomial norm squared times $\sum_{n<M}\Phi_n(1)^2/h_n$. Integrating over the circle and summing by the hockey-stick identity proves (1).

Take $m=3\,600\,000\,000$, $R=90m$, $M=2LR+1$, an integer. On each lattice coset of step $1/R$ in the support of $g$ there are at most $M$ consecutive sites. Apply (1) and integrate over a fundamental interval to bound the continuous difference of step $1/R$. In the source passband notation $I\ge4^{-m}\|\Delta^m g\|_2^2$. The elementary bounds $\binom{2m}m\ge4^m/(2m+1)$ and $\binom{M+2m}{2m+1}\le(M+2m)[e(M+2m)/(2m)]^{2m}$ therefore give
\[
 I\ge F_*\|g\|_2^2,\qquad
 F_*=\frac{A^{-2m}}{(M+2m)(2m+1)},\qquad A=\frac{e(M+2m)}{2m}<656.
\]

## Continuum prime norm and finite arithmetic

The squared-filter mixture requires the compressed prime translations on radius $L+6m/R=41/15$. Divide that interval into 1,024 equal half-open cells (endpoint choices have measure zero). For each prime power $q=p^k$ in the support cutoff, let $j$ be the certified floor of $\log(q)$ divided by cell width. The positive translation sends each cell to at most cells $i+j,i+j+1$ and the negative translation to at most $i-j,i-j-1$. Values outside the interval are zero. Thus the Schur ratio of any positive piecewise-constant weight is bounded by a finite row sum using the maximum in each pair of target cells. This is an upper bound on the full continuum translation form.

The [checker](check_weil_eight_thirds_nov18.py) upper-rounds each $\Lambda(q)/\sqrt q$ to a multiple of $10^{-8}$ and uses a positive integer cell vector. Its final rational row test needs no convergence assumption on the preliminary iterations. All 65 prime powers up to 236 were regenerated by the [prime-power checker](check_weil_50000_nov18.py); integer factor-product coverage is checked for every integer through 236. The maximum rational ratio is $41052016613461/2362243750000<17.4$.

## Remaining scalar budgets

At $|t|\ge t_1=255\,000\,000$, Binet's digamma bound $\Re\psi(1/4+it/2)-\log\pi\ge\log(|t|/(2\pi))-4/(3|t|)$ gives more than $17.5$. Everywhere the multiplier is at least $-7$. Thus the gamma-minus-prime complement is at least $I/10-26 I_{\rm low}$. The following three upper bounds apply per unit squared test norm, in order to the low-frequency loss, the pole loss, and the unverified exterior:
\[
 52\left(\sqrt{8/3}\ t_1/R\right)^{2m},\qquad
 16 V\,8^mR^{-2m},\qquad
 20 V T_0\log(2T_0)\,[9(1.001R/T_0)^4]^{2m},
 \quad V=2L e^L .
\]
For the first two, use the complementary binomial sum and the pole bound from the flat-passband proof. For the third, each exterior zero has $|y(z)|\le q_0=(1.001R/t)^2<1/8$ at $t=|\Re z|>T_0$. Its multiplier therefore obeys $|P(z)|\le [8(1+q_0)]^m(1.001R/t)^{4m}\le9^m(1.001R/t)^{4m}$. Dyadic summation of the zero count and $|\widehat g(z)|^2\le V\|g\|_2^2$ give the displayed exterior bound. Every zero through $T_0=3\,000\,175\,332\,800$ is on the line by the imported result and contributes nonnegatively to the passband part.

Each of the three losses is strictly smaller than $F_*/60$, by directed logarithmic comparison in the checker. The low and high exponential bases are below $0.001286$ and $0.001230$ respectively, whereas $1/A>1/656$. Subtracting the three losses from $I/10$ proves the lower bound.
