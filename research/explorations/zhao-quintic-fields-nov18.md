# The quintic fields in Zhao's strict spectral test

Exactly **616 quintic number fields** up to rational isomorphism have \(B_K<2\), assuming GRH for the tested Dedekind zeta functions. Here \(B_K\) sums \((1/4+\gamma^2)^{-1}\) over all signed zero ordinates with multiplicity. The [inventory](zhao-quintic-fields-nov18.json) specifies each passing field by a monic defining polynomial in ascending coefficient order. Its Olivier identifiers give the degree, number of real places, absolute discriminant, and occurrence in that source.

The [signature-specific upper bounds](zhao-field-catalogue-bounds-nov18.md) reduce the test to these finite discriminant intervals. The Bordeaux small-field archive credits Diaz y Diaz, Pohst, and Schwarz with exhaustive quintic enumerations to at least one million in the two signatures with a complex place, and to twenty million in the totally real signature. All three bounds lie inside those enumeration ranges. The extraction checks the first and last discriminant against each block's published index, checks record counts, and records the SHA-256 hashes of the downloaded blocks. [Table documentation](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/pub/numberfields/readme.pdf) · [Quintic source blocks](https://pari.math.u-bordeaux.fr/pub/numberfields/degree5/) · [Extractor](../../scripts/fetch_zhao_quintic_catalog_nov18.py)

For the initial rejection test at prime cutoff 16, the checker verifies the polynomial discriminant equals its catalog field discriminant times the squared integral-order index. At primes not dividing the index, modular factorization gives the prime-ideal residue degrees, counting each distinct factor once even when ramified. At primes dividing the index the maximum splitting contribution furnishes a lower bound for the field's spectral weight. Subsequent stages at 64, 256, 1,024, 4,096 and 16,384 use all exact residue degrees from PARI/GP. Every prime-stage acceptance or rejection is a strict Arb comparison against the two endpoints in the [finite explicit formula](zhao-field-prime-test-nov18.md). The 137 residual fields have strict enclosures from the Dedekind contour formula. The inventory includes those intervals and the number decided at every prime stage. As an independent catalog check, the three Olivier lists have exactly the same multiplicity at every absolute discriminant as the November 17, 2026 LMFDB snapshot in these same signature ranges. For each of the 616 passing fields the assembler ran PARI's exact isomorphism test against every LMFDB polynomial of matching signature and discriminant. In each case it found exactly one match, yielding the additional LMFDB label in the inventory. The adapted catalog identifiers come from the LMFDB Collaboration under CC BY-SA 4.0.
