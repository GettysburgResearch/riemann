## 4. Stable conjunction architecture

The stable joint pair weight is

\[
\pi_{ij}=r_ir_jL_iR_j.
\]

For concrete collar certificates `A_ij,B_ij` satisfying

\[
(H_{ij})_-^2\le A_{ij}B_{ij},
\]

put

\[
\mathcal A=\sum_{i<j}\pi_{ij}A_{ij},\qquad
\mathcal B=\sum_{i<j}\pi_{ij}B_{ij}.
\]

Then

\[
F_-\le\sqrt{\mathcal A\mathcal B}.
\]

This is the reusable analytic AND port.  The left certificate must retain the
same collar occurrence while exploiting least-owner information; the right
certificate must retain that occurrence while exploiting greatest-owner
information.  Independent marginals are not admissible.
