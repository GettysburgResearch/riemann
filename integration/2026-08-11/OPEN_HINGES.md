# Open hinges and research priorities

This roadmap ranks theorem interfaces, not PR activity. An item is high priority when it is explicit, independently falsifiable, supported by exact infrastructure and not already known to be merely an RH-equivalent restatement.

## Priority A — strongest live mechanisms

### A1. Source-specific policy Green debt

Construct an explicit cofinal balanced policy with

```text
Σ_n (-M_n)_+ d_G(n) = X^{o(1)},
M=s(I-P)^(-1),
```

or prove an equivalent signed Cycle-Debt theorem.

**Why it is live:** the frozen producer refutation attacks one stationary policy and pointwise/absolute observables. It does not attack recombined signed capacity debt.

**Binary falsifier:** an exact source-specific pole or dual witness forcing power-sized negative debt for the proposed policy.

### A2. All-generation capacity-faithful boundary recurrence

Separate fresh and inherited boundary, preserve arithmetic fibers, bind every transport to actual incoming capacity, recombine equal destinations before variation and prove a coefficient-one recurrence with polylogarithmic additive debt.

**Existing inputs:** exact decoder, first-boundary small native norm, zero-debt outer regions, support-halving geometry and explicit counterexamples to atomic closure.

**Binary falsifier:** a reachable boundary state whose optimized debt violates every proposed coefficient-one recurrence.

### A3. Recursive critical-hinge saturation

The top-half inversion for decreasing discretely convex targets exports a nonnegative strict-half residual. Prove that this residual belongs to a positive hinge cone admissible for the next scale.

**Binary falsifier:** a finite exact residual outside every proposed positive next-scale decomposition.

### A4. Adaptive endpoint packing with signed score loss

Use positive occupancy and the complete-prime-power state to construct feasible ordered transport satisfying

```text
L_X(d)=o(log^2 X).
```

Analyze signed entropy score, not unsigned slack.

**Binary falsifier:** a dual feasible score separating every packing in the proposed policy class by a fixed positive fraction of `log^2 X`.

### A5. Raw Brownian cofinal half-plane stability

Prove

```text
E[Q_N^z] != 0,  Re z>1/4,
```

for all sufficiently large `N`, or for an unbounded cofinal sequence.

Promising coordinates include the sampled derivative sum, reciprocal Hermite interpolation, beta-Dirichlet recursion and an aggregate canonical-system realization.

**Binary falsifier:** a directed certified zero with `Re z>1/4` for one relevant finite `N`, plus an argument that the failure persists cofinally if claiming a route-level obstruction.

### A6. Aggregate canonical system for the corrected Brownian mixture

Construct one Hermite–Biehler/canonical-system object for the complete symmetrized mixture. Do not rely on fiberwise positivity or finite reflected-tail domination.

**Binary falsifier:** a finite exact approximant violating the structural interlacing or total-positivity hypothesis of the proposed aggregate system.

## Priority B — live but closer to established RH-bearing walls

### B1. Q4 positive innovation plus exact integrated assembly

The target is not another local trace/determinant estimate. It requires:

1. an unconditional bound on the positive source-convolved innovation energy;
2. a common source-resolved integrated recurrence with every pushforward, terminal return, collar and delayed gauge;
3. an exact adapter to the resident vector pole-energy consumer.

The three false transfers in the current lineage must not be reused.

**Binary falsifier:** a zero-side or arithmetic model with bounded reviewed reserves/inertia but power-sized positive innovation in the claimed norm.

### B2. Uniform-Pascal inner/all-depth SHARP

Control the inner shrinking region or compress it to finitely many low-row zero-safe sources. Outer `255/256` positivity is already established.

**Binary falsifier:** an exact negative SHARP row in the claimed all-depth range or a filter zero that cancels a hypothetical off-line zeta pole.

### B3. CN3 / adjacent-dyadic Mertens flux

Find a parity-breaking or squarefree-component coupling for the compact signed three-coordinate source. Literal divisor flow is false.

**Status warning:** CN3 is fully RH-bearing. A proof must exploit structure not already equivalent to generic Mertens control.

### B4. Factor-64 51-state Pascal payment

Pay the explicit negative reward localized to states `13,...,63` using the uniform-Pascal occupation or a stronger monotone comparison.

**Status warning:** eventual factor-64 sign is RH-equivalent. The finite payment theorem must be an independent mechanism, not a reformulation.

### B5. Complete-kernel corrected floor

Close the full source-bearing cofinal inequality

```text
B_ker-Z_ker* C^{-1} Z_ker >= -epsilon_j G_ker,
epsilon_j→0,
```

with complete packet capture, radical tails and all arithmetic sources.

**Status warning:** in the complete-capture formulation this floor is RH-bearing/equivalent. Treat it as a hostile benchmark unless a new independent arithmetic mechanism appears.

### B6. Fredholm/heat prime-side positivity

For the corrected Darboux or minimal rank-one completion, prove the full prime-side Fredholm determinant or heat-pressure sign.

Later odd-sector, sine-Cauchy and Gaussian-Pontryagin reductions are not independently reviewed in this pass.

## Priority C — finite searches and conventional progress

### C1. Authenticated finite Robin/Nicolas/Li violation

Either produce one strict exact negative Riemann-data witness or prove a cofinal theorem for the unbounded canonical class.

### C2. Pick/Loewner feature completeness

Show that an expanding exact feature hierarchy becomes complete, or find a strict negative matrix. Existing declared tables are positive.

### C3. Growing prime-resonant Zeta23 banks

Finite coherent and no-alias banks are exhausted in the two-trace architecture. A valid continuation must control a genuinely growing bank, alias tax, all cross-period terms and prime off-diagonal terms.

### C4. Short-window / growing-conductor Zeta23 extension

Complete uniform trace, zero-counting, taper, pole, end-effect, conductor and omitted-factor estimates. Upstream Lean does not cover this extension.

## Research allocation recommendation

For the next creation wave, allocate most effort to A1–A5. Keep one independent lane on B1 because the Q4 finite infrastructure is unusually developed, but do not permit another full-proof claim unless the exact integrated current consumer is resident. Maintain B5/B6 as operator benchmarks and C1/C2 as finite falsification programmes.
