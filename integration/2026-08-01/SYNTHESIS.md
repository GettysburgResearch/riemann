# Integration synthesis

## Executive boundary

The review wave establishes a large collection of exact finite algebra, finite certified computations, correction records, and conditional reductions. It does **not** establish RH or its negation.

The strongest repository-wide conclusion is organizational and mathematical:

- many finite proof consumers are sound at exact frozen commits;
- several finite positive objects are genuinely certified;
- several attractive local-to-global, density, dimension, and workflow inferences are blocked or refuted;
- every plausible global route still contains an explicit RH-bearing analytic or arithmetic step.

A review verdict below is always shorthand for “at the exact frozen commit and within the review report's scope.”

## 2. Conditional and finite results

Many reviewed statements have the valid form:

```text
strict directed finite violation
+ exact source normalization
+ complete primitive coverage
+ reviewed implication theorem
=> RH is false
```

No current object satisfies every gate with a strict negative verdict.

Likewise, several positive programmes have the valid form:

```text
cofinal lower envelope / complete kernel capture / global form-density hypothesis
+ finite Schur or Ritz certificates at every required scale
=> RH
```

The finite consumers are often correct. The cofinal or global premise remains open.

Important finite-versus-global boundaries include:

- one positive matrix does not imply positivity of the continuum form;
- one finite zero slab does not decide a global Pick, Weil, screw, or direct-ξ functional;
- one finite ladder does not prove a limit;
- existence of a passing finite level for each frozen packet does not supply a uniform growing-packet theorem;
- an exact finite vector-to-Weil dictionary is not a density theorem for the source spaces;
- a conditional RH equivalence is not verification of either side.

## 3. Proposed extensions

### 3.1 Screw and square-cutoff criteria

PR #144 proposes countable dyadic FIR and finite-element completeness interfaces. Its global version needs a genuine dense-refinement/form-domain hypothesis. PR #202 proposes square-cutoff and beta-smoothed screw/Riesz criteria. Those criteria require the review-listed analytic corrections and exact source normalization before canonical promotion.

This is a valuable connection: a proposed cofinal matrix-positivity route already contains an RH-equivalent scalar arithmetic sign problem. Conditioning and Schur bookkeeping cannot make that scalar obstruction disappear.

### 3.2 Localized-Weil complete-kernel route

PRs #152, #155, #159, #161, #163, #168, #169, #177, #179, #186, #192, #199, #204, #206, #208, and #211 develop increasingly explicit finite decompositions.

The surviving architecture is:

```text
complete localized packet
= selected-zero-visible quotient
+ selected-zero-invisible kernel
+ positive ambient complement
```

The complement can be finitely augmented and Schur-eliminated. The visible quotient can be framed by certified line-zero evaluations. The final sign sits in the complete selected-real-zero kernel. Under false RH an off-line cardinal difference gives a persistent negative direction. Thus the vanishing corrected-kernel negative part is itself RH-bearing, not a bookkeeping lemma.

### 3.3 Terminal-prime and pole-free negative route

PR #165's scalar Barta no-go is strategically important: phase-blind localization keeps the negative polar potential while discarding the compensating terminal-prime Hankel phase. PRs #177, #181, #183, #184, #190, and #200 develop the phase-aware route.

The finite disproof interface is promising but incomplete. PR #181 lacks complete zero-shell enforcement. PR #200's retained directed interval is invalid because the endpoints were serialized with round-to-nearest output. A fresh outward-serialized finite cell is required before any sign can be used.

### 3.4 Direct-ξ, Pick, Loewner, and moment routes

The current value-only feature table is substantially closed by exact feasible anchors in PR #67. Further optimization over that unchanged table is low value. New progress requires genuinely new primitive information: new exact points, direct ξ values, jets, cross-height phases, certified zero deflation, or a different finite criterion.

Positive-anchor branches #124, #128, #132, #133, and #134 contain overlapping theorem forms. The canonical choice proposed by this pass is #134 for the one-positive-node one-scalar interval; #132's genuinely additional multi-anchor ladder can survive as an adapter. Old IDs must remain accessible through aliases.

## 4. Computational evidence

The repository contains extensive reconnaissance and several certified finite computations. They must stay in different classes.

### Certified finite computation

Examples include the exact fixed-vector carrier sign in #65, exact Pick-box positivity in #68/#71, finite direct-ξ production tables in #103/#105, and several standard-library Fraction proof objects. Their conclusions are exact but finite.

### Directed but source-qualified computation

Some artifacts use directed Arb/MPFR intervals yet inherit unreviewed source normalization, incomplete coverage, or insufficient primitive authentication. Directed arithmetic does not repair a logical or provenance gap.

### Ordinary high precision and reconnaissance

PRs such as #44, #182, and #196 contain useful scheduling evidence. Their numbers are not proof objects. Precision ladders and agreement between related implementations are diagnostics, not error bounds.

### Synthetic controls

Synthetic zero models, rational matrices, and exact checker regressions validate finite algebra and fail-closed behavior. They are not zeta or ξ results.
