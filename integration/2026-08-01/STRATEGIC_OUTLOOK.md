# Strategic outlook

**Timestamp:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** repository-wide interpretation, not a proof.

RH remains unsolved. The purpose of this outlook is to identify where the reviewed programmes genuinely meet, where they are incompatible, and what exact statements would have to be proved or computed for a serious resolution.

## 1. Scalar square-screw and terminal-prime programme

### Why it is promising

The screw function and the pole-free terminal-prime statistics compress a global zero question into explicit arithmetic signals with finite prime support at each scale.

PR #202 proposes square-cutoff and beta-smoothed criteria. PR #208 identifies the square-screw scalar as a principal coordinate of the D-0001 matrix. PR #165 explains why the phase-sensitive terminal-prime block is the part discarded by scalar Barta localization. PRs #181/#183/#184/#190/#200 develop finite disproof consumers.

This is a genuine connection, not merely thematic: the matrix programme cannot prove a cofinal positive result without controlling an RH-equivalent scalar coordinate.

### A decisive negative path

A finite disproof would require:

1. a reviewed exact screw or pole-free explicit-formula normalization;
2. a fixed compact window whose transform does not cancel an off-line zero;
3. every prime power in the declared annulus;
4. proof-grade selected-zero phases and multiplicities;
5. complete remaining zero-shell and trivial-zero bounds;
6. independently directed evaluation with outward serialization;
7. a strict interval separated from the RH-valid band.

PR #181 currently fails item 5. PR #200's retained object fails item 6. A new, source-authenticated finite cell could still be decisive.

### A positive/RH path

A proof of RH through this programme would require a global or cofinal one-sided bound such as:

```text
negative part of the square-screw or beta-cell statistic = n^(o(1)),
```

or eventual nonnegativity, with exact analytic transfer to a zero-free half-plane. No current PNT, zero-density, or phase-blind estimate proves that statement. A finite positive ladder is irrelevant to this missing theorem.

### Highest-leverage next work

Repair the analytic source once, then produce one small end-to-end pole-free cell with independently authenticated prime and zero sides. This is more valuable than widening a floating search.

## 2. Complete-kernel, radical, and Schur programme

### What the repository has achieved

The positive localized-Weil route has clarified its architecture substantially:

- finite ambient complements can be reduced or augmented;
- visible quotients can be conditioned by selected line-zero frames;
- exact cardinal/radical packets can be repaired at finite support;
- Schur and triangular factorization bookkeeping is available;
- the final selected-real-zero kernel has been isolated.

PR #199 proves the key strategic boundary: eliminating a positive complement cannot repair a negative kernel direction. Under false RH an off-line cardinal difference remains negative in the complete kernel.

PR #204 gives an explicit Möbius source extension and tail. PR #206 shows how two line-zero frames produce a joint corrected-residual certificate.

### Exact missing theorem

A serious positive proof must establish, on a complete capturing hierarchy,

```text
lambda_min(
  B_kernel - Z_kernel* C_positive^{-1} Z_kernel,
  G_kernel
) >= -epsilon_j,
epsilon_j -> 0.
```

Equivalently, one must prove a uniform source/form estimate for the complete enlarged zero-invisible kernel or a theorem excluding every off-line cardinal signature.

It is not enough to prove:

- a principal angle for one packet;
- small tails for each frozen finite rank;
- a dimension inequality;
- positivity of the visible block;
- positivity of the complement;
- a finite sequence of small negative numbers.

The hierarchy must be complete, and the rate must survive growing rank/support.

### Serious possible path

The most plausible internal route is to combine:

1. source-qualified localized-Weil form/core and normalization;
2. canonical deficit augmentation from #192;
3. exact finite zero frames and right inverses from #186/#206;
4. the explicit Möbius residual from #204;
5. a weighted trace/Schatten capture estimate from #163;
6. a joint corrected-residual bound rather than absolute tail smallness;
7. a diagonal theorem that is uniform enough to produce the displayed cofinal rate.

The open step is item 5–6 in the actual zeta/Suzuki metric. It is essentially RH-bearing.

## 3. Direct-ξ, Pick, Loewner, and Stieltjes programme

### Strength

This programme has compact finite disproof predicates and mature exact consumers:

- derivative-free secants and cross-Loewner minors;
- direct completed-ξ modulus inequalities;
- certified zero and total-count deflation;
- support-gap and moment cones;
- cross-height and positive-anchor extensions.

A strict directed negative can be small and independently replayed.

### Current limitation

PR #67 gives exact feasible anchors for the existing value-only table. Optimizing more dual portfolios on that unchanged primitive set cannot produce a robust separator. Many near-negatives were precision or conditioning ghosts.

### Exact next move

A new attack must enlarge the primitive information, not merely the optimizer:

- new exact ordinate windows;
- direct ξ rather than ξ'/ξ where scale matters;
- proof-grade jets;
- cross-height complex phases;
- complete selected-zero or support-gap deflation;
- a source-authenticated smooth target rather than a hard-window surrogate.

Every candidate still needs independent completed-ξ production, exact normalization, and a fixed rational/dyadic witness with a strict outward interval.

## 4. Carrier and finite-Weil programme

### What is established

PR #65 provides a complete finite positive certificate for one historical vector. PR #79 supplies postselection/Gram machinery. PR #89 shows why a leading vector can miss a coherent rank-two endpoint crossing.

### Fundamental blocker

D-0001 remains the shared source and normalization blocker. Until its finite Guinand–Weil dictionary, source signs, admissibility, and zero-side implication are independently audited, a negative carrier matrix cannot be promoted to an RH result.

### Possible paths

A negative route could still succeed through:

- a new exact vector or positive Gram portfolio selected after complete coefficient boxes;
- coherent endpoint packets rather than isolated events;
- a source-authenticated complete matrix rather than one fixed vector.

A positive route would need cofinal matrix positivity. PR #208 shows that this contains the square-screw arithmetic sign theorem in one coordinate, so generic conditioning alone cannot close it.

## 5. Robin/Nicolas arithmetic programme

### Strength

The repository has exact finite canonical reductions and strong pruning kernels. This route is comparatively independent of ξ and zero-computation infrastructure.

### Boundary

Finite verification through `10^54` or `10^100` is not RH. A single exact Robin violation above 5040 would disprove RH, but no such violation is present.

### Exact next steps

- recover and independently replay the complete #53 terminal stream;
- strengthen shared-budget canonical pruning with a second arithmetic backend;
- develop a theorem or search architecture that reaches a structurally complete infinite class, not merely a larger finite endpoint;
- keep CA/superabundant existence quantifiers distinct from pointwise claims.

The route remains useful for finite negative search and independent cross-checks, but no reviewed asymptotic closure is present.

## 6. Connections that should guide work

### Square-screw is a principal matrix coordinate

PR #208 links the scalar arithmetic obstruction to the D-0001 matrix. A matrix proof must pay the same arithmetic debt.

### Terminal-prime phase and visible Schur block are the same type of obstruction

The scalar Barta no-go and the localized-Weil visible-block analysis both show that phase-blind lower bounds discard the information that distinguishes RH from false RH. Future estimates must retain cross phases or an equivalent signed transform.

### Selected line-zero mass is reusable positive structure

Direct-ξ zero deflation, Xi-cardinal decomposition, and line-zero frame conditioning all use the same conceptual decomposition: certified critical-line zeros contribute explicit positive mass, while the unresolved residual carries the possible off-line signature. A shared typed zero-provenance object would reduce duplicated zero work.

### Negative results are integration assets

Feasible anchors, precision-ghost refutations, Barta no-go, dimension-only capture counterexamples, and hard-window artifact dominance identify whole classes of low-value computation. They should be indexed alongside positive theorems.

## 7. Incompatible shortcuts

The following combinations are invalid unless a new theorem explicitly bridges them:

- local zero-free slab → global functional positivity or negativity;
- finite grid → all-parameter Gaussian/CND/PSD equivalence;
- arbitrary positive completion → the fixed arithmetic scalar line;
- equal dimensions → captured low spectral subspace;
- hard-window sampled Ξ → smooth CCM target;
- phase-blind scalar potential → terminal-prime Hankel control;
- internally consistent JSON → authenticated primitive data;
- finite positive ladder → cofinal lower envelope;
- later repair → retroactive verification of the frozen source claim.

## 8. Priority ranking

1. **Source and provenance repair:** D-0001, Suzuki/CCM normalization, zero/count provenance, outward interval serialization.
2. **One end-to-end finite negative-route cell:** small enough for independent prime/zero replay.
3. **Complete-kernel trace/corrected-residual theorem:** the exact RH-bearing step in the positive programme.
4. **New primitive information for direct-ξ/Pick:** avoid re-optimizing closed feature tables.
5. **Independent replay of finite arithmetic regions:** valuable, but clearly finite.

A genuine resolution path is present only in the sense that several routes have reduced RH to explicit remaining statements. None of those remaining statements is proved at the cutoff.
