# Closure attempt and actual outcome

The preceding Euler counterfamily did not preserve Lambda*1=log. The initial
attack therefore used this native identity, rather than searching for another
positive comparison sharing only one safe moment. It produced the exact renewal
in PC6. Solving that renewal by an absolute majorant subtracts positive earlier
terms and yields a divergent sum of critical weights. It supplies no new native
lower bound. Signed use of that identity remains possible and is not refuted.

A fresh reading of #805 sections 5-6 suggested retaining the distributional
second derivative instead of bounding only the first derivative. The scalar
there is not ours. For our source, prime powers generate THREE jumps rather
than one Mobius hinge. Direct calculation gives a negative center jump; dropping
it would invalidate exact interpolation. The full curvature estimate proves
fourth-power capture and, more importantly, a lower bound on the width of a
negative excursion. A failure of RH cannot hide exclusively in arbitrarily
thin arithmetic spikes.

The resulting sufficient arithmetic task is now a bound on the COUNT of
negative values. It permits some failures and imposes no bound on their depths.
However it is still of RH strength, and this pass does not prove a sublinear
failure count. No claim of probabilistic independence of prime errors, no
absolute-value replacement of the signed discrepancy, and no extrapolation
from the bounded fixtures supplies that estimate.

First open theorem:

    for every epsilon>0, N_-(M) <= C_epsilon M^(1/2+epsilon), all M>=2.

An equivalent existing alternative remains D(m)>=0 eventually at every integer
m. A third alternative is eventual D(k^2)>=0 on the predetermined fourth-power
prime grid, justified here by complete Green interpolation. None is established
unconditionally. The universal incidence/count estimate is a new direction to
attack, not a finished end-to-end solution.

No broader refutation is claimed. In particular the native divisor identity,
the full functional equation and actual prime-power multiplicative structure
have not been shown incapable of proving the required sign-count estimate.
