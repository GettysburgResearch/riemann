The accepting arithmetic is standard-library integer/Fraction, inherited
outward dyadic log/pi/harmonic enclosures from the exact authenticated parent,
and exact rational/Gaussian-rational algebra. No binary floating-point
arithmetic, optimizer, zero list or approximate special-function backend is
used by acceptance. The parent's harmonic enclosure for Euler's constant is
coarser than 160-bit precision; its FULL interval width is propagated, not
silently relabeled a 160-bit accurate source constant.

terms; they are NOT the proof of the 10^11 endpoint. The infinite zero tail,
all real m coverage, and all-exponent higher-power remainder are proved
analytically, with the finite constants checked independently.
