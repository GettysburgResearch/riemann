## Classical external inputs

Charles M. Newman and Wei Wu, *Constants of de Bruijn--Newman type in analytic
number theory and statistical physics*, arXiv:1901.06596.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.06596
The finite weighted Lee--Yang theorem and entire/weak-limit framework are
classical inputs. Primary-source metadata were checked this pass; the
paper's complete theorem proof was not newly audited. The finite
random-cluster expansion used in CJB2 is derived explicitly in PROOF.md.

Biane--Pitman--Yor, *Probability laws related to the Jacobi theta and Riemann
zeta functions, and Brownian excursions*, arXiv:math/9912170.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9912170
The exact gamma/theta source identification is inherited through the pinned
repository proofs, not asserted for arbitrary gamma laws.

Hadamard factorization, Hurwitz, Cauchy derivative bounds, elementary
Euler--Maclaurin and Brouwer fixed-point theory are standard analytic inputs.
Their specific constants, domains and remainder applications are supplied in
PROOF.md or in the explicitly authenticated parent contracts. No new formal
proof of those general theorems or broad external novelty claim is made.
