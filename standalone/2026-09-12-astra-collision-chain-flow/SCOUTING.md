# Attempt history and excluded evidence

Ordinary mpmath/SciPy calculations were used only to choose rational trial
centers and to reject unpromising searches. None is an accepting primitive.

Initial attempts to add a small interacting trimer to the finite degree-fourteen
model did not produce a certified degree-sixteen solution. A four-spin relaxed
probability model achieved a small numerical residual but lay outside the
required ferromagnetic parameter class. Neither was counted as a valid Ising
construction or a mathematical obstruction.

The first infinite-chain idea matched two/four moments and the leading h log h
growth. During this pass #871 appeared with a stronger calibrated infinite
framework. The construction was therefore revised rather than presenting the
overlapping framework as new. A two-head shifted-harmonic six-moment seed was
also obtained, but did not calibrate all three growth coefficients; it is not
the delivered main theorem.

An early calibrated scout accidentally used hand-transcribed, incorrect target
cumulants and a mismatched logarithmic-tail coefficient. High-precision
reconstruction from the actual theta scout detected that mismatch. That trial
was discarded. The final scout used the complete theta targets and the correct
d(q)=7/[8log((1+q)/2)], found the (25,4,1,1) head, and proposed its rational
centers. The delivered certificate then recomputed the native source and the
entire infinite tail independently of those floating target values.

The first exact calibrated checker stopped because its strict interval input
contract rejected an internal Boolean Kronecker delta. The delta was converted
explicitly to an integer; no numerical target or theorem was changed. The
complete Euler–Maclaurin tail includes the omitted B6 boundary term as well as
the periodic remainder. Early partial work is not counted as passing validation.

No new gamma root, root track or source-current integral was numerically
certified. The gamma work consists of analytic source/flow arguments and exact
bounded algebra, including explicit infinite positive-series tails. No native
Möbius covariance campaign beyond the stated finite crossings was run.
