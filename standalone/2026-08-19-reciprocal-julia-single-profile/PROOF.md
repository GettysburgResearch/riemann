# Reciprocal-Julia single-profile reduction for FPCB23

This standalone packet proves the exact identities in `L-99130/L-99131` and the
conditional composition in `T-99130`. The central formulas are

\[
B_z=(1-2^{-s})(1-2^{-s-1})M_z,
\qquad
R_z=\zeta M_z=G_\diamond B_z,
\]

\[
A_{2,z}=R_z-K_2B_z,
\qquad
A_{3,z}^\sharp=R_z-K_3B_z,
\]

and

\[
5A_{2,z}+A_{3,z}^\sharp=6(R_z-B_z).
\]

At `z=5`, the scalar is the positive `2,3`-smooth lift of the primitive carrier
`C_diamond=1-B_diamond`.

This is an exact one-profile reduction, not a proof of the remaining envelope.
`RJCE23`, `FPCB23`, and RH remain unproved.
