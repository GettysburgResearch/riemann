## New finite reconstruction

The new checker is standard-library integer/Fraction arithmetic only. Its
retained result has four substantive finite parts:

- Nine complete small ferromagnetic graphs, with 784 enumerated configuration
  instances. Direct normalized Gibbs summation is compared with a separately
  implemented Markov moment/derivative transfer. The 481 exact identities
  include unchanged core marginals, conditional even moments, the complete
  prefix-tail mean and cross term, row-sum variance bounds, and the derivative
  of the compensating cloud weights. Counts are finite algebra instances,
  not distinct general theorems.
- Seven finite positive-transfer products with rational exponential fields.
  Their complete partition sums are compared with outward integer-square-root
  eigenvalue enclosures. These are controls of the uniform Perron comparison,
  not numerical evaluations of the actual infinite theta-model growth.
- All 49 entries of the exact rational inverse identity for the inherited
  seven-coordinate center. The row norm and original source-box scope are
  verified from the imported, hard-pinned data. This is NOT a new theta integral.
- Eighteen named integer bounds for the explicit analytic construction at
  N=10^1000, including the moment/Jacobian perturbation ceiling and whole-box
  contraction budget. The checker does not enumerate that many spins or
  evaluate the new root. The bounds depend on the paper estimates and imported
  native interval data described in the proof.

`--emit` is producer-only. `--check result.json` first authenticates every
regular file and the exact manifest inventory, enforces the separately
hard-pinned inherited source identities, regenerates the entire finite result,
and compares canonical JSON. Duplicate keys, floats/nonfinite numbers and
Boolean/integer aliases cannot be accepted as the expected mathematical record.
There is no `assert`-dependent accepting condition.

## Numerical/analytic limits

There is no numerical completed infinite root, new moment match above14, actual
Xi zero, high-field asymptotic fit, new theta density reconstruction, or global
Ising reachability certificate. N=10^1000 is a deliberately huge sufficient
analytic construction size, not a computational campaign. The transformed
model's sixteenth moment still differs from theta. The all-order step is
explicitly OPEN-CFC, not delegated to reviewers as a routine missing detail.
