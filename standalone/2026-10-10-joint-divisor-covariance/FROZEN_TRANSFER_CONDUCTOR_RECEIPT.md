## 4. Scope bound to the optimized-transfer source

The bound checked in the committed review is the positive
all-nonzero-row envelope for the raw and full arithmetic A2
families. With \(X=\sqrt{AB}\),
\(\ell=N(q/(q,f))Nf\), and
\(m=N(q/(q,f))^{1/3}(Nf)^{2/3}\), it is

\[
D^\epsilon\left[
HX+\ell H^2X^{5/12}U^{7/4}
+mH^{4/3}X^{2/3}U^2
+H^{1/6}X^2U^{-3}
+H^{2/3}X^{4/3}U^{-2}
\right].
\]

The review covers arbitrary rectangles within the written
smooth-scale contract, the exact mixed cube inverse, every
positive cutoff \(U\) including \(U<1\), all physical rows,
and both orientations of the raw rectangle. It checks the
moving masks and the correction labels

\[
q_t=qcde,\qquad f_t=ef,\qquad
X_t=\frac{X}{(Nc\,Nd)^{3/2}(Ne)^2},\qquad
U_t=\frac{U}{(Nc\,Nd)^{1/2}}.
\]

It checks every one of the five strictly convergent correction
norm sums, their normalization, and the displayed rational
optimization for \(q=f=1\). In particular the full arithmetic
A2 completion inherits the short/long raw envelope without a
polynomial loss from its correction labels.

This remains conditional on the stated mixed completed input,
