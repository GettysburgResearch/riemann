# Research decision after the critique

The critique's central distinction is correct: a source-convergence estimate, a stable finite inverse, or a reformulation of an RH-equivalent sign does not establish progress on that sign. The preceding beta-response packet (#884) proves a response theorem and a local motion calculation, not global zero confinement. This continuation does not describe those as an almost-complete RH proof.

This pass therefore targets a stated unconditional zeta quantity: the lower proportion of zeros that are simultaneously simple and on the critical line. The inherited seven-point geometry has already produced an external 67.3 percent lift and a repo 280-block refinement. Those are the benchmark, not discoveries to be claimed again.

The improvement proposed here comes from a specific loss in their proof: fixed block sizes were used to turn pair energy into the capped spectral defect. A sinc-square majorant makes that conversion exact on arbitrary separated sets. Nearby pairs can be paid separately. This removes the artificial block-size cutoff while retaining the actual source and full trace-norm comparison.

This does not establish RH. It changes a quantitative, unconditional conclusion rather than moving the same unproved premise to a new notation.

Some statements in the critique should not be adopted literally. A finite Gram upper approximation can bound the intrinsic entropy FROM ABOVE; it cannot in general force it to vanish. An absolute approximation error also does not imply that every growing window is invisible: it can give quantitative tracking of the actual zeros. Neither correction supplies zero centrality. They do not alter the main strategic diagnosis.

The present work does not verify every cited standing record, historical priority assertion or seven-thread summary. Lamzouri's published abstract confirms the 67.25 percent simple-critical baseline, and the actual matrix/trace inputs used here are read from the original primary proof. No competitive claim about one model or company settling RH is made.

Recommended disposition: review the new finite majorant/matching theorem and its complete zeta adapter first. If sound, it is a concrete proportional improvement. Further work could strengthen P7 or use the positive close-pair bonus; no lower bound for that bonus's occurrence among actual zeros is supplied here. Do not return to a global RH claim merely because the new constant is larger.
