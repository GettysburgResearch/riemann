# Independent review specification

## Frozen target

Review `T-91660` as a candidate proof of SONTR, not as an already accepted proof
of RH.

## Mandatory reconstruction order

### 1. Strict source boundary

Verify

\[
 K=\lfloor X/67\rfloor+1
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X/K<67,
\]

and confirm that every root squarefree divisor belongs to `P_61`, while the
prime `67` remains rough-owned.

### 2. Root target Hall

Recompute every active odd threshold and verify the uniform lower bound `7/20`.
Check the score/target derivative orientation and the row/target monotonicity
orientation. Reject the packet if any coordinate uses different Hall
coefficients.

### 3. Source ownership

Trace the product label

```text
endpoint fiber / P61 divisor / first rough owner / causal channel.
```

Reject on any duplicated label, on any root use of a rough prime, or on any
child ownership of a root row bonus.

### 4. Inner residual

Reconstruct the exact finite forcing plus delayed rough-copy identity below
`K`. Verify that the inner packet is retained recursively and is not replaced by
a continuum approximation or charged a second mismatch.

### 5. Finite realization

Recompute `C67<19`, the constants `5655/32<177`, `4452<5033`, and the safety
factor `(1+178/K)^(-1)`. Verify detail feasibility directly; use the positive
radix-four inverse for ordinary feasibility.

### 6. One global correction owner

Confirm that quantization, mismatch, safety, omission, taper, base correction
and the shared port are applied after the common parent sum and exactly once.
Do not infer detail order by subtracting unrelated ordinary inequalities.

### 7. Score and endpoint consumer

Reconstruct the bounded local correction debt, the subcritical recurrence and
the exact native benchmark conversion

\[
J_\Lambda(X)-\mathcal H(d_X)
\le4\log X+O(1).
\]

Finally verify the one-sided endpoint-to-RH sign orientation independently.
