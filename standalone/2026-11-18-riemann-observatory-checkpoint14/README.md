## Mathematical scope

The analytic work treats an explicitly specified Mellin strip norm and local test class. It distinguishes the universal ordinary symmetric signed threshold `p >= 0` from the absolute sampling threshold `p > 3`. The negative-log construction concerns one smooth test in every strictly weaker logarithmic space; the archived hypotheses, summation convention and proof must be reviewed together. Primary local claim addresses include `OBS-MELLIN-ALL-NEGATIVE-LOG-SIGNED-DIVERGENCE` and `THM-MELLIN-EXACT-SIGNED-LOG-BOUNDARY`.

The arithmetic constructions concern common bounded real or integer sequences and discrepancy budgets centered at `P/k` or each row's induced unit mean. They do not establish the corresponding prime-supported, `P/phi(k)`-centered statement or a lower bound for the full smoothed prime sum. Claims and findings retain their source-version and inspected-portion boundaries. Nothing here claims an RH proof/disproof, an improved zero-free or zero-density exponent, or established publication priority.

All local paper, theorem, connection and finding identifiers are scoped to this imported archive and its version. They are not entries in this repository's canonical claim registry. This deposit is separate from the archive proposed in [PR #1030](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/1030).

## Verify the import

From this package directory, with Python 3.10 or newer:

```sh
python3 -B verify_checkpoint.py
```

This standard-library verifier authenticates the fixed ZIP digest and size, exact extracted file set and bytes, manifest, archived structural consistency, rendered artifacts and all 24 source-code pins. A code pin authenticates the recorded producer's bytes; it does not rerun its computation. The historical structural PASS receipt is checked as a receipt, not independently reproduced by that check alone. See [reproducibility instructions](archive/riemann-observatory/experiments/reproducibility.md) for individual experiments and their arithmetic classes. Rebuilding, rerunning formal projects and fetching primary source caches are separate tasks.

Post-seal verification and delivery records live in [verification/](verification/). Their historical absolute workspace paths describe the original run, not required paths in a fresh clone. The wrapper verifier uses package-relative paths. The final QA and delivery records were produced after the seal and are deliberately outside the original ZIP.
