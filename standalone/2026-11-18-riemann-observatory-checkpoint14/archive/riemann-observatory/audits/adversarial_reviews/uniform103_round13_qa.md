## Exact threshold on a prescribed uniform mesh

The universal guarantee over all grids leaves a separate question for a specified grid sequence. The first tractable case, equally spaced grids, has a sharp criterion. Keep the hypotheses of Uniform85 and prescribe pitches \(d_n=1/m_n\) with positive integers \(m_n\). Write
\[
 b_*=\min(b,p),\qquad A_*=A_n d_n^{(b-p)_+},\qquad
 W_n=\max\{d_n,(\epsilon_n/A_*)^{1/b_*}\}.
\]
Then tightness of polygonal laws for every admissible array on these particular grids, with each array's own tight initial marginals, is equivalent to \(\epsilon_n/W_n\to0\). This assertion is sequencewise and is not a necessary condition for any given stochastic array.

For the upper bound, if \(b>p\), adjacent increments satisfy \(\|\Delta X\|_p\le C^{1/p}A_n^{1/p}d_n^{b/p}\). Over \(h/d_n\) adjacent increments, Minkowski therefore gives the effective fine bound \(CA_*h^p\) at all nodes. For \(b\le p\) the fine bound remains \(CA_*h^{b_*}\). If its crossover \((\epsilon_n/A_*)^{1/b_*}\) is at least the pitch and at most one, apply the two-scale proof with the effective fine bound. That proof uses a crossover in \((0,1]\) and otherwise makes no use of \(A_*\ge1\). If the effective crossover exceeds one, the row's full continuous increment bound is \(O(A_*h^{b_*})\) by fractional interpolation, and \(A_*<\epsilon_n\) gives vanishing oscillation in \(L^p\) by ordinary dyadic chaining. If the crossover is smaller than the pitch, use only the coarse estimate. For increments inside a fractional cell the error term becomes \(\epsilon_n(h/d_n)^p\) for \(h<d_n\); the interval splitting argument supplies the continuous error envelope \(\epsilon_n\min(1,(h/d_n)^p)\). Chaining makes the corresponding uniform error \(O((\epsilon_n/d_n)^{1/p})\). All three cases give the desired tightness.

For the lower bound, on any failing subsequence \(\epsilon_n/W_n\ge c>0\), whence \(W_n\to0\). Choose a radius \(D_n\) that is an integer multiple of \(d_n\) with \(W_n\le D_n\le 2W_n\). Put a tent of fixed height \(v\) and radius \(D_n\) at a uniformly chosen interior grid node at least \(D_n\) from the endpoints. At most \(O(D_n/d_n)\) possible centers affect a given endpoint and the number of eligible centers is comparable to \(1/d_n\). The absolute difference is at most \(v\min(1,h/D_n)\). Taking the \(p\)-th power and averaging gives
\[
 E|\Delta X|^p\ll v^p D_n\min(1,(h/D_n)^p).
\]
This is \(O(v^p\epsilon_n/c)\) for the coarse bound. Also \(A_* D_n^{b_*}\ge\epsilon_n\), and \(p\ge b_*\), giving the fine bound in the effective exponent up to the same fixed factor \(O(v^p/c)\). When \(b>p\), the effective bound is stronger than the original at any node distance \(h\ge d_n\), since \(A_*h^p\le A_n h^b\). Choose \(v\) small enough for the given \(C\). As in the classical triangular example, fixed marginals vanish while the paths retain height \(v\) over shrinking intervals, so tightness fails.

For example, take \(p=2,b=3,\epsilon_n=n^{-1},A_n=n^2\), so the arbitrary-grid criterion fails at equality. Pitch \(d_n=n^{-4}\) gives \(A_*=n^{-2}\) and a crossover greater than one, forcing the oscillation to vanish on that prescribed grid. At pitch of order \(n^{-1/2}\), the pitch dominates the original crossover and the coarse error divided by pitch tends to zero. At pitch \(n^{-1}\), the threshold fails and the random tent construction applies. Both unusually fine and relatively sparse grids can improve the guarantee when \(b>p\).

The same geometric input suggests a question for nonuniform grids. Can a local chain cost for superlinear powers and a packing bound for admissible tent centers characterize all arrays on a prescribed nonuniform mesh, with a comparable upper modulus? Uniform GRR controls the arbitrary-grid upper bound and Billingsley gives the original shrinking-triangle mechanism. These sources establish neither priority nor an earlier statement for the mesh-dependent formula here. This complement is derived in this note, with no new priority claim and no canonical admission requested. [Classical antecedents](uniform89_tightness_priority.md)
