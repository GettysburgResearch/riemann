## von Mangoldt's grouped zero remainder

Accepted THM-HR424, with the fixed-x and fixed positive contour-margin hypotheses. For beta_(j,±)=1/2-r±i alpha_j, local unit-interval count O(log gamma), bounded imaginary parts of alpha and positive lower ordinates, the exact identity J_h(beta)=[P_h(beta)-P_h(0)]/beta and integration by parts give uniform Perron errors with denominators 1+|h-gamma|. They stay bounded at a cutoff tie. Pairing the reciprocal beta terms is essential: their tail is O(Sum gamma^(-2)), while an unpaired harmonic tail need not converge. Splitting above/below h and near/far h/2 gives total grouped remainder O(log²h/h). This proves an increasing-height grouped-limit implication, conditional on the separately established limit of the full finite-height integral.
