## Proof and historical boundaries

- The Hankel argument uses a fixed small inner circle for continuation, a specified cut and outer contours separated from the pole lattice. It keeps the domain of the original real Mellin integral distinct from analytic continuation. The count section treats argument variation as a later proof dependency and separates the total count from the asserted abundance of real roots and RH.
- The \(G(w)=\Xi(\sqrt w)\) route is valid: the even Taylor series gives an entire function, and the theta estimate \(\log M_\Xi(R)=O(R\log(R+2))\) yields order at most \(1/2\) in \(w\). Genus zero and \(G(0)>0\) give the oppositely paired product, with multiplicities. The positive differentiated theta kernel and the nonnegative cosine on imaginary \(t\) indeed exclude purely imaginary centered zeros. No zero count asymptotic or RH is used in that repair.
- The distributional inverse needs the paired logarithm's polynomial majorant on a fixed right line, and the later ordinary von Mangoldt height formula plus the normally summable error is what identifies the pointwise midpoint representative. The \(O(\log T)\) local count and \(O_x(1/T)\) individual estimates justify the source's magnitude cutoff. The branch offsets cancel under conjugation. The corrected count uses \(-\log 2\), and no global logarithm branch is asserted.
- Finite Möbius inversion is an exact arithmetic identity. The last differentiated expression, tabulation and empirical weighted-count comparison are not promoted to asymptotic bounds. The supplementary rearrangement justification is credited to later arguments, not to the original proof.
