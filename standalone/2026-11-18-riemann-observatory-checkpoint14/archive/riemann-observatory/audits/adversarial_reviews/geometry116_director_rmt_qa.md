## Hankel cutoff and the empty band

If either Hankel row or column is at least \(M_{\rm cut}\), its Fourier index \(i+j+1\) is at least \(M_{\rm cut}+1\). Hence the first possibly omitted term in the Laurent exponential has order \(\lfloor M_{\rm cut}/k_*\rfloor+1\). The derivative-count factor in the convolution seminorm reduces the factorial by one index, giving the director's sum starting at \(\lfloor M_{\rm cut}/k_*\rfloor\). Interpolation between the positive compressions gives the claimed bound on log determinants with reciprocal common spectral lower bound, and log quotients add the two errors. Because \(H_N\) is uniformly bounded at fixed positive band, these constants are uniform on real compact \(z\) intervals.
