# Source and constant ledger for the fixed \(Q_2\) polynomial

18 November 2026. The numerical leading and lower-order coefficients are calculated by exact symbolic arithmetic. The error estimates retain their asymptotic conventions. Removing the nonprincipal contribution removes the Siegel step. It does not by itself provide numerical values for the stationary-phase or twisted continuous moment constants.

The principal RH contour in the companion proof avoids bounding \(1/\zeta\) by exploiting the auxiliary derivative before the shift. Only the classical log derivative on a fixed strip away from the critical zeros is needed, together with the truncated Euler formula for the two zeta factors. These are candidates for explicit arithmetic with existing dossier constants. The \(7/8+\varepsilon\) error exponent is a proved profile, but the scale and threshold for a practical enclosure still depend on the indicated numerical inputs, notably the explicit constants in the direct sharp-cutoff argument and stationary phase.
