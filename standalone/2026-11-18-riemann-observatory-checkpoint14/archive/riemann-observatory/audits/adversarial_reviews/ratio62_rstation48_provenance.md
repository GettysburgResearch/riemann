# Provenance of the claimed RH-only optimized Nyman–Beurling rate

Checked 2026-11-17 UTC. The primary manuscript's Theorem 1 claims that under RH the anchored and unrestricted optimized squared distances are each asymptotic to (log N)^(-1) times the sum of m(ρ)²/|ρ|² over distinct zeros. The construction alters the coefficients through zero-dependent corrections. I found no independent source with the same upper bound and hypotheses in the inspected citations. The platform Accept provides a public review status. The proof remains under independent audit and I found no corresponding journal or arXiv record under the exact title in the searches performed. ## Published mathematical ingredients

- Burnol's lower bound involves the multiplicity-squared sum. Bettin–Conrey–Farmer Theorem 1 obtains the matching first-order constant for the prescribed log taper under RH and an inverse derivative second moment O(T^(3/2−δ)). Proposition-level high contour and summation estimates in this line use the added moment. Maier–Rassias Theorem 1.1, confirmed from the six-page Cambridge PDF, imposes the same inverse derivative moment and a specific quartet of off-line zeros. It studies the full error of the same taper, with oscillatory terms at the off-line heights. It supplies no RH-only upper theorem for the optimized distance. [Burnol](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0103058) · [BCF](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.5191) · [Maier–Rassias](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-2017-070-3)
- Ng Lemma 3 gives good heights in intervals [n,n+1] and reciprocal control across the indicated bounded strip. Ng's summatory Möbius theorems separately impose RH and J_{−1}(T)≪T. Paper 48 §2.1 averages the RH Hadamard-product log defect on an arbitrary fixed length interval. Inoue gives reciprocal partial fractions and multiplicity-aware explicit formulae. These sources provide contour and reciprocal-zeta precedents. The cited passages do not realize the delayed cluster profile as a degree-N approximant in the original space. [Ng](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0310381) · [Inoue](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.00853)
- Balazard–de Roton Proposition 8 is used for short fixed-interval counts with multiplicity. Their own upper-bound theorem uses a shifted Möbius polynomial with a substantially larger error than 1/log N. Oliveira's fixed-polynomial setting and Alouges–Darses–Hillion's generalized kernels define different approximation problems. ## Spot check of the claimed arithmetic realization

Assuming the contour majorant and representative density in §2, the differentiated collective integrand in (54) costs H^(2+e)(1+|t|) y^(−2+δ). The profile vanishes until y=N^b and at the upper endpoint, and it is continuous at the joins. Summing quadrature errors interval by interval gives (58) by total variation. The exponent in the low weighted squared norm is (4+2e)a−2b(1−δ)+(1+2e)c = −0.1499595 with the printed parameters.
