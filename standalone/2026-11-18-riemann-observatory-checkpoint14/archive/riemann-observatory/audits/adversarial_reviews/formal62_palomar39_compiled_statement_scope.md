## Separate curve-boundary queries

The dedicated cached Lean query exited zero with both expected types and exactly propext, Classical.choice, and Quot.sound for both. [Raw type and axiom output](../../experiments/results/formal62_palomar39_curve_boundary_query.json) · [Query source](../../experiments/code/Palomar39CurveBoundaryAxioms.lean)

Let E be the exact piecewise combinedDensityExponent and a = 3/4. The first theorem says E(σ) < 1/2 for every real σ > a. The second says that for every real K there is an a < σ ≤ 1 such that E(a) − E(σ) > K(σ−a). Since E(a) = 9/16, the jump prevents a finite one-sided linear modulus across the join in that direction. A closed-interval monotone left-grid interpolation would use such a modulus to control the difference between the exponent at a grid point to the left and at the evaluation point. This obstructs that interpolation of this particular piecewise exponent. The separate global uniform theorem uses the smooth exponent (39/17)(1−σ). Other arguments for uniform estimates with the exact piecewise exponent remain open in this audit.
