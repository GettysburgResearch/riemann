# Challenge to the joint phase-kernel decay in de Roton (2006)

The printed Proposition 5.1, equation (12), pp.432–433, claims the same $O(x^{-1/2-1/(2d)})$ tail for $\varphi=U_F\chi$ and $\Phi=x\varphi'$. For zeta, Burnol's explicit $\varphi(x)=\sin(2\pi x)/(\pi x)$ gives $\Phi(x)=2\cos(2\pi x)-\varphi(x)$. In particular $\Phi(n)=2$ at every positive integer, and $\Phi\notin L^2(1,\infty)$ because a fixed-width neighborhood of each sufficiently large integer keeps the cosine away from zero. This contradicts the joint decay and blocks the specific citation of Proposition 4.1 in the original phase-to-jet step. [de Roton pp.432–433](../sources/prefix_formula62_generalL/deroton_lower.pdf) · [Burnol explicit phase kernel](../sources/equivalences/burnol.pdf)

The exact place is p.433 after $G_k=-xH_k'$. The Bessel derivative changes the power by $+1/d$ relative to the first Bessel term. Keeping this factor proves the claimed $\varphi$ decay for $k=0$. It gives a weaker $\Phi$ estimate for $k=1$. One can avoid the latter entirely. For $\Re w<1/2$, use the Bochner identity $\chi x^{-w}=\chi+w\int_0^1 v^{-w-1}{\bf1}_{(0,v)}\,dv$. The indicator has norm $\sqrt v$, and dilation invariance sends it to $\varphi(x/v)$. Differentiate in $w$ on compact sets and analytically continue the resulting pointwise integral by the $\varphi$ tail. This recovers the same cutoff jet model using $\varphi$ alone. The lower-bound theorem survives. The associated Hardy-derivative correction was adjudicated separately.
