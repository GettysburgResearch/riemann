# Priority and source check for the weighted Dirichlet closure

Search date 2026-11-17. The [director adjudication](adversarial_reviews/weighted_criterion_director_round5.md) gives the corrected theorem and counterexample. The closest antecedents establish the weighted fractional-part closure step. A 2026 platform preprint expressly questions the proof of the published reverse implication. In the inspected sources I found no statement with the same two-parameter weighted Dirichlet norm and the corrected threshold \(b=(1-\alpha)/2-a\), or with the counterexample at \((\alpha,a)=(-3,7/4)\). Historical priority for those parts remains unresolved.

## Prior criticism and overlap

The platform source *The endpoint case of the discrete Beurling criterion in weighted \(L^p\) and Lorentz spaces*, by span_of_dilates, [rStation paper 63 v3](https://rstation.ai/problems/riemann_hypothesis/papers/63/v3), appears on the public page with a date of 28 October 2026 and version v3. The [frozen public Markdown](../sources/ratio62_weightedprior/rstation_63_v3.md) has SHA-256 3907ee0125e47822cc11d3bbc889215e40fde3c989fc5c71ffa436bc914225e2. The same criticism already appears in the downloaded v1 and v2. The associated platform reviewer report says Accept. The [platform](https://rstation.ai/) describes itself as a research community for AI agents. No journal publication or external peer review was verified.

- Section 1 names Gallardo–Gutiérrez–Seco Theorem 5.5 and identifies the appeal to the converse of their Theorem 2.1 as a missing proof. That is prior explicit criticism of the published proof. The paper gives no direct counterexample at \((-3,7/4)\), radial witness, or corrected \(a\)-dependent threshold.
- Theorem 1 uses \(I_m=(1/(m+1),1/m]\), \(\rho_{1/k}(t)=\{1/(kt)\}-k^{-1}\{1/t\}\), and \(L^p((0,1),t^\gamma dt)\). Its range includes \(p=2,\ \gamma=2b-1,\ 1/2\le b<1\), for which \(\theta=(\gamma+1)/p=b\). It proves the exact arithmetic-core closure equivalence for constant 1 and these constrained generators with strict half-plane \(\Re s>b\). On \(I_m\) the generators equal \(\{m/k\}\), with \(\int_{I_m}t^{2b-1}dt\asymp m^{-1-2b}\). This supplies the coefficient-space approximation used in the reconstructed sufficiency. Its transfer to the weighted Dirichlet space uses the bounded multiplier and \(T_a\) operator. The endpoint \(b=1\) lies outside rStation's weighted \(L^2\) range.
- Proposition 3 attributes the Hilbert case also to [Dimitrov–Oliveira, Theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.07887). The actual preprint's Theorem 2, pp. 2–3, uses \(p_{\rm DO}\in(1,2]\), Dirichlet polynomials \(A_N\), and boundary norm \((2\pi)^{-1}\int |(1-A_N\zeta)(s)/s|^2\,dt\) at \(\Re s=1/p_{\rm DO}\), upon taking the principal character modulo 1. Its proof uses a shifted Dirichlet \(L\)-function, damped sums, and equivalent Cauchy weights under the line shift. Taking \(p_{\rm DO}=1/b\) gives the exact needed line and measure. Theorem 2 allows \(A_N(1)\ne0\).
- The transfer in rStation Proposition 3 checks directly. On \(\Re s=b<1\), the Mellin inverse of \(\zeta(s)A_N(s)/s\) is the negative sum of unanchored sawtooths. The tail on \(t>1\) is a multiple of \(t^{-1}\) with scalar of modulus \(|A_N(1)|\). In \(L^2(t^{2b-1}dt)\), its norm is \(|A_N(1)|/\sqrt{2-2b}\) and it is orthogonal to the error on \((0,1)\). Replacing \(A_N\) by \(A_N-A_N(1)\) cancels that tail and adds an error on \((0,1)\) of at most \(|A_N(1)|/\sqrt{2b}\), because \(\{1/t\}\le1\). Cauchy–Schwarz gives \(d_{\rm DO}\le d_{\rm anchored}\le b^{-1/2}d_{\rm DO}\) for every cutoff. The earlier unconstrained theorem therefore already supplies the arithmetic-core implication.

## Balazard–Saias Lemma 2

The 1998 Adv. Math. article is [PAPER-E007](../papers/PAPER-E007.md), DOI [10.1006/aima.1998.1760](https://doi.org/10.1006/aima.1998.1760), PII S0001870898917601. [PAPER-C101](../papers/PAPER-C101.md) is the authors' distinct 2000 exposition and cites E007.

The [Academia reproduction](https://www.academia.edu/114375539/Notes_sur_la_fonction%CE%B6de_Riemann_1) gives the journal header, article identifier and text of §2, pp. 315–316. Lemme 2 allows arbitrary zero-free threshold \(1/2\le b<1\), and proves an error of order \(x^{-\delta/3}(1+|t|)^\eta\) uniformly when \(x\ge2,\ b+\delta\le\sigma\le1\) for fixed positive \(\delta,\eta\). Its proof uses effective Perron inversion starting at \(1-\sigma+1/\log x\), moves to \(b-\sigma+\delta/2\), and takes the residue at 0. It controls the other three integrals by Lemme 1 on a strip separated from the zero-free boundary. The vertical bound has the form \(x^{-\delta/2}\log T[T^\nu+(1+|t|)^\nu]\). The horizontal bounds have the form \(x^{1-\sigma}[T^\nu+(1+|t|)^\nu]/T\). In the contour bounds one may take \(T=x^B\) with \(B>1+\delta\), then choose \(\nu<\min(\eta,\delta/(12B),1/4)\) as small as needed. The losses then preserve the claimed power saving, as in the existing [scoped proof audit](baez_uniform_dependency_round2.md). The extracted full Perron error display remains degraded.

The complete lemma statement and attribution are readable in Báez-Duarte's [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0202141v2), Lemma 2.1 on p. 3, equation (2.1), and the [2003 journal scan](https://www.bdim.eu/item?fmt=pdf&id=RLIN_2003_9_14_1_5_0), Lemma 1 on p. 6, equation (2.1). Both confirm the fixed-gap uniformity used in the reconstruction. The new [PAPER-E014](../papers/PAPER-E014.md) records the original Báez-Duarte preprint and journal relation.

The Elsevier article and PDF endpoints, repository-targeted searches and author listings yielded no formula-complete original PDF for E007. The Academia PDF button requires login. The independently checked quotation and the original's degraded HTML reproduction are distinct evidence.
