# Prior pointers for the repaired first-order operator

Date:17November2026. No additional selected work or whole-paper reading is claimed by this log.

The coefficient Hardy repair ofT_a suggests comparison with classical Cesàro/Volterra resolvent theory. The exact analytic identity is

f−f(0)=J(T_af)+a J(f/(1−z)), Jg(z)=integral_0^z g(w)dw.

This gives a concrete dictionary to the Volterra operator with symbol−log(1−z), with a finite-dimensional initial-value coordinate. It does **not** by itself transfer a published spectrum or establish matching Banach-space hypotheses.
