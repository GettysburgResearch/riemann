# Search log for the horizontal ratio slope

Execution date 2026-11-17. Scope and source judgments appear in the [audit](../audits/horizontal_ratio_slope_priority_ratio62.md). A checked passage is limited to the indicated result, even when the full paper was obtainable.

## Executed search families

- Exact problem, including English and French spellings. Queries included `"Balazard" "Roton" "precise asymptotic" zeta`, `"Balazard" "Roton" zeta ratio asymptotic J epsilon`, `"Sur un critère" "Jε" Balazard de Roton`, `"préciser le comportement asymptotique de" "de Roton" zeta`, `"0812.1689" Beurling rate asymptotic zeta` and `"S1793042110003307" zeta cited by asymptotic`. Read arXiv v1 §§2–4 and checked the official DOI metadata, HAL author's listing and zbMATH record.
- Burnol and Nyman–Beurling follow-ups. Queries for Burnol's analytic estimate, Hardy spaces, his bibliography, Bettin–Conrey–Farmer, Balazard's later arithmetic criterion, de Roton's habilitation and sequential Selberg-class generalization, and probabilistic Nyman–Beurling follow-ups. The University of Lille author page links to arXiv and to Burnol's personal bibliography. His personal website contains 2026 updates but is not a complete citation index.
- Ratio and negative-moment literature. Queries for `Riemann zeta quotient horizontal shift L2 critical line negative moments small shifts ratio`, `Riemann zeta ratio limit small horizontal shift multiplicities Nyman Beurling`, `"Riemann" "zeta" "small horizontal shift" ratio`, Rodgers' local ratios work, Farmer's distinct-zero paper, Bui–Florea negative moments, and `site:arxiv.org "zeta" "small shifts" ratios 2025 2026 critical line`. The matched modern papers concern high-height uniform means or microscopic asymptotics, rather than the fixed all-height weight. Rodgers v3 and Bui–Florea §1 were checked against their actual formulas. Farmer's [1995 journal paper](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v2i1r1/pdf/) proves lower bounds for counts of distinct zeros from counts of simple zeros of derivatives, with no ratio norm in the conclusion.
- General Hardy-space or real-zero tools. Queries combined outer functions, horizontal translation, Poisson weights, boundary defects, Clark measures, Laguerre–Pólya products, central binomial coefficients and boundary-root multiplicities. These surfaced standard ingredients and neighboring results but no primary theorem statement with the matching first variation. A complementary fresh-date query found Wunderlich's October 2026 Jensen and entropy paper, which was checked at its stated identities.
- OpenAlex, Crossref, zbMATH and site-restricted arXiv or rStation. The pre-existing [OpenAlex docket](../sources/ratio_slope_prior_round4/c103_forward_citations.json) has nine records, used below as a finite lead list. Crossref reports four references *to* the DOI in its index at retrieval. Its 17 outbound entries are distinct from that count. zbMATH Open's DOI search succeeds and returns Zbl document ID 5758250, title, year, contributors and linked references. The attempted standalone forward-index query timed out and does not establish the existence or absence of a zbMATH forward set. The tool also surfaced rStation paper 1/v11, which was already compared in the [independent skeptic audit](../audits/adversarial_reviews/horizontal_ratio_slope_skeptic_round4.md).
- Exploratory neighboring sources. Balazard's [arithmetic-function paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.04309) §5 uses orthogonal projections in the Weingartner form. Chhaibi–Najnudel–Nikeghbali [arXiv:1410.1440v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.1440) studies microscopic random matrix limits and ratio conjectures. These were screened at the scope and theorem level. The 2010 *Gazette* survey by Balazard was confirmed in the [SMF issue contents](https://smf.emath.fr/publications/la-gazette-des-mathematiciens-126-octobre-2010). A Numdam URL returned the 2021 Darses–Hillion paper and was discarded as evidence for the 2010 survey.
