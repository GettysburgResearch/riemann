## Fixed-half-plane audit handoff

The latest inspected manuscript states an unconditional fixed zero-free half-plane for all continuous unitary Hecke characters over all number fields. Such a theorem would exclude any exceptional real zero above the stated boundary. It is considerably stronger than a sharpening of a classical logarithmic zero-free region. The source claim remains pending a full independent audit.

The shortest path to the zeta consequence is the finite-order Theorem 8.14 with \(K=\mathbb Q\) and the principal character. Start with the joint moment bound in Theorem 8.12, rather than the later unitary theorem. In that bound, check the inverse and ordinary induction in §§8.5–8.9, particularly the fourth-power selection, the two Poisson comparisons, the fixed reference exponent \(51/49\), imprimitive zero extensions, common support and the ordinary-moment restoration. The fourteen strict constraints in §8.7 depend on inputs already established at each finite stage. Check that the model \(0.8733468\) from the cited Dirichlet PR 137 supplies the scalar construction only, and that the number-field moment induction actually supplies every analytic hypothesis for the Hecke coefficients.

Theorem 8.14 additionally uses the earlier bound in Liu's Hexagon 2611.00082v1 to fix \(\kappa_1=374887/500000\), then calls the earlier parametric Mellin comparison and continuation. The next check is to verify the whole numerator and nonvanishing Euler factor before moving contours and taking positive moments, and to confirm the earlier comparison is established with exactly the coefficients, moving moduli and uniform height losses claimed. The numerical infimum proof cannot substitute for either analytic input.

The general unitary branch additionally uses Gao Theorem 1 and its named conditional analytic inputs, plus Hu's theta comparison and principal-factor estimates. Proposition 9.2 claims that the paired unitary moment family survives the exceptional terms and all strict-stage inequalities. Theorem 9.3 requires a uniform positive saving across the fixed target family while allowing target-dependent seminorm and height degrees. The Weil extension in Corollary 9.5 uses virtual induction and exact norm-character pole accounting. These branches are stronger than the zeta consequence and can be reviewed separately.
