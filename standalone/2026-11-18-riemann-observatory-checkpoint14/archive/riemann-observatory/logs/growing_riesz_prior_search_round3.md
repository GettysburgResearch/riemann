Search cutoff:17November2026. This is a **targeted incomplete priority search**, not a claim of historical novelty. Searches were performed while reconstructing THM-E-W307–W313. Abstract/search hits were not treated as theorem audits.

Queries included exact combinations of “Möbius”, “logarithmic Riesz”, “Riesz means”, “log(x/n)”, “order”, “log log”, “Nyman”, “growing”, “varying order”, “increasing order”, “Dirichlet”, and “summability”. That negative search result is not a priority conclusion.

## Actual primary passages inspected

* PAPER-M002, Hardy–Riesz1915, printedpp21–23 and49–51: positive real-order typical means; explicit first-kind logarithmic versus second-kind arithmetic distinction; Theorem39/Lemma10 and shifted formula give the initial inverse-Laplace contour; Theorem40 gives the different Gamma quotient. Actual Cornell facsimile downloaded and title/series pages visually checked. This is the direct classical source for the method, not a newly invented summation scheme.
* PAPER-E011, Inoue arXiv1705.00853v2, introductionpp1–3 andLemma6/proofpp13–14: fixed **arithmetic** Riesz means and a multiplicity ceiling from a separate logarithmic growth exponent; a varying arithmetic-order conjecture is introduced afterTheorem2. The kernel differs from the logarithmic mean. The journal metadata is2019; the mathematical version actually read is2017v2.
* PAPER-C102, Conrey–Myerson arXivmath/0002254v1, pp1–3: the first-order Selberg logarithmic mollifier and fractional-part norm context. No moving-order theorem appears in those inspected pages; this statement does not describe uninspected portions as audited.
* PAPER-E001/PAPER-E007 and the audited Titchmarsh passages: RH-conditional exponential damping and uniform reciprocal-zeta/Perron estimates form the nearby constructive precedent. The new array keeps the same critical-line approximation purpose; no new RH implication beyond a standard converse is claimed.
* PAPER-E010 gives direct polynomial/Riesz approximation background. Its inspected fixed-polynomial constants are not collision-uniform, and equivalent norms must be distinguished from exact H² optima.
