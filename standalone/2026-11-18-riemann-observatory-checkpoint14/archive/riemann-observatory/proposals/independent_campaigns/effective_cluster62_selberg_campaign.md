# Fixed Selberg-class transfer

This campaign checks an explicit analytic interface for the retained-edge adaptive upper construction. Under GRH, each fixed Selberg-class function meets the interface after choosing the contour mesh in terms of its degree. The transfer permits arbitrary fixed multiplicities and a pole of any fixed order at 1.

A distributional Mellin shift proves that the complementary function belongs to \(L^2\) under the resulting critical-line growth bound. De Roton’s lower theorem then matches the transferred upper bound in the unanchored boundary norm. An exact higher-order pole calculation identifies the part of the complementary function supported at \(t>1\).
