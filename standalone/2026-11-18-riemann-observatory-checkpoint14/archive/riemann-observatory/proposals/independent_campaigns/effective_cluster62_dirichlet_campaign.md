# Fixed-character adaptive upper bound

The retained-edge rStation 48 v2 proof transfers to a fixed primitive Dirichlet character under GRH. The transfer independently chooses positive and negative good heights, uses the reciprocal coefficients \(\mu(n)\chi(n)\), and retains the same ordinary integer grid for the profile corrections. The center of the strip and a possible central zero require no exceptional weight. De Roton’s published theorem supplies the matching lower bound in the exact norm after checking the dilations.
