# Effective fixed-height cluster certificate

The campaign derives a finite-\(N\) upper bound for the RH-conditional Nyman–Beurling distance from rStation 48 v2. The proof localizes repaired singleton Laurent terms with explicit bounded variation and analytic-weight constants. It estimates the interaction with the delayed exterior tail on fixed zero windows before applying the norm bound on their complement. The contour tail itself contributes \(B Y^{-99/100}\) to the scaled squared norm, with all mixed terms tending to zero at fixed \(Y\).

The campaign also makes the fixed-profile quadrature and anchor estimates explicit and identifies effective inputs. Supplied uniform reciprocal and growth bounds and finite-height certificates yield a one-sided tolerance algorithm. A quantitative lower estimate is a separate dependency for two-sided certification. The proof leaves these input certificates unevaluated and makes no novelty or publication claim.
