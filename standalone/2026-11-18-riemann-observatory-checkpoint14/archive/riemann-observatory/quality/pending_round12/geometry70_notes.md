## Complete readings

**Serre, “Analogues kählériens de certaines conjectures de Weil,” pp. 392–394.** A morphism on an irreducible smooth complex projective variety scales the hyperplane class by \(q\). Degree-\(r\) renormalization \(q^{-r/2}f^*\) defines a real bigraded algebra morphism, preserving the ample class, top class and Poincaré pairing. The positive Hermitian metric is determined by those structures through the Hodge identities. The renormalization therefore acts unitarily, proving Theorem 1. The same pairing gives the complementary eigenvalues \(q^n/\lambda\). For Theorem 2, the compatibility condition (c) preserves the primitive decomposition. The modified transpose constructed using powers of Lefschetz is then the Hermitian adjoint in each degree, so its trace product with the correspondence is the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm. The note recovers Theorem 1 by the same reasoning and observes why degree one avoids the additional primitive-compatibility requirement. This is an existence and definiteness result for an actual complex geometry. Transferring it to an arithmetic zero-bearing object requires that object's trace, cohomology and positive form. [Original Annals scan](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~tongliu/seminar/Weil/Serre-1960.pdf)

**Queyrut, “Sur l’équation fonctionnelle des séries \(L\) d’Artin pour des caractères réels,” pp. 133–135.** The hypothesis is membership in the real-realizable virtual character ring, stronger than having real character values. The combined use of Brauer–Witt and Serre's reduction writes the character as a virtual integer combination of inductions of real-realizable characters of degree one or two. Multiplicativity and induction reduce root numbers to the low-dimensional cases. Rank one is trivial or quadratic. A conjugate pair contributes the product of conjugate unit root numbers. A faithful real irreducible two-dimensional case is dihedral, induced from a cyclic character and treated by the idèle-class theorem for a finite-order character of a quadratic extension trivial on base idèles. The note states the local product identity and multiplies it to get evaluation at a principal idèle, hence root number \(+1\). Its cited local identity and the underlying induction argument are imported in the form used by the exposition. The quaternion character cited on p. 133 has real values but no ordinary real form. [Original Mémoires scan](https://www.numdam.org/item/MSMF_1974__37__133_0.pdf)

**Koshikawa, “The Numerical Hodge Standard Conjecture for the Square of a Simple Abelian Variety of Prime Dimension,” arXiv v1, pp. 1–6.** Theorem 1.4 analyzes an absolutely simple \(g\)-dimensional abelian variety over a finite field with \(g>1\) odd and angle rank \(g-1\) or \(g\). In the rank-\(g-1\) case the relation lattice is rank one. Galois transitivity on the pairs of inverse normalized eigenvalue factors forces the coordinates of a generator to have the same absolute value. After swapping pair representatives, the primitive relation is an equal-power product relation. This confines the exotic contributions to the middle degree in the cases examined. The corresponding motivic orbit provides a rank-two piece for the square. The supersingular elliptic factor case decomposes the rank-four piece using an imaginary quadratic action on both factors. The proof lifts the pieces and forms by complex multiplication and applies Ancona's Theorem 4.1 with both relevant dimensions equal to two and a polarized Betti realization. The proposition concerns numerical cycle classes, and the ℓ-adic equivalence corollary concerns infinitely many primes. The final remark explains why simply lifting exotic classes to characteristic-zero algebraic cycles would fail. [Full manuscript](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03804v1)

## Scope distinctions across the sources

- **Local determinants and global orbit data.** Deninger's ICM Proposition 3.1 and Theorem 3.4 give local determinant realizations. His later Witt-vector construction gives flow orbit *packets* attached to closed points, with stabilizer generated by the point norm, and equivariant continuous bijections with the stated models. It gives neither a unique individual orbit per point nor the regularized determinant of a cohomological flow that recovers all global zeros. The construction has no fixed points. [ICM pp. 169–175](https://content.ems.press/assets/public/full-texts/books/246/chapters/online-pdf/978-3-98547-544-5-volume-1-chapter-4682.pdf) · [Arithmetic flow, Theorems 6.1 and 7.10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.06400v4)
- **A special value and a spectral divisor.** Weil–étale complexes and determinant-line statements probe integer arguments. Flach–Morin explicitly distinguish the finite generation and duality conjectures underlying their proper regular arithmetic scheme theory from the analytic hypotheses used in the functional-equation compatibility paper. The earlier fiber-product topos fails their listed integral conditions (d)–(e), even in low degrees. Those limitations locate a specific construction failure, not an impossibility for all other trace data. [Weil–étale complexes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.01277v2) · [Compatibility, Theorems 1.2 and 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.04829v1) · [Topos, §9.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.3833v1)
- **Definiteness and imported purity.** The K3-square and abelian-source records supply actual numerical or cohomological positivity in their stated settings. Ito–Ito–Koshikawa explicitly say their proof did not avoid the existing finite-field Weil conjecture. In the analytic trace-family sources, weight zero or already-pure finite-field zeros enter key hypotheses, before independence, Gowers-norm, or Gaussian fluctuation estimates. Thus neither statistical conclusions nor weight-preserving comparisons are new arithmetic positivity of classical ζ. [K3-square, Remark 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.10086v1) · [Finite-field Gowers theorem, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.3282v3) · [Zero relations, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.2118v1)
- **A precise obstruction and a possible coefficient category.** Omar's tame quaternion root-number calculation gives an actual odd-multiplicity Artin sector. Deninger's obstruction applies to real equivariant descent of that sector under the center multiplicity axiom. The positive-polarized categorical construction of Deligne–Milne is conditional on an available algebraic Tannakian category and polarization; its odd sector can be quaternionic. These two results together motivate a different coefficient target, without constructing the target's arithmetic zeta trace. [Omar source audit](../../audits/adversarial_reviews/geometry68_deninger_whole_advocate.md) · [Deligne–Milne, Theorem 4.29](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~dejong/tannakian/Deligne-Milne-Tannakian-Categories.pdf)

The unresolved transfer is a compatible all-place trace with the full completed zero divisor, together with a positive form on the identified spectral domain. The most concrete question in the orbit-packet construction is whether there is a canonical trace on individual packet fibers that contributes exactly one closed-point term and also yields the required archimedean and polar terms after regularization. The cited packet decomposition supplies the periods and stabilizers. It gives no such trace.
