Compared the derived taxonomy with the canonical bibliography for checkpoint 9. The current input has 992 canonical IDs, including 991 selected works. There are 27 exact area/topic rules and 55 manually reviewed scope overrides. Six selected works remain unclassified, down from 214. An exact subject map uses the existing curator label as metadata. A scope override uses the canonical reading record, its primary locator where available, and a family-specific reason. The input for that override must record primary text or excerpt access and a read scope. For shared headings that do not establish a particular family, such as “Sign conjectures, oscillation and spectral limits,” assignments come from individually recorded scopes. Entire versus stable polynomial methods use H where the subject tag alone does not establish a zeta connection. The Artin, motivic and point-counting headings describe geometric scope. A number-field transfer requires its own mathematical evidence.

Original family, area and topic fields are preserved in the per-paper export. G membership and its withheld tags are unchanged. The local corrections in the HLZ proof do not qualify it for G. The de Branges manuscript is identified by its proposed operator construction and its recorded unassessed status. No failure is inferred from its title or from an obstruction to a different construction. The Deninger record is assigned E on its verified arithmetic cohomological obstruction, without generalizing the result to all proposed realizations.

The six unclassified rows lack a sufficiently clear internal subject rule or verified scope override under this pass. In particular, full scan access and an unread proof do not turn into a primary-scope assignment. A validation compared the old and new ledgers for unchanged source/access state, audit level, whole-work status, year, original family and selection.
