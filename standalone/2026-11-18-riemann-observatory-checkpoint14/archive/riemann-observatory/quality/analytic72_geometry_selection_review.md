# Independent geometry70 selection review

Reviewed the source scope and theorem summaries of 49 pending records. All 49 staged PDFs reproduced the recorded SHA-256 hashes. Forty-three records received substantive corrections, tighter hypotheses or explicit flags for incomplete statement extraction. Six scoped summaries were retained. The read scopes and audit levels remain as deposited, including the three existing whole-work labels. This pass compared source passages and hypotheses. It did not audit all 49 proofs.

- **Real descent and coefficients.** Queyrut’s theorem concerns virtual characters realizable over the reals. The quaternionic degree-two character in the octic-field papers is real-valued and need not be realizable over the reals. The negative exact root number forces odd order, while equality to one belongs to a separate numerical or conjectural claim in that source. The obstruction requires ordinary real equivariant complexification plus prescribed Artin central multiplicities. Milne’s numerical motive category and Scholze’s proposed coefficient-category cohomology in the cited statements use the algebraic closure of the finite field. Scholze’s Conjecture 9.5 has an overbar visible on PDF p. 24 that the text extraction drops.
- **Flow and arithmetic trace.** GEO70-008 formerly described isolated fixed-point hypotheses for Theorem 3.3. Section 3.2 requires everywhere transversality and thus excludes fixed points. It gives a two-sided orbit trace with a zero-time Euler term. Theorem 3.4 is an operator-group theorem. GEO70-010’s positive-time statement is separately scoped and the text notes that the stated hypotheses force the conformal exponent to vanish. GEO70-012’s arithmetic trace comparison and its separate manifold theorem use different hypotheses. GEO70-016 distinguishes the inserted spectral trace in Proposition 3.3 from the previously established single local factor formula. GEO70-023 distinguishes the reciprocal-root statement in Theorem 5.1 from the Ramanujan simplification in Corollary 5.2.
- **Purity and finite-field applications.** GEO70-038 and GEO70-040 retain the weight-zero sheaf hypothesis and specify the bounds. GEO70-041 gives two distinct relation counts with the explicit k-dependent exponent. GEO70-043 concerns algebraic integrality of eigenvalues of a local Frobenius lift. GEO70-046 uses finite-field RH to define the zero angles and limits the Gaussian result to the indicated family and interval regimes. GEO70-033 explicitly records the authors’ statement that they did not attempt to avoid the Weil conjecture in their K3 argument.
- **Special values and attribution.** GEO70-025 lists the standing hypotheses in front of Theorem 1.4 and distinguishes the unconditional correction-factor calculation. GEO70-028 keeps the second finite-generation hypothesis for the torsion-and-pairing expression. GEO70-032 keeps the narrower scope for the perfect complex and the broader scope for the special-value formula. GEO70-049 was cross-checked against OCR of the rendered opening pages because the embedded text uses a broken character map.
