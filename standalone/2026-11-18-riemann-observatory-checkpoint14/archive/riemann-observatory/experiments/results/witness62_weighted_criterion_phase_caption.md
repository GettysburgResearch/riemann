# Weighted closure and its zero-free threshold

The corrected weighted criterion identifies the \(D_{\alpha+2}\) closure of the span of \((1-z)^{1-a}h_k\), \(k\ge2\), at the target \((1-z)^{1-a}\) with zero-freeness of \(\zeta(s)\) in \(\Re s>b\), where \(b=(1-\alpha)/2-a\). The shaded domain has \(-3\le\alpha\le-2\) and \(1\le a<(1-\alpha)/2\). The dashed upper edge is excluded.

The solid diagonal \(a=-\alpha/2\) gives \(b=1/2\), so closure there is equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis. Below that diagonal, \(b>1/2\) and closure is equivalent to zero-freeness in the smaller half-plane \(\Re s>b\). Above the diagonal, \(0<b<1/2\) and closure is impossible by existence and reflection of nontrivial zeros. The filled circle marks \((-3,1)\), where \(b=1\) and closure holds unconditionally. The diamond marks \((-3,7/4)\), where \(b=1/4\) and closure fails even though the half-plane \(\Re s>1\) used by the printed theorem is zero-free.

The published statement is Theorem 5.5, p. 26, of Eva A. Gallardo–Gutiérrez and Daniel Seco, *Zero-Free Regions of the Riemann Zeta Function and Approximation in Weighted Dirichlet Spaces*, Complex Analysis and Operator Theory 19 (2025), article 38. The corrected threshold and its proof are documented in the project’s director adjudication. The diagram illustrates that adjudicated result.
