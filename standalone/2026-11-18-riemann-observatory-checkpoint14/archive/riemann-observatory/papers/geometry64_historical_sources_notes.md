# Historical primary sources on ζ and the distribution of primes

This supplement adds 38 historical works after comparing titles, authors and identifiers with every existing paper-record track and the canonical bibliography. It includes 4 complete readings. The remaining entries record targeted reading and keep audit level 0. Dates follow the printed issue or volume where known. The source version is stated separately from the date of mathematical discovery or presentation.

The evidence spans Euler products and prime-counting asymptotics, inversion and explicit formulas, entire-function factorization, zero-free boundaries, finite computations and critical-line zero counts. Each entry states the result it supports and the remaining step for a global assertion about classical ζ.

## Variae observationes circa series infinitas

Leonhard Euler. Commentarii academiae scientiarum Petropolitanae 9, 160–188. original 1744 Latin publication scan with Euler Archive cover sheet. [Primary text](https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1071&context=euler-works)

Derives the multiplicative prime-product identity for reciprocal power sums and the divergence of the sum of reciprocals of primes. See Theorems 7–8, printed pp. 172–176.

The product is obtained in the region of convergence or via positive partial products. Its existence gives no zero exclusion inside the critical strip.

## Beweis des Satzes, daß jede unbegrenzte arithmetische Progression, deren erstes Glied und Differenz ganze Zahlen ohne gemeinschaftlichen Factor sind, unendlich viele Primzahlen enthält

Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet. Abhandlungen der Königlich Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften, 45–71. original journal reproduction hosted by Rutgers, with library cover. [Primary text](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~alexk/2022S572/Dirichlet1837.pdf)

Proves infinitude of primes in every reduced arithmetic progression by decomposing a progression into characters and establishing the required nonvanishing at the real point 1. See pp. 45–46, and the conclusion on pp. 69–71.

The nonvanishing argument is at a boundary point for Dirichlet series. It does not control nontrivial zeros of the ordinary zeta function.

## Sur la totalité des nombres premiers inférieurs à une limite donnée

Pafnuty Chebyshev. Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 17, 341–365. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1852_1_17__341_0.pdf)

Studies the prime counting function via factorials and the summatory logarithms of primes, and constrains possible asymptotics, including the constant of any existing prime-number-theorem ratio. See opening statement and closing estimates.

The arguments give constraints on prime counts and possible asymptotic expansions. They do not supply bounds on zeta zeros in the critical strip.

## Mémoire sur les nombres premiers

Pafnuty Chebyshev. Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 17, 366–390. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://archive.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1852_1_17__366_0.pdf)

Gives explicit upper and lower comparisons for prime counts and proves the stated prime-existence interval of Bertrand. See pp. 382–390, prime-count bounds and proof of Bertrand's postulate.

Finite intervals and constant-factor bounds are compatible with error terms far larger than the square-root scale in RH.

## Sur quelques problèmes relatifs à la distribution des nombres premiers

Edmund Landau. Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 28, 25–38. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/article/BSMF_1900__28__25_1.pdf)

Uses partial summation to move between weighted prime-counting statements. In particular the theorem for the sum of log p directly yields the corresponding logarithmic integral estimate without a second contour argument. See §1 and closing equations (13)–(14).

Partial summation preserves the scale of an available PNT remainder. It does not create the square-root scale necessary to constrain critical-strip zeros.

## Sur quelques inégalités dans la théorie de la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann

Edmund Landau. Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 33, 229–241. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.758.pdf)

Investigates inequalities for ζ near the edge of the critical strip and applies reciprocal-zeta estimates to sharpen the error for the squarefree counting function. See §§I and III, including (23)–(27).

The reciprocal-zeta estimate applies on its specified boundary and contours. The resulting squarefree asymptotic does not establish a zero-free critical half-strip.

Numdam metadata says intégrales, while the printed heading at p. 229 says inégalités. The title follows the printed article.

## Sur une propriété fonctionnelle de la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann

Jacques Hadamard. Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 37, 59–60. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.836.pdf)

Each of two shifted-argument identities, together with ζ(s+n) tending to 1, separately characterizes the zeta function. The iteration for (4) uses positive coefficients and majorizes the one for (3). See formulas (3)–(5), pp. 59–60.

Positivity controls the recursion and its convergence. It is not the positivity of a spectral form over all zeros.

Read both printed pages, including the Jensen antecedent, the two characterizing identities, their asymptotic normalization and the majorant argument.

## Le énième nombre premier comme valeur asymptotique d’une fonction déduite de la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann

Luigi Fantappiè. Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France 53, 226–234. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/bsmf.1091.pdf)

Recovers successive exponents and coefficients of a Dirichlet series from its real-axis asymptotics, and applies the extraction to functions of ζ to recover a fixed nth prime. See §§1–2 and applications to p_n in the later sections.

The limit sends real s to infinity for a fixed prime index. It gives no uniform inversion near the critical strip and no bound on off-line zeros.

## Sur l’ordre de grandeur de la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann dans la bande critique

Johannes G. van der Corput, Jurjen F. Koksma. Annales de la Faculté des Sciences de Toulouse 22, 1–39. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/AFST_1930_3_22__1_0.pdf)

Derives improvements for bounds on the size of ζ in the critical strip by estimating oscillatory sums arising from its analytic representation. See Introduction and exponential-sum estimate applied to ζ in the last sections.

An upper bound on the modulus of ζ in a strip leaves open the location of its zeros there.

## Geometrisches zur Riemannschen Zetafunktion

Andreas Speiser. Mathematische Annalen 110, 514–521. original Mathematische Annalen volume 110 scan with GDZ coversheet dated 1935. [Primary text](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kuberwastaken/riemann/94fa51e8f26794ef2d206c6c12e7adb4d85cbeaf/papers/analytic-progress/Speiser-1935-geometrisches.pdf)

Relates the Riemann hypothesis to the absence of nontrivial ζ′ zeros to the left of the critical line by studying the level curves of ζ. See pp. 517–519, discussion of level curves and branch points.

This transfers the needed global zero exclusion to a derivative and supplies a geometric way to phrase it. The exclusion itself is still required.

1934 appears in some indexes and is the receipt year. This entry follows the 1935 volume year recorded on the GDZ wrapper.

## Zeros of the derivatives of the Riemann zeta-function

Norman Levinson, Hugh L. Montgomery. Acta Mathematica 133, 49–65. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6174-11511_2006_Article_BF02392141.pdf)

The counts of ζ and ζ′ zeros in the left half of the critical strip up to height T differ by O(log T). The ensuing corollary gives an explicit derivative nonvanishing equivalent to RH. See Theorem 1, equations (1.1)–(1.2), and its corollary.

The comparison relates two potentially nonempty collections of zeros, so a separate argument is needed to exclude every ζ′ zero in that region.

## Asymptotic Formula for the Coordinates of the Zeros of Sections of the Zeta Function, ζ_N(s), Near s = 1

Norman Levinson. PNAS 70, 985–987. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC433407/)

For finite Dirichlet sections ζ_N, estimates the zeros near 1 at large N and establishes the stated left-of-1 position in that range. See Abstract and §1, and the concluding location argument.

The zeros of truncations and those of meromorphic ζ are distinct. Uniform convergence of the finite Dirichlet sums is available strictly to the right of 1, outside the intended critical region.

## Generalization of Recent Method Giving Lower Bound for N₀(T) of Riemann’s Zeta-Function

Norman Levinson. PNAS 71, 3984–3987. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC434311/)

Generalizes a lower-bound argument for critical-line zeros by replacing the linear polynomial in the approximate Dirichlet expression with P satisfying P(0)=0 and P(x)+P(1−x)=1. See p. 3984, the polynomial condition and equation (1).

The polynomial freedom affects bounds for the proportion of zeros on the line. It does not rule out individual exceptions.

## Almost All Roots of ζ(s) = a Are Arbitrarily Close to σ = 1/2

Norman Levinson. PNAS 72, 1322–1324. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC432525/)

For fixed complex a and T^(1/2)≤U≤T, all but O(U log T/log log T) roots of ζ(s)=a with T<t<T+U lie within (log log T)^2/log T of the critical line. The inner count has main term U log T/(2π). See main Theorem, p. 1322, with Lemmas 1–6 and proof on pp. 1322–1323.

The same clustering holds even for nonzero target values. A density-one asymptotic for a shrinking strip does not prove exact location of every zero.

Read all three printed pages, including the mean-square lemma, weighted root sums, the separate left and right tails, the a=1 modification and the closing note about Selberg's refinement.

## Deduction of Semi-Optimal Mollifier for Obtaining Lower Bound for N₀(T) for Riemann’s Zeta-Function

Norman Levinson. PNAS 72, 294–297. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC432290/)

Derives an Euler–Lagrange equation after smoothing an optimization of the dominant contribution in Levinson's mollified mean square. The paper reports a small improvement in the resulting lower bound. See equations (17)–(23) and (33), pp. 295–297.

The optimization treats only the dominant term and counts zeros on the line in bulk. It cannot exclude occasional off-line zeros.

Read all four printed pages, following the choice of dominant term, the boundary constraints H(0)=0 and H(log y)=1, the optimized profile, and the final comparison in (33).

## Généralisation du théorème de Ikehara

Hubert Delange. Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 71, 213–242. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1954_3_71_3_213_0.pdf)

For a nondecreasing summatory function with Laplace–Stieltjes transform having the specified one-sided boundary behavior and a controlled singular term, derives an asymptotic A exp(at)P(t). Theorem II relaxes the permitted boundary behavior. See Theorems I–II, pp. 213–216.

A Tauberian result derives asymptotics from boundary data. It neither supplies boundary data on the critical line nor excludes internal zeros.

## Distribution des nombres premiers et fonction ζ(s)

Hubert Delange. Séminaire Delange–Pisot–Poitou 1, Exposé 1, 1–18. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://archive.numdam.org/item/SDPP_1959-1960__1__A1_0.pdf)

Explains how the nonvanishing of ζ at Re s=1 suffices for the PNT via Ikehara and how stronger zero information produces sharper prime-counting remainders. See items 12–14, including the Ikehara application.

The Tauberian theorem only uses the line Re s=1. It yields no zero exclusion in the open critical strip.

## Un théorème sur les fonctions arithmétiques multiplicatives et ses applications

Hubert Delange. Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 78, 1–29. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1961_3_78_1_1_0.pdf)

Under its multiplicativity, boundedness and divergence conditions at primes, the paper proves cancellation of the mean over all integers and over squarefree integers, with applications to additive-function distributions. See §1.1, equations (2)–(3).

Prime-weighted mean cancellation is much weaker than the quantitative cancellation in the Möbius summatory function required for RH.

## Démonstration élémentaire du théorème des nombres premiers

Hubert Delange. Séminaire Delange–Pisot–Poitou 6(1), Exposé 6, 1–13. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/SDPP_1964-1965__6_1_A5_0.pdf)

Gives an elementary proof of the prime number theorem, phrased in terms of the asymptotic weighted prime sums and using Selberg's identity. See statement at items 1–2 and the final elementary argument.

The elementary prime number theorem gives an asymptotic of order x without an RH-scale bound for its remainder.

## Démonstration élémentaire du théorème des nombres premiers

Charles Pisot. Séminaire Bourbaki 1, Exposé 20, 123–127. 1952 printed seminar text of the December 1949 exposé. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/article/SB_1948-1951__1__123_0.pdf)

A contemporary seminar exposition of the elementary Erdős–Selberg proof, including the weighted identity and passage to the prime number theorem. See pp. 123–127, Selberg identity and deduction of the limit.

The result determines the main term. It gives no all-zero assertion about the interior of the strip.

## О нулях дзета-функции Римана

Sergei B. Stechkin. Математические заметки 8(4), 419–429. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://m.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=mzm&option_lang=eng&paperid=9606&what=fullt)

Proves ζ(σ+it)≠0 for σ≥1−1/(9.65 log t) with t≥12, using a trigonometric polynomial and a differenced logarithmic derivative. See abstract, introduction equation (1), and the concluding theorem.

An explicit region adjacent to 1 leaves the complementary part of the right half-strip uncontrolled.

## О функции ζ(s)

Ivan M. Vinogradov. Доклады Академии наук СССР 118(4), 631. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://m.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=dan&option_lang=eng&paperid=22700&what=fullt)

Announces a bound for ζ(1+it) from a new exponential-sum estimate, with the summed phase given by a multiple of ln x in the stated parameter range. See p. 631, equations (1)–(2).

The individual value bound is on the right boundary and does not exclude interior zeros.

## Sur la distribution des nombres premiers

Helge von Koch. Acta Mathematica 24, 159–182. original journal PDF with the Note additionnelle on printed p. 182. [Primary text](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/5070-11511_2006_Article_BF02403071.pdf)

Under RH, §7 proves π(x)−Li(x)=O_ε(x^(1/2+ε)). The added note sharpens it to O(x^(1/2) log x). See §7 and the Note additionnelle, pp. 181–182.

The estimate relies on the critical-line hypothesis. Using the remainder to deduce RH would require proving the bound independently.

## Équirépartition et zéros de la fonction zêta

Georges Griso. Séminaire Delange–Pisot–Poitou 19(2), Exposé 23, 1–7. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/SDPP_1977-1978__19_2_A1_0.pdf)

Removes RH from the equidistribution modulo 1 of logarithmic rescalings of the full ordinates, by combining Landau's weighted zero sums with a bound controlling the real parts, and gives a multidimensional extension for multiplicatively independent scales. See pp. 1–3, Landau sums (1)–(2) and Weyl criterion.

Uniform distribution modulo 1 of imaginary parts does not distinguish critical-line zeros from off-line zeros.

## Nombres premiers généralisés et équirépartition modulo 1

Jean-Pierre Borel. Séminaire Delange–Pisot–Poitou 19(1), Exposé 7, 1–7. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://archive.numdam.org/item/SDPP_1977-1978__19_1_A5_0.pdf)

Uses a Weyl criterion on generalized integers and generalized primes, with character hypotheses and estimates derived from the generalized prime number theorem. See §§1–3, Theorems 1–3.

The conclusion concerns distribution modulo 1 under generalized prime hypotheses. It places no restriction on every classical zeta zero.

## Inequalities for zeros of ζ(s)

Charles Ryavec. Acta Arithmetica 33, 255–260. Acta Arithmetica original printed article on two-page archive spreads. [Primary text](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa33/aa3334.pdf)

Constructs orthonormal families from a decomposition of the Mellin representation and applies Bessel's inequality to restrict possible zeros; Theorem 3 gives explicit zero-free regions. See Theorems 2–3, building on Lemma 1, pp. 255–260.

The inequalities depend on the constructed family and give the stated restricted regions, without covering the full off-line strip.

## On the zeros of the Riemann zeta-function and L-series

Kanakanahalli Ramachandra. Acta Arithmetica 34, 211–218. Acta Arithmetica original printed article on two-page archive spreads including neighboring article matter. [Primary text](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa34/aa3434.pdf)

Starting from one or two zeros with real part greater than 1/2 and ordinate above 100, gives lower bounds for a weighted sum over zeros in specified discs near the 1-line, subject to the stated scale restrictions. See §1, Theorems 1–2, pp. 211–212.

The conditional neighborhood constraints describe consequences of a hypothetical off-line zero. The bounds do not by themselves contradict its presence.

## The Zeros of the Riemann Zeta-Function

Edward C. Titchmarsh. Proceedings of the Royal Society A 151, 234–255. original journal scans on two-page spreads, 22 printed pages in 11 PDF pages. [Primary text](https://mwolf.pracownicy.uksw.edu.pl/Proc_Roy_Soc_Lond_151%281936%29.pdf)

Uses the Riemann–Siegel asymptotic formula with a controlled remainder to locate the first 195 zeros up to ordinate 390 on the critical line. See §1, pp. 234–235, and closing tables.

The computation has a finite ordinate cutoff. Higher zeros require separate control.

## Étude sur les propriétés des fonctions entières et en particulier d’une fonction considérée par Riemann

Jacques Hadamard. Journal de Mathématiques Pures et Appliquées 9, 171–215. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/JMPA_1893_4_9__171_0.pdf)

Analyzes the growth and genus of the completed Riemann function, viewed in the squared centered variable, to justify the product structure assumed in Riemann's memoir. See Part III, §§34–38, pp. 210–215.

Entire-function genus gives convergence and structure of a product over zeros. It supplies no theorem forcing the individual factors' zeros to lie on a line.

## Ω-Theorems for Riemann’s Zeta-Function at Harmonic Combinations of Points

Norman Levinson. PNAS 69, 1657–1658. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC426771/)

Shows simultaneous large-product lower bounds for zeta values at harmonic ordinates for each fixed integer r≥2. Discusses analogues for fixed real parts between 1/2 and 1 and for reciprocal zeta at 1. See main statement and coefficient argument, pp. 1657–1658.

Large values at selected ordinates are compatible with off-line zeros elsewhere and are logically distinct from zero-free bounds.

## Ω-Theorems for Quotients of Zeta-Functions at Combinations of Points

Norman Levinson. PNAS 69, 2528–2529. original PNAS printed page images assembled from PMC CDN into a PDF. [Primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC426980/)

For integers q,r in the stated ranges, proves limsup lower bounds for the absolute value of a quotient of zeta products on the 1-line, with factors indexed by harmonic scales. See Theorem A, p. 2528.

A lower bound along selected sequences on the 1-line cannot exclude zeros further left.

## Nouvelle démonstration pour la formule de Riemann sur le nombre des nombres premiers inférieurs à une limite donnée et démonstration d’une formule plus générale pour le cas des nombres premiers d’une progression arithmétique

Edmund Landau. Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 25, 399–442. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1908_3_25__399_0.pdf)

Gives a new derivation of Riemann's prime-power counting formula and its extension to primes in a reduced progression, using the appropriate Dirichlet L-series. See introduction, definition of f(x), and the later progression formula.

The explicit formula relates primes to a sum over zeros. It continues to require separate estimates or geometric information to locate the zeros.

## Zur Theorie der Dirichletschen Reihen

Oskar Perron. Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 134, 95–143. GDZ original volume pages extracted without altering printed pp. 95–143 (PDF pages 102–150 of the volume). [Primary text](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN243919689_0134/PPN243919689_0134.pdf)

Supplies rigorous uniqueness and inversion results for Dirichlet series with increasing real exponents. His integral recovers coefficient sums with a boundary convention for exponents at the cutoff. See opening and §§4–5, especially the coefficient-summation integral on pp. 103–104.

The inversion recovers sums from analytic data in a convergence half-plane. Moving the contour toward the critical line requires additional singularity and error control.

## Об одной теореме Харди–Литтлвуда в теории дзета-функции Римана

Jan Moser. Acta Arithmetica 31, 45–51. Acta Arithmetica original printed article on two-page archive spreads. [Primary text](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa31/aa3114.pdf)

Under its trigonometric-sum bound with exponent Δ, gives an odd-order critical-line zero in each sufficiently high interval of length T^(1/8+Δ/2) times an arbitrarily slowly increasing factor. See main theorem and (2)–(5), pp. 45–46.

The interval contains at least one critical-line zero. This is consistent with other zeros in that interval off the critical line.

The author returned to this result in the separate 1979 addendum, pp. 403–404, with a Lindelöf specialization and revised remainder estimate.

## О поведении функций Re{ζ(s)}, Im{ζ(s)} в критической полосе

Jan Moser. Acta Arithmetica 34, 25–35. Acta Arithmetica original printed article on two-page archive spreads including neighboring article matter. [Primary text](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa34/aa3413.pdf)

Assuming the stated trigonometric-sum bound, proves that sufficiently high intervals specified in (2) at fixed σ=1/2+δ in the admitted range contain an ordinate where Im ζ vanishes and an ordinate where Re ζ equals 1. See Theorems 1–2 and (1)–(8), p. 25.

Zeros of the imaginary part and real-target crossings are different level-set conditions. These results do not distinguish the locations of complex zeros of ζ.

## Добавление к работе «Об одной теореме Харди–Литтлвуда в теории дзета-функции Римана»

Jan Moser. Acta Arithmetica 35, 403–404. Acta Arithmetica original printed addendum on two-page archive spreads including neighboring article matter. [Primary text](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa35/aa35410.pdf)

Under Lindelöf, every sufficiently high critical-line interval of length T^(1/8+ε) contains an odd-order zero. The addendum also refines the earlier remainder estimate by changing the summation cutoff. See Theorem and (1)–(5), p. 403 and §2 p. 404.

The result is conditional and guarantees a critical-line zero in each interval. It does not locate all zeros there.

Read both printed pages. The first applies the Lindelöf exponential-sum formulation to the 1976 theorem and the second changes the truncation in its remainder analysis.

## Sur la fonction ζ(s) de Riemann et sur des fonctions analogues

Eugène Cahen. Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 11, 75–164. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/article/ASENS_1894_3_11__75_0.pdf)

Develops the half-plane and line of convergence for generalized Dirichlet series and treats formulas relating the abscissa to sums of coefficients. Perron's 1908 paper subsequently corrects some assertions and proof gaps. See Part I, §§5–8, and opening discussion of functional relations.

Convergence and analytic continuation concern the representation of a function. Further zero-location information is needed within the domain of continuation.

## Sur la distribution des entiers ayant certaines propriétés

Hubert Delange. Annales Scientifiques de l’École Normale Supérieure 73, 15–74. digitized original journal, proceedings or seminar publication. [Primary text](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.24033/asens.1041.pdf)

Uses a generalization of Ikehara to pass from a controlled singular expansion at s=1 to asymptotics with logarithmic powers, then applies it to sets classified by prime divisors. See Theorem a, pp. 25–26, and applications beginning at Theorem 1.

The analytic input is the local singularity at s=1 and control on its boundary line. It gives no general vanishing restriction in the critical strip.
