# Isolated zeros of the Riemann zeta function and zeros of linear combinations of Dirichlet $L$-functions

Youness Lamzouri. arXiv:2610.26841v2, 2026-10-26.

Theorem 1.1 claims \(\liminf B_0(T,c)/N(T)\ge0.6552\ldots\) for some fixed \(c>0\) and \(\ge0.50875\) for \(c=1/6\), where \(B_0\) requires each adjacent ordered-ordinate gap to exceed \(2\pi c/\log T\) at a zero with \(0<\gamma\le T\). For a distinct pair of primitive characters of the same conductor and parity, Theorem 1.5 claims at least 31% odd-multiplicity critical zeros for linear combinations with nonzero real coefficients and the source’s respective root-number phase factors.
