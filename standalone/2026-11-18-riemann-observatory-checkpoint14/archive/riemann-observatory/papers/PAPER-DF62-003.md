The checked density definition counts actual mathlib zeta zeros with analytic multiplicity, \(0<\Re\rho<1\), \(\sigma\le\Re\rho\) and \(|\Im\rho|\le T\). The formal statement requires existence of a finite set with exactly these members. In global_uniform, for every \(\varepsilon>0\) one constant works simultaneously for \(\sigma\in[1/2,1]\) and \(T\ge2\), with bound \(C_\varepsilon T^{(39/17)(1-\sigma)+\varepsilon}\). This improves the global coefficient \(30/13\) by \(3/221\).

For a fixed \(\sigma\), full_curve supplies \(A(\sigma)=3/(2-\sigma)\) on \([1/2,9/13]\), \(18/(7\sigma+3)\) on \((9/13,5/7]\), \(9/4\) on \((5/7,3/4]\), and \(3/(2\sigma)\) on \((3/4,1]\). The exact curve has a separate uniform statement only on \([1/2,3/4]\cup[3/4+\delta,1]\) for fixed \(\delta>0\). At \(3/4\) the exponent is \(9/16\), whereas Guth–Maynard already gives \(5/9<9/16\). The global comparison is therefore a comparison of uniform coefficients, beyond a pointwise comparison of complete envelopes.

The checked middle_large_values theorem uses \(N^{6/5}\le T\le N^{5/3}\), \(N\ge2\), and a 1-separated finite set \(W\) in a translate of \([0,T]\). A dyadic bounded-coefficient Dirichlet polynomial with absolute value at least \(V>0\) at \(W\) satisfies \(|W|\ll_\varepsilon N^\varepsilon(N^2/V^2+N^{18/7}T^{6/7}/V^4)\). The factor \(e^{-it\log N}\) in the formal polynomial does not affect the magnitude. At \(T=N^{3/2},V=N^{3/4}\) the second-term exponent is \(6/7\), versus \(9/10\) for the largest term in Guth–Maynard Theorem 1.1. The new estimate is limited to its stated middle-range scales.
