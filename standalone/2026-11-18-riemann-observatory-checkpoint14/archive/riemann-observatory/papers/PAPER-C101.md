# PAPER-C101 — The Nyman–Beurling equivalent form for the Riemann hypothesis

Authors: Michel Balazard, Éric Saias. Expositiones Mathematicae 18(2) (2000), 131–138; ESI 623 manuscript inspected. [Source](https://www.esi.ac.at/preprints/esi623.pdf). DOI: not verified.

Version: ESI Preprint623; body dated12November1998, cover27October1998. Accessed 2026-11-17. **Overall audit level 0; partial reading only.**

Scope: Introduction and §5 pp6–7, especially Question4; middle Hardy-space proof not audited.

Question4 p7 explicitly asks whether RH implies convergence for the fixed logarithmic Selberg coefficient array in the full critical-line norm. Question2 concerns arbitrary discrete arrays and Question3 unsmoothed Möbius sums.

The fixed-array question is not the discrete closure theorem. The paper predates Báez-Duarte’s solution of the arbitrary-array closure question.
