**Main result:** For B_lambda=span{{theta/t}:lambda<=theta<=1} in L²((0,infinity),dt), D(lambda)=dist(chi,B_lambda), liminf_{lambda↓0}D(lambda)*sqrt(log(1/lambda)) >= sqrt(sum_rho m_rho²/|rho|²) (Theorem1.3 p.4; Theorem5.5 pp.15–16). Proof assumes RH, since its failure makes the left side infinite; rho are distinct nontrivial zeros under that assumption. Polynomial analogue Theorem1.4: Q of positive degree with all roots alpha on the unit circle, multiplicities m_alpha, and fixed target polynomial P gives lim N inf_deg A<=N ||P-QA||²=sum_alpha m_alpha²|P(alpha)|².

**Proof architecture:** ["Finite-dimensional prediction error and jet evaluation Gram matrices (§2)", "Mellin invariant unitary phase operator V (§3)", "Boundary-limit Hilbert vectors Y_(s,k)^lambda and orthogonality to contracted zeta model (§4)", "Rescale by log(1/lambda)^(-k-1/2)", "Fixed finite set of zeros gives Cauchy Gram blocks, whose inverse top-left entries are m²", "Take lower bound for every finite zero set; then increase the finite set"]

**Limitations:** ["A lower bound only; no upper bound or complete spectral construction follows.", "Fixed finite-set Gram limits do not justify a uniform infinite-set limit.", "The conjectural disappearance of the continuous quotient component is explicitly speculative.", "D(lambda) allows continuous dilation parameters; discrete Dirichlet-polynomial distance is at least as large at lambda=1/N."]

**Historical importance:** Identifies multiplicity squared, not multiplicity, as the approximation obstruction; converts zero jets to asymptotic Gram blocks.

**Reusable lemma:** Theorem5.3: distinct fixed zeros have asymptotically vanishing cross Gram entries after scaling; same-zero derivative jets give 1/(k+l+1).

**Research questions:** ["Obtain uniform Gram estimates when zero separations shrink with the cutoff.", "Control any orthogonal complement beyond the constructed zero jets before asserting a matching upper bound."]
