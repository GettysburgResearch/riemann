- **Hardy–Littlewood 1921** (duplicate). The current statistics supplement already records the work.
- **Mertens 1874, Ein Beitrag** (duplicate). Present in the earlier classical history supplement.
- **S. Ikehara 1931** (duplicate). Present in the earlier classical corpus.
- **Titchmarsh 1936 and 1947** (full text unverified). Publisher or mirror responses were access pages or yielded no complete PDF. The 1935 article is a separately verified work.
- **Fekete 1926** (full text unverified). Metadata appeared under inconsistent authorship and no complete primary text was retained.
- **Pólya 1927 and Pólya–Schur 1914** (incomplete transfer). Volume downloads ended before the PDF cross-reference table. The title page alone was insufficient for an article-level receipt.
- **Ingham 1932** (partial substitute). Available vendor response was a 14-page sample from a later Russian reprint.
- **Jensen 1913** (full text unverified). Publisher endpoints and alternate links failed to yield a complete reproducible text.
- **Wiener 1932** (version unverified). The candidate transcription and catalogue matches did not provide the required cached full primary scan.
- **Levinson 1956, Levinson 1969, Spira 1965/1973, Berndt 1969/1970, Lehman 1966/1970** (full text unverified). Publisher link and metadata searches did not yield complete reproducible copies in this pass.
- **Moser and Ramachandra Acta links with informator.pdf** (wrong file). Some publisher links returned a Mathematical Institute information brochure. Archive scans were separately identified by printed title and pages for included papers.
- **Delange–Pisot–Poitou 1967–1968, Répartition des nombres premiers** (scope). The original is by Jean-Louis Nicolas. This short course summary was reserved for the discovery queue.
- **Astérisque 1977, Quelques progrès récents** (scope). The original is by Jean-Marc Deshouillers. This survey summarizes other authors’ results and was excluded in favor of direct primary work.
- **De la Vallée Poussin 1895, Démonstration simplifiée** (full text unverified). The Persée record was available, but the journal PDF endpoint did not complete.
- **Motohashi 1978 and Tsuji 1942** (full text unverified). Identified proceedings articles whose full-PDF requests returned placeholders or errors.
- **Landau 1909 Handbuch** (full text unverified). Full-text link failed to yield a verified original copy.
