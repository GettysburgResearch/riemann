# A zero density estimate for Dedekind zeta functions

Jesse Thorner, Asif Zaman. arXiv:1909.01338v2, 2022-01-06. Journal issue date 2023-04-12, International Mathematics Research Notices

For a finite nontrivial group \(G\) and a family \(\mathcal F_G(Q)\) of Galois \(K/\mathbb Q\) with group \(G\) and discriminant at most \(Q\), Theorem 1.1 sums the zero counts for \(\zeta_K/\zeta\). For \(\sigma\ge0,\ Q,T\ge2\), the result is \(O_{|G|}(m_{\mathcal F_G}(Q)(QT)^{(7/10)|G|^3(1-\sigma)}(\log QT)^{|G|^2})\), with \(m\) the maximal number of family fields intersecting one chosen member nontrivially.
