# Large zeta sums and zeros of the Riemann zeta function

Zikang Dong, Ruihua Wang, Weijia Wang, Hao Zhang. arXiv:2608.31060v1, 2026-08-31.

For \(T\le|t|\le2T,\ \exp(\sqrt{\log T})\le x\le\sqrt T\), a value \(|\sum_{n\le x}n^{it}|=x/N\) with \(1\le N\le(\log x)^{1/100}\) forces a common \(\phi\) with \(|\phi-t|\le cN\). For every \(cN^6\le L\le(\log x)/2\), its open disk about \(1+i\phi\) of radius \(L\log T/(\log x)^2\) has at least \(L/360\) zero copies. Theorem 1.2 runs in the reverse direction as a sufficient local zero count condition for a logarithmic saving in the arithmetic sum.
