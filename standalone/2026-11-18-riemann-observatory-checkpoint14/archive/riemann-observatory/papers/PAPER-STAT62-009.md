# Sharp conditional bounds for moments of the Riemann zeta function

Adam J. Harper (2013). arXiv:1305.4618v1 (2013-05-20).

Assuming RH, the upper bound for the 2kth critical-line moment has the sharp order \(T(\log T)^{k^2}\) for every fixed real \(k\ge0\). Adaptive prime-block lengths remove the extra logarithmic loss from the earlier Soundararajan approach.

Level 0. Theorem 1 and the initial upper polynomial proposition checked. Proof of this sharp fixed-k result outside scope. Private snapshot `sources/latest62_statistics/harper.pdf` (20 PDF pages).
