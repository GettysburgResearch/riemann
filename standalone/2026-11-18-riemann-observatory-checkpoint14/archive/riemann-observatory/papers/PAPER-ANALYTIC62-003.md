# More than five-twelfths of the zeros of $\zeta$ are on the critical line

Kyle Pratt, Nicolas Robles, Alexandru Zaharescu, Dirk Zeindler. arXiv:1802.10521v3, 2019-09-15. Journal issue date 2020-03, Research in the Mathematical Sciences

Theorem 1.2 proves more than \(5/12\) of zeta zeros are on the critical line. The numerical optimization in the introduction gives \(\kappa>0.417293\) and \(\kappa^*\ge0.407511\) for the simple critical proportion. These are different counts, both based on mollified twisted second moments.

Level 0. Theorem 1.2, surrounding optimization and statement of the mollified main moment checked. Long proofs outside scope. PDF pp. 1–60 available privately.
