## Selected works

**PAPER-SM2017-LOGGROUP.** Grigory M. Sklyar and Vitalii Marchenko, *Hardy inequality and the construction of infinitesimal operators with non-basis family of eigenvectors*, *Journal of Functional Analysis* 272(3), 1017–1043, February 2017. DOI `10.1016/j.jfa.2016.11.001`. Crossref confirms this issue date. OpenAlex reports 8 November 2016, also the Crossref DOI creation date. The exact first online publication date was unverified against the publisher text. The inspected current preprint is arXiv:1405.2731v4, posted 24 August 2016. Its SHA-256 is `288b923dc64a851b969d82c5e1db2b9d77db82eb7fa0910b957e3507c6bd3829`. The targeted passages are §2.1, Proposition 3 and §3.1 Theorem 6 in v4, with Theorem 8 and Proposition 6 in v2 checked for the historical statement. This is the exact logarithmic group under a unitary difference map, adjoint and reversal of the parameter. The publisher full text was inaccessible. [Journal identity](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2016.11.001) · [Current preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.2731v4) · [Crossref snapshot](../sources/finite_moment63_operator_antecedents/sklyar_marchenko_jfa_crossref.json) · [OpenAlex snapshot](../sources/finite_moment63_operator_antecedents/sklyar_marchenko_jfa_openalex.json)

The version relation is one work with four arXiv manifestations and a journal identity. The 12 May 2014 v1 uses the title variant *Hardy inequality and the construction of infinitesimal operators with non-Riesz basis family of eigenvectors* and adds a \(c_0\) condition to its displayed logarithmic generator domain. The 22 September 2014 v2 replaces this by the maximal first-difference domain. Its Proposition 6(4) makes an all-subsequence claim. The 3 November 2014 v3 limits that clause to infinite subsequences, where the sparse orthogonal witness still applies. The 24 August 2016 v4 removes that section and proposition, retains the entire-sequence result in Proposition 3, and renumbers the logarithmic group as Theorem 6. The historical clause supplies no error finding about the journal article. **PAPER-SM2021-LOGRESOLVENT.** Grigory M. Sklyar and Vitalii Marchenko, *Resolvent of the generator of the \(C_0\)-group with non-basis family of eigenvectors and sharpness of the XYZ theorem*, *Journal of Spectral Theory* 11(1), 369–386, 2021. DOI `10.4171/JST/344`. Crossref gives 12 March 2021. The EMS version of record has SHA-256 `682a60a548e39336f93a6a36c4f97d1be2fbfa879b17b5240e25fa619e79fbb7`. The targeted reading covered Theorems 1.2, 2.1 and 3.1, including (10) and the first-difference proof. The explicit resolvent and spectrum apply to the identified group. Compactness in this case follows from the displayed resolvent, by factoring its difference term through a diagonal tending to zero. [Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.4171/JST/344) · [Publisher PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/33788)

**PAPER-SMP2021-LINEARGROUP.** Grigory Sklyar, Vitalii Marchenko and Piotr Polak, *One Class of Linearly Growing \(C_0\)-Groups*, *Journal of Mathematical Physics, Analysis, Geometry* 17(4), 509–520, 2021. DOI `10.15407/mag17.04.509`. The publisher PDF has SHA-256 `7106af34dae09a0e3af4270b1f07a93fd292ff07c7bee93fd8c224d75e9f55de`. The targeted reading covered §1 and (1.2), Theorem 2.1 and Theorem 4.2. The paper explicitly reports two-sided linear growth for the logarithmic group. Its linear bounds are distinct from the pointwise norm formula. This is a separate article from the 2021 EMS resolvent paper and the JFA predecessor *Sharp polynomial bounds for certain \(C_0\)-groups generated by operators with non-basis family of eigenvectors*, DOI `10.1016/j.jfa.2020.108864`. The predecessor remains an unselected candidate because its full text was blocked. [Publisher record](https://jmag.ilt.kharkiv.ua/index.php/jmag/article/view/jm17-0509e) · [Publisher PDF](https://jmag.ilt.kharkiv.ua/index.php/jmag/article/download/jm17-0509e/932) · [JFA predecessor](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2020.108864)

**PAPER-BHS1965-CESARO.** Arlen Brown, Paul Richard Halmos and Allen Lowell Shields, *Cesàro operators*, *Acta Scientiarum Mathematicarum (Szeged)* 26(1–2), 125–137, 1965. The University of Szeged scan has SHA-256 `1e0d224b7cc980ef82a9211cdd974e154b83bb1f3bdb09bf351ea8f00a358f7b`. The targeted reading covered the introduction and Theorem 2, including the spectral-disk proof. Its open adjoint point spectrum yields the sharp lower bound after the compact perturbation in the logarithmic group. A matching title and authors appear in *Selecta* (1983), 207–219, DOI `10.1007/978-1-4613-8208-9_21`. That later chapter's text was unverified and its DOI is not the original article's identifier.
