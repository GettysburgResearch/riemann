# A remark on density theorems for Riemann's zeta-function

János Pintz. arXiv:2310.04544v1, 2023-09-30. Journal issue date 2023-12, Science China Mathematics

Writing \(N(1-\eta,T)\ll_\epsilon T^{\eta B(\eta)+\epsilon}\), the detector proof gives \(B(\eta)\le\max(4u_\ell,3v_\ell)\) on \(0<\eta<\min(1/3,2^{-\ell})\), with \(u_\ell=\mu(1-2^{-\ell})/(2^{-\ell}-\eta)\), \(v_\ell=\mu(1-2^{-(\ell-1)})/[2(2^{-\ell}-\eta)]\). Corollary 4 gives \(B(\eta)\le1/(1-4\eta)\) for \(0<\eta<1/4\); Corollary 1 conditionally gives \(N(\sigma,T)\ll_{\sigma,\epsilon}T^\epsilon\) for fixed \(\sigma>3/4\) under Lindelöf.
