# Explicit zero density estimate for the Riemann zeta-function near the critical line

Aleksander Simonič. arXiv:1910.08274v2, 2019-11-29. Journal issue date 2020-11, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

For the verification height \(H_0=3.0610046\times10^{10}\) used by the paper, Theorem 1 proves \(N(\sigma,2T)-N(\sigma,T)\le10395.2\,T^{1-(\sigma-1/2)/4}\log T+1.104\log^2T+0.173\log T\log\log T+0.51\log T\) for \(T\ge H_0,\ 1/2\le\sigma\le0.831\). The interval is dyadic rather than of sublinear length.
