# PAPER-L002 — Bombieri–Lagarias (1999)

**Complements to Li’s Criterion for the Riemann Hypothesis**, Enrico Bombieri and Jeffrey C. Lagarias. *Journal of Number Theory* 77(2), 274–287 (August 1999). [DOI 10.1006/jnth.1999.2392](https://doi.org/10.1006/jnth.1999.2392); [author-hosted manuscript](https://websites.umich.edu/~lagarias/doc/bombieri.pdf).

Accessed 2026-11-17. Complete PDF accessed; only manuscript pages 3–5 read in this independent reviewer track. Overall audit level **0**, with a scoped technical reconstruction of Theorem 1 and its corollary. No whole-paper substantive reading is claimed. Arithmetic explicit-formula sections remain unread here. The author's manuscript has 12 pages; its exact date/version relative to publication has not been verified.

Theorem 1, manuscript p.3, treats a multiset R with 1∉R and Σ(1+|Re ρ|)/(1+|ρ|)² finite. It makes Re ρ≤1/2 for all ρ equivalent to nonnegativity, for every integer n≥1, of Σ Re[1−(1−1/ρ)^(−n)]. A third equivalent condition permits a lower bound −c(ε)e^(εn) for each ε>0. The proof on pp.3–4 isolates finitely many roots of largest transformed modulus and uses simultaneous Diophantine approximation of their arguments. Corollary 1, pp.4–5, adds reflection/conjugation symmetry and excludes 0,1 to obtain critical-line location and the standard positive-power Li sums.

The distinction between inverse and positive powers is important: a direct derivative at s=1 gives the inverse-power expression root by root; reflection symmetry identifies the summed expressions. The universal quantifier over n is essential. The source does not claim that any finite prefix suffices.

The independently proved finite-prefix counterexample in [the skeptic audit](../audits/adversarial_reviews/li_skeptic.md) illustrates this quantifier issue in a broad entire-function class. It is not a counterexample for genuine arithmetic L-functions and has no novelty claim. A concrete next task is to identify an arithmetic condition that supplies a uniform positivity tail bound from finite data.
