# On the Distribution of Spacings Between Zeros of the Zeta Function

A. M. Odlyzko (1987). Mathematics of Computation 48(177), 273–308.

Early large-scale numerical tests of Montgomery’s conjecture and GUE spacing predictions compare zeros around the origin and a much higher block. The article also identifies deviations related to primes. It is computational evidence about sampled blocks.
