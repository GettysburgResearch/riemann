# Explicit zero density estimate near unity

Chiara Bellotti. arXiv:2311.05136v1, 2023-11-09.

Theorem 1.2 gives \(N(\sigma,T)\le C_1^\prime T^{57.8875(1-\sigma)^{3/2}}(\log T)^{10393/900}\) uniformly for \(\sigma\in[0.98,1]\), \(T\ge3\), with \(C_1^\prime\) tabulated by height. Theorem 1.3 gives \(C_1^\prime=4.45\times10^{12}\) for \(T\ge\exp(6.7\times10^{12})\) in the same real-part range. The power of \(\log T\) is part of the bound.
