# Zeros of principal L-functions and random matrix theory

Zeév Rudnick, Peter Sarnak (1996). Duke Mathematical Journal 81(2), 269–322.

Theorem 1.1 proves a smoothed n-level correlation asymptotic with Fourier support sum |ξ_j|<2/m for cuspidal GL(m) over Q, subject to its condition (1.7) for m>3. Theorem 1.2 adds RH for the transition to ordered normalized zeros. The support restriction is central to the GUE comparison.
