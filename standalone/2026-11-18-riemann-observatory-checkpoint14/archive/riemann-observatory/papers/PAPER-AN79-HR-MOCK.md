**Source locator** Printed pp. 2919–2922, introduction and Theorems 1–3; p. 2926, Theorem 7

For the microscopic periodization of a Schwartz test f, Theorem 1 gives convergence of the first m ordinary Haar U(N) moments to Gaussian moments when supp(f̂) ⊆ [−2/m,2/m], with mean ∫f and variance ∫min(|u|,1)|f̂(u)|²du for real f. Theorem 2 takes even real f and gives the corresponding support windows [−1/m,1/m] for Sp(N), with N even, and (−1/m,1/m) for SO(N). Theorem 3 gives exact mixed trace moments under the separate total index bounds N+1 and N−1. Theorem 7 attributes the balanced Fourier cumulant estimate to Soshnikov.

## Scope of reuse
