# The Fyodorov-Hiary-Keating Conjecture. II

Louis-Pierre Arguin, Paul Bourgade, Maksym Radziwiłł (2023). arXiv:2307.00982v1 (2023-07-03).

Theorem 1 bounds the probability of the maximum falling below e⁻ʸ log T/(log log T)^{3/4} by c⁻¹y⁻ᶜ for 0<y≤(log log T)^{1/10}, T≥100. Paired with Part I this gives tightness of the recentered maximum, which is weaker than identifying its limiting law.
