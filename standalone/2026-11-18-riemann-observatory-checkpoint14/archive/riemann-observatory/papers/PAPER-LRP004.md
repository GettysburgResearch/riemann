The Ingham mean is `I(x)=x^(-1)sum_(d<=x) d a_d floor(x/d)`. Its smoothing class consists of fixed functions `F` satisfying the printed endpoint, monotonicity and PNT-decay conditions. Equations3.18–3.19 express the fixed smoothing `sum a_k F(k/r)` by a Möbius-convolution kernel. Theorem3.4 transfers the stated Ingham summability to that fixed smoothing under the specified arithmetic assumptions.

An `N`-dependent logarithmic taper is not obtained by simply replacing this fixed `F` or changing arithmetic frequency to `log n`; uniformity and the weighted critical norm would need additional proof.
