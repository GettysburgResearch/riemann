# Lower bounds for the large deviations and moments of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line

Louis-Pierre Arguin, Nathan Creighton (2026). arXiv:2603.01711v1 (2026-03-02).

Theorem 1.1 gives an unconditional lower bound by a positive α-dependent multiple of the Gaussian tail for V∼α log log T, every fixed α>0. It complements the existing separate upper bounds and also treats primitive even characters of prime conductor at central points in Theorem 1.3.
