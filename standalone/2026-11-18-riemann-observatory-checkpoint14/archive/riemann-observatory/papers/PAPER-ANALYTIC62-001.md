# Explicit results on the distribution of zeros of Hecke $L$-functions

Jesse Thorner, Asif Zaman. arXiv:1510.08086v1, 2015-10-27. Journal issue date 2016-05, Journal of Number Theory

Theorem 1.1 gives the effective log-free bound \(\sum_{\chi(H)=1}N(\sigma,T,\chi)\ll\{e^{O(n_K)}D_K^2QT^{n_K}\}^{81(1-\sigma)}\) for \(1/2\le\sigma<1\) and \(T\ge\max\{n_K D_K^{-2/n_K}Q^{-3/(5n_K)},1\}\). Here \(H\bmod\mathfrak q\) is a congruence class group in a number field \(K\) and \(Q\) is the largest primitive conductor norm for characters trivial on \(H\). The exponent constant 74 applies for \(1-10^{-3}\le\sigma<1\).
