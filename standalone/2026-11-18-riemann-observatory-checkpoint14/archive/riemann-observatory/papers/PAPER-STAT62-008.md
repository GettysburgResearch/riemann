# A central limit theorem for the zeroes of the zeta function

Brad Rodgers (2014). International Journal of Number Theory, 483–511. arXiv:1205.0303v5 (2014-05-10).

Theorem 1.2 extends Fujii’s mesoscopic CLT to compactly supported real bounded-variation test functions with divergent integral of |x||η̂(x)|². The random height is uniform on [T,2T], and the scale n(T) tends to infinity with n(T)=o(log T). Variance is asymptotic to that integral truncated at ±n(T).
