# Zero-free regions inspired by work of Heath-Brown

Chiara Bellotti, Tim Trudgian, Andrew Yang. arXiv:2603.21490v1, 2026-03-23.

Theorem 1 excludes zeta zeros for \(t\ge3,\ \sigma>1-(4.896\log t)^{-1}\), followed by conjugation for negative heights. Its finite argument through \(\exp(76.47)\) and the explicit Littlewood region beyond \(\exp(76.463)\) overlap. The preprint also displays a 4.8594 variant whose text makes the conclusion depend on the thesis bound and on a stronger finite Lemma 2 through \(\exp(56.693)\).
