## Source results used

For a quantum evolution map with energy-independent vertex scattering and2B directed channels, equation(5.5) gives positive phase velocities, bounded by shortest/longest bond length. Their sum is twice the total undirected bond length. Equations(5.7)–(5.11) split the count into a linear Weyl term plus a branch-controlled oscillatory contribution. The bound following(5.11),p38, is |N^osc(k+iε)|≤B. Choice of logarithm branch and the order ε→0+,δ→0+ matter.

This is an exact finite-matrix mechanism, not merely a semiclassical analogy. One must distinguish the2B directed channels of this standard second-order quantum graph from the E first-order oriented edges of PAPER-S003; otherwise a factor two enters the density.
