# Maximum of the characteristic polynomial of random unitary matrices

Louis-Pierre Arguin, David Belius, Paul Bourgade (2017). Communications in Mathematical Physics. arXiv:1511.07399v3 (2016-06-03).

For Haar U(N), the full-circle maximum of the log modulus divided by log N tends to 1 in probability. The high-point set at level γ log N has logarithmic measure exponent −γ² for fixed 0<γ<1. These are matrix-ensemble results.

Level 0. Opening setup and Theorems 1.2–1.3 checked in primary paper; proofs and publisher-vs-arXiv comparison outside scope. Private snapshot `sources/latest62_statistics/abb.pdf` (39 PDF pages).
