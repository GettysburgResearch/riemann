# Multipliers of Bergman spaces into Lebesgue spaces

Daniel H. Luecking. 1986 Cambridge journal version, Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society 29(1), 125–131. [Publisher](https://doi.org/10.1017/S001309150001748X)
