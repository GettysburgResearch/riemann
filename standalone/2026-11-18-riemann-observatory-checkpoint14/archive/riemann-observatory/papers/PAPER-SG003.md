*Multipliers for Entire Functions and an Interpolation Problem of Beurling*, J.Functional Analysis162(2)(1999),400–415. DOI[10.1006/jfan.1998.3357](https://doi.org/10.1006/jfan.1998.3357). [Repository source](https://hdl.handle.net/2445/164425).

Actual repository manuscript dated15August1998 accessed. Read introduction and exact definitions/Theorem1,pp1–3; the proof sections were not read. OverallLevel0/partial source inspection, not a whole-paper reading.

Theorem1,p2 gives Bernstein interpolation iff separation, a two-sided Carleson condition and strict upper uniform density belowtau/pi. On the real line Carleson is vacuous and separation is Euclidean. The strict density margin permits an independently reconstructed upgrade toPW_pi²: multiply bounded Bernstein Lagrange functions by a squared-sinc envelope and apply Schur's test.
