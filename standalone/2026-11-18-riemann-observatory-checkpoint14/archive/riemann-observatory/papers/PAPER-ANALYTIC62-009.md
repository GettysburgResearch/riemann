# An explicit Vinogradov-Korobov zero-free region for Dirichlet L-functions

Tanmay Khale. arXiv:2210.06457v1, 2022-10-12. Journal issue date 2024-04-15, The Quarterly Journal of Mathematics

For every character modulo \(q\ge3\), Theorem 1.1 excludes zeros for \(|t|\ge10\) and \(\sigma\ge1-[10.5\log q+61.5(\log|t|)^{2/3}(\log\log|t|)^{1/3}]^{-1}\). Beyond an absolute effectively computable threshold it replaces \(10.5,61.5\) by \(10.1,49.13\). This high-height result does not rule out a possible low real exceptional zero.
