# Explicit bounds for the Riemann zeta function and a new zero-free region

Chiara Bellotti. arXiv:2306.10680v1, 2023-06-19. Journal issue date 2024-08, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

Theorem 1.1 bounds \(|\zeta(\sigma+it)|\) and \(|\zeta(\sigma+it,u)-u^{-(\sigma+it)}|\) by \(70.6995\,|t|^{4.43795(1-\sigma)^{3/2}}(\log|t|)^{2/3}\), for \(|t|\ge3,\ 1/2\le\sigma\le1,\ 0<u\le1\) in the Hurwitz case. Theorem 1.2 gives a Vinogradov–Korobov denominator 54.004 for \(|t|\ge3\) in this v1, and the subsequent sufficiently-large-height constant is 48.0718.

Level 0. Theorem 1.1, v1 numbers in Theorem 1.2, and asymptotic distinction checked. PDF pp. 1–37 available privately.

Source locator Theorems 1.1–1.2 and accompanying formula (1.4).

The March 2026 Bellotti–Trudgian–Yang preprint cites the journal work with 53.989 instead of v1’s 54.004. That is evidence of a version difference, not a comparison of the journal proof itself.
