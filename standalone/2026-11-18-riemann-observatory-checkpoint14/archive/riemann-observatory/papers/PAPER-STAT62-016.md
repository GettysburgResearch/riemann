# Convergence in law of the maximum of the Riemann zeta function on expanding intervals near the critical line

Frédéric Ouimet (2026). arXiv:2611.01905v1 (2026-11-01).

Theorem 2.1 states an unconditional Gumbel limit with scale 1/(2√(1+θ)), fixed θ>0, about a uniform random height in [T,2T]. Its interval radius is (log T)^θ and its real part moves to 1/2 from the right under the prime-power cutoff and displacement hypotheses in (2.1). Centering uses an exact upper quantile in the auxiliary Haar model. This is a distinct regime from the critical-line maximum.
