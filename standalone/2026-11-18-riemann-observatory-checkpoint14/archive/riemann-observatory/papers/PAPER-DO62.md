**Theorem 2, p. 3.** For a Dirichlet character χ modulo q and b=1/p with 1<p≤2, zero-freeness of L(s,χ) in Re s>b is equivalent to the distance of 1 from L(s,χ) times ordinary Dirichlet polynomials tending to zero on Re s=b in L²(dt/[2π(b²+t²)]). The principal character modulo 1 gives ζ. The polynomial is unconstrained at s=1. Theorem 1 on p. 2 uses real-variable dilates of the summatory profile of the shifted L-function, and Theorem 5 on pp. 11–13 uses the broader bounded-remainder class and continuous dilations.

**Theorem 3, p. 3 and pp. 17–18.** With finitely many fixed distinct real nodes t_j and polynomial values one at b+it_j, the minimum squared Cauchy norm is asymptotic to (log N)^(−1) Σ_j(b²+t_j²)^(−1). Lubinsky's orthogonal Dirichlet polynomials give a Gram kernel with dominant diagonal; the proof uses the minimum Euclidean norm solution to the interpolation equations. This concerns the fixed distinct-node interpolation model.
