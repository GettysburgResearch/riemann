# Mesoscopic fluctuations of the zeta zeros

Paul Bourgade (2010). Probability Theory and Related Fields 148, 479–500. arXiv:0902.1757v1 (2009-02-10).

A joint Gaussian limit for log zeta at bounded moving shifts identifies the covariance from the logarithmic separation of the shifts. Its zero-count corollary is mesoscopic and shares the endpoint covariance structure of a Haar-unitary analogue.
