# PAPER-A007 — Arias de Reyna: a human exposition and a trace-buffer audit

J. Arias de Reyna, [*Human Exposition of Claude's 2/3 Theorem on the zeros of zeta*, arXiv2610.24612v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24612v1), preprint. The arXiv landing and v1 header give17October2026; its internal PDF Date line says20October2026.29pages. Accessed17November2026.

**Evidence scope:** full text accessed; partial technical reading of introduction, window/parameter choices, Propositions15–18 and final Proposition32. Bibliographic verification complete. Whole-work substantive reading and L2/L3 are not claimed. The introduction's polemical assessment of another paper is not adopted as an audit conclusion.

The exposition reconstructs a Weil-form discretization, rank/inertia inequalities, traces and a prime-side Hilbert–Schmidt norm leading to a stated simple-critical-zero proportion0.67250. Its buffer is U=T^theta with0<theta<1/2. Proposition16 pp15–16 obtains trG=N+O(U sqrt(T)logT), and footnote1 says this bound is insufficient for the earlier paper's U=sqrt(T). Proposition18 bounds the tail norm by O(exp(L/2)/U^2); Proposition32 pp27–28 requires only U^2 much larger than sqrt(logT)/loglogT for its relative second-moment accuracy, so those parameter requirements can coexist.

A source comparison finds that AF Proposition4.2's exterior-grid distance argument cannot be applied to zeros in its exterior buffer, and also ignores one floor endpoint. An independent inside/outside localization repairs that trace estimate even at U=sqrt(T), obtaining O(sqrt(T)) relative to N(I), and O(sqrt(T)logT) relative to N(I'). Thus Arias's coarse estimate does not demonstrate failure of the trace claim or main theorem.
