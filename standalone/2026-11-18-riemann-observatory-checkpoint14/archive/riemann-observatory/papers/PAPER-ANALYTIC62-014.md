# A note on the zeros of $L$-functions associated to fixed-order Dirichlet characters

C. C. Corrigan. arXiv:2310.16518v2, 2023-10-31. Journal issue date 2024-10, Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society

For \(Q,T\ge2,\ 1/2<\sigma<1\), Theorem 2.1 sums zero counts over primitive quadratic characters of conductor at most \(Q\), giving \(O_\epsilon((QT)^\epsilon\min\{(QT)^{8(1-\sigma)/3},(Q^8T^5)^{(1-\sigma)/(6\sigma-3)}\})\). Theorems 2.2–2.3 handle orders 3, 6 and 4 with their own conductor exponents.
