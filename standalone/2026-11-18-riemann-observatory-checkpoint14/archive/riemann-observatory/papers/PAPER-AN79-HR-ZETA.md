## Reading and result

**Source locator** Printed pp. 668–670, Theorem 1.1, Lemmas 2.1–2.2, Proposition 2.3 and (2)–(6); title p. 667

Theorem 1.1 states convergence of the first m local zero-statistic moments to Gaussian moments, with mean ∫f and variance ∫min(|u|,1)f̂(u)²du, for real even f with f̂∈C_c∞ and supp(f̂)⊂(−2a/m,2a/m). Centers are averaged continuously at H=T^a, 0<a≤1, against a nonnegative mass-one weight w with compact Fourier support in the printed setup. The scaled statistic evaluates the entire extension of f at complex zero ordinates, and the paper does not assume RH. An accompanying scope review examines tail integrability and the sufficient rapidly decreasing subclass.

The cited note gives a translated band-limited density mixture with divergent log moment under the literally listed weight conditions and presents a sufficient Schwartz setting. Preserve this as an audit candidate separate from the source rendering.
