# Theorem on the “universality” of the Riemann zeta-function

S. M. Voronin (1975). Math. USSR-Izv. 9(3), 443–453; Russian original Izv. Akad. Nauk SSSR Ser. Mat. 39(3), 475–486.

The original universality work concerns uniform approximation by vertical zeta translates of a nonvanishing holomorphic target on an interior disc in the critical strip. Its role is the starting point for Bagchi’s distribution and recurrence line.
