# An unconditional $\mathrm{GL}(n)$ large sieve

Jesse Thorner, Asif Zaman. arXiv:1906.07717v6, 2021-03-10. Journal issue date 2021-02, Advances in Mathematics

Theorem 1.2 bounds the sum of \(N_\pi(\sigma,T)=\#\{\rho:\beta>\sigma,\,|\gamma|\le T\}\) over cuspidal \(\pi\) on \(\mathrm{GL}_n\) over \(F\) of analytic conductor at most \(Q\) by \(O_{n,[F:\mathbb Q]}((QT^{[F:\mathbb Q]})^{(7/10)n^4(1-\sigma)})\), for \(Q,T\ge1\), \(0\le\sigma\le1\). The log-free estimate is unconditional in this family and does not assume Ramanujan.

Level 0. Theorems 1.1–1.2 and family definition checked in v6. PDF pp. 1–17 available privately.

Source locator Theorem 1.2 and (1.4).
