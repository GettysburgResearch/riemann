# Random Matrix Theory and ζ(1/2 + it)

J. P. Keating, N. C. Snaith (2000). Communications in Mathematical Physics 214(1), 57–89.

Exact finite-size Haar-unitary characteristic-polynomial moments and a Gaussian limit provide the matrix side of the moment analogy. The proposed zeta moment leading term includes an Euler product and a Barnes G-factor; the latter comes from the CUE calculation.
