# Zero statistics, universality and extreme values

Bibliographic and source access checked 17 November 2026. The 16 selected works cover three related questions. The records identify versions and reading scope separately. [Machine-readable records](latest62_statistics_records.jsonl)

- **Analytic universality and recurrence.** [Voronin’s original universality theorem](PAPER-STAT62-001.md) and [Bagchi’s recurrence article](PAPER-STAT62-002.md) anchor the vertical-translation viewpoint. These two records rely on bibliographic sources and accessible previews. The first records distinct Russian and English pagination, and the second records the journal work separately from the contemporary ISI thesis entry.
- **Zero statistics and matrix comparisons.** [Odlyzko’s computed spacings](PAPER-STAT62-003.md) test microscopic correlations. [Rudnick–Sarnak](PAPER-STAT62-004.md) compute restricted-Fourier-support \(n\)-level statistics, with a separate RH requirement for their formulation with ordered real ordinates. [Keating–Snaith](PAPER-STAT62-005.md) derive exact matrix moments and use them in a zeta conjecture. [Bourgade](PAPER-STAT62-006.md) and [Rodgers](PAPER-STAT62-008.md) analyze mesoscopic fluctuations. Bourgade was read in full and has a technical companion covering both the zeta and CUE proofs. [Bourgade full reading](../audits/latest62_bourgade_fullread.md)
- **Moments and maxima.** [Fyodorov–Hiary–Keating](PAPER-STAT62-007.md) propose the freezing and second-order maximum model. [Harper](PAPER-STAT62-009.md) gives sharp-order conditional bounds for fixed global moments. [Arguin–Belius–Bourgade](PAPER-STAT62-010.md) treat the CUE leading maximum and high points, and [Arguin–Belius–Bourgade–Radziwiłł–Soundararajan](PAPER-STAT62-011.md) prove the leading typical critical-line maximum. [FHK I](PAPER-STAT62-012.md) and [FHK II](PAPER-STAT62-013.md) give complementary tails and tightness for unit-radius windows. The Part I record retains the acknowledged initial proof issue and the v2 submission note. Later results treat [shrinking mesoscopic windows](PAPER-STAT62-014.md), [one-point large deviations](PAPER-STAT62-015.md) and [the expanding-window maximum at displaced abscissae](PAPER-STAT62-016.md). The latter is a distinct range from the critical-line FHK problem.
