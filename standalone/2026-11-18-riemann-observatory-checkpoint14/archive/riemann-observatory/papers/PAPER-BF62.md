## Reading scope and relevance

Targeted Level 0 reading of the cover, title and acknowledgments, Lemmas 2.1 and 2.2 and their proofs on internal pp. 6–9, the entry to the Perron argument on internal pp. 30–31, and the terminal-connector discussion on internal p. 34. Scoped comparison against arXiv v1 included the corresponding near-line lemma. The full manuscript and other proof branches remain unread in this campaign.

- Lemma 2.1 invokes the Carneiro–Chandee extremal majorant in an RH explicit formula. The coefficient manipulation in (2.5)–(2.7) retains the logarithmic dependence of the prime-sum terms on the small shift.
- Lemma 2.2, (2.8), lets \(w=\log |t|\) and gives, under RH and \(0<\alpha=o(1/\log w)\), the reciprocal upper bound
  \[
  \log |\zeta(1/2+\alpha+it)|^{-1}
  \le \frac{w}{2\log w}\,K+O\!\left(\frac{w}{\log w}+\frac{w}{(\log w)^2}K\right),
  \quad K=\log\frac{1}{1-e^{-2\alpha\log w}}.
  \]
  This is the exact pointwise input for the optimized local Perron panel in the \(q=1\) candidate. The third regime also supplies the fixed-offset bound at \(\alpha=1/4\) used for its terminal connectors.
- The integer-power Perron deformation in §9.1 supplies historical proof context for the later Bui–Florea–Heycock Mertens contour. The local optimized twist in the archive is a separate transfer.
