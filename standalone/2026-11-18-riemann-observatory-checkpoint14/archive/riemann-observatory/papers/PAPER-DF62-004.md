# Joint marked moments and optimized zero-free half-planes for unitary Hecke L-functions

Baiying Liu. Hexagon 2611.00421v1, deposited 13 November 2026. The inspected registry shows v1 current. The complete primary PDF has 54 pages. [Pinned version](https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00421v1) · [Primary PDF](https://hexagonmath.org/pdf/2611.00421v1)

Targeted reading of the introduction and Theorem 1.1, the exact root and positive-comparison Theorem 7.2 on pp. 22–24, the finite-order Theorem 8.14 and proof on pp. 46–47, and the unitary argument in §9.3. Level 0. The extensive joint-moment proof, earlier analytic dependencies and any formal proof artifacts were outside full audit.

Theorem 1.1 states a strict zero-free half-plane \(\Re s>B_*\) for every continuous unitary Hecke character over every number field, with the simple norm-character pole at \(1+it\) allowed. Zeta is the principal case over \(\mathbb Q\). The boundary is \(B_*=(99r_*-14)/(12(9r_*-1))=0.87334585610671857680118\ldots\), where \(r_*\) is the isolated root in \((7523220538/10^{10},7523220539/10^{10})\) of
\[
 \left[\frac{19(3r-2)(9r+2)}{3(45r-14)}\right]^5=7r^4(r-2/3).
\]
Section 7 supplies a degree-nine polynomial and claims optimality within its positive comparison system. The statement does not include the boundary line. The common numerical boundary coexists with constants that may depend on the field and character.

Theorem 8.14 handles finite-order characters and their vertical norm twists. It starts with the earlier zero-free boundary to justify an input moment parameter and uses finite rational joint-moment constructions with strict margins at each target \(B>B_*\). It then descends from fields containing the twelfth roots of unity. Theorem 9.3 separately uses Hu's unitary theta comparison and Gao's mixed unitary character moment, with sign and angular factors retained, to cover general unitary characters. Neither conclusion follows from just the Guth–Maynard density bound.
