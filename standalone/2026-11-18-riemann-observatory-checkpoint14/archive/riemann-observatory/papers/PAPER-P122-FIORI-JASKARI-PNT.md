# Explicit bounds for the prime number theorem

Stable ID: PAPER-P122-FIORI-JASKARI-PNT. Authors: Andrew Fiori, Mikko Jaskari. Version: arXiv:2609.23222v2 (2026-10-08).

Theorem 1.1 bounds |ψ(x)−x|/x for x≥x0≥e³ using D≈0.1982767 and the tabulated factor L(x0), with L(e³)=0.2390. Theorem 1.4 separately transfers a stipulated half-plane to a PNT bound.
