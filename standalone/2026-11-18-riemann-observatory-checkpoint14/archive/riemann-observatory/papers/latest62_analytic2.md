# Analytic density and zero-free regions, 2016–2026

Source access and bibliographic cutoff 17 November 2026. This batch adds 22 works as individual paper notes and machine-readable records. It records the proof version separately from the journal issue date. The dated search included October and November 2026 work on density, isolation and critical-line proportions. No priority or best-bound determination is attached to this sample. [Machine-readable records](latest62_analytic2_records.jsonl)

- **Density and detection for zeta.** The [explicit dyadic count](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-006.md), [Kadiri–Lumley–Ng](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-007.md), [Maynard–Pratt](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-008.md), [Pintz](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-013.md), [Bellotti near unity](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-015.md), [Bellotti's finite-height log-free estimate](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-016.md), and [Chourasiya–Simonič](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-017.md) have different hypotheses and different height ranges. [Dong–Wang–Wang–Zhang](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-019.md) force a local zero cluster from a single large arithmetic sum. The two complete readings cover Pintz and Dong–Wang–Wang–Zhang. [Technical reading notes](../audits/latest62_analytic2_fullreads.md)
- **Hecke and Dirichlet families.** The [Hecke character counts](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-001.md), [Chebotarev repulsion application](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-002.md), [automorphic large sieve](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-004.md), [Dedekind quotient family](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-005.md), [Dirichlet Vinogradov–Korobov region](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-009.md), and [fixed-order character density estimates](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-014.md) specify which character, conductor and field parameters are summed.
- **Explicit zeta zero-free regions.** The [Mossinghoff–Trudgian–Yang region](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-010.md), [Yang's Littlewood region](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-011.md), [Bellotti's v1 explicit zeta bounds](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-012.md), and [Bellotti–Trudgian–Yang's March 2026 result](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-018.md) all depend on height. The March result stitches its \(4.896\) classical constant to a separate explicit Littlewood region. Its stronger displayed \(4.8594\) variant remains open within the proof scope inspected here.
- **Critical, simple, distinct and isolated zeros.** [Pratt–Robles–Zaharescu–Zeindler](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-003.md) prove the \(5/12\) critical-line proportion by a mollifier. The 2026 preprints concern three different quantities, the [distinct count](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-020.md), the [union of simple and critical zero copies](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-021.md), and [two-sided isolation in ordinate](PAPER-ANALYTIC62-022.md). Each recent theorem is attributed to its primary source with the inspected proof scope.

Pintz's Corollary 4 supplies a power-saving BGSTB weighted contribution on a fixed right slab bounded away from \(7/8\) and 1. Neither this slab nor a sublinear-height estimate settles the moving near-critical boundary over a full height block.
