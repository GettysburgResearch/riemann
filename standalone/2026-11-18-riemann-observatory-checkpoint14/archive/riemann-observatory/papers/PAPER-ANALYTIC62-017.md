# An explicit form of Ingham's zero density estimate

Shashi Chourasiya, Aleksander Simonič. arXiv:2507.15184v2, 2025-09-30.

For \(T\ge H_{\rm RH}\), Corollary 1 gives \(N(\sigma,T)\le B_1T^{3(1-\sigma)/(2-\sigma)}(\log T)^{(7-5\sigma)/(2-\sigma)}+B_2\log^2T+B_3\log T\). The constants apply on the 16 subintervals \([n/32,(n+1)/32]\), \(16\le n\le31\), of Table 1. The strength for moving near-line \(\sigma\) is uniform in each fixed table interval, with explicit logs and the Ingham power intact.
