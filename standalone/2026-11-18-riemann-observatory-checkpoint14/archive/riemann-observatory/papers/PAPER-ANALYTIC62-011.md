# Explicit bounds on $\zeta(s)$ in the critical strip and a zero-free region

Andrew Yang. arXiv:2301.03165v2, 2024-01-16. Journal issue date 2024-06, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

Theorem 1.1 gives \(|\zeta(\sigma_k+it)|\le1.546\,t^{1/(2^k-2)}\log t\) for \(k\ge4,\ \sigma_k=1-k/(2^k-2),\ t\ge3\). Corollary 1.2 excludes zeros for \(|t|\ge3,\ \sigma>1-\log\log|t|/(21.233\log|t|)\), an explicit Littlewood-type region used in later finite-height patching.

Level 0. Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.2 checked in v2. No independent verification of the numerical optimization or comparison to subsequent bounds. PDF pp. 1–51 available privately.

Source locator Equations (1.2), (1.6).
