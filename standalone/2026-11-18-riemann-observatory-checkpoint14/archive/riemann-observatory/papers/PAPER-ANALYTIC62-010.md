# Explicit zero-free regions for the Riemann zeta-function

Michael J. Mossinghoff, Timothy S. Trudgian, Andrew Yang. arXiv:2212.06867v1, 2022-12-13. Journal issue date 2024-03, Research in Number Theory

Theorems 1.1–1.2 exclude zeros for \(\sigma\ge1-[C(\log|t|)^{2/3}(\log\log|t|)^{1/3}]^{-1}\) with \(C=55.241\) for \(|t|\ge3\), and \(C=48.1588\) for sufficiently large \(|t|\). Theorem 1.3 excludes zeros for \(\sigma\ge1-(5.558691\log|t|)^{-1}\) and \(|t|\ge2\). These are distinct height-dependent regions.
