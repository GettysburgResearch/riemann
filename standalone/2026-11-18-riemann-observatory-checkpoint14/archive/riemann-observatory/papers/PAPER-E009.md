# PAPER-E009 — Montgomery's original pair-correlation paper

Hugh L. Montgomery, *The pair correlation of zeros of the zeta function*, in *Analytic Number Theory* (St Louis,1972), Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics24, American Mathematical Society,1973, pp181–193. DOI [10.1090/pspum/024/9944](https://doi.org/10.1090/pspum/024/9944). [Author-hosted original scan](https://public.websites.umich.edu/~hlm/paircor1.pdf). For H=TlogT/(2π), w(u)=4/(4+u²), the original theorem, **assuming RH**, studies F(α,T)=H⁻¹Σ T^{iα(γ−γ′)}w(γ−γ′), zeros counted with multiplicity. It proves F real/even, globally F≥−ε for sufficiently large T, and F=(1+o(1))T^(−2α)logT+α+o(1), uniformly0≤α≤1−ε for each fixedε>0. The closed endpointα=1 and explicit modern error are not proved in this source's theorem. Its α≥1 conjectural extension and full pair-correlation conjecture remain conjectural here.

The central construction is an explicit formula with a Cauchy kernel in t−γ, squared and integrated. At σ=3/2 the prime coefficients are Λ(n)√n/x for n≤x and xΛ(n)/n^(3/2) for n>x. A Montgomery–Vaughan mean value plus PNT gives T(logx+O(1))+O(xlogx). This error identifies the range accessible by this proof, and the α>1 discussion reduces the obstruction to weighted shifted-prime correlations. RH enters when the full zeros are replaced by1/2+iγ; removing horizontal displacements would invalidate that prime-side formula.

The sinc-square test has transform(a−|α|)_+/a². For fixed0<a<1 its weighted pair sum is(1/a+a/3+o(1))H. Nonnegative real summands bound the multiplicity second moment Σ_distinct m². Let T→∞ first, then a↑1: Σm²≤(4/3+o(1))H. Since N_simple≥2N−Σm², at least2/3 of zeros are simple underRH. This is the precise historical antecedent of later unconditional complex-zero inequalities; it does not become unconditional simply because an ordinate-only weighted sum is positive.
