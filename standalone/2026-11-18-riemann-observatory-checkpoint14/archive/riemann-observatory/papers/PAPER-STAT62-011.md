# Maximum of the Riemann zeta function on a short interval of the critical line

Louis-Pierre Arguin, David Belius, Paul Bourgade, Maksym Radziwiłł, Kannan Soundararajan (2019). Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics 72, 500–535. arXiv:1612.08575v3 (2018-01-14).

Theorem 1.1 gives an unconditional leading scale log log T for the maximum of log |ζ| over |t−u|≤1 for a proportion tending to one of t in [T,2T]. It records independent conditional work by Najnudel and does not establish the FHK second-order law.
