# Half-isolated zeros and zero-density estimates

James Maynard, Kyle Pratt. arXiv:2206.11729v2, 2023-05-29. Journal issue date 2024-10-07, International Mathematics Research Notices

Under Hypothesis F that all nontrivial zeta zeros lie on finitely many fixed vertical lines, Theorem 1 gives \(N(\sigma,T)\ll T^{2(1-\sigma)}\exp((\log\log T)^{O(1)})\). Half-isolated zeros admit short detecting polynomials, and the argument separates horizontal and vertical clustering. The hypothesis is weaker than RH but remains an extra restriction on zeta zeros.

Level 0. Hypothesis F, Theorem 1 and the beginning of the detector statements checked in v2. Full proof outside scope. PDF pp. 1–39 available privately.
