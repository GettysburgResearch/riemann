# More than 8/9 zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple or on the critical line

Cristian Cobeli, Alexandru Zaharescu. arXiv:2610.23467v1, 2026-10-16.

Theorem 1 claims \(\liminf N_{s\cup0}(T)/N(T)>8/9\), with \(N_{s\cup0}=N_s+N_0-N_{0s}\). This is a union, so a multiple critical zero contributes its multiplicity; the excluded copies are exactly the multiple off-axis zeros. It is distinct from a lower bound on the intersection of simple and critical zeros. The paper reports approximately \(0.88893\) for its optimized constant.
