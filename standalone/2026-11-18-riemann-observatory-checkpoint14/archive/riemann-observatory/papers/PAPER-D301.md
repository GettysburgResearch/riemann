## Main source content

- Theorem3.1,pp10–11: A rational p×m para-unitary function of McMillan degree d has a representation by rank-one Blaschke–Potapov factors and a constant (co)isometry, in the appropriate left/right orientation. The source allows arbitrary poles off the unit circle and hence factors from both sides; it is not solely an inner function theorem.
- Remarks after Theorem3.1,p11: An arbitrary displayed product of d factors can have degree smaller than d through cancellation; reciprocal scalar factors on the same projection can cancel to identity.
- Theorem2.1,pp5–6: In its Schur-stable/exterior-disc convention, lossless rational functions admit a minimal state realization with an isometric/coisometric colligation, unitary in the square case.

## Limitations
