# Freezing Transition, Characteristic Polynomials of Random Matrices, and the Riemann Zeta Function

Yan V. Fyodorov, Ghaith A. Hiary, Jonathan P. Keating (2012). Physical Review Letters 108, 170601. arXiv:1202.4713v2 (2012-04-27).

The short-interval maximum conjecture uses a logarithmically correlated field and predicts a −(3/4) log log log T correction to log log T on the zeta side. The freezing heuristic, Toeplitz-moment comparison and finite-height experiments are distinguished from a theorem about zeta maxima.
