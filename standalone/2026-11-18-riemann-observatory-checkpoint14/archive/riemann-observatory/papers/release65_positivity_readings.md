## Three complete substantive readings

- **PAPER-REL65-CARDON.** David A. Cardon, *Extended Laguerre inequalities and a criterion for real zeros*. Read all six pages of the author-hosted manuscript, including §§1–3, Theorem 2.1 and references. The theorem uses a real entire function of the form \(e^{-bz^2}f_1(z)\), with \(b\geq0\) and \(f_1\) of genus zero or one. It characterizes real zeros with shifted-product inequalities for an even monic auxiliary polynomial with nonnegative coefficients and a nonreal root. The proof develops the shifted-product coefficient positivity by approximation and obtains the converse by testing at a real center corresponding to a nonreal root. If the auxiliary polynomial has a zero at the origin, division by the common even power of \(f(x)\) applies first where \(f(x)\ne0\), with continuity extending nonnegativity to real zeros. The recorded hash pins the author-hosted manuscript actually read. arXiv:0911.1122v1 is a separate manifestation.
- **PAPER-REL65-2002-09906.** Iskander and Jain, *Hyperbolicity of Appell Polynomials of Functions in the δ-Laguerre-Pólya Class*, arXiv:2002.09906v1. Read all eight pages, including the shift-interlacing argument, discrete class and closure limits, sampling intervals, exponential generating function and final application. The discrete class builds in spacing in the shifted real-rooted polynomials, and closure uses locally uniform convergence. The partition application treats the first term of the Hardy–Ramanujan–Rademacher asymptotic and uses the stated \(\alpha<3/2\) constraint. Full partition coefficient conclusions require separate control of the other terms.
- **PAPER-REL65-SCHOENBERG.** I. J. Schoenberg, *On totally positive functions, Laplace integrals and entire functions of the Laguerre-Pólya-Schur type*, PNAS 33 (1947), 11–17. Read all seven scanned pages, §§1–5, Theorems 1–5 and references. The first page starts with the end of another communication. The note defines total positivity by ordered translation-kernel minors and distinguishes the integrable Pólya-frequency case. It presents the bilateral Laplace representation of a reciprocal Type II entire function in a regularity strip, with a sign choice in the converse and an exception for pure exponentials. Zero must lie strictly inside the strip for the Pólya-frequency criterion. The neighboring-strip residue rule, the one-sided Type I variant and the gamma, sine and other examples use their respective domains. Section 5 gives the variation-diminishing lemma used in the proof outline. The short communication states the full theorem and outlines its proof. The full reading is of this communication.
