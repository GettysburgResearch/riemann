# Explicit zero density for the Riemann zeta function

Habiba Kadiri, Allysa Lumley, Nathan Ng. arXiv:2101.12263v1, 2021-01-28. Journal issue date 2018-09, Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications

Theorem 1.1, (1.7), gives \(N(\sigma,T)\le(C_1/(2\pi d))T^{8(1-\sigma)/3}(\log(kT))^{2\sigma}(\log T)^{5-4\sigma}+(C_2/(2\pi d))(\log T)^2\) for \(T\ge H_0,\ \sigma>1/2+d/\log H_0\), with \(10^9/H_0\le k\le1,\ H\in[1002,H_0),\ \alpha>0,\delta\ge1,\ 1+\eta_0\le\mu\le1+\eta,\ \eta\in(\eta_0,1/2)\), \(d>0\), and \(\eta_0=0.23622\ldots\). The preceding displayed (1.6) retains the logarithm with smaller numerical value. Constants \(C_1,C_2\) are explicit in (4.72)–(4.73).
