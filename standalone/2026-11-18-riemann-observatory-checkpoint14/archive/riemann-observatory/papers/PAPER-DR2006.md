# PAPER-DR2006 · Anne de Roton

*Une approche hilbertienne de l’hypothèse de Riemann généralisée*. Bulletin de la Société Mathématique de France **134**(3), 417–445 (2006). [Publisher record](https://numdam.org/articles/10.24033/bsmf.2516/)

Theorem 2 proves a continuous-dilation lower bound for a Selberg-class function $F$ whose complementary function $\Psi_F(1/x)$ lies in $L^2(0,\infty)$. If $D_F(\lambda)$ is the $L^2(0,\infty)$ distance of $\mathbf 1_{(0,1)}$ to finite linear combinations of $\Psi_F(\alpha/x)$ with $\lambda\le\alpha\le1$, then
$$
\liminf_{\lambda\downarrow0}D_F(\lambda)^2\log(1/\lambda)
\ge\sum_{\substack{\rho\ \mathrm{distinct}\\\Re\rho=1/2}}\frac{m_F(\rho)^2}{|\rho|^2}.
$$
The paper's §6.1 disposes of the off-line case using Theorem 1. The quantitative Gram limit in §§6–7 uses the critical-line zeros. On each block of size $m=m_F(\rho)$ the limiting Gram matrix is Hilbert's matrix, whose inverse top-left entry is $m^2$.

For fixed primitive nonprincipal Dirichlet $\chi$, its bounded character partial sums give the integrability assumption. Plancherel and the restriction $\alpha=1/n$ then give the same lower constant in the logarithmic limit inferior for the ordinary $n\le N$ approximation of 1 by $L(s,\chi)A_N(s)$ in $dt/(2\pi(1/4+t^2))$ on the critical line. This transfer imposes no restrictions on the complex polynomial coefficients and assumes no GRH or simple zeros.
