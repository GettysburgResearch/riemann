# Additional primary sources on equivalent criteria and approximation

The eighteen records cover exact continuous and discrete criteria, fixed-polynomial versus optimized distances, Hardy-space operators, generalized kernels, autocorrelation and Li-type tests. The selected bibliography and all existing raw paper tracks were checked for duplicates. Beurling 1955, Li 1997, Bombieri–Lagarias 1999, Báez-Duarte's strong theorem, Balazard–Saias, Bettin–Conrey–Farmer and Burnol's multiplicity-squared approximation lower bound already have records. Seventeen added records have complete primary PDFs. The new Nyman thesis record is catalog-based and has no primary full text. Three of the accessible works received full readings. [Source records](prefix_skeptic62_criteria2_records.jsonl)

## Full readings and reusable results

**Burnol, exact continuous projection.** I read all four pages of arXiv v3, including the constant-factor normalization and references. Let $NB$ be the closed $L^2(0,1)$ span of the functions $\{\alpha/x\}-\alpha\{1/x\}$ for $0<\alpha<1$. The Mellin map sends the target to $1/s$ and the generators to multiples of $\zeta(s)/s$ by $\alpha-\alpha^s$. Beurling–Lax identifies the continuous invariant space with $B H^2(\Re s>1/2)$, where $B$ is the Blaschke product of the right-of-line zeros with multiplicity. The analytic continuation of the generators and their behavior far to the right rule out common singular and exponential factors. The Hardy kernel at 1 is $1/s$ in this convention. Multiplication by $B$ is isometric, and the projected target is $B(s)\overline{B(1)}/s$. The paper normalizes $B(1)>0$, so this is its Lemma 2.4. Since $\|1/s\|=1$, the exact continuous squared distance is
$$
1-|B(1)|^2=1-\prod_{\Re\rho>1/2}|(1-\rho)/\rho|^2.
$$
The same factorization, evaluated at 1 after canceling zeta's pole, gives the integral of $\log|\zeta(s)|$ on the line with the Cauchy weight. These are results about the continuous contraction-invariant space. The discrete closed span is smaller a priori, so the continuous identity supplies a lower bound by inclusion. [Original arXiv v3, Theorems 1.2–1.3 and Lemma 2.4](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/burnol_nyman.pdf)

**Manzur and Noor, a vertical transport of Hardy zero kernels.** I read all seven pages of arXiv v1, including the derivation in the appendix-style AI-assistance section. On disk $H^2$ with coefficients $a_j$, the Copson operator $(Ca)_k=\sum_{j>k}a_j/j$ has norm at most 2 by the discrete Hardy inequality. The two diagonal multipliers are $D_\tau e_j=(j+1)^{i\tau}e_j$ and $Q_\tau e_j=j[(j+1)^{i\tau}-j^{i\tau}]e_j$ for $j\ge1$, with $Q_\tau e_0=0$. Thus $T_\tau=D_\tau+CQ_\tau$ has norm at most $1+2|\tau|$. Taking coefficient differences checks $(I-S^*)T_\tau=D_\tau(I-S^*)$, where $S$ is the shift. Injectivity of $I-S^*$ gives the group law. For the zero-test kernel $k_s$, its first $j+1$ coefficients sum to $-(j+1)^{1-\bar s}/\bar s$. Substitution into the adjoint formula gives
$$
T_\tau^*k_s=\frac{\bar s+i\tau}{\bar s}k_{s-i\tau}.
$$
This is a vertical transport. Let $\mathcal N$ be the closed span of the disk Nyman generators $h_k$. For any fixed $\tau\ne0$ the paper shows RH is equivalent to $T_\tau h_k\in\mathcal N$ for one $k\ge2$, and to $T_\tau\mathcal N\subseteq\mathcal N$. In fact the single-generator condition, applied to every hypothetical off-line zero, forces $\zeta(\rho-in\tau)=0$ for all $n$. The factor $k^{1-\rho+in\tau}-1$ cannot vanish when $1/2<\Re\rho<1$. Such a progression contradicts the fixed-strip Ingham density $o(T)$. The paper also gives a quantitative distance test against any zero kernel whose translate is not a zero. Membership of $T_\tau h_k$ is the condition to be proved in this reformulation. [Original arXiv v1, §§2–6 and Theorem 5.1](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/manzur_noor.pdf)

**Maier and Rassias, a prescribed-taper asymptotic under a specified quartet.** I read all six journal pages. Theorem 1.1 concerns the explicit logarithmically weighted Möbius polynomial $V_N$ from Bettin–Conrey–Farmer. It assumes exactly one off-line quartet, with right members $\sigma_0\pm i\gamma_0$, and the reciprocal-derivative moment used by the inherited Perron formula. Splitting its zero residues into critical-line and off-line terms isolates a leading squared norm on scale $N^{2\sigma_0-1}/\log^2 N$, with a constant term and sine and cosine terms at $2\gamma_0\log N$. The offset phase comes from pairing the two right-hand zero residues in the squared norm. The result evaluates a specific residual under these assumptions. In particular its growth estimate for $V_N$ gives no lower bound of that size for the optimized infimum over arbitrary Dirichlet polynomials. [Published article, Theorem 1.1 and §§2–3](../sources/ratio62_rstation48/maier_rassias.pdf)

## Other exact distinctions captured by the records

- Bagchi gives the integer floor-difference formulation in a step subspace and corresponding Mellin and $\ell^2$ formulations. This identifies the coefficient and norm model used by rStation 47. Hedenmalm–Lindqvist–Seip use a different Hilbert space of square-summable Dirichlet coefficients to identify cyclicity of periodic dilates. Their multiplier and Riesz-basis conditions do not directly apply to the weighted critical-line residual. [Bagchi](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/bagchi.pdf) · [Hedenmalm–Lindqvist–Seip](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/hls.pdf)
- Ghosh–Kremnizer–Noor–Santos Theorem 4.8 proves the disk $H^p$ approximation implication to $\Re s>1/p$, $1<p\le2$, and explicitly leaves the converse open in general. Calderaro–Manzur–Noor–Santos Theorem 2 excludes nonzero members of the orthogonal complement in a local Dirichlet space. Theorem 15 constructs a complete system biorthogonal to the Nyman generators. Its completeness concerns that dual system. Manzur–Noor–Santos Theorem 12 requires reduction of a weighted composition operator and supplies an equivalent condition. Mere invariance under the existing semigroup is different. [Analytic spaces](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/ghosh.pdf) · [Orthogonality](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/noor_calderaro.pdf) · [Weighted composition](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/manzur_semigroup.pdf)
- Darses–Hillion Theorem 8 obtains a RH implication from randomized approximation together with the summability and coefficient-tail condition in its statement. Alouges–Darses–Hillion Theorems 3–5 distinguish an approximation condition supplied by convolution or polynomial approximation from the additional control necessary for a zero-free conclusion. Pyvovarov studies the exponentially damped Möbius family and explicitly leaves boundedness of its full norm unresolved. [Random criterion](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/dar_hil.pdf) · [Polynomial approximation](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/alouges.pdf) · [Damped family](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/pyvovarov.pdf)
- The primary Báez-Duarte–Balazard–Landreau–Saias working text treats multiplicative fractional-part autocorrelation. Bettin–Conrey give the period-function and cotangent reciprocity behind separate kernel identities. Corvalán's Theorem 24 concerns a weighted extrapolation-space closure criterion. Voros's two papers analyze the asymptotic distinction between the Keiper–Li behavior on RH and terms induced by off-line zeros, including a separate modified sequence in the later work. Sekatskii moves the real base parameter of Li-type positivity away from $1/2$. The papers each have separate norm, transform or summation conventions, as recorded. [Autocorrelation](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/bb_autocorr.pdf) · [Cotangent reciprocity](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/bet_conrey.pdf) · [Interpolation](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/corvalan.pdf) · [Li asymptotics](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/voros_sharp.pdf) · [Modified sequence](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/voros_closed.pdf) · [Shifted Li criterion](../sources/prefix_skeptic62_criteria2/sekatskii.pdf)

## Access qualifications

Its original mathematical pages were unavailable in the sources inspected, so no page of the thesis is claimed read. The catalog-listed length of 53 pages is separate from a retrieved PDF count. Vasyunin's biorthogonality article and the pre-Burnol Notes 3 lower-bound paper are directly relevant follow-up leads. The original full text was not obtained here, so they are not included as primary readings or used to repeat a source record.
