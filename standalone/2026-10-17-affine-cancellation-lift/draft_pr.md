# Proposed title
Research: signed affine cancellation lifts to the weighted completions

## Scope

Proposed complete deductions from the established logarithmically averaged
Elliott theorem, with new source-preserving mean-square truncation and CRT
adapters. No RH, full moment, zero-free improvement, global priority or
independent mathematical review is claimed.

## Results

In the rational specialization, native Mobius and the coupled, two-channel
and geometric completions satisfy complete logarithmic binary cancellation
on every pair of fixed nonproportional affine forms. This is uniform over
all powerful-prime phase profiles for fixed degrees, characters and masks.

The proof handles coefficients as large as p^2 at p^6 without applying a
bounded-function theorem illegally. The actual power-factor tail has square
prefix at most X/R, and its complete binary-correlation error is at most
C log(X)/sqrt(R). Fixed truncated terms retain the correct shared-prime masks
and reduce to the bounded theorem by CRT, with an exact nonzero determinant.

Every fixed additive-filter mean square consequently has leading term
(delta_F log X)*sum|w_j|^2. Every fixed nonzero additive-lag Gaussian covariance
is o(log D), and the full fixed-band signed energy is asymptotic to its
explicit diagonal. Opposite signs actually cancel here; no sign-selected
positive sector is advertised as cancellation.

The stronger ordinary-average conclusion is proved as a separate transfer
of the newly released ordinary affine Elliott statement. Its scope note,
comparators and final wrapper were read at openai/math pin
1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a, but that formal theorem was not
independently rebuilt. The primary logarithmic result does not depend on it.

## Remaining gap

The fixed-shift bounds are not uniform over the growing lags needed for the
full critical energy. The proof does not apply silently to aggregated
Eisenstein norm fibers or a growing physical character-row family.
