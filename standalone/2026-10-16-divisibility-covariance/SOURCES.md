## Classical primary reference resources consulted

  https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.15
- Section 25.2, zeta definitions and expansions:
  https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.2
- Section 27.4, Euler products and Dirichlet series:
  https://dlmf.nist.gov/27.4

The manuscript itself reconstructs the required positive-half-plane
continuations from bounded partial sums and the fractional-part integral.
It proves the real sign of zeta_K on (0,1) by elementary paired/alternating
series. It does not invoke a zero-free region, PNT, Selberg--Delange as a
black box, the functional equation, or an unproved inverse-zeta estimate.

Other classical tools explicitly used are unique ideal factorization,
Eisenstein norm-Euclidean ideal counting, elementary partial summation,
Gaussian Fourier/Mellin inversion, normal convergence of products, and the
residue theorem. These methods are not claimed as inventions. The specific
0/1/m weighted Euler source is inherited.

## What's different from the predecessor

inequality for every unequal pair. Here the entire divisibility sector is
bounded absolutely; an infinite prime-quotient subfamily is evaluated with
a power-saving error and fixed sign; the full positive square-coefficient
observable has a nonzero secondary term; and a complete complementary
signed family is forced to have positive logarithmic lower growth.

Those are new quantitative conclusions at the stated scope, not another
assertion of equivalence with RH. The remaining incomparable-core sum is
still not controlled at the required size.
