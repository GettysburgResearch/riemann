### Boundary calculation

Complete annuli for Y=3,15,63,255, totaling 69,564 output cells (overlap across
cutoffs is counted), are reconstructed. Trial factorization and a separate
sieve agree on the Mobius source; boundary-divisor enumeration is checked
against direct divisor inversion. Both product-first Newton and g*e reproduce
every declared output coefficient. There are 27,231 enumerated boundary
incidences before convolution with the outer prefix.

Full coarse least-prime Grams, positive and negative off-diagonal sums,
individual and merged large-prime energies, updated means and completed
energies are enclosed with denominator 2^80. The existing NSR26 four-bank
experiment at Y=255 is independently reproduced. Four small primitive-step
Gram calculations use exact Fraction double sums rather than just cell sums.

Regression coverage includes the sharp excluded endpoint, the known fixed
fake-prefix divisor failure at n=6, a sign-scrambled singleton antichain,
non-squarefree inputs, invalid inputs, duplicate JSON keys and canonical
numeric-type distinctions. The type/mutation tests are in-process checks,
not a claim of separate full expensive subprocess runs for every mutation.

### Harmonic calculation

The actual capped completion, full product source and native boundary adapter
are checked coefficientwise for Y=3,7,15,63,255. Complete distinct-product and
rough-parity (empty bank and {2,3}) covariance panels are computed for Y=3,7,15.
The log enclosures use 70 terms of the positive atanh series with its explicit
remainder and denominator 2^100. Deleting the centered products above B is
explicitly detected in each small panel. There is no assumption that they
vanish just because their uncentered harmonic floor does.

### L-family calculation

Three nontrivial finite-field examples check the paper's Fermat and elliptic
identities, the lambda map, two Frobenius descriptions, nonzero kernel values
and their signs. Inadmissible field parameters and the trivial cubic-residue
control (q,p)=(13,53) are rejected. Ten projector checks cover both characters
at five group orders. The degree-two Euler fixture checks 683 boundary cases,
a split-prime control, and sixteen direct good-inert-prime point counts.
Simple-center, retained off-line-pair and wrong-rank deflation controls pass.

verify_receipts.py, which authenticates all three canonical SHA-256 digests
