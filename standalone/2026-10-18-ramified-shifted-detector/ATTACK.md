# Attempts, what changed, and what remains

The initial target was stronger zero density by keeping L times its actual
mollifier together. A direct joint second moment expands to length roughly
sqrt(U)*A. In the explored positive-sieve exponent model this did not beat
#966: at sigma=3/4 it gives 13/18 rather than 43/60. This is only a statement
about that displayed upper-bound calculation, not an arithmetic lower bound
or a general impossibility theorem. Higher central numerator/mollifier
moments in the same model likewise did not supply the desired gain there.

The surviving improvement changes the REAL detector line instead. The
numerator fourth moment improves to max(1,13/6-2lambda), while the mollifier
has the genuine same-line square mass. The changed B^(lambda-sigma) price
is paid. Lambda=6/11 yields the exact 395/552 count. The floating parameter
search was used only to suggest candidates; no optimizer output is a premise
of the proof or executable verifier. No optimality is claimed.

A second calculation returned to the exact local Poisson source. CT's
ramified shell majorant sums l and m independently, although the underlying
C_(t,k)(j+6m) vanishes on most of that rectangle. Summing the valuation cones
first gives five rational functions with the SAME denominator 1-e. In this
step the large common-prime contribution was not declared absent: for j=5
there is a negative boundary term; for j=3 two different boundary terms;
and the actual theta=chi(u/p^j) remains a unit even when chi(u)=0.
The finite tests explicitly reject erasing these distinctions.

The new local domain reaches the critical face for the correction with the
remaining convergence margins. Below it the exact prime-square term gives
a real polynomial obstruction to uniform q_u^epsilon control; a weighted
family estimate is available instead. This suggests a mixed correction/
numerator calculation before a supremum, but does not prove the corresponding
inverse-denominator moment or justify a contour crossing its zeros.

No new global zero-free fraction is given in this pass. A complete retuning
would have to use all density thresholds, every actual contour and the
short-support reflection theorem. Merely adding the local domain to the
old table is not such a proof. In particular a better nonprincipal domain
does not change the principal H(s) at z=1/6 or make it converge at 1/2.

Review priorities are the five rational ramified factors and their source
normalization; the actual-Q0 dual AFE estimate; and the shifted rowwise
zero-detector maxima. These are mathematical obligations, not tasks that
more JSON test counts can discharge. The signed critical native estimate,
full all-order extraction, and RH remain open within this packet.
