# Remaining routes to a smaller numerical boundary

The packet improves a cubic estimate and proposes a larger Hecke family
at the same boundary. A smaller numerical boundary still needs an input
which changes the binding comparison.

1. **Preserve the coherent selected label sum.** At the physical terminal
   \(U=KL\), with quadratic conductor \(K\gg L\), the inherited target
   is \(KL\). The new pointwise quadratic bounds remain larger. Even a
   natural-count joint completed mean, used with Cauchy in the labels,
   only recovers \(KL\) on the central line. A strict saving must retain
   the prescribed label coefficients or prove a stronger statement for
   their particular smooth block. See
   [JOINT_CONDUCTOR_INTERFACE.md](mixed/JOINT_CONDUCTOR_INTERFACE.md).

2. **Retain the signs in the long inverse transform.** The unselected
   inverse Poisson step has dual row scale \(D^2/H\). The available
   positive canonical bound gives \(H+D\), so it does not extend the
   raw row-scale estimate into \(H<D\). The signs are present before
   the positive child enlargement. A centered identity or a direct
   estimate for that signed expression is a separate target; a better
   positive bound cannot simply be assumed from the new cubic mean.
   See [CENTERED_INVERSE_INTERFACE.md](mixed/CENTERED_INVERSE_INTERFACE.md).

3. **Use the full detector domain.** The inherited proposed
   \(U^{6/5+\epsilon}\) mixed consumer ranges over
   \(1\le r\le3/2\), \(0\le m\le1/2\),
   \(3/2\le r+m\le2\). Proving only its familiar corner is insufficient.
   A smaller improvement in the native long-inverse exponent near the
   binding cutoff may be enough for a new scalar comparison, but it
   must be supplied on a robust interval and then propagated through
   the whole continuous domain.

4. **Audit the new analytic assemblies before integration.** The complete
   upstream analytic recursion and initial seven-eighths step remain
   imported inputs; the closing tangent step now has an exact replay.
   The most focused new objects for external review are the actual
   four-channel cusp extraction, the full resummed reflection, the
   all-field constant-term transport, and the finite-place guarded
   physical probe. Their source conventions and reviewed hashes are
   recorded separately.

The current scalar endpoint cannot be improved merely by reusing the
already included additive Gram estimate, interpolating the recorded
numerator endpoints, or treating a restricted Mobius divisor sum as the
full convolution \(\mu*1\). These failed routes are documented so later
work can start at the remaining analytic problem.

RH requires a complete argument down to one-half. No such argument is
provided by the improvements above, by the signed factor-removal
threshold, or by the finite computations.

The later signed estimates narrow these targets but do not close them.
The [long-gcd tail](global/SIGNED_MOBIUS_GCD_TAIL.md) controls a range of
large common divisors; the short part leaves the jointly dependent
[reciprocal kernel](global/RECIPROCAL_PROJECTOR_KERNEL.md). The
[Mobius-complement factorization](type_i/SIGNED_MOBIUS_COMPLEMENT.md)
controls a long complementary variable after exact reflection, while
the full-divisor term remains. Any use in a prime decomposition must
also preserve the actual prime-detection coefficients and all new
ranges. The [opposite-cubic joint mean](mixed/OPPOSITE_CUBIC_JOINT_MEAN.md)
has a different row character from the binding sextic terminal.
