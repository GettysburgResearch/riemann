The reviewer authored the earlier centered interface but not this
reciprocal-kernel note.

The Euler product has the correct inverse local corrections. They
restore the natural zero at a prime where the two column exponents
cancel and equal one at a primitive-conductor prime. The Mellin
identity uses `D(1+z)` and `S^z`, consistent with the original `1/Ns`
normalization. Keeping the complete common smooth profile makes its
contour shift identical to the already reviewed signed-tail argument.

For the good squarefree columns, distinct ideals have a prime in their
symmetric difference, where the primitive tame cubic character remains
nontrivial. Thus the principal character occurs precisely on the
diagonal. The simple zero of the reciprocal at `w=1` there is valid,
and the note correctly distinguishes this single spectral value from
the full arithmetic divisor cancellation. It cannot annihilate an
arbitrary smooth diagonal profile by itself.

No defect was found. The reciprocal depends on both columns jointly;
its boundedness does not authorize replacing it by a separate bounded
coefficient in the pure cubic mean. The note isolates an unresolved
coherent kernel and does not supply a short-divisor or boundary gain.
