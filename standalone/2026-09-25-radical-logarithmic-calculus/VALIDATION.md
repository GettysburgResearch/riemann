# RLC35 validation record

## Executed replay

The final standard-library checker ran normally and optimized, with
byte-identical receipts:

```sh
python -I -S -B check.py --output receipt.json
python -I -S -B -O check.py --check receipt.json --output replay_optimized.json
cmp receipt.json replay_optimized.json
```

A separate receipt copy had the numerator of the first capped-source defect
changed by one. A complete fresh primitive replay rejected it with exit 1 and
`REJECT: receipt does not match primitive replay`. The tampered copy is not
part of the published packet. No Python `assert` is used for acceptance.

Environment: Python 3.13.5, Linux,
standard library only. These executions do not constitute an independent
mathematical review.

## What was actually checked

The receipt records **5,036 explicit predicates**. The
10,310 divisor-pair terms accumulated in the complete covariance panels are
reported separately as arithmetic coverage, NOT inflated into predicate counts.

- Every Möbius coefficient through 511 is generated by a prime sieve and
  authenticated by a separately implemented triangular Dirichlet inverse.
- Radical compatibility is tested with an unrelated rational kernel; a
  non-radical control genuinely fails the squarefree-support condition.
- Formal prime-log identities for all kernel orders 0 through 4 and every
  coefficient through 127, including the exact two-prime/prime-power pattern.
- Full rational d,e covariance sums at N=15,31,63 for two damping profiles:
  all 10,310 ordered pair terms are included. They are compared to direct
  weighted-source energies, not selected pair samples.
- 218 off-grid rational Abel identities, retaining the last partial cell.
- Nine complete symbolic quadratic-balance panels: orders 1,2,3 at N=7 and
  N=15; orders 1,2 at N=31; and order 1 at N=63. Positive bulk and endpoint
  terms are kept. The log-polynomial identity residual is exactly zero.
- 161 rational checks of the phase multiplier inequality and an exact
  maximizing frequency for tau=3/4, kappa=2. These validate the normalization;
  the all-real-parameter bound and its sharpness use the written Fourier proof.
- Full capped-source defects and tensor identities at native prefix lengths
  2,3,5,7,15, plus a complex Gaussian-rational phase control at length 5.
  The largest defect coefficient index is 259. All product coefficients up to
  each declared cutoff are included.
- Six complete finite bilinear kernel adapter pairings. A conjugated/Hermitian
  replacement is actually rejected on the complex phase control.
- Exact threshold, nonzero first defect, masked quadratic-error and terminal
  reciprocal-balancing controls. The wrong operator-composition rule and an
  altered primitive Möbius coefficient are genuinely rejected.

## Arithmetic contract

Acceptance uses integers, Fraction arithmetic, exact Gaussian rationals, and
formal multivariate polynomials in prime logarithms. No unproved algebraic
independence of prime logarithms is used: equality of formal polynomials is a
sufficient exact certificate, not a necessary criterion for equality of real
values.

The positive-term displays use outward intervals. Logarithms use a positive
atanh series with 64 terms and an explicit tail bound after reducing to
[1,2). Interval arithmetic is outward-rounded at 112 dyadic bits; published
endpoints are rounded outward to 40 bits. Floating values are display-only.
The exact integer endpoints and formal-polynomial hashes are authoritative.

The rational g(t)=(1-t)^4 product-kernel control is NOT the actual centered
harmonic kernel of MHB32. It tests the cutoff argument, not that kernel's
transcendental value, sign, or bound. The Gaussian phase is a completely
multiplicative arithmetic control; it is not asserted to be n^(i*tau) for a
single real tau, and no phase-norm bound is tested on that different character.

## Not established or not rerun

No independent mathematical review, proof-assistant build, repository-wide
validator, remote CI verdict, or rerun of the full RAB33/NJV34/MHB32/CQT32
campaigns is claimed. Universal analytic statements rely on the written
proofs; finite checks alone do not establish them. No native asymptotic energy
improvement, bounded inverse of the Hankel adapter, bound for its entire
residual, full composite covariance estimate, or RH proof is claimed.
