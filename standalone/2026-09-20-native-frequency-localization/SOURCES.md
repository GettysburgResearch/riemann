## Contribution and limits

Proposed additions: the complete q-weighted amplitude moment with explicit
constant; its all-denominator angular covariance bound; the full prime-power
budget; exact scale-local native assembly; and localization of any power-sized
energy growth to near-zero mixed-prime modes. The elementary ingredients are
classical. External novelty of this particular combination is unestablished.

The principal bound holds for capped nonnative sources too. Native inversion
binds the local outputs to mu, but the remaining near-zero arithmetic estimate
is not supplied by that binding. A bounded source, a finite validation run,
or an equivalence to a remaining estimate is not a full RH argument.
