## Endpoints, exceptions, and fixed parameters

All zero-free half-planes are open. A pure norm character has a translated
principal pole, not a zero there. Balancing is performed before the moment
argument; the common norm twist is restored afterwards. The two balanced
resonant types are excluded explicitly and are not recovered by a limiting
argument in radial parameters.

The shared exponent does not imply constants uniform as angular weights,
radial parameters, or number fields vary. The moving finite-conductor
uniformity needed within each fixed-family moment is separately proved.
The infinity tuples belong to actual Hecke characters and obey global
unit compatibility.
