matching a desired Bessel shape. The source's constant-term exponents

is sufficient even when the other places are spherical.

For the radial addendum I checked the direct shift (t-ig_v), reflected

## 3. What the finite replay proves

`verify_local_algebra.py` uses exact Gaussian rationals for a two-factor
matrix model of the complexified real Lie algebra. It checks the
opposite-factor brackets, nonzero same-factor controls, Weyl and compact
identities, exact Mellin-shift formulas at the stated representative
degrees, and the all-power Gaussian nonresonance determinant.

It uses rational interval arithmetic to isolate one simple root of the
stated quartic, with a strict sign change and a derivative interval
avoiding zero. Exact affine substitutions produce the principal and
Type-I exponent intervals. It also checks the cleared rational identity
behind the dual local estimate. The monotonicity and positivity domain
are supplied in the written analytic proof and this review.

The checker was run in ordinary and optimized Python with identical
output. Explicit exceptions enforce acceptance under either mode.
`LOCAL_ALGEBRA.json` is the output, not an external mathematical input.
`replay.py` verifies file coverage, byte counts and hashes, the local
inherited principal proof, local links, and byte-for-byte regeneration
of that receipt in isolated standard-library Python. The containing Git
commit binds the manifest; the manifest does not authenticate itself.

No computation here proves the existence of the automorphic
representation, a gamma-function integral, an inverse/product moment,
contour continuation, the scalar tangent comparison, or global
zero-freeness. No finite zero census or numerical fit is used as
evidence for an infinite theorem.
