# Stronger protection of the inherited principal residue

Date: 20 October 2026.

Status: a consequence of the source-dependent angular theorem in

## 1. The actual character and the new boundary

Fix a number field \(F\supset\mu_{12}\), the finite excluded set \(S\),
and a finite-order Hecke target \(\eta\). In Liu's principal correction,
put

\[
 \nu_\eta=(\overline\Lambda\eta)^6=\Lambda^{-6}\eta^6.
\]

The earlier packet proves, from the exact local correction in source
equation (13.9),

\[
 H_\eta(s)=L_F^S(6s-3,\nu_\eta)K_\eta(s),
 \qquad K_\eta\text{ holomorphic and nowhere zero for }
             \operatorname{Re}s>1/2.
 \tag{1}
\]

That statement includes the full target power \(\eta^6\). Its
Eisenstein-field case and the underlying extraction mechanism were
already present in PR #1006. The general-field proof resident in this
branch is
[principal_factorization.md](../2026-10-20-liu-all-field-artin-and-barrier/principal_factorization.md),
frozen originally at commit
`e2bd86d1d062ac1dc4140d52331797b81d7d825d`.

The character \(\nu_\eta\) has angular vector \(-6\lambda\).
It belongs to the nonresonant family generated by \(\Omega=\Lambda^6\),
since an integer multiple of \(6\lambda\) cannot be \(\lambda\)
or \(-\lambda\). The new angular theorem therefore supplies

\[
 L_F(z,\nu_\eta)\ne0\qquad(\operatorname{Re}z>B_\flat).
 \tag{2}
\]

Its nonzero infinity type excludes a pole. The finite Euler factors
omitted from \(L_F^S\) have zeros only on \(\operatorname{Re}z=0\).
Combining (1) and (2) proves

\[
 \boxed{H_\eta(s)\ne0\quad\text{for}\quad
  \operatorname{Re}s>c_\flat:=\frac{3+B_\flat}{6}
       =0.645814441173327\ldots .}
 \tag{3}
\]

This is an open half-plane. No assertion at its boundary is implied.

| Input protecting the extracted angular function | Region for \(H_\eta\) |
|---|---:|
| Absolutely convergent Euler product | \(\operatorname{Re}s>2/3\) |
| New preliminary inverse theorem, \(\beta=11/12\) | \(\operatorname{Re}s>47/72\) |
| New angular continuation, \(\beta=B_\flat\) | \(\operatorname{Re}s>(3+B_\flat)/6\) |

The improvement in the principal-protection threshold is exactly

\[
 \frac23-c_\flat=\frac{1-B_\flat}{6}
                  =0.020852225493339\ldots .
\]

## 2. Multiplicities and the principal Mellin quotient

Throughout \(\operatorname{Re}s>1/2\), identity (1) identifies the
zeros of the continued principal correction, including multiplicities,
with

\[
 s=\frac{\rho+3}{6},\qquad L_F(\rho,\nu_\eta)=0,
       \quad\operatorname{Re}\rho>0.
 \tag{4}
\]

The affine substitution has nonzero derivative, and its other factors
are holomorphic units there. Hence (4) preserves the order of each
zero. In particular, at a zero \(s_0\) of the target \(L_F(s,\eta)\)
with \(\operatorname{Re}s_0>c_\flat\), the principal quotient

\[
 \frac{H_\eta(s)}{L_F^S(s,\eta)}
\]

has a pole of exactly the target's zero multiplicity. This improves
the earlier \(2/3\) guarantee for absence of cancellation in this
specific quotient.

## 3. What is required to use this in a lower-boundary argument

The new protection does not estimate the direct compensated theta
average, the selected inverse moment, the remaining product moment,
or the reflected numerator. Continuing the principal factor farther
left does not move the full proof's other contours.

In particular, (3) is not a proof that a finite-order Hecke function
has no zeros to the right of \(0.6458144\). That number is the
boundary for the principal *correction* in (1). The finite-order
Hecke boundary remains \(B_\flat\).

The contribution is nevertheless an analytic improvement to a
previously identified repository obstruction: the relevant angular
factor now has a proposed zero-free theorem, instead of only its
Euler-product region. The complete mixed-moment target still needed
for the separate lower-boundary route is recorded in
[MIXED_MEAN_STATUS.md](MIXED_MEAN_STATUS.md).
