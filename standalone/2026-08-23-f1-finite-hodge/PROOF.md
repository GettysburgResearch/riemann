# Native Frobenius boxes, toric Hodge index, and the three-ray primitive trace

For one labelled prime interval of logarithmic length \(\lambda\),

\[
(I-e^{-\lambda/2}S_\lambda)f(t)
=
\int_0^\lambda e^{-u/2}(D+1/2)f(t-u)\,du.
\]

Tensoring this identity realizes the complete finite native Euler source as the
twisted corner boundary of the prime box. Causal zero extension cuts the fixed
box by the physical tropical half-space; it does not change the source.

The completion tangent is a divisor-valued current, one coefficient per
labelled prime. In the Chow ring of the toric product \((\mathbf P^1)^E\), the
primitive Hodge form is exactly minus the variance of those owner
coefficients. Its normalized spectral gap is one, independently of the number
or size of the primes.

For the three positive filtered SHARP rays of PR #719, the coefficient vector

\[
(17/2,-16,15/2)
\]

is primitive because its entries sum to zero. The physical current \(5A-G\) is
the corresponding mixed intersection. The exact primitive ray energy is

\[
\mathcal H(A,G)
=
\frac{49A^2-96AG+48G^2}{24},
\]

and

\[
\frac{769}{2}\mathcal H(A,G)-(5A-G)^2
=
\frac{(191A-192G)^2}{48}.
\]

Thus the geometry proves the optimal pointwise trace inequality and identifies
the orthogonal primitive mode. The remaining global theorem is the subpower
arithmetic packing of \(\sqrt{\mathcal H}\). This is a root-free same-source
near-collision problem. It is not proved here, and RH remains unproved.
