## Acceptance and actual refusals

The verifier requires a present regular manifest and exact flat-file inventory,
authenticates all eight other file hashes, rejects duplicate JSON keys and
floating/nonfinite numeric syntax, and compares canonical typed JSON with its
fresh complete expected reconstruction. Boolean/integer aliases therefore do
not pass Python's permissive numeric equality. No assert controls acceptance.
The manifest cannot by itself authenticate a malicious replacement of both
checker and manifest; immutable Git/source identities provide that boundary.

The self-test first reconstructs and accepts the pristine report, then runs12
DISTINCT resealed mutations through the same acceptance function. Mutations
alter the claimed RH/unbounded sign status, panel/index coverage, boundary,
mathematical values, native/fake designation, coefficient/energy identity or
numeric types. A duplicate-key parser case is separate. These are not12 full
subprocess runs. The executed command outputs are recorded in validation.json.

## Development history and limits

Initial noncertifying floating scouts evaluated a selected native panel list
and proposed the finite fake control. One larger scout timed out after the
Y=173 display; its incomplete remainder is not counted as replay. The final
producer and verifier reconstruct the selected finite protocol without those
floats. Compact result records retain primitive hashes instead of printing
very large repeated rational prefix lists; both programs recompute those lists.
No mathematical sample was removed on account of its sign.

An early logarithm factor-loop used a floating square-root bound on bounded
integers; it was replaced by math.isqrt before final validation. A development
manifest bypass was removed: a missing manifest is an error in the submitted
verifier. No final data value changed through those hardenings.

Normal and optimized executions of both implementations are recorded separately
in validation.json. They do not independently prove the analytic limit,
cofinality, or native all-scale covariance inequality. The programs and proofs
have the SAME author. No parent suite, full authenticated repository checkout,
repository-wide validator, external formalization, zero census, spectral
campaign, remote CI success or independent mathematical acceptance is claimed.
Direct repository access in the container failed DNS; connector reading and
publication are not a complete checkout. Any patch/ZIP fixture replay is
recorded separately outside this packet and preserves this scope.
