## Two qualifications to blanket negative conclusions

1. A locally uniformly convergent entire approximation can locate any fixed
   hypothetical nonreal limiting zero, with multiplicity, by Rouché once its
   error is sufficiently small on a zero-free enclosing contour. It does not
   prove the zero absent. Thus “the approximation cannot see any off-line zero”
   is too broad; the real missing result is zero confinement or a sign mechanism,
   not eventual detection of a fixed zero.

2. Finite certificates alone do not settle arbitrary high-zero behaviour. A
   finite certificate plus an independently proved complete complement bound
   can settle the particular global functional it covers. Therefore proposed
   entropy comparisons and claims about what no finite Gram can ever say need
   their precise class and source hypotheses. The numerical entropy comparison
   quoted in the critique was NOT independently adjudicated in this pass.

These points do not rescue an RH proof from the prior results. They keep the
criticism as precise as the positive claims should be. The specific four
literature “records,” the entire gamma and branching programmes, and the
unseen theta/Ising thread report have not all been independently audited here.

## What should be attacked next, rather than another equivalence

The existing all-order RH objects remain legitimate research targets, but a
new representation should not be scored as progress on them unless it proves
an estimate not previously available. For this numerical route, the relevant
next target is a stronger *proved* local pressure or multi-profile inequality,
with a closed BGST normalization and a comparison against the current external
claim. A scalar second-moment optimization alone cannot supply that gain; it
already has its known Montgomery--Taylor optimum. The present calculation uses
extra local overlap information rather than pretending that optimum moved.

The next work product should be either an actual improvement over an audited
benchmark, a proof repairing a genuine analytic gap, or a source-specific
inequality with a strictly stronger unconditional consequence. A new tuple of
letters for the same unresolved RH-strength sign is not such a product.
