# Review specification

Reviewers should reconstruct:

1. the exact \(n=24\) divisor-cube residue;
2. the \(P=30\) rough-store mismatch;
3. the coefficient formulas \(a_2,a_3\);
4. the Riesz identities and integer-cell recurrence;
5. the fixed-row Mellin transform;
6. the exact common-zero elimination for \(P_2,P_3\);
7. the Landau quantifiers for eventual nonnegativity.

The deterministic checker is a regression and falsification aid.  It does not
prove `TRP23` or RH.
