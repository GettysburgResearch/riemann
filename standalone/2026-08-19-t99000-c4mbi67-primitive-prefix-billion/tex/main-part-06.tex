\subsection{Why a PSD completion is not enough}
A symmetric completion

\]
proves $|A|\le\Gamma$, not $A\ge0$.  The matrix with $\Gamma=1$ and
$A=-1/2$ is positive definite and has negative off-diagonal.  Hence an exact
Tao/Schur magnitude port still needs an oriented cross-scale comparison.

\subsection{Why the finite theorem is not an asymptotic tail}
The barrier in \eqref{eq:Ddef} is the fixed number $6$.  Classical zero-free
regions give cancellation estimates for $B_{1/2}(t)$ that are subexponential
relative to $\sqrt t$, but those upper bounds still grow without bound and
cannot be compared to $6$ for all large $t$.  Thus no standard PNT error term
extends Theorem \ref{thm:billion} to infinity.
