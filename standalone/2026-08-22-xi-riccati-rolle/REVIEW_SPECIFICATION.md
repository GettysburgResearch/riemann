# Hostile review specification

A reviewer should reject the packet if any of these fail:

1. the interval zero count is not exactly the sign variation of adjacent
   critical values;
2. a wrong extremum does not carry coefficient two in the global polynomial
   count;
3. the finite-domain formula omits either endpoint defect or the winding
   `W-1`;
4. the Riccati recursion has a sign or normalization error;
5. positive residues do not coincide with wrong extrema;
6. Rouche plus reality does not force the unique disk zero to be real;
7. compact convergence is represented as growing-box uniformity;
8. the final count is not explicitly an even integer below two;
9. a fixed finite Pick reserve is charged more than once;
10. any finite replay is represented as proving Xi-specific asymptotics or RH.

The reviewer is not asked to prove `GBOX104500` or `RPCH104500`.  Those are
explicit open theorems.
