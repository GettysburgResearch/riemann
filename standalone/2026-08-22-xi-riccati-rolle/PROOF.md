# Xi Riccati–Pick Reverse-Rolle Defect Cascade

## A self-contained theorem packet

### The real theorem

For a real analytic function with simple critical points and nonzero critical
values, its real zeros are exactly the sign-changing edges between consecutive
critical values and the two interval endpoints.

A wrong extremum is a positive local minimum or a negative local maximum.  Each
wrong extremum removes two crossings, except where an explicit endpoint bit
records the missing side.  Thus

\[
N_\mathbb R(f;(a,b))
=N_\mathbb R(f';(a,b))+1-2E-B_--B_+.
\]

For a generic real polynomial,

\[
N_{\rm nr}(p)-N_{\rm nr}(p')=2E(p).
\]

### The complex theorem

On a conjugation-symmetric Jordan domain,

\[
O(F)=O(F')+2E+B_-+B_++W(F,F')-1,
\]

where `W` is the boundary winding of `F/F'`.  Iteration gives an exact integer
ledger down the derivative ladder.

### The Riccati theorem

For

\[
h_k=-F_{k+1}/F_k,
\]

\[
F_{k+1}^2-F_kF_{k+2}=F_k^2h_k',
\]

\[
h_{k+1}=h_k-h_k'/h_k.
\]

Wrong extrema are negative diagonal Pick nodes and positive residues of
`F_k/F_(k+1)`.

### The high-derivative theorem

Ki's locally uniform cosine limit implies by Rouche and conjugation symmetry
that every sufficiently high Xi derivative has only simple real zeros on every
fixed scaled compact box.  This holds simultaneously for every fixed finite
band of subsequent derivatives.

### The exact RH gate

If a high derivative is real-rooted in the complete original-height rectangle
and the cumulative Riccati-Pick/winding charge is below two, then Xi has no
off-real zero there.  Proving this for arbitrarily large height yields RH.

The growing-box cosine theorem and cumulative charge theorem remain open.
