## 3. Mathematical conclusions carried by this binding

### Native component and localization results

The acceptance of `MIDDLE_JOINT.md` is relative to its pinned imported
analytic premises and the retained earlier coefficient lemmas. It
covers the exact fixed-k return, the Möbius coefficient allocation,
the joint buffered estimates, the marked inverse and ordinary fourth
moments with their different coefficient restrictions, and the
separate asymmetric identity with cutoff `alpha=1/250`.

The resulting literal remaining primal band is

`U^.735 < Nm < 2 U^.773`,

on the stated compatible zero/amplitude box and
`1.16 <= r <= 1.17`. The complementary count margins are the exact
fractions `23/16000`, `371959/307200000`, and `1099/768000`.
This does not prove the remaining band estimate. The ordinary
positive-slot coefficient restriction, exclusion of inducing
characters, common masks and frequency buffers remain hypotheses;
the wider coefficient scope of a different marked theorem is not
silently substituted.

The thin-band witness count remains open. The displayed boundary
`Re(s)>0.8748856` is a conditional target depending both on that count
and on the imported analytic framework. It is not an unconditional
zero-free theorem of this commit.

### Integer bridges and summed triangle counts

The acceptance of `THREE_EDGE_INTEGER_BRIDGE.md` covers the exact
fixed-endpoint determinant parametrization, its integer divisibility
conditions, the separate resonant cases, the actual unwrapped root
windows, and the Eisenstein divisor/area extension. Fixed endpoint,
determinant and basis data are held independent of the root variable.

For closed integer triangles, the root integral is an exact
**multiplicity** count with mean
`W gcd(j_0,j_2)/|j_0|`; it is not obtained by treating candidate
existence as the number of candidates. The gcd-weighted distinct
product sum has the asserted inverse-Y bound with logarithms. Its
transfer to source list and line weights is a separately reviewed
result, not a self-certification by the author of that transfer.

No complete-residue law for an arbitrary integer path, arbitrary
surrounding-history bound, or full signed operator estimate follows
from this fixed-data theorem. The raw source kernel still has factor
`d_0/D`, with no additional per-edge `1/Y`.

### Root-count refinement and the full common-D consumer

The acceptance of `ROOT_COUNT_REFINEMENT.md` covers the complete
Fourier and progression argument, including frequency axes, zero
modes, the exact frequency gcd, and the residue progression's
`gcd(u,S)` factor. The normalized error for a prescribed matrix
class is an **absolute** error
`O_epsilon(H^(kappa-1+epsilon))`, uniform in squarefree S; it is not a
relative error with respect to that class's small main density.

Summing the prescribed projective-line and parity classes costs
`S^2`. The `kappa=3/4` version includes the elementary Kloosterman
proof. The `kappa=1/2` version explicitly imports the classical
Weil--Salié bound; this review does not describe that imported theorem
as newly proved here.

The common-D consumer retains both endpoint lists and has aggregate
error exponent `(4J+13) beta-(1-kappa) delta`. For fixed
`J=K=1`, minimum root coordinate scale `x^(1/4)`, and the prime
band `[x^(1/800),x^(1/400)]`, the elementary result gives

`T_common^arith <<_epsilon x^(-1/800+epsilon) + x^(-1/50+epsilon)`.

This is a fixed-power bound for the specified original raw common-D
three-edge position-return class. The HLC root scale x has not been
identified with the native detector scale U. Changing shared lists,
two-edge contractions, the full centered comparison, surrounding
histories and the native Eisenstein coefficient transfer remain
outside the conclusion.
