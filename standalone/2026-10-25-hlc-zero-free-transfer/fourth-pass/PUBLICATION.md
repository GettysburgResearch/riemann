## What the packet establishes

The native analysis pays a larger coherent ratio head, its actual
overlap with the original far range, and the low/high complements
of a literal primal band. The remaining factor has
`U^.735<Nm<2U^.773` on the specified narrow zero/amplitude box.
The complete same-sixth-power-free-core contribution to its squared
mean is already within budget. The remaining signed off-diagonal
aggregate is written with the actual Eisenstein shifts, contents,
character conjugation and prime–Möbius coefficients.

The finite and arithmetic HLC work proves complete line-return
identities, moving-center CRT averages, integer/Eisenstein candidate
counts, and a paid common-shared-list three-edge raw root return.
The last result allows the two endpoint prime lists to differ and
retains the original root vector and one line draw per prime.

The new root-count proof improves the prescribed-line discrepancy
to `O_epsilon(S^2 H^(-1/4+epsilon))` by an elementary argument
reproduced in the note. Its classical variant explicitly imports
the Weil--Salié bound and gives exponent `-1/2+epsilon` instead.
For the explicit fixed-depth polynomial bands, the elementary
version pays a common-shared-list arithmetic contribution
`O(x^(-1/800+epsilon)+x^(-1/50+epsilon))`.

The HLC scale x has not been identified with the native U. Changing
shared lists, immediate two-edge contractions, arbitrary surrounding
histories, the centered comparison and the full native coefficient
transfer remain additional mathematical tasks. The target
`Re(s)>0.8748856` is conditional on the displayed open thin-band
count and the imported analytic premises.

framework, the classical Weil--Salié theorem, the open thin-band
estimate, RH or a new zero-free region. No GitHub Actions runs were
returned for the mathematical commit when checked; no CI success
