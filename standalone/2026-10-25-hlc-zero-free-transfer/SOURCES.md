## Native versus conditional claims

The finite CRT formula, prime-only coprime identity, affine moment
identities, and double-Stieltjes identity are proved within this packet.
The prime variance limit additionally uses classical PNT,
Bombieri--Vinogradov, and Mertens estimates. The zero-free extraction
and bootstrap use standard complex analysis with an argument supplied
in SHIFT_BRIDGE.md.

The missing full averaged-correlation estimate and complete affine
correlation estimate are not proved. Numerical examples using
\(7/8\) or \(B_{\flat}\) are conditional on explicitly stated summatory
premises; the exact decimal alone is not treated as evidence for them.

No literature-priority claim is made for the elementary identities or
analytic techniques. Their contribution here is the exact application,
the retained arithmetic coefficients, and the checked limits of the
proposed composition.
