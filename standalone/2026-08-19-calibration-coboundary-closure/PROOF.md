# Calibration-coboundary closure — standalone proof packet

## The eureka

The endpoint construction has two exact objects at every typed state:

```text
E = full signed native equality frame,
P = positive endpoint frame,
A = E-P = primitive compact calibration.
```

The endpoint-nested Hall/random-key construction is

```text
P = J + P T,       J>=0,
```

with one positive nilpotent child operator `T`.  Therefore

```text
E = J + E T + (A-A T).
```

The signed calibration is a coboundary.  Resolving the finite tree gives

```text
E = J(I-T)^(-1) + A.
```

The first term is nonnegative; the second is one bounded root calibration.
No descendant calibration accumulates.

## Canonical calibration

For the compact endpoint inverse,

```text
w_f=x^(-1/2)(x f'-f),
d(Vf)=x dw_f.
```

Every derivative-jump atom and both homogeneous modes are therefore part of
one canonical finite-variation potential.  Splitting off the positive
absolutely continuous factor-67 density gives the exact frame `E=P+A`.

## Analytic finish

For every fixed row `j`,

```text
c_X(j)=D_X(j)+A_X(j),
D_X(j)>=0,
A_X(j)=O_j(1).
```

The Mellin transform of `A_X(j)` is holomorphic in `Re s>0`.  The full row has
the reciprocal-zeta transform

```text
C_j/s^2 + P_j(s+1/2)/(s^2 zeta(s+1/2)).
```

A sufficiently large fixed row has nonzero numerator at any proposed off-line
zero.  The same pole survives in the transform of the nonnegative row `D`.
Landau forbids it; functional-equation symmetry gives the proposed RH
conclusion.
