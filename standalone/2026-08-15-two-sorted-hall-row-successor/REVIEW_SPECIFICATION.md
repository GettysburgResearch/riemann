# Review specification

Reject the packet immediately if:

1. any Hall bonus is assigned positive target or inherited declared score;
2. a bonus is sent to rough ownership or a child;
3. the residual source is not target-exact and score-superordinate;
4. the two sorts are restricted on different endpoint cells;
5. the bonus is not an identity channel in the finite comparison;
6. more than one bulk quantizer is used;
7. comparison, complement, terminal reserve, and `Y_4` cost do not share one row identifier;
8. any `q<K` column is omitted;
9. the terminal margin is not positive;
10. the endpoint inequality is reversed or the forbidden benchmark bridge is imported.

The exact finite checker is a type/algebra regression, not a replay of the analytic Hall census or RH proof.
