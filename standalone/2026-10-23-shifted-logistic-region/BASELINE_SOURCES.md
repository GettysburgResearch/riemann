# Primary sources and dependency boundaries

1. Bellotti, Trudgian and Yang, *Zero-free regions inspired by work of
   Heath-Brown*, arXiv:2603.21490v1, March 2026:
   https://arxiv.org/html/2603.21490v1 . Theorem1 gives the comparison
   constant4.896; the kernel shape, explicit-formula setup and detector
   strategy motivate this packet. The thesis-dependent4.8594 statement
   in their Theorem2 is not an input. Their numerical detector is not
   imported. The exact logistic adapter is proved here, including its
   nonzero first derivative and signed archimedean term. The kernel's
   autocorrelation and positivity are proved here directly.

2. Andrew Yang, *Explicit bounds on zeta(s) in the critical strip and a
   zero-free region*, arXiv:2301.03165, published in J. Math. Anal. Appl.,
   DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmaa.2024.128124 . Corollary1.2 supplies
   the zero-free region sigma>1-loglog|t|/(21.233log|t|), |t|>=3.
   This provides the finite upper height for the minimum-zero argument.
   No unpublished coefficient19.62 is used.

3. David Platt and Timothy Trudgian, *The Riemann hypothesis is true up
   to 3*10^12*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society53 (2021),
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09765 , DOI
   https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12460 . This is the imported rigorous
   finite-height zero verification H=3*10^12. This packet does not
   rerun their computation or authenticate its primitive zero data.

4. Habiba Kadiri, *Une région explicite sans zéros pour la fonction
   zêta de Riemann*, Acta Arithmetica117 (2005), Proposition2.1,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0401238 , DOI
   https://doi.org/10.4064/aa117-4-1 . Author's full text:
   https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~kadiri/articles/zeta-acta-04-09-07.pdf .
   The zero-first-derivative explicit formula is the classical input
   reproduced by BTY Lemma7. The adapter here supplies the extra
   shift argument needed for the actual logistic kernel.

5. R. Sherman Lehman, *On the difference pi(x)-li(x)*, Acta Arithmetica11
   (1966), Lemmas1–2, DOI https://doi.org/10.4064/aa-11-4-397-410 .
   A modern primary treatment of the zero-sum estimate is Brent,
   Platt and Trudgian, https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13791 , author PDF
   https://maths-people.anu.edu.au/~brent/pd/rpb276-MC-preprint.pdf .
   The explicit tail is integrated again in this packet; evaluated
   constants from BTY's tail calculation are not assumed.

The three user-provided research reports are inspiration and search
guides. Their predictions, expert intuitions, and secondary summaries
are not mathematical dependencies. No raw report is republished here.

The classical functional equation, trivial-zero locations, nonvanishing
on Re(s)>=1 (apart from the pole), and the standard gamma/digamma
identities are used with their conventional zeta normalization.

External-source status is distinct from internal verification: the
finite arithmetic checks establish the explicitly stated inequalities,
not the correctness of an imported theorem. Agent reviews are neither
journal peer review nor repository integration approval.
