## 2. What was actually attempted

I kept the original theta cumulants and varied actual finite spin models,
not arbitrary positive quadrature weights. The scouting sequence tested:

1. A single dimer with variable coupling or unequal observable weights.
2. Several dimers at the fixed nonzero coupling J=(1/2)log(3/2).
3. Connections between the dimers, and attempts at degree eighteen and above.

The successful degree-sixteen candidate has five dimers and six independent
sign groups. A QR choice of eight active coordinates fixed three pair scales;
high-precision Newton iteration proposed the rational center. None of those
nondirected operations is acceptance evidence. The final certificate freshly
reconstructs the full theta moments and proves a strict whole-box contraction.
The exact root is defined without an optimizer or a precomputed zeta value.

The higher-order and larger-coupling scouts did not produce another certified
root. Their nonconvergence is not a theorem that those model families cannot
work. The full code-driven certificate proves only the displayed target order
and the explicit small connected-coupling interval. It does not certify a
path connecting the old root, the new one, or the different star root.

## 3. Why this is more than another target-directed derivative

The earlier derivative theorem showed that one could begin moving toward the
sixteenth target. It did not show that the total permitted motion reached it.
D16 encloses an actual solution at that target and a whole target interval
around it, while retaining every lower moment. The coupling constraints are
satisfied by construction, not checked only at a floating approximate answer.

The five nonzero dimers account for about 19.1163% of the variance. Connections
with j<=10^-150 are a separate, analytically justified device to make the full
finite graph connected; they are not misreported as a substantial variance
transfer to the full graph. The large correlations already present in the
five pairs do the moment-fitting work.

The smallest substantive failure that would invalidate the finite claim is
failure of the strict contraction enclosure for the ACTUAL normalized theta
moments. Full covariance, all theta/time tails, exact root parameters and the
row-sum norm matter. The raw moment/cumulant conversion matters again for the
robust target interval, where a changed sixteenth cumulant contributes to
moment eighteen through the 153*delta term.

## 4. What does NOT follow

The new eighteenth-moment mismatch is positively bounded away from zero.
Neither small numerical residuals nor a positive moment Jacobian makes the
constructed law theta. The earlier bounded-lattice-component theorem already
prevents using an unbounded sequence of independent fixed-size clusters as a
blind induction to the full source. A connected graph with negligible bridges
at each finite order does not by itself resolve that limiting obstruction.

The #880 completion theorem may be applied at its own proposed scope to this
new nonsingular finite jet, yielding an infinite connected growth-calibrated
sixteen-moment model. That is a CONDITIONAL application of the earlier proof,
not a fresh independent audit. It adds no higher-moment equality, and is not
needed for the new finite root theorem. There is no reason to keep paying for
new copies of that generic construction until the native moment order advances.

## 5. The full-problem next step

A useful next attempt is a graph-preserving enlargement in which the correlated
component size grows AND the lower-moment fiber is followed with rigorous
bounds until the next native target is reached. It must account for positivity
of weights and couplings, loss of Jacobian rank, and the total attainable
motion; initial slope or parameter count is insufficient.

Alternatively one could replace finite Ising realization by a broader proven
Lee--Yang-preserving source class, but class membership for the unchanged theta
source must then be established rather than assumed. The new reciprocal-xi
probability and Newton-tail descriptions identify potentially relevant
structures; this packet does not silently use their missing sign/feasibility
statements to finish its own argument.

The exact sufficient all-order statement and full Lee--Yang/complex-tail/
Hurwitz consumer are in PROOF Section 7. They remain conditional on producing
the models. No full RH proof, new zero-free region, or belief that the last
step is routine is claimed. The advance is a completed next native target,
with an explicit robustness interval, rather than an unproved all-order leap.
