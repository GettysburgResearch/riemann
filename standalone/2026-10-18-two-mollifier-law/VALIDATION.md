## What the attempted final step revealed

The vertical-cap calculation was explored as a way of recovering a zero's
singularity after capping. Its exact growth is C^(1-1/(2km)), not C and
not an uncapped divergence at finite C. With the current polynomial caps
that is compatible with the family main term and error. No cap-removal
or RH assertion follows.

During final prose review, the fixed-member qualification was made explicit:
W supported in(1,2) eventually omits each fixed member. One can adjoin any
fixed finite set and absorb its full capped contribution in the stated
error, but that still does not exclude its zeros. This distinction is in
PROOF Sections1 and8 and is not delegated to reviewers as a routine gap.
