## Primary literature read and mathematical use

1. M. N. Huxley and N. Watt, *Mertens Sums requiring Fewer Values of the
   Mobius function*, arXiv:1807.05890v1; Chebyshevskii Sbornik 19(3), 2018,
   pp. 19--34. https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.05890
   The abstract and the first three PDF pages were read, including equations
   (1.5)--(1.10), the general inclusion-exclusion theorem, and its proof.
   Their short-source quadratic and higher-order identities are direct
   antecedents of this calculation. The present proof reconstructs a smoothed,
   odd-source version with the parent's terminal correction and exact pole
   annihilation. Their spectral estimates are NOT imported. We do not claim
   discovery of the hyperbola identity or of Dirichlet-Newton inversion.

2. NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions, section 5.9(ii),
   especially the digamma integral representations 5.9.12--5.9.16.
   https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.9
   Together with Frullani's elementary logarithm integral, these give the
   positive integral for psi(m+1/2)-log(m). The bound of its integrand by t/24
   is proved explicitly in PROOF.md; no computed special-function value is
   used. The remainder and operator-norm assertions are analytic proofs,
   not machine-checked claims or numerical eigenvalue estimates.

3. Luis Baez-Duarte, *Arithmetical Aspects of Beurling's Real Variable
   Reformulation of the Riemann Hypothesis*, arXiv:math/0011254.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011254
   The parent's terminal source and integrated-Mertens statistic are in this
   classical natural-approximant lineage. This pass checked the bibliographic
   boundary and uses the parent's explicit source statement, not a purported
   unconditional approximation theorem. No strong L2 convergence is claimed.

   M(x)=O_epsilon(x^(1/2+epsilon)) is used ONLY for the converse in HP26.5.

   *Bounding sums of the Mobius function over arithmetic progressions*,
   arXiv:1406.7326. https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.7326
   This pass checked that bibliographic source and retains the hypothesis
   RH explicitly; it does not reprove Littlewood's implication. No GRH or
   arithmetic-progression estimate is imported into the unconditional work.

5. Classical existence of a nontrivial zero on the critical line, and the
   reflection symmetry and simple pole at 1 of zeta, enter the pole and lower

   https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.4 ; https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.10
