1. break the MPFR inclusion contract or reproduce a nonpositive event;
2. find a compact/tail domain gap at `166000`;
3. exhibit a canonical terminal leaf not covered by AVLT;
4. find a squarefree source colour with duplicate/missing ownership;
5. show that the exact stopping tree does not telescope to the native source;
6. find a hidden Volterra/canonical family transition;
7. produce a negative outer native row below 67;
8. find an error in the row Mellin transform;
9. produce a common open-strip zero of `P_2,P_3`;
10. identify a failed Landau hypothesis.

Review must preserve the #541 normalization firewall and the negative oriented
child regression.  Neither is contradicted by the source-only construction.
