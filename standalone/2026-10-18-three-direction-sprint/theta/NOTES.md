rules out independent infinitely divisible padding and finite/countable
mixtures of such limits, so Poissonization is not an automatic escape.
Proving a whole-function identity in any surviving class, including its
complete arithmetic remainder, remains open.

No argument here turns agreement with a finite or formal asymptotic list
into equality of the actual xi function. No new zero-free region has
