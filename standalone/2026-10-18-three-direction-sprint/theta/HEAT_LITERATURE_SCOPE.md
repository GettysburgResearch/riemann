# Heat-trace attribution and exact scope

The heat-positivity conclusion in GLOBAL_HEAT_POSITIVITY.md is previously
stated and proved by Nicholas G. Polson, [On Hilbert's 8th Problem,
arXiv:1708.02653v24](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.02653v24), Theorem A and
Sections5--6. His W is exactly this packet's k: both count one exponential
per positive-height zero, with multiplicity. The source also proves
decrease, which this packet does not independently establish. Its proof
uses published zero verification below1000. The proof here independently
reconstructs positivity using a freshly directed complete census below20
and explicit prime and Jensen tails.

The canonical function in [v26, Theorem2.1 and Section6]
(https://arxiv.org/html/1708.02653v26) has the same normalization and is
likewise positive and nonincreasing. Thus the Bernstein and scalar
canonical-sign consequences in HEAT_BERNSTEIN_COROLLARY.md are also
subsumed by prior reciprocal-clock structure. They are independently
proved here, not claimed as discoveries.

Neither prior finite derivative claims nor finite Hankel computations
are imported into this packet. Complete monotonicity of k and the full
unscaled canonical Hankel tower remain open here. The frozen native
proof and certificate retain their original bytes; this attribution note
records the subsequent primary-source comparison.
