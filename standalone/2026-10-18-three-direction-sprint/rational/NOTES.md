# Research attempts and acceptance boundaries

This is a record of attempts, not a list of established zeta results.
The proposed proofs are in `PROOF.md`; finite and floating work has its
own declared scope.

1. **An unconditional arbitrary-contraction estimate is impossible.**
   The actual top-prime-pair coefficients are positive and exactly
   `2/(pl)`. Their centered modes have a uniform positive lower bound
   on a full output window. This gives energy `>>Y^2/log(Y)^4` and kills
   a large class of absolute-value, worst-case-vector or independent-sector
   routes. It does not kill the unweighted target, whose complement can
   cancel this sector.
2. **Within-stratum moment balances produce a real estimate but lose
   capture.** The exact prime-incidence identity exposes a common old
   profile, a log-prime profile and a complete square correction. Two
   prime moments plus a moving-prefix maximal estimate control a defined
   probe space at a much smaller native prefix. But making every top-pair
   response equal to one forces constant prime weights, contradicting
   the balancing row. This is an exact algebraic rejection of using that
   probe theorem as a global bound.
3. **Different old strata cancel the common profile while keeping the
   bad pair.** Keeping all top primes at weight one and middle primes
   at `-2h1/h2` cancels the common profile since `M1=1`, `M2=1/2`.
   Every top pair still has response two. Complete rectangular and
   square/core costs are small on the initial witness window. This is
   the first target-preserving component saving.
4. **Extending the short inverse directly is false.** The exact missing
   term is `D_p(x)=1_(x>=pY)[m(x/p)-m(Y)]`. The rectangular inverse
   identity gives this continuation through every strict native output
   cell, including cells above Y^2. Its naive future-energy cost is too
   large for the proposed improvement budget. No short inverse is used
   past its domain without this term.
5. **Adaptive multiple strata are algebraically feasible in finite data,
   but the analytic conditioning theorem is missing.** Fixing the top
   response and canceling both the common profile and exact future defect
   gives two nuisance rows. Small numbers of strata exhibit rank failures
   or large coefficients in the floating scout; those failures are
   recorded. With 16 strata a directed all-cell finite certificate at
   Y=517 succeeds. A larger floating scout samples 685 directed-sign-
   authenticated native crossings through 65536 at 152095 declared cells:
   no sampled constraint failure, worst minimum-Euclidean coefficient
   about 3.7061. This is reconnaissance, not cofinal conditioning.
6. **Moving only the active middle primes avoids the future defect by
   construction.** The largest coefficient can grow like Y/log Y, so a
   fixed-coefficient large sieve would be invalid. Instead the exact
   harmonic mass remains `sum |w_p|/p=3h1`. Pointwise R and Ramanujan-tail
   bounds pay all square, core and low-denominator terms. This proves the
   complete-mask compensator through almost half the output step, with
   the explicit exponent `4rho-2`. It does not control the reweighted
   remainder or the upper half of the native step.
7. **A global output-filter cancellation really is small but cancels
   the target itself.** Inverting a middle-prime dilation and the short
   old polynomial cancels the full unknown old source globally. The
   surviving R-polynomial has no support divisible by two distinct top
   primes. The filtered middle term contributes exactly the negative
   target coefficient. This is a concrete failed route under the user's
   target-versus-nuisance acceptance test.
8. **A planar zonotope gives the exact adaptive acceptance test.**
   The minimum coefficient box is characterized by signed two-row minors,
   not solely by a smallest singular value. A directed all-cell finite
   certificate at Y=517 proves existence with budget 9/4, sharper than
   the explicit Euclidean budget 4. Rank-one and zero-row cases have
   separate exact conditions. No uniform arithmetic budget is inferred.
9. **Near-product transport finally gives a complete global sector
   removal.** Approximate each interior top product by even products of
   nearby squarefree old factors with a fixed odd sum. Tolev's uniform
   short-interval squarefree density gives many legal references. Diffuse
   probability over every good reference controls the pair load without
   a Hall matching. The full kernel is tiny pointwise on the entire native
   horizon because each harmonic jump localizes a distinct integer target.
   The decisive improvement is to KEEP the smaller lattice-strip boundary
   term: it proves coefficient mass `(Q+Q^2/Y)log^8 Q`, so every native
   low-denominator annulus is cheap despite growing pair weights. The
   shared-square core support is at most the balanced strip width, giving
   a subpower complete core cost. The final compensator removes every top
   pair globally with subpower energy. This is the enlarged pair-weighted
   source class, with altered aggregate zeros explicitly retained. The
   full complementary rough source remains open.

10. **All old products can be compressed, not only one prime sector.**
    Apply diffuse near-product transport at every dyadic product scale.
    Keep the actual signed z_Y(d), all old factorizations and all masks.
    A global R-dilation modulus and every-jump horizon bound first give
    complete error energy Y^(14/33+epsilon). The new reference resource
    Q^(3/2) pays the low-denominator projection for rho<4/7. Low products
    have exactly zero rough projection and the full top corner costs O(1).
11. **A compact transport flux removes that artificial power floor.**
    Its sup norm is O(G/D), its variation is O(1), and its support has
    size O(D), up to explicit divisor losses. Mellin integration by parts
    cancels the R multiplier's 1/s exactly. A BV fractional Sobolev bound
    and the corrected unconditional Weyl estimate give decaying global
    error at every transported scale. The complete native source and
    its new balanced source differ by subpower energy, with all original
    physical windows and denominator/core masks retained. A new exact
    all-signed-source flux checker specifically tests this normalization.
    A coordinating-track coefficient refinement combines the sharp strip
    count with the aggregate product-coefficient l2 resource to give
    Q^(4/3+epsilon), extending the allowed fixed rho range to (1/2,3/5).
12. **The remaining reference signs are not a free new resource.**
    The weight formula cancels the apparent mu(a)mu(b) reference signs;
    the genuine arithmetic is the local signed original convolution z_Y.
    Generic bilinear Mobius cancellation cannot be inserted. Transporting
    the actual positive top-prime sector yields a balanced source with
    energy >>Y^2/log(Y)^4 despite the same legal strip and weight caps.
    Thus a uniform improving theorem for the whole balanced class is
    false. The complete signed complement still has to cancel this sector.
13. **Iterating into squares does not close the problem.**
    Square products have nonsquare reduced divisors. Replacing R_(r^2)
    by S_(r^2) introduces every lower R_(re) term, and the new square-mode
    mass no longer has the original reciprocal-square saving. Diagonal
    references also lose r<=|a-b|, which was the essential small-core
    resource. This proposed closure was rejected rather than used.
14. **Mask first, then compress: the polynomial core budget survives.**
    Exact finite Mobius inversion converts each actual annular masked
    source into R coefficients alpha_(j,d)<<Y^epsilon/d. The compact
    flux theorem applies directly, while all original Q/core masks remain
    inside alpha. Low d are directly cheap by R(k/d)<=d/k; the top corner
    retains its original old-pair sparsity because q=d is its only possible
    multiple. This proves subpower complete error even for #1002's
    energy-adaptive polynomial ORIGINAL core cutoff. No different new
    reference core estimate is silently assumed. The target is its full
    annular balanced R source, whose arithmetic estimate remains open.
15. **A single-index alternative accepts a tolerable error explicitly.**
    Transport the already-masked alpha coefficients to square products
    r^2 using every squarefree r in a short interval BELOW sqrt(d). All
    roots are legal old inputs, including the upper corner. Flux/Weyl
    gives complete error energy Y^(2/3+epsilon), and the actual diagonal
    coefficient is a moving average of exact signed square-shell sums.
    This has no new core projection. It is compatible with a hypothetical
    5/6 contraction but has a 2/3 zero-boundary floor; RH is not inferred.

desired contraction.
