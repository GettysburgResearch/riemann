# What was checked, and what checking does not prove

`verify.py` is a standard-library-only finite source reconstruction. It uses
exact rational arithmetic and formal independent prime logarithms/Euler gamma.
No binary floating-point comparison participates in acceptance. Explicit
runtime guards remain active under `python -O`; ordinary `assert` is not used.

## Primitive sources and complete coverage

The literal old prefix is generated by trial-factor Möbius values, while an
independent sieve generates the continuation. All ordered products in the
prefix rectangle are compared with a divisor reconstruction. Every complete
C_Y(q), including zero coefficients, is compared with the exact ordered
three-mask factorization. The uniform masked bound and full coefficient mass
bound are checked rationally over the declared finite prefixes.

The prefixes are the union of 2,3,4,8,16,32,63 and ALL 20 native reciprocal
crossings through 63. All 27 panels include all their products, denominators,
and every integer cell Y+1<=k<(Y+1)^2: 30,602 cells total. The first excluded
coefficient is independently checked and the strict endpoint is enforced.
Cutoffs at rho=5/8 are computed by integer powers, never rounded powers.
The finite core schedule is max(1,floor(log_2 Y)).

The checker reconstructs the low/high denominator partition and the stronger
low/paid-core/rough-core partition through their complete Jordan coefficients
and centering constants. Both reconstruct the same literal native m(k) on
every declared cell. It checks the positive square coefficient identity,
prime-cube vanishing, and unique squarefree-core decomposition. Ramanujan
sums are independently compared through two integer formulas; their centered
real identity is checked formally at integers and at a rational point inside
each tested cell, with the complete floor convention retained.

## Actual negative executions

`replay.py` freshly runs both ordinary and optimized checks and requires
byte-identical reports. It then launches nine separate optimized processes
with corruptions: omit the overlapping-prime assignment; delete mode
centering; delete balance gamma; include the forbidden endpoint; remove
q=1; delete above-output products; impose input coprimality; omit output
cells; duplicate the paid-core sector. Every process must exit nonzero at
its specified mathematical or coverage guard. A recorded rejection list is
not accepted instead of executing the corrupted checker.

`CHECKS.json` records the complete panel and predicate counts. Counts include
repeated guards and setup; they are not a count of independent theorems.
`REPLAY.json` binds that exact report and records the executed rejection
receipts. Proof and checker share an author, even when a second agent
independently reconstructs the proof or reruns this program.

## Explicit non-computational boundary

Finite arithmetic checks do not establish the infinite coefficient majorant,
frequency-spacing estimate, square/core L2 inequalities, all measurable-mask
quantifier, PNT balance limit, Landau cofinality, Mellin Plancherel theorem,
or #848's all-prefix exponent limit. Those require the written proofs and
their stated classical/source dependencies. No actual zeta value, zero,
asymptotic trend, Mellin integral quadrature, native critical upper bound,
RH proof, repository-wide validation, or remote CI acceptance is produced.
