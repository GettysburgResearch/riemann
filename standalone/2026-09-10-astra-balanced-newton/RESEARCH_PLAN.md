# Attack plan and exact failed shortcuts

**Research proposal, not completed RH.** The aim is to control the actual
arithmetic scale map, not to supply another equivalent criterion and stop.

## 1. Why change direction after the Euler completion?

PR836 identifies E_Y as the exact immutable cost of a prefix. Its large finite
Euler repairs approach that cost but cannot decrease it. Optimizing the same
late tail further is therefore not an attack on the missing arithmetic bound.

Here a Newton update grows the actual inverse prefix from Y to (Y+1)^2-1.
Recompletion then produces a new finite state from that entire prefix.
The old full-line residual is not raised to successively larger powers.
Every scale has its own exactly reconstructed coefficients and paid norm.

This distinction matters because PR812 disproves reusing one fixed sufficiently
regular compact controller to extend the horizon. Our quadratic stage has no
new feedthrough atom: it is explicitly an ordinary cumulative function with a
bounded tail. Nonetheless its being in L2 is not a subquadratic estimate.

## 3. The genuine estimate to try

Write for b<=k<b^2

    V_Y(k)=2 A_c(k)+sum_(r,s)c_r c_s psi(k/(rs)).

The output to control is not just one bilinear value:

    E_Y + sum V_Y(k)^2/[k(k+1)]
      +2b^2 [u_Y+sum V_Y(k)/[k(k+1)]]^2.

Every c used here obeys the complete finite arithmetic system

    sum_(d|n)c_d = delta_(n=1),  1<=n<b,
    sum c_n=0, sum c_n/n=0,

and has the explicit two-event late tail. This leaves one prescribed source
per Y, not an arbitrary vector in a moment-zero plane.

A serious next attempt should use those divisor equalities BEFORE applying
Cauchy--Schwarz to the quartic energy. Expanding the square creates four
indices and k-dependent phase correlations. Isolate exact coincidences and
near-coincidences of reciprocal products, preserving the signed c coefficients
and the k endpoints. A general matrix/operator norm on all balanced vectors
cannot provide the desired gain: BNR26-6 gives a matching quadratic-scale
countermodel in that larger class.

One possible proof architecture is a source-preserving decomposition into a
controlled energy budget, a summable collar/boundary ledger, and a remaining
signed correlation term. The budget must charge every original coefficient
once and must retain the signed u update. Neither a random Mobius surrogate
nor independent re-drawing of prime phases is allowed. No such decomposition
with a subquadratic bound is presently proved.

## 4. Why even a slowly accumulating improvement matters

At this square-scale map, an off-critical power-law energy is compatible with
quadratic growth from one scale to the next. The target is to beat that critical
exponent on the literal sources. A fixed improvement from two to 3/2 would
be sufficient, but is stronger than necessary.

Section 5 of PROOF.md permits losses C_j whose logarithms are summable after
normalization by 2^j, and arbitrarily small nonnegative gains delta_j provided
sum delta_j diverges. Thus a programme that proves only a decreasing gain
of order 1/j could still close the full problem. This is a conditional scalar
summability result, not an assertion that the arithmetic produces such gains.

Finite tests should record the exact joint E,u outcome, not a fitted exponent
for E alone. The present 66-case test has comfortable margins while its energies
are small; this is too short a range to support an asymptotic extrapolation.
The complete recursion is a tool for falsifying candidate inequalities, not
an all-source theorem simply because its coefficient production is exact.

## 5. Failed shortcuts retained in this packet

- Treat b^2 as another reproduced coefficient: false in general; its exact
  discrepancy is e(b)^2. The next canonical prefix ends at b^2-1.
- Kill the raw tail merely because the input has finite support: false; the
  raw coefficient sequence is infinite and is paid by (3.3).
- Drop the collar term on b<=k<2b: changes the actual update.
- Use midpoint Fourier sawtooth at integer arguments without the divisor
  boundary correction: changes the arithmetic scalar.
- Assume two moment zeros imply a generic subquadratic estimate: contradicted
  for every even b>=16 by the family in Section 6.
- Interpret a finite positive pilot or a relative Euler-tail improvement as
  an unbounded native energy gain: neither supplies the missing implication.
