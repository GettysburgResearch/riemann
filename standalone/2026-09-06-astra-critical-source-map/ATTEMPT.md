# End-to-end attempt and the step not completed

Status: complete component derivations, explicit unsuccessful global step.
This is not an RH proof and does not claim independent mathematical acceptance.

## What was actually constructed

The parent PR804 constructed a positive Jordan measure on the safe side, but
its positive exponential moments diverge. This pass does NOT repeat the
invalid step of evaluating those exponential features at the critical line.

Instead it supplies two constructions tied to the actual quotient:

1. A finite-order causal desmoothing of the exact Jordan distribution,
   including its deterministic centering term, produces the local impulse
   k_a. This operation is meaningful locally; its global bound in L2(mu_a)
   is NOT proved.
2. A positive factorial source d_b and an explicit causal gamma-filtered
   output n_a satisfy N_a=Theta_a D_b and have exactly equal boundary
   moduli. Their complete rational-filter Grams agree. This constructs an
   actual causal isometry V_a in the inherited Lebesgue L2 metric.

The second construction controls the norm exactly, but on the invariant
subspace generated by d_b. The subspace is not asserted to be the entire
ambient space. Its orthogonal complement consists of the source's missing
Hardy modes. These are not bookkeeping terms: an off-line zeta zero creates
such a missing mode.

## First closure attempt: extend the finite-time contraction

The exact convolution is strictly contractive on L2(0,T) for EVERY
0<a<1/2 when 0<T<=1/32. I attempted to concatenate these intervals.
The block form of a causal convolution has off-diagonal memory terms, which
cannot be dropped. Even the scalar-block matrix

    L=[[1/2,0],[1,1/2]]

has both diagonal blocks strictly contractive, but

    det(I-L^*L)=-7/16<0.

Thus local contraction does not propagate by itself. The general explicit
bound (C22) retains all arithmetic delays but grows with T; it supplies no
uniform global contraction. No new sign estimate for the memory block was
obtained in this attempt.

## Second closure attempt: use the exact isometry and positive source

The exact source map might appear to settle the old optical-theorem problem.
It does not: source completeness has to be proved in the SAME metric.
The synthetic positive input/output pair in (D10) satisfies every equal-Gram
identity, with uniformly well-conditioned finite Toeplitz Grams, but leaves
an exact normalized input-domain defect 3/4. Its quotient has genuine poles
and is not Schur. This rules out the generic implication, not the literal
factorial-source cyclicity conjecture.

An arbitrary Hilbert-space extension of the isometry would not suffice: it
must retain causal translation covariance and the exact transfer Theta.
Choosing bases on complementary subspaces does not establish that property.

The Hardy factorization also gives the exact critical kernel identity

    K_Theta(z,w)=[K_Ca(z,w)-K_Bb(z,w)]/
                               [B_b(z)conjugate(B_b(w))],
    K_F(z,w)=[1-F(z)conjugate(F(w))]/[z+conjugate(w)].

Both K_Ca and K_Bb are positive. Their DIFFERENCE has not been proved positive.
Discarding the second term would erase precisely the input-domain deficit.
This is the same type of error the Reviewer D source-sign audit identified,
now visible in an explicit constructed map.

## Third closure attempt: invert the factorial source

The causal inverse is completely explicit in (D12). I tried truncating its
Mobius expansion to synthesize an outer input. The finite convolution algebra
is correct, but the regular part contains the signed sums in (D13), and the
inverse also contains delta impulses. It is not an admissible L2 synthesis
vector. Neither positivity of d_b nor mu*1=epsilon supplies a bound for the
norm of the resulting approximation coefficients.

The safe finite-entry theorem (D8) pays the source cutoff uniformly in rank.
It does not pay matrix inverse conditioning, and (D9) retains the synthesis
coefficient norm. No estimate proving eps_N->0 was obtained. This is where
this construction attempt stops.

## Exact sufficient completion and its implication

A sufficient single explicit theorem is

    eps_N(1/2;1)->0,

where eps_N is the original-metric projection error (D3) for the prescribed
factorial-source Cayley-filter hierarchy. The standard Hardy cyclicity theorem
makes this equivalent to D_(1/2) being outer. There are no singular inner
factors, so it excludes every zero of zeta in Re s>1/2. Reflection gives RH.
Alternatively, prove eps_N(b_j;1)->0 for all b_j=1/2+2^(-j-2); then (C16)
excludes every right-side zero by choosing b_j below its real part.

A uniform global bound ||k_a*f||_2<=||f||_2 on ALL of H for the same dyadic a
would also suffice. The transfer would then be an analytic contractive Hardy
multiplier. For a hypothetical zero rho=1/2+d+i gamma with d>0, choose small
a<d. Isolatedness ensures xi(rho-2a)!=0 for all sufficiently small a, whereas
the denominator of Theta_a vanishes at d-a+i gamma. Its resulting pole
contradicts an analytic bounded transfer. Multiplicities are retained; no
fixed zero-spacing or countable-set avoidance premise is needed.

The density/contractivity estimate is NOT supplied. The norm on the known
source domain, the local bound, and the finite Gram approximation are proved
components. The completion premise is not installed as a theorem or hidden
inside the word 'map'.

## Relationship to existing work

Suzuki already provides arithmetic kernels for shifted Xi quotients, and the
repository already relates one outer-vector Hardy defect to a crossed-zero
model space (PR403 L-91333/34). We do not claim those general mechanisms as
new. The additions here are the literal positive factorial input, an explicit
causal output with the exact same source norm, a locally defined desmoothing
from PR804, a uniform short-time bound, and a coefficient-sensitive finite
Toeplitz hierarchy built from elementary integer cells.

The resulting approximation condition is in the classical Nyman--Beurling
lineage, not an independently easier condition proved in this packet. This
reallocation of the remaining burden is useful for implementation and review,
but is not by itself evidence of proximity to an RH proof.
