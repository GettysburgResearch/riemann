## External primary sources

1. Adam J. Harper, Ashkan Nikeghbali, Maksym Radziwill,
   *A note on Helson's conjecture on moments of random multiplicative functions*,
   arXiv:1505.01443v1 (2015).
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.01443
   Theorem 3 (PDF page 3) gives the Steinhaus (k-1)^2 logarithmic exponent and
   its Birkhoff-polytope constant; Theorem 4 (PDF page 6) gives the Rademacher
   q(q-3)/2 exponent, which becomes k(2k-3) at q=2k. The introductory statements
   and comparison discussion were read; page 3 was visually inspected. The
   full external proof was not reconstructed. These results are cited for
   classical credit, not used as unproved adapters to physical sextic rows.

2. Winston Heap, Sofia Lindqvist,
   *Moments of random multiplicative functions and truncated characteristic
   polynomials*, arXiv:1505.03378 (2015).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.03378
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.03378
   Abstract and introductory context were inspected for the independent
   moment-asymptotic attribution. No complete proof replay is claimed.

3. Classical lattice Poisson summation, in the Euclidean Fourier convention
   explicitly reconstructed in #942's BANDLIMITED_PRUNING.md. The primary
   exposition pinned there is Andrew V. Sutherland, MIT 18.785 Fall 2021,
   Lecture 17, Theorem 17.8:
   https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2021fa/LectureNotes17.pdf
   The new note supplies the uniform small/large scale estimates it uses.
   It does not claim a new Poisson theorem.

Matching-polytope dimensions, dyadic counting, finite character orthogonality,
and squarefree coprimality densities are established methods. The contribution
being proposed is their explicit composition with this source's exact masks,
aliases, and row-height constants, not a global novelty claim for the methods.
