## What the new calculation changes

Write the coupled local polynomial as

    1-(1-q^(-1))A-D,
    A=a_p q^(-s), D=b_p q^(m/2-1-ms).

The ratio to the two simple factors (1-A)(1-D) is

    1+A(q^(-1)-D)/[(1-A)(1-D)].

At the critical weight, both nonconstant numerator terms have cost q^(-3/2), not q^(-1). This is the summable improvement. All remaining nonsummable critical cost lies in the explicit powerful factor, whose truncated mass is the elementary harmonic sum over Nd<=T^(1/m). The result is a single logarithm, with a remainder constant uniform in m.

The Gaussian no longer needs to be replaced by an unspecified compact test. The exact source equality and the convergence of the full prime-restoration sum are written in the proof notes. The compensation now retains the correlated degree-1 and degree-m branches rather than acting on only a squarefree approximation.

## Direct closure attempt

At order four, D=sum E|V|^4 is positive. The tempting estimate

    |I|<=4(sum E|U|^4)^(3/4) D^(1/4)

is correct for a finite number of steps. Young then bounds I-D by a constant times sum E|U|^4. But those are moments AFTER partial restoration, not the already-bounded initial reference G. Bounding them is an additional arithmetic assertion. For an infinite Gaussian restoration that unweighted U sum can diverge, even though the exact signed injection converges.

This is the precise point where the attempted direct proof fails. Positivity supplies a useful compensating term, not the adverse signed correlation estimate. There is no currently established inequality I<=D+O(H X^(k+epsilon)) in this packet.

## Full-proof target now exposed

One promising direction is to transport the TWO cofactor scales together through the theta/Poisson calculation and estimate the signed injection relative to its full mixed compensation. The correct local object is V_1+V_6 before the fourth power. A theorem for V_1 and V_6 separately is not automatically a theorem for this object.

For ordinary RH it is enough to control the scalar principal Gaussian source over the stated auxiliary field and descend. This avoids demanding an unnecessarily large all-character/all-order family at the outset. However, the scalar critical estimate is still as substantive as the required zero exclusion. The present work makes its exact arithmetic operation available; it does not establish it.
