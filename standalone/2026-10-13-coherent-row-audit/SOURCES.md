## External primary input actually used

Ben Green and Terence Tao, *Quadratic Uniformity of the Mobius Function*,
arXiv:math/0606087v2 (22 September 2007), page 2, equations (1.1) and (1.2):

https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0606087

The PDF was retrieved and its parsed page 2 inspected in this pass. Only its
classical Mobius bounds for the constant function and a fixed Dirichlet
character are used. Its quadratic nilsequence theorem is NOT an input.
PROOF.md Lemma 3.1 supplies the convolution, exclusions and smoothing adapters
to the Eisenstein field, rather than misquoting a varying-Hecke-modulus bound.
Those named classical estimates are imported, not independently reproved.
The constants may be ineffective; this is not a numerical certificate.

Classical credit: Euler convolution, Dirichlet hyperbola, partial summation,
lattice counting, Cauchy, and positive Gram decompositions are standard tools.
Their source-specific composition, the mask-preserving perturbation estimate,
and the complete row-resolved census are the contribution claimed here.
