## Primary literature and attribution decisions

Each lane has detailed source information in its own proof or `SOURCES.md`. The following discoveries materially changed the attribution in this pass:

1. [Neo.K/Aletheia, RH Game 10](https://amral.evemisslab.com/riemann/semi-autonomous/secv/rh-game-10/) already describes the Laguerre-to-Cayley-to-Toeplitz/Herglotz-to-Schur chain and a qualitative large-anchor detection difficulty. That representation and qualitative warning are credited, not claimed as newly discovered. The proposed additions here are the proved cofinal scale, exact test-space completeness threshold, explicit all-order unconditional margin, and finite boundary-transport formula. The visible page and search excerpts were inspected; its full linked Markdown was not successfully retrieved.

2. [Scott Schubr, Collision-uniform kernel asymptotics for Báez–Duarte zero packets, edition 4](https://research.luminaryoddities.com/collision-uniform-kernel-asymptotics-baez-duarte-zero-packets/v/4/files/Collision_uniform_arithmetic_packets.pdf) contains stronger kernel/jet and growing-packet asymptotics than the independent calculation begun by the arithmetic lane. The parent retrieved the full 19-page PDF and checked the matching source normalization and leading kernel in Sections 1–3. This is enough to establish direct overlap; it is not a full independent audit of that paper. Sections 2–4 of our arithmetic note carry no novelty claim. The signed Lévy classification and exact noise threshold are developed separately.

3. [Dan Romik, Orthogonal polynomial expansions for the Riemann xi function, arXiv:1902.06330v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1902.06330) supplies the classical theta/Fourier normalization and related approximation background. Its full PDF was retrieved, and the normalization and universal-factor background were inspected. The exact sampling construction here is not asserted to be unprecedented among every possible approximation scheme.

4. NIST DLMF supplies the [Bessel I series](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.25.E2), [Bessel K integral](https://dlmf.nist.gov/10.32.E9), [digamma Binet formula](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.9.E15), and [Laguerre generating function](https://dlmf.nist.gov/18.12.E13). These are classical inputs. The source-specific consequences are proved in the packet.

5. [Manzur–Noor, A Hardy-space operator acting on zeta-zeros, arXiv:2610.03568](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03568) was identified as a relevant current direction. Only its abstract was accessible in the attempted retrievals, so no statement about its precise operator is imported into a proof.
