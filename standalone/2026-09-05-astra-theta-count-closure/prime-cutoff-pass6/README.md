# Prime-cutoff completion attempt: negative cutoffs, positive full-source tests

**The requested all-matrix completion was NOT obtained. RH is not proved.**
This add-only research note contains complete proposed proofs for a specific
failed completion method and a restricted signed prime-tail theorem. It is
not another purported solution with an unnamed final lemma. Independent
mathematical review and external novelty assessment remain pending.

## Principal results

Set c=5/4 and R_m(A)=4c^m A/(A+1)^m. Its inverse Laplace transform is an
explicit real polynomial times exp(-t), contained in the prescribed dyadic
hierarchy, and R_m(0)=0. Let g_m(x)=R_m(x^2+1/4)^2 and I_m=int_R g_m.

**PC-1, actual finite source:** keep the exact gamma integral and all prime
powers n<=X in the Weil form. For EVERY m>=42 and 2<=X<=exp(m),

    Q_X(f_m)<-I_m/pi<0.

Thus every fixed prime cutoff fails positivity on the actual prescribed
rational hierarchy. These are not negative values of the full xi form.
The proof uses no zero data: exact Gamma-integral moment inequalities
prove Fourier positivity through frequency m; a digamma bound settles
the sign. In particular m=42, X=10^18 is an exact negative cutoff case.
A beta-integral variance gives an additional square-root-frequency bound.

**PC-2, the failure is not rank one:** for every fixed finite X, the
endpoint-removed arithmetic cutoff has negative subspaces of arbitrarily
large dimension, already within zero-endpoint rational tests. It is a
trace-class signed operator; it is NOT the compression of the full operator.

**PC-3, full source has the opposite sign on the same generators:** using
only verified critical zeros through 100, the parent's replayed zero
between 14 and 15, and a bound for the whole remaining zero tail,

    Q(f_m,f_n)>15*196^2*(5/908)^(m+n)>0,     every m,n>=3.

This is a direct reproof of a consequence of the already-established b=2
mixed layer, not a new general positivity theorem. In particular the
literal infinite prime tail must satisfy

    sum_(n>X) Lambda(n)n^-1/2 ghat_m(log n)<-2 I_m,
                         m>=42, 2<=X<=exp(m).

Thus the tail supplies indispensable SIGNED cancellation even for tests
whose full form is provably positive. The finite prefix gives the wrong
answer. For this specific truncation, log X>m is necessary, not asserted
sufficient, optimal, or a lower bound for analytic acceleration methods.

The nonnegative cone generated by these f_m has positive full form. Its
linear span is dense, but positivity on a cone or on every pair of
generators is not positivity on their arbitrary signed span. An exact
three-atom synthetic example has all these pair entries positive and a
negative vector in EVERY three-consecutive-generator block. It is not xi.

## What this says about completion

The proof attempt tested a direct route from finite arithmetic cutoffs to
positive source matrices. PC-1 and PC-2 rule out that simple route. PC-3
pays the actual tail for a restricted cone, not for the full real vector
space. The source inequality for arbitrary signed coefficients remains
open. No new all-rank sign, zero-free region, or RH proof is claimed.
