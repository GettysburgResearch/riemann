## What was attempted

The preceding domain packet had the explicit inverse h, the locally exact
source output d*h_T, and an RH-equivalent cross-energy inequality. A
parallel contribution supplied another compact inverse. Rather than enlarge
the input domain with incompatible output assignments, this pass keeps the
ORIGINAL d and its original Lebesgue L2 metric and attacks the signed energy.

There were three concrete approaches:

1. Replace the double cross-sum by an exact, fixed-dimensional dissipative

2. Determine whether a bounded or linear inverse-energy target is legitimate
   before attempting to prove it.
3. Evaluate the complete finite-input future energy without large tail

## What is constructed and proved

WORK_IDENTITY.md gives the rational four-state matrices A,C,Q, and

    J_N = D_N + W_N = H(log N) + x_N*Qx_N.

Q is positive definite, its Lyapunov identity is exact, and x_N*Qx_N is the
ENTIRE future of the stopped N-event input. No incoming arithmetic event
beyond N is represented by that future. The work W_N can be generated by
O(N) event updates after a sieve; no N-by-N Gram inversion is used.

This does not confuse a passive system with a bounded forced solution.
Passivity supplies W_N>=-D_N, whereas the desired estimate is an UPPER
bound for W_N along its particular Mobius forcing. In fact W_3>1/20, so
universal diagonal domination already fails on the actual source.

The new resonance proof shows that H(T)>=cT, and J_N>=c log N, eventually
unconditionally. It also gives the full T^(2m-1) rate associated with each
critical-line multiplicity, with a sharp general finite-packet constant.
Thus bounded inverse energy is impossible even if RH is true. A linear
upper bound would assert additional simplicity/reciprocal-derivative facts;
it is not an innocuous strengthening of RH. The correct subpower target
allows such polynomial-in-logarithm growth.

The parallel compact inverse is subject to the same theorem: its input
norm sum_(k<=N)[sum_(n<=k)mu(n)/n]^2 is at least c log(N+1) eventually.
This strengthens its qualitative divergence theorem, without claiming
its factorial OUTPUT must have unbounded norm.

## The endpoint that remains open

The new work formula writes the sufficient target without an unknown
normalization or a deleted endpoint:

    W_N <= C_epsilon N^epsilon
    for every epsilon>0 and every integer N>=1.

No proof of this estimate is present. Equivalently, J_N must be subpower.
An unbounded subpower subsequence would suffice. We have NOT established
one; the fifteen finite checkpoint results are not a subsequence theorem.

If the bound were proved, D_N=O(log N) would imply H(T)=exp(o(T));
Young's inequality would bound the full locally exact output y_T=d*h_T
by exp(o(T)); the source zero-evaluation lower bound would exclude every
zero right of 1/2. Reflection would give RH, and then the source domain
would fill the half-line Hardy space. This is a complete CONDITIONAL chain,
not a missing step delegated to a reviewer.

The only upper bound supplied without an arithmetic assumption is
J_N<(385/512)N. It does not yield the required exponent zero. The exact
Q energy and its finite dimension do not improve this exponent by themselves.

## Why the source-map hypotheses are not bypassed

A small-time bound for the actual critical convolution cannot be composed
without memory cross terms. The state storage here is one concrete example
of why all such memory terms must be retained. Moreover the finite state
represents the stable INVERSE FILTER phi, not the entire Xi transfer k_a.
Calling it a finite-dimensional model of the full Xi operator would be false.

The positive Jordan measure from JGC26 concerns a different safe-half-plane
source. No bounded transfer from that metric to W_N has been constructed.
Importing its positivity as a sign for the Mobius work would silently assume
the missing arithmetic map.

The resonance theorem has no linear-independence hypothesis. Its finite
frequency orthogonality is elementary integration over a growing interval,
with fixed frequencies and fixed packet. It does not replace the actual
Möbius source by independent random prime signs. No family-average estimate
is used as an individual bound.

## Exact finite work accomplished

The directed producer handles all 65536 events and fifteen checkpoints,
using integer enclosures at 160-bit dyadic precision. The storage identity
supplies every checkpoint's infinite finite-input tail analytically.
At N=65536 the bounds include

    0.721 < J_N < 0.723,
    0.718 < H(log N) < 0.720,
    0.002 < future storage < 0.003.

These decimal inequalities abbreviate rational endpoints with denominator
1000, and are explicitly tested. They are not estimates of an infinite
limit. Actual values can oscillate with the cutoff; no fitted power law
is used or claimed.
