# Validation and trust boundary

## Mathematical status

The new pressure inequality has a complete interval proof with an infinite-domain
reduction. The zero-proportion statement additionally uses the explicitly listed
published unconditional analytic inputs in PROOF Section 5. It does not assume
RH or a new unproved arithmetic estimate. Independent referee/code review is
still required. A numerical PASS is not that review.

## Complete accepting computation

The full table consists of 96,033 point rows and 48,016 closed-cell rows. Pi,
square root, sine and cosine are freshly enclosed with MPFR-directed arithmetic.
The accepting search then exhausts 920,463 six-dimensional boxes, with 459,867
splits, 460,596 accepted leaves, and no unresolved terminal cells. Both coverage
accounting identities are checked. No optimizer result determines acceptance.

The MPFR 160-bit table's complete SHA256 is
`de981e9296864f40af784fc608b65a6d2dde075d19a4b7244ba7c9b136a351f9`.
The 256-bit table's complete SHA256 is
`833b3834e334bf80b7eaa673b0a5ecfa15e5ea5714e81c7f582087e700a811bc`.
Their only different row is the precision header. All 144,049 numerical payload
rows agree byte-for-byte. result.json binds the shared payload hash.

The initial complete runs used GCC 14.2.0 at -O3 for the 160-bit build/search,
and GCC -O2 for the 256-bit table with Clang -O2 for the second search. Both
search receipts agree exactly. Full accepting replays of the final packet use
verify.py normally with GCC/160 and optimized Python with Clang/256. A clean
archive and add-only patch replay are recorded in the outer delivery receipt.

MPFR 4.2.2 and IEEE-754 binary64 with nextafter are trusted arithmetic. Fast-math
and floating contraction are disabled. An ordinary LDL screen only rejects use
of the convexity accelerator; all convexity acceptance is repeated with outward
interval LDL. The matrix lower bound is a sum of rank-one incidence matrices,
not an entrywise Hessian assumption.

The supplied header-free MPFR declaration is restricted to the tested LP64 ABI,
with a compile-time structure-size check. This is not a portable Windows build.
No claim is made about untested compilers, architectures or rounding modes.

## Independent bounded controls

rational_audit.py uses standard-library integers/Fractions, outward 256-bit
quantization, Machin pi with complete alternating remainders, integer square
roots and full sine/cosine Taylor remainders. It independently reconstructs
all displayed scalar constants, a rational near-sharp witness, nineteen
selected point rows and thirty-nine selected cell sample points. It uses the
sinc derivative formulas rather than the C++ quotient derivative formula.
These selected tests do not certify the entire table by themselves.

The finite controls include seven complete window-count panels and 105 exact
spectral panels, plus five strict JSON/type comparisons. Three actual search
commands test a false higher global target, a truncated table, and a malformed
integer parameter; each must return its specified nonaccepting status. These
are different checks, not hundreds of independent analytic theorems.

## Non-proof exploration and disclosed failed runs

Ordinary SciPy optimization selected candidate pressures and witness points.
More extensive starting configurations disproved early optimistic guesses
0.004 and 0.0039 at pressure 1/3000. The non-directed scout table and its
unfinished searches are NOT accepting evidence and are not distributed as
certified tables. Complete subsequent directed runs established 0.003825 and
0.003826 at that pressure, followed by the final tuned inequality.

An initial exact-Fraction primitive audit was interrupted by its time limit
because denominator growth was excessive. The final audit uses explicitly
outward dyadic quantization and completed. The interrupted invocation is not
counted as an acceptance. The final audit's numerical operations remain rigorous.
