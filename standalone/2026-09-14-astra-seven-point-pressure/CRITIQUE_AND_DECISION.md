# Response to the critique and research decision

## The calibration I accept

Much of my earlier output established exact constructions or reformulated the
same unresolved arithmetic positivity/cancellation requirement. Finite Ising
moment matching does not supply an unbounded moment-extension theorem. Making
all iterates square-integrable does not make their norms small. Constructing a
Fredholm determinant does not put its spectrum on the required ray. Those are
real distinctions, but expanding their formal machinery is not a measured
advance toward excluding every exceptional zeta zero.

The previous sixteen-moment packet was uploaded at PR886, head
d998605c0718e332ac503baf078704b862f8250e, with the matching published subtree
2b95816590f4594c2510614ca95bc1f6e3a915fd. I have not withdrawn that finite result;
I am changing the research priority rather than pretending it implies an
all-order mechanism.

This pass does NOT independently authenticate the critique's seven unpublished
reader reports. Nor have I verified every numerical historical comparison in
it. In particular, sweeping formulations about what no finite Gram matrix can
constrain require the exact norm, target and certificate direction. The useful
central criticism does not depend on accepting every such sentence literally.

## Why the simple-zero route is different

The stability-enhanced rank inequality retains an additional nonnegative
spectral defect. A local inequality for the ACTUAL overlap kernel converts that
defect into an improvement of a numerical zero proportion, using the same
published unconditional trace inputs. It need not assume RH, a hypothetical
all-order positivity theorem, or a new mean-square cancellation estimate.

The old seven-point mechanism and the 269/280 lifts are already in ainta and
the repository; neither is newly discovered here. I read the actual rank proof,
the kernel sampling proof, the reviewer block repair and the interval-cover
code, not only the PR descriptions. The official source paper's Theorem D and
outside-window tail/count interface were read in parsed text and page images.
Their analytic results are imported, not claimed as newly or formally proved.

Lamzouri's current primary abstract confirms the 67.25% benchmark. The scalar
support-one optimality boundary is not evaded by choosing a better scalar
window: our window remains unchanged. The lift uses extra Gram overlap
geometry already retained by the ainta stability refinement. I make no claim
that this mechanism extends to density one.

## Failed approaches and the accepted result

Ordinary SciPy exploration first suggested stronger constants. Broader starts
found counterexamples to the attempted 0.004 and 0.0039 bounds at pressure
1/3000. Those numerical searches were scouts, not certificates.

A new MPFR table and exhaustive convex/interval cover proved 0.003825, then
0.003826, at that old pressure. Pressure exploration suggested 1/3433. The final
full certificate establishes 0.00344425 at this new pressure, and an independent
rational witness gives a value below 0.003444256. The smallest accepted value
is not inferred from a fit or sample grid.

The selected pressure is not proved globally optimal. The final block size 308
is fixed and its consequence is certified directly; no optimization result is
needed as a premise. Tuning the pressure and improving the quadratic lower
certificate give a modest strict improvement. They do not constitute a new
major analytic method.

## What genuine progress means here

The completed output is a testable proposed increment in a known asymptotic
bound. It could be wrong if the numerical enclosure, pinching transfer, or
published input is wrong, and all three are exposed for review. It does not
replace the missing RH proof by another equivalent criterion.

The increment is about 21.2 per million in the asymptotic guaranteed fraction,
not a proof about any specific block of one million zeros, and not a statement
about the remaining zeros. The route still cannot exclude an exceptional set
of density zero. I will not characterize this as being close to RH.

The next valuable review is a genuinely independent implementation of the
new pressure certificate and a check of the exact zeta interface. Another
unverified larger constant, higher Ising moment, or source-coordinate change
would not improve the result merely by making the programme longer.
