## What the checks reconstruct

The ten groups cover rational transform identities; two exact norms; three
null moments; the full correlation polynomial; ten tail-antiderivative
identities; all 512 finite Mobius convolution equations through 512; 24
Cauchy-jet matrices (multiplicities 1 through 6 at four rational real parts);
101 finite calibrations of the stated Gram floor; 100 cutoff-threshold
calibrations; and the directed finite inverse-source experiment.

These are bounded verification exercises. The written proofs, not a finite
sweep, carry the universal quantifiers. Counts do not represent independent
mathematical theorems.

The directed experiment uses Python integers at denominator 2^128, rational
atanh-series logarithms with full remainders, and integer-square-root
inequalities. It integrates every logarithmic cell [log n,log(n+1)] for
1<=n<=63. Each polynomial-exponential antiderivative is evaluated with
outward integer intervals; e^(-2log n)=1/n^2 is exact. It proves

    1/2 < H(log64) < 13/25,
    h(log2)<-4/5.

No zeta/gamma evaluator, ordinary floating-point output, or numerical
quadrature supplies this certificate. The first inequality concerns the
entire truncated inverse INPUT norm H. The full OUTPUT obeys the separately
proved Young bound ||y_T||^2<=36H(T); its exact norm was not numerically
computed.

## Not executed / not proved

No Lean, Comparator, external proof kernel, GitHub CI, high-ordinate zero
census, all-rank numerical matrix campaign, or full positive-domain theorem.
No previous mathematical suites were rerun; three used parent manuscripts
were checked byte-for-byte against their remotely fetched Git blob IDs.

No subexponential cross-correlation estimate, global source completeness,
critical transfer contractivity, or RH proof was produced. The damping
amplification theorem is analytic/existential; no finite numerical coefficient
witness for the actual pair was generated.

Ordinary non-directed exploratory calculations helped choose the rational
filter; they are not retained evidence and no proof depends on them. The
actual retained mathematical checks are exact or outward interval arithmetic.
