## Actual imported analytic facts

1. The usual meromorphic zeta function, Euler product for Re s>1, its
   functional equation, and localization of nontrivial zeros. The parent
   already fixes this normalization. Elementary Dirichlet inversion is
   independently written in CONSTRUCTION.md.
2. Paley--Wiener for the Laplace transform, Beurling invariant-subspace
   factorization, and absence of singular inner factors for this particular
   meromorphic factorial transform. The exact application is imported from
   the source-pinned CSM26 construction, not asserted newly kernel-checked.
3. Only for the reverse implication RH => the energy bound: Littlewood's
   classical RH => M(x)=O_epsilon(x^(1/2+epsilon)). A modern primary paper
   giving a stronger conditional result is K. Soundararajan, *Partial sums
   of the Moebius function*, arXiv:0705.0723v2. Its RH hypothesis is mandatory.
   The paper's abstract/publication record was inspected in this pass; a new
   line-by-line audit of its proof or PDF was not performed.

## Primary literature checked for overlap and boundaries

- Jean-Francois Burnol, *A note on Nyman's equivalent formulation of the
  Riemann Hypothesis*, arXiv:math/9910055v3. Its projection norm is expressed
  by a product over right-of-line zeros with multiplicity. This is prior art
  for the parent projection-defect interpretation.
- Jean-Francois Burnol, *On an analytic estimate in the theory of the Riemann
  Zeta function and a Theorem of Baez-Duarte*, arXiv:math/0202166. Its uniform
  zeta-ratio bound on the critical line is conditional on RH. It is not
  imported as an unconditional damping estimate here.
- Masatoshi Suzuki, *A canonical system of differential equations arising
  from the Riemann zeta-function*, arXiv:1204.1827v2. This is prior art for
  the actual shifted Xi quotient and the innerness/canonical-system target.
- F. Alouges, S. Darses, E. Hillion, *Polynomial approximations in a generalized
  Nyman--Beurling criterion*, arXiv:2006.02953. The distinction between
  approximation and coefficient control is relevant prior context, not an
  imported completion of either in the present metric.
