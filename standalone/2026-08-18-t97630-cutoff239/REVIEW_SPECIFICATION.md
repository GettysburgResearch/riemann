1. a missed noninteger activation breakpoint;
2. a failed directed endpoint at or above 239;
3. a tail interval not covered by the active-divisor enumeration;
4. a low child observed separately after recombination;
5. one high child counted twice;
6. failure to preserve the literal `-p^{-1/2}` rough coefficient;
7. a root marginal different from the annular scalar;
8. use of the PR #566 reserve scalar as Hall-complement scalar;

itself.
