# External inputs and literature boundary

Read/accessed 2026-09-06. No priority or comprehensive novelty claim is made.

* CSM26 in PR804 at 4a88ee5ed8be897ed88c85e69e7650ab7e3ddd01:
  exact factorial-source transform, original L2 domain, Hardy cyclicity and
  absence of singular inner factors. Source bytes are pinned in SOURCES.json.
  The new compact construction also rederives its transform directly.
* Classical zeta analytic continuation, functional equation, critical-strip
  localization, the Dirichlet inverse mu*1=epsilon, and Hardy Paley--Wiener/
  inner-outer theory are used at their standard scopes. No innerness of the
  critical Xi quotient is assumed.
* RH -> M(x)=O_epsilon(x^(1/2+epsilon)) is imported ONLY in the converse
  direction of CD26.T3 and in conditional rate (10b). A precise primary
  exposition is Tao, 254A Notes 2 (2014), Exercises 32-33:
  https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2014/12/09/254a-notes-2-complex-analytic-multiplicative-number-theory/
  Partial summation in the proof supplies the corresponding m(x) bound.
* Existence of a critical-line zero is imported in CD26.T4. Hardy's 1914
  theorem is stronger (infinitely many). Original note: Comptes rendus 158,
  pp.1012-1014, 'Sur les zeros de la fonction zeta(s) de Riemann'. Original
  page transcription consulted at:
  https://fr.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AComptes_rendus_hebdomadaires_des_s%C3%A9ances_de_l%E2%80%99Acad%C3%A9mie_des_sciences%2C_tome_158%2C_1914.djvu/1014
  No zero ordinate, simplicity theorem or finite verification is required.
* Stirling normalization is classical, e.g. NIST DLMF 5.11:
  https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.11 . The all-y error bound is not imported as an
  asymptotic: it is proved with the periodic B2 identity in Section 6.
* Laguerre orthogonality follows from Rodrigues by integration by parts;
  finite polynomial checks independently reproduce the used normalization.

Context, NOT hypotheses of the new proofs:

Luis Baez-Duarte, 'Arithmetical Aspects of Beurling's Real Variable
Reformulation of the Riemann Hypothesis', arXiv:math/0011254 (2000),
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011254 . The abstract explicitly addresses
integrated Mobius identities and the failure of pointwise/L1 approximation
to imply L2 approximation. This is directly relevant prior art. Abstract and
metadata inspected; no independent full-paper novelty comparison claimed.

Jean-Francois Burnol, 'A lower bound in an approximation problem involving
the zeros of the Riemann zeta function', arXiv:math/0103058v2, Advances in
Mathematics 170 (2002), 56-70, doi:10.1006/aima.2001.2066,
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0103058 . Zero-based Hilbert approximation lower
bounds are established prior art. Abstract/metadata inspected; its detailed
estimates are not imported as a proof of CD26.

The packet's contribution is the precise CSM26 compact realization, its
source-qualified global cost and full-tail certificates, not a claim to have
invented the Nyman--Beurling strategy or proved an independently easier RH.
