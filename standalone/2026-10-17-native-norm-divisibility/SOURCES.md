## Classical inputs reconstructed here

The proofs use unique factorization of ideals, the elementary splitting
law in Q(omega), norm-Euclidean lattice counting, the Euler-product identity
zeta_K=zeta*L(chi_-3), elementary continuation to Re s>0, Gaussian Mellin
inversion, residue calculus, and positive Stieltjes tail estimates.
The particular statements needed are derived in THEOREMS.md, not replaced
by an unspecified source-dependent signed estimate. The sum of reciprocal
prime squares is bounded by the convergent integer square series.

The integer interval implementation proves logarithm and exponential
bounds by Taylor/atanh remainder estimates. It does not rely on an external
software claim that ordinary high precision constitutes interval arithmetic.
The elementary proof of the infinite tail is separate from finite replay.

An external web search for short-interval Mobius bounds was made during
reconnaissance. No such theorem, nor any recent quasi-RH result, is used as
an analytic premise. No external manuscript PDF or Lean library was rebuilt
or reviewed in this pass. There is no global literature-priority claim.
