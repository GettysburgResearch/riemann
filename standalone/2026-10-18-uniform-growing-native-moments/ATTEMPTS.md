# Alternative attempt retained as a search outcome, not a theorem

Before developing the uniform native theorem, this pass investigated extending
#981's certified degree-18 ferromagnetic realization to degree 20. The original
282-spin factorized law, its 18 rational parameter centers, and the native
theta integral were used. New target moments were estimated with high-precision
mpmath quadrature, NOT directed intervals; they are not accepted source data
for a computer-assisted theorem.

At the original parameter center the first nine normalized even cumulants
matched to the precision expected from its published center, while the tenth
had an exploratory discrepancy about -1.6299567732423e-14. Several dimer/trimer
parameterizations and preconditioned corrections were tried. A 240-step local
continuation increased eta approximately from 0.0282540 to 0.0428320 while
retaining the lower nine exploratory equations; the degree-20 discrepancy
remained about -1.615004e-14 and did not cross zero. Bounded least-squares
searches also failed to find a valid exact degree-20 extension. Their numerical
residuals are not certificates and their failure is not a mathematical
nonexistence result.

No degree-20 theorem, global path, cofinal realization, or negative obstruction
is claimed. This experiment is NOT an analytic premise of the native theorem.
The successful degree-18 result belongs to #981 and was not produced here.
The frozen new packet does not distribute exploratory moments as if they were
a certified oracle. Work on this alternative can resume with a separate full
source reconstruction and existence certificate, neither supplied by the
present search.
