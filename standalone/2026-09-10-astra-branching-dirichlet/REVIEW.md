# Independent-review questions

This packet is not a submitted RH proof. Please distinguish the following.

1. **Whole-source Gamma–Dirichlet factorization.** Check the shared U, shapes
   a_n and 2a_n, fixed *rate* k, independence of gamma total and beta ratio, and
   the lower support bound 4^-n. The rate is not changed to preserve mean of
   the gamma factor; the bounded factor supplies the required mean.
2. **Meromorphic continuation and entire correction.** Verify the exact two
   pole strings of H_n, that J_n is positive at every real pole, and that the
   formula (22) is entire without implicit totalized-pole multiplication.
   Check that Gamma(A_n+1/4)^2 is the correct normalization in the limit.
3. **Global compact convergence.** Retain the eventual-depth quantifier in
   the inverse-moment estimate. Check uniform integrability of positive and
   negative complex powers and holomorphy on expanding domains. BOR26's
   inverse-moment bound is an explicitly inherited component.
4. **Actual phase test.** Verify Beta(5,5), Gamma(10,rate5/2), c0=pi/15,
   central-moment recurrence, the factor 5/8, the chain-rule factor 1/2,
   conjugation in (24), and the two full Cauchy remainder budgets. Reused
   parent interval code is authenticated but is not an independent backend.
5. **Logical boundary.** Negative phase derivative does not imply an
   off-central zero. The reciprocal-gamma correction preserves the critical
   strip zeros but does not establish any zero location. The cofinal
   confinement theorem remains entirely open. More finite moment or phase
   panels would not prove that theorem.

The finite code regenerates arithmetic results; it does not certify all
analytic lemmas or the correctness of inherited BPY/Hardy ingredients.
No external novelty or independent acceptance is claimed.
