The research is add-only. Original parent files, main, the integration candidate,
canonical status, Lean, workflows and repository settings must remain unchanged.
Local clean-archive and temporary-Git patch roundtrip receipts, when provided,
record those bounded delivery checks separately from the numerical witnesses.
They are not full repository checkouts. Final publication records exact commit,
packet tree and read-back identities in the PR and external delivery receipt.

zero computation, actual xi spectrum, numerical all-N rate constant, remote CI,
all-order zero-confinement bound, or RH proof is claimed. The new all-N rate and
safe-half-plane correction are analytic claims, not a fitted convergence trend.
