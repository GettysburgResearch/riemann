# Complete integral enclosures for two finite reciprocal-gamma zeros

Status: proposed computer-assisted component proofs, pending independent review.
The two functions below are finite approximants, **not the Riemann xi function**.
The second is at an intermediate gamma time, not an integer-stage assertion.

## 1. Exact functions and inputs

For distinct rates a in one of the two lists

    (1,4,9,16),       (1,4,90/7),

let f be the density of the sum of independent shape-two gamma variables with
those rates. In this document f is a FINITE density, not PROOF.md's limiting f.
Partial fractions of product_a (a/(q+a))^2 give exactly

    f(x)=sum_a b_a (x+c_a) exp(-a x),
    b_a=a^2 product_(b!=a)(b/(b-a))^2,
    c_a=-2 sum_(b!=a)1/(b-a).                          (C1)

Every coefficient is rational. Positivity on x>0 is supplied by convolution,
not the signs of the individual terms. Set

    x=pi exp(2t), y=pi exp(-2t), h(t)=sqrt(f(x)f(y)),
    I(z)=integral_0^infinity h(t) cos(zt) dt.           (C2)

The square root is positive on the real line. I is entire; the normalized
full-line transform is I(z)/I(0), and I(0)>0. A zero of I is therefore a zero
of precisely the reciprocal transform in the research statement.

The exact centers, given as terminating decimal rationals in certificate.py,
are

    c4 = 28.0555855384091825810295021076097455522309
        +2.6219979332686197954925378014004831147962 i;
    cu = 26.8135855368140614010181412691765400069731
        +0.4209949404628029929584207065487732398251 i.

Both radii are exactly 10^-12. The rates of the second law are those of
X_2+(7/10)G/9. No special-function or zeta oracle defines a center or density.

## 2. Holomorphic square roots on every quadrature circle

The 256 closed cells partition [0,2]. Their centers are (2j+1)/256,
0<=j<256, and their half-width is delta=1/256. Around each center take a
complex disk of radius rho=1/32. We prove that f(x)f(y) is nonzero on a
neighborhood of each closed disk. This justifies the local Taylor square root;
no global holomorphic square root on the whole t plane is assumed.

### Large reciprocal argument

For any point of such a disk,

    Re(x)> (31/10)(15/16)(511/512)>29/10=:R0,
    |x|<2 Re(x).

Here pi>31/10, exp(-1/16)>15/16 and cos(1/16)>511/512. The rate-one term
b_1(x+c_1)exp(-x) is nonzero. Its coefficient c_1 is negative and R0+c_1>0.
The sum of absolute ratios of all other terms to it is at most

    sum_(a>1) (b_a/b_1)
       [(2+|c_a|/R0)/(1+c_1/R0)]
       2^(-floor((a-1)R0)).                           (C3)

Indeed |x+c_a|<=2 Re(x)+|c_a|, |x+c_1|>=Re(x)+c_1,
and exp(-(a-1)Re(x))<=2^(-floor((a-1)R0)), using e>2.
The rational expression in (C3) is less than 1/2 for both lists, as explicitly
reconstructed in results.json. Thus f(x) cannot vanish anywhere in the disk.

### Small reciprocal argument

A convolution of d shape-two gamma variables also has the entire simplex formula

    f(y)=C y^(2d-1) E exp(-A y),
    C=product_a a^2/(2d-1)!,
    A=sum_a a U_a,    (U_a) has Dirichlet(2,...,2) law. (C4)

In particular 1<=A<=16. Because the real disk centers are nonnegative,

    |Im(y)| <= pi exp(1/16) sin(1/16) < pi/15.

The last inequality uses exp(1/16)<16/15 and sin(1/16)<1/16. Factor
exp(-[(max a+min a)/2]y) out of the expectation in (C4). The remaining
integrand has argument of modulus strictly less than pi/2. Its real part is
strictly positive, with positive integration weights. It cannot integrate to
zero. Since y is nonzero, f(y) is nonzero too.

A simply connected zero-free disk has a holomorphic square root. Its sign is
fixed by the positive center value. The coefficients generated by the square
root recurrence therefore are the actual Taylor coefficients of h there.

## 3. A complete analytic Taylor error

From (C4), for Re(x),Re(y)>0,

    |f(x)|<=C |x|^(2d-1)exp(-Re(x)).

The product xy=pi^2 is exact. Therefore on the quadrature disks

    |h(t)|<=C pi^(2d-1) exp(-Re(x+y)/2)<66*4^7<2^21.

The code checks C<66. Since |Re(z)|<29 and |Im(z)|<3 at both centers,

    |Im(zt)| < 29/32+3(2+1/32)=7.

Thus |sin(zt)|,|cos(zt)|<=exp(7)<3^7<2^12, while |t|^2<5<2^3.
The common upper bound M=2^36 covers all three analytic integrands

    h(t)cos(zt),       -t h(t)sin(zt),       -t^2 h(t)cos(zt).

Every cell is integrated by its degree-40 Taylor polynomial. No cell is skipped.
Cauchy's coefficient estimate bounds the complete tail by
M(delta/rho)^41/(1-delta/rho). The total integration length is two, so each
complete quadrature error is strictly less than

    2*2^36*(1/8)^41/(1-1/8) < 2^-84.                 (C5)

Interval Taylor coefficients account for all numerical primitive and rounding
errors separately. Spectral derivatives are integrated directly; they are not
finite differences of the computed function.

## 4. The whole remaining time tail

Conditioning on the rate-one gamma variable gives

    f(x)<=K x exp(-x),
    K=product_(a>1)(a/(a-1))^2<=4.

The exact rational K is checked for both laws. Consequently

    h(t)<=4pi exp(-pi cosh(2t)).                       (C6)

For t>=2, j=0,1,2, we have t^j<=exp(t). Since |Im(z)|<3,
substitution v=exp(2t) gives

    integral_2^infinity t^j h(t)exp(3t)dt
      <=2pi integral_(exp4)^infinity v exp(-pi v/2)dv
       <8 integral_54^infinity v exp(-3v/2)dv
        =(16/3)(54+2/3)exp(-81)<2^-105.              (C7)

The elementary bounds exp(4)>54, pi>3 and 2pi<8 are retained. The last
exponential is itself outwardly enclosed by the integer interval primitive.
This pays the entire infinite tail in I and its first two derivatives.

On the full strip |Im(z)|<=3, the third derivative also has the uniform bound

    |I'''(z)| <=4pi exp(-pi+9/(4pi))
                   integral_0^infinity t^3 exp(-pi t^2)dt
                =(2/pi)exp(-pi+9/(4pi))<1.          (C8)

This uses cosh(2t)>=1+2t^2 and 3t<=pi t^2+9/(4pi). No finite time cutoff is
used in (C8), and no reciprocal transform normalizer is needed for it.

## 5. The accepting inequality counts a zero

At each exact center c, the full interval calculations enclose I(c), I'(c),
and I''(c), with (C5),(C7) added in both real and imaginary coordinates. They
prove

    r |I'(c)| - |I(c)| - (r^2/2)|I''(c)| - r^3/6 > 0,
    r=10^-12.                                       (C9)

For |z-c|=r, Taylor's theorem with (C8) compares I(z) to I'(c)(z-c).
Rouche gives exactly one zero, counted with multiplicity, inside the disk.
It is therefore simple. Both disks are strictly off the real axis; the second
lies inside 0<Im(z)<1/2. Conjugation and evenness give the reflected disks if
needed, but the certificate counts only the two displayed disks.

For orientation, not as substitute arithmetic, the computed slope lower bounds
are about 1.7542e-9 and 4.0002e-10. The Rouché margins exceed 10^-21 and 10^-22,
respectively. Every exact interval endpoint and margin is retained in results.json.

## 6. Arithmetic implementation and independent review boundary

interval.py uses integers with endpoints in units 2^-512. Multiplication and
division round both endpoints outward; division through zero is rejected.
Square roots use integer square-root brackets. Pi uses the Machin identity and
256 terms of each alternating arctangent series, including the first omitted
term. Exponentials reduce to absolute argument at most 1/16, sum degrees 0--96,
add remainder 2/[16^97*97!], and square back. Sine/cosine reduce by pi/2, prove
the reduced interval has absolute bound one, and use Taylor expansions with a
complete remainder. The quadrant selector is not trusted without that guard.

Polynomial exponential and square-root recurrences propagate complete interval
coefficients. The computations use neither mpmath nor any author module from
the original packet. The earlier numerical scout used a different quadrature
scheme and arithmetic; it is not itself accepting evidence.

The checksum wrapper authenticates this declared packet and compares strict typed
JSON against a fresh complete reconstruction. Checksums do not prove the analytic
circle, tail or Rouché arguments; those are supplied above for review. This is one
new directed arithmetic implementation, not two independent numerical backends.
Normal and optimized executions test the same implementation.

No complete zero census, certified collision location, continuous root track,
new zeta zero, or cofinal confinement estimate is established by these two disks.
