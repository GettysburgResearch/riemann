# 12. Research and review agenda

## 1. The smallest remaining arithmetic target

Fix omega=1 and retain the actual source
\[
U_N(u)=\sum_{n\le N}\mu(n)n^{-1/2}e^{-(u-\log n)^2}.
\]
The fixed-Gaussian argument gives the exact interface:
\[
\exists N_r\to\infty:\quad\int_{\mathbb R}|U_{N_r}(u)|^2du\le N_r^{o(1)}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\mathrm{RH}.
\]
It is equivalent to RH, not currently proved. The original covariance target at j_N=ceil(log^4N) is another equivalent interface. A successful continuation must prove a native upper estimate, not simply another implication to RH.

## 2. Review before strengthening the claims

Independent reviewers should check these load-bearing compositions at the published head:

1. **Hierarchy/analytic reviewer:** the fixed-modulus PNT summation and Abel limits in the digit natural-boundary argument; both masks in the centered criterion; branch and meromorphic obstructions; Tauberian step for G-generated integers. Treat the direct multiple-residue disagreement separately.
2. **Transform/energy reviewer:** normalizations of F, Q_j, G_omega and the two Fourier conventions; complete multiplier constants; the actual finite sources and endpoints; the Pólya–Gamma factor of 2.
3. **Zero-witness reviewer:** bilateral Laplace domains, Fubini hypotheses, nonzero target transform, early leakage, tail Cauchy–Schwarz, all-prefix quantifiers, supremum not attained, Theta=1 case, and the critical-multiplicity adjoint argument.
4. **Arithmetic/code reviewer:** exact packet extraction, independent factorization, rational square-root budgets, unchanged artifact hashes, finite scopes, and nonpositive-covariance counterexample. Tests are supporting evidence, not a substitute for the infinite proofs.

The global upper bound is substantive research, not a routine final reviewer task. A reviewer may approve a component without approving a composition or closing RH.

## 3. Candidate arithmetic attacks and acceptance tests

### A. Preserve cancellation in an exact inverse identity

Start from the finite Newton identity in Chapter 07, not arbitrary divisor-bounded Type-II coefficients. Retain 2P_y-zeta P_y^2 together, track the complete collar and product support, and estimate its fixed-Gaussian norm. A useful new result must improve the actual signed fractional-part energy or a proved adapter of it. A coefficient-blind bilinear bound is excluded by the positive counterexample.

### B. Source-exact moment grouping with a signed remainder

The extraction algorithm is available. Improve the TOTAL bound by exploiting signed residual energy or correlations between groups, rather than increasing cancellation order while taking the residual absolutely. A finite experiment should report all coefficients, reconstruction, packet diameter/moment orders, total group budget, residual energy, mixed terms, and the exact comparison to the original energy. Reject a result that reports only favorable isolated groups.

### C. A cofinal relative-growth inequality

The exponent theorem makes a relative improvement at unbounded cuts sufficient. Seek, for fixed q>1 and delta>0,
\[
1+Q_0(\lfloor N^q\rfloor)
\le N^{o(1)}(1+Q_0(N))^{q-\delta}
\]
on an explicitly justified unbounded set. Crossings may help endpoint control but do not bound accumulated energy. A pointwise endpoint condition alone is not a proof of this inequality.

### D. Local covariance by resolution without premature absolute values

The telescoping sech-resolution decomposition keeps all signs. Combine fixed-ratio arithmetic on coarse scales and moving-shift analysis on fine scales, but pay their overlap and the complete scale sum. A gain for each fixed scale with uncontrolled depth-dependent constants is not enough.

### E. A weighted low-frequency estimate for the actual inverse source

Equation (7) in Chapter 09 makes fixed-Gaussian energy a narrow weighted critical-line Dirichlet-polynomial norm. A source-specific estimate there may be better targeted than a generic additive short-interval theorem. It must not assume reciprocal-zeta zero freedom in order to establish it.

## 4. Independent hierarchy questions still worth pursuing

The failure to close RH does not make the original questions irrelevant.

- Prove infinitude and then density for the seed-5 nested Golomb sieve, with a precise growing-modulus theorem. Distinguish it from the direct multi-residue rule.
- Resolve the printed direct-rule extension and the competing heuristic using the actual primary text and a complete error analysis.
- Identify further naturally motivated fractional or iterated-log families without encoding the target into their definition.
- Study infinite-stage Golomb continuation or a correctly centered discrepancy; finite-stage L-function formulas need uniform conductor/tail control.
- Investigate vector-valued shifted multiplicative systems at p-1,p-2,..., rather than a scalar log(P) relation refuted by prime-zeta zeros.
- Characterize the analytic and combinatorial behavior of the integer-generator product beyond the proved half-plane and its essential singularities.

These may yield useful mathematics even if their first-order densities do not control the critical source norm.

## 5. What would count as genuine progress toward completion?

A new native upper bound with a documented gain in exponent or a demonstrably sufficient cofinal contraction; a source-exact transfer that turns an already proved estimate into such a gain; or a genuinely new distribution theorem controlling the missing signed covariance.

What would not count: reproducing an equivalent criterion; a finite positive kernel; a better-conditioned surrogate; a random-sign heuristic; a numerically tiny packet; a principal-character estimate without the full family cost; a logarithmic improvement presented as a fixed power saving; or an oracle completion using future coefficients without charging for them.
