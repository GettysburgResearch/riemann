# Source-qualified claim ledger

IDs beginning ZIC are local navigation IDs, NOT additions to the canonical
registry. `PROPOSED PROOF` means a written argument asserting the stated
unconditional component; it does not mean independent review or accepted
mathematical novelty. `IMPORTED` keeps the classical hypotheses. `FINITE`
means only the stated finite computation. `OPEN` is never promoted by a
successful test or a changed title.
