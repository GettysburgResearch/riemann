## What is new in this submission's proof, without a priority claim

The uniform harmonic replacement and its full-norm comparison, their exact
rational quadratic-in-log2 evaluation, the odd terminal source's first Green
interval comparison, and the explicit fourth-power sampling constant are
proved in PROOF.md. Hilbert-space inequalities, alternating-series estimates,
divisor inversion, distributional interpolation and Mellin analytic continuation
are classical tools. This is not a claim of discovery of Nyman–Beurling,
Riesz smoothing, natural approximants, or an unconditional Mertens bound.

No external paper is copied into the repository. All mathematical imports
are separately labeled. Finite rational and directed checks are not a formal
proof of the analytic estimates, Littlewood's theorem, or RH.
