## Primary literature examined

1. **Yuanda Xu and Hejian Sang**, [A matching lower bound for the sextic
   large sieve and structured prime moments, arXiv:2610.17675v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.17675v2).
   The fractional-window proof uses its full sixth-power-free
   arbitrary-coefficient sieve and the earlier packet's explicitly
   qualified ordinary-moment adapter. Its stated prime theorem supplies
   a comparison only on its canonical squarefree row subfamily.

2. **Guangshi Lü, Zhiwei Wang and Jie Wu**, [On the Hardy–Littlewood–Chowla
   Conjecture, arXiv:2610.20242v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.20242v1).
   Examined as a possible new input. Its coordinate conjugacy and
   qualitative fixed-shift cancellation were not substituted for a
   native coefficient transfer or a fixed-power bound. None of the
   completed new theorems depends on accepting its main theorem.

3. **Winfried Kohnen**, [A simple note on the number-theoretic function
   Omega(n) and Dirichlet L-functions, European Journal of Mathematics
   9, 33 (2023)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40879-023-00629-w).
   Consulted to delimit priority for fractional Euler-series/zero-order
   criteria. Its introduction cites Wolke's earlier full-remainder RH
   criterion. This packet claims no novelty for that general principle.

4. **The source-qualified Liu boundary input**, [arXiv:2610.26835v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26835v1).
   Relevant only when importing a fixed zero-free half-plane into the
   conditional norm-twist lemma or the marked baseline discussion. The
   proofs explicitly state those hypotheses; they do not re-prove the
   imported boundary or move it.

The integer-mark positive theorem uses elementary prime upper bounds and
zeta continuation, and its matching resonance uses the classical prime
number theorem. It requires no imported fixed zero-free half-plane. The
spectral consumers use the classical explicit formula and the complete
pinned Weil source; their open all-scale hypotheses are stated separately.
