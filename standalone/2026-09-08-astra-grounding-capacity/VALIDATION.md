# GCP26 validation and trust boundaries

The infinite asymptotic and the identification of the optimal constants are
paper proofs. Finite checks do not machine-prove them, RH, or a source adapter.
No external zeta/zero evaluation, PNT computation, large arithmetic residual
minimum, or full historical campaign is executed here.

## Numerical primitives

check.py uses only Python's standard library, integer/Fraction arithmetic and
160-bit outward dyadic intervals. Every log p is range-reduced and computed
by 100 terms of the atanh expansion, with the entire positive geometric
remainder retained. Sums, products and reciprocals round outward. No floating
value participates in acceptance. The secular equation is tested at two exact
rational endpoints with every denominator strictly positive.

A separate mpmath reconnaissance at 45 digits selected readable brackets at
P=13 and inspected P=2,3,5,7,29. Its values are not evidence and are not imported
by the exact code. This was not an actual large-P asymptotic test.

## Bounded coverage

* The sieve and independent trial division agree for every integer through 257
  (55 primes).
* Eight complete finite graph panels cover 52 vertex instances and 91 edge
  instances. The graph matrices and tensor eigenbases are reconstructed by
  separate formulas; every eigenvector, orthogonality, root contrast, Green
  energy, anchored norm, and shifted-resolvent identity is checked. Log-prime
  rates in THESE algebra panels are explicitly formal positive rational rates.
* Each panel also checks six exact coherent Schur tests (48 total), including
  both signs of the retained scalar. These are general graph controls.
* Twenty-four one-prime secular identities use independent rational rates.
* The two ACTUAL logarithmic certificates use all six primes <=13 and all 63
  nonempty visible subsets in each reservoir. Both G intervals and both secular
  root enclosures are checked. The geo certificate relies on the paper's
  infinite-exponent spectral reduction; code does not enumerate that reservoir.

Normal and optimized final stdout are required to agree byte-for-byte.
Rejection tests use real subprocess exits, not assert statements. Per mode,
six altered results and four altered packets are tested after two pristine
controls. Hash rejection authenticates bytes; it does not prove that a changed
mathematical assertion is false. No parent executable or old suite is rerun.

## Execution notes

The first output-writing command had an AttributeError in its CLI path
(args.write_text instead of args.write.write_text); it produced no result file.
The following optimized invocation therefore also failed to find the result.
A shell comparison of those empty stdout files is NOT counted as a replay.
The path was corrected and all final commands use fail-fast orchestration.
No mathematical inequality or source was changed to force the certificates.

The flat eleven-file inventory and ten manifest hashes are checked separately.
Publication will record the actual remote commit and readback outside this
packet, avoiding a self-referential hash. A temporary local Git roundtrip and
ZIP extraction can verify byte reproduction, but are not full-repository,
Windows, remote CI, Lean or independent-referee validation.
