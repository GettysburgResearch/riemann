## Classical context (not new discoveries)

Euler logarithmic cancellation, Dirichlet convolution, absolute Euler kernels,
Minkowski, and circle Parseval are classical. Finite-order removal of Euler
logarithms is not claimed as a new general method. The contribution is the
explicit compensated family, its quantified physical comparisons and finite
native evaluation in this programme; no exhaustive priority audit is claimed.

- M. Aymone and V. Sidoravicius, *Partial sums of biased random multiplicative

  https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.1157
- M. Aymone, *Random multiplicative functions: The Selberg-Delange class*,

These works connect biased random coefficients, zeta powers, and friable
asymptotics. No result from their proofs is needed for Theorems 1--2 or the
fixed-scale stability proof; no full-paper re-verification is claimed.
