# T97200 — RJTE finite separators and future-prime completion port

This is a standalone review surface for the reciprocal-Julia boundary problem.

Main results:

* independent reconstruction of the PR #570 inputs;
* correction of the complete-monotonicity scope;
* exact finite Euler boundary overshoot;
* moment, SDP, martingale, Schur and Bellman formulations;
* exact reconstruction of TFPE/ACBI as the factor-four average of RJTE;
* directed trace-only counterexample;
* future-prime quotient-profile recurrence and finite directed diagnostic.

No all-scale closure theorem and no RH claim are made.
