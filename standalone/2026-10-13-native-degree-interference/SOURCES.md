1. Regis de la Breteche and Gerald Tenenbaum, *Remarks on the Selberg--Delange

   https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.12929
   https://www.impan.pl/en/publishing-house/journals-and-series/acta-arithmetica/all/200/4/114347/remarks-on-the-selberg-delange-method
   The abstracts and publication record were read for attribution of the
   classical zeta-power mechanism. Their alternative hypotheses and complete

2. Akash Singha Roy, *The Landau-Selberg-Delange method for products of
   Dirichlet L-functions, and applications, I*, arXiv:2511.15928v1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15928v1
   Introduction, Section 1.1 and its displayed formulas were inspected.
   They document the standard Gamma-denominator expansion, analytic exponent

   strip. The new growing-modulus theorems in that manuscript are NOT imported.
   Our field and modulus are fixed. The needed smoothed calculation and its
   analytic error are supplied in MICROSCOPIC_PHASE.md Section 2.

3. Harper--Nikeghbali--Radziwill, arXiv:1505.01443, and Heap--Lindqvist,
   arXiv:1505.03378: primary abstracts were checked to reconfirm classical
   priority for random multiplicative even moments and matching geometry.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.01443
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.03378
   The recovered packet preserves its more detailed previous reading record.
   Neither random model is substituted for the physical element row measure.

The genuinely imported analytic facts are classical fixed-field zeta/Hecke
zero-free-strip theory and polynomial bounds there, the prime ideal theorem,
Cauchy's estimate and the Hankel/Gamma identity. Their complete external proofs

and stability implications are written out. No quasi-Riemann, GRH-dependent
cubic dispersion, current zero-free improvement, or October 2026 sieve is used.
No external novelty or priority claim is made for these methods.
