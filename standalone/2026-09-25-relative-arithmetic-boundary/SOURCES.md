# Sources and attribution

## Primary mathematical literature

1. Joachim Cuntz, *C*-algebras associated with the ax+b-semigroup over N*, arXiv:math/0611541 (2006). Sections 2–4 supply the established affine/Cuntz relations and canonical KMS framework. These are not new objects invented in this pass.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611541
2. Anders Björner, *A cell complex in number theory*, arXiv:1101.5704 (2011). Theorem 3.1 gives the odd-squarefree shell description of Betti numbers. The explicit filtered chain splitting in the note is consistent with this known result; no priority claim is made. The classical PNT input used for the asymptotic completion obstruction is standard, and also appears in this paper's number-theoretic preliminaries.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5704
3. Nadia S. Larsen and Iain Raeburn, *From filters to wavelets via direct limits*, arXiv:math/0602450 (2006). Context for the established connection between Cuntz relations, filter banks and real multiresolution; not a theorem establishing RH or the proposed comparison.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0602450
4. Alain Connes, Caterina Consani and Matilde Marcolli, *The Weil proof and the geometry of the adeles class space*, arXiv:math/0703392 (2007). Prior structural/cohomological context; this note does not reproduce or complete that construction.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703392
5. Alain Connes and Caterina Consani, *Weil positivity and Trace formula, the archimedean place*, arXiv:2006.13771 (2020). Prior source-sensitive positive-trace comparison in the archimedean setting. A global source-faithful comparison is not supplied merely by citing this work.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771
