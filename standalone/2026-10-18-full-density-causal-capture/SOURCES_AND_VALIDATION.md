## What was actually checked

The standard-library producer enumerates all 311 integral Eisenstein ideals
through norm 512 and all 157 exterior ideals through that norm. A separately
coded omega-stable HNF enumeration agrees with every norm fiber. The two
constructions share mathematical field conventions, not code for enumerating
ideals. Split conjugates and the inert norm 4 above 2 are retained.

All native coefficient convolutions use complete declared ideal prefixes,
including prime powers beyond the mask cap. Three phase profiles are tested:
principal, abstract sixth roots, and literal zeros. The two nonprincipal
profiles are NOT represented as complete actual Kummer-character censuses.
Every accepting arithmetic operation is in integers/Fractions in Q(omega).

Twenty-four complete finite critical-weight Gram assemblies retain every
mask and cross term in their declared ranges. They use the weighted coefficient
space with norm sum |f(n)|^2/Nn. This is a finite dilation-semigroup model of
the norm identities, NOT quadrature of the infinite Gaussian energy. The
finite upper-horizon countercontrol records that a shifted vector can leave
the observed prefix entirely; a full-line norm is not substituted.

Normal and optimized runs reproduce CHECKS.json byte-for-byte. Seven separate
optimized corrupted executions are recorded in MUTATIONS.json: wrong inert
norm, double-counted shared radical, erased masks, inverse sign, dropped
prime powers, wrong row population, and dropped upper horizon. Counts include
repeated finite source identities, not independent analytic theorem cases.
The complete replay authenticates all files before executing these checks.

## Review boundary

Proof and code have the same author. No independent mathematical referee,
separate research agent, Lean verification, actual L-value or zero computation,
complete Gaussian numerical evaluation, repository-wide validator, or remote
CI is claimed. The operator theorem is not the unproved native energy estimate.

Priority review: the normalized divisor-count bound for radicals both below
and above B; absolute convergence of the weighted kernel AND its inverse;
causal finite-horizon composition; all-p constants; the normalization after
arbitrary WHOLE-trajectory deletion; and the full two-index energy kernel.
The deletion theorem must not be restated for masks that switch with D inside
the norm. The ideal population is unrestricted b, not sixth-free a.
