## Primary papers

- A. Connes, W. D. van Suijlekom,
  [Quadratic Forms, Real Zeros and Echoes of the Spectral Action,
  arXiv:2511.23257v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.23257v1).
  Theorem 5.6 concerns a positive matrix in its specified distribution
  class with a one-dimensional even kernel. The log-variance proof
  states the rank-one reduction from the full legal resolvent explicitly.
  The coordinator and independent reviewers inspected this primary
  theorem; it is not treated as a generic-matrix statement.
- A. Connes, C. Consani, H. Moscovici,
  [Zeta Spectral Triples, arXiv:2511.22755v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22755v1).
  Full source and theta framework, through the pinned repository
  authentication. No completed proof of RH is attributed to this paper.
- D. R. Johnston, A. Yang,
  [Some explicit estimates for the error term in the prime number theorem,
  arXiv:2204.01980v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2204.01980v2).
  Theorem 1.4, equation (1.7), supplies exactly the stated explicit
  Chebyshev bound. The coordinator verified that formula and its
  threshold \(x\ge23\) in the primary paper. Its verification computations
  were not rerun here.
- M. Suzuki,
  [Weil's quadratic form via the screw function,
  arXiv:2606.09096v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09096v1).
  Historical and source context. Its introduction records Yoshida's
  earlier even-test positivity implication. No novelty is claimed for
  the classical even-sector criterion.
- Baiying Liu,
  [Zero-free half-planes for Hecke L-functions over number fields,
  arXiv:2610.26835v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26835v1).
  The inherited comparison benchmark and critical detector class.
  This packet does not certify the full external proof.

The paired canonical product, Fourier coefficient identities,
Gaussian estimates, fixed-test contour shift, Laplace argument, ideal
convolution, Jacobi completion and exact prime-discrepancy formula are
proved in the new notes. Imported source-specific moment and real-zero
