# Hostile reconstruction specification

Reject at the first occurrence of:

1. a source coefficient, parity or path-weight mismatch in the stopped-hazard emitter;
2. a source atom entering two first-owner paths;
3. an oriented child observed before terminal coupling;
4. a Target–Lorenz coefficient depending on the physical coordinate;
5. a frontier mass ratio reaching `1/8`;
6. a rough-lift marginal substituted for the native source;
7. a signed retained-cell defect inserted into the positive source cone;
8. q/4q detail formed before total-row assembly;
9. repeated thinning, root correction or child-capacity promotion;
10. a native sparse-dual price at least `3457`;
11. the wrong one-sided endpoint orientation;
12. use of the forbidden `J_Lambda-4sqrt(X)` bridge.

A floating numerical fit is not a proof of the source marginal.  Conversely,
the exact branchwise child counterexample is expected and does not address the
projective compiler unless it appears after terminal coupling.
