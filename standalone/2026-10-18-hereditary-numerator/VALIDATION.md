## New checker

The ordinary and optimized verifier outputs are byte-identical to CHECKS.json.
The detailed reports from --details also agree in both modes. There are 9,237
explicit finite predicates, including repeated setup, domain and diagnostic
checks; this number is not a count of independent analytic theorems.

The checks include:
- Every one of the 1,625 norm monomials in the 112 density choices, and an
  independent reconstruction of the hereditary Holder-elimination coefficients.
- Every one of the 113 exhaustive Poisson classes, its lower-threshold count,
  actual selected moment, positive top-dyad power, normalization and constant
  margin; every principal, small, horizontal/original and large-range budget.
- 1,856 strict contour-domain guards and 348 rational Bernstein inequalities
  over the ENTIRE interval 0<e<=10^(-9). A zero left-end margin is allowed only
  where positivity on the open interval is established. e=0 is not an analytic
  application of the result.
- Exact crossing/end-point identities, 1,296 actual-conductor exponent checks,
  and finite diagnostics of the same-subset Lipschitz bound. The all-real
  statements rest on the written convexity/monotonicity proofs, not these samples.
- 148 abstract sextic-character/mask tests using residues modulo 7 and 13,
  retaining nonunit zeros and shared-prime c*d^6 cases. These are finite algebra
  fixtures, not a CT character census, full number-field computation or L-values.

The smallest strict middle-class constant margin is
62985667611961/1068883733358000000, approximately 0.0000589. The proof/checker use
that rational number, not its decimal approximation. The full detailed report
is reproducible rather than stored as a very large redundant JSON file.

Nine separately executed optimized corrupted models fail at their designated
gates, recorded in MUTATIONS.json: replacing selected moments by full-family
moments; dropping the actual dual conductor factor; counting a class at its upper
threshold; omitting a middle class; retaining the old Mellin shift; deleting the
coefficient-square weight; replacing the selected mollifier norm by U; using zero
large-tail gap; and erasing the sixth-power mask. These are corrupted FINITE
models, not formal proof perturbations.

Exploration used floating-point optimization. During checker development one
zero-valued integer division would have introduced a Python float in a density
term. It was caught before freezing; the final model uses Fraction throughout
and explicitly rejects floating output. A descriptive SS/L0S label in the draft
5/7 example was also corrected to match the independently reconstructed Holder
monomial. No uncorrected report or claimed theorem was published from those drafts.
