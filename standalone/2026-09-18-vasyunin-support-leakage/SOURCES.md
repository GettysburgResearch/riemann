## Primary mathematical sources

**[B] Michel Balazard**, *An arithmetical function related to Baez-Duarte's
criterion for the Riemann hypothesis*, arXiv:1812.04309v1 (2018).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.04309

Read the weighted sequence isomorphism, Section 2 (Vasyunin's construction,
biorthogonality, coefficient functionals), and the question about analytic
divisibility and approximation. This packet's d_r equals minus Balazard's
f_r under the step-function-to-sequence map. The family is explicitly
credited as existing mathematics. We reproduce the elementary proof of its
specialized identities rather than treating a citation as a new theorem.

Balazard credits **V. I. Vasyunin**, *On a biorthogonal system associated
with the Riemann hypothesis*, St. Petersburg Math. J. 7 (1996), 405--419.
The original 1996 paper was not independently read in this session. The
attribution is made through Balazard's exposition, not a claimed original
source audit.

**[G] Bhaskar Bagchi**, *On Nyman, Beurling and Baez-Duarte's Hilbert space
reformulation of the Riemann hypothesis*, arXiv:math/0607733v1 (2006).
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0607733

Read Theorem 1 (exact weighted sequence formulation), the shifted Mobius
construction in the proof of Theorem 2, and Remark 4 (squarefree-only
sufficiency under RH), including the subsequent unitary sequence model.
The squarefree-sufficiency direction is an imported theorem; its proof
assumes RH and is not used as an unconditional construction. The PDFs'
relevant mathematical pages were also inspected as rendered pages.

**L. Baez-Duarte**, *A strengthening of the Nyman--Beurling criterion for
the Riemann hypothesis*, arXiv:math/0202141.
https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202141

Background attribution for the strengthened criterion, not a separately
reproved input. Bagchi supplies the exact formulation used here.

The log-mollifier growth implication discussed in the predecessor is not
needed to prove SRL-1 through SRL-7. Section 8's target-location lemma is
proved directly from the absolutely convergent Euler product logarithmic
derivative and dyadic Cauchy--Schwarz. No conditional mollifier asymptotic
or reciprocal-zero-derivative estimate is imported.
