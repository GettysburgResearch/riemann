2. **Cubic cloning (Section 3).** Check all path/edge assignments, unused

   actual bridge interactions, not exactly the unbridged law. Verify the

   weights are handled by small positive clone weights. No fixed-support or

3. **Arithmetic factor obstruction (Section 4).** The Gaussian contradiction
   uses all quadratic exponential moments, not mere sub-Gaussian tails. A
   finite LATTICE factor forces a full progression of zeros. Apply the external
   theorem only AFTER the limiting lattice scale has been fixed. A claim for
   arbitrary incommensurate finite supports would exceed the proof.

4. **Component escape (Section 5).** Check both subsequence cases: an
   infinitesimal cloud yields a genuine Gaussian factor, whereas a persistent
   block has bounded nonzero field scale by its two extreme probabilities.
   Every independent complementary law is retained and made tight before
   taking a limit. The conclusion concerns variance, not vertex proportions
   or existence of one giant component. The weak-bridge extension requires
   the sum of deleted couplings to tend to zero, not their maximum.

5. **Bath rate (Section 6).** Check the complete theta-tail moment argument,
   combinatorial factor (L)_r, and the condition L>=r(r-1). The limiting
   coefficient is e/(8sigma^2), with sigma in the parent's exact convention.
   No finite threshold for the seed bath is evaluated.

6. **Random cluster and ending (Sections 7--8).** Retain 2^k in the cluster
   law, the covariance in kappa_6, and the variance of the quadratic cluster
   mass in kappa_4. These terms are not unsigned errors that may be discarded.
   OPEN-COLLECTIVE is the still-unproved source statement. The familiar
   Lee--Yang limit theorem does not establish it.
