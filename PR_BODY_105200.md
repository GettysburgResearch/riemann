```text
a_m=(-S_m''(w_m))^(-1/2) ~ sqrt(log(m)/m).
```


```text
|Re z|<=C/a_m, |Im z|<=H.
```

`a_m T_m->0` window.

Every Xi derivative in the band is real- and simple-rooted in the common
natural box. At every real zero `c` of `Xi^(m)`,

```text
Xi^(m-1)(c)/Xi^(m+1)(c)
 = - M_(m-1)/M_(m+1) (1+o(1))
 = -(1+o(1))/w_m^2
```

uniformly. Hence the critical-residue coherence tends to one, the open
`RCMV104530` condition is proved throughout the high band, and the exact
reverse-Rolle transfer constant is `1-o(1)`.
