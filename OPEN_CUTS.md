# Open problems and useful next contributions

RH remains open here. The project has several exact criteria and substantial subresults; the decisive task is to prove a missing estimate for the actual source, not to count reformulations. [Approaches](PROGRAMMES.md) explains the relationships, and [current statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md) fixes the hypotheses.

<a id="fixed-detector"></a>
## 1. Fixed-detector negative mass and signed cancellation

For a fixed zero-safe arithmetic detector $F$, establish

$$\int_1^Y F_-(x)\,\frac{dx}{x}=O_\epsilon(Y^\epsilon)\quad\text{for every }\epsilon>0.$$

Keep the exact source, multiplier and cutoff fixed, and recombine signed carriers before taking norms. A potential local target is a bound for the interior derivative energy plus **both** excursion-boundary contributions in the [corrected Poincare estimate](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#mellin). Merely counting negative intervals on a logarithmic horizon does not supply the missing bound.

Equivalent or sufficient coordinates have their own source restrictions: critical SHARP weighted variation, wavelet signed dispersion, half-divisor near-collision, physical occupancy and Q4 annular correlations. The exact same-kernel translations are useful; swapping measures, freezing a different weight or taking absolute values too early is not.

**Concrete output:** one precisely normalized source estimate, or a counterexample to a proposed intermediate bound, with the exact implication it would support. A finite positive experiment alone is not the estimate.

<a id="arithmetic-tail"></a>
## 2. Unbounded arithmetic tails

For Robin's inequality, control the canonical class with unbounded prime support and exponents. A bounded dynamic-programming envelope is not such a tail theorem. For rough-number/Bellman routes, the fixed-$P_{61}$ theorem does not pay growing-prime uniformity or the dynamic critical block.

**Concrete output:** a lemma with explicit dependence on support, exponent or roughness parameters, showing where an existing bound remains uniform; or an exact finite certificate restricted to a clearly declared range. Use the [corrected cap/feasibility statement](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#robin), not a traversal total without its complete stream.

<a id="full-positivity"></a>
## 3. All-order or full-domain positivity

For the actual-xi Pick programme, bridge the gap from low orders to the complete required hierarchy. For the energy-Schur route, the reviewed candidate supplies the actual unit-window sign and fixed-window lower/upper approximation. Extend the source-qualified certificate beyond that window, or prove a propagation bound covering an unbounded prescribed family. Preserve the distinction between an H^-1 coercive bound and an L2 spectral gap. For First-Hermite positivity, cover the whole complement of the proved region, including bounded centers and arbitrarily large heat parameters.

**Concrete output:** an error-enclosed actual residual matrix respecting exact constraints and all cross terms, or a source-specific propagation lemma that genuinely covers a missing domain. Positive upper Galerkin matrices, finite zero tables, a narrow transition curve and adaptable capture alone do not settle these goals.

<a id="descent"></a>
## 4. Xi derivative descent and source transport

The corrected high-derivative entry and finite residue/selector results do not give low-order descent. The missing work is uniform signed transport with the complete source, endpoint terms, winding/pole contributions and the relevant parameter regime. A premise of raw xi innerness may already imply RH.

**Concrete output:** a single exact adjacent-order or source-to-index inequality with quantified loss, or a valid counterexample within its stated class. Do not promote finite phase efficiency to a cofinal limit. The asserted 90-percent-gate refutation is not an established global obstruction.

<a id="family-transfer"></a>
## 5. Principal-member and native-source transfer

Convert a family, function-field, geometric or generalized-object result into an estimate for the actual principal arithmetic source. State the family, physical measure, ramified factors, normalizations, masks and conductor dependence. Nonprincipal control can miss an arbitrarily large principal component.

Specific smaller burdens include the native Poincare ladder's relative precision, an authenticated Chow witness/action/projection calculation, and corrected complete Epstein interval contours. These are distinct proof or evidence gaps, not all synonyms for RH.

**Concrete output:** one checked source adapter, an explicit relative error bound in the required depth regime, or a reproducible corrected certificate. A fitted local factor, generic degree law or normalized positive shape is not an arithmetic realization theorem.

<a id="causal"></a>
## 6. Corrected Euler products below the classical line

The retained causal norm theorem is on $\Re s=1$. The next target is suitable local boundedness of the corrected products **together** on compact subsets of $\Re s>1/2$, with the exact source and correction conventions. The proposed RH-facing adapter and that arithmetic bound remain separate from the accepted line-one component.

**Concrete output:** a signed discrepancy estimate with explicit dependence on distance from the critical line. Separate norm bounds on exponentially large factors below one do not meet the target.

<a id="native-coherent-energy"></a>
## 7. Native residual, intrinsic-floor and coarse-energy estimates

The reviewed [native-source candidate](research/integrated/native_sources/README.md) makes several stopping points explicit. Construct prefix-preserving finite polynomials with subpower complete floor-residual energy, or prove a fixed power saving in the exact annular failure count. In the factorial-source route, estimate the actual intrinsic floor or the growing-horizon **output** error rather than merely conditioning its finite Gram. In the prime route, control the accumulated square-cell levels after the summable local detail is removed.

The simple stopped-Mobius source has the exact coherent energy

$$J_N=\sum_{k<N}\frac{M(k)^2}{k(k+1)}+\frac{M(N)^2}{N}
=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{\mu(n)^2}{n}+2\sum_{n\le N}\frac{\mu(n)M(n-1)}n.$$

Its signed-work upper bound is not supplied by a divisor gap: this norm lies in the channel the gradient annihilates. A useful contribution is a genuinely native estimate for that work, or a source-faithful adapter with a quantified loss. A uniform polylogarithmic graph-to-physical comparison on the entire stated coefficient class is already ruled out; a special estimate for a selected native minimizer is not.

**Concrete output:** one exact-source inequality with its correct norm, complete future and unbounded quantifier; or an independent bounded certificate with no extrapolation.
