## Result

For a real entire \(F\) on a regular rectangle, put

\[
P_F=\frac{F}{F'},
\qquad
Q_F=\frac{F^2}{F'F''}.
\]

The two real critical-residue moments are exactly

\[
\mathcal M_{1,F}=-\Phi_{1,F}+C_{1,F},
\qquad
\mathcal M_{2,F}=B_F-C_{2,F}-D_{2,F},
\]

where \(\Phi_{1,F}\) and \(B_F\) are the complete boundary charges of \(P_F\)
and \(Q_F\). The three corrections expose nonreal first residues, nonreal
algebraic squared residues, and adjacent-derivative poles.

At each fixed regular \(T\), a sufficiently thin strip makes both nonreal
critical corrections vanish exactly. The remaining difficulty is controlling
the boundary charges and adjacent-derivative debt along a justified,
potentially shrinking strip sequence.

When \(R_F\mathcal M_{2,F}>0\), therefore

\[
\mathfrak C_F
=
\frac{(-\Phi_{1,F}+C_{1,F})_+^2}
{R_F(B_F-C_{2,F}-D_{2,F})}.
\]

For \(F=\Xi^{(k-1)}\), this is the exact fixed-window transfer-safe coherence
statistic, conditional on the stated window hypotheses. It agrees with the
literal squared RCMV104530 inequality only after the first moment is known to
be positive.

The polynomial \(x^5-x^3+x\) has no real critical point but has global first
boundary charge \(-2/25\). Its nonreal correction is also \(-2/25\), so the
real carrier is zero. Dropping that correction manufactures a false positive
carrier.
