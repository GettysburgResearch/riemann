# T-105240 — positive companion energy

Checkpoint four on PR #731 turns the square-defect contour into a positive
real-axis energy.

Put

```text
E = F_(k+1) + i delta F_k
H = F_(k+1) + lambda F_(k-1)
T = H^2 / (F_k E).
```

At every real zero of `F_k`, `T` has residue `(1+lambda rho)^2`.  On the
real axis,

```text
-Im T = delta H^2 / (F_(k+1)^2 + delta^2 F_k^2) >= 0.
```

A toothed lower-half-plane contour gives the exact decomposition

```text
square defect
  = positive Lorentz/Clark energy
  + bottom-and-side flux
  - companion-pole charge.
```

The line energy is a quadratic function of `lambda`; its optimum is one
weighted correlation coefficient.  The remaining 90% problem is therefore a
specific weighted mean-value estimate plus the already-owned companion
corrections, not a discrete interpolation problem.
