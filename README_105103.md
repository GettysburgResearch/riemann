fixed-window contour identities.

At an arbitrary denominator event \(a\), factor

\[
F=w^mU,\qquad F'=w^rV,\qquad F''=w^sW,\qquad w=z-a.
\]

Then

\[
\operatorname{Res}_a\frac F{F'}
=[w^{r-m-1}]\frac UV,
\qquad
\operatorname{Res}_a\frac{F^2}{F'F''}
=[w^{r+s-2m-1}]\frac{U^2}{VW}.
\]

Both coefficients follow from a finite exact Taylor recurrence. On a regular
rectangle, the union \(Z(F')\cup Z(F'')\) is partitioned once into simple
noncommon \(F'\)-events, isolated simple \(F''\)-events, and merged
exceptional events. The latter contribute
\(\Lambda_1^{\mathrm{mrg}},\Lambda_2^{\mathrm{mrg}}\), giving

\[
\mathcal M_1^{\mathrm{snc}}
=-\Phi_1+C_1^s+\Lambda_1^{\mathrm{mrg}},
\qquad
\mathcal M_2^{\mathrm{snc}}
=B-C_2^s-D_2^s-\Lambda_2^{\mathrm{mrg}}.
\]
