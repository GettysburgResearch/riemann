# T-105340 — Oriented shifted-ratio transfer

This checkpoint replaces the unnecessarily strong requirement of proving a
separate explicit formula for every shifted function `xi' - alpha xi`.
The single meromorphic ratio

\[
\mathcal M_\alpha(s)=\frac{\xi'(s)-\alpha\xi(s)}{\xi'(s)+\alpha\xi(s)}
\]

has exactly the oriented divisor needed by the low-order Pick current. Its
alpha derivative at zero is the complete confluent `xi/xi'` tangent; common
xi factors cancel; the functional equation folds the left safe line onto the
right one; and the frozen reciprocal Dirichlet source has a power-saving
coefficient tail.

No new zero proportion and no RH theorem is claimed.
