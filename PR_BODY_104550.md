## T104550 fixed-order Laguerre orientation bridge

This continuation gives a direct, source-locked route from Conrey's
`alpha_3>0.9873` result to a lower bound for `alpha_2`.

For

```text
L_2(t)=Xi'''(t)^2-Xi''(t)Xi''''(t),
```

nonnegative sign at a real `Xi'''` zero is exactly the Rolle-generating
orientation for `Xi''`.  If a proportion `q` of the real `Xi'''` zeros has this
sign, then

```text
alpha_2 >= (2q-1) alpha_3.
```

In particular, pointwise `L_2>=0` gives

```text
alpha_2 >= alpha_3 > 0.9873.
```

The expression has the exact kernel representation

```text
L_2(t)=4 Fourier[K_2](2t),
K_2(x)=integral y^2 [u^2 Phi(u)]_(x+y)[u^2 Phi(u)]_(x-y) dy.
```

`K_2` is nonnegative, and `L_2` is positive definite with positive Gaussian
averages.  The final theorem is positive definiteness of `K_2` itself.  It is
not proved here.
