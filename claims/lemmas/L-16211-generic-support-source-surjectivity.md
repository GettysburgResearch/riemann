## 1. Source range on the circle

Let

```text
L=log mu=2log lambda,
C_L=R/LZ.                                                (L-16211.1)
```

Let `S_0^ev` be the even Schwartz source space with

```text
f(0)=0,
integral_R f=0,                                          (L-16211.2)
```

and let

```text
R_L=Sigma_mu E(S_0^ev) subset L2(C_L).                   (L-16211.3)
```

Connes--Consani define `C_L` to be a zeta cycle exactly when `R_L` is not dense.
Their Theorem 1.1 identifies the rotation spectrum of

```text
closure(R_L)^perp                                        (L-16211.4)
```

with critical-line zeta-zero ordinates. In particular a Fourier character

```text
exp(2pi i kx/L)                                          (L-16211.5)
```

can belong to the orthogonal complement only if

```text
zeta(1/2+i 2pi k/L)=0.                                   (L-16211.6)
```

## 2. Generic support theorem

Assume

```text
zeta(1/2+i 2pi k/L)!=0
for every nonzero integer k.                             (L-16211.7)
```

Then

```text
boxed:
closure(R_L)=L2(C_L).                                    (L-16211.8)
```

The exceptional lengths lie in the countable set

```text
L=2pi |k|/gamma,                                         (L-16211.9)
```

where `gamma` is a critical-line zero ordinate and `k` is a nonzero integer.
Therefore every cofinal support sequence may be perturbed arbitrarily slightly
to satisfy (L-16211.7).

## 3. Exact finite projection consequence

Let `P_N` be the ordinary Fourier projection onto

```text
E_N(L)=span{exp(2pi i kx/L): |k|<=N}.                    (L-16211.10)
```

Under (L-16211.7),

```text
boxed:
P_N R_L=E_N(L)                                           (L-16211.11)
```

for every finite `N`.

Indeed, continuity of `P_N` and (L-16211.8) imply that `P_NR_L` is dense in
`E_N(L)`. It is a linear subspace of a finite-dimensional space and is therefore
closed, so it equals all of `E_N(L)`.

Thus every finite CCM Fourier vector has an exact arithmetic-source preimage
after projection. There is no nonzero **algebraic** background sector defined
as the ordinary orthogonal complement of the complete projected source range.

## 4. Exceptional lengths and finite cutoffs

Even when `L` is a zeta-cycle, the finite projection remains surjective provided
no obstructing character occurs inside the cutoff:

```text
zeta(1/2+i 2pi k/L)!=0,
0<|k|<=N.                                                 (L-16211.12)
```

More generally,

```text
E_N(L) intersect closure(R_L)^perp
```

is spanned by the finitely many obstructing characters satisfying
(L-16211.6).

## 5. What this does to gate 3

The previous source/background split could suggest that the finite CCM space
contains mysterious directions not generated by exact radical sources. At a
generic support this is false at the algebraic level:

```text
boxed:
finite Fourier space = projected exact-source range.     (L-16211.13)
```

Consequently the background-gap gate should be reformulated as a quantitative
**source-frame coercivity** theorem. One must control the source preimages and
the Weil form on the complete projected range; one need not posit a separate
non-source Hilbert sector.

Equivalently, one may choose a low repaired packet `S_low` and define its
background as the projected source directions outside that packet. Gate 3 then
asks for the first generalized eigenvalue of this high-source complement, not
for positivity of an unrelated space.

## 6. The missing quantitative theorem

Density and finite surjectivity supply no bound for a right inverse of

```text
P_N Sigma_mu E.                                          (L-16211.14)
```

Near a zeta-cycle the smallest singular value can be arbitrarily small. Hence
(L-16211.11) does not by itself imply

```text
g_background>=c a_lambda d_8.                           (L-16211.15)
```

The exact remaining gate is now:

```text
boxed:

construct a source right inverse whose tail/Hardy energy is controlled at
relative mode-8 scale.                                   (L-16211.16)
```

This is a quantitative frame theorem. It is strictly sharper than an undefined
background positivity assertion.
