## 1. The real critical-line entire function

Put

\[
 \Xi(z)=\xi(\tfrac12+iz).
 \tag{L-91332.1}
\]

Then `Xi` is a real entire even function of order one. On the real axis,

\[
 \xi(\tfrac12+ix)=\Xi(x),
 \qquad
 \xi'(\tfrac12+ix)=-i\Xi'(x),
 \qquad
 \xi''(\tfrac12+ix)=-\Xi''(x).
 \tag{L-91332.2}
\]

Define the meromorphic logarithmic-derivative function

\[
 \boxed{
 m(z)=-\frac{\Xi'(z)}{\Xi(z)}.
 }
 \tag{L-91332.3}

Its first boundary derivative is

\[
 \boxed{

 =\frac{\Xi'(x)^2-\Xi(x)\Xi''(x)}{\Xi(x)^2}

 \tag{L-91332.4}

\[
 \mathcal L_1[\Xi](x)
 =\Xi'(x)^2-\Xi(x)\Xi''(x)
 \tag{L-91332.5}
\]

is the first Laguerre curvature.

## 5. Exact RH characterization

The following are equivalent:

1. the Riemann Hypothesis;
2. every zero of `Xi` is real;
3. `m=-Xi'/Xi` is a Nevanlinna/Herglotz function on the upper half-plane;
4. `m` is analytic on the upper half-plane and every finite Pick matrix

   \[
   \left(
    \mathcal P_m(z_j,z_k)
   \right)_{j,k}
   \tag{L-91332.14}
   \]

   is positive semidefinite.

### Proof

If every zero is real, the canonical product for the real entire order-one
function `Xi` expresses `m`, up to a real constant, as the locally uniformly
convergent sum of terms

\[
 -\frac1{z-\gamma}
\]

with real `gamma` and the standard real convergence corrections. Every such
term has nonnegative imaginary part in the upper half-plane, so `m` is
Herglotz.

Conversely, a Herglotz function is analytic in the upper half-plane and can
have meromorphic poles only on the real boundary with nonpositive residues.
A nonreal zero of `Xi` in the upper half-plane would be an interior pole of
`m`; real conjugation would place the conjugate zero in the lower half-plane.
Thus Herglotzness excludes every nonreal zero and gives RH.

The equivalence between Herglotzness and positivity of (L-91332.14) is the
Nevanlinna–Pick kernel theorem.
