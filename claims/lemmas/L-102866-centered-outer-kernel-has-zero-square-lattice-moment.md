# L-102866 — The centered outer kernel has a zero square-lattice moment

Claim ID: `L-102866`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT ANALYTIC LEMMA**  
Created: 2026-08-24  
Depends on: `L-102732`, `L-102737`, `T-102780`  
RH status: **not assumed**

Let

\[
R_L(y)=\frac{P_{-8}(y)-P_0(y)}{16}
\]

be the one-atom, carrier-centered outer/Lorentz kernel. Equivalently,

\[
R_L=J P_2\bigl[(S_--8)^2-S_-^2\bigr]/16.
\]

Since

\[
[(S_--8)^2-S_-^2]/16=4-S_-
\]

on the active half-line,

\[
\boxed{R_L=JP_2\bigl[(4-S_-)\mathbf1_{[1,\infty)}\bigr].}
\tag{L-102866.1}
\]

`L-102732` gives

\[
\operatorname{supp}R_L\subset[1,8].
\]

## 1. Exact piecewise derivative

Writing `x=sqrt(y)`, the logarithmic derivative

\[
K_L(y)=D R_L(y)
\]

is

\[
\boxed{
K_L(y)=
\begin{cases}
8-4\sqrt y,&1\le y<2,\\
-8(1+\sqrt2)+4\sqrt2\sqrt y,&2\le y<4,\\
8\sqrt2-2\sqrt y,&4\le y<8,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}}
\tag{L-102866.2}
\]

The endpoint values of `R_L` vanish at `1` and `8`.

## 2. Zero continuous square-lattice moment

The Mellin multiplier of `R_L` contains the factor

\[
(1-2^{-s})^2/s.
\]

It therefore has a removable zero at `s=0`. Equivalently, direct integration
of (L-102866.2) gives

\[
\boxed{
\int_1^8 R_L(y)\frac{dy}{y}=0.
}
\tag{L-102866.3}

This is the exact moment seen by a lattice variable entering through its
square.

## 3. Uniform lattice remainder

For `Z>0`, put

\[
\mathscr L_R(Z)
=\sum_{m\ge1}\frac1m R_L\!\left(\frac Z{m^2}\right).
\]

Define

\[
g_Z(x)=x^{-1}R_L(Z/x^2).
\]

The support of `g_Z` is contained in

\[
\sqrt{Z/8}\le x\le\sqrt Z,
\]

and the change of variables `y=Z/x^2` gives

\[
\int_0^\infty g_Z(x)\,dx
=\frac12\int_1^8R_L(y)\frac{dy}{y}=0.
\]

Writing `x=sqrt(Z)t`,

\[
g_Z(x)=Z^{-1/2}h(t),
\qquad
h(t)=t^{-1}R_L(t^{-2}),
\]

where `h` is one fixed compact bounded-variation function. The elementary
sum-integral inequality therefore yields

\[
\boxed{
|\mathscr L_R(Z)|
\le C_R Z^{-1/2},
}
\tag{L-102866.4}

with the explicit finite constant

\[
C_R=\operatorname{Var}_{(0,\infty)}h+\|h\|_\infty.
\]

No cancellation in an arithmetic coefficient is used.

## Meaning

Every complete lattice generated by a square-core Type-I variable is paid by
`Z^(-1/2)`. This is the exact zero-moment resource needed for the square-core
Vaughan decomposition in `L-102867`; it is stronger than the unfiltered
prime-carrier subtraction and is fixed independently of every owner, phase and
hypothetical zeta zero.
