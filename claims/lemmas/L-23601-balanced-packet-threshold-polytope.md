## 2. Exact finite source

Fix `K>=6`, an output block `[J,J+1]`, the safe high-order window `H_K`, and

\[
 V=\lceil e^{J/K}\rceil.
\]

Apply `L-23201` independently to both inverse-zeta factors in the reflected
Selberg packet.  Through the complete endpoint, every coefficient is a finite
sum of terms

\[
 \mu(d_+)\mu(d_-)
 \prod_{i=1}^{r_+}r_V(n_{+,i})
 \prod_{j=1}^{r_-}r_V(n_{-,j}),
 \qquad r_\pm<K,
\tag{L-23601.1}
\]

with no analytic inverse-zeta remainder.  Expand

\[
 r_V(n)=-\sum_{\substack{e\mid n\\e\le V}}\mu(e).
\tag{L-23601.2}
\]

After this expansion, every sign in one source row is a product of actual
Möbius signs.  No absolute value has yet been taken.

For each destination type `tau`, let `Omega_(K,tau,J)` be the complete finite
set of expanded tuples, including every binomial coefficient, transition row,
cutoff convention, and multiplicity.  The reflected source is

\[
 Q_{K,\tau,J}(x)
 =\sum_{\omega\in\Omega_{K,\tau,J}}
  \sigma(\omega)a(\omega)
  H_K\bigl(x-L(\omega)\bigr),
\tag{L-23601.3}
\]

where `sigma(omega) in {+1,-1}`, `a(omega)>=0`, and `L(omega)` is the complete
logarithmic product coordinate.  Equation (L-23601.3) is the actual signed
source vector; it is not an arbitrary coefficient model.

## 3. Prefix coordinates

Within each ordered residual word write

\[
 y_i=\log n_i,
 \qquad
 s_i=y_1+\cdots+y_i.
\tag{L-23601.4}
\]

The triangular map

\[
 (y_1,\ldots,y_r)\longmapsto(s_1,\ldots,s_r)
\tag{L-23601.5}
\]

is unimodular at the formal exponent-lattice level and invertible over the
physical logarithmic cells.  In these coordinates:

- ordering is `s_(i-1)<=s_i`;
- a first crossing is one coordinate interval for one `s_i`;
- every prefix-product cutoff is a coordinate upper or lower bound;
- factor-size cells become differences of adjacent coordinate bounds;
- residual-word complexity is a finite type label, not a new geometric
  inequality.

Thus the `O(K)` prefix and first-crossing surfaces are **coordinate surfaces**.
They do not increase the global coupling rank.

## 5. Signed valuation

Let `mathscr A_K` be the algebra generated by translations in all prefix
coordinates and by the polynomial-exponential source densities occurring after

\[
 \mathcal V_{K,\tau}(\mathcal P;F)
 =\sum_{\omega\in\mathcal P\cap\Omega_{K,\tau,J}}
  \sigma(\omega)a(\omega)F(\omega).
\tag{L-23601.8}
\]

Additivity is with respect to disjoint cell decompositions, including boundary

The point of (L-23601.8) is that every balanced face belongs to one finite
polyhedral valuation.  A face is not declared terminal merely because several

## 6. Full-cube and threshold parts

For a finite prime set `P`, the untruncated squarefree Möbius operator is

\[
 \prod_{p\in P}
 \left(I-p^{-1/2}T_{\log p}\right).
\tag{L-23601.9}
\]

Truncation at `d<=V` is exactly the intersection of the Boolean cube with the
weighted halfspace

\[
 \sum_{p\in S}\log p\le\log V.
\tag{L-23601.10}
\]

Consequently every residual expansion is a finite sum of:

1. complete cube differences, for which every toggle is present; and
2. threshold-boundary valuations, where the product cutoff removes part of the

The complete-cube part is consumed by the null quotient in `L-23602`.  The
threshold part is not discarded; it is included in the polytope
`mathcal P_(K,tau,J)` and is localized by `L-23603`.
