## 1. Purpose

`L-91359` proves global monotonicity of

\[
 \Phi_j(Y)=\frac{Q_Y(j)}{5\sqrt Y-3},
 \qquad 2\le j\le66,
\]

and closes the child-inactive part of the causal packet. `R-91311` correctly refutes continuous monotonicity of the child-active causal ratio for arbitrary nearby real arguments.

The actual arithmetic arguments are not arbitrary nearby reals. They have the form

\[
 z=\frac yd,
 \qquad d\mid P_{61},
 \qquad d\le y<67,
\]

and distinct active divisors have a fixed logarithmic separation. This theorem uses that gap to close the complete unbounded rough-prime tail.

## 2. Exact causal identity

Put

\[
 S(Y)=5\sqrt Y-3,
 \qquad r=p^{-1/2},
\]

and define

\[
 \mathcal R_{p,j}(z)
 =\frac{Q_{pz}(j)-rQ_z(j)}{S(pz)-rS(z)}.
 \tag{L-91681.1}
\]

Extend `Phi_j(Y)` by zero on `1<=Y<j`, consistently with `Q_Y(j)=0`. Since `Q_Y=Phi_j(Y)S(Y)`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal R_{p,j}(z)
 =\Phi_j(pz)
  +\theta_p(z)[\Phi_j(pz)-\Phi_j(z)],
 }
 \tag{L-91681.2}
\]

where

\[
 \theta_p(z)=\frac{rS(z)}{S(pz)-rS(z)}.
 \tag{L-91681.3}
\]

For `p>1,z>=1`,

\[
 \boxed{0\le\theta_p(z)<\frac1{p-1}.}
 \tag{L-91681.4}
\]

After multiplying by `sqrt(p)`, the upper bound reduces to `p>sqrt(p)`. The correction in (L-91681.2) is nonnegative because `Phi_j` is increasing.

## 5. Arithmetic separation

\[

 \qquad d_2\le y<67,
\]

\[
 \boxed{

 =\log\frac{d_2}{d_1}
 \ge\delta_0:=\log\frac{66}{65}.
 }
 \tag{L-91681.12}

\[

 \tag{L-91681.13}
