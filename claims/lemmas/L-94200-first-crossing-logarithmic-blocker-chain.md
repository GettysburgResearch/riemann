# L-94200 — First-crossing control gives a logarithmic endpoint blocker chain

**Status:** PROPOSED UNCONDITIONAL LEMMA — INDEPENDENT RECONSTRUCTION REQUIRED
**Created:** 2026-08-16
**Depends on:** `L-94100`, `L-94101`
**RH status:** unproved pending review

For the live backward endpoint-detail greedy, let

\[
R_T(q)=\rho^{(T+1)}(q)/\Delta_T(q),\qquad 2\le q<T,
\]

and let `r` be the least minimizer. The proposed first-crossing estimate is

\[
[R_T(j+1)-R_T(j)]_+\le 96/j\qquad(r\le j<T-1).
\]

Consequently the diagonal blocker loss

\[
\ell_T=R_T(T-1)-\min_{q<T}R_T(q)
\]

satisfies

\[
\ell_T\le96\log(2X).
\]

The complete proof, including the kernel integral, normalized cross-ratio estimates and the least-minimizer cancellation, is in Sections 4 and Appendix B of the standalone manuscript. The last cancellation is the principal review target. The statement is false for arbitrary nonnegative residuals; it is asserted only for the live residual generated by `L-94101`.
