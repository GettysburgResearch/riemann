# L-92101 — Conjugate Bernstein pair

Claim ID: L-92101
Status: PROPOSED EXACT FUNCTION-CLASS REDUCTION
RH status: unproved

Let p(t)=F(sqrt(t))/sqrt(t), Z(t)=1/p(t), and Zstar(t)=t/Z(t)=t p(t).

For a nonzero complete Bernstein function Z, the conjugate t/Z is also complete Bernstein. Therefore both Z and Zstar are increasing and concave.

The exact two-node determinant from L-92000 is controlled by monotonicity of Z and Zstar. The exact three-node determinant is a positive prefactor times the product of the second divided differences of Z and Zstar. Thus order two and order three are the first derivative and concavity shadows of one complete-Bernstein hierarchy.

A nonnegative function is complete Bernstein exactly when it is operator monotone. Equivalently, every finite Loewner matrix of Z is positive semidefinite. Subject to L-92100, positivity of these matrices on all finite rational packets t>1/4 is RH-equivalent.

Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.
