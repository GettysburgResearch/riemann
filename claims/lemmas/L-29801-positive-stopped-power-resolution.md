## 1. Exact finite stopped-power resolution

Fix an integer endpoint `X>=2` and put

\[
 w_X(q)=q^{-1/2}\log(X/q),
 \qquad 1\le q\le X.
\tag{L-29801.1}
\]

For `1<=Y<X`, define the stopped critical power

\[
 p_Y(q)=q^{-1/2}\mathbf1_{q\le Y}
\tag{L-29801.2}
\]

and the positive endpoint weight

\[
 \ell_Y=\log\frac{Y+1}{Y}>0.
\tag{L-29801.3}
\]

Then for every `q<=X`,

\[
\boxed{
 w_X(q)=\sum_{Y=1}^{X-1}\ell_Yp_Y(q)
       =\sum_{Y=q}^{X-1}\log\frac{Y+1}{Y}\,q^{-1/2}.
}
\tag{L-29801.4}
\]

The proof is the finite telescope

\[
 \sum_{Y=q}^{X-1}\log\frac{Y+1}{Y}=\log\frac Xq.
\]

Thus the critical logarithmic source does not need to be propagated as a signed derivative with respect to a Mellin exponent.  It is a finite positive combination of stopped pure powers.

This replacement is load bearing.  The logarithmic Jordan channel in PR #286 is harmless analytically, but its exponent derivative can produce signed coefficient corrections.  Equation (L-29801.4) removes that sign issue at the finite source level.

## 2. Positive finite differences of a pure power

For real `s>0`, let

\[
 p_s(x)=x^{-s},
\]

and use the forward-decrease convention

\[
 \Delta_hf(x)=f(x)-f(x+h),
 \qquad h>0.
\]

Repeated use of the fundamental theorem of calculus gives

\[
\boxed{
 \Delta_h^mp_s(x)
 =(s)_m
 \int_{[0,h]^m}
 (x+t_1+\cdots+t_m)^{-s-m}
 \,dt_1\cdots dt_m
 \ge0.
}
\tag{L-29801.5}
\]

Here `(s)_m=s(s+1)...(s+m-1)`.  In particular, for every arithmetic progression `x+jh`,

\[
 \Delta_h^mp_s(x+jh)>0
\tag{L-29801.6}
\]

and the sequence in `j` is decreasing.

Consequently the alternating Euler remainder has a fixed sign:

\[
\boxed{
 0\le
 \sum_{j\ge K}(-1)^{j-K}\Delta_h^mp_s(x+jh)
 \le \Delta_h^mp_s(x+Kh).
}
\tag{L-29801.7}
\]

The same statements hold after integrating against any nonnegative measure in `s` or multiplying by a nonnegative source coefficient.
