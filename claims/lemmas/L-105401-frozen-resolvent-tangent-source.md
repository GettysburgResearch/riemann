# L-105401 — Frozen resolvent tangent source

Let

\[
R_L=(L\delta_1-\Lambda)^{-*},\qquad C_J=(\Lambda\log)*R_L.
\]

Differentiating the inverse identity gives

\[
\boxed{\partial_LR_L=-R_L*R_L},
\qquad
\boxed{\partial_LC_J=-C_J*R_L}.
\]

The leading coefficient `-Lambda` in the frozen xi-prime source is independent
of `L`; hence

\[
\boxed{e_1=\partial_L\operatorname{xiCoeff}(L)=-C_J*R_L.}
\]

Its Dirichlet series is `-B(s)/(L-A(s))^2`. The trace and Hilbert--Schmidt
statistics are therefore one-copy and two-copy correlations of the same
literal tangent source.
