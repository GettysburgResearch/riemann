## 2. Triangular boundary B-spline

For `alpha<beta`, define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B_{\alpha,\beta}(t,u)
=
\frac{
(\min\{t,u\}-\alpha)
(\beta-\max\{t,u\})
}{\beta-\alpha}
\mathbf1_{[\alpha,\beta]^2}(t,u).}
\tag{L-26202.3}
\]

This kernel is nonnegative. If `L_(alpha,beta)g` is the affine interpolant of
`g` at the two endpoints, then Peano's identity gives

\[
\boxed{
L_{\alpha,\beta}g(t)-g(t)
=
\int_\alpha^\beta
\mathcal B_{\alpha,\beta}(t,u)g''(u)\,du
\ge0.}
\tag{L-26202.4}
\]

No Hankel or complete-monotonicity assertion is involved.
