## 1. Exact squared-shift form

Let

\[
(S_{\ell^2}f)(Y)=f(Y/\ell^2)
\]

and let `Pi_(<=ell)` denote restriction to the monoid generated by primes at
most `ell`.  Then each boundary owner acts as the positive squared shift

\[
\boxed{
{1\over\ell}S_{\ell^2}\Pi_{\le\ell}.
}
\tag{L-102881.2}

The prefix factors `d,e` contribute only the positive harmonic envelope

\[
\sum_{d,e\le U}{1\over de}\ll(\log(2U))^2.
\tag{L-102881.3}

All fixed filters, owner projections, Euler/half-divisor/Wick gauges and the
centered radial cost commute with (L-102881.2).

## 2. Ordered renewal histories

Iterating the boundary disintegration produces nondecreasing prime histories

\[
q\le\ell_1\le\ell_2\le\cdots\le\ell_j.
\]

The corresponding coefficient and scale are

\[
{1\over\ell_1\cdots\ell_j},
\qquad
{Y\over(\ell_1\cdots\ell_j)^2}.
\]

For every fixed `epsilon>0`, the total weighted scale factor is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{q\le\ell_1\le\cdots}
\prod_i\ell_i^{-1-2\varepsilon}
&=\prod_{\ell\ge q}
(1-\ell^{-1-2\varepsilon})^{-1}\\
&\le\zeta(1+2\varepsilon)<\infty.
\end{aligned}
\tag{L-102881.4}

No contraction constant below one is required; source order changes the
iteration from an ordered geometric series to a convergent Euler product.
