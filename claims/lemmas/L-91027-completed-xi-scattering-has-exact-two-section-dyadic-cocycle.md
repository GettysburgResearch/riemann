# L-91027 — The completed xi scattering ratio has an exact two-section dyadic cocycle

## 1. Complex carrier extension

For `a>0` and a complex carrier `z`, define

\[
 \Theta_a(z)
 =\frac{\xi(1/2-a+iz)}{\xi(1/2+a+iz)}.
 \tag{L-91027.1}
\]

It is meromorphic in `z`.  On the real axis it is unimodular wherever finite.

## 2. Exact dyadic factorisation

A direct cancellation of the intermediate xi value gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Theta_a(z+ia)
 &=\frac{\xi(1/2-2a+iz)}{\xi(1/2+iz)},\\
 \Theta_a(z-ia)
 &=\frac{\xi(1/2+iz)}{\xi(1/2+2a+iz)}.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{L-91027.2}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta_{2a}(z)
 =\Theta_a(z+ia)\Theta_a(z-ia).
 }
 \tag{L-91027.3}
\]

More generally, for `a,b>0`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta_{a+b}(z)
 =\Theta_a(z+ib)\Theta_b(z-ia).
 }
 \tag{L-91027.4}
\]

This is the completed nonlinear radial scattering cocycle.

## 3. Exact delay recurrence

Put

\[
 \mathcal T_a(z)
 =i\,\partial_z\log\Theta_a(z).
 \tag{L-91027.5}
\]

Away from poles and zeros, logarithmic differentiation of (L-91027.4) gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal T_{a+b}(z)
 =\mathcal T_a(z+ib)+\mathcal T_b(z-ia).
 }
 \tag{L-91027.6}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal T_{2a}(x)
 =\mathcal T_a(x+ia)+\mathcal T_a(x-ia)
 }
 \tag{L-91027.7}
\]

for every real carrier at which the terms are finite.

On the boundary, `T_a(x)=2 Re xi'/xi(1/2+a+ix)` by `L-91023`.
Thus the completed dyadic soft count is generated by one meromorphic delay
function sampled at two conjugate interior sections.

## 4. Exact zeta/source cocycle inside the completion

Factor `Theta_a=Gamma_a Q_a` as in `L-91023`.  Substituting into
(L-91027.4) separates it into

\[
 Q_{a+b}(s_x-a-b)
 =Q_a(s_x-a-b)Q_b(s_x+a-b),
 \tag{L-91027.8}
\]

which is precisely the generalized-Jordan cocycle, and the matching elementary
gamma/pole identity.  Hence the positive divisor isometry of `L-91014` is the
source-space realization of the zeta part of the exact completed two-section
scattering law.

## 5. Inner-function interpretation under a zero-free strip

If xi has no zero in

\[
 \Re s>\frac12+a,
\]

then, after the standard choice of upper-half-plane orientation,
`Theta_a` is a meromorphic inner/Hermite--Biehler scattering function.  Its
kernel

\[
 \frac{1-\Theta_a(z)\overline{\Theta_a(w)}}
      {-i(z-\overline w)}
 \tag{L-91027.9}
\]

is positive semidefinite.  The failure of that positivity is exactly an
off-line pole/zero crossing.

Equation (L-91027.3) therefore expresses scale doubling as the product of two
conjugate sections of one inner scattering object.  A successful proof of the
Cauchy recurrence may be sought as a Schwarz--Pick/Julia curvature inequality
for this product, with the positive source cocycle providing its state-space
realization.

## 6. Off-line singularity transport

A zero

\[
 \rho=\frac12+d+i\gamma
\]

is a real-axis pole of `Theta_d` at `x=gamma`.  Under the dyadic cocycle it
appears in exactly one of the two interior factors whenever the radial interval
crosses depth `d`.  This gives the scattering version of the sharp depth
projectors on PRs #394 and `L-91016`.
