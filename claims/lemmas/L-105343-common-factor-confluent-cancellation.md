# L-105343 — Common-factor and confluent cancellation

Claim ID: `L-105343`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT**

If `F` has a zero of multiplicity `m`, then `F'` has multiplicity `m-1` and
both `E_alpha` and `E_-alpha` have the same persistent factor of multiplicity
`m-1`. It cancels from `M_alpha`. Equivalently,

\[
\mathcal M_\alpha=(F'/F-\alpha)/(F'/F+\alpha)
\]

extends meromorphically across every zero of `F` and tends to one at a pole of
`F'/F`.

At a zero of `F'` of multiplicity `r`, `F/F'` may have a pole of order `r`.
The tangent in L-105340 takes the complete residue after multiplication by
`Psi'`; this is the confluent Hermite block. No analytic selection of
individual split zero branches is required.
