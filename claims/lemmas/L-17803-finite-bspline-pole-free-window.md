## 1. Finite equal-box density

Fix an integer `J>=1` and define the normalized box

\[
 b_J(u)=J\,\mathbf 1_{[0,1/J]}(u).
 \tag{1}
\]

Let

\[
 F_J=b_J^{*(2J)}.
 \tag{2}
\]

Then `F_J` is a nonnegative probability density supported in `[0,2]`, is a
spline of degree `2J-1`, and is `C^(2J-2)`. Its exact formula is

\[
 \boxed{
 F_J(u)=\frac{J^{2J}}{(2J-1)!}
 \sum_{k=0}^{2J}(-1)^k\binom{2J}{k}
 (u-k/J)_+^{2J-1}.}
 \tag{3}
\]

All knots and polynomial coefficients are rational.

## 2. Pole-free signed window

Put

\[

\]

and define

\[
 \boxed{

 \tag{4}
\]

It is supported in `[2,4+h]`. Its Laplace transform is

\[
 \boxed{
 g_J(z)=e^{-2z}
 \left(\frac{1-e^{-z/J}}{z/J}\right)^{2J}

 \tag{5}
\]

The removable value is used at `z=0`.

The box factor has zeros only at

\[
 z=2\pi iJk,\qquad k\ne0,

and the last factor has zeros only on `Re z=1/2`. Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 g_J(1/2)=0,
 \qquad
 g_J(z)\ne0\quad(0<\operatorname{Re}z<1/2).}
 \tag{6}
\]

raw prime statistic generated by `G_J` is equivalent to RH.
