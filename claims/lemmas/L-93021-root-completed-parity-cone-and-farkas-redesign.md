## 1. Root functional

For a finite carry target \(t(q)\), define

\[
 \rho_2(t)
 =
 \sum_{q=2}^{X}b_2(q)t(q),
 \qquad
 b_2=(\varepsilon-\delta_2)*\mu.
 \tag{L-93021.1}
\]

If \(d_e\ge0\) is a positive quarter-balanced split flow with carry loads
\(t(q)\), the two-contact identity of `L-32401` gives

\[
 \rho_2(t)
 =
 -\sum_e d_e
 \left[
 \mathbf1_{j(e)=1}
 +
 \mathbf1_{n(e)-j(e)=1}
 \right]
 \le0.
 \tag{L-93021.2}
\]

Thus \(\rho_2(t)\le0\) is a necessary condition for a positive realization.
It is not a generic sufficient condition; Section 5 records an exact
counterexample.

## 2. Minimal bottom completion

Suppose the target is specified first on columns \(q\ge3\). Put

\[
 \rho_2^{>2}(t)
 =
 \sum_{q=3}^{X}b_2(q)t(q)
 \tag{L-93021.3}
\]

and define the minimal root completion

\[
 \boxed{
 \beta(t)
 =
 \frac12[\rho_2^{>2}(t)]_+.
 }
 \tag{L-93021.4}
\]

Set

\[
 \widetilde t(2)=\beta(t),
 \qquad
 \widetilde t(q)=t(q)\quad(q\ge3).
 \tag{L-93021.5}
\]

Since \(b_2(2)=-2\),

\[
 \rho_2(\widetilde t)
 =
 \rho_2^{>2}(t)-2\beta(t)
 \le0.
 \tag{L-93021.6}
\]

If the positive part in (L-93021.4) is nonzero, the completed target lies
exactly on the root face:

\[
 \rho_2(\widetilde t)=0.
 \tag{L-93021.7}
\]

No smaller nonnegative value at column two can satisfy the necessary root
inequality.

## 3. The positive interior cone

Let \(E_X^\circ\) be the allowed quarter-balanced splits whose two children
are at least two. Let

\[
 C_X^\circ
 =
 \left\{
 \sum_{e\in E_X^\circ}x_e\chi_e:
 x_e\ge0
 \right\}
 \subseteq\mathbb R^{X-1}.
 \tag{L-93021.8}
\]

The **root-completed transverse producer problem** is

\[
 \boxed{
 \widetilde t\in C_X^\circ.
 }
 \tag{L-93021.9}
\]

The use of the interior cone is forced on the root face. If
\(\rho_2(\widetilde t)=0\), then (L-93021.2) shows that every positive
realization has zero coefficient on every unit-child split.

Let \(A_X^\circ\) be the carry-incidence matrix of \(E_X^\circ\).
The problem is the finite system

\[
 A_X^\circ x=\widetilde t,
 \qquad
 x\ge0.
 \tag{L-93021.10}
\]

A feasible point is a positive source certificate. If no feasible point
exists, the exact Farkas alternative supplies a vector \(y\) such that

\[
 (A_X^\circ)^Ty\ge0,
 \qquad
 \langle y,\widetilde t\rangle<0.
 \tag{L-93021.11}
\]

Thus the redesigned gate is fail-closed at every endpoint.

Whenever feasible, a solution may be chosen on at most
\(\operatorname{rank}(A_X^\circ)\) splits. For the actual critical target the
coefficients live in the field generated by the declared square roots and
logarithms; source equality must be symbolic or directed, as required by
`R-93016`.

## 4. Exact relation to Cycle Debt

For the full critical target

\[
 w_X(q)=q^{-1/2}\log(X/q),
 \tag{L-93021.12}
\]

apply (L-93021.4) to its columns \(q\ge3\), and write the result as
\(\beta_X\).

Assume the endpoint-specific transverse producer (L-93021.9) is feasible.

If \(\beta_X\le w_X(2)\), add the nonnegative bottom split
\((2,1,1)\) with coefficient \(w_X(2)-\beta_X\). This gives a nonnegative
exact realization of the full target.

If \(\beta_X>w_X(2)\), subtract the bottom split with coefficient
\(\beta_X-w_X(2)\). Every other split remains nonnegative, and the entire
negative capacity debt is

\[
 2^{-1/2}[\beta_X-w_X(2)]_+.
 \tag{L-93021.13}
\]

The universal root witness in `R-93021` gives the matching lower bound.
Indeed

\[
\begin{aligned}
\rho_2(w_X)
&=
\rho_2^{>2}(w_X)-2w_X(2),\\
\frac1{2\sqrt2}[\rho_2(w_X)]_+
&=
2^{-1/2}[\beta_X-w_X(2)]_+.
\end{aligned}
\tag{L-93021.14}
\]

Consequently, conditional only on the positive transverse realization,

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak N_X
 =
 \frac1{2\sqrt2}
 [\rho_2(w_X)]_+.
 }
 \tag{L-93021.15}
\]

This is not assumed as an unconditional identity: the critical
root-completed transverse producer remains to be proved.

Equation (L-93021.15) explains the finite OPB separator. The only allowed
one-dimensional debt after a positive transverse completion is the
zero-safe dyadic root scalar; every other negative coefficient has been
eliminated.

## 5. Root neutrality alone is insufficient

The special shape of the critical target is load-bearing. At \(X=6\), take

\[
 t(2)=1,\qquad t(6)=1,
 \qquad t(3)=t(4)=t(5)=0.
 \tag{L-93021.16}
\]

Since

\[
 b_2(2)=-2,\qquad b_2(6)=2,
\]

the target is root neutral:

\[
 \rho_2(t)=0.
 \tag{L-93021.17}
\]

Nevertheless it is not in the positive interior cone. The exact Farkas
separator

\[
 y(2)=-1,\qquad y(4)=1,
 \qquad y(3)=y(5)=y(6)=0
 \tag{L-93021.18}
\]

pairs nonnegatively with every interior split row at \(X=6\), while

\[
 \langle y,t\rangle=-1.
 \tag{L-93021.19}
\]

Therefore no theorem of the form

```text
root functional <= 0  implies positive balanced fragmentation
```

can close the route.

The root scalar has transform

\[
 \frac{1-2^{-z-1/2}}{z^2\zeta(z+1/2)}.
 \tag{L-93021.20}
