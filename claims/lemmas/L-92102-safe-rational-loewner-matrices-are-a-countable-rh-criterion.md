# L-92102 — Safe rational Loewner matrices

Claim ID: L-92102
Status: PROPOSED RH-EQUIVALENT CRITERION
RH status: unproved

For t>1/4 define Z(t)=sqrt(t)/[(xi'/xi)(1/2+sqrt(t))]. For distinct nodes t_i, form the Loewner matrix with off-diagonal entries [Z(t_i)-Z(t_j)]/[t_i-t_j] and diagonal entries Z'(t_i).

Subject to the analytic continuation interface of L-92100, RH is equivalent to positivity of every such matrix on every finite positive rational packet.

Under RH, Z is complete Bernstein and therefore operator monotone. Conversely, finite Loewner positivity gives a Pick continuation agreeing with the actual safe function. An off-line centered zero would create a nonreal zero of Z at lambda squared, which is impossible for the nonconstant Pick continuation.

Every entry uses xi'/xi and its derivative at real arguments greater than one. False RH therefore has a finite safe rational witness.

Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.
