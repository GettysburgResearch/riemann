## 1. Native allowance kernel

Put

\[
h(Z)=\log Z\,\mathbf1_{Z\ge1}.
\tag{L-91665.1}
\]

Then the native ordinary and radix-four allowances are

\[
w_X(q)=q^{-1/2}h(X/q),
\tag{L-91665.2}
\]

\[
\Omega_X(q)=q^{-1/2}[h(X/q)-h(X/(4q))].
\tag{L-91665.3}
\]

Let

\[
\mathcal M_{67}
=\{m\ge1:(m,P_{61})=1\}
\]

be the multiplicative monoid generated by primes at least `67`, including
`m=1`.

## 2. Exact rough renewal of the ordinary capacity

The positive rough kernel of `L-91363` is

\[
H_P(Z)
=\sum_{\substack{m\le Z\\m\in\mathcal M_{67}}}
 m^{-1/2}\log(Z/m).
\]

Using (L-91665.1), this is exactly

\[
\boxed{
H_P(Z)
=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_{67}}m^{-1/2}h(Z/m).
}
\tag{L-91665.4}
\]

Therefore the canonical finite-Euler ordinary response satisfies

\[
\boxed{
C_{P,X}(q)
=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_{67}}m^{-1/2}w_{X/m}(q).
}
\tag{L-91665.5}
\]

Separating the unit element gives

\[
\boxed{
C_{P,X}(q)
=w_X(q)
+\sum_{\substack{m\in\mathcal M_{67}\\m>1}}
 m^{-1/2}w_{X/m}(q).
}
\tag{L-91665.6}
\]

Every term is nonnegative. The canonical overfill is thus exactly the complete
positive rough-child allowance, not an uncontrolled error.

## 3. Exact rough renewal of the radix-four capacity

Apply the radix-four detail linearly to (L-91665.5). Since endpoint scaling and
the detail operator commute,

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_{P,X}(q)
=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_{67}}m^{-1/2}\Omega_{X/m}(q).
}
\tag{L-91665.7
}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_{P,X}(q)
=\Omega_X(q)
+\sum_{\substack{m\in\mathcal M_{67}\\m>1}}
 m^{-1/2}\Omega_{X/m}(q).
}
\tag{L-91665.8}
\]

This recovers the compact equality firewall of `L-91661`: no nonunit rough
integer is active when `X/q<=67`.

## 4. Least-rough-prime form

Partition every nonunit `m in M_67` by its least prime `p>=67`, writing
`m=pn` with all prime factors of `n` at least `p`. Equations
(L-91665.6)--(L-91665.8) become exact least-prime child decompositions:

\[
C_{P,X}
=w_X+
\sum_{p\ge67}p^{-1/2}
 \sum_{n\in\mathcal M_{p+}}n^{-1/2}w_{X/(pn)},
\tag{L-91665.9}
\]

and analogously for `Theta` and `Omega`.

These are the allowance-coordinate counterparts of the positive source stopping
line in `L-91362`.
