## 1. Exact finite datum

For integer `X`, define

\[
 d_X^\star(t)
 =\sum_{k\le X/t}
  \frac{\mu(k)}{\sqrt{kt}}
  \log\frac{X}{kt},
\]

\[
 b_X^\star(n)=\sum_{m=n}^{X}d_X^\star(m),
 \qquad
 c_X=\mathcal R b_X^\star.
\]

The exact native normalization is

\[
 C_{c_X}=w_X,
 \qquad
 \Xi_{c_X}=\Omega_X,
 \qquad
 \mathcal H(c_X)=J_\Lambda(X).
\tag{L-91880.1}
\]

No positivity of `c_X` is assumed.

Put

\[
 K=\left\lfloor\frac X{67}\right\rfloor+1,
 \qquad W=10000.
\]

Let

```text
inner finite cells:       m <= K+1;
outer continuum cells:    m >= K+2;
retained outer cells:     K+2 <= m <= X-W-3;
top outer cells:          X-W-2 <= m <= X-1.
```

All interval endpoints are integers.

## 2. Outer Volterra identity

For real `s>=1`, put

\[
 g_s(m)=\left(\sqrt m-\frac m{\sqrt s}\right)\mathbf1_{m\le s},
 \qquad
 p_s=\mathcal Rg_s\ge0,
\]

and, for `x>=1`,

\[
 \ell_x(k)=k^{-1/2}\left(2\sqrt{x/k}-1\right)>0.
\]

The one-colour Volterra identity gives

\[
 \overline c_{X,\mathrm{out}}
 =\int_{K+2}^{X}\frac2s
   \sum_{k\le X/s}\mu(k)\ell_{X/s}(k)p_s\,ds.
\tag{L-91880.2}
\]

On this interval `1<=X/s<67`. The directed factor-67 theorem gives

\[
 L(X/s):=\sum_k\mu(k)\ell_{X/s}(k)>\frac{159}{500}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
 \overline c_{X,\mathrm{out}}
 =\int_{K+2}^{X}\frac{2L(X/s)}s p_s\,ds
 \ge0.
}
\tag{L-91880.3}
\]

Split it into the retained and top positive rows

\[
 \overline c_{X,\mathrm{out}}
 =\overline c_{X,\mathrm{ret}}
  +\overline c_{X,\mathrm{top}}.
\tag{L-91880.4}
\]

## 3. Exact adjacent defect

For every outer integer cell put

\[
 \varepsilon_X(m)
 =d_X^\star(m)-\int_m^{m+1}d_X^\star(t)\,dt.
\]

Define the cumulative outer defect

\[
 E_{X,\mathrm{out}}(n)
 =\sum_{\substack{m\ge K+2\\m\ge n}}
   \varepsilon_X(m).
\tag{L-91880.5}
\]

Then

\[
 E_{X,\mathrm{out}}(n)-E_{X,\mathrm{out}}(n+1)
 =\mathbf1_{n\ge K+2}\varepsilon_X(n).
\tag{L-91880.6}
\]

There is no artificial cutoff atom at `K+1`.

Let `c_{X,\mathrm{in}}` be the exact finite component row generated by all occurrences with finite-cell index `m<=K+1`. Cellwise summation and integration give the exact hybrid identity

\[
\boxed{
 c_X
 =c_{X,\mathrm{in}}
 +\overline c_{X,\mathrm{ret}}
 +\overline c_{X,\mathrm{top}}
 +\mathcal R E_{X,\mathrm{out}}.
}
\tag{L-91880.7}
\]

The first three terms are arithmetic/row objects. The last term is a signed observation comparison.

## 4. Exact native occurrence marginals

The inner source marginal consists of the literal finite occurrences

\[

 \qquad
 m\le K+1,
 \quad km\le X.
\]

After retaining the sign `mu(k)`, each has coefficient

\[
 |\mu(k)|(km)^{-1/2}\log\frac{X}{km}.

\[
 d\Sigma_{X,\mathrm{out}}^\pm(s,k)

\tag{L-91880.8}

The two marginals are disjoint and, together with the signed defect in (L-91880.7), reconstruct the exact native row. Neither is the `P_61` row-first rough lift.
