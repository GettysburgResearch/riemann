## 1. Setting

Let `mathfrak D` be the closed quotient form on `H_in` generated by a closed radical relation, and let

\[
 \mathcal T:\operatorname{Dom}\mathfrak D\to H_{\rm out}
\]

be its canonical minimum-tail operator. Thus

\[
 \mathfrak D(v,w)
 =\langle\mathcal Tv,\mathcal Tw\rangle_{H_{\rm out}}.
\tag{L-19857.1}
\]

Let `Q` be the closed localized Weil form on the same domain. By `L-19856`,

\[
 Q(v,w)=Z(\mathcal Tv,\mathcal Tw),
\tag{L-19857.2}
\]

where `Z` is the exact exterior zero-side form.

## 2. Tail-space relative estimate

Assume that for all canonical tails

\[
 a=\mathcal Tv,
 \qquad
 b=\mathcal Tw,
\]

one has

\[
 \boxed{
 |Z(a,b)-c\langle a,b\rangle|
 \le\eta c\|a\|\|b\|,}
\tag{L-19857.3}
\]

with `c>0` and `0<=eta<1`.

Then (L-19857.1)--(L-19857.3) give

\[
 |Q(v,w)-c\mathfrak D(v,w)|
 \le\eta c
 \mathfrak D(v)^{1/2}\mathfrak D(w)^{1/2}.
\tag{L-19857.4}
\]

Taking `v=w`,

\[
 \boxed{
 (1-\eta)c\mathfrak D
 \preceq Q
 \preceq(1+\eta)c\mathfrak D.}
\tag{L-19857.5}
\]

Thus relative local-Weyl control on the canonical exterior tails is already the desired relative closed-form comparison. No source-basis condition number or finite congruence occurs.

## 3. Whitened operator form

On the closure of `Ran mathfrak D^(1/2)`, inequality (L-19857.3) is equivalent to

\[
 \left\|
 \mathfrak D^{-1/2}
 (Q-c\mathfrak D)
 \mathfrak D^{-1/2}
 \right\|
 \le\eta c.
\tag{L-19857.6}
\]

The inverse is understood on the quotient by `ker mathfrak D`. Vertical zero-interior radical tails are already removed by the minimum-tail construction.
