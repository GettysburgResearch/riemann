## 1. Reflected Hermitian source

For a real safe window `H_K`, put

\[
 W_{K,\alpha}(t)=|\widehat H_K(\alpha+it)|^2\ge0.
\tag{L-23602.1}
\]

The conjugate-product Selberg subtraction of `L-9516` gives the exact identity

\[
 2\int_{\mathbb R}W_{K,\alpha}(t)
 \left|\frac{\zeta'}\zeta
   \left(\frac12+\alpha+it\right)\right|^2dt
 =\mathcal V^{\rm ref}_{K,J},
\tag{L-23602.2}
\]

where the right side is the complete signed arithmetic forcing after the two
Möbius inverse factors, every transition row, and the pole/archimedean adapters
are included.  It is a Hermitian quadratic form.  The cross terms in
(L-23602.2) may not be replaced by separate scalar Selberg squares.

For each destination cell, `L-23601` writes this forcing as a valuation on the
threshold polytope `mathcal P_(K,tau,J)`.

## 2. Null quotient

Let `mathscr N_K` be the finite-dimensional space generated by

\[
 u^re^{\lambda u},
 \qquad
 0\le r\le K,
 \qquad
 \lambda\in\{0,1/2,-1/2\},
\tag{L-23602.3}
\]

with the exact subset of exponents dictated by the chosen pole-free window and
its reflected autocorrelation.  The high-order safe-window construction gives

\[
 \int H_K(u)f(u)du=0
 \qquad(f\in\mathscr N_K),
\tag{L-23602.4}
\]

and the corresponding two-leg statement for the autocorrelation kernel.

High-order Euler summation writes every complete unrestricted lattice variable
as

```text
continuous polynomial-exponential principal part
+
periodic-Bernoulli remainder
+
endpoint evaluations.
```

The principal part belongs to `mathscr N_K` and vanishes in (L-23602.4).  The
periodic remainder is the already closed Type-I/terminal family.  What remains
in the balanced source is therefore an endpoint valuation modulo
`mathscr N_K`.

Denote this quotient valuation by

\[
 \overline{\mathcal V}^{\rm ref}_{K,\tau}.
\tag{L-23602.5}
\]

## 3. Difference factors

A complete Möbius toggle in one logarithmic coordinate acts by

\[
 \nabla_h=I-e^{-hD},
 \qquad D=\frac d{du}.
\tag{L-23602.6}
\]

In a geometric-series or tangent-cone transform, the corresponding free edge
has denominator

\[
 1-e^{-hD}.
\tag{L-23602.7}
\]

Thus exact signed recombination supplies the numerator factor that cancels the
free-edge denominator.  This is an algebraic cancellation before evaluation;
it is not a small-denominator estimate.

For a complete cube of toggles,

\[
 \prod_i\nabla_{h_i}
\tag{L-23602.8}
\]

annihilates every polynomial of total degree below the number of toggles.  The
high-order window extends the same statement to the declared
polynomial-exponential principal parts.

## 4. Line-cone vanishing

Let `C` be a tangent cone appearing in the Brianchon--Gram decomposition of a
balanced cell.  Suppose `C` contains a line in a coordinate or prefix direction
`v` for which the complete signed source contains the corresponding toggle.
Then the exponential generating expression for the two rays `+v` and `-v`
contains the same denominator with opposite orientations.  The factor
(L-23602.6) makes the quotient valuation zero.

Equivalently,

\[
 \boxed{
 C\text{ contains a source-complete prefix line}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \overline{\mathcal V}^{\rm ref}_{K,\tau}(C)=0.}
\tag{L-23602.9}
\]

This is the standard `simple valuation` property needed by Brion localization,
but here it is source bound: a line is killed only when the actual packet
contains both Möbius sides and the relevant null companion.

A missing sibling row, a cutoff convention that removes one toggle, or an
unreviewed null companion invalidates (L-23602.9) and must be reported rather
than silently completed.

## 5. Positive reflected diagonal

The only nonnull bulk term that is not a line cone is the conjugate Hermitian
diagonal already present on the left side of (L-23602.2).  It is nonnegative.
The proposal moves it to the energy side; it is never bounded by coefficientwise
absolute values.

Thus the balanced valuation splits exactly as

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{reflected packet}
 =\text{nonnegative Hermitian diagonal}
  +\text{pointed endpoint valuation}
  +\text{strict lower-scale rows}
  +\text{Euler-small rows}.}
\tag{L-23602.10}
\]

The pointed endpoint valuation is the object localized in `L-23603`.

## 6. Why positive-dimensional faces are not counted

A tangent cone at a positive-dimensional face contains the linear span of that
face.  In cumulative-prefix coordinates, every such span contains either:

1. a coordinate/prefix direction carrying a complete signed toggle; or
2. a direction tangent to the fixed total-product level, on which the
   reflected autocorrelation is constant and the zero-mass Möbius valuation
   vanishes.

Under the complete source manifest these are exactly the hypotheses of
(L-23602.9).  Hence nonvertex faces disappear **as valuations** before any face
cardinality or free-coordinate estimate.

This is the proposed repair of the endpoint-count gap in `L-9517`: the proof
does not assert that every face has bounded dimension.  It proves that every
positive-dimensional face has zero reflected null-quotient valuation.

## 7. Finite verification interface

For every destination, a proof object must list:

```text
all source toggles,
all null companions,
all prefix-coordinate directions,
all tangent-face lineality bases,
and the numerator factor assigned to each line direction.
```

The consumer must verify that every nonvertex cone has at least one certified
line direction and that the matching numerator factor is present in the exact
signed packet.

Mandatory mutations:

- delete one Möbius sibling;
- reverse one residual sign;
- remove one transition row;
- replace the reflected square by two scalar squares;
- expose an `Omega(K)`-dimensional face.

The first four must fail.  The last must still pass if its lineality is fully
matched, demonstrating that the argument is not hidden endpoint counting.
