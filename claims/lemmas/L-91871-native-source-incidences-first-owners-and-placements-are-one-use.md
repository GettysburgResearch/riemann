atom may be split fractionally among several edges, but no unit of its target
mass may be copied.

incidence laws are

\[
 (\Pi_B)_-=\Sigma_{X,B}^-,
 \qquad
 (\Pi_B)_++R_B=\Sigma_{X,B}^+.
\tag{L-91871.4}
\]

At one anchored terminal leaf let `Pi_ell` couple the complete odd demand to the
selected even submeasure and let `R_ell` be the positive residual. Then

\[
 (\Pi_\ell)_-=O_\ell,
 \qquad
 (\Pi_\ell)_++R_\ell=E_\ell.
\tag{L-91871.5}

anchored finite source before these leaf couplings are applied. Summing
(L-91871.4)--(L-91871.5) gives the complete native input marginals exactly.

Thus “counted once” means that outgoing nonnegative incidence weights sum to the
incoming source mass. It does not mean that an even atom cannot be fractionally
split across Hall edges.
