## 1. All metric-compatible connections

Let `C=C*>0` and `C'=C'^*`.  Retain

\[
 \Gamma_0=\frac12C^{-1}C'.
\]

A matrix `Gamma` satisfies

\[
 C'=\Gamma^*C+C\Gamma
\]

if and only if

\[
 \boxed{
 \Gamma=\Gamma_0+C^{-1}J,
 \qquad
 J^*=-J.
 }
 \tag{L-91903.1}

Indeed, `C(Gamma-Gamma_0)` must be skew Hermitian, and every skew-Hermitian
`J` gives such a connection.

Thus all completed connection freedom is finite-dimensional and linear.

## 2. Affine covariant derivative

Put

\[
 N_0=D'+\Gamma_0D-D\Gamma_0.
\]

For the connection (L-91903.1),

\[
 \boxed{
 \nabla_JD
 =N_0+C^{-1}JD-DC^{-1}J.
 }
 \tag{L-91903.2}

Define

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal R(J)
 =-\Big[
  (\nabla_JD)^*CD+D^*C(\nabla_JD)
 \Big].
 }
 \tag{L-91903.3}

Because (L-91903.2) is affine in `J`, so is the Hermitian matrix
`R(J)`.

## 3. Finite semidefinite feasibility problem

For a fixed safe-real packet and displacement `u`, completed covariant
dissipation is exactly the LMI

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{find }J\text{ with }J^*=-J
 \quad\text{such that}\quad
 \mathcal R(J)\succeq0.
 }
 \tag{L-91903.4}

All coefficients are explicit finite combinations of

```text
rational Cauchy matrices;
safe values of xi and xi'/xi;
packet nodes and the horizontal displacement.
```

No zero data enter.

A directed rational approximation to `J`, together with directed intervals
for the safe Xi values, would give a finite positive certificate for one
packet.

## 4. Continuous packet flow

To propagate the Pick cone over an interval of `u`, one needs a locally
integrable selection

\[
 u\longmapsto J_u
\]

satisfying (L-91903.4).  If the selection is continuous, `L-91902` gives the
bounded-real evolution directly.  Measurable bounded selections suffice after
reading the parallel transport as an absolutely continuous evolution.

The source-ordered programme asks for more than pointwise SDP feasibility:
`J_u` should be assembled functorially from the returned/detail ports of the
completed arithmetic Julia cascade and should respect restriction and
refinement of finite packets.

## 5. Dual obstruction

By finite-dimensional semidefinite duality, failure of (L-91903.4) is witnessed
by a positive matrix `Y>=0` such that

\[
 \operatorname{tr}(Y\mathcal R(0))<0
\]

and

\[
 \operatorname{tr}(Y\mathcal R(J))
 =\operatorname{tr}(Y\mathcal R(0))
\]

for every skew-Hermitian `J` direction.  Equivalently, `Y` annihilates the
linear commutator range generated by

\[
 C^{-1}JD-DC^{-1}J.
\]

Thus the completed connection problem admits finite hostile certificates on
both sides:

```text
primal: one skew gauge with a positive remainder;
dual: one positive packet invisible to every allowed gauge.
```

This gives a concrete discovery and verification interface.

## 6. Firewall

An arbitrary feasible `J` defined from the already-positive target Pick matrix
would be circular.  A conclusion-producing construction must come from the
explicit arithmetic source channels or from a safe analytic formula
independent of target positivity.

Likewise, pointwise feasibility on a finite search table is not the universal
all-packet theorem.
