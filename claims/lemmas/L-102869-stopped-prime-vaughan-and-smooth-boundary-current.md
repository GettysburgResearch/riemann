## 1. Exact finite-Euler Vaughan identity

Let

\[
Z_q(s)=\prod_{p<q}(1-p^{-s})^{-1},
\qquad M_{U,q}(s)=\sum_n\mu_{U,q}(n)n^{-s}.
\]

Since

\[
A_{U,q}=1-Z_qM_{U,q},
\]

the algebraic identity

\[
2M_{U,q}-Z_qM_{U,q}^2+\frac{A_{U,q}^2}{Z_q}
=\frac1{Z_q}
\]

gives, coefficientwise,

\[
\boxed{
\mu^{<q}
=2\mu_{U,q}
-\mu_{U,q}*\mu_{U,q}*\mathbf1^{<q}
+a_{U,q}*a_{U,q}*\mu^{<q}.
}
\tag{L-102869.1}

Moreover,

\[
a_{U,q}(n)=0\qquad(n\le U).
\]
