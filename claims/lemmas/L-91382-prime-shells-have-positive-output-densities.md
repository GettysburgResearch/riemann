## 1. Rough-number potential

Let `P` be a finite squarefree product and let `p` be a prime not dividing
`P`.  Put

\[
 H_P(Z)=
 \sum_{\substack{n\le Z\\(n,P)=1}}
 n^{-1/2}\log(Z/n),
 \qquad H_P(Z)=0\ (Z<1).
\]

Define the multiplicative shell potential

\[
 K_{P,p}(Z)=H_P(Z)-H_P(Z/p).
\tag{L-91382.1}
\]

Separating the terms at `Z/p` gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 K_{P,p}(Z)={}&
 (\log p)
 \sum_{\substack{n\le Z/p\\(n,P)=1}}n^{-1/2}\\
 &+\sum_{\substack{Z/p<n\le Z\\(n,P)=1}}
 n^{-1/2}\log(Z/n).
\end{aligned}}
\tag{L-91382.2}
\]

Hence `K_(P,p)(Z)>=0`.

## 2. Monotonicity

On every interval avoiding activation points,

\[
 \partial_{\log Z}K_{P,p}(Z)
 =\sum_{\substack{Z/p<n\le Z\\(n,P)=1}}n^{-1/2}
 \ge0.
\tag{L-91382.3}
\]

The potential is continuous at every activation point because an entering
logarithmic ramp vanishes there.  Thus `K_(P,p)` is globally nondecreasing.

## 3. Ordinary and detail shell capacities

For the canonical finite-Euler packet, the ordinary shell response is

\[
\boxed{
 C^{\rm sh}_{P,p;X}(q)
 =q^{-1/2}K_{P,p}(X/q)\ge0.
}
\tag{L-91382.4}
\]

Its radix-four response is

\[
\boxed{
 \Theta^{\rm sh}_{P,p;X}(q)
 =q^{-1/2}
 [K_{P,p}(X/q)-K_{P,p}(X/(4q))]
 \ge0,
}
\tag{L-91382.5}
\]

by the monotonicity in Section 2.  This is positivity in the actual signed
radix-four coordinate.

## 4. Literal entropy shell

Let the positive finite-Euler entropy density of `L-91363` be

\[
 \lambda_P(n)\ge0,
 \qquad
 \mathcal E_P(X)=
 \sum_{n\le X}\lambda_P(n)n^{-1/2}\log(X/n).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal E_P(X)-\mathcal E_P(X/p)={}&
 (\log p)
 \sum_{n\le X/p}\lambda_P(n)n^{-1/2}\\
 &+\sum_{X/p<n\le X}
 \lambda_P(n)n^{-1/2}\log(X/n)
 \ge0.
\end{aligned}}
\tag{L-91382.6}
\]

Thus the shell benchmark is generated by a positive arithmetic density.

prove that the canonical shell row is coefficientwise nonnegative, nor that an
