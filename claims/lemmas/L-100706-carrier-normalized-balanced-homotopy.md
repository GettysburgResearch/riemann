# L-100706 — Carrier-normalized balanced homotopy isolates a zero-carrier transition integral

Claim ID: `L-100706`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT FINITE IDENTITY + ALL-SCALE POSITIVE SQUARED ENDPOINT**  
Created: 2026-08-21  
Depends on: corrected `L-100703`, `L-100704`  
RH status: **not assumed**

Fix a finite prime-label multiset `Lambda`, retaining the second copy of `67` as a separate label.  For a label `ell` over `p=p_ell`, put

\[
X_\ell=p^{-1/2}U_p,
\qquad a_\ell=p^{-1},
\]

\[
H_{\ell,t}=I-tX_\ell-(1-t)X_\ell^2,
\qquad
h_\ell(t)=1-ta_\ell-(1-t)a_\ell^2.
\tag{L-100706.1}
\]

The scalar `h_ell(t)` is the exact value of the local operator on the critical half-order carrier.  Define

\[
N_{\ell,t}={H_{\ell,t}\over h_\ell(t)},
\qquad
C_\Lambda(t)=\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}h_\ell(t),
\qquad
N_{\Lambda,t}=\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}N_{\ell,t}.
\]

Then

\[
H_{\Lambda,t}=C_\Lambda(t)N_{\Lambda,t}.
\tag{L-100706.2}
\]

## 1. Exact carrier-zero derivative

A direct calculation gives

\[
\boxed{
{d\over dt}N_{\ell,t}
=
{(1-a_\ell)(X_\ell-a_\ell I)(X_\ell-I)
 \over h_\ell(t)^2}.
}
\tag{L-100706.3}
\]

Indeed, if `x` denotes `X_ell`,

\[
(a-a^2)(1-tx-(1-t)x^2)
-(1-ta-(1-t)a^2)(x-x^2)
=(1-a)(x-a)(x-1).
\]

Thus the derivative insertion vanishes exactly at the critical character `x=a`.  It also vanishes at `x=1`; no critical carrier is present in (L-100706.3).

Product differentiation yields

\[
\boxed{
{d\over dt}N_{\Lambda,t}
=
\sum_{\ell\in\Lambda}
{(1-a_\ell)(X_\ell-a_\ell I)(X_\ell-I)
 \over h_\ell(t)^2}
\prod_{q\in\Lambda\setminus\{\ell\}}N_{q,t}.
}
\tag{L-100706.4}
\]

## 2. Exact endpoint reconstruction

At `t=1`, `H_(ell,1)=E_ell=I-X_ell`; at `t=0`, `H_(ell,0)=Q_ell=I-X_ell^2`.  Integrating (L-100706.4) gives

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}E_\ell
=
{C_\Lambda(1)\over C_\Lambda(0)}
\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}Q_\ell
+
C_\Lambda(1)
\int_0^1 {d\over dt}N_{\Lambda,t}\,dt.
}
\tag{L-100706.5}
\]

This is a carrier-normalized version of the balanced homotopy.  The power-sized critical carrier is entirely in the first, positive squared endpoint; the transition integral has a literal local zero at the critical character before the other labels are summed.

## 3. Positive squared endpoint

Apply (L-100706.5) to any positive final critical Bernstein carrier `K_m`, `m>=3`.  In the all-prime limit, the squared endpoint has one label of critical owner cost `1/p^2` for every prime and a second such label at `67`.  Since

\[
\sum_p{1\over p^2}+{1\over67^2}<1,
\]

adjacent-level pairing gives

\[
\boxed{
\left(\prod_{\ell\in\mathcal L}Q_\ell\right)K_m(X)>0
\qquad(X>0).
}
\tag{L-100706.6}
\]

Hence every negative excursion of the native critical state must be generated by the carrier-zero term in (L-100706.5).

## 4. Correct remaining theorem

The finite critical normalization satisfies

\[
C_\Lambda(1)=\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}(1-p_\ell^{-1}),
\qquad
C_\Lambda(0)=\prod_{\ell\in\Lambda}(1-p_\ell^{-2}).
\]

For labels through scale `Y`, `C_Lambda(1)` is of order `1/log Y` while `C_Lambda(0)` stays bounded away from zero.  Therefore the remaining estimate is a carrier-zero, logarithmically normalized transition packing—not a comparison of two power-sized regional terms.

The theorem does not bound that transition integral.  `R-100705` proves it is not pointwise positive after native completion.
