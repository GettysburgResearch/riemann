# L-9311 — Zero-anchor one-moment extension of direct-xi response cones

Claim ID: L-9311  
Title: Adding the critical-line node `u=0` raises the response degree by one while introducing exactly one new moment  
Status: PROPOSED  
Authoring agent: `gpt56-01-o`  
Created: 2026-07-26  
Dependencies: L-7501; L-9303 or L-9307; L-9308; L-9310  
Scope: exact finite direct-completed-xi tables with a certified nonzero critical-line anchor  
Related counterexample candidates: none

## Setup

Fix a real ordinate `T`. Let

\[
 H_T(u)=\left|\xi\!\left(\frac12+\sqrt u+iT\right)\right|^2,
 \qquad u\ge 0,
\]

and let `F_T(u)` denote the logarithmic modulus after any already-validated
critical-line or total-count deflation. The deflation terms are finite sums of
`log(u+B)` with `B>0`, so they remain finite at `u=0`.

Assume a proof-grade direct-xi rectangle proves

\[
 H_T(0)>0.
\]

Hence `F_T(0)` is defined.

Let

\[
 0<u_1<\cdots<u_n
\]

be old nodes and put

\[
 D(y)=\prod_{i=1}^n(y+u_i).
\]

For a zero-sum coefficient vector `beta`, define its response polynomial

\[
 P_\beta(y)
 =-\sum_{i=1}^n\beta_i\frac{D(y)}{y+u_i}.
\]

For each `0 <= k <= n-2`, let `beta^(k)` be the unique old-node portfolio with

\[
 P_{\beta^{(k)}}(y)=y^k,
\]

and write its directed scalar value as

\[
 a_k=\sum_{i=1}^n\beta_i^{(k)}F_T(u_i).
\]

Now adjoin the zero anchor

\[
 u_0=0,
 \qquad
 \widetilde D(y)=yD(y),
\]

and define the new moments `b_0,...,b_{n-1}` by the corresponding portfolios
whose new response polynomials are `1,y,...,y^(n-1)`.

## One-new-moment identity

For every `0 <= k <= n-2`, zero-extend the old vector `beta^(k)` by assigning
coefficient zero to the new node. Its scalar contraction does not change. Its
new response polynomial is

\[
 -\sum_{i=1}^n\beta_i^{(k)}
   \frac{yD(y)}{y+u_i}
 =yP_{\beta^{(k)}}(y)
 =y^{k+1}.
\]

By uniqueness of the new response portfolio,

\[
 \boxed{b_{k+1}=a_k\qquad(0\le k\le n-2).}
\]

Thus adjoining `u=0` introduces exactly one new scalar, `b_0`. Every higher
new moment is already present in the old certificate.

## Exact formula for the new scalar

On the `n+1` nodes `0,u_1,...,u_n`, the response-`1` coefficients are

\[
 \widetilde\beta_i^{(0)}
 =-
 \frac{1}{\displaystyle\prod_{j\ne i}(u_j-u_i)},
\]

where `u_0=0`. Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 b_0
 =\sum_{i=0}^{n}\widetilde\beta_i^{(0)}F_T(u_i).
 }
\]

This formula is invariant under adding a common constant to all `F_T(u_i)`;
therefore one exact common power-of-two scaling of all completed-xi values
cancels automatically.

The coefficients are large for clustered nodes. Production must use directed
logarithm intervals and directed final accumulation. An ordinary floating
barycentric contraction is discovery evidence only.

## Degree-15 specialization

Take `n=16`, so the old table supplies `a_0,...,a_14` and the zero-anchored
table supplies `b_0,...,b_15` with

\[
 b_{k+1}=a_k.
\]

Define

\[
 A=(a_{i+j+1})_{0\le i,j\le 6},
 \qquad
 v=(a_0,a_1,\ldots,a_6)^{\mathsf T},
\]

and

\[
 H_{\rm old}=(a_{i+j})_{0\le i,j\le 7}.
\]

The two half-line moment matrices for every response polynomial of degree at
most 15 are

\[
 H_0(b_0)
 =
 \begin{pmatrix}
 b_0 & v^{\mathsf T}\\
 v   & A
 \end{pmatrix},
\]

and

\[
 H_1=(b_{i+j+1})_{0\le i,j\le7}=H_{\rm old}.
\]

Assume `A` and `H_old` are positive definite. Then the Schur-complement
criterion gives

\[
 \boxed{
 H_0(b_0)\succeq0
 \iff
 b_0\ge \theta,
 \qquad
 \theta=v^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}v.
 }
\]

Consequently the complete degree-15 cone is decided by one directed scalar:

* if a proof-grade interval has `sup b_0 < inf theta`, RH is false;
* if directed matrix arithmetic proves both `H_0` and `H_1` positive
  semidefinite, the entire degree-15 scalar polynomial response cone is closed
  for this exact table.

## Explicit witness below the threshold

Put

\[
 c=A^{-1}v,
 \qquad
 q(y)=1-\sum_{j=0}^{6}c_jy^{j+1}.
\]

The coefficient vector of `q` is `(1,-c^T)^T`, and therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 L(q^2)
 =b_0-v^{\mathsf T}A^{-1}v
 =b_0-\theta.
 }
\]

Thus a strict negative Schur complement directly yields the nonnegative
response polynomial

\[
 P(y)=q(y)^2
\]

as a finite counterexample witness. No eigensolver, fitted off-line zero, or
SDP solution is part of the final certificate.

## Why this decides the full degree-15 cone

L-9310 gives the exact univariate half-line representation

\[
 P(y)=\sum_r a_r(y)^2+y\sum_s b_s(y)^2
\]

for every real polynomial `P>=0` on `[0,infinity)`. For degree at most 15, all
polynomials in the two sums have degree at most seven. Their values are exactly
the quadratic forms generated by `H_0` and `H_1`.

Therefore

\[
 H_0\succeq0,
 \qquad
 H_1\succeq0
\]

is necessary and sufficient for nonnegativity of every degree-at-most-15
response in the finite table.

## Directed interval certificate

For interval moments, the production checker does not infer positivity from a
midpoint Schur complement. It must either:

1. prove exact rational positive definiteness of the midpoint matrices after
   subtracting `delta I`, and prove the complete interval-box operator radius
   is below `delta`; or
2. emit an exact rational coefficient vector whose interval quadratic-form
   upper endpoint is strictly negative.

This makes the certificate fail closed if the zero-anchor contraction or a
moment interval is too wide.

## Existential relevance

Every off-critical zero still creates a positive-axis singularity in the
zero-anchored direct-xi logarithm. Adjoining `u=0` changes only the rational
response denominator and cannot remove that singularity. The full degree-15
cone is therefore a genuine enlargement of the degree-14 attack, not a
reparameterization of the same finite family.

## Gap audit

* `H_T(0)>0` must be certified; a midpoint nonzero value is insufficient.
* The zero primitive and old table must have the same exact ordinate,
  completed-xi normalization, common scale, and count-deflation provenance.
* The identity `b_(k+1)=a_k` requires coefficient zero at the new node; it does
  not hold for an arbitrary additional positive node.
* A positive result closes only this exact node table, count profile, ordinate,
  and degree bound.
* Any negative result still requires independent completed-xi reproduction and
  review of the inherited canonical-product/count-deflation implications.
