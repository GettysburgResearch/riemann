# L-29303 — Transition-band cone reduction

Status: PROPOSED EXACT REDUCTION

The first-entrance source is not required to be coordinatewise positive. Let Sigma_(X,n) be the source on [n,2n) obtained by stopping the size-biased binary-ternary fragmentation spine at first entrance.

Define C_n as the cone generated by:

1. balanced Pascal cycles with zero node divergence;
2. squarefree collector moves preserving proper-power coordinates;
3. parity/factor-five transition generators.

The proposed reduction is:

Sigma_(X,n) is feasible for balanced carry transport iff

Sigma_(X,n) belongs to C_n.

The proof boundary is explicit:

- first-entrance localization is exact;
- cycle-space quotient is exact linear algebra;
- collector and parity generator completeness are open arithmetic statements.

This avoids the false requirement that every intermediate Abel-smoothed or local kernel must be positive.

No RH claim is made.
