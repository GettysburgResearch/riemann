source measure `nu` and carries, linearly in `nu`:

```text

```

The resident kernel formulas have nonnegative target, score and component-row

\[

\[
 \boxed{P(\nu)=P(\theta\nu)+P((1-\theta)\nu)}

## 3. Additivity

For positive measures `nu_1,nu_2`,

\[

\tag{L-91412.2
}

and only then passed through the finite quantizer, collar, omission and common
