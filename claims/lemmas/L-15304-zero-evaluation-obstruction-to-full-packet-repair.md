## Hardy evaluation setup

Write the logarithmic representative of a multiplicative vector as `f(t)`, and
for `0<tau<1/2` use

\[
 \|f\|_\tau^2
 =\int_{\mathbb R}|f(t)|^2\,2\cosh(2\tau t)\,dt. \tag{1}
\]

Its Fourier--Mellin transform is

\[
 \widehat f(z)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(t)e^{-izt}\,dt.
\]

For `|Im z|<tau`, Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
 |\widehat f(z)|\le C_{z,\tau}\|f\|_\tau,
\quad
 C_{z,\tau}^2=
 \frac{\pi}{4\tau\cos(\pi\operatorname{Im}z/(2\tau))}. \tag{2}
\]

In particular, at a critical-line ordinate `gamma`,

\[
 C_{\gamma,\tau}=\sqrt{\frac\pi{4\tau}},       \tag{3}
\]

independently of `gamma`.

## Radical evaluation identity

Let `rho` be any actual nontrivial zeta zero and let `z_rho` be its centered
coordinate in the chosen Mellin convention. If

\[
 r=\mathcal E(f)
\]

is an exact global radical vector from the codimension-two source space, then
its transform contains the zeta factor and therefore

\[
 \widehat r(z_\rho)=0.                         \tag{4}
\]

Split

\[
 r=k+t,                                       \tag{5}
\]

where `k` is the localized truncation and `t` the discarded tail. Then

\[
 \boxed{\widehat k(z_\rho)=-\widehat t(z_\rho)} \tag{6}
\]

and hence

\[
 |\widehat k(z_\rho)|
 \le C_{z_\rho,\tau}\|t\|_\tau.              \tag{7}
\]

For a finite set `Z={z_1,...,z_m}` of certified zeros, define

\[
 \mathcal V_Z f=(\widehat f(z_1),...,\widehat f(z_m))
\]

and

\[
 C_Z=\left(\sum_{j=1}^mC_{z_j,\tau}^2\right)^{1/2}. \tag{8}
\]

Then every radical truncation satisfies

\[
 \boxed{\|\mathcal V_Zk\|_2\le C_Z\|t\|_\tau.} \tag{9}
\]

For `m` certified critical-line zeros,

\[
 C_Z=\sqrt{\frac{m\pi}{4\tau}}.               \tag{10}
\]

No RH assumption is used: each member of `Z` is required to be an independently
certified actual zeta zero.

## Finite-subspace obstruction

Let `U` be a finite-dimensional subspace of localized vectors, equipped with the
same Hardy norm, and define its minimum zero-evaluation singular value

\[
 \sigma_Z(U)=
 \inf_{\substack{u\in U\\\|u\|_\tau=1}}
 \|\mathcal V_Zu\|_2.                        \tag{11}
\]

Suppose `u in U` is normalized and `k` is a localized radical truncation with

\[
 \|u-k\|_\tau\le d,
 \qquad
 \|t\|_\tau\le\varepsilon.                  \tag{12}
\]

Using (2), (9), and the triangle inequality,

\[
 \sigma_Z(U)
 \le\|\mathcal V_Zu\|_2
 \le C_Z(d+\varepsilon).
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 d\ge\frac{\sigma_Z(U)}{C_Z}-\varepsilon.}   \tag{13}
\]

If `sigma_Z(U)>0` and the radical tails tend to zero, the whole unit sphere of
`U` cannot be approximated by those truncations with vanishing error.

## Singular-value decomposition of the correct target

Choose a basis of `U` and form the exact or directed evaluation matrix

\[
 M_Z=(\widehat u_j(z_i))_{i,j}.               \tag{14}
\]

For a threshold `delta>0`, split `U` into:

1. the small-singular subspace generated by right singular directions of
   `M_Z` below `delta`;
2. the evaluation-visible complement, whose minimum singular value is at least
   `delta`.

Only the first subspace can plausibly be approximated by small-tail radical
truncations. The second must be handled by a direct lower-floor certificate; it
cannot be silently absorbed into a growing radical packet.

A proof-facing implementation need not trust a floating SVD. It may freeze a
rational basis for the proposed near-kernel, verify its evaluation intervals,
and prove a rational Gram lower bound on the declared complement.

## Consequence for the current positive path

The sufficient approximation target in `O-14303` and Issue #156 should not be
read as a requirement to approximate the **entire** generalized-prolate packet.
That can be false even under RH, because radical limits retain every certified
zeta zero while a generic packet direction does not.

The corrected architecture is:

```text
complete finite low-symbol packet
        -> certified-zero evaluation SVD
        -> radical-like near-kernel block
           + evaluation-visible residual block
        -> exact radical repair only on the near-kernel
        -> direct finite lower bound on the visible block
        -> block Temple--Schur composition.
```

This can reduce the truly unresolved radical-like dimension far below the crude
bad-symbol rank cap.

## Proof boundary

- The theorem is finite Hilbert-space algebra plus a standard Hardy evaluation
  bound.
- The zero set must consist of proof-grade actual zeta zeros with matching
  normalization.
- Evaluation at off-critical zeros uses the full constant in (2) and requires
  `|Im z|<tau`.
- A small singular value is a scheduling statistic until enclosed with directed
  arithmetic.
- The theorem is an obstruction/refinement, not a lower bound for the
  evaluation-visible block and not a proof of RH.
