## 2. Hall residual and edge-owned Hall bonus

Define the unused positive mass at a positive source occurrence by

\[
 u_x(e)=T_x(e)-\sum_o t_x(o,e)\ge0.
\tag{L-91820.2}
\]

For every component profile `rho`, finite algebra gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_eT_x(e)\rho(e)-\sum_oT_x(o)\rho(o)
 =
 \sum_eu_x(e)\rho(e)
 +
 \sum_{o,e}t_x(o,e)[\rho(e)-\rho(o)].
}
\tag{L-91820.3}

The first term is the **Hall residual**.  It is owned by the original positive occurrence `e`.

The second term is the **Hall bonus**.  It is owned by the actual transport edge `(o,e)`, not inserted as a coordinatewise complement.  Frozen monotonicity gives

\[
 \rho_{x,j}(e)-\rho_{x,j}(o)\ge0
 \qquad(e\le o),
\]

so every row bonus is nonnegative.  The score ratio is ordered in the favorable direction as well.  In the target coordinate, the edge bonus is identically zero and (L-91820.3) is target-exact.

Thus the same Hall coefficients simultaneously produce a positive target-exact residual, a score-superordinate residual, and a nonnegative bonus in every declared row.
