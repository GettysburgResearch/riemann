## 1. Setup

Let `H` be a complex Hilbert space with inner product antilinear in the first
variable. Let `q` be a Hermitian sesquilinear form on a vector space containing
`e` and a finite-dimensional subspace `V`.

Assume that `e` is in the radical of the full form:

```text
q(e,x)=0                     for every admissible x.       (L-15102.1)
```

Let

```text
p = P_V e,
r = e-p,
```

where `P_V` is the Hilbert-space orthogonal projection. Let `A_V` be the unique
self-adjoint operator on `V` representing the restricted form:

```text
<A_V x,y> = q(x,y),          x,y in V.                    (L-15102.2)
```

Define `b_V in V` by

```text
<b_V,y> = q(r,y),            y in V.                      (L-15102.3)
```

Then

```text
A_V p = -b_V.                                             (L-15102.4)
```

Thus the failure of the localized target `p` to be a zero eigenvector is
exactly the pairing of the omitted part `r` against the finite space.

## 2. Scalar-free weighted residual

Let `W` be any positive-definite self-adjoint operator on `V`, and let `p!=0`.
For `y in V`, define

```text
R_W(y;p)^2
 = <W^(-1)y,y>
   - |<W^(-1)y,p>|^2/<W^(-1)p,p>.                        (L-15102.5)
```

Equivalently,

```text
R_W(y;p)
 = inf_(c in C) ||W^(-1/2)(y-cp)||                       (L-15102.6)

 = sup_(v in V, <v,p>=0)
       |<y,v>| / ||W^(1/2)v||.                           (L-15102.7)
```

Let

```text
mu = <A_V p,p>/<p,p>,
z  = (A_V-mu I)p.
```

Then adding a scalar multiple of `p` does not change (L-15102.5), and
(L-15102.4) gives the exact identities

```text
R_W(z;p) = R_W(b_V;p)                                    (L-15102.8)

           = sup_(v in V, <v,p>=0)
               |q(r,v)|/||v||_W.                         (L-15102.9)
```

where `||v||_W=||W^(1/2)v||`.

## 3. Proof

For every `y in V`, radicality and `e=p+r` give

```text
0=q(e,y)=q(p,y)+q(r,y)=<A_V p,y>+<b_V,y>.
```

Since this holds for every `y`, (L-15102.4) follows.

The right side of (L-15102.5) is the squared distance of `W^(-1/2)y` from the
one-dimensional subspace generated by `W^(-1/2)p`; minimizing the quadratic in
`c` gives (L-15102.6). Hilbert-space duality gives (L-15102.7): write a unit
vector orthogonal to `W^(-1/2)p` as `W^(1/2)v/||v||_W`, for which the
orthogonality condition is exactly `<v,p>=0`.

Now

```text
z = -b_V-mu p.
```

The infimum in (L-15102.6) is invariant under adding any scalar multiple of
`p`, so `R_W(z;p)=R_W(b_V;p)`. Substituting (L-15102.3) into the dual formula
(L-15102.7) proves (L-15102.9). QED.
