## 1. Balanced contraction identity

Let `H` be a Hilbert space, let

\[
0\preceq R\preceq I
\]

be a positive contraction, and let `V` be unitary. Put

\[
\boxed{
K=[V,R^{1/2}]R^{1/2}.
}
\tag{L-105623.1}
\]

Then

\[
VR=R^{1/2}VR^{1/2}+K.
\tag{L-105623.2}
\]

Taking Hermitian parts gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
I-\Re(VR)
={}&(I-R)
+R^{1/2}(I-\Re V)R^{1/2}
-\Re K.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{L-105623.3}
\]

The first two terms on the right are positive semidefinite. Hence every
negative direction created by the physical unitary is carried by the single
commutator term `Re K`.

In particular, the weighted form inequality

\[
\boxed{
\Re K\preceq I-R
}
\tag{L-105623.4}
\]

is sufficient for

\[
\Re(VR)\preceq I.
\tag{L-105623.5}
\]

The stronger positive phase term
`R^(1/2)(I-Re V)R^(1/2)` may be retained rather than discarded.

\[
(Rf)(\xi)=r(\xi)f(\xi),
\qquad 0\le r\le1,
\]

and let `V` be multiplication by a unimodular function `U(a)` in the spatial
coordinate. In Fourier coordinates `V` is convolution by `widehat U`. Then

\[
\boxed{
[V,R^{1/2}](\xi,\eta)
=
\widehat U(\xi-\eta)
\bigl(\sqrt{r(\eta)}-\sqrt{r(\xi)}\bigr).
}
\tag{L-105623.7}
\]

Whenever the commutator is Hilbert--Schmidt,

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\|[V,R^{1/2}]\|_{\mathcal S_2}^2

|\widehat U(\xi-\eta)|^2
|\sqrt{r(\eta)}-\sqrt{r(\xi)}|^2

={}&
\int_{\mathbb R}|\widehat U(\kappa)|^2
\left[
\int_{\mathbb R}
|\sqrt{r(\eta+\kappa)}-\sqrt{r(\eta)}|^2d\eta

\end{aligned}

\tag{L-105623.8}
\]
