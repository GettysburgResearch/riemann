## 1. Centered terminal distribution

Define the pole-subtracted terminal distribution

\[
 d\mathcal T(t)
 =\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\delta_{\log n}(t)
  -e^{t/2}\,dt.
 \tag{1}
\]

For a compactly supported smooth window `F`, put

\[
 D_F(a)=\langle\mathcal T,F(2a-\cdot)\rangle.
 \tag{2}
\]

For the profile packet of `L-15610`, every scalar contraction of the centered
matrix `E_a` has the form

\[
 \boxed{c^*E_ac=2D_{C_{\phi_c}}(a).}
 \tag{3}
\]

Thus the matrix norm bound

\[
 \|G_V^{-1/2}E_aG_V^{-1/2}\|\le\theta_a
 \tag{4}
\]

is exactly a simultaneous bound for the finite family of terminal-prime Hankel
windows generated by the profile packet.

## 2. Laplace transform

For `Re z>1/2`,

\[
 \int_0^\infty e^{-zt}\,d\mathcal T(t)
 =-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}\left(z+\frac12\right)
  -\frac1{z-1/2}.
 \tag{5}
\]

The zeta pole is canceled. The remaining nontrivial poles occur at

\[
 z=\rho-\frac12.
 \tag{6}
\]

Consequently, for the translated statistic, a zero

\[
 \rho=\frac12+\eta+i\gamma,
 \qquad \eta>0,
 \tag{7}
\]

contributes a term of the form

\[
 e^{2\eta a}
 e^{2i\gamma a}\widehat F(\eta+i\gamma),
 \tag{8}
\]

plus its conjugate. Here `widehat F` denotes the corresponding Laplace transform
of the compact window.

## 3. Zero-free-window implication

Suppose one declared scalar window `F_*` has Laplace transform nonzero in

\[
 0<\operatorname{Re}z<\frac12.
 \tag{9}
\]

The dyadic infinite-convolution window of `L-15405` is an explicit example after
the declared shift and convolution-square operations.

If

\[
 \boxed{\sup_{a\ge a_0}|D_{F_*}(a)|<\infty,}
 \tag{10}
\]

then RH follows.

### Proof

If a zero with `eta>0` existed, its residue in (5) would be nonzero after
multiplication by the zero-free window transform. Shifting the inverse Laplace
contour across that pole gives the exponentially growing oscillatory term (8).
No finite sum of poles with strictly smaller real part can cancel it identically;
for poles on the maximal real part, the resulting nonzero exponential
polynomial has unbounded modulus on the positive ray. Hence (10) is impossible.
The functional equation supplies the symmetric half of the critical strip, so
all nontrivial zeros lie on the line. QED.

This is the one-window criterion proved independently in PR #165.

## 4. Matrix visible gate implies the one-window gate

Assume the endpoint profile packet contains a vector `c_*` whose convolution
window is `F_*`, with

\[
 c_*^*G_Vc_*>0.
\]

Then (4) gives

\[
 |D_{F_*}(a)|
 \le\frac12\theta_a\,c_*^*G_Vc_*.
 \tag{11}
\]

Therefore any cofinal visible-block theorem proving a uniformly bounded
`theta_a` for this packet already proves RH. In particular, the stronger target

\[
 \theta_a\longrightarrow0
 \tag{12}
\]

is not a routine consequence of plunge-region dimension estimates; it contains
the RH-sensitive arithmetic step.

## 5. Exact visible Schur formulation

Let the finite local/background part of `L-15610` have directed floor
`sigma_a^2 G_V`, let the ambient complement satisfy `C>=h_aM`, and let the
visible--ambient cross obey

\[
 Z^*M^{-1}Z\preceq\zeta_a^2G_V.
\]

Then the complete visible block is positive whenever

\[
 \boxed{
 \theta_a+\frac{\zeta_a^2}{h_a}<\sigma_a^2.}
 \tag{13}
\]

Equivalently, in the one-radius form of `L-15307`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \theta_a+\omega_a+\frac{\omega_a^2}{h_a}<\sigma_a^2.}
 \tag{14}
\]

Equations (13)--(14), together with the radical-row estimates in `L-15306`, are
an exact plunge-sized visible-block completion. The unresolved quantity is the
directed terminal-prime norm `theta_a`.

## 6. What localization theory does and does not supply

Sharp localization results control:

- the number of concentration eigenvalues in the plunge region;
- the rank needed to represent a weighted deficit operator to a selected
  operator-norm tolerance;
- the conditioning of the finite packet after a source-codimension repair.

They do not bound (3), because its signed phase is the pole-subtracted prime
error. A dimension bound without capture of the terminal-prime Hankel matrix is
insufficient; the exact counterexample in repository `R-15601` demonstrates
this at finite rank.

## 7. Safe proof targets

The following are valid ways to establish (13):

1. **Directed terminal matrix:** enclose every entry of `E_a` and certify (12)
   by rational LDL.
2. **Universal window plus residual packet:** use the zero-free scalar window to
   control the maximal exponential mode and certify the orthogonal residual by
   an operator norm.
3. **Certified-zero notches:** suppress a finite critical-line background with
   proof-grade zero balls, then bound the remaining zero sum and every filter
   zero in the open strip.
4. **Complete local-Weyl block:** prove a source-specific matrix asymptotic that
   retains both endpoint phases and every off-line displacement before taking
   absolute values.

A phase-blind PNT estimate, a fixed finite real-zero frame, or a plunge count
alone cannot establish the gate.

## 8. Gap audit

- The implication (10) `=> RH` is a criterion, not a proof of (10).
- Under RH the selected window statistic has a bounded almost-periodic zero
  expansion subject to the standard convergence gates, but RH is not assumed in
  the positive route.
- A scalar statistic can cross zero along subsequences even if an off-line zero
  exists. The operator/phase-complete packet must control the full norm, not one
  favorable phase sample.
- No cofinal directed bound for `theta_a` is proved here.
- RH is not claimed.
