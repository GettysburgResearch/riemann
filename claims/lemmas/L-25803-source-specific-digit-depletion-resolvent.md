## 1. Top source and nilpotent residual

Retain

\[
r_V=\varepsilon-\mathbf1*\mu_V
\]

and

\[
T_{K,V}=\Lambda*r_V^{*(K-1)}.
\tag{L-25803.1}
\]

Every nonzero coefficient of `r_V^K` is supported above `(V+1)^K`. Therefore,
coefficientwise for `n<=X<=V^K`,

\[
\boxed{
r_V*T_{K,V}
=\Lambda*r_V^{*K}=0.}
\tag{L-25803.2}
\]

This is the source-specific nilpotence used below.

## 2. Reciprocal-free potential and fixed-scale dipole

Fix an integer `Q>=2` and put

\[
a_Q=\mathbf1*(\varepsilon-\delta_Q).
\]

Define the reciprocal-free potential

\[
\boxed{
Z_{K,V,Q}=a_Q*T_{K,V}}
\tag{L-25803.3}
\]

and the fixed-scale dipole

\[
\boxed{
D_{K,V,Q}
=(\varepsilon-\delta_Q)*T_{K,V}.}
\tag{L-25803.4}
\]

Using `mu_V*1=epsilon-r_V`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mu_V*Z_{K,V,Q}
&=\mu_V*\mathbf1*(\varepsilon-\delta_Q)*T_{K,V}\\
&=(\varepsilon-r_V)*(\varepsilon-\delta_Q)*T_{K,V}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The term containing `r_V*T_(K,V)` vanishes by (L-25803.2). Hence

\[
\boxed{
D_{K,V,Q}
=\mu_V*Z_{K,V,Q}
\qquad(n\le X).}
\tag{L-25803.5}
\]

This is the exact source factorization used by `PADT`.

## 3. Exact geometric recovery

Convolving (L-25803.5) with

\[
H_Q=(\varepsilon-\delta_Q)^{-1}
=\sum_{j\ge0}\delta_{Q^j}
\]

gives the coefficientwise identity

\[
\boxed{
T_{K,V}
=H_Q*\mu_V*Z_{K,V,Q}
\qquad(n\le X).}
\tag{L-25803.6}

This identity is unavailable for a generic source. It uses exactly one fact:
`r_V*T_(K,V)=0` on the finite coefficient range.

Equation (L-25803.6) is retained as an algebraic audit. `R-25802` proves that a
current-block estimate for `Z` may not be turned into a norm estimate for `T` by
charging `mu_V` absolutely across the whole shifted recovery wedge.

The proof-facing use is instead the one-step relation

\[
\boxed{T=\delta_Q*T+D.}
\tag{L-25803.7}

## 4. Reciprocal-free analytic form

The elementary identity

\[
\mathbf1*\Lambda=\ell
\tag{L-25803.8}
\]

implies

\[
\boxed{
Z_{K,V,Q}
=(\varepsilon-\delta_Q)*\ell*r_V^{*(K-1)}.}
\tag{L-25803.9}

In Dirichlet-series notation, put

\[
R_V(s)=1-\zeta(s)M_V(s).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
Z_{K,V,Q}(s)
=-(1-Q^{-s})\zeta'(s)R_V(s)^{K-1}.}
\tag{L-25803.10}

There is no reciprocal zeta factor in (L-25803.10). Every nontrivial-zero pole
of the top source has been canceled before the signed `mu_V` transport is
estimated.

The fixed-scale dipole still has the complete RH-bearing source:

\[
D(s)
=-(1-Q^{-s}){\zeta'(s)\over\zeta(s)}R_V(s)^{K-1}.
\tag{L-25803.11}

Thus the arithmetic theorem is precisely the source-specific estimate of
`mu_V*Z`, not generic control of `Z` or generic inversion of its positive
kernel.

## 5. Physical positive-kernel realization

Let `Q_T` be the square-root normalized logarithmic field generated by
`T_(K,V)` and a fixed compact safe window. Let `C_Q` be the positive kernel of
`L-25802`. Then

\[
\left(\partial+\frac12\right)(\mathcal C_Q*Q_T)
\]

is exactly the physical field generated by `Z_(K,V,Q)`.

Thus the potential has two simultaneous descriptions:

1. the reciprocal-free signed source (L-25803.9);
2. the derivative of a convolution with a nonnegative causal kernel.

The dipole field satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T(x)
=Q^{-1/2}\mathcal T(x-\log Q)
+\mathcal D(x).}
\tag{L-25803.12}

The proposal uses the positive kernel to organize the physical two-frequency
Gram and the exact `mu_V` factorization to build the signed transport.

## 6. Scale-adapted radix

Fix one constant

\[
0<\delta_0<\frac13
\]

and choose the preferred dyadic radix

\[
L(J)=\left\lfloor{\delta_0J\over\log2}\right\rfloor,
\qquad
Q=2^{L(J)}.
\tag{L-25803.13}
\]

Then

\[
\log Q=\delta_0J+O(1)
\tag{L-25803.14}
\]

and the shifted member of (L-25803.12) lies at logarithmic scale

\[
(1-\delta_0)J+O(1).
\]

The normalized geometric inverse satisfies

\[
{1\over1-Q^{-1/2}}=1+O(e^{-\delta_0J/2}),
\]

but no absolute recovery estimate is inferred from this fact.

The dyadic choice also supplies the exact bit-layer and parity channels of
`L-25806`.
