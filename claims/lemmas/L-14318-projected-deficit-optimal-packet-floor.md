## Statement

Let `I` be a bounded interval of length `L`, let

\[
 \widehat w(\xi)=\int_I w(x)e^{i\xi x}\,dx,
 \qquad
 \|w\|_2^2=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}|\widehat w(\xi)|^2\,d\xi,
 \tag{L-14318.1}
\]

and let `s:R->R` be a measurable real symbol.  Fix a real level `G` and assume

\[
 d_G(\xi):=(G-s(\xi))_+\in L^1(\mathbb R).
 \tag{L-14318.2}
\]

On `H=L2(I)`, define the positive trace-class weighted-deficit operator

\[
 D_G=P_I\mathcal F^{-1}M_{d_G}\mathcal F P_I,
 \tag{L-14318.3}
\]

with the Fourier normalization in (L-14318.1).  Thus

\[
 \langle D_Gw,w\rangle
 =\frac1{2\pi}\int d_G(\xi)|\widehat w(\xi)|^2\,d\xi.
 \tag{L-14318.4}
\]

Let `K` be any finite-dimensional exact subspace of `H`, let

\[
 P=P_{K^\perp},
 \qquad
 D_{G,K}=PD_GP|_{K^\perp},
 \tag{L-14318.5}
\]

and list the eigenvalues of `D_(G,K)` in nonincreasing order, with
multiplicity,

\[
 \lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\cdots\geq0.
 \tag{L-14318.6}
\]

### A. Exact optimal rank-r packet

For `r>=0`, let `U_r subset K^perp` be a spectral subspace generated by `r`
top eigenvectors of `D_(G,K)`. Then every

\[
 w\perp K\oplus U_r
 \tag{L-14318.7}
\]

satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac1{2\pi}\int s(\xi)|\widehat w(\xi)|^2\,d\xi
 \geq\bigl(G-\lambda_{r+1}\bigr)\|w\|_2^2,}
 \tag{L-14318.8}
\]

where `lambda_(r+1)=0` after the rank is exhausted.

Moreover this packet is min--max optimal:

\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_{r+1}
 =\inf_{\substack{U\subset K^\perp\\\dim U=r}}
   \sup_{\substack{w\perp K\oplus U\\\|w\|_2=1}}
   \langle D_Gw,w\rangle.}
 \tag{L-14318.9}
\]

Thus, at a fixed symbol level `G`, no other rank-`r` enlargement of `K` can
certify a better complement floor from the inequality
`A_s >= G I-D_G`.

### B. Exact packet-leverage trace and rank cap

For real frequency `xi`, put

\[
 e_\xi(x)=e^{-i\xi x}\quad(x\in I),
 \qquad
 c_K(\xi)=\frac{\|P e_\xi\|_2^2}{2\pi}.
 \tag{L-14318.10}
\]

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{Tr}D_{G,K}
 =\int_{\mathbb R}c_K(\xi)d_G(\xi)\,d\xi.}
 \tag{L-14318.11}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_{r+1}
 \leq\frac1{r+1}
       \int c_K(\xi)d_G(\xi)\,d\xi,}
 \tag{L-14318.12}
\]

and, for every `eta>0`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \#\{j:\lambda_j>\eta\}
 \leq\frac1\eta\int c_Kd_G.}
 \tag{L-14318.13}
\]

This is the weighted, packet-relative version of the generalized-prolate rank
cap.  The geometry is determined by the actual deficit `(G-s)_+`, not by a
binary bad-frequency set.

### C. Arbitrary exact packet certificate

The spectral packet need not be constructed to certify a particular finite
candidate.  For any finite `U subset K^perp`, if

\[
 P_{(K\oplus U)^\perp}D_GP_{(K\oplus U)^\perp}
 \preceq\eta I,
 \tag{L-14318.14}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 q_s(w)\geq(G-\eta)\|w\|_2^2
 \quad(w\perp K\oplus U).}
 \tag{L-14318.15}
\]

This is a finite rational-LMI interface after `D_G`, the packet, and all
uncertainty radii have been enclosed.

### D. Robust principal-angle transfer

Let `S` and `S_tilde` be finite-dimensional subspaces with orthogonal
projections `P_S,P_tilde_S`, and suppose

\[
 \|P_S-P_{\widetilde S}\|_{op}\leq\varepsilon.
 \tag{L-14318.16}
\]

If

\[
 \|P_{\widetilde S^\perp}D_GP_{\widetilde S^\perp}\|_{op}
 \leq\widetilde\eta,
 \qquad
 \|D_G\|_{op}\leq\nu,
 \tag{L-14318.17}
\]

then

\[
 \boxed{
 \|P_{S^\perp}D_GP_{S^\perp}\|_{op}
 \leq\widetilde\eta+2\varepsilon\nu,}
 \tag{L-14318.18}
\]

and therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 q_s(w)\geq
 (G-\widetilde\eta-2\varepsilon\nu)\|w\|_2^2
 \quad(w\perp S).}
 \tag{L-14318.19}
\]

At the pointwise leverage level one also has

\[
 \boxed{
 |c_S(\xi)-c_{\widetilde S}(\xi)|
 \leq\frac{L\varepsilon}{2\pi},}
 \tag{L-14318.20}
\]

and hence

\[
 \boxed{
 \left|\operatorname{Tr}D_{G,S}
       -\operatorname{Tr}D_{G,\widetilde S}\right|
 \leq\varepsilon\operatorname{Tr}D_G.}
 \tag{L-14318.21}
\]

Equations (L-14318.18)--(L-14318.21) are the required proof-facing bridge from
a certified principal-angle/subspace-distance estimate to a rigorous symbol
complement floor.

## Proof

Since `s>=G-d_G`, Plancherel gives the form inequality

\[
 A_s\succeq G I-D_G.
 \tag{L-14318.22}
\]

For `w in K^perp`, the deficit quadratic is represented by `D_(G,K)`.  The
spectral theorem and Courant--Fischer give

\[
 \sup_{\substack{w\perp K\oplus U_r\\\|w\|=1}}
 \langle D_Gw,w\rangle=\lambda_{r+1}
\]

and the optimality identity (L-14318.9).  Combining with (L-14318.22) proves
part A.  Part C is the same argument with a supplied packet and a verified
operator upper bound.

The weak integral decomposition

\[
 D_G=\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}
 d_G(\xi)|e_\xi\rangle\langle e_\xi|\,d\xi
 \tag{L-14318.23}
\]

is positive and trace class because `d_G in L1` and `||e_xi||^2=L`.
Cyclicity of the trace gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \operatorname{Tr}D_{G,K}
 &=\operatorname{Tr}(PD_GP)\\
 &=\frac1{2\pi}\int d_G(\xi)
   \langle Pe_\xi,e_\xi\rangle\,d\xi\\
 &=\int c_Kd_G,
 \end{aligned}
\]

which proves (L-14318.11).  The eigenvalue and counting bounds follow from
`(r+1)lambda_(r+1)<=Tr D_(G,K)` and Markov's inequality for the positive
eigenvalue sequence.

For part D, abbreviate

\[
 P=P_{S^\perp},\qquad \widetilde P=P_{\widetilde S^\perp}.
\]

Then

\[
 PDP-\widetilde P D_G\widetilde P
 =(P-\widetilde P)D_GP
 +\widetilde P D_G(P-\widetilde P),
\]

so its operator norm is at most `2 epsilon nu`.  This proves
(L-14318.18) and (L-14318.19).  Finally,

\[
 c_S(\xi)-c_{\widetilde S}(\xi)
 =\frac1{2\pi}
  \langle(P-\widetilde P)e_\xi,e_\xi\rangle,
\]

whose absolute value is at most `epsilon L/(2pi)`.  Integration against `d_G`
and `Tr D_G=L/(2pi) integral d_G` prove the last two assertions. QED.

## Exact finite certificate adapter

Let `D_0` be a rational symmetric midpoint and suppose the exact positive
deficit matrix satisfies

\[
 \|D_G-D_0\|_{op}\leq\delta.
 \tag{L-14318.24}
\]

Let `B` be a rational full-column-rank basis of the exact candidate complement,
with ordinary Gram `S_B=B^TB`.  It is sufficient to certify by exact rational
LDL

\[
 \eta S_B-B^TD_0B-\delta S_B\succeq0.
 \tag{L-14318.25}
\]

Then the exact complement floor is `G-eta`.  If the exact target packet is only
known within projection distance `epsilon` of the certified packet and
`||D_G||<=nu`, subtract the additional moat `2 epsilon nu`.
