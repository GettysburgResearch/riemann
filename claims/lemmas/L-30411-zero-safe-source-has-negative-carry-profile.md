## 2. Zero-safe inverse-zeta source

\[
\boxed{

\tag{L-30411.3}

\[

\[

\[

## 6. Exact atomized carry profile

For integers `n>=1`, `0<=j<=n`, and `q>=1`, put

\[
\chi_{n,q}(j)
=\left\lfloor{n\over q}\right\rfloor
 -\left\lfloor{j\over q}\right\rfloor

\tag{L-30411.15}
\]

Since

\[
\mathbf1*b_\alpha
=\varepsilon-\alpha\delta_2,
\tag{L-30411.16}
\]

one has, for every integer `x>=0`,

\[
\boxed{
S_\alpha(x)
:=\sum_{q\le x}b_\alpha(q)
 \left\lfloor{x\over q}\right\rfloor
=\mathbf1_{x\ge1}-\alpha\mathbf1_{x\ge2}.}

Therefore the complete carry image is

\[
\boxed{
Y_{\alpha,n}(j)

\tag{L-30411.18}

The `q=1` term may be included because `chi_(n,1)=0`.
