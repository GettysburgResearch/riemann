# L-99422 — Coboundary cancellation leaves one uniformly bounded root calibration

Claim ID: `L-99422`
Status: **PROVED ON THE FROZEN COMPACT ENDPOINT INPUTS**
Created: 2026-08-19
Depends on: `L-99420`, `L-99421`, PR #641 fixed-row estimates
RH status: **not assumed**

Fix a component row \(j\ge2\).  Let \(A_X(j)\) be the primitive root
calibration in (L-99421.9).

## 1. Retained-cell term

For \(K_X=\lfloor X/67\rfloor+1\), the exact adjacent-cell identity gives

\[
\left|\mathcal R E_X^I(j)\right|
<
\frac{38}{j(j-1)\sqrt{K_X+1}}
<
\frac{38\sqrt{67}}{j(j-1)\sqrt X}
\tag{L-99422.1}
\]

whenever the fixed row lies below the retained cells.  The finitely many
remaining activation patterns are compact.

## 2. Knot and boundary terms

By `L-99421`, the compact distributional correction is generated by one
bounded-variation state \(w_f\) and two scalar boundary values.  The compact
factor-67 fibre has finitely many source types and activation knots.  The
endpoint packet observed in one fixed row has the form

\[
p_s(j)=a_j+b_js^{-1/2}
\tag{L-99422.2}
\]

outside finitely many low activation regimes and is therefore uniformly
bounded.

The compact root source has finite mass (the frozen directed estimate
\(M_{67}<16\) is more than sufficient).  Hence there exists a constant
\(B_j<\infty\), independent of \(X\), such that

\[
\boxed{|A_X(j)|\le B_j.}
\tag{L-99422.3}
\]

No descendant factor appears in (L-99422.3): by `L-99420` all child
calibrations telescope.

## 3. Mellin consequence

For any \(X_0>1\),

\[
\mathcal A_j(s)
=\int_{X_0}^{\infty}A_X(j)X^{-s-1}\,dX
\tag{L-99422.4}
\]

converges absolutely and locally uniformly in \(\Re s>0\).  Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal A_j(s)\text{ is holomorphic in }\Re s>0.}
\tag{L-99422.5}
\]

The theorem is strictly stronger than summing a bounded local defect over the
tree: the complete resolved defect is the single root potential \(A_X(j)\).
