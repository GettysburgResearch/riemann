# L-99003 — Self-contained Mellin–Landau consumer

**Status:** proved in the accompanying manuscript.  **RH is not assumed.**

Let \(\mathcal A_X\) be as in L-99000.  For \(\Re s>1/2\), put
\(z=s+1/2\). Direct termwise integration gives
\[
\boxed{
\int_1^\infty \mathcal A_X X^{-s-1}\,dX
=\frac{1-4^{-s}}{s^2}
 \left[6-
 \frac{3(1-2^{-z})(2-2^{-z})}{\zeta(z)}\right].}
\]
If \(\mathcal A_X\ge0\) for all sufficiently large \(X\), then every
nontrivial zero of \(\zeta\) lies on \(\Re z=1/2\).

The manuscript proves all ingredients used here: the Dirichlet-series
calculation, the specialized Landau singularity theorem for an eventually
nonnegative Laplace density, the absence of real zeta zeros in \((0,1)\), and
the functional-equation symmetry obtained from the Jacobi theta identity.
No zero-density theorem, PNT error term, or form of RH is imported.
