# L-92103 — Critical zeros form a passive parallel-impedance network

Claim ID: L-92103
Status: EXACT RH-SIDE NETWORK SYNTHESIS
RH status: unproved

Under RH, every positive zero ordinate gamma of multiplicity m defines the linear impedance

Z_gamma(t)=(t+gamma^2)/(2m).

Its admittance is 1/Z_gamma=2m/(t+gamma^2). The complete Xi admittance and impedance are

p(t)=sum_gamma 1/Z_gamma(t),
Z(t)=1/p(t).

Thus the critical zero ensemble is a parallel passive network. Finite parallel sums of complete Bernstein functions remain complete Bernstein, and the infinite network follows by local uniform convergence. Equivalently, p is the Stieltjes/Weyl admittance of a positive Krein string and Z is its Dirichlet-to-Neumann impedance.

A reflected off-line orbit creates conjugate nonreal poles of p and cannot be synthesized by a positive string.

Consequently RH is equivalent to existence of a positive string or passive parallel network whose safe impedance is the explicit function Z(t). This is a realization target, not a proof that the arithmetic source generates the string.

Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.
