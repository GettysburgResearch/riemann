# L-99817 — Inactive rough Euler factors are exactly triangularly silent

Claim ID: `L-99817`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT ACTIVATION REDUCTION**  
Created: 2026-08-20  
RH status: **not assumed**

Let `Phi` be zero extended below one and let

\[
\mathcal E_P=\prod_{p\in P}(I-p^{-1/2}U_p).
\]

Fix `y>=1`. If `p>y`, then `y/p<1`, hence

\[
U_p\Phi(y)=\Phi(y/p)=0.
\]

More generally, because all shifts commute and every further shifted argument is at most `y/p<1`, the factor `(I-p^{-1/2}U_p)` acts as the identity at `y` on every term generated by the other primes.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_P\Phi(y)
=\mathcal E_{P\cap[1,y]}\Phi(y).
}
\tag{L-99817.1}

Thus the many-prime sign problem is triangular in the endpoint: only active primes `p<=y` matter. Ordering them increasingly,

\[
67<=p_1<...<p_k<=y<p_{k+1},
\]

the value at `y` depends only on the first `k` primes.

This exact activation reduction allows induction on the number of active rough primes. It also prevents inactive configured primes from being counted as a source or energy cost.
