# L-105224 — Real-rooted derivative data admit a correction-free de Branges residue-moment Gram

Claim ID: `L-105224`  
Status: **PROPOSED EXACT FINITE-POLYNOMIAL THEOREM; independent review pending**  
Created: 2026-08-23  
Depends on: `L-105220--L-105222`; finite de Branges kernel calculus  
RH status: **not assumed**

Let `p` be a real polynomial of degree `n>=3`, and put

\[
G=p',
\qquad
m=\deg G=n-1.
\]

Assume that `G` has `m` distinct real zeros

\[
c_1,\ldots,c_m.
\]

No real-rootedness hypothesis is placed on `p`.

## 1. The derivative de Branges space

Define

\[
E(z)=G(z)+iG'(z).
\tag{L-105224.1}
\]

Since all zeros of `G` are real and simple,

\[
\Im\frac{G'(z)}{G(z)}<0
\qquad(\Im z>0),
\]

and therefore

\[
|E(z)|>|E^\#(z)|
\qquad(\Im z>0).
\]

Thus `E` is Hermite–Biehler. Its finite de Branges space is the set of
polynomials of degree less than `m`, with norm

\[
\|f\|_E^2
=
\int_{\mathbb R}
\frac{|f(x)|^2}{G(x)^2+G'(x)^2}\,dx.
\tag{L-105224.2}
\]

At a zero `c` of `G`, the reproducing kernel is

\[
\boxed{
K_c(z)=
\frac{G'(c)G(z)}{\pi(z-c)},
\qquad
K_c(c)=\frac{G'(c)^2}{\pi}.
}
\tag{L-105224.3}
\]

The `m` kernels are an orthogonal basis. Consequently every polynomial
`f`, `deg f<m`, satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\|f\|_E^2
=
\pi\sum_{G(c)=0}
\frac{|f(c)|^2}{G'(c)^2}.
}
\tag{L-105224.4}
\]

## 2. Safe-pole subtraction

Fix a real centre `a` and put

\[
\lambda_h=a-ih,
\qquad h=1,2,3,
\]

\[
g_a(z)=
\frac{6}{
(z-\lambda_1)(z-\lambda_2)(z-\lambda_3)
}.
\tag{L-105224.5}
\]

Then, for real `x`,

\[
|g_a(x)|^2=\Omega_a(x).
\tag{L-105224.6}
\]

The residues of `g_a` are

\[
\boxed{
(\gamma_1,\gamma_2,\gamma_3)=(-3,6,-3).
}
\tag{L-105224.7}
\]

Because `G` is real-rooted, `G(lambda_h)!=0`. Define

\[
\boxed{
u_a(z)
=
g_a(z)G'(z)
-
G(z)\sum_{h=1}^3
\frac{\gamma_hG'(\lambda_h)}
{G(\lambda_h)(z-\lambda_h)}
}
\tag{L-105224.8}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
v_a(z)
=
g_a(z)p(z)
-
G(z)\sum_{h=1}^3
\frac{\gamma_hp(\lambda_h)}
{G(\lambda_h)(z-\lambda_h)}.
}
\tag{L-105224.9}
\]

Every apparent pole at `lambda_h` is removable.

The rational functions

\[
g_a\frac{G'}G
\quad\text{and}\quad
g_a\frac pG
\]

decay respectively as `O(z^-4)` and `O(z^-2)`. Their complete partial
fractions have no polynomial part. Multiplication by `G` therefore gives

\[
u_a(z)
=
G(z)\sum_{G(c)=0}\frac{g_a(c)}{z-c},
\tag{L-105224.10}
\]

\[
v_a(z)
=
G(z)\sum_{G(c)=0}
\frac{g_a(c)\rho_c}{z-c},
\qquad
\rho_c=\frac{p(c)}{G'(c)}.
\tag{L-105224.11}
\]

Hence `u_a,v_a` are polynomials of degree less than `m`, and

\[
u_a(c)=g_a(c)G'(c),
\qquad
v_a(c)=g_a(c)p(c).
\tag{L-105224.12}
\]

## 3. Exact correction-free moment Gram

Define the actual positive-weight real moments

\[
N_a=\sum_c\Omega_a(c),
\]

\[
A_a=-\sum_c\Omega_a(c)\rho_c,
\]

\[
B_a=\sum_c\Omega_a(c)\rho_c^2.
\tag{L-105224.13}
\]

Equations (L-105224.4) and (L-105224.12) give

\[
\boxed{
\|u_a\|_E^2=\pi N_a,
}
\tag{L-105224.14}
\]

\[
\boxed{
\langle v_a,u_a\rangle_E=-\pi A_a,
}
\tag{L-105224.15}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\|v_a\|_E^2=\pi B_a.
}
\tag{L-105224.16}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\pi^2(N_aB_a-A_a^2)
=
\|u_a\|_E^2\|v_a\|_E^2
-
|\langle v_a,u_a\rangle_E|^2
=
\|u_a\wedge v_a\|^2.
}
\tag{L-105224.17}
\]

No zero of `p''`, no Bézout interpolant, and no nonreal critical correction
appears. The only hypothesis is that the derivative `p'=G` is already
real-rooted, exactly as in a downward reverse–Rolle induction.

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
B_a-\frac{A_a^2}{N_a}
=
\frac1\pi
\min_{\alpha\in\mathbb R}
\|v_a+\alpha u_a\|_E^2.
}
\tag{L-105224.18}
\]

Thus the residue dispersion is the squared distance from one physical
safe-point-subtracted vector to the carrier line generated by the other.

## 4. Safe-point kernel realization of the count vector

For `w_h=bar(lambda_h)=a+ih`, the de Branges kernel calculation gives

\[
\boxed{
u_a
=
-\pi
\sum_{h=1}^3
\frac{\gamma_h}{G(\lambda_h)}
K_{w_h}.
}
\tag{L-105224.19}
\]

Thus the count vector is a fixed three-kernel combination and its norm is
determined by a `3x3` safe-point kernel Gram.

The second vector is the analogous three-pole subtraction of the antiderivative
`p`. Its norm is the exact conclusion-bearing residue second moment.

## 5. Scope

The theorem is finite and exact. For an entire Xi descent it suggests replacing

```text
entire Bézout interpolation
+ nonreal critical corrections
+ adjacent-derivative debt
```

by one de Branges exhaustion of the pair `(u_a,v_a)`, conditional on the
already-known real-rootedness of the derivative at that step. Establishing
that cofinal entire-space passage and the required angle estimate remains open.
