## 1. Generic moving-carrier setup

Let

\[
A(s)=\sum_{n\ge2}a(n)n^{-s},
\qquad a(n)\ge0,
\qquad a(1)=0,
\]

converge absolutely on `Re s=sigma`, and let `L(s)` be holomorphic there. Put

\[
B(s)=L(s)-A(s),
\qquad q(s)=B(s)^{-1},
\]

and assume

\[
\Re B(s)>0.
\tag{L-105610.1}
\]

For `u>=0`, define the Dirichlet-convolution exponential

\[
\boxed{
 c_u
 =\exp_*(u a)
 =\sum_{k\ge0}{u^k\over k!}a^{*k}.
}
\tag{L-105610.2}
\]

For each fixed integer `n`, the sum is finite because `a(1)=0`. Hence

\[
\boxed{c_u(n)\ge0.}
\tag{L-105610.3}
\]

Absolute convergence gives

\[
\boxed{
 e^{uA(s)}
 =\sum_{n\ge1}c_u(n)n^{-s}.
}
\tag{L-105610.4}
\]

## 2. Exact Laplace resolution

The elementary identity

\[
{1\over B(s)}
=\int_0^\infty e^{-uB(s)}\,du
\]

gives

\[
\boxed{
q(s)
=\int_0^\infty
 e^{-uL(s)}
 \sum_{n\ge1}c_u(n)n^{-s}\,du.
}
\tag{L-105610.5}
\]

Thus the actual complex carrier is not frozen. It appears as one common scalar
phase and damping factor at every Laplace parameter `u`, while every arithmetic
coefficient remains nonnegative.

## 3. Exact Hardy-multiplier derivative

Differentiate (L-105610.4):

\[
-uA'(s)e^{uA(s)}
=\sum_{n\ge1}c_u(n)\log n\,n^{-s}.
\tag{L-105610.6}
\]

For every real `h>=0`, differentiating (L-105610.5) under the absolutely
convergent integral yields

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
q(s)-h q'(s)
=\int_0^\infty e^{-uL(s)}
 \sum_{n\ge1}c_u(n)
 \bigl(1+h\log n+h uL'(s)\bigr)n^{-s}\,du.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{L-105610.7}
\]

The coefficient splits into

```text
positive arithmetic part:  1+h log n;
common carrier drift:       h u L'(s).
```

In particular, the full archimedean carrier phase `exp(-iu Im L(s))` is common
to every source coefficient at fixed `u`. Any estimate uniform in an arbitrary
global phase may be applied before the `u` integration.

## 4. Completed-zeta specialization

On a right safe line write

\[
{\xi'\over\xi}(s)=L_\xi(s)-A_\zeta(s),
\qquad
A_\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\ge2}\Lambda(n)n^{-s},
\tag{L-105610.8}
\]

where

\[
L_\xi(s)
={1\over s}+{1\over s-1}
-{1\over2}\log\pi
+{1\over2}\psi(s/2).
\tag{L-105610.9}
\]

Then (L-105610.7) is an exact representation of the actual rung-zero source

\[
q_0-hq_0',
\qquad q_0={\xi\over\xi'},
\]

through a positive family `c_u` generated by the literal von Mangoldt source.
No carrier freezing, Taylor replacement of `L_xi`, or finite Euler truncation
is used.

## 5. Base descent and arithmetic frequency

Replacing `sigma` by `sigma-delta` multiplies the `n`th coefficient in
(L-105610.7) by

\[
\boxed{n^\delta=e^{\delta\log n}.}
\tag{L-105610.10}
\]

This is exactly the backward-Poisson amplifier `e^(delta |D|)` of `L-105601`
on the arithmetic spectrum `|xi|=log n`. The moving-carrier resolution is
therefore compatible coefficient by coefficient with the zero-height descent.
