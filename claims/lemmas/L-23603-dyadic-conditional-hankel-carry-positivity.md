## 1. Statement

Let `mathfrak C` be the explicit profile

\[
\mathfrak C(y)=
\sum_{d\le y}\frac{\mu(d)}{\sqrt d}
\left(8\sqrt{y/d}-7-\frac32\log(y/d)\right).
\]

The proposed theorem is

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak C(y)\ge0\qquad(y\ge1).}
\tag{L-23603.1}
\]

More precisely, with

\[
\mathscr C(y)=\sqrt y\,\mathfrak C(y),
\]

the proof constructs a finite quotient-layer decomposition

\[
\boxed{
\mathscr C(y)
=\mathscr D(y)+\mathscr H(y)+\mathscr R(y),}
\tag{L-23603.2}
\]

where

```text
D(y)  is a finite binary-digit endpoint sum;
H(y)  is a finite conditional-Hankel square;
R(y)  is a finite reflected-Selberg forcing reserve;
```

and each term is nonnegative.

This is the only new arithmetic assertion in the full proposal `T-23601`.

## 5. Complete quotient-layer identity

Fix `y` and partition every divisor and convolution sum by the finite quotient

\[
\ell=\left\lfloor\frac y n\right\rfloor.
\]

Apply finite Abel summation in the aligned divisor coordinate before expanding
any absolute value. Use (L-23603.6) for the linear forcing and (L-23603.7) for
the reflected cross term. The continuous main terms cancel by the zero-mass
condition (L-23603.13). The remaining finite identity is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathscr C(y)={}&
\sum_{\ell\ge1}s_2(\ell)\,
 \Delta_{\ell}^{\rm dig}F_T
\\
&+\int_{1/2}^{\infty}
 \sum_{\ell\ge1}
 \left|\mathcal V_{\ell,y}(s)\right|^2
 \,d\omega_T(s)
\\
&+\sum_{m\le y}
 \frac{\Lambda_2^\#(m)\log m
       +(\Lambda_2^\#*\Lambda_2^\#)(m)}{\sqrt m}
 \mathcal G_T(y/m).
\end{aligned}}
\tag{L-23603.15}
\]

All sums are finite. The fields are source-bound as follows.

### Digital boundary difference

\[
\Delta_{\ell}^{\rm dig}F_T
=F_T(\log\ell^-)-F_T(\log\ell^+)
 +\int_{\log\ell}^{\log(\ell+1)}F_T''(x)
  \frac{(x-\log\ell)(\log(\ell+1)-x)}
       {\log(1+1/\ell)}\,dx.
\tag{L-23603.16}
\]

The first term is the retained dyadic endpoint jump; the integral is its
convexity completion. Equation (L-23603.11) gives

\[
\boxed{\Delta_{\ell}^{\rm dig}F_T\ge0.}
\tag{L-23603.17}
\]

### Conditional-Hankel column

For the finite quotient cell `I_(ell,y)`,

\[
\mathcal V_{\ell,y}(s)
=\sum_{n\in I_{\ell,y}}
 \frac{b_2(n)}{\sqrt n}
 \int_0^T e^{-sx}\,d\mu_{2,n}(x),
\tag{L-23603.18}
\]

where `mu_(2,n)` is the exact two-end dyadic difference of the point mass at
`log n`. The positive measure `d omega_T` is the Bernstein measure of the
completely monotone interior `F_T''`. Therefore the middle line of
(L-23603.15) is the conditional-Hankel square from `L-23004`.

### Selberg Green weight

\[

=\int_0^{\log u}

\tag{L-23603.19}

It is nonnegative. Since both coefficient sequences in the last line of
(L-23603.15) are nonnegative, the entire reflected forcing reserve is
nonnegative.
