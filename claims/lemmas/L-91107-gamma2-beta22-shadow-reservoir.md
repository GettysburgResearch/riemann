## 1. Gamma-sum realization

The BPY variable satisfies

\[
 \Sigma_2
 =\frac{2}{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{X_n}{n^2},
 \qquad X_n\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}\Gamma(2,1),
 \qquad
 \Sigma_2\stackrel d=\frac2\pi Y^2.
\tag{L-91107.1}
\]

Consequently

\[
 Z=\frac12\log\Sigma_2+\frac12\log\frac\pi2
\]

under the `Sigma_2^{1/4}`-tilted law.

## 2. Two-copy beta--gamma algebra

Take two independent un-tilted copies `X_n^+,X_n^-`. Put

\[
 G_n=X_n^++X_n^-,
 \qquad
 U_n=\frac{X_n^+}{G_n},
 \qquad
 V_n=2U_n-1.
\]

The beta--gamma algebra gives independently over `n`

\[
\boxed{
 G_n\sim\Gamma(4,1),
 \qquad
 U_n\sim\operatorname{Beta}(2,2),
 \qquad
 G_n\perp U_n.
}
\tag{L-91107.2}
\]

Write

\[
 A=\frac1{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{G_n}{n^2},
 \qquad
 D=\frac1{\pi^2}\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{G_nV_n}{n^2}.
\tag{L-91107.3}
\]

Then exactly

\[
\boxed{
 \Sigma_2^+=A+D,
 \qquad
 \Sigma_2^-=A-D.
}
\tag{L-91107.4}
\]

The two-copy half-size bias multiplies the product Gamma--Beta law by

\[
\boxed{
 (\Sigma_2^+\Sigma_2^-)^{1/4}
 =(A^2-D^2)^{1/4}.
}
\tag{L-91107.5}
\]

Thus all nontrivial coupling is an explicit even tilt of a sum of independent bounded beta coordinates.

## 3. Exact Beta Stein identity

The transformed beta coordinate has density

\[
 \rho(v)=\frac34(1-v^2)\mathbf1_{(-1,1)}(v).
\]

For every polynomial, and then every smooth test with integrable derivative,

\[
\boxed{
 \mathbb E[Vf(V)]
 =\frac14\mathbb E[(1-V^2)f'(V)].
}
\tag{L-91107.6}
\]

Equivalently the Jacobi generator

\[
 \mathcal L_\beta f
 =\frac14\bigl[(1-v^2)f''-4vf'\bigr]
\tag{L-91107.7}
\]

is symmetric and nonpositive in `L^2(rho)`, with carré du champ

\[
\boxed{
 -\langle f,\mathcal L_\beta f\rangle
 =\frac14\mathbb E[(1-V^2)|f'(V)|^2]\ge0.
}
\tag{L-91107.8}
\]

For finitely many modes the product generator is the sum of these positive local energies.

## 4. The exactly tilted Dirichlet form

Condition on a finite Gamma reservoir `G=(G_n)` and put

\[
 H_G(V)=(A^2-D^2)^{1/4}.
\]

The two-copy half-biased beta law is proportional to

\[
 H_G(V)\prod_n\rho(V_n)dV_n.
\]

Its canonical reversible Dirichlet form is

\[
\boxed{
 \mathcal E_G(f,f)
 =\frac14\sum_n
 \frac{\displaystyle\int
 H_G(V)(1-V_n^2)|\partial_{V_n}f(V)|^2
 \prod_k\rho(V_k)dV_k}
 {\displaystyle\int H_G(V)\prod_k\rho(V_k)dV_k}
 \ge0.
}
\tag{L-91107.9}
\]

No RH input enters this form. Passing to infinitely many modes is a separate closability/tail step.

\[
