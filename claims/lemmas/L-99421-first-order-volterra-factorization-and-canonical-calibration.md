# L-99421 — The distributional Volterra correction has one first-order calibration potential

Claim ID: `L-99421`
Status: **PROVED EXACT DISTRIBUTIONAL THEOREM**
Created: 2026-08-19
Depends on: PR #638 distributional Green formula
RH status: **not assumed**

Let \(f\) be continuous and piecewise \(C^1\) on a compact interval
\([a,b]\), with \(f'\) of bounded variation.  Put

\[
D=x\frac d{dx},
\qquad
w_f(x)=x^{-1/2}(D-1)f(x)
      =\sqrt x\,f'(x)-\frac{f(x)}{\sqrt x}.
\tag{L-99421.1}
\]

Let \(V\) be the endpoint inverse

\[
Vf=\frac{2x^2f''-xf'+f}{2\sqrt x}
\tag{L-99421.2}
\]

in the distributional sense.

## 1. First-order factorisation

Direct distributional differentiation gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
x\,dw_f
={}&x^{3/2}\,df'
-\frac12\sqrt x\,f'(x)\,dx
+\frac12\frac{f(x)}{\sqrt x}\,dx.
\end{aligned}
\]

This is exactly the measure represented by (L-99421.2).  Therefore

\[
\boxed{d(Vf)(x)=x\,dw_f(x).}
\tag{L-99421.3}
\]

Equivalently, at the Euler-operator level,

\[
2D^2-3D+1=(2D-1)(D-1).
\tag{L-99421.4}
\]

If \(f'\) jumps at \(t\), then \(w_f\) jumps by
\(\sqrt t\,[f']_t\), and (L-99421.3) gives the exact atom

\[
\boxed{
d(Vf)(\{t\})=t^{3/2}\bigl(f'(t+)-f'(t-)\bigr).
}
\tag{L-99421.5}
\]

Thus the complete knot ledger is generated by one bounded-variation
first-order state, not by independent atom choices.

## 2. Green representation and the two modes

Integrating (L-99421.3) yields the exact Green formula

\[
f(x)=A_a\sqrt x+B_ax+
\int_{(a,x]}
\frac{2(x-\sqrt{xt})}{t^{3/2}}\,d(Vf)(t),
\tag{L-99421.6}
\]

with homogeneous nullspace

\[
\ker V=\operatorname{span}\{\sqrt x,x\}.
\tag{L-99421.7}
\]

The constants \(A_a,B_a\), the one-sided values of \(w_f\), and the finite
variation measure \(dw_f\) determine the complete endpoint frame.

## 3. Canonical positive/frame split

On the compact factor-67 fibre, the absolutely continuous inverse density is
the exact density \(L(x)\) of the frozen endpoint identity and satisfies

\[
L(x)>\frac{159}{500}>0
\qquad(1\le x<67).
\tag{L-99421.8}
\]

Let \(P\) be the row obtained by integrating the endpoint-nested canonical
packet against this positive absolutely continuous part.  Put every remaining
term in (L-99421.6)—the singular part of \(x\,dw_f\), the two homogeneous
modes, and the exact retained-cell discrepancy—into \(A\).

Then, by finite Fubini and (L-99421.6),

\[
\boxed{E=P+A}
\tag{L-99421.9}
\]

in the literal source labels and in every physical component row.  This is not
a remainder defined after comparison: it is the canonical distributional
frame split.


On the complete retained cells this split is the exact cell identity

\[
c_X=c_{X,A}+\overline c_{X,I}+\mathcal R E_X^I,
\qquad
\overline c_{X,I}
=\sum_{m\in\mathcal I_X}\int_m^{m+1}
\frac{2L(X/s)}s\,p_s\,ds.
\tag{L-99421.10}
\]

The positive term is part of \(P\); \(\mathcal R E_X^I\) is part of \(A\).
The finite anchored and activation-boundary terms are supplied by the
homogeneous and singular terms in (L-99421.6).  No Target--Lorenz terminal
orientation, Volterra derivative child, or branchwise positive oriented child
is used in this frame split.

## 4. Optional cumulative-tail form

For \(s\in[a,b]\), integration by parts gives, with the standard left-limit
endpoint convention,

\[
d(Vf)([s,b])
=bw_f(b)-s w_f(s^-)-\int_s^b w_f(t)\,dt.
\tag{L-99421.10}
\]

Consequently all upper tails of the endpoint inverse can be certified from the
single first-order state \(w_f\).  If those tails are nonnegative, direct
Stieltjes transposition gives a fully positive equality frame.  The
holomorphic-defect route does not require this stronger sign.
