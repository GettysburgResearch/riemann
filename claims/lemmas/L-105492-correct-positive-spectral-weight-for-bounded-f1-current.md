\[

Every factor in (L-105492.4) is strictly positive for real \(t\).

## 2. Exact Plancherel identity

The bounded kernel is in \(L^2(dX/X)\), so ordinary Plancherel now applies:

\[
\boxed{
\int_{\mathbb R}
e^{-x/2}|H_U^\diamond(e^x)|^2dx

\frac1{2\pi}\int_{\mathbb R}
\Omega_K(t)|\mathscr Q_U^\diamond(t)|^2dt.

\tag{L-105492.5}
\]

Moreover,

\[
\boxed{
\Omega_K(t)\asymp\frac1{1+t^2}.

\tag{L-105492.6}

normal-ordered analytic square. The missing denominator in historical
