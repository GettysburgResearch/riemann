## 1. The continuum endpoint frame

For a fixed node \(m\) and a real endpoint \(T\ge m\), the parabolic seed satisfies
\[
\boxed{
 \partial_T b_T(m)
 =\frac{2\sqrt m}{T}-\frac{2m}{T^{3/2}}.
}
\tag{L-91107.1}

Let \(X>0\), put
\[
 \theta=\frac mX,
\]
and let \(\lambda(u)\) be a continuum endpoint-weight density on \(0<u\le1\). The normalized seed profile generated by the endpoint frame is
\[
\boxed{
 f(\theta)
 =\int_\theta^1\lambda(u)
 \left[
  \frac{2\sqrt\theta}{u}
  -\frac{2\theta}{u^{3/2}}
 \right]du.
}
\tag{L-91107.2}

Indeed, set \(T=Xu\) in the integral of \(\lambda(T/X)\partial_Tb_T(m)\).

Write
\[
 A(\theta)=\int_\theta^1\frac{\lambda(u)}u\,du,
 \qquad
 B(\theta)=\int_\theta^1\frac{\lambda(u)}{u^{3/2}}\,du.
\]
Then
\[
 f(\theta)=2\sqrt\theta\,A(\theta)-2\theta B(\theta).
\tag{L-91107.3}

Differentiation gives
\[
 f'(\theta)=\frac{A(\theta)}{\sqrt\theta}-2B(\theta),
\]
\[
 f''(\theta)=\frac{\lambda(\theta)}{\theta^{3/2}}
 -\frac{A(\theta)}{2\theta^{3/2}}.
\]
Eliminating \(A,B\) yields the exact inverse
\[
\boxed{
 \lambda(\theta)
 =\mathcal E f(\theta)
 :=\frac{
 2\theta^2f''(\theta)-\theta f'(\theta)+f(\theta)
 }{2\sqrt\theta}.
}
\tag{L-91107.4}

This is the continuum counterpart of the finite adjacent-butterfly operator in `L-91101`–`L-91105`.

In logarithmic scale \(t=-\log\theta\), the numerator factors as
\[
 2\theta^2f''-\theta f'+f
 =(2\partial_t+1)(\partial_t+1)f.
\tag{L-91107.5}

Thus the endpoint inverse is a factored Euler/Bessel operator.
