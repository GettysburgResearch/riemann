## 1. Canonical production order

For one fixed `K`:

1. choose `R_K=K^3` and emit the complete safe-window coefficients and hash;
2. emit the exact double finite-resolvent source manifest;
3. expand every residual `r_V(a b)` into truncated `a` and unrestricted `b`;
4. classify by residual depth and apply `L-24901` before any first-crossing norm;
5. retain only the top-top corner plus declared strict lower-scale sources;
6. apply one deterministic whole-tuple radial split, not recursive Type-I
   bookkeeping;
7. emit the oriented incidence matrix and cancel internal siblings exactly;
8. assemble the complete reflected block matrix before widening or Schur
   elimination;
9. export the Schur-reserve blocks `G,C,D` and prove a strict reserve;
10. export every boundary row, its paid-coordinate set, and its lower-scale
    fiber;
11. prove the maximum simultaneous charge bound;
12. export the fixed-ratio shell projection;
13. replay the recurrence and exponent arithmetic with a small exact checker.

## 2. Preferred top-corner geometry

The proposed producer groups the `K` truncated slots by cumulative logarithmic
mass. Stop at the first prefix crossing one half of the output logarithm. The
crossing slot has size at most `V`, while the two aggregate products have strict
scales

\[
\le\left({1\over2}+{1\over K}+o_K(1)\right)J.
\]

All fiber coordinates other than the crossing slot remain inside the reflected
Gram. This is the preferred mechanism for an `O(1)` charge; it is not yet a
proof of the reserve.

## 3. Candidate token vocabulary

A first implementation should attempt the following absolute token set:

```text
two reflected logarithmic marks
left/right compact-ratio endpoints
one output-product endpoint
one radial crossing slot
one residual-support slot on each reflected side
```

This nominates, but does not pre-prove,

\[
C_{\rm ref}\le8.
\]

A reviewer should not accept the number until every actual surviving row is
injected into the token set.

## 4. Reserve production

The producer must assemble a source-bound matrix identity of the form in
`L-24904`. A numerical or symbolic solver may nominate an LDL, SOS, or Schur
factorization. The repository accepts only:

- exact rational/dyadic matrices or directed intervals;
- an independently reconstructed source basis;
- a strict lower endpoint `kappa_0>0`;
- no same-scale target hidden in `D` or the boundary block;
- a proof that every omitted row has already canceled, Euler-closed, or routed
  below the declared scale.

The target reserve should be uniform in `J`. Uniformity in `K` is needed only
along the final certified sequence.

## 5. Small-order attack

The immediate adversarial program is:

```text
K=4: emit the complete top-top source and all cells;
K=6: test the eight-token charge vocabulary;
K=8: test whether new simultaneous charges appear;
symbolic K: prove the incidence and charge injection by induction.
```

One row with unbounded simultaneous paid defect rejects the proposed constant.
One Schur pivot tending to zero without a compensating lower-scale route rejects
the reserve.

## 6. Mutation tests

The checker must reject at least:

- deleting one top-depth tuple;
- changing one Möbius sign;
- orienting an internal face twice in the same direction;
- applying Euler across a live residual cutoff;
- routing a row to scale `>(1-delta)J+C_K`;
- setting the reserve coefficient to zero;
- placing `E_K(J)` itself on the right side;
- understating a two-coordinate charge as one;
- dropping the fixed-ratio shell projection;
- inferring RH from a finite order list.

## 7. Honest status

logic. The actual top-corner reserve and bounded charge injection remain open.
A pushed branch is a full proposed proof architecture, not a verified proof of
RH, until those source-bound objects are emitted and independently replayed.
