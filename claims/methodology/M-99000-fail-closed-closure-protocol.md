# M-99000 — Fail-closed closure protocol

A claimed closure must separately verify:

1. the exact source dictionary \(q\), its Möbius inverse \(a\), and every
   endpoint convention;
2. the primitive-prefix/window and four-band identities;
3. the Mellin numerator and non-cancellation at every hypothetical off-line
   zero;
4. eventual sign at all real endpoints, not a finite scan;
5. the Landau and functional-equation consumer without assuming a PNT error
   equivalent to the desired zero-free region;
6. reproducible directed arithmetic for every finite certificate.

The packet passes 1, 2, 3, 5, and the finite form of 6.  Item 4 remains open.
No manuscript wording may promote T-99000 from a billion-endpoint theorem to an
all-scale result.
