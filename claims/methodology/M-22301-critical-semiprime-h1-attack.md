## 1. Functional-analytic location

For fixed `sigma>0`, the formal prime ray

\[
 f_\sigma(s)=\sum_p\frac{\log p}{p^{1/2+\sigma}}p^{-s}
\]

belongs to `mathscr H^2_0`, and its square belongs to the weak product

\[
 \mathscr H^2_0\odot\mathscr H^2_0.
\]

The usual embedding theorem for Dirichlet `mathscr H^2` evaluates on
`Re s=1/2`. The required orbit is `s=it`, one half-plane farther left.
`R-22301` proves that no universal weighted identity-orbit embedding exists.

Thus the missing theorem is a **restricted arithmetic Carleson estimate for one
prime-supported ray**, not a general theorem of Dirichlet Hardy spaces.

Primary literature interfaces:

- O. F. Brevig, *An embedding constant for the Hardy space of Dirichlet
  series*, arXiv:1606.03101;
- O. F. Brevig and K.-M. Perfekt, *Weak product spaces of Dirichlet series*,
  arXiv:1510.02019;
- F. Bayart and O. F. Brevig, *Composition operators and embedding theorems for
  some function spaces of Dirichlet series*, arXiv:1602.03446.

These works explain the available half-plane embedding and the subtlety of
weak products/multiplicative Hankel forms. They do not provide (M-22301.1).

\[

## 4. Multiplicative Hankel/weak-product route

only against symbols generated by the fixed compact window and its derivative.
The target is a restricted multiplicative Hankel estimate on the single vector
`f_sigma`, not a Nehari theorem for the whole weak product.

\[

 \le C_\sigma\|f_\sigma\|_{\mathscr H^2}^2

critical identity-orbit norm.

The matrix-embedding phenomena in weak-product theory warn that arbitrary
