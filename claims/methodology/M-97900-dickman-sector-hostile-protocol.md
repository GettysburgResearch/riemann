# M-97900 — Hostile protocol for the Dickman power-sector theorem

Reject the packet if any of the following occurs:

1. the native coefficient `m^(-1/2)` is replaced by a source-mass surrogate;
2. the product activation `m<=Y` is replaced by an unrestricted Euler product;
3. cumulative rough parity `mu(m)` is dropped;
4. the constant or remainder in `b(Y)=a sqrt(Y)+c+O(Y^(-3/2))` is multiplied by the square-root main term;
5. repeated-prime tuples are retained in the squarefree limit without an error bound;
6. convergence of prime harmonic measure is used outside a fixed compact power window;
7. positivity of the scalar is promoted to target-preserving `CSHT67`;
8. the fixed-power theorem is used at the root threshold `p_0=67`, where
   `log p_0/log Y -> 0`;
9. numerical convergence is promoted to the asymptotic theorem;
10. RH is declared established.

The first review target is the uniform error estimate in `L-97901`; the second
is the exact Dickman identification in `L-97900`; the third is the scope
separation between scalar RBLPTE and full CSHT.
