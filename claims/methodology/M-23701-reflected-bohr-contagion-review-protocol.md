## Literature boundary

The MRSTT contagion/scaling machinery is an important model for the inverse
step, but its published almost-all short-interval theorem does not imply
`BCT(K)`.  A production proof must explicitly establish:

- deterministic propagation from one bad packet;
- promotion of approximate log relations to source-bound integer relations;
- the absolute rank ceiling;
- compatibility with the signed packet and first-cell firewall.

## Promotion rule

The proposal may be promoted beyond `FULL PROPOSAL / BCT OPEN` only after an
independently reconstructed symbolic theorem or unbounded proof-producing
family proves the rank bound for every surviving balanced face.  Finite checks
at `K=6,8,...` are valuable falsification tests but do not prove the limit.
