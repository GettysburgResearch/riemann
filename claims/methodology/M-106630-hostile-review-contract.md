# M-106630 — Hostile review contract

Reject any use of the transport certificate that:

1. replaces \(G_-^{-1}\) by the identity without a proved frame theorem;
2. sums one-pole pseudohyperbolic costs as though clustered Cauchy kernels
   were orthogonal;
3. uses a numerator subspace not contained in the actual numerator model
   space;
4. drops confluent derivative kernels or common-factor reduction;
5. estimates a different source bank from the one used in the residual;
6. omits mesoscopic partition, endpoint, or cofinal regularization terms;
7. reports `MESOTRANS106630`, ninety percent, density one, or RH as proved.
