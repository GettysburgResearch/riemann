## 9. Exact checker gates

### Support gate

- verify the top support floor;
- reject every claimed nonzero shifted row beyond the endpoint;
- require a nonvacuous fixed dyadic control.

### Source gate

- duplicate-free product coverage;
- exact Möbius and dyadic coefficients;
- complete collision recombination;
- all fiber cross terms.

### Frequency gate

- independent `t,s`;
- exact physical block kernel;
- no premature diagonal specialization.

### Module gate

- exact commutation for every declared fiber-linear map;
- explicit commutator for every noncommuting cutoff;
- no total-product carry operation disguised as anchor-only module linearity.

### Reserve gate

- full PSD/LMI certificate;
- strict boundary source floor;
- no pure carry-window pole cancellation;
- no hidden same-scale boundary on the right.

### Recurrence gate

- every destination below a fixed additive or multiplicative scale gap;
- total recurrence weight at most one;
- no undeclared current-scale row.

### Firewall gate

- unit-source fiber;
- bottom charges;
- parity three-tap identity;
- dyadic shell;
- `2/3` first cell;
- same-sign Möbius cube.

## 10. Automatic mutations

The checker must reject:

1. `Q>=V/2` presented as a nontrivial top dipole;
2. deletion of the identity term in either fixed dyadic inverse;
3. replacement of `omega_2*H` by rowwise `omega_2` signs;
4. omission of an `h != h'` cross term;
5. one-frequency diagonalization;
6. a carry-window reverse frame across a zeta zero;
7. deletion of the unit-source fiber;
8. a cutoff moved through convolution without a commutator;
9. a Schur reserve lifted after inversion rather than as a full LMI;
10. a recurrence coefficient sum above one;
11. loss of a Mertens mutation;
12. finite evidence promoted to RH.
