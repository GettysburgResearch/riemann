2. reproduce the fixed-notch prime-carrier asymptotic;
3. subtract each carrier as an exact source packet;
4. retain carrier cancellation until after recombination;

2. write every moving-cutoff source transition;

uniform Perron gap.  A bridge identity, a regional norm, an owner count, or a
