# M-99450 — Hostile three-gate reconstruction protocol

Review in this order.

## Gate 1 — compact scalar target

1. Recompute \(\mu(n)\) for \(1\le n\le66\).
2. Recompute every exact \(A_t\) and outward \(B_t\) interval.
3. Confirm the minimizing endpoint rule from the sign of \(A_t\).
4. Verify that \(t=13\) is the minimum and its lower bound exceeds \(7/20\).
5. Reconstruct the nested-neighborhood Hall implication.

## Gate 2 — one common child source

1. Rebuild the positive SHARP row atom.
2. Check the counterexample to raw cutoff.
3. Verify \(dM_Z=R_{Z\mid Y}dM_Y\).
4. Check pointwise \(\sum_i\alpha_iR_i<1/8\).
5. Confirm one random key gives all rows and all children.
6. Ensure the Hall bonus has no child coordinate.

## Gate 3 — signed calibration

1. Form \(A=E-P\) before any recursion.
2. Verify the same typed \(\mathsf T\) acts on \(E,P,A\).
3. Check the local identity \(E=J+E\mathsf T+(A-A\mathsf T)\).
4. Resolve the nilpotent operator and confirm exact telescoping.
5. Recheck double clamping of the canonical continuum seed.
6. Inspect the finite anchored/omission registry and its uniform fixed-row
   bound.

## Analytic endgame

1. Recompute the finite Dirichlet polynomial \(P_j(z)\).
2. Derive its large-\(j\) leading term with a controlled remainder.
3. Check that bounded calibration is Mellin-holomorphic in \(\Re s>0\).
4. Reprove Landau's real-abscissa lemma for a nonnegative Mellin density.
5. Check that no positive-real singularity remains.

Immediate falsifiers:

```text
one Hall threshold below 7/20;
one child implemented by a bare support indicator;
one row-dependent child operator;
one Hall bonus copied recursively;
one local calibration not equal to A-AT;
one nonzero canonical knot or homogeneous mode;
an unbounded anchored root ledger;
one off-line zero canceled by every P_j;
a claim that the finite replay establishes RH.
```
