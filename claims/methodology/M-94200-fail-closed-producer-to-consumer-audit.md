# M-94200 — Fail-closed producer-to-consumer audit for RH packets

Status: **METHODOLOGY**

A proposed RH composition should be rejected unless every scalar is typed at
all four levels:

1. **source:** the coefficients or positive/signed measure being spent;
2. **physical observation:** ordinary rows and transformed detail columns;
3. **arithmetic benchmark:** the exact prime or prime-power scalar paired by
   the dual;
4. **consumer:** the Mellin/zero statement that receives the bound.

For a transformed target \(\Omega=\mathcal D C\), always compute the adjoint
pairing before naming the benchmark:

\[
\langle Y,\mathcal DC\rangle
=
\langle\mathcal D^*Y,C\rangle.
\]

Do not identify a positive-capacity benchmark with a separate seed score merely
because both have the same continuum leading term.

Required hostile tests:

```text
zero-row test:
    set d=0; every claimed score/slack equality must remain true;

exact-adjoint test:
    reindex the finite sums and identify the literal arithmetic weight;

missing-gap test:
    subtract the two candidate benchmarks at the first nontrivial endpoint;

saturation test:
    if physical slack is zero, determine what arithmetic scalar remains;

pole test:
    verify that the surviving scalar still carries every hypothetical
    off-line-zero pole;

scope test:
    finite feasibility, asymptotic arithmetic cancellation, and endpoint
    consumption must be separate theorem arrows.
```

The zero-row test at \(X=3\) is decisive for PR #530.
