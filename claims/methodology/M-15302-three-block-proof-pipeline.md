# M-15302 — Proof-producing triangular three-block pipeline

## Objective

At each support, certify the exact localized Weil block

```text
R = radical-like exact packet
V = certified-zero-visible finite packet
E = ambient multiband complement
```

without separately widening every cross map.

## Gate 1 — one declared metric

Freeze positive Grams

```text
G_R, G_V, M
```

and express every matrix, interval radius, source tail, and assembly error in
those coordinates. Mixed ordinary-L2, Hardy, and coefficient norms are rejected
unless a directed Loewner adapter is supplied.

## Gate 2 — ambient complement

Certify

```text
C >= h M,   h>0.
```

The proof may come from the multiband symbol packet, packet-leverage deficit, or
another complete ambient theorem. A Ritz minimum is not an ambient floor.

## Gate 3 — visible Schur floor

Do not require `Z` to be small. Certify directly

```text
B_V - h^-1 Z* M^-1 Z >= beta G_V,  beta>0.
```

A useful two-stage route is

```text
B_V >= beta0 G_V
Z* M^-1 Z <= theta beta0 h G_V, theta<1.
```

`L-15305` may supply `beta0` from certified line-zero mass minus an absolute
zero-tail budget.

## Gate 4 — recompute the corrected radical cross

Form exactly

```text
X_tilde = X - h^-1 Z* M^-1 Y.
```

No certificate may replace this by `X` or widen the two summands independently
before subtraction.

## Gate 5 — one radical correction

Certify

```text
h^-1 Y* M^-1 Y
+ beta^-1 X_tilde* G_V^-1 X_tilde
<= kappa G_R.
```

For exact global-radical truncations, this matrix is the squared dual norm of one
external-tail functional against the triangular positive metric. The preferred
analytic proof bounds that functional directly.

## Gate 6 — radical diagonal and assembly

Certify

```text
B_R >= -e G_R
```

and one lower-floor assembly radius `delta`. The final exact output is

```text
floor >= -(e+kappa+delta).
```

## Gate 7 — cofinal ledger

For every retained support record separately:

```text
e
h
raw visible floor beta0
relative Z charge theta
Schur visible floor beta
Y dual correction
corrected-X dual correction
kappa
assembly radius delta
final epsilon=e+kappa+delta
```

The proof target is a symbolic envelope showing `epsilon -> 0`; a finite trend
is reconnaissance only.

## Immediate production packet

1. Use the first modest multiband support with a complete directed outer floor.
2. Construct the certified-zero evaluation Gram on the finite visible packet.
3. Split out a rational near-kernel and freeze the visible basis.
4. Compute the complete `V/E` Schur floor.
5. Insert one exact Gaussian/Hermite radical direction, then ranks 2, 4, 8.
6. Recompute `X_tilde`; compare its norm with the naive `X` norm to measure
   cancellation.
7. Run `X-15304` and retain every failed gate.
8. Only after a stable finite architecture is observed, prove the uniform
   growing-packet Gaussian envelope.

## Nonclaim

This methodology closes the finite algebra and trust boundary. It does not
supply the production zero-tail bound, the growing-packet estimate, or a proof
of RH.
