## 1. The exact quantity that must be bounded

With the notation of `L-9514`,

\[
\boxed{
2\int_X^{2X}|E^{\rm AN}(x)|^2dx
=\int_X^{2X}|E_\varphi(x)|^2dx
 -\int_X^{2X}x^2|f(x)|^2dx
 +\left[x|E^{\rm AN}(x)|^2\right]_X^{2X}.}
\tag{R-9504.1}
\]

The desired theorem is that the complete right side is
`O_epsilon(X^(2+epsilon))`.

## 2. Refutation of separate-asymptotic subtraction

Assume two independent estimates

\[
\int_1^X|E_\varphi(x)|^2dx
 =\beta X^3+O(R_1(X)),
\]

\[
\int_1^Xx^2|f(x)|^2dx
 =\beta X^3+O(R_2(X)).
\]

Subtracting them yields only

\[
O(R_1(X)+R_2(X)),
\]

not the correlated remainder in (R-9504.1). Unless both errors are already
`O_epsilon(X^(2+epsilon))`, this gives no critical analytic-energy bound.

In particular, an error of the form

\[
X^3\exp(-c\sqrt{\log X})
\]

or even

\[
X^{3-\delta}
\quad(0<\delta<1)
\]

is still asymptotically much larger than `X^(2+epsilon)` for small `epsilon`.
The equality of the two leading cubic constants is therefore insufficient.

The endpoint flux

\[
X|E^{\rm AN}(X)|^2
\]

is also load-bearing and cannot be discarded before it has been controlled or
combined exactly with the two bulk terms.

## 3. Refutation of direct arithmetic-to-analytic transfer

The arithmetic packet has Fourier coefficients

\[
 a_{d,h}\asymp {\mu(d)\over d h}
\]

at rational frequency `h/d`. After one Volterra integration the analytic packet
has coefficients

\[
 b_{d,h}\asymp {\mu(d)\over h^2}.
\]

Thus the divisor weight `1/d` is lost. Any dispersion theorem whose proof uses
the square summability generated by that divisor weight cannot simply be
relabelled as an analytic-part theorem.

At denominator scale `d,e asymp D`, a phase-blind Farey large sieve then pays
for frequency spacing `D^-2` on an interval of length only `D`; this is exactly
one power too large. The missing power is the Möbius-weighted near-resonance
cancellation, not a harmless integration constant.

## 4. Refutation of full-period-to-local transfer

`L-9513` proves the exact full-period Bohr bound

\[
\mathcal B_D\ll D
\]

for the truncated periodic packet. This does not imply that every physical
interval of length `D` has integral `O(D^2)`.

A trigonometric packet with frequencies separated by only `D^-2` can remain
coherent throughout an interval of length `D` while having a small full-period
mean. The common period of the rational-frequency packet is exponentially
larger than `D`. Consequently neither translation averaging nor the full-period
Jordan-square factorization controls a specified dyadic physical interval
without an additional local-to-Bohr theorem.

## 5. Refutation of entrywise near-resonance bounds

After exact resonances are extracted, the local square mean contains the signed
cluster

\[
\sum_{d,e\asymp D}\mu(d)\mu(e)
 \sum_{h,k\ne0}c_h\overline{c_k}
 \mathcal J_D(h/d-k/e).
\tag{R-9504.2}
\]

Taking absolute values before summing over the common Farey cell loses the
Möbius cancellation. The resulting generic large-sieve or Hilbert-inequality
bound has size one full power above the RH scale.

The same failure occurs on the prime side: the entrywise absolute balanced
semiprime ledger is exponentially larger than the final doubly centered block.

## 6. Valid remaining routes

A legitimate proof must do at least one of the following before taking absolute
values:

1. prove the Möbius-weighted common-cell estimate in `L-23002`;
2. prove the doubly centered prime Type-II recurrence of `T-15120`;
3. prove the curvature-corrected one-sided transport inequality of PR #218;
4. derive a new joint virial identity whose remainder is manifestly positive or
   contractive at scale `X^(2+epsilon)`.

Every one of these is an RH-bearing arithmetic theorem. None follows from the
separate cubic asymptotics or the existing full-period Bohr identity.

## 7. Scope

This refutation does not show that the critical estimate is false. It prevents
three incomplete arguments from being presented as its proof:

```text
same leading cubic constant -> critical difference,
Volterra integration -> unchanged dispersion bound,
full-period mean -> every critical local interval.
```

All three implications require precisely the signed near-resonance theorem that
remains open.
