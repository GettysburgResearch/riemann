Claim ID: `R-95100`  
Status: **EXACT INTERFACE FIREWALL**

Three inferences are forbidden.

1. **Internal positivity does not imply exact fragmentation.** A positive
   policy occupation may leave `(-3 tau,2 tau)` at nodes two and three.
2. **One fixed policy is not structurally safe.** The repository's finite-atomic
   resonance theorem and the continuum symbol of fixed branching laws retain
   reciprocal-zeta modes. Large finite positivity is not a cofinal sign proof.
3. **The annular-SHARP candidate is not imported as a theorem.** PR #535's
   finite checker verifies triangular inverse values, but it does not replay the
   claimed four-block Peano identity; the cut-block domain and edge
   coefficients in the load-bearing display are not supplied as an executable
   or line-by-line derivation.

The terminal-control bank repairs the first interface exactly. It does not
repair the second or prove the independent dyadic root-scalar bound.
