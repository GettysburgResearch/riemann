# R-105340 — Separate shifted-family explicit formulae are unnecessary

The previous frontier demanded a uniform explicit formula for each of
`xi'-alpha xi` and `xi'+alpha xi`, followed by differentiation. This is
strictly stronger than needed and introduces avoidable branch, seam, and common
factor obligations.

The quotient `(xi'-alpha xi)/(xi'+alpha xi)` already carries the exact oriented
divisor. Its alpha tangent is `-2 xi/xi'`, common factors cancel, and the
functional equation folds both safe lines. The surviving task is the direct
Wick-preconditioned safe-line trace/HS estimate, not shifted-family existence.
