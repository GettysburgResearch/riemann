realization, every Herglotz function has a DtN realization, or every positive
de Branges kernel has a canonical system. Those theorems begin after the target
positivity is known.

The proof must establish the source-ordered optical identity before invoking
abstract realization theory.

stationarity.

positive realization; the global tilted boundary identification remains
