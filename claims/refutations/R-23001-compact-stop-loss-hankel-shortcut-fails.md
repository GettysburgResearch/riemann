# R-23001 — The compact stop-loss Hankel shortcut fails

Claim ID: `R-23001`  
Title: The exact compact Selberg adjoint for a dyadic stop-loss row cannot have a positive Hankel kernel  
Status: **PROVED SCOPE CORRECTION**  
Authoring agent: `gpt56-pro-09-o`  
Created: 2026-08-07  
Dependencies: `L-20213`; `L-23001`; elementary endpoint asymptotics  
Scope: the proposed row-wise closure of the dyadic transport margin by one compact positive-Hankel adjoint

## 1. The tempting shortcut

The dyadic prime ramp is generated by

\[
 k_T(y)=(T-y)_+.
 \tag{R-23001.1}
\]

With the centered Selberg convention of `L-20213`, a lower bound for the prime
row would follow from a test `f_T` satisfying

\[
 \mathscr L^*f_T=-k_T
 \tag{R-23001.2}
\]

and

\[
 f_T(x+y)\succeq0
 \tag{R-23001.3}
\]

as a Hankel kernel. The natural first attempt is the unique solution supported
in `[0,T]`.

That attempt is impossible.

## 2. Exact compact adjoint solution

For a test supported in `[0,T]`, put

\[
 J(y)=\int_y^T e^{u/2}f(u)\,du.
\]

The equation

\[
 \mathscr L^*f=k
\]

is equivalent on `0<y<T` to

\[
 -yJ'(y)+2J(y)=e^{y/2}k(y),
 \qquad J(T)=0.
 \tag{R-23001.4}
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 J(y)=y^2\int_y^T{e^{u/2}k(u)\over u^3}\,du,}
 \tag{R-23001.5}
\]

and

\[
 \boxed{
 f(y)={k(y)\over y}
 -2ye^{-y/2}\int_y^T{e^{u/2}k(u)\over u^3}\,du.}
 \tag{R-23001.6}
\]

This is the formula already implicit in `L-20213`.

## 3. The sign needed for a lower bound is impossible

Set

\[
 k=-k_T.
\]

As `y` approaches `T` from below,

\[
 \int_y^T{e^{u/2}(T-u)\over u^3}\,du
 =O((T-y)^2).
\]

Therefore (R-23001.6) gives

\[
 \boxed{
 f_T(y)=-{T-y\over T}+O((T-y)^2)<0}
 \tag{R-23001.7}
\]

for all `y<T` sufficiently close to `T`.

But positivity of the Hankel kernel requires every diagonal value to be
nonnegative:

\[
 f_T(2x)\ge0.
\]

Choose `x<T/2` with `2x` in the interval where (R-23001.7) is negative. This
contradicts (R-23001.3).

Thus

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{the exact compact adjoint for }-k_T
 \text{ is not positive Hankel}.}
 \tag{R-23001.8}

## 4. Stronger analytic obstruction

A continuous positive-Hankel function on the complete half-line is exponentially
convex and admits a bilateral/Laplace representation by a positive measure on
its domain of convergence. In particular it is real analytic in the interior.
A nonzero such function cannot have a compact right-hand support.

Hence the failure is structural, not a defect of the explicit solution
(R-23001.6): no nonzero compactly supported positive-Hankel test can solve an
exact stop-loss adjoint equation on the whole half-line.

## 5. What survives

The failure does not invalidate the Selberg–Hankel programme. It forces one of
the following genuinely global replacements:

1. use the noncompact completely monotone exponential resolvents of `L-23001`;
2. synthesize the stop-loss row by a controlled **signed** exponential measure;
3. work blockwise so positive reserve from one row pays a neighboring negative
   exponential coefficient;
4. prove the two-sided prime-pair/Carleson estimate of PRs #216/#222/#224;
5. use a matrix-valued reflected correlation kernel rather than the analytic
   convolution square.

## 6. Consequence for a proposed RH proof

The line

```text
solve the compact stop-loss adjoint
+ invoke Hankel positivity
=> dyadic prime-row lower bound
```

must not appear in a proof of RH.

The exact remaining theorem is a **critical correlation theorem**, not a missing
ODE calculation. It is stated in `L-23002`.

## 7. Proof boundary

All formulas and the endpoint sign contradiction are exact. No zeta-zero
hypothesis, asymptotic prime theorem, or floating computation is involved.
