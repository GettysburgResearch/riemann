Claim ID: `R-99100`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT TWO-ATOM FIREWALL**

Let the parent have two atoms,

\[
 P=\delta_a+\delta_b.
\]

Put

\[
 D_1=\frac35\delta_a,
 \qquad
 D_2=\frac35\delta_a.
\]

\[
 D_1(\Omega)+D_2(\Omega)=\frac65\le2=P(\Omega).
\]

\[
 (D_1+D_2)(\{a\})=\frac65>P(\{a\})=1.
\]

Thus no disjoint carving of one parent can realize both children. Global mass compatibility and separately valid marginals are insufficient; the exact condition is the pointwise Radon--Nikodym bound in `L-99100.1`.
