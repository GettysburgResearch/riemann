# R-90601 — Raw Brownian cofinal half-plane stability fails by high-frequency vertical limits

Claim ID: `R-90601`  
Status: **REFUTATION — DEPENDS ON PROPOSED-COMPLETE THEOREM L-90601**  
Created: 2026-08-11  
Target refuted: the cofinal/all-large raw Brownian stability target of PR #343

## 1. Refuted statement

PR #343 reduces its route to the existence of an unbounded sequence `N_j`—more strongly, all sufficiently large `N`—such that

\[
 \mathbb E[Q_{N_j}^z]\ne0
 \qquad(\operatorname{Re}z>1/4).
 \tag{R-90601.1}
\]

By the exact formula of `L-34003`, the non-elementary zero set is that of

\[
 H_N(z)=\sum_{n=1}^NC_{N,n}n^{-2z}(z+\alpha_{N,n}).
 \tag{R-90601.2}
\]

## 2. Refutation

`L-90601` proves that for every fixed

\[
 1/4<\sigma<1/2
\]

and every sufficiently large `N`, `H_N` has infinitely many zeros whose real parts tend to `sigma` and whose imaginary parts tend to infinity. Choosing, for example, `sigma=3/8`, every sufficiently large `N` has infinitely many zeros in `Re z>1/4`.

Therefore:

\[
 \boxed{\text{There is no unbounded stable subsequence }N_j.}
 \tag{R-90601.3}
\]

The all-large and cofinal raw Brownian stability antecedents are both false.

## 3. Why finite-height scans missed the obstruction

The obstruction is generated by Bohr/Kronecker vertical limits of the leading Dirichlet polynomial

\[
 F_N(z)=\sum_{n=1}^NC_{N,n}n^{-2z}.
\]

For each fixed `N`, the violating zeros occur at unbounded imaginary heights. Local-uniform convergence

\[
 m_N(s)\longrightarrow2\xi(s)
\]

on every bounded set remains true and is not contradicted. A scan through any fixed height can therefore look perfectly stable while the same finite approximant has infinitely many violating zeros much higher up.

A concrete diagnostic already appears at moderate height:

\[
 H_{63}(z)=0
\]

near

\[
 z=0.2508380937103903162
   +55.8235433904112608\,i.
 \tag{R-90601.4}
\]

This numerical root is not needed for the analytic refutation.

## 4. What survives

Retained exactly:

- finite Brownian gamma construction;
- Dirichlet-average factorization;
- explicit exponential numerator `H_N`;
- reciprocal Hermite interpolation;
- serial-exponential recurrence and forced negative-integer zeros;
- minimum/order-statistic representation;
- local-uniform convergence to `2 xi`;
- fixed-zero displacement asymptotic `L-90602`.

Refuted:

- all-`N` right-half-plane stability;
- all-large-`N` stability;
- stability on any unbounded subsequence;
- the raw Brownian route from such stability to RH.

Unaffected:

- the symmetrized Brownian/Robin programme, whose proposed endpoint is a different aggregate canonical-system theorem;
- the Riemann Hypothesis itself.

## 5. Corrected frontier

The raw one-sided approximants remain useful as locally convergent analytic models, but not as globally stable finite approximants. Any future Brownian route must either:

1. change the producer so that its finite Dirichlet numerator has no Bohr-torus zeros in the RH-facing half-plane;
2. impose a height-dependent truncation and prove a zero-free region only below that height, with a diagonal Rouché scheme covering all xi zeros;
3. return to the symmetrized/canonical-system route.

The original cofinal stability target should be marked `FALSE`, not `OPEN / RH-BEARING`.
