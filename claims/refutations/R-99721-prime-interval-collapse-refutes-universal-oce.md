# R-99721 — A live prime interval refutes every universal branchwise OCE estimate

Claim ID: `R-99721`  
Status: **PROVED UNCONDITIONAL ASYMPTOTIC SEPARATOR**  
Created: 2026-08-20  
Depends on: PR #659 compact ratio-eight kernel  
RH status: **not assumed**

Let `K` be the compact kernel of `L-99722`. On `1<=y<2`,

\[
K(y)=4\sqrt y-3\ge1.
\]

For a large real `X`, let

\[
\mathcal P_X=\{p\text{ prime}:X/2<p\le X\}
\]

and work in the free labelled subset space generated by these genuine rough
primes. Put

\[
F_X=\prod_{p\in\mathcal P_X}(I-p^{-1/2}U_p)e_\varnothing
\]

and define the physical evaluation

\[
O_X(e_A)=K\!\left(X/\prod_{p\in A}p\right).
\]

If `X>16`, the empty subset is inactive because `K(X)=0`. Every subset of
size at least two is inactive because

\[
\prod_{p\in A}p>X^2/4>X.
\]

Only singleton subsets survive, and therefore

\[
\boxed{
O_X(F_X)
=-\sum_{X/2<p\le X}\frac1{\sqrt p}K(X/p).
}
\tag{R-99721.1}
\]

All summands have the same sign and magnitude at least `X^(-1/2)`. Hence

\[
|O_X(F_X)|
\ge\frac{\pi(X)-\pi(X/2)}{\sqrt X}.
\tag{R-99721.2}
\]

On the other hand, orthogonality of the labelled subset atoms gives

\[
\|F_X\|^2
=\prod_{X/2<p\le X}\left(1+\frac1p\right)
=\exp(O(1/\log X)).
\tag{R-99721.3}
\]

The classical prime-number theorem, or the corresponding Chebyshev lower
bound on dyadic prime intervals, yields

\[
\pi(X)-\pi(X/2)\gg X/\log X.
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\frac{|O_X(F_X)|}{\|F_X\|}
\gg\frac{\sqrt X}{\log X}.
}
\tag{R-99721.4}
\]

Thus no universal estimate of the form

\[
|O_XF|\le X^{o(1)}\|F\|
\]

can hold for all native future-prime profiles, even after the compact
zero-safe filter and even though the labelled Euler energy is bounded.

## Exact finite witness

At `X=200`, take the 21 primes

```text
101,103,107,109,113,127,131,137,139,149,151,
157,163,167,173,179,181,191,193,197,199.
```

Then

\[
|O_{200}(F_{200})|^2\ge\frac{21^2}{200}=\frac{441}{200},
\]

while direct rational multiplication gives

\[
\|F_{200}\|^2
=\prod_{100<p\le200}\left(1+\frac1p\right)<\frac65.
\]

The squared collapse ratio is therefore larger than

\[
\frac{441/200}{6/5}=\frac{147}{80}>1.
\]

The retained verifier checks this inequality exactly.

## Binding consequence

This does not refute a source-specific estimate for the **complete** scalar
source. It proves that such an estimate must retain cancellation between
different future-prime blocks, small-prime base colours, and incomparable
squarefree cores. A branchwise contraction, a diagonal large sieve, or the
free Littlewood--Paley norm alone cannot prove OCE67.
