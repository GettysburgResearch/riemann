## Refuted statement

The first X-3901 draft recorded two no-remainder curvature negatives under the
decimal labels

```text
4709203636354.15
4709203636352.15
```

and described later positive exact-point evaluations as refutations of those
screens.

That provenance claim is false. The negative scratch values were not bound to a
committed program, exact decimal grid, or semantic digest. Re-evaluating the
listed decimal ordinates with PR #44's committed `approximate_critical_curvature`
implementation gives large **positive** values, not the stored negatives.

## Reproduction audit

A deterministic moment-corrected nonuniform FFT was built from the same
Riemann--Siegel main sum. Its exact decimal parameters are

```text
center = 4709203636353.6309
spacing = 0.01
points = 65536
signed offsets = -327.68 .. 327.67
Taylor order = 18
```

The builder reduces the high-precision center phase modulo `2*pi` before any
binary64 cast. It computes the logarithmic moments needed for `Z`, `Z'`, and
`Z''` in one frequency build. Its nomination-level Taylor remainders for the
three raw moment sums are approximately

```text
3.92e-10
4.57e-9
5.49e-8.
```

The minimum no-remainder curvature on the whole grid is positive:

```text
+30.742555369264693
```

near offset `+0.01`. Direct entry-by-entry reconstruction at the exact decimal
point gives approximately

```text
+30.742489343830005.
```

Additional direct comparisons throughout the window agree away from the
expected loss of relative accuracy when the no-remainder Hardy sum is very
small.

## Root-cause boundary

The original scratch implementation and exact binary inputs were not retained,
so one specific coding fault cannot be proved. Plausible causes include stale
scratch formulas, binary64 ordinate construction at a scale with roughly
`0.001` ulp, and high-phase reduction before semantic serialization. The lack of
a frozen artifact is itself the decisive failure.

The later positive simultaneous-jet values remain ordinary point controls, but
they must not be described as escalations of these two nonreproducible negative
rows.

## Consequence

- The two curvature rows are withdrawn.
- They never received a candidate identifier and support no mathematical claim.
- O-3901 is corrected to distinguish reproducible Pick/moment precision
  artifacts from this provenance failure.
- Future scans must serialize exact decimal or dyadic inputs before evaluation
  and regenerate every retained row from committed code.

## Adversarial rule

A floating anomaly without all of the following is not a retained experiment:

1. exact serialized inputs;
2. committed source and dependency fingerprint;
3. deterministic rerun command;
4. semantic output digest;
5. successful exact-point replay of the same input.
