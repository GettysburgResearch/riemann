# R-105410 — Finite Hodge sign does not bound the cofinal physical trace

Claim ID: `R-105410`

Status: **PROVED EXACT SCOPE FIREWALL**

A positive-semidefinite Gram kernel controls the sign of the quadratic form,
not its size. The two-point matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}1&1-\varepsilon\\1-\varepsilon&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

is positive definite for every `0<epsilon<2`, while its value on `(1,1)` tends
to `4`, not to zero. Repeating the same block `N` times makes the positive form
linear in `N` without violating positivity or the primitive signature.

Therefore the finite Hodge-index theorem and the positivity of
`mathcal K_T` do not imply the subpower bound in `F1ATO105405`.

Likewise, estimating the two primitive coordinates on different source
partitions is invalid: their carrier and owner transfers cancel only on the
common literal source ledger.

The arithmetic near-collision estimate is a genuine cofinal theorem, not an
omitted corollary of Hodge–Riemann.
