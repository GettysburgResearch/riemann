## 2. Linear-time Möbius source factorization

The direct multiple-Möbius sum in (R-32706.2) factors exactly. Put

\[
 M_{1/2}(Y)=\sum_{k\le Y}{\mu(k)\over\sqrt k},
\qquad
 L_{1/2}(Y)=\sum_{k\le Y}{\mu(k)\log k\over\sqrt k}.
\tag{R-32706.4}
\]

Then, with `Y=floor(X/m)`,

\[
\boxed{
U_X(m)
={1\over\sqrt m}
\left[
 \log{X\over m}\,M_{1/2}(Y)-L_{1/2}(Y)
\right].}
\tag{R-32706.5}
\]

Thus the selected row `n=43` needs Möbius data only through

\[
\left\lfloor{X\over43}\right\rfloor=697,674,
\]

followed by one `O(X)` four-child recurrence. There is no `O(X log X)` multiples transform in the verifier.
