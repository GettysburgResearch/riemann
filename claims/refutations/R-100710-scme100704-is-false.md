# R-100710 — `SCME100704` is false

Claim ID: `R-100710`  
Status: **REFUTATION**  
Depends on: `L-100710`  
RH status: **not assumed**

`SCME100704` asserts

\[
\int_{2^L}^{2^{L+1}}|G_{\rm sh}(X)|^2{dX\over X}=2^{o(L)}.
\]

By `L-100710`, for all sufficiently large `X`,

\[
|G_{\rm sh}(X)|
\ge {\kappa_0\over2}{\sqrt X\over\log X},
\qquad
\kappa_0=8\log2(1-2^{-1/2})^2>0.
\]

Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{2^L}^{2^{L+1}}|G_{\rm sh}(X)|^2{dX\over X}
&\ge {\kappa_0^2\over4}
 \int_{2^L}^{2^{L+1}}{dX\over\log^2X}\\
&\gg {2^L\over L^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]

In fact the asymptotic in `L-100710` gives

\[
\boxed{
\int_{2^L}^{2^{L+1}}|G_{\rm sh}(X)|^2{dX\over X}
\sim {\kappa_0^2\over(\log2)^2}{2^L\over L^2}.
}
\]

This is not `2^(o(L))`. Hence

\[
\boxed{\mathrm{SCME100704}\text{ is false}.}
\]

The counterexample is the literal singleton-prime part of the submitted short
sector; no mutation of the hypotheses is involved.
