## 1. Statement

Put

\[
 L=\log R,
 \qquad
 H_R=\lfloor cL^2\rfloor,
 \qquad
 N_R\le C L^2,
 \qquad
 t_k=\frac{2\pi k}{\ell_R},
 \qquad
 \ell_R\asymp L.
 \tag{R-19843.1}
\]

Let `mathcal P_R` be the signed low prolate source packet generated by the even
modes of indices at most `2H_R`, with the two exact source constraints imposed.
Let

\[
 \mathscr S_R
 =P_{N_R}\Sigma_{\mu_R}E\bigm|_{\mathcal P_R}.
 \tag{R-19843.2}
\]

Then, for every fixed `M>0`,

\[
 \boxed{
 \sigma_{\min}(\mathscr S_R)
 \le C_MR^{-M}}
 \tag{R-19843.3}
\]

for all sufficiently large `R`, in the ordinary low-prolate coefficient metric
and finite Fourier coefficient metric.

The same conclusion holds after multiplying the sampled Mellin values by zeta,
because on the relevant ordinate window

\[
 |t_k|=O(L)
 \tag{R-19843.4}
\]

and the unconditional convexity bound is only polynomial in `L`.

Consequently the natural low-prolate frame cannot satisfy

\[
 \sigma_{\min}\ge(\log R)^{-C},
 \qquad
 \sigma_{\min}\ge R^{-C_0},
 \quad\text{or even}\quad
 \sigma_{\min}\ge R^{-o(1)}.
 \tag{R-19843.5}
\]

This is independent of exact zeta-cycle resonances. It is a bandwidth mismatch
between a degree-`Theta(L^2)` Mellin--Hermite space and an ordinate window of
width `Theta(L)`.

## 2. Exact Hermite Mellin model

Let `h_(2n)` be the normalized even Hermite functions. Mellin Plancherel and the
classical Hermite integral give

\[
 \mathcal M h_{2n}(t)
 =c_n\,
 \Gamma\!\left(\frac14+\frac{it}{2}\right)
 P_n^{(1/4)}\!\left(\frac t2;\frac\pi2\right),
 \tag{R-19843.6}
\]

where `P_n^(1/4)` is the Meixner--Pollaczek polynomial of degree `n` and the
constants `c_n` make the functions orthonormal in `L2(dt)`.

Therefore the Mellin image of

\[
 \operatorname{span}\{h_0,h_2,\ldots,h_{2(H-1)}\}
 \tag{R-19843.7}
\]

is exactly

\[
 \Gamma(1/4+it/2)\,\mathbb C_{H-1}[t],
 \tag{R-19843.8}
\]

with the gamma-weighted `L2` norm. Stirling gives

\[
 \left|\Gamma\!\left(\frac14+\frac{it}{2}\right)\right|^2
 \asymp(1+|t|)^{-1/2}e^{-\pi|t|/2}.
 \tag{R-19843.9}
\]

Both Fourier-sign sectors correspond to the parity of the polynomial degree;
the complete signed packet contains all degrees.

## 3. A polynomial invisible on the sampled window

Let

\[
 A=CL,
 \qquad
 m=\left\lfloor\frac{H-3}{2}\right\rfloor,
 \qquad
 Q_H(t)=(t^2-A^2)^m.
 \tag{R-19843.10}
\]

Its degree is at most `H-3`. On `|t|<=A`,

\[
 |Q_H(t)|\le A^{2m}.
 \tag{R-19843.11}
\]

For

\[
 t_0=\frac{8m}{\pi},
 \tag{R-19843.12}
\]

one has `t_0 asymp H asymp L^2` and `t_0>=2A`. Integrating the weighted norm on
`[t_0,t_0+1]` and using (R-19843.9),

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|\Gamma Q_H\|_2^2
 &\ge
 c\,(t_0^2-A^2)^{2m}
 t_0^{-1/2}e^{-\pi(t_0+1)/2}\\
 &\ge
 \exp\{4m\log t_0-O(m)\}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{R-19843.13}
\]

while

\[
 \sup_{|t|\le A}
 |\Gamma(1/4+it/2)Q_H(t)|^2
 \le\exp\{4m\log A+O(L)\}.
 \tag{R-19843.14}
\]

Since `t_0/A asymp L`, normalization gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{|t|\le A}
 \frac{|\Gamma(1/4+it/2)Q_H(t)|}
      {\|\Gamma Q_H\|_2}
 \le\exp\{-cH\log L\}.}
 \tag{R-19843.15}
\]

There are only `O(H)` sample points. Hence the discrete sample norm of the
normalized Hermite combination is also

\[
 \le\exp\{-cH\log L\}.
 \tag{R-19843.16}
\]

## 4. Imposing the two exact source constraints

The preceding polynomial has three unused top-degree slots. Consider

\[
 Q_H^{(r)}(t)=t^r(t^2-A^2)^m,
 \qquad r=0,1,2.
 \tag{R-19843.17}
\]

All three obey (R-19843.15), with a change only in the constant. The two source
constraints are two linear functionals on their inverse Mellin transforms.
There is a nonzero linear combination of the three lying in the common kernel.
After normalization, the constrained vector still satisfies

\[
 \left(
 \sum_{|k|\le N_R}|\mathcal Mh(t_k)|^2
 \right)^{1/2}
 \le\exp\{-cH\log L\}.
 \tag{R-19843.18}
\]

If one constraint degenerates on this three-space, the kernel only becomes
larger. Real and signed-sector bases are obtained by taking real/imaginary and
even/odd degree combinations.

## 5. Transfer from Hermite to low prolate modes

For every fixed `J`, the low prolate angular operator is a regular perturbation
of the harmonic oscillator in the window `n<=CL^2`. Rayleigh--Schrodinger
iteration, or the all-orders Fuchs/Dunster expansion, gives a coefficient-space
isomorphism

\[
 U_{R,J}:
 \operatorname{span}\{h_0,h_2,\ldots,h_{2(H-1)}\}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal P_R
 \tag{R-19843.19}
\]

with

\[
 \|U_{R,J}\|+\|U_{R,J}^{-1}\|\le L^{C_J}
 \tag{R-19843.20}
\]

and, in the weighted norm controlling one Mellin point evaluation on
`|t|<=CL`,

\[
 \|U_{R,J}h-h\|_{\rm Mellin,CL}
 \le C_JL^{C_J}R^{-J}\|h\|_2.
 \tag{R-19843.21}
\]

The boundary at `|x|=lambda` is exponentially farther than the
`O(sqrt H)=O(L)` oscillator window and contributes less than every fixed power
of `R`. The two exact constraints are repaired inside a fixed number of anchor
modes; (R-19843.21) is unchanged up to `L^(C_J)`.

Apply `U_(R,J)` to the constrained vector in Section 4. Equations
(R-19843.18)--(R-19843.21) give

\[
 \left(
 \sum_{|k|\le N_R}
 |\mathcal M(U_{R,J}h)(t_k)|^2
 \right)^{1/2}
 \le
 e^{-cH\log L}+C_JL^{C_J}R^{-J}.
 \tag{R-19843.22}
\]

For any prescribed `M`, choose fixed `J>M+2`; the right side is `O_M(R^-M)`.
This proves a superalgebraically small singular direction for the Mellin sample
map.

## 6. Multiplication by zeta

The finite arithmetic map has Fourier coefficients

\[
 \widehat{E(f)}(t_k)
 =\zeta(1/2-it_k)\mathcal Mf(t_k).
 \tag{R-19843.23}
\]

On `|t_k|<=CL`, the convexity bound and the fixed compact low-ordinate region
give

\[
 \max_k|\zeta(1/2-it_k)|\le L^C.
 \tag{R-19843.24}
\]

Multiplying (R-19843.22) by this polynomial factor and increasing `J` proves
(R-19843.3).

The conclusion does not depend on whether a sample lands close to a zeta zero;
zeros can only make the singular value smaller.

## 7. Consequences for the reviewed proposal

The following chain is invalid:

```text
first O(log^2 R) low prolate modes
 -> quantitatively conditioned complete arithmetic frame
 -> same tails retain d4/d6
 -> RH.
```

The first arrow is false in the natural coefficient metric. Generic finite
surjectivity cannot repair this asymptotic conditioning failure.

The exact source-frame theorem `L-19840` is not contradicted: it constructs a
different logarithmic interpolation source space. What is unavailable is an
isomorphism from that frame to the low prolate tail frame with losses smaller
than the `d_4/d_6` reserve.

## 8. Remaining viable repairs

This refutation leaves three structurally different possibilities:

1. a **hybrid frame**: retain only finitely many low prolate target modes and use
   a separate high-mode or logarithmic reservoir for the complete complement;
2. a **source-free direct operator theorem** that proves the required finite
   Weil gap without identifying every finite direction with a low prolate
   source;
3. a different target/gap hierarchy with an exponentially larger separation
   capable of paying the complete frame condition number.

Each requires a new composition theorem. None is supplied by `T-19807` or by
simply weakening polylogarithmic conditioning to `R^(o(1))`.
