# R-100300 — All supercritical shifted powers do not force the critical hinge

Claim ID: `R-100300`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT FIREWALL**  
Created: 2026-08-20

Let

\[
\nu=\delta_2-3\delta_1.
\]

For every \(a\in[0,1]\) and every real \(m\ge2\),

\[
\int(t-a)_+^m\,d\nu(t)=(2-a)^m-3(1-a)^m.
\]

For \(a<1\),

\[
\frac{2-a}{1-a}\ge2,
\]

so \((2-a)^m\ge4(1-a)^m>3(1-a)^m\). At \(a=1\) the negative term vanishes.
Thus every shifted supercritical power is positive.

But the critical linear hinge at \(a=0\) is

\[
\int t_+\,d\nu(t)=2-3=-1<0.
\]

Therefore neither the all-real \(m\ge2\) positivity theorem nor the complete
centered-Bernstein hierarchy can, by an abstract limiting, interpolation, or
moment argument, prove the linear critical sign. The prime-harmonic step must
use the literal arithmetic source.
