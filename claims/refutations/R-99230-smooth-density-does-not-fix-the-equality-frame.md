# R-99230 — Smooth-cell inverse density does not determine the equality frame

Claim ID: `R-99230`  
Status: **PROVED EXACT FIREWALL**  
Created: 2026-08-19  
RH status: **unproved**

For arbitrary constants `c,d`,

\[
V(f+c\sqrt x+dx)=Vf
\]

on every open activation cell and distributionally away from the lower
boundary. Yet the equality observable changes by

\[
c\sqrt x+dx.
\]

In particular a claimed `4sqrt(X)` score is a pure homogeneous boundary mode
and cannot be inferred from positivity of the open-cell inverse density.

A piecewise-smooth function with a derivative jump gives an independent
firewall: its smooth density may vanish identically while `d(Vf)` has a
nonzero knot atom. Both boundary modes and all knot atoms must therefore be
included in a complete proof object.
