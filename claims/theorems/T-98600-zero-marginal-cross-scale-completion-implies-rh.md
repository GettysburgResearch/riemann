\tag{T-98600.1}

\[

\tag{T-98600.2}

\[
E_v^+=\Gamma_v+U_v,
\qquad
E_v^-=\Gamma_v-U_v.
\tag{T-98600.3}

Both are nonnegative. The desired scalar sign is exactly

\[
U_v\ge0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
E_v^+\ge E_v^-.
\tag{T-98600.4}

## 3. Zero-marginal cross-scale completion barrier

Define **ZMCSCBI67** to be the following source-computable theorem.

For every sufficiently large root `X`, construct from the four-profile data on
`Q_X`:

1. Hilbert feature spaces `H_X^+` and `H_X^-`;
2. feature vectors `phi_v^plus` and `phi_v^minus` satisfying

   \[
   \|\phi_v^+\|^2=E_v^+,
   \qquad
   \|\phi_v^-\|^2=E_v^-;
   \tag{T-98600.5}
   \]

3. source-computable prime-transition operators on those features, covariant
   with the exact four-profile quotient recurrences;
4. a contraction

   \[
   W_X:\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{\phi_v^+:v\in Q_X\}
        \longrightarrow
        \overline{\operatorname{span}}\{\phi_v^-:v\in Q_X\}
   \tag{T-98600.6}

   \[
   W_X\phi_v^+=\phi_v^-
   \tag{T-98600.7}

unknown completed source, or `W_X` defined post hoc from the desired root

Assume ZMCSCBI67. By terminal positivity and (T-98600.7), backward induction on
the finite quotient DAG gives

\[
\phi_v^-=W_X\phi_v^+
\]

at every critical state, including the root. Since `W_X` is contractive,

\[
E_v^-=\|\phi_v^-\|^2

\[
\boxed{U_v\ge0}
\tag{T-98600.8}
\]

at every state and in particular at the root.

The root scalar is the exact `GPC67` observable of PR #589. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathrm{ZMCSCBI67}
\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{GPC67}
\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{RH}.

\tag{T-98600.9}
\]
