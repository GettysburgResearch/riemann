# T-99000 — Billion-endpoint rational primitive-prefix certificate

**Status:** proved by exact integer enclosure and replayable source.

Let \(C(t)\) be the primitive prefix of L-99000.  Then
\[
\boxed{C(t)\ge0\qquad(1\le t<1,000,000,001).}
\]
More sharply, for every integer \(2\le N\le10^9\),
\[
C(N)\ge\frac{1226685126915}{2^{40}}
=1.1156636236728445510379970073699951171875\ldots>0.
\]
The smallest certified lower enclosure occurs at \(N=48,433\).  At that same
endpoint the exact enclosure is
\[
\frac{1226685126915}{2^{40}}
\le C(48,433)\le
\frac{1226685458193}{2^{40}}.
\]
Since \(C\) is constant on every half-open interval \([N,N+1)\), the integer
certificate proves the all-real statement.

For each \(n\), the checker computes the exact integer
\[
q_n=\left\lfloor\frac{2^{40}}{\sqrt n}\right\rfloor
\]
by verifying
\[
q_n^2n\le2^{80}<(q_n+1)^2n
\]
with unsigned 128-bit arithmetic.  It accumulates the lower endpoint for
\(a(n)/\sqrt n\) using \(a(n)q_n\) when \(a(n)>0\) and
\(a(n)(q_n+1)\) when \(a(n)<0\).  All cumulative comparisons are signed
128-bit integer comparisons.  The Möbius values are generated by a linear
least-prime-factor sieve; its sentinel is safe because every composite below
\(10^9\) has least prime factor below \(\sqrt{10^9}<65535\).

Consequently \(\mathcal A_X\ge0\) for every real
\(1\le X<1,000,000,001\).  This is a finite theorem, not an all-scale RH
proof.
