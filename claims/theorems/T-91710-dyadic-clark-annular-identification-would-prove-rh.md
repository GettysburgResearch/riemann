\[

\[
\Lambda_j^{\rm ann}

be the crossed-zero Clark entropy of `L-91620`.

> **Dyadic Clark–Annular Identification (`DCAI_j`).** Construct a
> source-ordered isometry
>
> \[
> \mathfrak U_j:
> \mathcal I_j^{\rm arith}
> \longrightarrow
> \mathcal K_j^{\rm crit}
> \oplus
> \mathcal K_j^{\rm st}
> \oplus
> \mathcal K_j^{\rm hyp}

> \mathcal E_j

>
> \[
> \boxed{
> \mathscr L_j^{\rm arith}
> =
> \mathscr L_j^{\rm crit}
> +
> \mathscr L_j^{\rm st}.
> }
> \]
>
> Every logarithmic term must be generated by that same map.

Then the nonnegative terms

\[
\Lambda_j^{\rm ann}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathscr L_j^{\rm aux}
\]

vanish. If `DCAI_j` holds for every `j`, all horizontal dyadic annuli are
zero free and RH follows.

The theorem is weaker than full CPPD because it asks only for one radial
innovation at a time, but stronger than equality of scalar telescopes because
it preserves generation provenance.
