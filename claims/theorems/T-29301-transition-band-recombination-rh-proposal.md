\[

## Transition-band theorem

Let \(\mathcal C_{n}\) be the cone generated by:

1. balanced Pascal cycle transports;
2. squarefree collector transports preserving proper-power rows;
3. parity/factor-five transition generators.

Then

\[
\boxed{\Sigma_{X,n}\in\mathcal C_n}
\]

for every \(X,n\) implies Global First Entrance Positivity.

## Why this is stronger than GFEP

The transition source may contain negative coordinates. The theorem allows cancellation before sign evaluation. This is exactly the operation that fixed Abel kernels failed to perform.
