## 1. Typed packet cone

Let `C_X` be the positive cone generated by the paired balanced/reserve source packets and their positive restrictions at endpoint `X`.  Retain the additive positive mass `m_X` and the optimal signed packet deficit `Delta_X`, with

\[
 \Delta_X(cP)=c\Delta_X(P),
 \qquad
 \Delta_X(P+Q)\le\Delta_X(P)+\Delta_X(Q).
\tag{T-91307.1}
\]

These are the exact convex-cone properties proved in `L-91406/T-91401`.

## 2. Sole producer hypothesis

Assume there is an absolute finite-window constant `C_fin` such that every complete labelled `P_61` forcing packet in `L-91362.4` has a nonnegative ordinary/radix-four capacity-faithful row realization satisfying

\[
\boxed{
 \Delta_X(\mathbf F_{61}^{(a)})
 \le C_{\rm fin}\,
 m_X(\mathbf F_{61}^{(a)}),
}
\tag{T-91307.2
}

uniformly in `X` and in each of the two fixed channel labels.

The row, target, score, terminal collar and any common endpoint port must all be included in the same typed packet.  This is the **Complete Finite-Forcing Producer** (`CFFP`).

No producer theorem is assumed for arbitrary rough restrictions.

## 3. One exact reset

Apply `L-91362` to a packet `P_X`.  It gives

\[
 P_X=F_X+\sum_b c_bP_{Y_b},
 \qquad
 c_b>0,
 \qquad
 Y_b\le X/67,
\tag{T-91307.3}
\]

as an exact positive source decomposition with

\[
 m_X(F_X)+\sum_b c_bm_{Y_b}(P_{Y_b})=m_X(P_X).
\tag{T-91307.4}
\]

Choose a CFFP realization for `F_X` and arbitrary near-optimal child realizations.  By the exact same-index functor `L-91361`, their scaled sum is nonnegative and capacity faithful for the complete parent packet.  Scores add with the identical source coefficients.  Hence

\[
\boxed{
 \Delta_X(P_X)
 \le C_{\rm fin}m_X(F_X)
 +\sum_bc_b\Delta_{Y_b}(P_{Y_b}).
}
\tag{T-91307.5
}

No affine lift, fractional physical column, scalar-native child identification, or hidden hazard observation is used.

## 4. Packet-envelope iteration

Normalize `m_X(P_X)=1` and let `Lambda(X)` be the worst positive normalized packet deficit through scale `X`.  From (T-91307.4)--(T-91307.5),

\[

\[

\[
