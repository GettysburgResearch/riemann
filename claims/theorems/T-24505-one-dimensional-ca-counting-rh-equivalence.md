# T-24505 — RH is equivalent to one explicit CA threshold-counting inequality

Claim ID: `T-24505`  
Status: `PROPOSED — complete equivalence from Lagarias/Robin and L-24522`  
Scope: full Riemann Hypothesis  
Issue: #245

For `epsilon>0`, define

\[
Q_p(\epsilon)=\frac{p^\epsilon-p^{-1}}{p^\epsilon-1}
\]

and

\[
A_p(\epsilon)=\#\{m\ge1:p^m<Q_p(\epsilon)\}.
\]

Put

\[
X(\epsilon)=\sum_pA_p(\epsilon)\log p
\tag{T-24505.1}
\]

and

\[
M(\epsilon)=\int_\epsilon^\infty X(t)dt.
\tag{T-24505.2}
\]

At an item-slope tie, retain both adjacent values of `X`.

Let `n(epsilon)` denote the exact prime product with exponent `A_p(epsilon)`, so

\[
\log n(\epsilon)=X(\epsilon).
\]

Then RH is equivalent to the family of elementary inequalities

\[
\boxed{
\epsilon X(\epsilon)+M(\epsilon)
\le
\log\left(
\frac{H_{n(\epsilon)}+e^{H_{n(\epsilon)}}\log H_{n(\epsilon)}}
{n(\epsilon)}
\right)
}
\tag{T-24505.3}
\]

for every `epsilon>0` and both sides of every tie.

## Proof

By `L-24522`,

\[
\epsilon X(\epsilon)+M(\epsilon)
=\log\frac{\sigma(n(\epsilon))}{n(\epsilon)}.
\]

The numbers `n(epsilon)` and their tie variants are exactly the colossally
abundant numbers by `L-24521`. Robin's localization theorem, imported through
`T-24502`, says that Lagarias's inequality holds for all integers exactly when
it holds on this CA family. Therefore (T-24505.3) is equivalent to the Lagarias
inequality, and `T-24502` makes it equivalent to RH.

## Why this formulation is useful

All factorization and maximization quantifiers have disappeared. The left side
is generated by one explicit decreasing step function. The exponent threshold
is a closed inequality in `p,m,epsilon`, and the complete divisor ratio is its
layer-cake integral.

A future proof may therefore target:

1. an upper envelope for `X(epsilon)` with a signed remainder;
2. a coupled comparison between `X(epsilon)` and the harmonic term at
   `n(epsilon)`;
3. a block inequality across item-slope jumps.

No enumeration of arbitrary integers remains.

## Dependency boundary

The reduction from the CA family to every integer is the Robin theorem imported
through Lagarias. The threshold formula and layer-cake identity are native
finite algebra from `L-24521/L-24522`.

## Status boundary

This is a complete one-dimensional RH equivalence, not a proof that its
inequality holds.
