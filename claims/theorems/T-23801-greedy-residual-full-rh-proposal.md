## 2. Sole open theorem

Let `rho_X^gr` be the final residual of the greedy producer, and put

\[
\mathcal R_X^{\rm gr}
=\sum_{q=2}^X
\left(2-\frac{64}{\sqrt q}\right)_+
\rho_X^{\rm gr}(q).
\]

The only new arithmetic theorem required is:

> **GR — Greedy Residual theorem.** For every `epsilon>0`,
> \[
> \boxed{
> \mathcal R_X^{\rm gr}
> \le C_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon}
> \tag{T-23801.2}

> for all sufficiently large square endpoints `X=N^2`.

GR is a finite inequality. Its statement uses only floors, logarithms, square
roots, minima, and finite subtraction.

It is strictly weaker than Carry Saturation. If every greedy pivot is diagonal,
then the residual is zero and GR is immediate. GR also permits off-diagonal
pivots and a nonzero residual, provided their total positive potential is
subpolynomial.

## 7. Why GR is the correct final theorem

The finite Möbius decoder of `L-23802` shows that the exact triangular inverse
contains a smoothed reciprocal-zeta channel. Full coefficient positivity is
therefore a deep and possibly unnecessarily strong target.

GR asks less:

- the producer is automatically nonnegative and feasible;
- no exact inverse coefficient is assumed positive;
- only the residual visible to one sharp-leading potential is controlled;
- the allowed error is exactly the subpolynomial scale required by Landau;
- the first critical Mertens obstruction is retained inside the quotient-layer
  residual rather than hidden by an unsigned norm.

## 8. Proposed attack on GR

At a nonzero greedy step, at least one column is saturated. An off-diagonal
pivot can force later rows to vanish only when they still see that saturated
column. The final residual is therefore generated by a descending modular pivot
forest.

A proof should combine three ledgers.

### 8.1 Pivot charging

Charge each residual column to the first saturated pivot that blocked its
diagonal row. Prove that the positive potential contracts along every charge
edge, except for a bounded set of quotient-transition endpoints.

### 8.2 Complete quotient layers

On

\[
\frac{X}{r+1}<m\le\frac Xr,
\]

the exact Möbius decoder uses only `mu(1),...,mu(r)`. Recombine the entire layer
before any absolute value. The desired theorem is a block bound for the charged
residual, not termwise positivity of the Möbius summands.

### 8.3 Continuum defect

`L-23802` supplies the mass-eight continuum inverse. Compare each finite layer
with its continuum cell, using exact Euler summation. The bulk cancels in the
potential of `L-23803`; only quotient endpoints and modular holes remain. The
proposed all-layer estimate is

\[
\sum_r E_r(X)=X^{o(1)}.
\tag{T-23801.9}
\]

This proves GR.

A proof may instead establish the stronger diagonal residual-ratio invariant,
which would give exact Carry Saturation and zero residual.
