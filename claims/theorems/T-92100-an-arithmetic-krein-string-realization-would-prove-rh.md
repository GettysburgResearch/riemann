# T-92100 — An arithmetic Krein-string realization would prove RH

Claim ID: T-92100
Status: FULL CONDITIONAL RH PROPOSAL / POSITIVE STRING OPEN
RH status: unproved

Let

Z(t)=sqrt(t)/[(xi'/xi)(1/2+sqrt(t))],  t>1/4.

Construct from the declared prime Clark, paired-eta, compact dyadic/gamma, and completed archimedean source channels a positive measure nu and constants a,b>=0 such that

Z(t)=a+b t+integral_0^infinity t/(t+s) d nu(s),

with integral (1+s)^(-1) d nu(s) finite.

Equivalently, construct a positive Krein string whose Dirichlet-to-Neumann map is the safe arithmetic impedance Z.

This representation makes Z complete Bernstein. L-92100 then makes p=1/Z Stieltjes, forces every centered Xi zero onto the imaginary axis, and proves RH.

The realization must be source ordered. It may use the exact arithmetic Julia cascade, coefficient-one returned states, gamma connection, and conservative auxiliaries. It may not define nu from the unknown zero measure or from a square root of the target Weil kernel.

A finite approximation route is valid: construct positive strings Z_N converging locally uniformly to Z on the slit plane, with source-side error control. The complete-Bernstein class is closed under such limits.

Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved.
