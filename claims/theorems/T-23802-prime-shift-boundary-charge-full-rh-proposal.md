\[

The **Boundary Charge Transport theorem** asserts that these local parent routes can be refined into a global nonnegative, no-double-spend allocation of the complete negative trajectories such that, at every time `0<=t<=T`,

1. each negative node's boundary jump and subsequent interior evolution is fully charged;

3. every routed edge lands at index at most half the child index;

Equivalently, the allocation produces the pointwise certificate
