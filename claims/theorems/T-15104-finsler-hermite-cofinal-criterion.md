## Statement

Let

\[
 k_\lambda
\]

be the localized exact Hermite-radical targets from `L-15101`, and let

\[
 p_{\lambda,N}
\]

be exact finite even target vectors in the CCM/CvS special-matrix coordinates.
Assume that for a sequence

\[
 \lambda_j\to\infty,\qquad N_j\to\infty,
\]

their finite transforms satisfy

\[
 \widehat p_{\lambda_j,N_j}
 \longrightarrow \Xi
 \tag{T-15104.1}
\]

locally uniformly on `C`.

Let `Q_j` be exact finite real symmetric special matrices in the same nodes,
parity, and normalization, and normalize each target by

\[
 \eta^{\mathsf T}p_j=1.
\]

Assume every target coordinate used in the completion is nonzero. Form the exact
target-pinned pencil

\[
 T_j(c)=A_j+cB_j
\]

from `L-15107`.

If, for every sufficiently large `j`, one of the following equivalent finite
pass conditions holds,

1. a rational scalar `c_j` is produced for which

   \[
   T_j(c_j)\succeq0,\qquad
   \ker T_j(c_j)=\mathbb Rp_j;
   \tag{T-15104.2}
   \]

2. the exact restricted matrix has positive rational LDL pivots;

3. the `L-15107` definite-slope case applies, or in the indefinite case

   \[
   x^{\mathsf T}A_jx>0
   \quad
   \text{for every }
   0\ne x\perp p_j
   \text{ with }
   x^{\mathsf T}B_jx=0;
   \tag{T-15104.3}
   \]

then the Riemann Hypothesis is true.

## Proof

The scalar update preserves the finite special divided-difference form and
parity, while (T-15104.2) gives a one-dimensional even kernel generated by the
prescribed target.

The finite Connes--van Suijlekom theorem therefore proves that every zero of the
finite target transform

\[
 \widehat p_{\lambda_j,N_j}
\]

is real.

By (T-15104.1), these entire functions converge to `Xi` locally uniformly.
Hurwitz's theorem then implies that `Xi` has no nonreal zero. This is the
Riemann Hypothesis.

`L-15107` proves the equivalence between the three finite pass conditions. ∎

## Why this improves the preceding positive criterion

`T-15103` supplies a transparent graph-weight sufficient condition. The present
criterion replaces that screen by the complete scalar-pencil decision:

- no natural finite ground-state identification is needed;
- no spectral gap or eigenvector residual is needed;
- negative individual graph edges are allowed;
- exact finite success is one rational LDL certificate;
- exact finite failure can carry a rational null-cone or threshold-conflict
  obstruction.

The remaining global task is now stated as the cofinal isotropic-cone inequality
(T-15104.3), together with the already explicit target-convergence gate
(T-15104.1).

## Proof boundary

This theorem is conditional. The repository has not proved:

1. a cofinal sequence satisfying (T-15104.3);
2. exact production data `Q_j,p_j` at unbounded levels;
3. the complete target-convergence gate in the final production normalization;
4. all imported CvS/CCM normalization and form-domain interfaces independently.

No finite computation, however large, may be reported as RH without these
cofinal gates.

## Production protocol

At each selected level:

1. export exact/directed `Q_j` and `p_j`;
2. normalize `eta^T p_j=1`;
3. build `A_j,B_j`;
4. use an ordinary eigensolver only to nominate `c_j`;
5. freeze `c_j` rationally;
6. replay exact rational/directed LDL on `p_j^\perp`;
7. preserve the finite transform and an exact Sturm real-root check as an
   independent audit;
8. track the target approximation in a strip norm strong enough for
   local-uniform convergence.

A proof requires a theorem making this pass cofinal, not an extrapolated trend.
