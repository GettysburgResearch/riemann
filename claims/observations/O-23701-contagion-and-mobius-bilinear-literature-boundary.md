## 1. Higher-uniformity contagion

K. Matomäki, M. Radziwiłł, X. Shao, T. Tao, and J. Teräväinen,

> *Higher uniformity of arithmetic functions in short intervals II. Almost all
> intervals*, Invent. Math. 244 (2026), 967–1091,
> DOI `10.1007/s00222-026-01408-6`, arXiv `2411.05770`.

The paper develops a type-II contagion/scaling mechanism: a structured
correlation on many nearby intervals forces compatible structure on larger or
smaller scales, with a nilsequence inverse analysis separating major and minor
arcs.  This is the closest located methodological match to `L-23702`.

The published theorem is not `BCT(K)`:

- it is an almost-all-interval statement;
- its coefficients and norms are not the exact signed packet of this proposal;
- it does not promote every approximate logarithmic prime relation to an exact
  tuple/product relation;
- it does not prove a rank bound independent of the Heath–Brown order;
- its quantitative cancellation is not, by itself, the square-root Mertens
  bound encoded by the first critical cell.

Therefore the paper is a method source, not a proof dependency that closes RH.

## 2. Multiple Möbius sums

W. D. Banks and I. E. Shparlinski,

> *Multiple sums with the Möbius function*, Q. J. Math. 77 (2026), 109–127,
> DOI `10.1093/qmath/haaf049`, arXiv `2506.08787`.

This work obtains nontrivial estimates for several multi-variable Möbius sums
and emphasizes that the corresponding binary problems remain substantially
harder.  That boundary matches the repository’s experience: terminal and
higher-dimensional free-lattice rows can be treated, while the balanced binary
correlation is the decisive obstruction.

No theorem in that paper is asserted here to imply `BCT(K)` or RH.

## 3. Why the proposed connection is genuinely different

The proposal combines three ingredients that are separate in the located
literature:

1. an exact finite inverse-zeta/Heath–Brown source manifest;
2. a Hermitian reflected square on the prime Kronecker orbit;
3. deterministic contagion ending in exact collisions, Euler lattices,
   lower-scale packets, or bounded-rank resonance faces.

The new and unproved statement is the last item, especially the absolute rank
ceiling.  It is properly classified as **POSSIBLY NOVEL FORMULATION / UNVERIFIED**,
not as an established consequence of the cited papers.

A reviewer should compare the proposed `BCT(K)` proof, if supplied, against the
