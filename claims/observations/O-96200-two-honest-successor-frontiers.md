# O-96200 — Two honest successor frontiers after hardening

Status: **PROPOSED STRATEGIC OBSERVATION**

The hardening leaves two distinct, noninterchangeable closure fronts.

## A. Declared-score affine–Volterra transport

Construct a source-owned positive row together with a declared-score functional \(\mathcal S\) satisfying
\[
\mathcal S(\text{native source})=J_\Lambda,
\qquad
\mathcal S-\mathcal H=o(\log^2X),
\]
while preserving all ordinary/detail capacities. Physical \(Y_4\) slack alone cannot provide this.

## B. Fixed-row Mellin–Landau bypass

Prove one fixed-row sign theorem \(c_X(j)\ge0\) and use the reciprocal-zeta transform of PR #542. This avoids \(J_\Lambda\), \(P_\Lambda\), and every factor-67 source interface. The finite prime-sieve positivity proof must be repaired by an explicit global cross-product transport.

These routes share the canonical row and the Volterra/Möbius source, but neither may be cited as confirmation of the other.
