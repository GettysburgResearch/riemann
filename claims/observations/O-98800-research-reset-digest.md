# O-98800 — Research-reset digest

The useful content of the earlier positive-coupling attempts is not that a Hall
edge is a complete packet.  It is the combination of three narrower facts:

1. compact target Hall is finite and source-owned;
2. the target-normalized component profiles give a nonnegative row bonus;
3. the exact native score is a positive functional of final detail use.

These facts naturally live in different categories.  Keeping them separate
removes the false score edge and avoids asking a signed analytic correction to
be positive source data.

The candidate is therefore best understood as a **typed compiler**:

```text
positive source measure + positive row-valued measure
 -> one positive row
 -> one nonnegative native capacity complement
 -> one exact positive dual price.
```

The proposal should inspire both proof and refutation work even if its
common-parent realization ultimately fails review.
