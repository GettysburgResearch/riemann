# O-91910 — Three-node determinant reduction

RH status: unproved.

For safe real nodes x1<x2<x3, set ti=xi^2, gi=F(xi)/xi, h(t)=t g(t), and k(t)=1/g(t). Direct algebra factors the determinant of H_ij=(F(xi)+F(xj))/(xi+xj) as

\[
\det H=\frac{g_1g_2g_3 V^2\,h[t_1,t_2,t_3]k[t_1,t_2,t_3]}{(x_1+x_2)^2(x_1+x_3)^2(x_2+x_3)^2},
\]

where V=(t2-t1)(t3-t1)(t3-t2). Thus the three-node sign is the product of two scalar second divided differences. This is an exact algebraic reduction; it does not prove positivity or RH.
