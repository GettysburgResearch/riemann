# O-23002 — Repair target for the reflected Selberg–Möbius proposal

Claim ID: `O-23002`  
Status: **PROPOSED RESEARCH MAP**  
Date: 2026-08-07

The reflected identity on PR #226 is the first repository construction that
produces the required Hermitian square directly from Selberg coefficient
algebra.  `R-23005` shows, however, that its terminal endpoint count does not
control the all-truncated balanced Möbius source.

A repaired full proposal must add both of the following.

## A. Coupled reflected `BTP(K)`

For every recombined balanced double-resolvent packet, prove a recurrence

\[
E_{K,\tau}(J)
 \le e^{(\varepsilon_K+o_K(1))J}
 \left[1+\max_{u\le(1-\delta)J+O_K(1)}M_K(u)\right],
\qquad \varepsilon_K\to0,
\]

or a tensor version with

\[
\varepsilon_K/(1-\kappa_K)\to0.
\]

The proof must use the reflected Selberg square before absolute values and must
retain all packet cross terms.  The aggregate scalar Selberg identity alone is
not enough.

## B. Exact first-cell decoder

Export an actual source map from the balanced proof object to

\[
M(D)-M(\lfloor2D/3\rfloor),
\]

or to a higher geometric difference together with the exact cutoff-difference
operator which creates it.  Merely increasing the identity order does not change
the exact fixed-logarithm source from `mu` to `Delta^K M`.

The likely productive coordinate is the common meromorphic quotient exposed in
`L-23007`: all finite inverse orders share one `1/zeta` principal part, while
adjacent-order differences are reciprocal-free.  A valid cross-order argument
must use the reciprocal-free differences to pay the forcing while retaining one
controlled representative of the common Möbius quotient.

This is narrower than the original `L-23002` but remains RH-bearing.
