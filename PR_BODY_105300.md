## Exact finite result

For monic `p` with squarefree derivative, let

```text
U = multiplication by p (p'')^(-1) mod p' on K[z]/(p').
```

Then the eigenvalues of `U` are exactly `p(c)/p''(c)`, `p'(c)=0`, and

```text
Tr U^r = sum_c (p(c)/p''(c))^r,

det(lambda I-U)
 = Res(p',lambda p''-p)/(n^2 Res(p',p'')).
```

For real-rooted `p`, the residues have the electrostatic form

```text
rho_c = -1 / sum_j (c-x_j)^(-2).
```

This removes the auxiliary `p''`-zero poles from the residue moment
coordinate. PR #723's cross-residue term remains an exactly equivalent contour
ledger.
