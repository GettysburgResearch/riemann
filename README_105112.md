# T-105112 selector-domain repair

This separately identified erratum repairs an overbroad or ambiguous literal
reading of the T-105109 selector-weighted quotient equalities.  Constant
modulus is available on the boundary of the exact domain used to construct
the selector.  Hence, for a compact rectifiable arc
\(E\subset\partial\Omega\),

\[
\|W_{1,*}F/F'\|_E=\tau_1/m_1(E),
\qquad
\|W_{2,*}F^2/(F'F'')\|_E=\tau_2/m_{12}(E).
\]

On an arbitrary edge, the correct statement uses the actual weight norm:
\(\|Wh\|_E\le\|W\|_E\|h\|_E\).  If the edge lies in
\(\overline\Omega\), maximum modulus gives the further upper envelope
\(\tau\|h\|_E\), not equality in general.

The exact fixture \(F=e^{z^4/4}\), \(W_*=z^2\) has unit-disk selector norm
one.  On the radius-\(1/2\) upper semicircle, the broad formula predicts
eight while the actual weighted norm is two.  On the unit upper semicircle,
the same-domain equality gives one.
