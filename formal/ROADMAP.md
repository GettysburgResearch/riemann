# Formalization roadmap

## formal-v0.1

Target a trusted conditional spine rather than an unsupported RH claim:

1. Mathlib's canonical `RiemannHypothesis` proposition.
2. Exact fixed-row and fixed `5:3` numerator algebra.
3. Native-source and normalization firewalls.
4. First-owner and half-divisor finite identities.
5. A generic or explicitly conditional Mellin-Landau consumer.
6. A theorem of the form `FixedDetectorNegativeMass -> RiemannHypothesis` with the open premise explicit.
7. Three-point Pick determinant algebra and selected exact no-go theorems.
8. Comparator and axiom audits for every headline declaration.

## Later phases

- state all 139 canonical claims precisely;
- formalize finite algebra and exact refutations;
- formalize analytic consumer infrastructure;
- formalize reviewed unconditional analytic theorems;
- formalize reviewed post-freeze deltas only after a later scientific integration;
- close the final formal RH theorem only when every open premise has a trusted proof.

Corrections receive new declarations or versioned namespaces. Historical statements are never silently strengthened.
