# Useful failed approaches and what remains viable

A counterexample to a proposed mechanism is not a counterexample to RH. A defective proof or certificate also does not automatically make its intended theorem false. The distinctions below are part of the cumulative mathematical record, not a list of reviewer findings to memorize.

## 1. Positive models need not preserve the arithmetic question

Positive floor-kernel combinations can cancel the reciprocal-zeta factor that made a detector relevant. Contracted or reweighted children can preserve locations without reproducing the native Euler coefficients. The refutation of the particular completed Euler–Beta semiprime construction does not refute a different balanced source.

A positive prime atom can even have a **negative elementary Wick observation**: at the specified safe scale and prime-2 atom the diagonal is $-1/128-\log(2)/4<0$. This refutes pointwise elementary PSD, not the sign of the complete integrated form. Pure first-chaos support similarly does not imply a contraction from the prescribed source vectors; rescaling features by two already separates those properties.

**Use the lesson:** keep a literal source identity, signed compensation and every cross term. Require an actual Gram-domination or source-transport theorem, not just positivity in a convenient model. [Current source/operator qualifications](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#operators), [native-source manifest](research/integrated/sharp_native/README.md).

## 2. Finite or local positivity does not settle a global criterion

A local simple-zero census requires complete multiplicity and endpoint accounting. It cannot by itself retire a global functional without a locality/complement theorem. A fixed positive Pick box excludes that box only. Adaptable cardinal capture does not prove positivity on the complete test space.

Fixed-degree high-carrier Fredholm positivity is asymptotically blind in the reviewed class. The Brownian Bohr obstruction rules out the named finite/cofinal producer class, not every conceivable height-dependent construction. Uniform-center heat tuning fails because displacement changes the parity/generator structure; fixed-center phase-sensitive estimates are a different target.

**Use the lesson:** state a complete domain, cofinal hypothesis or new source-specific propagation theorem. Do not extrapolate a finite ladder. [Boundary proofs](research/integrated/corrections/proof-boundary-corrections.md), [operator obstructions](research/integrated/operator_no_go/README.md), [heat scope](research/integrated/heat_hermite/README.md).

## 3. Linear-algebra shortcuts can reverse the conclusion

For $C\succ0$, the Schur correction $B-Z^*C^{-1}Z\preceq B$ cannot rescue a negative direction already in $B$. In the infinite-dimensional problem, an unbounded inverse cannot be assumed; energy continuity must be proved first. Positive Galerkin effective matrices are upper approximations, not lower certificates.

All principal minors characterize finite Hermitian PSD, but leading principal minors alone do not: $\operatorname{diag}(0,-1)$ is an exact counterexample. At the Birman–Schwinger threshold, membership in the spectrum is not necessarily an eigenvalue. Finite Farkas separation must use the dual sign matching its primal inequalities, and rational witnesses require rational-data hypotheses.

**Use the lesson:** use exact inertia/pivoted factorizations, justified energy completion and a certified lower residual bound. [Current matrix and operator statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#operators), [dual convention](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#q4).

## 4. An estimate can survive while its proposed proof fails

The wavelet Poisson identity needs the factor $(X/8)^2$ on its boundary term; the older energy has a separate proof, not the same formula. The Q4 mean-value estimate survives through a signed Hilbert argument; its unsigned $1/|m-n|$ proof does not. The Robin bounded-tail envelope survives with cap and nonempty-subtree assumptions. The fixed-$P_{61}$ bias theorem survives with replacement interval evidence, while its old $1/40$ lower factor is false.

**Use the lesson:** read the [current repaired statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md) rather than combining a corrected conclusion with an obsolete intermediate formula. Originals and replacement evidence remain available separately.

## 5. Changing parameters, measures or specialization changes the theorem

A family of individually bounded fixed-filter inverses need not control the actual varying-row inverse. Fixed-order asymptotics do not establish growing-order positivity. Periodized/counting-measure estimates do not automatically hold in the physical measure. Generic recurrence degrees and torsion-entry results do not imply persistence through every specialization or boundary exception.

Nonprincipal family control can vanish while the principal component is large. Function-field purity and local-factor agreement therefore do not supply principal-member extraction or a compatible global number-field object. In filtered complexes, higher-page differentials need lower-page zigzags; the literal higher components are not enough.

**Use the lesson:** prove the required uniformity or adapter, with its constants and exceptional cases. [Q4/filter boundaries](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#q4), [family/transfer statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#families).

## 6. Some alleged obstructions are themselves unproved

The claimed cofinal refutation of the 90-percent derivative-descent gate is not accepted: its lower inequality fails, and finite phase data do not prove the asserted limsup. The exact mesh identity and diagnostics remain useful. The native growing Poincare ladder and numerical Epstein certification are held for particular proof/rounding/coverage gaps; this does not declare every intended conclusion false.

All-deviation Gaussian tails for the literal tilted source were withdrawn in favor of the specified local-quadratic/exterior-exponential estimates. A finite threshold-density spike invalidates a pointwise asymptotic but not the separately proved integrated transform result. [Detailed corrected component account](reviews/A/FINAL_AUDIT.md), [structural and density corrections](reviews/B/REPORT.md).

## 7. Formal or numerical success must refer to the intended object

The old actual-xi formal input has incompatible removable-point values; an additional enumeration assumption excludes empty or finite off-line spectra. This is a source-fidelity defect, not a Lean-kernel contradiction or a refutation of the intended informal theorem. The corrected paper source theorem remains distinct from a compiled inhabited replacement.

Nearest-rounded output is not an outward enclosure. A checker accepting internally consistent derived data does not automatically authenticate primitive values or complete coverage. A graph node representing an inequality does not prove its missing arithmetic application.

**Use the lesson:** test nonvacuity, normalization, primitive containment and rejection behavior as separate contracts. [Formal status](FORMAL_STATUS.md), [computational evidence](COMPUTATIONS.md), [implication map](PROOF_GRAPH.md).

Exact counterexamples, source versions and correction histories are retained in the [integration/audit records](integration/README.md). A useful next contribution strengthens an unrefuted source-specific route or supplies a missing bounded certificate; it need not propose a new global architecture.
