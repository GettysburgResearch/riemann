# Scientific status

**The Riemann Hypothesis remains unproved in this project. No accepted argument proves or disproves it, and no certified Riemann-data violation is available.** The important progress is in exact arithmetic, analytic criteria, finite/source-specific positivity, computational certificates and obstructions to tempting shortcuts.

This is the cumulative interpretation of the integrated baseline. The [results catalogue](RESULTS.md) explains significance; the [current statement guide](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md) states hypotheses and proof routes. It is not a claim that every historical branch has been exhaustively checked.

## What the status labels mean

**Established at stated scope** means an integrated mathematical component retains its recorded review, including required repairs and named classical inputs. A finite theorem may hold for every input in its finite domain; an all-scale theorem may still concern a fixed source or a noncritical parameter range.

**Conditional theorem** means the implication is supported, not that its hypotheses have been established for zeta. **Certificate-backed result** includes its primitive-input, arithmetic, coverage and checker assumptions. **Active proposal or held result** is not part of accepted mathematics; a held computation is not thereby false.

## How earlier results and later corrections are combined

Earlier accepted components remain visible even when a later audit did not revisit them. Their review is **inherited**, not newly repeated. A correction controls the exact affected statement, evidence or implication; it does not erase unrelated results in the same family. Conversely, an older `VERIFIED` label cannot rehabilitate a subsequently rejected formula or certificate.

For example, the Robin reduction survives while the bounded-tail statement requires its exponent-cap and feasibility hypotheses; the fixed-$P_{61}$ theorem survives with replacement interval evidence; the two wavelet energies remain distinct; and the formal xi defect does not invalidate unrelated finite Pick algebra. These qualifications appear beside the [current statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md), not only in historical reports.

This editorial account introduces no new theorem, proof approval or stronger quantifier. A combined argument requiring a previously unreviewed bridge remains a proposal until that bridge receives its own review. Existing source-only results retain their exact source and dependency limits in the [extended research index](research/RESULTS_INDEX.md#inherited-source-results).

## Research frontier versus verification work

The mathematical frontier is [literal signed cancellation, all-order/full-domain positivity, or a source-faithful transfer that provides it](OPEN_CUTS.md). Reproducing a certificate, closing an imported-source interface, and repairing a Lean implementation are valuable but different tasks.

The [formal track](FORMAL_STATUS.md) retains an unresolved actual-xi source-fidelity problem. The old totalized function gives the wrong removable values, and its off-line enumeration excludes empty or finite spectra. The conditional paper repair does not itself complete the Lean replacement. RH-bearing arithmetic assumptions remain explicit inputs, not proved instances.

[Active branches](PROGRAMMES.md#active-research) may contain later proposed progress beyond this baseline. Their changing titles or heads do not alter the statuses above. [Integration/audit records](integration/README.md) hold exact source decisions and execution receipts; [release readiness](RELEASE_READINESS.md) separately records publication requirements. Neither repository validation nor publication readiness is a mathematical proof verdict.
