# Fixed 5:3 scalar

The single fixed scalar is

\[
W_X=5c_X(2)+3c_X(3).
\]

Its Mellin numerator is

\[
-3(2^{-z}-1)(2^{-z}-2),
\]

which is zero-free for `Re(z)>0`. Unlike the old moving-row construction, this detector is selected once and for all.

The open arithmetic theorem is one of:

\[
W_X\ge0\quad\text{eventually},
\]

or

\[
\int_1^T (W_X)_-\,\frac{dX}{X}=T^{o(1)},
\]

or a fixed nonnegative-plus-holomorphic-defect decomposition. The factorization alone proves none of these.
