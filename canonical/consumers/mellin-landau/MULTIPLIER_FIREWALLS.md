# Multiplier and source firewalls

The review wave established the following binding firewalls.

1. **Moving row.** Selecting a sufficiently large row after introducing a hypothetical zero is not one fixed detector unless the producer has already supplied all rows simultaneously.
2. **Positive floor kernel.** Möbius inversion can make a floor-kernel expression positive while its Mellin multiplier cancels the reciprocal-zeta factor.
3. **Root-containing positive squares.** A positive excess does not control an unknown signed root term that is itself RH-sensitive.
4. **Smoothing width.** A box or smoothing multiplier must be fixed and zero-free in the required half-plane.
5. **Native source.** Auxiliary RN location, full child capacity, or `p^{-1/2}` activity cannot replace the literal normalized Euler coefficient.
6. **Carrier recombination.** A cancelling physical carrier must be recombined before taking separate norms or one-sided estimates.
7. **Finite scope.** A finite positive scan cannot supply the eventual or subpower hypothesis.

These are canonical mechanism exclusions, not stylistic preferences.
