This review artifact records two exact finite-dimensional obstructions.  The
first is an exact counterexample to a displayed equality in `L-91350`.  The
second is an exact type obstruction to the claimed composition of the hidden

## 1. `L-91350.2` drops the parent activation cutoff

`L-91350` defines

\[
K_a(d;p,y)
=d^{-1/2}[a\sqrt{py/d}-3]\mathbf1_{d\le py}
-p^{-1/2}d^{-1/2}[a\sqrt{y/d}-3]\mathbf1_{d\le y}
\]

and the shifted-eight Hall margin

\[
\mathcal H_{a,t}^{(8)}(p,y)
=\sum_{e\le t+8,\,\mu(e)=1}K_a(e;p,y)
-\sum_{o\le t,\,\mu(o)=-1}K_a(o;p,y).
\]

It then introduces the **untruncated** prefixes

\[
A_t=\sum_{e\le t+8,\mu(e)=1}\frac1e
    -\sum_{o\le t,\mu(o)=-1}\frac1o,
\qquad
B_t=\sum_{e\le t+8,\mu(e)=1}\frac1{\sqrt e}
    -\sum_{o\le t,\mu(o)=-1}\frac1{\sqrt o}
\]

and asserts the exact identity

\[
\mathcal H_{a,t}^{(8)}(p,y)
=a\sqrt y\left(\sqrt p\,A_t-\frac{A_t(y)}{\sqrt p}\right)
-3B_t+\frac{3B_t(y)}{\sqrt p}.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Equation (1.1) is false whenever some source in the declared parent prefix is
larger than `py`.  The correct parent coefficients are `A_t(py)` and `B_t(py)`,
not `A_t` and `B_t`.

### Exact admissible counterexample

Take

\[
t=79,\qquad p=83,\qquad y=1,\qquad a\in\{4,5\}.
\]

These parameters satisfy all hypotheses of `L-91350`.  The positive sources

\[
85=5\cdot17,\qquad 86=2\cdot43,\qquad 87=3\cdot29
\]

are squarefree divisors of `P_79`, have Möbius sign `+1`, and lie below
`t+8=87`.  But

\[
85,86,87>py=83,
\]

so their actual contributions to `K_a` and hence to
`\mathcal H_{a,79}^{(8)}(83,1)` are exactly zero.

The right side of (1.1), however, includes all three through `A_t,B_t`.  It
therefore exceeds the true Hall margin by

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_a
=\sum_{e\in\{85,86,87\}}
\left(\frac{a\sqrt{83}}{e}-\frac3{\sqrt e}\right)
=\sum_{e\in\{85,86,87\}}
\frac{a\sqrt{83/e}-3}{\sqrt e}>0.
}
\tag{1.2}
\]

For `a=4`, positivity follows already from

\[
16\cdot83>9\cdot87;
\]

for `a=5` it is even stronger.  Thus (1.1) fails at an exact
hypothesis-matching point.

## 2. A hidden hazard child is not the `r`-scaled canonical terminal child

Let

\[
r=p^{-1/2}\in(0,1).
\]

On the canonical hidden section used by `L-91337`, the pure reserve state is

\[
I(0,1)=(0,1,0,2)^T.
\]

The least-prime hazard matrix from `L-91336` scales the two hidden modes by

\[
h^X=\frac1p=r^2,
\qquad
h^Y=\frac1{\sqrt p}=r.
\]

Hence the hazard branch is

\[
H_pI(0,1)=(0,r^2,0,2r)^T.
\]

For the positive hidden target and score functionals of `L-91337`,

\[
m_\Psi(X^+,X^-,Y^+,Y^-)=X^++Y^-,
\qquad
m_S(X^+,X^-,Y^+,Y^-)=2X^++X^-.
\]

The hazard branch therefore has

\[
\boxed{T_{\rm haz}=2r,\qquad S_{\rm haz}=r^2.}
\tag{2.1}
\]

A canonical terminal child with the coefficient `r` asserted in the direct
Euler splice has

\[
rI(0,1)=(0,r,0,2r)^T
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{T_{\rm child}=2r,\qquad S_{\rm child}=r.}
\tag{2.2}
\]

The targets agree, but the child score exceeds the complete hazard score:

\[
S_{\rm child}-S_{\rm haz}=r-r^2>0.
\]

Thus a decomposition

\[
\text{hazard branch}
=r\times\text{canonical child}
+	ext{positive score-superordinate residual}
\]

is impossible.  Its residual would have target zero and score

\[
r^2-r<0.
\]

This is the same exact obstruction retained in `R-91307`, now applied to the
composition claimed in Sections 3--7 of `T-91304`.

The direct Euler identity

\[
D_P(py)=rD_P(y)+D_{Pp}(py)
\]

is an exact identity for the complete `P_79` packet.  It does not, by itself,
identify an arbitrary pointwise hidden-hazard submeasure with an `r`-scaled
complete canonical packet.  A valid proof must either:

1. group hazard and survival pieces before applying the direct Euler split;
2. prove an exact positive map from every colored branch measure to a scalar
   canonical child plus a separately budgeted residual;
3. avoid scalar endpoint losses and formulate a measure-valued branching
   consumer for the actual restricted child packets.

No such theorem is supplied in the frozen DAG.

Disposition:

```text
hidden hazard partition                           VERIFIED
complete one-prime Euler packet split              VERIFIED
hazard branch = r canonical child + positive rest  FALSE as a literal claim
compatibility/regrouping theorem                   UNPROVEN / GAP
scalar endpoint-loss recurrence                    UNPROVEN / GAP
```

## 3. Mass fractions do not by themselves weight signed endpoint loss

Even after a positive source partition, a source-mass fraction is not
automatically a coefficient for the signed endpoint loss

\[
\mathfrak L_Y=J_\Lambda(Y)-\mathcal S_Y(d).
\]

For an exact abstract example, take a normalized two-atom packet with equal
source masses.  Let a feasible packing pay the first atom completely and leave
the second atom with positive loss `H`.  The full packet has loss `H`.  The
positive restriction to the second atom has source-mass fraction `1/2` but still
has loss `H`, not at most `H/2`.

Therefore a statement of the form

\[
\text{child source mass}=\theta\times\text{parent source mass}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{child loss}\le\theta\mathfrak L_Y
\]

requires an additional packet-homogeneity or domination theorem.  It is not a
consequence of positive disintegration alone.  This is especially important
here because `T-91101` permits signed losses and endpoint weights above one.

`T-91304` starts with pointwise functions `theta_b(n)` but later writes scalar
`theta_b` without defining a measure, normalization, or equality between the
normalized branch packet and the native packet defining `mathfrak L_(X_b)`.
The direct Euler coefficient `r` is also absent from the displayed scalar
recurrence.  Dropping an `r<1` factor is not harmless when a child loss can be
negative.

This is an independent open interface even after repairing `L-91350`.
