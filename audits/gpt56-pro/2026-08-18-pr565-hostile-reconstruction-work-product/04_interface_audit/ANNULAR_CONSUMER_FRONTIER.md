# Annular Mellin-Landau consumer frontier

The inspected candidate uses the annular difference

\[
A_X=R_X-R_{X/4},
\]

which formally contributes the Mellin factor

\[
1-4^{-s}.
\]

For `Re(s)>0`, `|4^{-s}|=4^{-Re(s)}<1`, so this factor is nonzero. The scalar numerator recorded in the recovery packet is

\[
-3(1-2^{-z})(2-2^{-z}),\qquad z=s+\tfrac12.
\]

Its factors vanish only on the real-part lines forced by `2^{-z}=1` or `2^{-z}=2`, so they do not cancel a nontrivial zeta zero in `0<Re(z)<1`.

These are durable algebraic observations. The Landau conclusion still requires a proved nonnegative producer plus all continuity, growth, Fubini, abscissa, and pole hypotheses. The pass did not complete those hypotheses into a standalone publication after the producer interface failed, so the consumer remains conditional in this archive.
