saddle and `a_m=(-S_m''(w_m))^(-1/2)`. The packet proves the uniform Gaussian
model

```text

```

on every fixed natural window `|Re z|<=C/a_m`, not only on windows satisfying
`a_m T_m->0`.

Consequences on a constant-fraction high-derivative band:

```text
all Xi^(m) zeros in the natural box are real and simple;
rho_(m,c)=Xi^(m-1)(c)/Xi^(m+1)(c)
         =-M_(m-1)/M_(m+1) (1+o(1)) uniformly;
residue coherence tends to one;
RCMV104530 holds in the entire high band with every margin <1;
reverse-Rolle transfer is 1-o(1).
```

The theorem does not descend to fixed low derivative order and does not prove
RH.
