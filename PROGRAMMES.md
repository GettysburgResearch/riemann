# Approaches and how they connect

The common problem is to control the **actual arithmetic or analytic source**, not merely a positive model resembling it. The routes below share tools, but an identity between two representations is not automatically a bound in either one. [RESULTS](RESULTS.md) states what has survived; [OPEN_CUTS](OPEN_CUTS.md) turns the missing estimates into tasks.

<a id="arithmetic"></a>
## 1. Arithmetic inequalities and signed Möbius sources

The Robin route works directly with divisor sums. Canonical reduction and finite tail bounds organize a complete search domain; the unbounded tail remains the problem. This route is distinct from proving signs of a Möbius-weighted transform.

The native-source route builds exact Euler/owner identities, fixed-prime bases, compact kernels and rough-number recursions. SHARP positivity for all real powers $m\ge2$ and the fixed-$P_{61}$ bias theorem are genuine results. Critical power one, future-completed source signs and dynamic Bellman bounds are not inherited from them.

**Connection:** suitable signed negative-mass bounds feed the fixed Mellin consumer. A finite allocation or a change of source must first be shown to preserve that exact detector. [Arithmetic statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#robin) · [native-source theorem](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#sharp).

<a id="cancellation"></a>
## 2. Harmonic analysis of the critical signed source

Compact wavelets, Q4 annularization, half-divisor decompositions, Vaughan identities and physical occupancy organize the same difficult cancellation in different coordinates. Their exact identities and diagonal estimates are useful precisely because they expose the remaining signed terms.

**Connection:** some coordinates agree modulo a proved integrable error, but only with the same kernel, cutoff and signed recombination. Periodized or counting-measure estimates do not transfer to a different physical measure without a theorem. Carry-hinge and four-band routes retain related but separately defined boundary problems. [Corrected wavelet account](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#wavelet) · [Q4 and combination tools](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#q4).

<a id="xi"></a>
## 3. Xi geometry, finite witnesses and derivative descent

Value-only xi responses lead to secants, Pick matrices, Loewner minors and finite witness basins. Low-order safe-axis positivity gives constraints on what a successful all-order argument must add; it does not settle the whole hierarchy.

High-derivative and reverse-Rolle programmes instead try to transport controlled high-order geometry back toward the original function. Finite residue/selector identities and corrected saddle regimes survive. The source-specific low-order signed transport, endpoint/winding contributions and cofinal control remain missing.

**Connection:** both use zeros and logarithmic derivatives, but arbitrary-height RH-necessary tests, safe-axis low-order positivity and high-derivative entry are different statements. Raw innerness assumptions may already contain RH and cannot be treated as cheaply available input. [Current xi statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#xi).

<a id="operators"></a>
## 4. Weil forms, heat and full-source operators

This route seeks positivity of the completed arithmetic form on a complete test class. Finite/block identities, specified cardinal capture, safe-line transforms and heat regions constrain the problem. Energy-space Schur elimination makes a finite effective form account for the entire positive infinite sector on a fixed window.

**Connection:** capture tells us a test family can detect an exceptional direction; it does not prove that no such direction exists. A rigorous continuum lower certificate is stronger than a sampled matrix, but a single window still does not control every required scale. First-Hermite work has its own complete-domain requirement. [Current operator account](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#operators).

<a id="families"></a>
## 5. L-function families and generalized structures

Dirichlet characters, function fields, GL2 twists, local trace formulas and generalized L-objects test which structural features are useful and which cannot determine the principal zeta member. Retained work includes linear Euler restoration, fixed-conductor identities, typed finite measures, local Fourier geometry, recurrence/cofactor algebra and conditional filtered-complex constructions.

**Connection:** purity in a function field, an average over a family or a nonprincipal bound does not provide the principal-member estimate. A source-faithful extraction must account for family size, weights, ramification, masks, conductor growth and every relevant cross term. Generic algebraic statements need not survive every specialization. [Representative statements](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#families).

<a id="causal"></a>
## 6. Horizon-preserving Euler corrections

The causal Euler/Dickman construction preserves the range in which a finite Euler product is exactly the arithmetic source and corrects its omitted-prime continuum contribution. Its retained norm theorem is at $\Re s=1$, on classical PNT/Mertens inputs.

**Connection:** the stronger half-plane target would be useful only for the corrected product as a whole. Positivity of a correction density and separate bounds on large factors below one do not provide that target. The all-half-plane adapter is not newly accepted by this map. [Current component](research/integrated/CURRENT_RESULTS.md#causal).

<a id="active-research"></a>
## Active research is separate from the integrated baseline

Use [open research PRs](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Aopen) and [programme issues](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/issues) to find current work. An open PR may contain a mixture of already reviewed components and later unreviewed extensions. Only the exact scopes linked from [RESULTS](RESULTS.md) belong to the integrated scientific account.

Examples of threads worth inspecting are the [xi/native atlas #765](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/765), [generalized-object work #766](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/766), [Chow/source work #769](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/769), [cross-world structures #781](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/781), [operator continuation #792](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/792), and [causal completion #793](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/793). These are discovery links, not whole-branch endorsements.

The newer [dilation-observability and harmonic endpoint proposal #805](https://github.com/GettysburgResearch/riemann/pull/805), observed at `ea66cd5b152e7960ad51ea51b154734eebc9b96a`, is **outside this integrated baseline**. Its proposed harmonic/full-energy transfers and fixed-grid criterion require independent review; its author reports that the decisive subpower estimate remains open. This is a dated discovery example, not a promise that its live head or status will remain unchanged.

Check overlapping work before starting. Publish a scoped contribution through [the existing contribution process](CONTRIBUTING.md); no new programme or review wave is required to contribute one useful result.
