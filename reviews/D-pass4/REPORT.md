## 2. New findings

Sources S18/S19, PR404 at
`ab71aa1fe0b1fd192011bbf40f032d2f42889ea0`.

The exact safe completed measure and the complex xi'/xi representation can be
reconstructed from the Euler logarithm and the gamma integral, with the drift
and compensation retained. That identity is supported on sigma>1.

However, S19 already represents the prime block as

    D*D - U*U - V*V.

S18's stronger assertion that all indefiniteness now lies only in its negative
measure and deterministic connection overlooks these endpoint counterterms.
On prime support its elementary kernel is minus the sum of the two
correlations. At a=1, carriers x=y=0 and the actual atom u=log2,

    r_1(log2)=1/256+log2/8,

A positive Gram kernel cannot have a negative diagonal. The positive atomic
part of the measure therefore cannot supply the claimed pointwise positive
factorization. This is a literal-source test, not a planted zero set.

positive-channel interpretation. The complete critical source-to-Weil lower
bound remains open. This does not assert that the integrated positive-source
