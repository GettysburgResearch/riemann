**Status:** Source-interface and algebra review, not a proof of a new zero-free region or of a lower bound for the complete inverse polynomial.

The first version's full-field zeta factorization needed a correction: for the original presentation with additional S-deleted Euler factors, it is not exactly the primitive Artin/Dedekind factorization. An independent reviewer identified the missing factors at primes in S outside the primitive conductor. The author corrected the full-field identity to use the primitive inducing character, and stated the finite S-deletion factors separately. The subsequent S-good coefficient identities remain scoped to the good Euler product.

Subject to the stated primitive/local and reciprocity conventions, independent source and arithmetic checks found no further error in the primitive ramified coefficient `-z`, the Mellin omega argument with a nonnegative phase-aligned smooth weight, the nonvanishing of the finite good-region Euler factors for `Re rho>0`, or the squarefree signed-triple factorization. The review does not validate imported analytic inputs or infer a lower bound for the full inverse from one isolated term; cancellation in the remaining terms is an explicit gap.
