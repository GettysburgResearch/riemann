## Recovered checkpoint E — one-sided Hardy tangent gap (T-105420)

`L^2(0,L0)`, zero-extended translation by `x` has numerical radius

```text
cos(pi/(ceil(L0/x)+1)).
```

A positive tangent source therefore has an explicit strict phase gap. The gap
vanishes in a two-sided/unitary model. A hard one-sided frame has dimension
`T L0/(2 pi)+O(1)`; after the T-105310 budget, support ratio
`lambda>18367/20000` is record-capable. Smooth-frame conditioning and the
