## Result

For a real entire function F, put

\[
Q_F(z)=\frac{F(z)^2}{F'(z)F''(z)}.
\]

On the counterclockwise rectangle

\[
\Omega_{T,\eta}=\{z:|\Re z|<T,\ |\Im z|<\eta\},
\]

the exact real critical-residue second moment is

\[
\boxed{
M_{2,F}(T)
=B_F(T,\eta)-C_F(T,\eta)-D_F(T,\eta),
}
\]

where:

- \(B_F=(2\pi i)^{-1}\int_{\partial\Omega}Q_F\,dz\);
- \(C_F\) is the algebraic squared-residue sum over nonreal zeros of \(F'\)
  inside the rectangle;
- \(D_F\) is the cross-residue sum over zeros of \(F''\) inside the rectangle.

Schwarz reflection expands the boundary term into

\[
B_F
=-\frac1\pi\int_{-T}^{T}\Im Q_F(x+i\eta)\,dx
+\frac1{2\pi}\int_{-\eta}^{\eta}
\left[Q_F(T+iy)-Q_F(-T+iy)\right]\,dy.
\]

If F is even or odd, then \(Q_F\) is odd and the endpoint term simplifies to
\(\pi^{-1}\int_{-\eta}^{\eta}Q_F(T+iy)\,dy\).

For \(F=\Xi^{(k-1)}\), \(k\ge1\), this is exactly the height-truncated second
moment in the draft PR #720 residue-coherence gate, conditional on the
regular-rectangle hypotheses and with every omitted term exposed. Relevant
Xi-derivative simplicity and common-zero exclusions are not asserted.

Draft PR #720 L-104523.3 already contains the individual local-circle
squared-residue formula and warns about \(F''\)-poles. L-105101's new content
is the complete finite-rectangle closure and orientation-explicit flux split.
