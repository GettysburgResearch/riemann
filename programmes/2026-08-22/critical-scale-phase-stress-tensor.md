## Mission

The latest review wave compresses most live conclusion-facing arithmetic routes into three middle interfaces:

```text
CV   critical one-sided scale variation;
XD   signed cross-core dispersion;
OCC  physical near-collision occupancy/collapse.
```

This programme will not attack them as three independent RH-equivalent criteria. It will try to prove that they are the three boundary fluxes of one fixed, source-faithful scale–phase conservation law.

## Central construction

Construct one fixed analytic mother packet `Phi` and fixed finite zero-safe operators `A(D), B(D)` such that

```text
A(D) Phi = the activation-free CV kernel;
B(D) Phi = the corrected same-K1 compact-wavelet/XD kernel.
```

Both channels must act on the same native arithmetic source, with no zero-dependent detector, moving Mellin multiplier, coefficient renormalization, or post-collapse source substitution.

In logarithmic scale `u=log X`, introduce the phase lift

```text
F(u,theta)
 = sum_n beta(n) n^(-1/2)
   Phi(u-log n) exp(-i theta log n).
```

The target is an exact source-level stress-energy identity

```text
partial_u E + partial_theta J
 = -Q_bulk + R_source,
```

where `Q_bulk >= 0` and `R_source` consists only of explicit source-owned activation, collar, carrier, and finite-boundary terms.

Interpretation:

```text
scale flux       -> CV;
phase flux       -> XD;
mixed flux       -> OCC.
```

The identity must be established before regional absolute values and before labelled products collapse to physical integer products.

## Work packages

### 1. Common multiplier and mother kernel

Compute the exact Mellin multipliers of the controlling CV and same-`K1` XD kernels. Find their minimal common zero-safe factorization

```text
m_CV = A m_Phi,
m_XD = B m_Phi,
```

with finite, fixed, subpower-invertible `A,B`. A pole, zero, or critical-mass inverse in this step is an immediate redesign trigger.

### 2. Native source and exact carrier quotient

Lift the sequential/least–greatest-owner source before physical collapse. Keep accumulated parity, the two labelled copies of 67, and every moving-cutoff transfer atom. Remove deterministic prime or homogeneous carriers only by exact source partitions and retain the compensating coordinate until recombination.

### 3. Local stress tensor

Construct

```text
T = [[|partial_u F|^2,
      Re(partial_u F conjugate(partial_theta F))],
     [same,
      |partial_theta F|^2]].
```

Derive its divergence and identify every boundary term. The desired local theorem is a strict determinant reserve, not merely positivity of the two diagonal entries.

### 4. Arithmetic region assignment

Use the existing library by region:

```text
small primes          finite Euler squaring;
mesoscopic products   Dickman/Stieltjes/Bellman corridors;
balanced coprime      Vaughan/half-divisor/Gram coordinates;
activation knots      Volterra/Farkas identities;
finite terminal range retained exact finite certificates only.
```

Every estimate must retain one source normalization and one-use ownership.

### 5. Strict Perron contraction

On logarithmic blocks, derive

```text
[V_(j+1)]   [a_j b_j] [V_j]   [epsV_j]
[D_(j+1)] <=[c_j d_j] [D_j] + [epsD_j]
```

with summable/subpower error and uniformly

```text
a_j < 1,
d_j < 1,
b_j c_j < (1-a_j)(1-d_j).
```

The strict determinant inequality is the conclusion-producing theorem. It must come from a source-owned mixed reserve, not from spending one positive reserve in both diagonal estimates.

### 6. Fixed analytic consumer

Feed the result into one detector fixed in advance: preferably the fixed `5:3` scalar, with simultaneous rows `2,3` as an independent audit. No row or scalar may be chosen after a hypothetical zero.

- `p^-1/2` where the normalized source requires `p^-1`;
- a detector selected after a hypothetical zero;

- a positive square retaining the unknown root term;

- omission of moving-cutoff source atoms;
- reuse of one reserve in both matrix diagonals;
- finite computation promoted to a global tail theorem.
