# Programme — Xi Reverse–Rolle Defect Cascade

**Status:** research programme specification only  
**Base:** `main@677203992eb0168920365ee45ae9db76bfa97dcf`  
**Review context:** actual-Xi Pick/Loewner review in PRs #708 and #710  
**RH status:** unproved

## Mission

Turn Levinson’s reverse-Rolle intuition into a local, weighted theorem for the Riemann Xi function.

A global statement such as “almost all zeros of a derivative lie on the critical line” is not sufficient: a derivative may have only real zeros while its parent has none. The missing theorem must measure the extra critical points that are not generated by crossings of the preceding derivative and must transport complex off-line defect down the derivative ladder.

## Core objects

Write

```text
Xi(t) = xi(1/2 + i t),
F_k(t) = Xi^(k)(t).
```

Define the Laguerre defect

```text
L_k(t) = F_(k+1)(t)^2 - F_k(t) F_(k+2)(t).
```

At a real zero of `F_(k+1)`,

```text
L_k(t) = -F_k(t)F_(k+2)(t).
```

A zero of `F_(k+1)` with `L_k(t)<0` is an extra positive minimum or negative maximum rather than the Rolle zero forced between consecutive zeros of `F_k`.

Let `E_k(I)` count these extra extrema, with multiplicity and explicit boundary conventions.

## First target — exact real reverse-Rolle identity

Prove an exact interval theorem of the form

```text
N_0(F_k; I)
 >= N_0(F_(k+1); I)
    - E_k(I)
    - explicit boundary/multiplicity terms.
```

The theorem must be an equality or sharp inequality after classifying:

```text
Rolle-generated derivative zeros;
extra extrema measured by L_k<0;
multiple zeros;
interval-boundary events.
```

This is purely real-variable and should be completed before any density input is used.

## Second target — complex defect transport

Define a local weighted off-line defect `D_k(T)` for zeros of the entire function corresponding to `F_k`, penalizing horizontal displacement from the critical line and retaining multiplicity.

Seek an Xi-specific inequality

```text
D_k(T) + E_k(T)
 <= C D_(k+1)(T+Delta)
    + explicit boundary error.
```

A source-free theorem of this form is false for general polynomials. Its proof must use Xi-specific structure:

```text
functional equation and reality on the line;
Cartwright/order-one growth;
Hadamard product and zero symmetry;
Pick/Loewner or Laguerre-minor positivity;
quantified control of boundary argument variation.
```

## Third target — connect to actual-Xi Pick minors

The matrices reviewed in PRs #708/#710 should be reinterpreted as finite pieces of the Laguerre-defect hierarchy.

Tasks:

1. Express `L_k` and its higher analogues as confluent Pick/Loewner/Hankel minors.
2. Determine exactly what the verified actual-Xi order-three PSD theorem controls in the reverse-Rolle ledger.
3. Identify the first new determinant needed for one downward derivative step.
4. Prevent reuse of one critical-orbit reserve across derivative levels.

The objective is not to infer all orders from order three, but to extract a local defect-transfer mechanism that can be iterated with quantified loss.

## Fourth target — growing-order derivative concentration

Reconstruct the Levinson–Conrey derivative-zero method with uniformity when the derivative order grows with height.

The desired range is initially

```text
k <= c log T / log log T.
```

The output must be stronger than a proportion:

```text
weighted off-line defect of Xi^(k) below T < 1,
```

or an equivalent statement forcing no off-line zero in the relevant region.

Every Vandermonde, zero-count, mollifier, threshold, and order-height constant must be explicit. Fixed-order asymptotics do not suffice.

## Fifth target — descend from high derivative to Xi

Choose `k=k(T)` so that the high derivative has zero off-line defect below height `T`. Apply the local reverse-Rolle transport successively:

```text
Xi^(k) -> Xi^(k-1) -> ... -> Xi.
```

Prove that the accumulated `E_j`, boundary, multiplicity, and growth losses remain below one. Then Xi has no off-line zero below `T`; letting `T` grow would yield RH.

## Required deliverables

1. Exact real reverse-Rolle counting theorem.
2. Polynomial and entire-function mutation suite showing the hypotheses are necessary.
3. Xi-specific complex defect measure and transport inequality.
4. Dictionary between Laguerre defects and actual-Xi Pick/Loewner minors.
5. Uniform growing-order derivative-zero theorem.
6. Quantified downward cascade with a closed error ledger.
7. Fixed-height computational fixtures only as diagnostics, never as the global proof.

## Immediate falsifiers

Reject or redesign at the first occurrence of:

- replacing a local defect by a global percentage;
- assuming a converse to Rolle for arbitrary real entire functions;
- ignoring multiple zeros or interval boundaries;
- using a fixed derivative order to control arbitrarily high zeros;
- extrapolating Pick order three to all orders without a recurrence;
- changing the derivative order after observing a hypothetical zero without uniform estimates;
- spending one verified critical-orbit reserve at several cascade levels;
- using a numerical zero census as the tail theorem.

## Success criterion

A successful programme proves a quantified local defect cascade and a growing-order high-derivative zero-free theorem whose losses remain summable down to `Xi`.

Until both pieces are proved, derivative line concentration is motivation and structure—not a proof of RH.
