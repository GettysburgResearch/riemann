## Part II — new multilevel descent theorem

For `F_k = Xi^(k)`, let `R_k` be the simple real-zero count in a regular
window, and at each real zero `c` of `F_k` define

```text
rho_(k,c) = F_(k-1)(c)/F_(k+1)(c).
```

For a derivative block `1 <= k <= K`, let

```text
A = sum_k (-sum_c rho_(k,c))_+
B = sum_k sum_c rho_(k,c)^2
R = sum_k R_k
C_block = A^2/(R B).
```

Then exact reverse Rolle and Cauchy--Schwarz give

```text
R_0 >= R_K - 2 R (1-C_block) - K.
```

Thus a high-order line proportion `p_K` and block defect `D_K` imply

```text
p_0 >= p_K - 2 D_K.
```

In particular `D_K < (p_K-0.9)/2` yields more than 90%, while
`p_K -> 1` and `D_K -> 0` yield density one.

The new theorem does not prove the required low-order block-coherence estimate;
it isolates one joint first/second residue-moment target instead of K separate
one-step hypotheses.
