# X-103020 — Star/Plücker reduction replay

The replay checks, for depths `4<=k<=100`:

- the exact minimum-pair star potential;
- its zero sum and monotonicity;
- the complete-graph gradient identity;
- the row-zero cycle condition;
- the exact star and cycle energies.

The analytic one-sided star theorem uses `L-103001` and is proved in the claim file. The replay does not prove `PLC103020`, `BCI102990`, or RH.
