## Packet

At each level, use the unnormalised even basis

```text
b0=e0,
bk=e(-k)+e(k).
```

The exact metric is `diag(1,2,...,2)`. The source-corrector is the all-ones
column, and the source-valid complement is spanned by `-2 e0 + ek`. Therefore

```text
G_W=1+2N,
G_E=2 I+4 J.
```

The consumer emits and replays

```text
Q_W,P_W,E_W,Z_W,C,X_N,R_N,G_W
```

and accepts a level only after exact interval LDL closure of both `C-hG_E` and
`D_N-mG_W`.

## Scope

The bundled ladder has nine genuinely growing finite levels. It is a production
and scaling artifact, not an unbounded theorem and not an RH claim.
