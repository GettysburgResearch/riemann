# X-102710 — Homotopy gauge replay

This standard-library replay checks:

- Euler and half-divisor path endpoints;
- equality of their critical linear coefficient;
- the exact local gauge ratio through order 18;
- absence of a linear term in the transfer and inverse-transfer series;
- root-freeness of the half-divisor tangent;
- the half-order prime-carrier constant used by the no-go theorem.

It does not prove `PHDNC102710` or RH.
