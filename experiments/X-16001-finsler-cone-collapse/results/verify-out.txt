Interpretation (see claims/lemmas/L-16002 and claims/observations/O-16001):
  These propositions establish that the working note's finite gate is
  satisfiable for every ONE-SIGNED target and every special Q, so the
  Finsler condition is vacuous there.  P5 shows why: for a one-signed
  target the interpolation polynomial is real-rooted automatically, by a
  Nevanlinna argument.

  IMPORTANT - do not over-read this.  An earlier reading of these same
  propositions concluded that the whole cofinal programme collapses,
  because Polya's Phi is positive so the targets 'must' be positive.
  That inference is FALSE.  The Connes-van Suijlekom coordinates are
  Fourier COEFFICIENTS, not point samples: the finite target is
  xi_j = (-1)^j F(2 pi j) ~ (-1)^j Xi(2 pi alpha j), which alternates in
  sign, so n_- is about n/2 and the isotropic cone is genuinely nonempty.
  The minimal instance in P4 likewise does NOT refute Connes-van
  Suijlekom Theorem 5.6: the theorem's transform is the WINDOWED one,
  2 e^{-iz/2} sin(z/2) sum_j xi_j/(z - 2 pi j), whose zeros here are all
  real -- not the exponential sum 8/10 + (2/10) cos z used in P4b.
  See O-16001 for the full adjudication.  The scope of these
  propositions is one-signed coefficient vectors, which the programme
  does not produce; they serve as a boundary marker and a screening test.
