## 1. Review objective and verdict discipline

This pass began from the older “third-pass assessment” but did not inherit its rankings. Every load-bearing item was compared with its current branch head and with the newer review/correction wave through PR #255.

The distinction used throughout is:

```text
FALSE       an exact hypothesis-matching counterexample or incompatible identity exists;
UNPROVEN    the supplied derivation is incomplete, the production object is absent,
            or an RH-bearing estimate is still only stated;
VERIFIED    the displayed claim was independently reconstructed at its declared scope;
VERIFIED WITH FIXES
            the claim survives after a specific normalization, scope, or source-selection repair.
```

No stronger conclusion is inferred from a failed proof. In particular, a false conditional-Hankel representation does not make the carry profile negative, and a false absolute-rank surrogate does not make every source-specific signed contraction false.
