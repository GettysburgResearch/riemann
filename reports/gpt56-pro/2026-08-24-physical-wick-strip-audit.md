# Physical Wick/strip audit

The attempt to promote the conditional 92.10% model to an unconditional zeta
theorem exposed two hidden interfaces.

First, the frozen symbol was written without its half-order coefficient even
though all diagonal energies used `/n`.  The corrected symbol and scaled
simplex law are now explicit.  Degree four still has residual energy below
`1/7000` at the full scale `alpha=2`.

Second, pointwise zero-freeness is not required for a compression lower bound,
but this does not solve the contour problem.  Off the real axis a Hermitian
compression polarizes a scalar multiplier as `p p#`, not `p^2`.  The
one-sided source identity therefore needs a genuine Hardy/strip intertwiner.

The safest conclusion-facing replacement for the old 99/101 transfer is a
negative-trace cut.  The right safe-line resolvent is accretive; if the
complete strip-transfer remainder has normalized negative trace below 1/20,
the full-signature theorem gives more than 90%.

That signed estimate remains open.  No unconditional 90% claim is made.
