## 8. PR #243 does not close the carry route

PR #243 is the closest-looking single proof candidate because it asserts an explicit profile positivity

\[
\mathfrak C(y)\ge0
\]

and a quotient-layer decomposition

```text
carry profile
= digital endpoint sum
+ conditional-Hankel square
+ reflected Selberg reserve.
```

If that identity and the separate nonnegativity of all three channels were correct, its Mellin/Landau consumer would indeed prove RH.

However, the current proof contains an exact local failure.

### 8.1 Reviewed spline

On the first open dyadic cell the kernel generator is

\[
f(t)=8e^t-7e^{t/2}-\frac32t e^{t/2}.
\]

PR #243 proves alternating derivative signs and infers that `F_T''` has a positive Bernstein representation.

### 8.2 Exact negative Hankel determinant

A positive Laplace/Bernstein representation requires log-convexity:

\[
k k''-(k')^2\ge0.
\]

For `k(x)=F_T''(x)=f''(T-x)`, exact evaluation at the right-hand cell limit gives

\[
f''(0)=\frac{19}{4},
\qquad
f'''(0)=6,
\qquad
f''''(0)=\frac{109}{16},
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
 k k''-(k')^2
 =\frac{19}{4}\frac{109}{16}-36
 =-\frac{233}{64}<0.}
\]

Thus the asserted positive Bernstein measure does not exist for the displayed kernel, and a local two-point Hankel Gram has negative determinant.

This is recorded in `R-9510` and exact replay `X-9516` on the review branch.

### 8.3 Consequence

The pointwise positivity of the carry profile is not disproved.  It is itself an RH-bearing statement.  What is refuted is the current derivation of its conditional-Hankel square.  Therefore PR #243 cannot be borrowed as the missing positivity theorem for PRs #244, #246, #247, or #248.

Its own finite checker explicitly states that it does not verify the quotient-layer identity, continuum positivity, a cofinal minorant, or RH.

## 11. Why the open theorems cannot be chained circularly

Several tempting compositions are invalid.

### 11.1 Equivalence plus equivalence is not an estimate

Knowing that the fixed-ratio shell, parity comb, analytic-totient moment, square screw, and balanced packet have the same rightmost-zero exponent does not show that the exponent is zero.

### 11.2 Aggregate positivity is not packet coercivity

A reflected Selberg identity for the complete source does not control packet self-energies without an exact coupled source map and a strict Schur reserve.

### 11.3 A positive continuum seed is not a finite feasible minorant

The phase-frame, Gamma-carry, and parabolic seeds require a mass-preserving nonnegative correction.  None of the current branches proves that correction uniformly at all scales.

### 11.4 Geometric dimension is not signed arithmetic cancellation

The exact Möbius hypercubes show that the hard source can have `Omega(K)` independent short coordinates all of the same sign.  A bounded face count, bounded rank, or lineality argument cannot replace cross-parity arithmetic cancellation.

### 11.5 The first-cell firewall prevents silent deletion

Any stitched proof must reproduce the exact fixed-ratio Mertens increment.  A route that sends all genuine terminal rows to an exponentially small class while also calling the first cell “terminal” has conflated two distinct objects.
