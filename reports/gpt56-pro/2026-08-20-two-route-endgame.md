# Two-route endgame after the latest RH repository wave

The latest work rules out several apparent shortcuts:

- the historical paired stopping line loses native rough coefficients;
- positive priority flux is power-sized;
- root-containing Hardy/GCD squares are exactly RH-equivalent;
- the minimal-wavelet \(L^2\) abscissa is the rightmost-zero abscissa;
- supercritical positivity does not interpolate through the prime-harmonic
  wall.

Two mechanisms remain worth attacking.

The first is the centered-Bernstein critical envelope. The new contribution is
an exact logarithmic-area formula for every negative activation cell. This
weakens the pointwise `PATG100200` demand to one explicit sum of local Turán
deficits.

The second is the ordinary-Möbius minimal wavelet after one extra half-order
notch. The notch creates a true zero moment. A Vaughan identity then pays every
complete-lattice/Type-I term with an integrable error and leaves one compact
balanced trilinear.

Both terminal estimates are RH-equivalent and remain open. The advance is that
they contain no hidden source, normalization, activation, analytic-consumer, or
unbalanced-range interface.
