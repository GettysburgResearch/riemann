# Cone-valued Bellman disintegration

The score-debt theorem in T99100 is best viewed as one coordinate of a larger
ordered-cone recursion. The same child measure that carries target mass must
also carry literal score, every ordinary/radix-four capacity, and all owner
labels. Once that requirement is enforced, positive endpoint integration is
merely a virtual root and the complete tree closes by one backward induction.

This removes the need for a distinguished rough-prime path and also removes the
logical gap between row-only integration and child-capacity recursion. It does
not prove the equality endpoint measure or local inequalities of PR #620.
