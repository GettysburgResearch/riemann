# Critical-variation descent recovery

## Executive result

The attempted final step from globally positive quadratic SHARP powers to the
critical linear power produces an exact distributional descent, not an
unconditional sign theorem.

The central formula is

\[
dG_2
=\sum_n\beta(n)n^{-3/2}\delta_{\log n}
 +3e^{-u}\mathfrak H_1(e^u)du,
\qquad
G_2(u)=e^{-u}\mathfrak H_2(e^u).
\]

This converts the negative mass of the linear transform into the critically
weighted downward variation of a positive bounded-variation function.
Ordinary BV is insufficient, as shown by an exact dyadic-jump mutation.

The packet then proves that the required subpower weighted variation is
**equivalent to RH**. It therefore records the sharp mathematical boundary
instead of relabelling RH-scale cancellation as a residual estimate.

## Why this is useful

The result removes several false directions:

- positivity of every fixed supercritical power is not enough;
- ordinary convergence/BV is not enough;
- pairwise Euler positivity is not enough;
- a source-blind square-energy estimate is not enough.

Any true closure must control the critical downward variation through a new
source-specific arithmetic mechanism.
