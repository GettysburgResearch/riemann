# The phase-locked PIG filter is the critical adjoint square of the two-row dyadic dipole

The factor-four spectral half-filter

\[
(1-y)(1-y/2)
\]

is exactly the dyadic opposite-parity source

\[
\omega_2
 =\mu-\frac32\delta_2*\mu+\frac12\delta_4*\mu.
\]

Its average-carry image is supported only at rows two and three. The unique phase-locked factor-16 source is obtained by applying the critical adjoint

\[
(1-2y)(1-4y).
\]

The result has an exact fourteen-row carry image and no row above fifteen.

For the critical hinge inverse coefficients `c_X`, the resulting finite scalar is

\[
\sum_{n=2}^{15}Y_*(n)c_X(n),
\]

and, on aligned endpoints, is exactly

\[
-\frac16\left[
 C_X-3\sqrt2 C_{X/2}+4C_{X/4}
\right],
\qquad C_X=5c_X(2)+3c_X(3).
\]

Its Mellin numerator is the critically phase-locked polynomial `Q_*`, so critical subpower growth of this finite charge is equivalent to RH.

This is a structural consolidation, not a proof of the critical bound. The surviving theorem is now one fixed, phase-locked, finite-row estimate.
