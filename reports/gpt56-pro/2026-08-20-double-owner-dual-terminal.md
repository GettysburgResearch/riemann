# Double-owner dual terminal closure

## Decision

The implication matrix in PR #691 is strongest when treated as a genuine
matrix, not as three prose regions. The new two-sided convex theorem attaches a
nonnegative probability weight to every least/greatest-owner block and removes
all cross-block Hilbert interference.

This produces two natural terminal routes.

## Route A: deterministic long interval

Long endpoint intervals contain an interior Euler product but also enough room
to complete only that interior source. Finite squaring leaves the endpoint
owners untouched. Divisor restriction exposes the sole remaining sign and the
positive renewal theorem removes all later dilation ambiguity.

The exact open statement is a one-sided logarithmic estimate for this
endpoint/divisor packet (`LRNM100704`).

## Route B: centered short interval

Diagonal and short endpoint intervals are already confined to the fixed
ratio-eight shell. Factoring the minimal-wavelet activation zero and centering
the phase circle remove the root. Applying the double-owner Hilbert inequality
to the growing moment direct sum removes every cross-block interaction.

The exact open statement is the logarithmic L2 estimate `SCME100704`.

## Why the combination matters

Neither mechanism is asked to cross its hostile region:

```text
squaring/renewal is not asked to desquare a short physical shell;
phase/moment energy is not asked to control an arbitrary long future profile.
```

The exact scalar identity is `G_mu=G_short+G_long`, so

```text
(G_mu)_- <= |G_short| + (G_long)_-.
```

This proves the combination theorem without a new calibration, collar or
normalization interface.

## Scientific boundary

two estimates, and only these two disjoint matrix pieces, are sufficient for
the existing minimal-wavelet Mellin--Landau consumer.
