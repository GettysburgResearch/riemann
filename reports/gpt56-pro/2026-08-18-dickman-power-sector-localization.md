# Research digest — Dickman localization of the factor-67 critical saddle

## Choice

I attacked the least-prime geometry rather than another shallow stopping depth.
The threshold-flexible no-go proves that every subcritical count depth fails,
while the root Bellman theorem shows that only the zero-hinge scalar is
logically required. The highest-leverage question was therefore whether a
large class of source states could be closed directly without all-coordinate
Hall.

## New theorem

For the literal native state

\[
F(Y,z)=\sum_{P^-(m)\ge z}\mu(m)m^{-1/2}b(Y/m),
\]

and any fixed `theta>0`, uniformly for `Y^theta<=z<=Y`,

\[
F(Y,z)=a\sqrt Y\,\rho(\log Y/\log z)+o_\theta(\sqrt Y).
\]

The limiting function is derived from the exact product-activation simplex and
is proved positive by its integral-average recursion. Thus every fixed
power-scale least-prime state is eventually positive.

## Portfolio change

- the former `theta>e^-1` terminal theorem is superseded by all `theta>0`;
- state-wise RBLPTE is closed throughout every fixed power sector;
- any negative state sequence must have `log p0/log Y -> 0`;
- full CSHT67 remains stronger than necessary and is not inferred from scalar
  positivity;
- the remaining obstruction is the subpower least-prime harmonic saddle.

## Sharp remaining obligation

Prove the native scalar in the subpower critical core. An all-coordinate Hall
flow is one possible sufficient mechanism, but the exact minimal target remains
root RBLPTE67.
