# Low-order Levinson descent: Hermite–Pick inertia assault

## Finding

The summable high-derivative tail does not address the only genuinely hard
step: descent from Xi-prime to Xi. The prior scalar residue-coherence quotient
is also too lossy. An exact all-real cubic has every critical residue correctly
oriented but coherence `16/25`.

## Replacement

The finite algebra admits an exact fixed-window entire analogue. The contour
moment matrix of `-F/F'` has signature equal to good minus wrong extrema, and
nonreal derivative pairs cancel hyperbolically. This removes the need to bound
the nonreal algebraic-square correction before counting signs.

For a finite polynomial, work in the critical algebra `R[x]/(p')` and use the
trace form weighted by `-p/p''`. Its signature is the real-root count of `p`.
The construction is coefficient exact, treats every off-real critical pair as
one hyperbolic block, and admits sign-blind polynomial congruence
preconditioning.

This produces a new matrix route in the same spirit as the recent two-trace
record, but with genuinely new low-order derivative/critical-value data. A
normalized trace ratio above `0.83625` beats the `0.67250` record. If first two
moments remain sharp, the exact sign-resolvent formula supplies a higher
spectral hierarchy.
