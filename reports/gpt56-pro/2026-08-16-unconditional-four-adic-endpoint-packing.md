# Research reset report — unconditional four-adic endpoint-packing proposal

The repository was treated as a theorem library rather than a lineage to preserve. The pass inspected the integrated route map and the live PR graph through PR #532, including factor-67, Target-Lorenz, Q4/First-Hermite, Cauchy-Jordan/Clark, Brownian/Hermite, endpoint/carry, Pick/Loewner, and Robin/Nicolas families.

The selected composition discards all source-tree and numerical-tail machinery. It begins from the unconditional positive endpoint atoms and the exact native detail compiler of PR #530. The new contribution is `L-94200`, a first-crossing estimate for the live triangular greedy. Combined with the exact diagonal bound and sparse native dual, it gives a logarithmic rather than support-dependent endpoint deficit.

A tempting simplified dual `Y_4(q)=4/(7q^2)` was rejected during the pass because it made the zero row spuriously prove RH. The exact dual is instead the von Mangoldt lift

\[
Y_4(q)=\sum_{k=0}^{v_4(q)}2^k\Lambda(q/4^k),
\]

which restores the correct normalization.

The proposal has no named open premise, but its new symbolic first-crossing proof has not been independently accepted. RH remains unproved until that proof and the inherited endpoint consumer are reconstructed.
