# Shifted xi-prime deformation of the low-order Pick matrix

The low-order residue matrix is the linear response of the xi-prime zero set
to `xi' -> xi'-alpha xi`.  This gives a direct bridge to the already formalized
xi-prime explicit formula: the shifted safe-line coefficients are
`C(N;L-alpha)`, and their derivative is the T-105320 reciprocal source after
one exact Hardy division.

This closes the source-typing and coefficient-family part of the low-order
Gram transfer.  The remaining issue is a uniform oriented shifted explicit
formula with error stable under alpha differentiation.  The coefficient-side
radius and H1/H2/H3 derivatives already exist; the zero-side/seam, horizontal
boundary, and canonical-product/common-zero charges remain.

The pass proves no new zero proportion and no RH result.
