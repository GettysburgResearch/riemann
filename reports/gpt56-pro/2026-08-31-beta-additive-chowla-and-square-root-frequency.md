# Beta additive-Chowla and square-root-frequency checkpoint

This checkpoint continues PR #779 without changing its native source.

## New exact arithmetic coordinate

The fixed stationary tent square is

```text
diagonal
+ 2 * complete weighted additive Möbius correlation aggregate.
```

After `g=(n,h)`, each term is a primitive correlation

```text
mu(a) mu(a+r) / [g sqrt(a(a+r))]
```

with exact squarefree, coprimality, factor-67, horizon, and ratio restrictions.
The maximal aggregate has power exponent `2 Theta-1`.

## New spectral coordinate

The same square is the exact sinc-squared second moment of the literal q-free
Möbius Dirichlet polynomial. The classical mean-value theorem pays all
frequencies above

```text
Y^(1/2) log(2Y)^B,  B>1/2,
```

at subpower cost. The retained square-root window still has exponent
`2 Theta-1`.

## Scientific significance

The final beta problem is now simultaneously:

- one calibrated average of additive Chowla-2 correlations; and
- one compact-kernel Dirichlet-polynomial norm on a square-root frequency
  window.

This opens direct interfaces to averaged Möbius-correlation and bilinear
Dirichlet-polynomial methods while retaining a quantitative zero-free-plane
payoff for every power improvement.

## Boundary

The low-frequency/additive cancellation remains RH-equivalent. Paying the
frequency tail, reducing the feature count, or controlling finitely many
shifts does not prove it. RH remains unproved.
