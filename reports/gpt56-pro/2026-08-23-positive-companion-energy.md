# Positive companion energy as the low-order Levinson target

The square-defect field can be chosen so that its real-axis contribution is
not merely explicit but nonnegative:

```text
-Im [(F_(k+1)+lambda F_(k-1))^2 /
     (F_k(F_(k+1)+i delta F_k))]
 = delta (F_(k+1)+lambda F_(k-1))^2 /
   (F_(k+1)^2+delta^2 F_k^2).
```

The optimized bulk is one weighted regression coefficient between adjacent
Xi derivatives.  All remaining loss is carried by the lower boundary/sides
and canonical companion poles.  This produces the concrete mean-value target
`LERC105240` for the 90% programme.
