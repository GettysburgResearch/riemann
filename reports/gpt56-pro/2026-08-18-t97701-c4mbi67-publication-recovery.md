algebra gives

\[
\mathrm{BLPTE67}(X)
\iff U_{\rm full}(X)\ge0
\iff \mathcal A_X\ge0.

`B_(1/2)(t)=sum_(n<=t) mu(n)/sqrt(n)`.  That exact sign is `C4MBI67`.
