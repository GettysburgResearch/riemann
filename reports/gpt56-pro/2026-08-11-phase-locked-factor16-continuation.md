# Phase-locked factor-16 continuation of the PIG/endpoint programme

## Result

The factor-16 endpoint criterion admits a uniquely distinguished replacement:

\[
Q_*(y)=(1-y)(1-2y)(2-y)(1-4y).
\]

It is simultaneously:

- exactly supported on `[X/16,X]` in the endpoint kernel;
- zero-safe in the open critical strip;
- critically phase locked;
- uniformly coercive after square-root normalization;
- the source polynomial of a positive inverse Dirichlet system;
- zero-bare after one finite prefix;
- related to the ordinary complete-prime current by only a fixed dyadic gauge.

The critical identity is

\[
2^{2s}Q_*(2^{-s})
 =16|1-2^{-s}|^2|1-2^{-s-1}|^2>0
\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s=1/2).
\]

At quartic order this polynomial is forced uniquely by exact annular support and phase lock.

## Strategic implication

The endpoint route no longer needs to choose between compact support, pole visibility, and a well-conditioned critical metric. One finite source has all three. Its normalized five-scale operator is the exact adjoint square of

\[
1-\frac{3}{2\sqrt2}S+\frac14S^2
\]

and has spectral interval

\[
\left[\frac{43}{2}-15\sqrt2,
      \frac{43}{2}+15\sqrt2\right].
\]

Thus any source-specific bound proved for the factor-four half-filter

\[
\mathcal H(X)-\frac32\mathcal H(X/2)+\frac12\mathcal H(X/4)
\]

returns to the original critical endpoint energy with no lost mode and no ill-conditioned inversion.

## Honest boundary

This is a structural advance, not a proof of RH. The remaining theorem is an arithmetic bound for the compact annular current or its positive reflected product Gram. Generic Hilbert-space coercivity cannot supply that bound.
