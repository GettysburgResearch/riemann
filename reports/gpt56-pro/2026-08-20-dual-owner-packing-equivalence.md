# Dual owner-packing attack

The Hardy and divisor alternatives were attacked in parallel. Exact root-term
inspection showed that both already contain the canonical filtered scalar.
A converse analysis under RH then proved that all additional tails and divisor
renewals are automatically subpower. Therefore both full criteria are exactly
RH-equivalent.

The useful new structural theorem is the positive local factorization of every
divisor restriction through the base duplicate-67 packet. It clarifies DGOC's
multiplicative geometry and gives the right uniform RH converse, but does not
orient the base scalar.

The attempted proof therefore did not establish either alternative
unconditionally. It did prove that future work must attack the signed
squarefree-core root before inserting it into either positive norm.
