# Conjunctive implication bridges

PR #692 supplies a typed hypergraph; PR #691 supplies the genuine
least-owner × greatest-owner interval matrix and already closes every short
ratio-eight cubic interval. This packet adds the cross-class gluing rules.

The first bridge chooses the shifted-square homotopy **by source sector**:
short sectors use `c=-1`, eliminating activation atoms; long sectors use
`c=0`, eliminating the pre-activation collar. The scalar mixing no-go does not
apply because the sectors are disjoint before observation.

The second bridge is regionwise Schur. Instead of asking one theorem to prove
a global first-owner estimate and another to prove a global largest-owner
estimate, partition the centered long-interval matrix by exact provenance.
For each region only the geometric mean of its integrated row and column
marginals is paid. Finite squaring, divisor renewal, activation calculus, and
phase/wavelet methods can therefore supply different halves on different
regions.

The third bridge is scalar coarea:

```text
bad-set logarithmic sparsity x derivative energy
    -> subpower logarithmic negative mass.
```

Activation/Turan methods are adapted to occupancy; phase-Hasse,
minimal-wavelet, largest-prime, and Vaughan methods are adapted to derivative
energy.

The new elementary interval bound

\[
\sum_{x<p\le8x}\frac1p<\frac34\qquad(x\ge67)
\]

also supplies a direct short-block contraction.
