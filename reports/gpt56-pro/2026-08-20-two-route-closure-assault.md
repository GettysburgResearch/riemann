# Two-route closure assault

## Decision

The live graph was reduced to two routes that are both conclusion-facing and
mechanistically distinct.

### Route A: quadratic upper envelope

The positive transform \(\mathfrak H_2\) is compared to its exact Euler-product
asymptote. The defect is a convergent all-integer Euler source. Its Mellin
transform preserves every off-line reciprocal-zeta pole while remaining
analytic on the positive real axis.

The main new simplification is the finite activation formula `FEAG99980`.
All one-prime, two-prime, inactive-tail, and fully coactive components are
closed. Only mixed activation of three or more labels remains.

### Route B: phase-sensitive cross-core flow

Averaging the priority-Hasse flow over all orders produces a canonical edge
kernel. Its phase transform is an explicit finite Euler product. The potential
difference contributes \(1-p^{i\gamma}\), so the complete neutral cube
residual vanishes identically before physical collapse.

The surviving `PSCP99990` theorem is therefore a nonzero-phase packing problem,
not a root-containing positive square and not independent cube matching.

## Research boundary

Neither route was promoted beyond its proof. No RH-equivalent estimate is
silently imported. The branch is intended to let separate collaborators attack
a finite activation inequality and an explicit nonzero-phase Euler-product
integral in parallel.
