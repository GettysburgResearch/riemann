# Implication matrix: where combinations genuinely matter

## Executive conclusion

The repository has many detectors but only two recurring middle obstructions.

```text
CV  critical signed/weighted variation;
XD  signed cross-core dispersion.
```

Several recent routes that looked independent collapse under exact translation:

```text
AFCD -> ACAD;
LPMW <-> BVD modulo L1;
phase-Hasse signed packing = minimal wavelet;
HTOC and DGOC retain the unknown root term.
```

This does not make RH-equivalent reframings useless.  Their leverage is
different:

```text
AFCD       positive quadratic descent and variation;
ACAD       C1 activation cells and upper-envelope geometry;
LPMW       unique largest-prime ownership;
BVD        Type-I removal and balanced trilinear coordinates;
phase      one-prime edge geometry and Fourier symbols;
MWOC       compact ordinary-Mobius shell and Mertens frame.
```

The implication matrix should therefore be read as a portfolio of coordinate
systems on two hard middle objects.

## Strongest conjunctive packets

### Packet 1: finite completion plus collar variation

Finite Euler squaring makes the small-prime critical core strictly
subcritical.  The large-prime sector becomes depth one.  An oriented
source-faithful variation estimate on that collar would combine with both
proven statements and close AFCD.

### Packet 2: largest-prime plus Vaughan

The smooth largest-prime sector is integrable.  The zero-moment Vaughan Type-I
sector is integrable.  The two remaining terminal forms differ only by an
integrable function, so collaborators may choose whichever coordinates better
expose cancellation.

## Main warning

A complete RH-equivalent criterion is not a missing minor lemma merely because
it has a new name.  But equivalence also does not remove its research value:
the matrix records which coordinates expose ownership, activation, phase, or
bilinear structure that another coordinate hides.
