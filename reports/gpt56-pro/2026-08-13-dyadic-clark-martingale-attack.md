## Result

The safe generalized-Jordan prime source is now an exact dyadic
coefficient-one martingale in logarithmic Clark coordinates.

The main identities are

\[
\ell_{a+b,\sigma}
=
\ell_{a,\sigma}
+
\ell_{b,\sigma+2a}
\]

and

\[
\ell_{2^{j+1}a,\sigma}
-
\ell_{2^ja,\sigma}
=
\int(1-e^{-itu})\,d\nu_j(u),
\qquad \nu_j\ge0.
\]

Every generation has an explicit compound-Poisson first-chaos space, and the
all-generation ledger is their orthogonal direct sum.

This puts the arithmetic source in exactly the same additive dyadic
coordinate as the annular crossed-zero Clark entropy. The remaining theorem
is generationwise source-to-model identification, not control of an infinite
Euler product.
