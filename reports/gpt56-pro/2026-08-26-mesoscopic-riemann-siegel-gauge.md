# Mesoscopic Riemann--Siegel gauge: quantitative repair

The exact Riemann--Siegel gauge factorization is sound. Its attachment to the
closed endpoint-height ledger was not: finite-window Rouché continuity gives
an unquantified threshold, so \(1/\vartheta'=O(1/\log T)\) need not be small
enough in windows containing very tight zero clusters.

The repair freezes the phase-speed reciprocal on
\((\log T)^B\) mesoscopic windows. The companion coefficient is constant on
each window, so the rank-one determinant/height theorem applies directly.
The total shift-height term is

\[
\sum_j\lambda_jN_{5,j}=O(N/\log T)=o(N).
\]

At the same time

\[
|1-\lambda_j\vartheta'(t)|
\ll(\log T)^{-B-1},
\]

so the common carrier is matched to arbitrary fixed logarithmic accuracy.
The endpoint cross-ratio remains a finite packet through \(\zeta^{(6)}\), and
its Wronskian still loses the Xi amplitude connection exactly.

The macroscopic denominator height is again at most \(3N/4000+o(N)\), and the
entire deep model space above height \(1/100\) costs at most \(3N/40+o(N)\).

bound `MESORSGAUGE106620`.
