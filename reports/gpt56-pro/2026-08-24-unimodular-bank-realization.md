# Unimodular bank realization and one-gate reduction

The T-105530 two-channel bank is the first column of an explicit determinant-one
polynomial matrix.  This supplies an exact holomorphic left inverse, so the
bank introduces no zeros, no source-dimension loss, and no strip partial index.
The construction works for arbitrary bounded or nonnormal source operators.

Combining the exact realization with the already pinned right/left folding,
coefficient-freezing, and reciprocal-tail estimates proves the horizontal Xi
bank transfer.  Since the normalized source contraction is O(1/log T), the
horizontal anchor is I+O(log(T)^-3), improving the fixed 9139/9216 reserve to
one asymptotically.

The former two open gates are therefore reduced to the single signed matrix
companion/vertical estimate MATRIXLERC105541.  The result does not erase the
Xi quotient winding: a holomorphic carrier still integrates to zero on the
closed contour.  Ninety percent and RH remain unproved.
