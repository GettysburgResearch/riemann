# Two-sort nilpotent row resolvent: all-depth hardening of the direct-integral candidate

## The idea

The factor-67 recursion should not be audited generation by generation. At a
fixed endpoint it is a finite, well-founded labelled tree. Put every positive
residual source in one source cone, every target-free Hall bonus in a separate
row-only sort, and let the child operator act only on the source cone.

The local physical-row identity is

\[
E=J+ET.
\]

Because every child endpoint drops by at least a factor of 67, `T` is
nilpotent. Therefore

\[
E=J(I-T)^{-1}
\]

with a finite polynomial inverse. This resolves the entire tree before score,
capacity, omission, thinning or cubature is applied.

## Why it matters

The row-only Hall bonus never needs a declared score. Literal score is a linear
functional of the resolved physical row. All ordinary/detail responses and
provenance labels are preserved by the same identity. Exact cubature, if
desired, is applied once to the final finite-dimensional row.

This replaces the most fragile all-depth interface in PR #620. It does not
independently authenticate the compact Hall inequalities, direct endpoint
measure, discrepancy bounds or endpoint consumer.

## Status

The abstract theorem and exact finite replay are unconditional. The application
is a candidate hardening pending hostile reconstruction. RH remains unproved.
