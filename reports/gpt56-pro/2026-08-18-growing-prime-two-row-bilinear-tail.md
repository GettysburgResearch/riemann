# Growing-prime two-row tail: uniform finite-sieve closure and one sharp bilinear gate

The fixed-sieve theorem in PR #579 can be made uniform. For the exact finite Euler projection through primes `p<=z`, the negative prefix is bounded by

\[
D_{j,z}\le2Q_j\sqrt{(j+1)P_z}\prod_{3<p\le z}(1+1/p),
\]

while a literal coprime block gives an all-real positive lower bound. The largest cutoff certified by that inequality satisfies `z_*(X)~log X`.

The full row then factors exactly into the positive truncated row minus one least-prime future tail. Its sharp two-row normalization is

\[
\Gamma_{23}(X)=\max_j \mathcal G_j(X;z_*)/C_{j,z_*}(X).
\]

Eventual two-row positivity is equivalent to `Gamma_23<=1`. This is the new `FPCB23` frontier.

`LAPBR67` is false and cannot be the extremal configuration. Squaring a dyadic block of `FPCB23` produces the same coprime Type-II sign geometry as `SACF`, but loses the phase. The sharp dual of the two-row cone lies on an individual row ray, whereas the completed-parity Lorenz hinge prices a different target-plus-scalar cone.

This packet proves a uniform growing-cutoff theorem and replaces loosely related tail conjectures by one explicit all-depth future-prime correlation. It does not prove that correlation or RH.
