# Even-endpoint fourth-derivative advance

## Main change

The endpoint all-pass construction is not special to `Xi''`.  For every fixed
even order `K=2m`, the complete derivative chain from `Xi^(K)` to `Xi`
telescopes to one endpoint symbol

\[
U_{0,K}
 =\frac{(\Xi-i\lambda\Xi')
         (\Xi^{(K)}+i\lambda\Xi^{(K+1)})}
        {(\Xi+i\lambda\Xi')
         (\Xi^{(K)}-i\lambda\Xi^{(K+1)})}.
\]

All intermediate companions cancel, and

\[
N_K-D_K
 =2i\lambda(\Xi\Xi^{(K+1)}-\Xi'\Xi^{(K)}).
\]

For even `K`, the actual Xi numerator is `i xi` times a nonnegative
exterior-square density.  At `K=4`, that density is

\[
{1\over2}\int
 (u-v)^2(u^2+v^2)\Phi(u)\Phi(v)\,du.
\]

## Exact signed-tail normal form

The remaining quantity is no longer an unnamed contour term.  For a rational
all-pass symbol it is exactly

\[
\int_H^\infty(\xi-H)
 (|\widehat{U-1}(-\xi)|^2-|\widehat{U-1}(\xi)|^2)\,d\xi,
\]

and, by residues, the difference of two positive
Cauchy--exponential Grams.  Every endpoint residue is

\[
{2i\lambda
 (\Xi\Xi^{(5)}-\Xi'\Xi^{(4)})(z)
 \over
 [ (\Xi+i\lambda\Xi')
   (\Xi^{(4)}-i\lambda\Xi^{(5)})]'(z)}.
\]

The endpoint denominator also has the exact centered barycenter

\[
\sum_{D_4(z)=0}\operatorname{Im}z=-4\lambda.
\]

Thus a large positive index requires a substantial population of shallow
upper companion zeros or compensating lower-depth mass.
