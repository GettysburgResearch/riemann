# Carry/CRCTP research reset

The reset asked whether root completion itself was the missing full theorem. The exact answer is no, but the boundary is now sharp.

1. Root completion is exactly sufficient for the **signed** interior split span.
2. It is not sufficient for the positive cone; `R-95040` is an exact six-node Farkas separator.
3. For nonnegative high-node source mass, the positive cone generated by trees terminating at nodes two and three has a complete scalar interval criterion.

This removes a major ambiguity from CRCTP. A future proof must construct a positive transform of the actual source into that interval cone, or return an exact separator. The root reciprocal-zeta scalar remains an independent RH-bearing channel.

No unconditional RH proof is claimed.
