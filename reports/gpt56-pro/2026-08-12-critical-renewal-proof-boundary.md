# Critical renewal proof boundary

The requested factor-four integral has now been classified exactly.

1. Its integrand is the delayed polynomial `H(S)f`, where
   `H(y)=(1-y)(2-y)` and `f(t)=e^{-t/2}int_1^(e^t)h_omega`.
2. `H(S)` is boundedly invertible because `||S||=2^-1/2`.
3. The Fourier--Laplace transform of `f` is
   `Theta_omega(z)/(1/2-iz)`.
4. The denominator is one fixed outer Hardy vector. Therefore the integral is
   finite exactly when `Theta_omega` is meromorphic inner.
5. In the inner case the complete filtered norm is exactly `9/4`, so the target
   tail is at most `9/4`.
6. This proves the integral unconditionally for `omega>=1/2`, and under RH for
   every `omega>0`.
7. In the hard range `0<omega<1/2`, the integral is itself the zero-free/innerness
   theorem. No positive-Jordan diagonal proof is possible: the positive diagonal
   expansion diverges on primes, and all finiteness must come from cross-scale
   cancellation.

RH remains unproved.
