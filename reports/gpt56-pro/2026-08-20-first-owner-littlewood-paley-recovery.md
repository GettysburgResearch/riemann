The exact contribution is the Hilbert identity

\[
\left\|\prod_i(I-r_iU_i)f\right\|^2
=s_k^2\|f\|^2+
\sum_i r_is_{i-1}^2
\left\|(I-U_i)\prod_{h>i}(I-r_hU_h)f\right\|^2,
\]

whose terms are the coefficient-exact future-completed first-owner currents. The free labelled current energy is bounded by

\[
2\left\{\prod_i(1+r_i^2)-1\right\}.
