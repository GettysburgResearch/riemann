higher chaoses.  Its second-order Duhamel formula assigns every squarefree
occurrence canonically and equally among its actual unordered prime pairs.

For depth `k`, each of the `C(k,2)` pairs owns exactly `1/C(k,2)` of the same
signed coefficient.  Repeated-label pair diagonals have activity `1/p` and are
polylogarithmic.

The resulting free pair energy is

```text

```

Collapsing all pair-owner copies of one occurrence costs exactly `C(k,2)`, at
most `O(log^2 Y)`.  Existing half-divisor bounds then make every equal-product
multiplicity subpower.
