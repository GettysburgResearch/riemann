## New result

`L-14316` uses only the support interval. For a normalized time-limited vector,

```text
p_w(xi)=|hat(w)(xi)|^2/(2 pi ||w||^2)
```

is a probability density with height at most `L/(2 pi)`. Minimizing the symbol
against the larger class of all densities satisfying this cap gives the
bathtub lower floor

```text
B_L(s)=sup_G [G-L/(2 pi) integral (G-s)_+].
```

On Suzuki's scaled interval, this is the average of the lowest `pi` units of
exact symbol measure. It is an ambient lower bound, not merely a complement
bound.

The weighted deficit operator also gives

```text
# eigenvalues below G-epsilon
<= L/(2 pi epsilon) integral (G-s)_+.
```

Thus the integrated deficit simultaneously supplies a scalar floor and a
finite weighted low-packet rank cap.

## Why this improves PR #152
