# Five-adic renewal pivot

This pass intentionally abandons the failed atomization paradigm.

The main discovery is that the boundary is not a collection of expensive atoms. It is a self-similar signed renewal source.

## Core equation

The factor-five dilation gives

\[
r_X(5a)+...+r_X(5a+4)=5^{-1/2}r_{X/5}(a).
\]

The remaining four residue channels are adjacent transports. Above the X/5 threshold their coefficients are positive and their total adjacent-tree debt is bounded.

Therefore the only genuine obstruction is the finite inner residue transport.

## Research attack

Replace the infinite boundary theorem with a finite automaton:

residue mod 5 -> local Pascal cycle -> lower scale.

The completion target is a rational certificate proving the local transport has no amplification.

## Status

This is not a proof of RH. It is a sharper closing theorem than previous boundary formulations because it isolates a single finite obstruction.
