# Transition-band breakthrough attempt

The prior local positivity attacks were abandoned after exact counterexamples at Abel orders 1-4. The remaining object is the first-entrance transition source.

The new hypothesis is that arithmetic cancellation should occur after recombination, not before.

The target theorem is Transition Band Recombination:

Sigma_(X,n) belongs to the cone generated by:
- divergence-free Pascal cycles,
- proper-power-neutral squarefree collectors,
- factor-five parity generators.

A proof would imply the existing GFEP/producer positivity chain and therefore the existing RH conditional chain.

Current status:
- exact Green/Markov spine: established;
- first entrance localization: established;
- cone generators: individually defined;
- completeness of generators: open;
- RH: unproved.

This is a research proposal, not a completed proof.
