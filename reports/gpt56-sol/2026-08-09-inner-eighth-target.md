# SHARP inner-eighth target

After `L-32304`, every possible negative average-carry inverse coefficient lies in `j<=T/8`.

The first local Mobius state sign change appears in quotient cell eight. Thus future work should not spend effort re-proving positivity of the outer quotient cells or invoking generic convexity. The exact remaining sign mechanism is the compensation

\[
(j+2)u_T(j)-ju_T(j+1)+\frac{2S_T(j)}{j-1}\ge0
\]

when `u_T(j)` may itself be negative.

The low-row combination `5c_T(2)+3c_T(3)` is independently RH-equivalent on PR #326, so the inner-eighth theorem is expected to require genuinely correlated Mobius cancellation.
